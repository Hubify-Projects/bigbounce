FULL ABSTRACT (from the paper — reference only; ~5500 chars, DO NOT paste, exceeds the 1920 limit):
We present a multi-survey autoencoder anomaly catalog produced by applying the BigAE framework to 37.3 million sources and CMB map patches across six astronomical archives (DESI DR1, SDSS DR18, LAMOST DR10, eROSITA DR1, Planck, NEOWISE; ACT DR6 quarantined). The primary deliverable is a validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies (268,319 point-source), a process-volume figure (anomaly candidates surviving per-survey validation gates across a full-instrument-stream scan, not confirmed physical detections) whose like-for-like science-target benchmark is 2,468 DESI anomaly clusters on validated science-target spectra (~0.92x the largest published single-survey catalog). The full inclusive Path-C unique catalog contains 377,482 anomalies (377,282 point-source + 200 Planck CMB map-patch sky regions); the 268,519 validated subset excludes the LAMOST exploratory tier (~113,000 objects; 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact). eROSITA (298 objects; 1.2% injection-recovery, irreproducible score axis) and synthetic Gaia DR3 (500 objects) are excluded from all counts. DESI clears three independent validation gates: 5-fold cross-validation Jaccard 0.862 (PASS), OOD Jaccard 0.732 (PASS), and injection-recovery 99-100% at 5-sigma for the broad/extended anomaly class. SDSS and Planck likewise pass detector-sensitivity injection-recovery gates. An archival cross-match of DESI top-1,000 anomalies against 18 all-sky catalogs yields a genuine novelty fraction of 17.8% +/- 1.2% (Wilson 68% CI). Two cosmological applications are secondary demonstrations: multi-tracer f_NL bias measurement yields sigma(f_NL) = 8.14 (central forecast, envelope [3.92, 8.98]); NANOGrav 15-yr KDE MCMC yields gamma = 2.567 +/- 0.382, placing the matter-bounce prediction gamma=3.0 at +1.13 sigma. The catalog, model weights, and reproducibility scripts are publicly released at https://huggingface.co/datasets/bamfai/bigbounce-anomaly-catalog with the arXiv posting.
