## Observable 1: Scalar Bispectrum f_NL = -35/8

**Source of the prediction:** Entirely from MATTER CONTRACTION DYNAMICS (w = 0, epsilon = 3/2). Generated at horizon crossing during the contracting phase, BEFORE the bounce. The relevant calculation is standard GR perturbation theory with Bunch-Davies vacuum in a matter-dominated background.

**Role of the bounce:** The bounce must TRANSMIT the pre-bounce bispectrum to the expanding phase without modification. For superhorizon modes (k/k_bounce ~ 10^{-56}), this transmission is trivially T ~ 1 regardless of the bounce details.

**Formalism sensitivity:** The prediction is generated in a regime where LQC quantum corrections are NEGLIGIBLE (rho << rho_c during the contraction phase when modes exit the horizon). The formalism choice only matters near the bounce (rho ~ rho_c), where our modes are 56 orders of magnitude above the horizon.

**Classification: FORMALISM_INSENSITIVE**

The only way formalism could matter is if quantum corrections modify mode evolution at rho << rho_c (far from the bounce). This would violate the correspondence principle -- all LQC formalisms must reduce to classical GR far from the bounce by construction. This is not a subtle or model-dependent argument. It is a foundational requirement of any quantum gravity theory.

## Observable 3: Tensor-to-Scalar Ratio r

**Source:** r ~ 10^{-4} from LQC dressed-metric corrections at the bounce. The tensor suppression is generated by the quantum-corrected z''/z during the bounce itself.

**Formalism sensitivity:** This IS formalism-dependent. The dressed-metric and hybrid approaches give different effective potentials for tensor modes near the bounce, leading to different r values. The regularization of inverse-volume terms and the vacuum prescription at the bounce both affect the tensor transfer function.

**Classification: MODESTLY_SENSITIVE** -- but r ~ 10^{-4} is undetectable by any planned experiment:
- LiteBIRD threshold: r ~ 10^{-3}
- CMB-S4 threshold: r ~ 10^{-3}
- No proposed experiment reaches r ~ 10^{-4}

The sensitivity is theoretically interesting but observationally irrelevant. Even if the hybrid formalism gave r ~ 10^{-3} (a 10x increase), it would be at the very edge of detector thresholds and would not constitute a clean discriminator.

## Observable 5: Initial State Dependence

Different formalisms may prescribe different initial states (vacuum definition at the bounce). The dressed-metric approach defines a natural vacuum via the quantum-corrected geometry. The hybrid approach defines a vacuum via the Fock space for perturbations on the quantum background. These can differ.

**But:** Our modes are set during contraction by the standard Bunch-Davies vacuum at horizon crossing. The initial state at the bounce is irrelevant for modes that are already 56 orders superhorizon. The mode functions are determined by their classical evolution during contraction, not by their quantum state at the bounce.

The initial-state question matters only for modes that are near-horizon at the bounce (k ~ k_LQC). These modes are at frequencies ~ 10^{11}-10^{12} Hz today -- permanently unobservable.

**Classification: FORMALISM_INSENSITIVE** for our observables
