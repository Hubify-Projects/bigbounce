All distinctive geometric effects are bounded by:

```
g_geometric ≤ O(1/M_Pl)
```

For cosmology (E ~ H₀ ~ 10⁻³³ eV): suppression ~ (H₀/M_Pl)² ~ 10⁻¹²².

**Observable effects are generic (scalar-tensor). Distinctive effects
are unobservable (Planck-suppressed). The circle closes.**

## What Remains Open

The structural barriers identified (lock, duality, universality,
suppression) all apply to **single-field geometric mechanisms coupled
through the connection**. Routes that might evade ALL FOUR barriers
simultaneously:

### 1. Multi-field geometric sectors
Multiple torsion modes with mutual interactions could potentially
break the lock through inter-field couplings not tied to M_Pl.
But the suppression theorem still applies to each mode's coupling
to matter.

### 2. Non-perturbative geometric effects
Instantons, topology change, or non-perturbative torsion condensates
might produce effects not captured by the EFT analysis. But these
typically produce exponentially suppressed effects (e^{-S_inst}).

### 3. Fundamentally different geometric frameworks
Higher-dimensional geometry (Kaluza-Klein with torsion), teleparallel
gravity with non-trivial boundary terms, or gravitational theories
with non-minimal matter-geometry coupling beyond the connection
prescription.

### 4. Abandoning distinctiveness
Accept that geometric dark energy IS generic scalar-tensor, and
focus on UV completion / naturalness arguments rather than
distinctive low-energy signatures. This is intellectually honest
but loses the geometric motivation.
