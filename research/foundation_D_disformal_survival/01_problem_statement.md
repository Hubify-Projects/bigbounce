# Foundation D — Problem Statement

**Date:** 2026-03-14

---

## The Obstruction Chain

### Foundation A: Mass-Coupling Lock

PGT propagating torsion modes have m and g controlled by the same
kinetic normalization parameter. R = m/g = const. Making the mode
light simultaneously kills its coupling.

### Foundation B: Topological-Shift Duality

The ALP architecture breaks the lock, and the Nieh-Yan form is
non-topological in MAG. But for pseudoscalars with linear geometric
couplings: mass protection (shift symmetry) and geometric content
(non-topological density) are mutually exclusive. On FRW (T₀ = 0),
the non-topological correction vanishes and Model B = Route T1.
After field elimination: generic ALP.

### Foundation C: Generic Scalar-Tensor Collapse

Environmental mass (m² ~ ξR) evades both the lock and the duality
for scalars. Conformal/gauge symmetry protects m₀ = 0. Mass m ~ H
on FRW. But after field elimination: standard conformally coupled
quintessence. On FRW, T₀ = Q₀ = 0, so the only surviving geometric
quantity is R(g) — the same Ricci scalar as in GR. Generic collapse.

### The common endpoint

Foundations B and C both converge to the same conclusion:

**After geometric fields (torsion, non-metricity) are eliminated,
the surviving effective theory is a CONFORMAL scalar-tensor theory.**

The geometric origin constrains the EFT parameters but does not
produce novel EFT OPERATORS. All weakly coupled scalars/pseudoscalars
from gravitational theories reduce to known EFT at low energies.

## Why Disformal Survival Is Logically Different

Conformal scalar-tensor theories have effective metrics of the form:

```
g̃_μν = A(φ) g_μν     [conformal]
```

This is the ONLY structure in Brans-Dicke, quintessence, and f(R)
gravity. It is what Foundations B and C reduce to.

The next level of complexity is DISFORMAL:

```
g̃_μν = A(φ) g_μν + B(φ) ∂_μφ ∂_νφ     [disformal]
```

This goes BEYOND conformal scalar-tensor theory. It produces:
- Modified propagation speeds for different matter species
- Screening mechanisms not available in conformal theories
- Novel cosmological phenomenology (modified sound speeds,
  kinetic braiding effects)

**The question:** Does torsion/non-metricity elimination from
geometric theories produce disformal effective couplings that
a conformal scalar-tensor theory cannot reproduce?

If YES: the geometric origin produces novel EFT operators (disformal
terms) — a genuine escape from the scalar-tensor universality of
Foundation C.

If NO: the geometric origin is confined to conformal scalar-tensor
territory, and no amount of geometric structure can produce
distinctive low-energy predictions.

## Success Criteria

A successful Foundation D candidate must:

1. **SURVIVE FIELD ELIMINATION** with a genuinely non-conformal
   effective structure. After torsion and/or non-metricity are
   integrated out, the remaining effective metric for matter must
   contain a B(φ)∂_μφ∂_νφ term or equivalent derivative-dependent
   structure.

2. **REMAIN NONTRIVIAL ON FRW.** The disformal term must not vanish
   on homogeneous cosmological backgrounds. If it only matters in
   dense matter or local environments, it is not a dark-energy
   mechanism.

3. **NOT REDUCE TO GENERIC DISFORMAL EFT.** A generic Horndeski or
   DHOST theory with freely chosen functions can produce any disformal
   coupling. The geometric origin must CONSTRAIN the disformal
   functions in a specific way (consistency relations, fixed ratios,
   specific functional forms).

4. **LEAVE A DISTINCTIVE SIGNATURE.** Modified GW speed, anomalous
   fermion propagation, spin-dependent light cones, or specific
   relations among observable parameters that are fixed by the
   geometric couplings.

5. **AVOID PATHOLOGIES.** No ghosts, no unbounded gradient
   instabilities, no superluminal propagation (or at least a
   controlled interpretation of apparent superluminality within the
   EFT validity domain).

---

## Failure Criteria

- **CONFORMAL_ONLY:** No disformal term survives elimination.
- **FRW_VANISHES:** Disformal term exists but vanishes on FRW
  (because T₀ = Q₀ = 0 or because ∂_i φ = 0).
- **GENERIC_DISFORMAL:** Disformal term exists and survives on FRW
  but is equivalent to a freely parameterized Horndeski theory.
  No geometric fingerprint.
- **PATHOLOGICAL:** Disformal structure introduces ghosts or
  instabilities not present in the conformal sector.
