## 0. Where `λ` was hiding, and the convention map

Li, Quintin, Wang & Cai 2016 (arXiv:1612.02036) define, in their Eqs. (2.11)–(2.12),

> `Σ ≡ X P_{,X} + 2X² P_{,XX} = M_Pl² H² ε / c_s²`,  `λ ≡ X² P_{,XX} + (2/3) X³ P_{,XXX}`,

which are **identical to Chen, Huang, Kachru & Shiu 2007 (hep-th/0605045) Eqs. (4.7)–(4.8)** —
the same `Σ` and `λ`, the same `λ/Σ` ratio. No convention translation is needed between the
two papers; the lab's own lane (a) vertex table already carries `λ` in exactly this normalisation
(`c_{V1} = −a³[Σ(1 − 1/c_s²) + 2λ]/H`, `../../cubic_bounce_transmission/lane_a_vertex_table/cubic_vertex_table.py`).

The critical textual fact, and the reason row 14's result was `λ = 0`-conditional in a way that
was not visible in the equation itself: **Li+2016's Eq. (4.19) has `λ` already eliminated.**
Immediately below it they write *"where we have used `ε = 3/2` and `λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/6c_s²` for the
matter contraction stage. Now the only free parameter in the total shape function is the sound
speed `c_s`."* That substitution is derived in their Appendix A: Eq. (A.19),

> `λ/Σ = (1/3)[ (1 + c_s²)(1 + 2s/3)/(2c_s²) − 1 ]`,  `s ≡ ċ_s/(c_s H)`,

which at `|s| ≪ 1` becomes Eq. (A.20) `λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/(6c_s²)`. For comparison they quote
**DBI: `λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/(2c_s²)`**. So `λ` is not absent from Eq. (4.19); it is present through
`c_s` on one particular line in the `(c_s, λ)` plane. Row 19 puts `λ` back as a free parameter.

## 2. Does `r` depend on `λ`? No — exactly, and for a structural reason

`c_s² = P_{,X}/(P_{,X} + 2X P_{,XX})` (Garriga & Mukhanov 1999) and `Σ = X P_{,X} + 2X² P_{,XX}`
are built from `P_{,X}` and `P_{,XX}` alone; `λ` is the first quantity requiring `P_{,XXX}`, an
independent datum of the Lagrangian. The script differentiates explicitly:

> `∂Σ/∂P_{,XXX} = 0`,  `∂c_s²/∂P_{,XXX} = 0`,  `∂λ/∂P_{,XXX} = 2X³/3 ≠ 0`.

The quadratic action — hence `P_ζ`, `P_h`, and `r = 16ε c_s^{2ν−2} = 24 c_s` (row 10 / row 14 /
Li+2016 Eq. 3.18) — is therefore **exactly `λ`-independent**. `λ` is a pure bispectrum knob:
it can move `f_NL` but buys no tensor suppression whatsoever.

   dressed-metric `H_3` of Agullo et al. 2017. The `λ`-independence result of §3 is a **parity**
   statement about a symmetric bounce and is robust to the value of `ε_eff`; an asymmetric
   bounce would not cancel V1 exactly and is not covered here.
4. `s` is set to zero in the bounce integrator (constant `c_s`). §5 shows the only `λ` that could
   help requires `s = O(1)`, which is outside both this lane's and Li+2016's validity — that is
   an argument against the tuned branch, not a computation of it.
5. Planck 1σ `|f_NL| ≤ 5.1` is used as the window; the `2σ` and SPHEREx variants would move the
   `c_s` boundaries by tens of percent and none of them by the `~300×` needed.
