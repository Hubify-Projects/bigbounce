## Why

The A3 paper currently borrows the *observed* CMB numbers — `r < 0.036`
(BICEP/Keck 2021) and `n_s = 0.9649` (Planck) — as if they were properties of
the modelled background. They are not: they are anchors. Ledger row 10 (R6
audit item R6-10) requires the model's own tensor-to-scalar ratio and scalar
tilt, computed for the dust contraction and propagated through each of the
three A2 bounce backgrounds with the lab's own mode machinery.

Separately, `r = 0.84` appears in two *different* senses in the repo and the
two have been conflated:

- `research/track_a3_multichannel/survey_reach_fnl.py:46` — `R_OVERLAP = 0.84`,
  the **noise-weighted bounce-vs-local bispectrum shape overlap** from P2. This
  is a shape correlation coefficient, not a tensor-to-scalar ratio.
- `research/track_a3_multichannel/r5_15_tensor_omega_nhz.py:73` — case
  `B_scenario_matter_bounce_r0.84`, used as a **tensor-to-scalar ratio**
  attributed to "Cai et al. 2009 matter-bounce r = 0.84", and echoed in
  `paper/main.tex:769` as the "unresolved r = 0.84 matter-bounce scenario".

Provenance of the tensor-sense 0.84 is to be established in step 1; the working
hypothesis is that it is a mis-carried copy of the shape-overlap number.

## Plan

1. **Provenance.** Establish where the tensor-sense `r = 0.84` came from; record
   the finding either way (no tuning, no retro-fitting).
2. **Contraction-phase spectra.** For the dust contraction (w = 0, a ∝ (−η)²),
   evolve scalar `v_k = z ζ_k` and tensor `μ_k = a h_k` with adiabatic (Bunch–
   Davies) ICs deep in contraction; read off `n_s`, `n_t`, and `r_before`.
3. **Transfer through each A2 bounce background.** Re-use the A2 background and
   integrator (`research/cubic_bounce_transmission/a2_transmission_linear.py`,
   `lane_b_numerical/bounce_cubic_inin.py`): `T_ζ` already exists; compute the
   tensor transfer `T_h` from `h'' + 2(a'/a)h' + k² h = 0` on the same
   background, hence `r_after = T_h² P_h,before / (T_ζ² P_ζ,before)`.
4. **CMB verdict.** Compare `r_after` to `r < 0.036` and to the quoted 0.84.
5. **Propagation.** Re-run `r5_15_tensor_omega_nhz.py` with the model's own
   `r_after` to a NEW output file (never overwrite the existing JSON).
6. **Deliverables.** `row10_r_ns.py`, `results.json`, `.log`, `.png`, this note
   with a per-background table, a manifest under

   `programs/bounce-theory.json`, and a ≤5-line ledger row-10 status.
