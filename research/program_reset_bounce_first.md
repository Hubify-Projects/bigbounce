## 2. Why Local FRW Geometric Dark-Energy Routes Are Structurally Blocked

The four barriers are not model-specific failures. They reflect
structural properties of how gravitational degrees of freedom interact
with matter:

**Lock (A):** In any gravitational theory where new d.o.f. arise from
the connection, their mass and coupling are both determined by the
gravitational constant. Making a mode light enough for DE simultaneously
kills its coupling to matter.

**Duality (B):** For pseudoscalar modes linearly coupled to geometric
4-forms, mass protection (shift symmetry) requires the 4-form to be
topological — but topological 4-forms carry no geometric content.
Geometric content breaks shift symmetry and destabilizes the mass.

**Universality (C):** On FRW backgrounds, torsion and non-metricity
vanish (T₀ = Q₀ = 0). Only curvature R(g) survives. All geometric
scalars reduce to standard scalar-tensor EFT, indistinguishable from
non-geometric models.

**Suppression (D):** Connection coupling provides one ∂φ per vertex.
Disformal requires two. The structural mismatch means all distinctive
geometric effects are Planck-suppressed: (H/M_Pl)² ~ 10⁻¹²².

The common thread: **local geometric novelty is invisible on
homogeneous FRW backgrounds.** The symmetry of FRW erases the
geometric content that distinguishes spin-torsion gravity from
standard scalar-tensor theory.

## 3. Core Insight

**The spin-torsion bounce cosmology may be correct, but dark energy
likely emerges from a different layer of the theory.**

The bounce mechanism (spin-torsion repulsion at ~10⁷² kg/m³) operates
at Planck-scale densities where torsion is dynamically dominant. Its
physics is robust and structurally sound.

Dark energy operates at ~10⁻²⁶ kg/m³ — a factor of 10⁹⁸ lower.
Attempting to extract a DE mechanism from the same local geometric
sector that drives the bounce requires bridging this gap with a
single field. Foundations A–D demonstrate that no such bridge exists
within minimal connection-coupling frameworks.

The bounce and dark energy are likely **different layers** of the
same cosmological history, not different manifestations of the same
local mechanism.

## 4. New Program Direction: Bounce-First Cosmology

The program pivots from:

> "Derive Λ directly from local torsion geometry"

to:

> "Determine what dark-energy mechanisms are compatible with
> a spin-torsion bounce cosmology"

This is a **bounce-first** approach:

1. Accept the spin-torsion bounce as the core early-universe foundation
2. Ask what constraints, initial conditions, or auxiliary sectors the
   bounce cosmology naturally provides or requires
3. Test whether any dark-energy mechanism is **preferred, prepared,
   or constrained** by the bounce history
4. Accept that dark energy may originate from a sector that is
   **compatible with** but not **derived from** the torsion geometry
