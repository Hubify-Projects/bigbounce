## Why the Minimal Model Is Observationally Inert

Branches H through K systematically explored the minimal
spin-torsion bounce (Einstein-Cartan gravity + Dirac fermions
on FRW) and found NO observable signature:

| Branch | Sector | Result | Root cause |
|--------|--------|--------|-----------|
| H | Tensor amplitude | P_T ~ 10⁻⁶⁴ | (a_b/a_0)² ~ 10⁻⁶⁵ dilution |
| H | Tensor chirality | Δχ = 0 exactly | Parity-even interaction |
| I | DE compatibility | Trivially compatible | ρ_DE/ρ_crit ~ 10⁻¹²² |
| J | State selection | Liouville prevents | Phase-space conservation |
| K | Scalar transfer | T(k) = 1 | Time-reversal symmetry |

**The common root cause is SCALE SEPARATION:**

```
Bounce scale:    ρ_crit ~ 0.21 M_Pl⁴    →  k_b ~ GHz today
Observable scale: k_CMB ~ 0.01 Mpc⁻¹     →  f ~ 10⁻¹⁸ Hz

Gap: k_CMB / k_b ~ 10⁻²⁸
```

The bounce is a Planck-duration event at the Planck scale.
Observable cosmological perturbations are 28 orders of magnitude
larger in wavelength. The bounce is sub-resolution for every
cosmological probe.

## Why Scale Separation Is Now THE Barrier

Nine structural barriers have been cataloged, but they all
reduce to ONE meta-barrier:

**The UV→IR gap.** Planck-scale physics does not imprint
sub-Planck observables because:

1. **Amplitude suppression:** Signals dilute as (a_b/a_0)^n
   with n = 2 (tensors) or 4 (energy density). This gives
   factors of 10⁻⁶⁵ to 10⁻¹³⁰.

2. **Spectral localization:** The bounce effective potential
   is a bump at k ~ k_b. It affects modes k ~ k_b and is
   transparent to k ≪ k_b.

3. **Time-reversal protection:** The symmetric bounce maps
   growing → decaying modes, preventing spectral distortion
   at long wavelengths.

4. **Phase-space conservation:** The bounce is Hamiltonian at
   H ≈ 0, preventing dissipative or selective effects.

All four mechanisms derive from the same fact: the bounce is
too short (t ~ t_Pl), too small (λ ~ l_Pl), and too symmetric
to communicate information to cosmological scales.

## Why UV→IR Bridge Mechanisms Are Logically Different

Previous branches tested the minimal model AS IS. Branch L asks:

> What is the MINIMAL extension that breaks the UV→IR gap?

This is a MODEL-BUILDING question, not a model-TESTING question.
We are not looking for effects hidden in the minimal model.
We are asking what new ingredient could bridge the gap.

A UV→IR bridge must defeat AT LEAST ONE of the four suppression
mechanisms above:

| Suppression | Bridge strategy |
|------------|----------------|
| Amplitude dilution | Lower the bounce scale (less dilution) |
| Spectral localization | Create a prolonged phase (wider potential) |
| Time-reversal protection | Break the bounce symmetry |
| Phase-space conservation | Add dissipation or irreversibility |

## Success Criteria

A viable Branch L candidate must satisfy ALL of:

### S1. Observational visibility
The extension must create an imprint at a scale or in a channel
accessible to current or planned experiments (CMB, GW detectors,
LSS, PTA, spectral distortions, BBN).

### S2. Essential bounce role
The bounce must be ESSENTIAL to the mechanism — not merely
decorative backstory. If removing the bounce leaves the mechanism
intact, the bounce plays no real role.

### S3. Theoretical coherence
The extension must be ghost-free, avoid gradient instabilities,
and have a sensible effective field theory. No hand-inserted
miracles.

### S4. Distinctiveness
The predicted signal must differ from generic inflationary or
spectator-field predictions. If the result is "just inflation
plus a bounce that doesn't matter," this is not a positive result
for the bounce.

### S5. Naturalness
The required couplings and parameters must be technically natural
or at least not absurdly fine-tuned. A mechanism requiring
ξ ~ 10⁻¹²² is dead.

## Failure Criteria

- **FAIL_SCALE_SEPARATION:** The extension doesn't actually beat
  the UV→IR gap. Observable effects remain at k ~ k_b or are
  suppressed by (a_b/a_0)^n.

- **FAIL_NOT_BOUNCE_SPECIFIC:** The mechanism works without the
  bounce. The bounce is a removable ingredient.

- **FAIL_PATHOLOGY:** The extension introduces ghosts, gradient
  instabilities, or superluminal propagation.

- **FAIL_TOO_TUNED:** The mechanism requires couplings or initial
  conditions tuned to the DE or CMB scale without protection.

- **FAIL_JUST_INFLATION:** The mechanism reduces to "inflation
  happens after the bounce" with the bounce providing only
  initial conditions.
