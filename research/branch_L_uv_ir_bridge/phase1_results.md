## The Surviving Candidate

### Candidate A: PGT Lower-Scale Bounce

**Mechanism:** In Poincaré gauge theory, torsion propagates with
mass m_T. The bounce critical density becomes:

```
ρ_crit^{eff} ~ m_T² M_Pl²
```

For m_T ~ 10³–10⁹ GeV, bounce features move to the LISA–LIGO/ET
gravitational wave bands (f ~ 10⁻³ to 1 Hz).

**Why it survives all tests:**

| Test | Result | Reason |
|------|--------|--------|
| L5 (cheap kill) | Pass | No quick kill; amplitude requires calculation |
| L1 (scale separation) | **Pass** | f_b in LISA/LIGO bands for viable m_T |
| L2 (bounce role) | **Pass** | Bounce IS the signal source; LQC gives different features |
| L4 (distinctiveness) | **Pass** | Oscillatory GW features ≠ inflation, ≠ phase transitions |
| L3 (coherence) | Conditional | Ghost-free regions exist; overlap with target m_T unverified |

**Outstanding question:** Does the Foundation A mass-coupling lock
permit m_T in the 10³–10⁹ GeV range while maintaining ghost freedom?

## Why MIXED and Not PROMISING or WEAK

### Not PROMISING because:
1. Only ONE candidate survives, with a significant unresolved
   condition (ghost-free + lock compatibility).
2. The surviving candidate requires going beyond Einstein-Cartan
   to Poincaré gauge theory — a genuine theory extension, not a
   minimal modification.
3. The GW signal amplitude is uncertain and may be below detector
   sensitivity even if the parameter space is viable.

### Not WEAK because:
1. The surviving candidate is genuinely bounce-SPECIFIC (not just
   bounce-necessary). Removing or replacing the bounce changes the
   prediction qualitatively.
2. The observable channel (GW spectral features) is concrete and
   experimentally accessible (LISA, ET).
3. All three possible Phase 2 outcomes (positive, constraint,
   no-go) are publishable.
4. The candidate builds naturally on Foundation A (already computed),
   so Phase 2 extends existing work.

### Not CLOSED because:
A concrete, well-defined calculation remains that could yield a
positive result. Unlike Branches H–K, where all routes led to
null results within the minimal model, Branch L identifies a
specific extension with a specific observable that has not yet
been computed.

## The Structural Lesson

The screening reveals a sharp dichotomy:

**Mechanisms that beat scale separation generically (contraction-era
spectrum generation, inflation, relic production) don't need the
bounce.**

**The only mechanism that is bounce-specific (lowering ρ_crit via
PGT) must change the gravitational theory itself.**

This is the TENTH structural lesson of the program:

> **Lesson 10 (UV→IR Specificity Dilemma):** Observable signatures
> require either (a) physics that works without the bounce (generic)
> or (b) modifying the gravitational theory beyond EC (non-minimal).
> The minimal EC bounce is trapped between irrelevance and
> incompleteness.
