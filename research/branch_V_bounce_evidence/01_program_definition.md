**Created:** 2026-03-17

## Why We Are Pivoting

The first 15 branches (A–O) established a comprehensive closure:
- **All direct routes from bounce to late-time observables are structurally blocked** (13 barriers, 5 failure modes)
- The bounce itself is theoretically robust (nonsingular, finite curvature invariants, well-posed initial value problem)
- The sole surviving prediction (ALP birefringence) is indirect — it is motivated by ECH but not unique to it

This is an intellectually honest result, but it is not the *end* of the program. The closure assessment tested whether the **minimal** symmetric radiation bounce has distinctive signatures. It found T(k) = 1, n_T = 0, Δχ = 0 — complete observational transparency.

**The pivot:** Instead of asking "does the minimal bounce predict anything?", we now ask: **"What is the smallest physically motivated extension that makes a spin-torsion bounce distinguishable from a singular Big Bang?"**

This is the right question because:
1. No real bounce is exactly symmetric — quantum corrections, spectator fields, and phase transitions all break the idealization
2. The minimal model's transparency is a *theorem about the minimal model*, not about bouncing cosmologies generically
3. Several well-studied bounce frameworks (matter bounce, ekpyrotic, LQC with holonomy corrections) DO produce distinctive spectra — we need to understand what they add and whether ECH can accommodate similar extensions

## What Counts as "Evidence"

For Branch V, evidence means a calculation showing that a specific, physically motivated extension of the ECH bounce produces:

1. **A distinctive scalar power spectrum feature** (oscillation, suppression, enhancement) at scales accessible to CMB/LSS
2. **A modified tensor spectrum** (blue tilt, chiral asymmetry, or enhanced amplitude) detectable by future experiments
3. **A specific non-Gaussianity signature** (f_NL template, bispectrum shape) that differs from slow-roll inflation predictions
4. **An induced gravitational wave background** from enhanced scalar perturbations (PBH-scale or otherwise)
5. **A reheating prediction** that constrains N_eff or produces specific spectral distortions

The evidence must satisfy THREE criteria:

### Criterion 1: Physical Motivation
The extension must arise from:
- Known physics at Planck scale (quantum corrections, backreaction, phase transitions)
- Structural features of ECH that are absent in the minimal model (propagating torsion modes, non-minimal couplings)
- Generic expectations from UV completions (extra fields, higher-order operators)

**Not acceptable:** Ad hoc modifications designed to produce a desired spectrum.

### Criterion 2: Calculability
The extension must produce predictions that are:
- Quantitatively computable (ODE or PDE system with well-defined initial conditions)
- Parameterized by at most 2–3 new parameters beyond the minimal model
- Checkable against existing data (Planck, ACT, DESI, PTA)

### Criterion 3: Distinctiveness
The prediction must:
- Differ from standard slow-roll inflation predictions at > 2σ for at least one observable
- Not be reproducible by generic scalar field models without the bounce
- Have a clear null test (what observation would falsify the extension?)

## Success Criteria

Branch V succeeds if it identifies **at least one** extension satisfying all three criteria AND produces a Phase 1 calculation (analytic + numerical) for the leading observable.

### Grading Scale

| Grade | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **A** | Extension produces > 3σ distinctive prediction at Planck/ACT scales; paper-ready |
| **B** | Extension produces 2–3σ prediction; needs next-generation data (LiteBIRD, CMB-S4) |
| **C** | Extension produces detectable-in-principle prediction; needs 2+ generations of experiments |
| **D** | Extension is calculable but prediction is generic (could arise without bounce) |
| **F** | Extension fails Criterion 1 (unmotivated) or is incalculable |

### Failure Criteria

Branch V is **closed** if:
1. All extensions in the channel map (file 02) score D or F
2. Every calculable extension reduces to a known model (matter bounce, ekpyrotic, etc.) with no ECH-specific content
3. The best candidate requires fine-tuning of > 3 parameters to produce detectable signals

## Guiding Principle

> **The true mission of this project is to provide positive, novel, and meaningful evidence/theory support that a spin-torsion bounce is a better early-universe paradigm than a singular Big Bang.**

This is not about proving the bounce happened. It is about identifying the *sharpest possible test* — the observation that would most decisively distinguish a spin-torsion bounce from no bounce at all.
