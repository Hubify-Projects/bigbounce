**Date:** 2026-03-14

## Verdict: FOUNDATION_C_PARTIAL_BUT_WEAK

---

## Executive Summary

The environmental mass mechanism (m² ~ ξR) does evade both the
mass-coupling lock (Foundation A) and the topological-shift duality
(Foundation B) for SCALAR fields. The mass tracks the Hubble scale
(m ~ H) on FRW backgrounds, which is cosmologically relevant.

But: after all geometric fields are eliminated, the surviving theory
is standard scalar-tensor gravity (quintessence / Brans-Dicke /
conformally coupled scalar). No distinctive geometric fingerprint
survives to low energies. The mechanism works as dark energy but does
not produce anything new beyond known scalar-tensor models.

The geometric origin provides MOTIVATION (symmetry-protected m₀ = 0)
but not PREDICTION (nothing distinguishes it from a generic scalar-
tensor theory with the same coupling).

### Candidate A: Conformally Coupled Scalar

```
m²_eff = R/6,   g = α/M_Pl
```

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| Lock | **PASS** — m depends on R (env.), g depends on α/M_Pl |
| Duality | **PASS** — scalar, conformal symmetry protects m₀=0 |
| FRW | **PASS** — m ~ H in matter/DE era, m = 0 in radiation |
| Reduction | **FAIL** — standard conformally coupled quintessence |
| Naturalness | **PASS** — conformal symmetry, radiative corrections O(H⁴/M_Pl²) |

**Overall: FAIL_GENERIC_COLLAPSE**

The mechanism works — m ~ H is natural and cosmologically relevant.
But it IS standard scalar-tensor gravity. A field theorist writing
S = -½(∂φ)² - (R/12)φ² + (α/M_Pl)φT without any geometric
motivation produces the same theory.

### Candidate B: PGT 0⁺ Torsion + ξR Correction

```
m²_eff = M_Pl²/(16π|t₂|) + ξR,   g = 1/(M_Pl√|t₂|)
```

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| Lock | FORMALLY PASS but ξR/m₀² ~ 10⁻¹²⁰ |
| FRW | **FAIL** — bare PGT mass dominates by factor 10¹²⁰ |

**Overall: DEAD ON ARRIVAL**

The mass-coupling lock makes the PGT bare mass ~M_Pl. The
environmental correction ~H is irrelevant. Adding ξR to a locked
PGT mode does not produce a cosmologically light field.

**Lesson:** The lock must be broken BEFORE environmental corrections
can matter. Foundation A's lock is not circumvented by adding
curvature-dependent terms.

Curvature-dependent kinetic normalization rescales m and g by the

the ratio, not of the individual values.

## Structural Findings

### 1. The Lock-Environment Incompatibility

For PGT torsion modes (Foundation A), the bare mass is ~M_Pl. The
environmental correction ξR ~ H² is 10¹²⁰ times smaller. The
environmental mechanism CANNOT overcome the PGT lock — it would
need ξ ~ 10¹²⁰, which is absurd.

**The environmental mass route requires a field with m₀ = 0
(protected by symmetry), NOT a PGT torsion mode.**

### 2. The Scalar-Tensor Universality

All surviving candidates (A, C, D) reduce to scalar-tensor theories
after field elimination. This is because:

- On FRW, torsion T₀ = 0 and non-metricity Q₀ = 0.
- The only geometric quantity surviving on FRW is R(g) — the
  metric Ricci scalar, which is the same in GR and any metric theory.
- Therefore, any environmental mass term on FRW is of the form
  m² ~ ξR(g) — standard non-minimal coupling.

**No torsion or non-metricity effect survives on FRW. The geometric
origin is invisible in cosmology.**

### 3. The Duality Is Evaded for Scalars

The topological-shift duality (Foundation B) applies only to
pseudoscalars with linear couplings to topological densities. For
SCALARS, conformal or gauge symmetry protects m₀ = 0 without
requiring topological structure. Foundation C successfully evades
the Foundation B obstruction.

However: the duality evasion is a necessary condition, not sufficient.
Evading the duality means the mechanism CAN work, not that it
produces distinctive predictions.

### 4. Kinetic Normalization Cannot Break the Lock

Candidate E proves that curvature-dependent kinetic normalization
Z(R) does NOT break the mass-coupling lock. Both m and g scale with
1/√Z, so their ratio is independent of Z. This is a general result:
any mechanism that modifies only the kinetic sector preserves the
lock.

## Strongest Surviving Candidate

**Candidate D (Weyl scalar)** is the strongest on theoretical grounds:
- Gauge-protected m₀ = 0 (most robust protection mechanism)
- m ~ H on FRW (cosmologically relevant)
- Lock cleanly broken

But it is phenomenologically equivalent to Candidate A (conformally
coupled scalar) and produces no distinctive geometric predictions.

## Recommended Next Move

The research program has now tested three foundations, each addressing
a different structural level:

1. **Foundation A:** The propagating torsion mode exists but is locked.
2. **Foundation B:** The lock breaks but the coupling is generic.
3. **Foundation C:** The mass is environmental but the theory is generic.

The common endpoint: **after field elimination, everything reduces to
known EFT (generic ALP or generic scalar-tensor).**

This suggests the problem is not finding the RIGHT geometric theory
but confronting a THEOREM-LIKE obstruction: at low energies, all
weakly coupled scalars/pseudoscalars from gravitational theories
reduce to known EFTs. The geometric origin is a UV completion, not
an IR signature.

### Three possible directions

**Direction 1: Accept generic EFT, look for parameter correlations.**

Instead of seeking a distinctive coupling structure, look for
PARAMETER CORRELATIONS imposed by the geometric origin. For example:
in f(R) gravity, the scalaron mass and coupling are related by the
form of f(R). In PGT, the torsion mode parameters are constrained
by ghost-freedom. These correlations might not be reproducible by a
generic scalar-tensor theory — they represent the geometric "fine
structure" within the EFT.

**Direction 2: Explore higher-derivative / disformal couplings.**

Torsion elimination in PGT can produce DISFORMAL couplings
(g̃_μν = Ag_μν + B∂_μφ∂_νφ) that are specific to the geometric
theory. These go beyond the scalar-tensor framework and could
provide distinctive predictions (modified gravitational wave speed,
novel screening). This is the only escape from the scalar-tensor
universality identified in the distinctiveness analysis.

**Direction 3: Abandon the single-field approach.**

The research program has been looking for a single geometric degree
of freedom (one torsion mode, one pseudoscalar, one scalar) to serve
as dark energy. Perhaps the geometric theory's predictions are richer
in the MULTI-FIELD sector (multiple torsion/non-metricity modes with
correlated masses and couplings). The correlations between multiple
modes might be the geometric fingerprint.

### Recommended immediate action

**Direction 2 (disformal couplings from torsion elimination)** is the
most concrete and testable. It requires:

1. Take the PGT action with a propagating 0⁺ or 0⁻ mode.
2. Eliminate torsion from the fermion sector (Dirac equation in PGT).
3. Check whether the effective metric for fermion propagation acquires
   a disformal piece from the torsion field.
4. If yes: this is a distinctive geometric coupling that goes beyond
   standard scalar-tensor theory and could produce novel predictions
   (gravitational wave speed ≠ c, modified light cone for neutrinos).

This would be **Foundation D**.
