# Branch H: Bounce-Only Early-Universe Program

**Date:** 2026-03-15

---

## Motivation

Foundations A–G established that the spin-torsion bounce has NO
connection to late-time dark energy. But the bounce itself remains a
valid, well-motivated early-universe mechanism:

- **Singularity avoidance:** Replaces the Big Bang singularity with
  a finite-density bounce at ρ_crit ~ M_Pl⁴
- **Minimal theory:** Einstein-Cartan gravity + Dirac fermions.
  No new fields, no new parameters, no new symmetries
- **Concrete mechanism:** Spin-torsion repulsion provides the
  repulsive force at Planck density
- **Falsifiable:** Makes predictions independent of dark energy

## What This Branch IS

Study the spin-torsion bounce as a STANDALONE early-universe theory.

Questions to answer:
1. What are the observational signatures of the bounce?
2. How do bounce predictions differ from inflation?
3. What constraints do current data place on the bounce?
4. Is the bounce compatible with the observed CMB, BBN, and LSS?
5. What future experiments could confirm or rule out the bounce?

## What This Branch Is NOT

- NOT a dark energy program (closed by A–G)
- NOT an attempt to derive Λ from geometry
- NOT a competitor to ΛCDM for late-time cosmology
- NOT inflation — different mechanism, different predictions

## Relationship to Paper 1

Paper 1 ("Geometric Dark Energy") treated the bounce as motivation
for a DE framework. The phenomenological predictions (ΔN_eff, S₈
tension, birefringence) were derived from an EFT that is now
understood to be generic (Foundation C: scalar-tensor universality).

Branch H asks: what predictions come FROM THE BOUNCE ITSELF,
not from an EFT inspired by it?

## Success Criteria

A Branch H result is successful if it:
1. Identifies at least one observable that is DISTINCTIVE to the
   spin-torsion bounce (not shared by generic bouncing models)
2. Shows the prediction is within reach of current or planned
   experiments
3. Survives consistency checks (BBN, CMB, stability)

## Failure Criteria

Branch H fails if:
- All bounce signatures are degenerate with inflation
- All distinctive predictions are at unobservable scales
- The bounce requires fine-tuning to match observed cosmology

## The Bounce Physics

### Modified Friedmann equation
```
H² = (8πG/3)(ρ - ρ²/ρ_crit) + Λ/3 - k/a²
```

### Critical density
```
ρ_crit = 8 ρ_Pl / (3 κ²) ~ 0.21 M_Pl⁴
```
where κ is the spin-torsion coupling constant.

### Bounce conditions
- H = 0 at ρ = ρ_crit (for k = 0, Λ = 0)
- Ḣ > 0 (expansion begins)
- All curvature invariants finite: R ~ M_Pl², R_μνρσ R^μνρσ ~ M_Pl⁴

### Key parameters
| Parameter | Value | Origin |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| ρ_crit | ~ 0.21 M_Pl⁴ | EC gravity + Dirac fermions |
| T_bounce | ~ 10¹⁹ GeV | Planck scale |
| Δt_bounce | ~ t_Pl | Planck time |
| a_min | Model-dependent | Depends on pre-bounce history |
