**Date:** 2026-03-15

## Observable 1: Primordial Gravitational Wave Spectrum

### Mechanism
The bounce generates a characteristic gravitational wave (GW)
spectrum through:
- Parametric amplification of tensor perturbations during the
  bounce transition
- The bounce duration Δt ~ t_Pl sets a characteristic frequency
- The spectrum shape differs from inflationary (not scale-invariant)

### Distinctive signature
```
P_T(k) ~ k^{n_T}  with  n_T > 0  (blue-tilted)
```
Inflation generically predicts n_T < 0 (red-tilted, consistency
relation n_T = -r/8). A BLUE tilt is a smoking gun for a bounce.

### Scale
- Characteristic frequency: f_bounce ~ 10¹⁰ Hz (Planck scale,
  redshifted to today)
- CMB-scale modes: depend on pre-bounce contraction history
- LISA/ET band: may be accessible if spectrum extends to lower f

### Current constraints
- CMB: r < 0.036 (BICEP/Keck 2021)
- Direct detection: none yet at relevant frequencies
- BBN: ΔN_eff < 0.4 constrains integrated GW energy density

### Assessment
**HIGH PRIORITY.** Blue-tilted GW spectrum is the single most
distinctive bounce prediction. Computation requires solving tensor
perturbation equations through the bounce.

## Observable 2: Scalar Perturbation Spectrum

### Mechanism
Scalar perturbations passing through the bounce undergo:
- Mode mixing (growing → decaying and vice versa)
- Amplification from the bounce curvature
- Possible generation of non-Gaussianity

### Distinctive signature
- Scale-dependent spectrum (not Harrison-Zel'dovich)
- Characteristic oscillations from bounce dynamics
- n_s prediction depends on pre-bounce contraction

### Current constraints
- Planck: n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, A_s = (2.10 ± 0.03) × 10⁻⁹
- The bounce must produce near-scale-invariant spectrum somehow

### Challenge
Reproducing n_s ~ 0.96 requires a LONG matter-dominated contraction
before the bounce (ekpyrotic or matter-bounce scenarios). The
spin-torsion bounce mechanism itself does not determine the
pre-bounce history.

### Assessment
**HIGH PRIORITY BUT DIFFICULT.** Requires specifying the full
cosmological history, not just the bounce itself. The bounce alone
is not predictive for scalar perturbations without a pre-bounce
model.

## Observable 3: Parity Violation from Spin-Torsion Coupling

### Mechanism
Torsion couples to the axial current of Dirac fermions:
```
S_torsion ~ ∫ √g  T^μ J_{5μ}
```
This is P-odd (parity-violating). At the bounce (maximum torsion),
parity violation is maximal.

### Distinctive signature
- EB and TB correlations in the CMB
- Circular polarization of gravitational waves
- Leptogenesis from parity-violating bounce dynamics

### Current constraints
- Planck: α_EB = 0.30° ± 0.11° (2.4σ hint of birefringence)
- But: birefringence from the bounce occurs at reheating, not at
  recombination. The signal propagates to low energy differently
  than late-time birefringence.

### Assessment
**MEDIUM PRIORITY.** The parity violation is real and physical, but
connecting it to observable birefringence requires careful
computation of how the signal propagates from Planck scale to CMB
scale. May be washed out by intervening physics.

## Observable 4: BBN Modifications from Bounce Dynamics

### Mechanism
If the bounce leaves ANY imprint on the radiation bath at T ~ MeV:
- Modified expansion rate during BBN
- Modified neutrino-to-photon ratio
- Extra radiation degrees of freedom (ΔN_eff)

### Distinctive signature
- ΔN_eff from spin-torsion contribution to energy density
- Modified He⁴ abundance
- Possible deuterium abundance shift

### Current constraints
- Planck + BBN: N_eff = 2.99 ± 0.17
- D/H = (2.527 ± 0.030) × 10⁻⁵

### Challenge
The bounce occurs at ρ ~ M_Pl⁴. BBN occurs at ρ ~ (MeV)⁴. The
gap is 10⁷⁶ in energy density. Unless the bounce leaves a RELIC
that persists to BBN, there is no modification.

### Assessment
**LOW PRIORITY.** Scale separation makes bounce modifications to
BBN negligible unless there is a specific relic or phase transition
that connects the two eras. This is the same scale separation
barrier (Foundation E) applied to BBN instead of DE.

## Observable 5: Bounce-Specific Non-Gaussianity

### Mechanism
The nonlinear dynamics of the bounce generate non-Gaussian
perturbations through:
- Mode-mode coupling at the bounce
- The ρ² term in the Friedmann equation
- Higher-order torsion effects

### Distinctive signature
- f_NL with specific momentum dependence (shape)
- Bounce non-Gaussianity peaks at scales that cross the bounce
- Different shape from inflationary (equilateral, local, folded)

### Current constraints
- Planck: f_NL^local = -0.9 ± 5.1, f_NL^equil = -26 ± 47

### Assessment
**MEDIUM PRIORITY.** Non-Gaussianity is a genuine bounce
prediction, but computing the specific shape requires a full
perturbation theory through the bounce, which is technically
challenging. Most useful as a DISCRIMINATOR between bounce models,
not as a detection channel.

## Observable 6: Trans-Planckian Censorship and the Bounce

### Mechanism
The Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture (TCC) states that
sub-Planckian quantum fluctuations should never become
super-Hubble classical perturbations. In inflation, this
constrains the duration of inflation. In a bounce:

- Pre-bounce contraction brings modes from super-Hubble to
  sub-Hubble
- The bounce transition may or may not cross the Planck threshold
- TCC compliance is DIFFERENT for bouncing vs inflationary cosmologies

### Distinctive signature
- Constraints on the maximum scale factor ratio a_max/a_min
- Possible imprint on the maximum observable scale
- Different tensor-to-scalar consistency relation

### Assessment
**LOW-MEDIUM PRIORITY.** Theoretically interesting constraint on
the bounce, but not directly observable. Most useful as a
CONSISTENCY CHECK rather than a detection channel.

## Observable 7: Bounce-Induced Magnetic Fields

### Mechanism
If the bounce breaks conformal invariance of electromagnetism
(through spin-torsion coupling or curvature effects):
- Primordial magnetic fields generated at the bounce
- Characteristic coherence length set by bounce dynamics
- Amplitude set by the bounce energy scale

### Distinctive signature
- Primordial B-field with specific spectrum
- Faraday rotation of CMB polarization
- Contribution to blazar observations

### Current constraints
- Lower bounds from blazar observations: B > 10⁻¹⁶ G (debated)
- Upper bounds from CMB: B < few nG at Mpc scales

### Assessment
**LOW PRIORITY.** Requires conformal symmetry breaking, which is
not automatic in EC gravity. Interesting but speculative.
