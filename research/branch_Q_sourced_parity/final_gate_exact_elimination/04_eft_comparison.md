## The ECH Reduced Action (from exact torsion elimination)

After torsion elimination with gamma(x) = gamma_0 + phi/f_phi, the complete
bosonic effective Lagrangian (integrating out torsion, keeping fermions and
photons) is:

```
L_ECH = (1/2 kappa) R
      + [1/2 + Z_T(gamma_0 + phi/f_phi)] (partial phi)^2
      - V(phi)
      + C_1(gamma_0 + phi/f_phi) * (1/f_phi) * partial_mu phi * J^{5,mu}
      + C_2(gamma_0 + phi/f_phi) * (kappa/4) * (J^5)^2
      - (1/4) F_{mu nu} F^{mu nu}
      + [alpha N_eff / (4 pi f_eff)] phi F Ftilde  [1-loop, from ABJ anomaly]
```

where Z_T, C_1, C_2 are known closed-form functions of their argument
(rational functions of gamma_0 + phi/f_phi), and f_eff includes the
canonical normalization correction.

## The Generic ALP EFT

The most general ALP effective Lagrangian consistent with a shift symmetry
phi -> phi + const (broken only by V and the anomaly term) is:

```
L_ALP = (1/2 kappa) R
      + (1/2) Z(phi/f_a) (partial phi)^2
      - V(phi)
      + [c_psi(phi/f_a) / f_a] partial_mu phi * J^{5,mu}
      + [G(phi/f_a)] (J^5)^2
      - (1/4) F_{mu nu} F^{mu nu}
      + (c_gamma / f_a) phi F Ftilde
```

where Z, c_psi, G are arbitrary functions (Taylor-expandable) and c_gamma is
a constant fixed by the anomaly.

**Match: IDENTICAL.**

The ECH torsion-eliminated action is EXACTLY of the generic ALP form. The only
content specific to ECH is:

1. **Specific functional forms** for Z_T, C_1, C_2 in terms of gamma_0 and f_phi.

3. **The restriction** that c_gamma arises only from the ABJ anomaly (no tree-level
   phi-F-Ftilde).

None of these constitute a new operator. They are coefficient predictions within
the standard ALP operator basis.

## Could the coefficient predictions be tested?

### Coefficients that are in principle measurable:

1. **f_eff (effective decay constant):** Measurable from birefringence. But f_eff
   depends on both f_phi and gamma_0. Since gamma_0 is unknown, f_eff is
   effectively a free parameter. Measuring f_eff does not test the ECH relation.

2. **The four-fermion coupling G(phi):** This involves kappa ~ 1/M_Pl^2,
   making it a Planck-suppressed contact interaction. It is unmeasurable at
   any foreseeable experiment.

3. **The phi-dependence of the four-fermion coupling:** The variation of G
   with phi/f_phi is a dimensionless O(1) effect, but G itself is
   Planck-suppressed. So the phi-dependent correction is also Planck-suppressed.
   Unmeasurable.

4. **The non-canonical kinetic term:** This affects the phi self-interactions
   and the effective potential after field redefinition. But the self-interactions
   are O(1/f_phi^2) ~ O(1/M_Pl^2), and the effective potential is unknown
   anyway. Unmeasurable.

5. **The ABJ anomaly coefficient:** Universal. Not a test of ECH vs. generic ALP.

### Coefficients that could distinguish ECH from generic ALP:

**NONE.** Every measurable quantity (f_eff, c_gamma) is a single number that
can be matched by a generic ALP with the appropriate parameter choice. The
ECH-specific relations connect unmeasurable coefficients (Planck-suppressed
operators) to the measurable ones.

## The Mathematical Reason

The exact torsion elimination is an ALGEBRAIC operation (torsion is non-propagating).
Algebraic elimination of an auxiliary field cannot change the operator content of
an effective theory -- it can only change the COEFFICIENTS.

More precisely: the torsion equation of motion is:

```
T = (algebraic function of gamma, kappa) * [J^5, d phi]
```

Substituting back:

```
L_reduced = (known functions of gamma_0, phi/f_phi) * [standard operators built from phi, J^5, (partial phi)]
```

The operators available are fixed by the field content (phi, psi, A_mu) and
the symmetries (Lorentz, gauge, approximate shift). Torsion elimination cannot
generate new operators because torsion is not a propagating degree of freedom --
it has no independent dynamics that could create new structures.

This is a theorem, not an approximation. No amount of higher-order calculation
in phi/f_phi can change this conclusion.
