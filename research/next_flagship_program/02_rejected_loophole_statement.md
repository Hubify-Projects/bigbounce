## The Loophole

Appending a phenomenological late-time dark energy parametrization (e.g., CPL: w(z) = w0 + wa(1-a)) to a bounce cosmology framework, treating the early-universe bounce and the late-time dark energy sector as independent modules.

---

## 1. Why It Was Scientifically Tempting

Three converging pressures made this loophole attractive:

**Observational pressure:** DESI DR1 (2024) and DR2 (2025) reported 2.8-4.2 sigma frequentist preference for w0 > -1 and wa < 0 when combined with CMB and supernova data. This created demand for models with dynamical dark energy, and adding w0wa to any framework is a simple way to accommodate this signal.

**Fit-level gap in Paper 1:** Our Paper 1 ("Geometric Dark Energy from Spin-Torsion Cosmology") derives an effective dark energy contribution from ECH spin-torsion coupling but produces w = -1 at late times. The DESI hint for w != -1 is not accommodated. Adding w0wa freedom would have brought our model into the DESI-preferred region.

**Trivial compatibility:** Branch I analysis confirmed that any reasonable late-time DE model is trivially compatible with the bounce — the two sectors are separated by ~122 orders of magnitude in energy density and do not interact. There is no technical obstruction to adding w0wa.

---

## 2. Exactly How It Would Have Saved Paper 1

**Before the splice:** Paper 1 predicts w = -1 at late times. Against DESI DR2 + CMB + SNe, this is ~2.8-4.2 sigma worse than w0waCDM in frequentist model comparison. The bounce provides Delta-Neff ~ O(0.01) from spin-torsion density, but this does not help the w = -1 tension.

**After the splice:** Adding w0 and wa as free parameters would:
1. Absorb the DESI w(z) signal, improving chi-squared by Delta-chi^2 ~ 8-16
2. Allow the model to sit in the DESI-preferred (w0 > -1, wa < 0) quadrant
3. Preserve all bounce-sector predictions (Delta-Neff, primordial spectrum) unchanged
4. Claim "bounce cosmology with dynamical DE fits DESI better than LCDM"

**Concrete estimate:** For DESI DR2 + Planck + PantheonPlus, w0waCDM improves Delta-AIC ~ -6 to -8 over LCDM. Our model with w0wa appended would inherit this improvement verbatim, since the bounce sector and DE sector are completely decoupled (Branch I result).

---

## 3. Why It Improves Fits Without Solving the Problem

**The improvement comes entirely from the w0wa freedom, not from the bounce.**

Adding w0wa to "bounce + LCDM" gives exactly the same fit improvement as adding w0wa to plain LCDM. The bounce contributes nothing to the dark energy sector. This is confirmed by:

- **Foundation F (attractor-sensitivity dilemma):** If the DE sector has an attractor, bounce initial conditions are erased. The late-time w(z) remembers nothing about the bounce.
- **Foundation G (cyclic incompatibility):** The bounce cycle cannot select the vacuum energy. Lambda is external to the bounce dynamics.
- **Branch I (122-order separation):** The energy scales of the bounce (rho_c ~ 0.21 M_Pl^4) and dark energy (rho_Lambda ~ 10^-122 M_Pl^4) are causally disconnected. No mechanism transmits information between them.
- **IR vacuum program (all 4 routes closed):** All minimal routes to deriving w = -1 from the ECH framework were exhaustively tested and failed. The framework is phenomenological at the DE level.

**The logical structure of the loophole:**
1. Bounce addresses the singularity and primordial perturbations
2. DE addresses the late-time expansion history
3. The two are spliced together with no causal or theoretical connection
4. The fit improvement is attributed to "bounce cosmology" but is actually from the w0wa parametrization alone

---

## 4. Why We Rejected It

**This loophole would have saved Paper 1 at fit level, but we rejected it on first-principles grounds because it replaces derivation with phenomenological late-time freedom.**

Specifically:

**It violates the research standard we are trying to meet.** Our goal is not "a model that fits data" — it is "evidence that bounce cosmology is scientifically competitive with inflation." Adding w0wa does not advance this goal because inflation can do the same thing (and does: w0waCDM is framework-agnostic).

**It is not falsifiable as a bounce prediction.** If the DE sector is a free function, the bounce model cannot make a wrong prediction about dark energy. An unfalsifiable prediction is not a prediction.

**It conflates compatibility with derivation.** The fact that the bounce is compatible with w0wa (Branch I) does not mean the bounce explains w0wa. Gravity is compatible with your shoes, but your shoes do not explain gravity.

**It reduces to the exact failure mode identified in our own analysis.** The IR vacuum program explicitly lists "the framework is a phenomenological parameterization, like wCDM or CPL" as a failure mode. Implementing the loophole would actualize the identified failure.

**The literature already does this.** arXiv:2601.03028 (bounce inflation + CPL, January 2026) does exactly this splice and fits DESI DR2. The Odintsov-Oikonomou program (2009.09947, 2010.13580, etc.) "unifies" bounce + DE via Lagrangian reconstruction. In every case, the DE sector is not derived from the bounce — it is added or reconstructed. We do not want to produce another entry in this category.

---

## 5. Why This Distinction Matters

The distinction between "derived from the bounce" and "added to the bounce" is the difference between:

- A theoretical prediction that tests the bounce hypothesis
- A parameter extension that tests nothing about the bounce

If bounce cosmology is to be taken seriously as a scientific program, it must generate predictions that CAN be wrong. Adding free DE parameters removes the ability to be wrong about DE, which is exactly the regime where LCDM is weakest and where a theoretical framework could add value.

**The research integrity standard:**
A bounce cosmology paper that fits DESI data via w0wa is scientifically equivalent to a LCDM paper that fits DESI data via w0wa. The word "bounce" adds no scientific content to the DE sector. Presenting it as a bounce success would be overclaiming.

**What counts as a genuine bounce contribution to DE:**
The bounce would contribute to DE understanding only if:
1. The bounce dynamics derive or constrain w(z) from first principles, OR
2. The bounce predicts a specific relationship between early-universe observables and late-time w(z), OR
3. The bounce excludes certain DE models on theoretical grounds that data alone cannot

Our A-G investigation found that none of these hold for ECH, LQC, or generic matter bounce.

## Summary Statement (For Manuscript Insertion)

> We explicitly considered and rejected the strategy of appending late-time dark energy freedom (e.g., CPL w0wa parametrization) to our bounce framework. While this would improve cosmological fits — particularly against DESI DR2 data, which shows 2.8-4.2 sigma frequentist preference for dynamical dark energy — the improvement would derive entirely from the added DE parameters, not from the bounce physics. Our systematic investigation (Foundations A through G) established that the bounce and late-time dark energy are causally disconnected: no mechanism transmits information from the bounce epoch (rho ~ M_Pl^4) to the dark energy epoch (rho ~ 10^-122 M_Pl^4). Adding w0wa to a bounce model produces the same fit improvement as adding w0wa to LCDM, with no additional theoretical content. We therefore restrict our claims to observables that the bounce physics genuinely controls.
