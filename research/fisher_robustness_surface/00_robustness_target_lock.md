# 00: Robustness Target Lock

## Signal: f_NL^local = -4.375
## Estimator: Scale-dependent bias (galaxy power spectrum)
## Surveys: SPHEREx-like (photometric), MegaMapper-like (spectroscopic multi-tracer)

## Significance Thresholds
- **Decisive detection:** ≥ 5σ (discovery-level)
- **Strong evidence:** 3-5σ
- **Suggestive hint:** 2-3σ
- **Non-test:** < 2σ

## Fragile Assumptions to Scan
1. Galaxy bias b₁ (range: 1.5-4.0)
2. Photo-z scatter σ_z/(1+z) (SPHEREx: 0.003-0.1)
3. Minimum k scale k_min (range: 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻² h/Mpc)
4. Number of tracers / tracer contrast (1 vs 2 tracers)
5. Number density n_g (range: 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻² (h/Mpc)³)

## What We Want to Map
For each point in the grid: σ(f_NL) and the corresponding detection significance |f_NL|/σ.
The surface shows WHERE the science case lives or dies.
