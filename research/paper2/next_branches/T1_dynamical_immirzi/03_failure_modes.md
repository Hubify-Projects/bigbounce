# Branch T1 — Failure Modes

**Branch:** T1 — Dynamical Immirzi / Pseudoscalar Extension

---

## Predeclared Failure Modes

### FM-T1-1: Field redefinition equivalence
**Fires when:** After torsion elimination, the dynamical θ sector reduces to a standard axion-like particle minimally coupled to gravity + a standard four-fermion interaction. No novel Holst content survives.
**Why this kills the branch:** If the result is just "GR + ALP + four-fermion," then we've added nothing beyond well-studied ALP dark energy models. The connection to the Holst/parity-odd sector is cosmetic.
**Likelihood:** Medium-high (30–40%). Many "promote γ to a field" proposals reduce to standard ALP models after torsion elimination.

### FM-T1-2: Ghost / wrong-sign kinetic term
**Fires when:** The effective kinetic term for θ after torsion elimination has the wrong sign, indicating a ghost instability.
**Why this kills the branch:** A ghost makes the theory inconsistent. No physical predictions are possible.
**Likelihood:** Low (5–10%). The Mercuri construction is designed to avoid this, but it needs explicit verification.

### FM-T1-3: No stable minimum
**Fires when:** V_eff(θ) is monotonic, has only saddle points, or has a runaway direction.
**Why this kills the branch:** No vacuum-like contribution without a stable minimum.
**Likelihood:** Medium (20–30%). Depends heavily on the assumed V(θ).

### FM-T1-4: Transient only
**Fires when:** θ dynamics contribute to the early universe but decay to zero at late times, with no frozen residual.
**Why this kills the branch:** We need a late-time vacuum term, not just early-universe dynamics.
**Likelihood:** Medium (25–35%). Many dynamical dark energy models have this problem.

### FM-T1-5: Reproduces known model
**Fires when:** The effective theory after all eliminations is identical to an already-studied model (e.g., dynamical Chern-Simons, quintessence, k-essence) with no novel spin-torsion content.
**Why this kills the branch:** No new physics; the spin-torsion framing is purely linguistic.
**Likelihood:** Medium (20–30%).

### FM-T1-6: Fine-tuning survives
**Fires when:** A stable vacuum minimum exists but requires fine-tuning of V(θ) parameters comparable to the bare cosmological constant problem.
**Why this kills the branch:** Replaces one fine-tuning with another; no progress.
**Likelihood:** Medium-high if Gate T1-2 is reached (40%).

## Expected Outcome Distribution

| Outcome | Probability |
|---------|------------|
| FM-T1-1 or FM-T1-5 fires (no new content) | ~45% |
| FM-T1-3 or FM-T1-4 fires (no viable vacuum) | ~25% |
| FM-T1-6 fires (fine-tuning) | ~15% |
| Gate T1-3 survives (genuinely new viable route) | ~15% |

These are honest estimates. The most likely outcome is that the dynamical Immirzi field reduces to known models after torsion elimination.
