## Motivation

In the minimal EC+Holst+Dirac model, the Barbero-Immirzi parameter γ is a fixed constant. After torsion elimination, all γ-dependence enters through the four-fermion interaction S_4f, which is quartic in ψ and does not contribute at one loop (Branch G v1 closure). The condensate route fails because the S/P channel is repulsive at γ = 0.274 (Track B closure).

A structurally different approach: promote γ to a dynamical pseudoscalar field θ(x). This is motivated by:

1. **Mercuri (2009):** The Barbero-Immirzi parameter can be promoted to a pseudoscalar field coupled to the Nieh-Yan topological density. The resulting dynamics resemble an axion-like particle (ALP) with gravitational couplings.
2. **Taveras & Yunes (2009):** In the first-order formalism, the dynamical Immirzi field couples to the Pontryagin density, producing Chern-Simons modified gravity at low energies.
3. **Calcagni & Mercuri (2009):** The Barbero-Immirzi field can be seen as a torsion-axion with a shift symmetry, whose potential could be generated by non-perturbative effects.

The key structural difference from the minimal model: with γ → θ(x), the Holst term becomes dynamical, and the torsion equation of motion changes. The four-fermion interaction now involves θ, and the effective action after torsion elimination has a qualitatively different structure.

## Field Content and Action

The proposed starting action is (following Mercuri 2009, Calcagni-Mercuri 2009):

```
S = S_grav[e, ω, θ] + S_ferm[e, ω, ψ] + S_θ[e, θ]
```

where:
- `S_grav = (M_Pl²/2) ∫ [ε_{IJKL} + (2/θ(x)) η_{I[K}η_{L]J}] e^I ∧ e^J ∧ F^{KL}[ω]`
- `S_ferm = ∫ d⁴x |e| [ψ̄ iγ^μ D_μ ψ - m ψ̄ψ]`
- `S_θ = -(1/2) ∫ d⁴x |e| [∂_μ θ ∂^μ θ + V(θ)]`

Key differences from minimal model:
1. θ(x) replaces the constant γ in the Holst term
2. θ has its own kinetic term and potential V(θ)
3. After torsion elimination, the four-fermion interaction now involves θ(x), producing fermion-θ couplings
4. The Nieh-Yan density ∂_μ θ × (topological term) may generate an effective Chern-Simons coupling

- depends nontrivially on the dynamics of θ (not just its VEV),

### Q-T1-5: Parity-odd sector connection

### Q-T1-6: Stability and consistency
Does the dynamical θ sector introduce ghosts, gradient instabilities, or other pathologies?

## Gate Structure

### Gate T1-1: Genuine new low-energy structure
The reduced action after torsion elimination with dynamical θ contains terms that are:
- absent in the minimal (constant γ) model,
- not equivalent to simply adding a standard ALP/axion to GR + four-fermion,
- structurally connected to the Holst/parity-odd sector.

**Kill criterion:** If the dynamical θ sector, after torsion elimination, reduces to standard ALP dark energy with no novel Holst connection, Gate T1-1 fails.

### Gate T1-2: Stable vacuum-like contribution
V_eff(θ) has a minimum at finite θ that:
- is stable (positive-definite Hessian),
- contributes a positive vacuum energy,
- survives as R → 0 (late-time limit).

**Kill criterion:** If V_eff(θ) has no stable minimum, or only transient dynamics with no late-time vacuum contribution, Gate T1-2 fails.

### Gate T1-3: Viable cosmological signal
The vacuum-like contribution from Gate T1-2:
- produces |1 + w| < 10⁻² at late times, OR
- produces a distinctive parity-odd observable (birefringence, EB correlation) that is testable.

**Kill criterion:** If the signal is either too large (ruled out) or too small (undetectable with foreseeable experiments), Gate T1-3 fails.
