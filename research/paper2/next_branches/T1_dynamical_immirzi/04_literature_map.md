## Key Questions the Literature Must Answer

1. **Is torsion still algebraic with dynamical θ?** Mercuri (2009) claims yes. Verify.
2. **What is the reduced action after torsion elimination?** Expected: standard gravity + ALP-like θ + modified four-fermion. Exact form needed.
3. **Has V_eff(θ) already been computed?** Check Calcagni-Mercuri and Lattanzi-Mercuri.
4. **Does the Nieh-Yan coupling survive in 4D?** Some papers claim it's a total derivative. Clarify.
5. **Is there a known ghost problem?** Check Magueijo-Zlosnik-Kibble for stability analysis.
