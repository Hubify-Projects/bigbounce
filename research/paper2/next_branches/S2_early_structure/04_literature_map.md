## Key Questions

1. Does the EC bounce produce non-Bunch-Davies initial conditions? What is the specific modification to P(k)?
2. At what k does the bounce leave its imprint? Is this in the CMB window or at smaller scales?
3. Has anyone computed perturbations through a spin-torsion bounce? (Poplawski's work is mostly background-level.)
4. What are the tightest existing constraints on features in P(k) at CMB scales?
5. Are spectral distortion probes (PIXIE/PRISM) sensitive to bounce features?
