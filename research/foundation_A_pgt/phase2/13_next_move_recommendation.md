## Assessment of Remaining Options

### Option 1: Continue Foundation A (Conformal PGT Extension)

**What it is:** Build a conformal (Weyl-symmetric) PGT model where the
torsion mass is forbidden by conformal symmetry and generated only by
controlled Weyl symmetry breaking at an intermediate scale v_c ~ 10^7 GeV.

**Effort:** 4-8 weeks. Requires constructing a new model, not just
analyzing existing literature.

**Probability of success:** LOW (~15%). Conformal PGT may still suffer
from the decoupling limit (the mechanism that kills matter coupling is
structural, not dependent on the symmetry-breaking pattern). Even if the
mass is natural, the mode would still be ultra-weakly coupled.

**Recommendation: DO NOT PURSUE.** The decoupling limit is a structural
feature of the mass-coupling relation m_B g_eff ~ constant/M_Pl^2.
Naturalizing the mass does not fix the phenomenological emptiness.

### Option 2: Move to Foundation B (Alternative Framework)

**What it is:** Investigate a different theoretical framework for
geometric dark energy that avoids the PGT decoupling limit. Candidates:

- **Metric-affine gravity (MAG):** Larger gauge group allows more
  torsion modes with potentially different mass-coupling relations.
  The decoupling limit might not apply if the mass and coupling are
  controlled by independent parameters.

- **Teleparallel gravity extensions (f(T)):** Torsion-only framework
  (no curvature). Different kinetic structure may avoid decoupling.

- **Non-Riemannian cosmology with non-metricity:** Uses the full
  connection decomposition (torsion + non-metricity). Richer structure
  may provide independent mass and coupling parameters.

**Effort:** 2-4 weeks per framework for preliminary assessment (Phase 1
equivalent).

**Probability of success:** UNKNOWN. These frameworks have different
structural features that may or may not avoid the PGT obstruction.

**Recommendation: POSSIBLE, but only if a specific structural argument
can be made for why the framework avoids the decoupling limit BEFORE
investing in the full analysis.**

**Effort:** 1-2 weeks to finalize Paper 1.2.

**Probability of success:** HIGH for producing a publishable paper.

## The Honest Bottom Line

Foundation A asked: "Can propagating torsion in PGT provide a viable
geometric dark energy mechanism?"

The answer is: **No, not within standard PGT.** The framework provides
a consistent, ghost-free, propagating torsion mode. But the same
parameter that makes it light also makes it invisible. And no symmetry
protects its lightness. The result is a fine-tuned field with no
distinctive observational signature — the cosmological constant problem
in different notation.

This is a clean negative result. It is scientifically valuable because
it closes a natural avenue and sharpens the requirements for any
future geometric dark energy proposal. But it does not provide a
foundation for the Paper 1.2 phenomenology.

**The paper should be published on the strength of its phenomenological
analysis and structural lessons, not on a claimed theoretical foundation
that does not exist.**
