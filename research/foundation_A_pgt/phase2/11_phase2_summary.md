## The Three Findings

### Finding 1: The Decoupling Limit (Phase 2A)

Large |t_3| is a decoupling limit, not a strong-coupling limit. As
|t_3| increases:

```
mass:      m_B ~ M_Pl / sqrt(|t_3|)      -> decreases
coupling:  g_eff ~ 1/(M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|))  -> decreases equally fast
unitarity: sqrt(s_unit) ~ M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|) -> rises
strong coupling: Lambda_sc >= M_Pl        -> stays high or rises
```

The theory is perturbatively consistent at any |t_3|. But at the values
needed for cosmological mass (|t_3| ~ 10^58 for meV), the matter
coupling is 10^29 times weaker than gravity. The torsion mode is
cosmologically inert except through its gravitational stress-energy.

**Impact:** Eliminates distinctive torsion observables (birefringence
through matter coupling, spin-dependent forces, thermal production).
Only gravitational quintessence channel survives.

### Finding 2: No Mass Protection (Phase 2B)

No symmetry within PGT protects the 0- torsion mass. Five candidates
investigated and excluded (U(1) gauge, shift, chiral, topological,
SUSY). One candidate (conformal PGT) is conceivable but unbuilt.

Graviton loops generate delta m^2 ~ M_Pl^2/(16 pi^2), requiring
fine-tuning of 1 in 10^57 for m ~ meV. The 't Hooft naturalness
criterion fails (m_B = 0 does not restore a symmetry).

**Impact:** The mass hierarchy is as unnatural as the cosmological
constant problem itself. PGT does not solve the hierarchy — it
rewrites it.

### Finding 3: Non-Generic Structure Survives (Phase 2B, Document 09)

Model B reduces to GR + massive Proca pseudovector (not GR + ALP).
Three structural differences survive reduction:
- 3 DOF vs 1 DOF
- Axial current coupling vs F F~ coupling
- Vector perturbation modes vs scalar-only modes

But all distinctive features are suppressed at large |t_3| by the
decoupling limit.

**Impact:** Model B is structurally richer than previously known
(different from both generic ALP and generic scalar quintessence)
but operationally equivalent to generic quintessence at cosmological
parameter values.
