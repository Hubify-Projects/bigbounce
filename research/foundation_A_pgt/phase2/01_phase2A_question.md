# Phase 2A — Question and Decision Criteria

**Date:** 2026-03-13
**Program:** Foundation A, Phase 2A (EFT / Unitarity / Consistency)
**Subject:** Model B — 0- pseudoscalar axial torsion mode in quadratic PGT

---

## Exact Question

Can the dimensionless coupling |t_3| be taken to be enormously large
(|t_3| >> 1, up to ~ 10^{58} for DE-scale mass or ~ 10^{118} for
Hubble-scale mass) while maintaining:

1. A well-defined effective field theory with perturbative control?
2. Absence of unitarity violations in the regime of cosmological interest?
3. A physically meaningful (not vacuous) parameterization?

---

## Decision Criteria

**PHASE2A_CLEAR:** Large |t_3| is:
- Not forbidden by any unitarity or EFT consistency argument
- Physically interpretable (either as a decoupling limit, a large kinetic normalization, or a technically natural hierarchy)
- Compatible with perturbative control at energies E << M_Pl

**PHASE2A_CONDITIONAL:** Large |t_3| is not immediately forbidden, but:
- The strong-coupling scale drops below some observationally relevant scale, OR
- The interpretation requires caveats that weaken the physical content, OR
- Additional conditions (curvature-squared terms, UV completion) are needed for consistency

**FOUNDATION_A_CLOSED:** Large |t_3| is:
- Incompatible with unitarity (scattering amplitudes violate partial-wave bounds at accessible scales), OR
- Equivalent to a trivially decoupled theory (the torsion mode becomes non-interacting and cosmologically irrelevant), OR
- Reveals a structural inconsistency that cannot be circumvented within EFT

---

## What Phase 2A Is NOT

Phase 2A does not ask whether the tiny mass is *natural* — that is Phase 2B's question.
Phase 2A asks only whether the theory is *consistent* at large |t_3|. Naturalness and
consistency are separate issues. An unnatural but consistent theory is allowed to proceed
to Phase 2B. An inconsistent theory is closed regardless of naturalness.
