# Phase 2B — Question and Decision Criteria

**Date:** 2026-03-13
**Program:** Foundation A, Phase 2B (Shift Symmetry / Naturalness)
**Subject:** Model B — Can the 0- pseudoscalar mass be symmetry-protected?

---

## Context from Phase 2A

Phase 2A found that large |t_3| is a consistent decoupling limit. The
theory is perturbatively well-defined but the torsion-matter coupling
g_eff ~ 1/(M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|)) is suppressed to irrelevance at
cosmologically interesting masses.

Phase 2B proceeds because the theory is not closed — but with the
understanding that even a positive naturalness result cannot restore
distinctive torsion observables through the matter coupling channel.

---

## Exact Question

Is there a symmetry of the PGT action (or a natural extension thereof)
that:

1. Sets the 0- axial torsion mass to zero (or parametrically small) at
   tree level?
2. Is respected by radiative corrections at 1-loop (technical naturalness)?
3. Has a controlled breaking pattern that generates m_B << M_Pl without
   fine-tuning |t_3|?

---

## Decision Criteria

**PHASE2B_NATURAL:** A symmetry exists that:
- Protects m_B = 0 at tree level (exact or approximate)
- The symmetry is broken only by small, calculable effects
- The resulting mass hierarchy m_B / M_Pl << 1 is technically natural
  in the 't Hooft sense (setting m_B = 0 increases symmetry)

**PHASE2B_CONCEIVABLE:** A symmetry candidate exists that:
- Could protect the mass in principle
- Has not been demonstrated to work in the full PGT framework
- Requires further calculation (2-loop, nonperturbative, or UV completion)
  to confirm

**PHASE2B_NO_PROTECTION:** No symmetry is available because:
- The PGT action structure does not admit a symmetry that sets t_3 -> infinity
- Radiative corrections generate delta m_B^2 ~ M_Pl^2 / (16 pi^2) regardless
  of tree-level mass
- The mass hierarchy is fine-tuned with no prospect of resolution within PGT

---

## Candidate Symmetries to Investigate

1. **Axial shift symmetry** A_mu -> A_mu + c_mu: analogous to ALP shift
   symmetry phi -> phi + c
2. **Conformal/Weyl symmetry**: may forbid mass terms entirely
3. **Chiral symmetry of the matter sector**: may protect the axial coupling
   structure
4. **Topological protection**: if the 0- mode is related to a topological
   invariant (cf. Holst term), its mass might be protected
5. **Supersymmetric extension**: SUSY PGT could protect the mass through
   non-renormalization theorems

---

## What Phase 2B Is NOT

Phase 2B does not ask whether the decoupled torsion mode is cosmologically
useful — that was Phase 2A's finding (only through gravitational sector).
Phase 2B asks only whether the mass hierarchy |t_3| >> 1 can be made
natural. A natural but phenomenologically empty theory is a valid outcome.
