## Evidence Summary

### What Phase 2A found

1. **Large |t_3| is a decoupling limit** (Document 02).
   The canonically normalized matter coupling is:
   ```
   g_eff ~ 1 / (M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|))
   ```
   As |t_3| increases, the mass decreases AND the coupling decreases
   simultaneously. The ratio m_B / g_eff ~ M_Pl^2 is fixed.

2. **No unitarity violation** (Document 03).
   The unitarity violation scale rises as sqrt(s_unit) ~ M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|),
   far above M_Pl at cosmologically interesting |t_3|. Tree-level unitarity
   places no upper bound on |t_3|.

3. **No strong-coupling problem** (Document 04).
   All strong-coupling scales are at or above M_Pl. The loop expansion
   parameter is suppressed by 1/|t_3|. The EFT is under perturbative
   control at all sub-Planckian energies.

4. **The torsion mode is observationally decoupled from matter** (Document 02).
   At m ~ meV: g_eff ~ 10^{-29} / M_Pl (10^{29} times weaker than gravity).
   At m ~ H_0: g_eff ~ 10^{-59} / M_Pl (10^{59} times weaker than gravity).
   The mode cannot thermalize, cannot produce detectable fifth forces,
   cannot generate observable birefringence through matter coupling.

## Why PHASE2A_CONDITIONAL (Not CLEAR or CLOSED)

### Why not PHASE2A_CLEAR?

CLEAR would mean: large |t_3| is fully consistent AND physically meaningful
with no significant caveats.

The theory IS consistent (no unitarity, ghost, or strong-coupling problems).
But it is NOT physically meaningful in the sense required for the program.
The distinctive torsion signatures — birefringence through matter coupling,
spin-dependent forces, thermally produced torsion radiation — all require
the torsion-matter coupling g_eff, which is suppressed to irrelevance at
large |t_3|. The torsion mode becomes indistinguishable from generic
quintessence.

This is a significant caveat that weakens the physical content. CLEAR
would overstate the result.

### Why not FOUNDATION_A_CLOSED?

CLOSED would mean: large |t_3| is incompatible with unitarity, OR
the theory is trivially decoupled, OR there is a structural inconsistency.

The theory is NOT incompatible with unitarity. There IS no structural
inconsistency. The remaining question is whether "trivially decoupled"
applies.

The torsion mode IS decoupled from matter at large |t_3|. But it is NOT
trivially decoupled in the gravitational sector. The field still has
energy density and pressure. If given appropriate initial conditions
(or if produced non-thermally, e.g., during inflation), it can affect
the expansion history purely through its stress-energy tensor. This is
the quintessence channel: the field's gravitational effects are not
suppressed by g_eff.

Whether this constitutes "trivial" decoupling is a judgment call:
- If the standard is "distinguishable from a generic scalar quintessence
  field," then YES, it is trivially decoupled — the geometric origin in
  PGT is an action-level label with no observational consequence.
- If the standard is "can affect cosmology at all," then NO — it can
  still drive w(z) ≠ -1 through its gravitational stress-energy.

The CONDITIONAL verdict reflects this ambiguity. The theory lives, but
only as gravitational quintessence without distinctive torsion character.

## The Gravitational-Only Channel

There remains exactly one path forward for Model B at large |t_3|:

**Gravitational quintessence.** If the 0- torsion field:
1. Has appropriate initial conditions (set during inflation or a phase transition)
2. Rolls slowly enough to approximate w ~ -1 at late times
3. Has a mass m_B ~ H_0 to meV

...then it can be the dark energy without ever coupling to matter at
detectable strength. Its equation of state w(z) would be determined by
its mass and initial conditions, not by its matter coupling.

### Is this worth pursuing?

Arguments FOR:
- The quintessence equation of state from a massive pseudovector may have
  non-generic features (parity-odd perturbation structure)
- The field IS geometrically distinct from a fundamental scalar — it
  carries Lorentz indices and descends from the spin connection
- If combined with the birefringence of gravitational waves (not requiring
  matter coupling), there could be a distinctive prediction

Arguments AGAINST:
- Without matter coupling, the field is observationally identical to
  generic quintessence — only the Lagrangian knows it's torsion
- The mass and initial conditions are free parameters — no predictivity
- This is precisely the "description, not derivation" problem that
  Paper 1.2 already suffers from
- Pursuing this reduces Foundation A to "PGT provides a consistent field
  that can be used for quintessence" — true but not a research contribution

## Implications for Phase 2B

Phase 2A does not close Foundation A. The theory is consistent. But the
conditional finding severely constrains what Phase 2B can accomplish:

- Even if Phase 2B finds a shift symmetry that makes |t_3| >> 1 natural,
  the mode would STILL be observationally decoupled from matter
- Naturalness of a cosmologically irrelevant field is an academic question
- The only scenario where Phase 2B matters is if the gravitational-only
  channel produces distinctive predictions

**Phase 2B should proceed, but with calibrated expectations.** The
question is no longer "can we make the mass natural?" but "IF the mass
is natural, does the resulting cosmology have any distinctive feature
that survives the decoupling limit?"
