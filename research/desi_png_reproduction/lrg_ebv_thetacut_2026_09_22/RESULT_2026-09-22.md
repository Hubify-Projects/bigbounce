## 1. Headline

**The row stays a null.** On the θ-cut convention it reads
**Δf_NL = −17.269, σ_Δ = 13.060, raw Δ/σ = −1.322, √2-corrected

precision of the quantity being compared to the threshold.** The honest
statement is that E(B−V) at 0.8 < z < 1.1 is a **persistent ~1σ imaging
sensitivity that no convention choice available here pushes clearly away
from its threshold** — it is called a null because the pre-registered rule
says so, not because the question is closed.

published-sample-matched −1.14 ± 6.90. *(Counterfactual, recorded for the

## 3. What the θ-cut convention actually does — the approximation-free anchor

The θ-cut is a change to the **estimator applied to the data**, not only to
the window. On the official full-sample products it lowers P_0 over
0.003 ≤ k ≤ 0.08 by a median 3.7 % with a ~5 % tilt across the range, while
the EZmock covariance diagonal moves by ≤ 0.32 %.

Fitting the **complete θ-cut triple** (measured P_ℓ + window + covariance,
all official, no approximation anywhere) against the complete untreated
triple, same z-bin, same convention:

| p | untreated | θ-cut | δ_θ | in units of σ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | −0.801 ± 10.374 | **+1.092 ± 10.329** | **+1.893** | **+0.18σ** |
| 1.6 | −1.574 ± 20.518 | **+2.222 ± 20.878** | **+3.796** | **+0.19σ** |

b₁ falls 2.21329 → 2.18832; χ²/dof rises 1.350 → 1.359; 48 fitted bins in
both. **The θ-cut convention moves this z-bin's f_NL by about a fifth of a
sigma and flips its sign — a real shift, well inside the error bar, and the
first time this lab has measured it.** It is quoted here as a convention
systematic, not as a better measurement: the untreated convention remains
the one LS11's and v5's numbers are on, and mixing the two would break the
LRG–QSO comparability that was the point of opening the channel.

## 4. How the split test was put on the θ-cut convention, and its cost

LS11's split power spectra were measured from the catalogues with
`pypower.CatalogFFTPower` **without** the θ-cut, so swapping only the
window and covariance is not a self-consistent fit. Three things were done
about that, all pre-registered:

- **D1 (primary).** Each cap's half was put on the θ-cut convention by that
  cap's **official transfer** T_ℓ^C(k) = P_ℓ^{C,θcut}/P_ℓ^{C,untreated},
  built from the official NGC and SGC spectra and interpolated onto the
  measured k grid, applied **before** the n_data-weighted NGC+SGC
  combination; then the θ-cut window and θ-cut covariance. *Disclosed
  approximation:* the transfer is measured on the full cap, not on each
  E(B−V) half.
- **V (validation).** The same fit with the two caps' transfers **swapped**
  — a deliberately wrong-geometry transfer, and therefore a conservative
  proxy for the unmodelled half-to-half geometry dependence. It moves the
  corrected Δ/σ by **ε_T = 0.051** (p = 1.0) and **0.054** (p = 1.6), and
  does **not** change the verdict word. By the pre-registered rule the D1
  verdict is **ROBUST**.
- **D2 (diagnostic, labelled inconsistent).** θ-cut window + covariance on
  the untreated data vectors. Reported only to separate the window/covariance
  side from the data side; it is **not** the θ-cut answer and at p = 1.6 it
  is the one treatment that crosses the threshold (−1.0019).

Transfer diagnostics (official products only): over the fit range the
GCcomb ratio runs ℓ=0 0.935→0.986, ℓ=2 0.947→0.999; the cap-to-cap spread
max|T^NGC − T^SGC| is **0.016 at ℓ=0** and **0.255 at ℓ=2** (SGC's P_2 is
small and its ratio is correspondingly noisy — this is what ε_T is
measuring). The ℓ=4 ratio was clipped to [0.5, 1.5] as pre-registered,
touching **4 of 77** fine bins in range per cap (5.2 %, well under the 25 %
that would have triggered fallback 3); ℓ = 0, 2, 4 are all kept, on LS11's
convention.

**What was NOT done, and why it is named rather than faked.** A genuine
θ-cut re-measurement of the four split halves needs the direct small-angle
D–D, D–R and R–R pair sums; the R–R term alone for one half at this z-bin
is ~10⁸ pairs (6.0 × 10⁶ randoms, ~17 angular neighbours each within 0.05°)
against 300 k-bins. DESI runs this on NERSC. It is out of this lane's
budget and is the named next step.

## 6. Is the QSO-side E(B−V) behaviour consistent? Yes — but it cannot arbitrate

`LEDGER4_RESULT_v5_2026-09-04.md` §2 gives the QSO E(B−V) row at p = 1.6:
f_NL(high) = −20.70 ± 21.17, f_NL(low) = −19.01 ± 20.26, **Δ = −1.69,
σ_Δ = 29.30, raw Δ/σ = −0.06, √2-corrected −0.04** — an emphatic null.

Comparing the two channels' **Δ** at matched p = 1.6, with LS11's own
comparison rule (|T| < 2 AGREE), treating the channels as independent:

T = (Δ_LRG − Δ_QSO)/√(σ_ΔLRG² + σ_ΔQSO²)
= (−35.351 + 1.69)/√(26.650² + 29.30²) = **−0.850** → **AGREE**
(−0.601 if both σ_Δ carry the √2 correction).

**But the agreement is uninformative in the direction that matters.** The
QSO test's σ_Δ = 29.30 is 1.10× the LRG test's at the same p, on a
different tracer over a different volume (QSO z_eff = 1.491 against LRG
z_eff = 0.919). Had the QSO channel carried a Δ of exactly the size the LRG
channel sees (−35.35), it would have registered at raw Δ/σ = −1.21,
**√2-corrected −0.85 — still below the same threshold, and on the same
null side of it.** So the QSO row's emphatic null is not evidence against
the LRG row: that test does not have the power to resolve a shift of this
size either. **The QSO channel neither confirms nor contradicts the LRG
E(B−V) row.** This is the same asymmetry LS11 recorded in the other direction for
`WEIGHT_SYS`, which is a 3.1σ systematic in QSO and a null in every LRG
bin.

## 8. Standing open items after this round

1. **A genuine θ-cut split measurement** — `pypower` with
   `direct_selection_attrs={'theta': (0., 0.05)}` on the four E(B−V)
   halves. It is the only thing that removes the transfer approximation
   (ε_T ≈ 0.05) entirely. ~10⁸ R–R pairs per half; needs a GPU/HPC venue,
   not this laptop.
2. **A split-specific covariance** is the bigger lever and would help every
   one of the fifteen rows: it removes both the disclosed √2 correction and
   the elevated split χ²/dof, which together are what make these Δ/σ values
   unreadable below ~±0.2. Without it no amount of convention-swapping can
   settle a row sitting 0.07σ from its threshold.
3. **The `-rotated` official variants remain untouched** (LS11 §6 item 6
   named both; this lane closed only `_thetacut0.05`). The rotated products
   also require the corresponding rotation on the data side, so the same
   consistency problem applies and the same transfer construction would be
   needed.
4. **The θ-cut anchor shift (+0.18σ, sign-flipping) is now measured but not
   propagated.** LS11's headline and the LRG–QSO comparison stay on the
   untreated convention, deliberately; moving the channel to the θ-cut
   convention would require re-running the QSO side the same way.
5. Ledger row 4's other LS11 open items (no cross-bin covariance; QSO
   Galactic latitude still marginal; neither channel discriminates −35/16
   from −35/8) are **untouched by this lane**.

**Never tuned toward a verdict.** The threshold, the decision rule, the
robustness rule and the propagation rule were fixed in `34db5010` before any
f_NL was computed, and none was changed afterwards. The result that the row
stays a null and the result that it straddles its threshold are both
reported as measured.
