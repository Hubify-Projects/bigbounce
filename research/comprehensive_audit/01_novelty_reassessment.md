# 01: Novelty Reassessment — N4 Consideration

## The Question: Does Any Result Qualify for N4 (Flagship Breakthrough)?

### N4 Definition
A result that introduces genuinely new physics, a new prediction not previously made, or a paradigm-shifting discovery. Examples: detecting a new particle, deriving a new fundamental relation, predicting a previously unknown observable.

### Candidates for N4

**1. ECH Perturbation-Transparency Theorem (currently N3)**
- Proves that minimal ECH scalar perturbations reduce exactly to Mukhanov-Sasaki
- Holst term is topological when torsion vanishes
- γ (Barbero-Immirzi) invisible in all scalar + tensor observables

**N4 case:** This is a structural no-go that formally closes a class of theories. It's analogous to the Weinberg no-go for the cosmological constant — except our result is for a specific gravity theory (ECH), not a general principle.

**N4 counterargument:** The result follows from well-known properties of Einstein-Cartan theory (torsion is algebraic for scalar matter). The novelty is in the EXPLICIT APPLICATION to perturbation theory and the SYSTEMATIC verification across scalar + tensor sectors, not in new mathematical structure. Published mathematical GR literature has noted related points.

**Verdict: N3 is correct.** The result is a strong structural theorem, not a breakthrough discovery. It closes a question that some researchers implicitly assumed was open, but it doesn't introduce new physics.

**2. The Combined 14-Barrier + ECH Closure Architecture**
**N4 case:** The COMBINATION of 14 barriers + ECH transparency across both sectors, documented as a systematic audit, is more comprehensive than any single prior work.

**N4 counterargument:** Each individual barrier draws on known physics. The novelty is in compilation and systematic coverage, not in fundamental discovery. A literature-aware theorist might have expected most of these closures.

**Verdict: N2-N3 as a BODY of work.** The compilation is impressive but not breakthrough-level.

**3. Bayesian Kinematic-vs-Parametric Comparison (currently N2-N3)**
**N4 case:** The specific argument that bounce gets f_NL for free (0 params) while inflation needs ≥2 tuned params, quantified with 800K MC Bayes factors, is a genuinely new quantitative contribution.

**N4 counterargument:** The Bayesian methodology is standard. The signal (f_NL = -35/8) is not new. The comparison is new but the TECHNIQUE is not.

**Verdict: N2-N3 is correct.** Novel application of standard methods.
