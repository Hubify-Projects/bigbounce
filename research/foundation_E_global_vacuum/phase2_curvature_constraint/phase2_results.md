## The Question

Does the global curvature constraint ∫√g R = χ₀ link bounce-era
curvature to the late-time cosmological constant?

## The Answer

**No. The bounce contribution to ∫√g R is negligible by ~10³²
orders of magnitude. The constraint relocates fine-tuning to χ₀
without solving the CC problem or connecting to bounce physics.**

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Bounce curvature is irrelevant to the global integral

The bounce has R ~ M_Pl² but occupies a spacetime volume
a_min³ × t_Pl. The matter era has R ~ H² but occupies a³_eq × t_Λ.
The volume-weighted integral is dominated by late-time evolution:

```
I_bounce / I_matter ~ 10⁻³²
```

The bounce is 32 orders of magnitude subdominant. Including the
dark energy era makes the disparity infinite (divergent integral
for eternal de Sitter).

**The structural reason:** ∫√g R = ∫ a³ R dt weights by VOLUME (a³),
which exponentially favors late times over early times. No matter
how large R is at the bounce, the tiny a_min³ kills its contribution.

### 2. Λ is determined by χ₀, not by the bounce

The self-consistency equation:
```
Λ = (χ₀ M̃_Pl² + I_matter) / (4V₄)
```

depends on χ₀ and the full post-bounce cosmological evolution.
The bounce contributes negligibly to both I_matter and V₄.

### 3. χ₀ must be tuned

∫√g R is NOT a topological invariant. It cannot be fixed by
topology or boundary conditions. The parameter χ₀ must be tuned
to reproduce Λ_obs, exactly as Λ itself must be tuned in standard
GR.

The mechanism RELOCATES the fine-tuning from Λ to χ₀. Same number
of tuned parameters, more mathematical complexity, no additional
explanatory power.

### 4. The constraint is worse than hand-inserted Λ

The curvature constraint adds:
- A global variable λ (shifts M_Pl², constrained by BBN/CMB/solar system)
- A self-consistency equation (transcendental)
- Potential acausality (requires future boundary data)
- A divergence problem (∫√g R → ∞ for eternal de Sitter)

It does NOT add: an explanation for Λ, a prediction, or a bounce link.

### 5. Higher-curvature variants are pathological

The integral ∫√g R² IS bounce-dominated (bounce contributes 10^{77.5}
times more than the matter era). But constraining ∫√g R² = χ₀ leads
to fourth-order field equations with Ostrogradski instabilities. The
bounce-dominated regime is available only at the cost of pathological
dynamics.

## The Scale Separation Barrier

Foundations A–D identified four structural barriers. Foundation E
reveals a fifth:

| # | Barrier | Source |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Mass-coupling lock | Foundation A |
| 2 | Topological-shift duality | Foundation B |
| 3 | Scalar-tensor universality | Foundation C |
| 4 | Planck suppression | Foundation D |
| **5** | **Scale separation** | **Foundation E** |

**Scale separation:** The bounce operates at (ρ ~ M_Pl⁴, a ~ a_min,
Δt ~ t_Pl). Late-time cosmology operates at (ρ ~ H₀², a ~ 10⁶⁰ a_min,
Δt ~ H₀⁻¹). Any GLOBAL quantity (spacetime volume, integrated
curvature, averaged Lagrangian) is dominated by late-time evolution.
The bounce contributes negligibly to all global spacetime averages.

This is not a failure of the specific mechanism — it is a structural
property of volume-weighted integrals in expanding cosmologies.

## What This Rules Out

Any attempt to connect bounce physics to late-time Λ through:
- Spacetime 4-volume integrals (sequestering)
- Curvature integrals (∫√g R constraint)
- Lagrangian averages (⟨L_matter⟩)
- Any volume-weighted global quantity

These are all dominated by late-time expansion, not by the bounce.

## What Remains

### Route 1: Non-volume-weighted connections

The bounce could influence late-time physics through:
- Topology (topological invariants don't depend on volume weighting)
- Discrete data (number of phase transitions, vacuum selection)
- Initial conditions (set at the bounce, evolve to late times)

These are NOT volume-weighted integrals and are therefore NOT
subject to the scale separation barrier in the same way.

**This is Foundation F territory: bounce-linked DE through initial
conditions or symmetry breaking, not through global averages.**

### Route 2: Accept scale separation as a structural result

The bounce and dark energy are separated by 10⁶⁰ orders of
magnitude in energy scale. NO direct connection survives this gap
through any mechanism involving spacetime integration.

If this is the correct conclusion, then:
- The spin-torsion bounce is a valid early-universe mechanism
- Dark energy is an INDEPENDENT problem
- The program's contribution is the systematic identification of
  structural barriers (A–D + scale separation)

## Recommended Next Move

**Open Foundation F: Bounce-Linked DE Through Initial Conditions.**

Foundation E showed that GLOBAL (volume-integrated) connections fail
due to scale separation. Foundation F tests whether LOCAL connections
(initial conditions, symmetry breaking patterns) set at the bounce
can influence late-time DE without being volume-diluted.

Key difference: initial conditions are set ONCE at the bounce and
evolve forward. They are not averaged over spacetime volume.
A scalar field rolling from bounce-era initial conditions toward a
late-time attractor could, in principle, produce a specific DE
prediction — if the attractor is bounce-specific.

If Foundation F also fails, the program should accept that the bounce
and dark energy are independent problems and write the closure
document for the full A–F sequence.
