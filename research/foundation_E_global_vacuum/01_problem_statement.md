# Foundation E — Problem Statement

**Date:** 2026-03-14

---

## Why Local Geometric Mechanisms Failed

Foundations A–D tested whether propagating geometric degrees of freedom
(torsion modes, non-metricity, disformal effective metrics) can produce
dark energy on FRW backgrounds. Four independent structural barriers
were identified:

1. **Mass-coupling lock (A):** Geometric mass and coupling are both
   fixed by G. Light modes have negligible coupling.
2. **Topological-shift duality (B):** Mass protection requires topological
   coupling, which carries no geometric content.
3. **Scalar-tensor universality (C):** On FRW (T₀=Q₀=0), all geometric
   scalars reduce to generic scalar-tensor EFT.
4. **Planck suppression (D):** Connection coupling provides one ∂φ per
   vertex. Distinctive geometric effects are O(H/M_Pl)² ~ 10⁻¹²².

These barriers are structural, not model-specific. They apply to any
single-field mechanism coupled to matter through the gravitational
connection on homogeneous FRW backgrounds.

---

## Why Global / Nonlocal Vacuum Mechanisms Are Logically Different

The barriers above all target **local propagating fields on FRW.**
A global or nonlocal vacuum mechanism operates differently:

- It does not require a light propagating scalar → no mass-coupling lock
- It does not rely on pseudoscalar-4-form coupling → no topological-shift duality
- It is not a scalar-tensor EFT → does not collapse to generic universality
- It does not couple through the connection → no Planck suppression of distinctive effects

Instead of asking "what field drives DE?", the question becomes:
**"what constrains or selects the vacuum energy?"**

This is a qualitatively different question. The vacuum energy problem
is not about dynamics (what field rolls) but about cancellation (why
do radiative contributions not dominate). A global mechanism addresses
the cancellation directly.

---

## How This Coexists With Bounce Cosmology

The spin-torsion bounce operates at Planck-scale densities (ρ ~ M_Pl⁴).
A global vacuum-energy mechanism operates across ALL energy scales,
constraining the vacuum contribution from each era.

Compatibility requirements:

1. The global constraint must not interfere with bounce dynamics
   (torsion-driven repulsion at ρ ~ M_Pl⁴)
2. The constraint must survive through the bounce (it cannot be
   "reset" or invalidated by the contraction-expansion transition)
3. The residual Λ determined by the mechanism must be consistent
   with observed dark energy (ρ_Λ ~ 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴)

If the bounce SETS or INFLUENCES the global constraint, there is a
natural connection between early-universe torsion physics and late-time
dark energy — without requiring a local geometric DE mechanism.

---

## The Goal

Test whether a global vacuum-energy constraint mechanism can be
embedded in or naturally linked to spin-torsion bounce cosmology.

This is NOT about:
- Deriving Λ from torsion geometry (Foundations A–D closed this)
- Finding another scalar field (scalar-tensor universality absorbs these)
- Inserting Λ by hand (trivially "works" but explains nothing)

This IS about:
- Dynamically neutralizing vacuum energy radiative contributions
- Having the neutralization be compatible with bounce dynamics
- Producing at least one structural or observational consequence

---

## Success Criteria

A viable Foundation E candidate must satisfy ALL of:

1. **Vacuum energy control:** Radiative contributions to Λ are
   dynamically neutralized, cancelled, or constrained without
   fine-tuning each contribution individually

2. **No hand-inserted Λ:** The residual cosmological constant is
   determined by the mechanism, not put in by hand. (A residual Λ
   that depends on the full history of the universe is acceptable;
   a Λ that must be separately tuned is not.)

3. **Bounce compatibility:** The mechanism is consistent with
   spin-torsion bounce dynamics. It does not require conditions
   that conflict with the bounce phase (e.g., it does not require
   Λ = 0 exactly, which would conflict with observations).

4. **At least one consequence:** The mechanism produces at least
   one structural prediction, consistency condition, or observable
   consequence beyond "Λ exists." Examples:
   - A relation between bounce-era parameters and Λ
   - A constraint on the number or type of phase transitions
   - A modified vacuum energy evolution history
   - A connection between the CC and other physical constants

## Failure Criteria

| Failure mode | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| FAIL_HIDDEN_TUNING | Mechanism relocates fine-tuning to boundary conditions, auxiliary fields, or initial data |
| FAIL_LOCAL_COLLAPSE | After analysis, mechanism reduces to a local scalar-tensor theory |
| FAIL_NO_PREDICTIVITY | Mechanism has no testable consequence beyond fitting Λ |
| FAIL_BOUNCE_INCOMPATIBLE | Mechanism conflicts with spin-torsion bounce dynamics |
| FAIL_ACAUSAL | Variational principle is ill-posed or requires future boundary data in a physically unacceptable way |
