## Tier 1: Core Mechanisms (Must Read)

### Vacuum Energy Sequestering

1. **Kaloper & Padilla, "Sequestering the Standard Model Vacuum Energy" (2013)**
   - arXiv: 1309.6562
   - Key mechanism: Global Lagrange multipliers enforce cancellation
     of vacuum energy from matter loops
   - Strengths: Elegant, minimal, handles arbitrary constant vacuum
     shifts
   - Weaknesses: Requires spacetime-volume integration (global/acausal?),
     residual Λ determination unclear
   - Bounce relevance: High — the global constraint naturally
     includes all cosmological eras

2. **Kaloper & Padilla, "Vacuum Energy Sequestering: The Framework and
   Its Cosmological Consequences" (2014)**
   - arXiv: 1406.0711
   - Extended analysis with cosmological evolution
   - Key result: Residual Λ is a HISTORICAL quantity — depends on
     the full 4-volume of spacetime
   - Critical for understanding how bounce era enters the constraint

3. **Kaloper & Padilla, "Sequestration of Vacuum Energy and the End
   of the Universe" (2015)**
   - arXiv: 1502.07977
   - Consequence: Universe must eventually transition to a crunching
     phase. The 4-volume must be finite.
   - Important: Does a bounce cosmology provide the required finite
     4-volume naturally?

### 4-Form / Flux Mechanisms

4. **Bousso & Polchinski, "Quantization of Four-Form Fluxes and
   Dynamical Neutralization of the Cosmological Constant" (2000)**
   - arXiv: hep-th/0004134
   - Key mechanism: Large number of 4-form flux sectors allows
     fine scanning of Λ
   - Strengths: Based on well-understood string theory ingredients
   - Weaknesses: Requires enormous number of vacua (~10⁵⁰⁰),
     anthropic reasoning
   - Bounce relevance: Low — primarily a landscape mechanism

5. **Brown & Teitelboim, "Neutralization of the Cosmological Constant
   by Membrane Creation" (1988)**
   - Key mechanism: Membrane nucleation adjusts 4-form flux
   - Strengths: Concrete dynamical mechanism
   - Weaknesses: Nucleation rates may be too slow
   - Bounce relevance: Medium — bounce could trigger nucleation

### Nonlocal Gravity

6. **Deser & Woodard, "Nonlocal Cosmology" (2007)**
   - arXiv: 0706.2151
   - Key mechanism: f(□⁻¹R) modification of GR
   - Strengths: No new d.o.f. at fundamental level
   - Weaknesses: Can be rewritten as scalar-tensor with auxiliaries
   - Bounce relevance: Low — primarily a late-time mechanism

7. **Maggiore & Mancarella, "Nonlocal gravity and dark energy" (2014)**
   - arXiv: 1402.0448
   - RT model: m²R□⁻²R nonlocal modification
   - Key result: Produces dark energy without cosmological constant
   - Weaknesses: Auxiliary field formulation IS a scalar-tensor theory
   - Bounce relevance: Low

## Tier 2: Critiques and No-Go Analyses (Important)

8. **Kaloper, Padilla, Stefanyszyn, & Rosa, "Sequestering Effects
   on and of Vacuum Decay" (2016)**
   - arXiv: 1604.04000
   - Addresses vacuum decay in sequestering framework
   - Critical for understanding stability

9. **Lombriser & Lima, "Challenges to Self-Acceleration in Modified
   Gravity from Gravitational Waves and Large-Scale Structure" (2017)**
   - Post-GW170817 constraints on nonlocal gravity models
   - Many nonlocal models already excluded by c_GW = c constraint

10. **Weinberg, "The Cosmological Constant Problem" (1989)**
    - Rev. Mod. Phys. 61, 1
    - Classic review establishing the problem
    - Key no-go: Weinberg's no-go theorem against adjustment mechanisms
    - Sequestering evades Weinberg's theorem by using GLOBAL variables

11. **Padilla, "Lectures on the Cosmological Constant Problem" (2015)**
    - arXiv: 1502.05296
    - Comprehensive review of approaches and their failures
    - Essential for understanding what has already been tried

12. **Burgess, "The Cosmological Constant Problem: Why It's Hard to
    Get Dark Energy from Micro-physics" (2013)**
    - arXiv: 1309.4133
    - EFT perspective on CC problem
    - Clarifies what "naturalness" means for Λ

## Tier 3: Adjacent Ideas (Background)

13. **Unimodular gravity approaches**
    - Key idea: Fix √g = 1, Λ becomes an integration constant
    - Not truly a solution — Λ is undetermined, not explained
    - But clarifies the role of the measure in the CC problem

14. **Radiative stability analyses of sequestering**
    - Various papers examining whether sequestering survives
      perturbative corrections
    - Status: debated, not definitively resolved

15. **Backreaction / averaging mechanisms (Buchert, Räsänen, etc.)**
    - Key idea: Averaging inhomogeneities generates effective Λ
    - Mostly excluded as primary DE mechanism (insufficient magnitude)
    - Not relevant to this program unless combined with another mechanism

16. **Landscape / anthropic approaches (Bousso, Susskind, etc.)**
    - Background for 4-form mechanisms
    - Not the primary target unless 4-form route is pursued

17. **Self-tuning approaches in extra dimensions**
    - Brane-world self-tuning mechanisms
    - Mostly shown to have hidden tuning (Weinberg theorem applies)
    - Background for understanding what doesn't work
