## 1. Setup

The matter-bounce contracting phase is driven by a canonical scalar field with
$w=0$. The standard realisation is an exponential potential

$$V(\phi)=V_0\,e^{-\lambda\phi/M},\qquad M=\text{reduced Planck mass},$$

whose scaling solution has $w=\lambda^2/3-1$, so $w=0 \Leftrightarrow \lambda^2=3$,
$a\propto(-t)^{2/3}$, $\epsilon\equiv-\dot H/H^2=3/2$, and $c_s^2=1$ (canonical).

The script derives the separate-universe autonomous system **from the field
equations**, quoting no formula. With $x\equiv\dot\phi/(\sqrt6 MH)$ and
$Y\equiv V/(3M^2H^2)$, and the Friedmann constraint $x^2+Y=1$:

$$\frac{dx}{dN}=(1-x^2)\left(\frac{\sqrt6\,\lambda}{2}-3x\right),
\qquad
\frac{d\ln|H|}{dN}=-3x^2 .$$

This holds for **either sign of $H$** because $Y>0$ regardless, so it is valid in
contraction. Fixed point $x_\ast=\lambda/\sqrt6$, $\epsilon_\ast=3x_\ast^2=\lambda^2/2$.

### Why $\zeta$ is not conserved here (and the naive $\delta N$ fails)

At the scaling solution the script computes

$$c_a^2=\frac{\dot p}{\dot\rho}=\frac{\lambda^2}{3}-1=w=0,
\qquad\text{while}\qquad c_s^2=1 .$$

Since $c_a^2\neq c_s^2$, the intrinsic non-adiabatic pressure
$\delta p_{\rm nad}=(c_s^2-c_a^2)\delta\rho_{\rm com}$ is nonzero, and by the exact
nonlinear long-wavelength equation
$\dot\zeta=-\big(H/(\rho+p)\big)\,\delta p_{\rm nad}+\mathcal O(\nabla^2)$,
**$\zeta$ is not conserved on super-Hubble scales**. Correspondingly (Step 2 of the
script) the long-wavelength mode $\int dt/a^3\propto 1/(-t)$ **grows** as $t\to0^-$:
the mode that decays in expansion dominates in contraction.

**Consequence:** the textbook $\delta N$ shortcut — "$\zeta$ is conserved, evaluate
$\delta N$ once, stop" — is *invalid* for this background. This is the failure mode
the ledger item anticipated, and it is stated up front rather than papered over.
What remains valid, and is what is used, is the **full separate-universe
integration**: $\delta N$ between a flat initial slice and a uniform-density final
slice, obtained by integrating the exact nonlinear patch dynamics through the
growing phase.

## 2. Gauge / slicing choices (stated explicitly)

| choice | value |
|---|---|
| initial slice | spatially flat (uniform $a$, hence uniform $N_i$) |
| final slice | uniform density; for a single field $\rho=3M^2H^2$, so uniform $\lvert H\rvert$ |
| $\zeta$ | $=\delta N$ between those slices (nonlinear Salopek–Bond / Lyth–Malik–Sasaki) |
| evolution variable | $s=\ln\lvert H\rvert$, which **increases** monotonically during contraction |
| $f_{\rm NL}$ definition | $\zeta=\zeta_g+\tfrac35 f_{\rm NL}\zeta_g^2$ (Cai *et al.* Eq. 20; Li *et al.*) |

From $d\ln|H|/dN=-3x^2$,

$$N_f-N_i=\int_{s_i}^{s_f}\left(-\frac{1}{3x(s)^2}\right)ds .$$

Local patch data on the flat slice are $(x_i,s_i)$. The $s_i$ direction is a pure
local time shift contributing $\zeta=\tfrac23\delta s_i$ with **no growth**; the
physical growing mode is the off-attractor displacement $u_i\equiv x_i-x_\ast$.

## 3. The exact second-order integration

Writing $u=x-x_\ast$ and $\epsilon=3x_\ast^2$, the exact patch ODE is

$$\frac{du}{ds}=\frac{u\,(A-2x_\ast u-u^2)}{(x_\ast+u)^2},\qquad A\equiv1-x_\ast^2,$$

with series coefficients (computed, not quoted)

$$\alpha=\frac{3-\epsilon}{\epsilon},\qquad
\beta=\frac{2}{x_\ast A},\qquad
\frac{du}{ds}=\alpha u-\alpha\beta u^2+\mathcal O(u^3).$$

At $\epsilon=3/2$: $\alpha=1$, i.e. $u\propto e^{s}=\lvert H\rvert$ — exactly the
$\int dt/a^3$ growing mode of §1. Solving to second order,

$$u(\sigma)=u_i e^{\alpha\sigma}-\beta u_i^2\left(e^{2\alpha\sigma}-e^{\alpha\sigma}\right),
\qquad \sigma=s-s_i,$$

(the ODE residual at $\mathcal O(u_i^2)$ is verified to be exactly $0$), and
expanding the $\delta N$ integrand,

$$-\frac{1}{3(x_\ast+u)^2}+\frac{1}{3x_\ast^2}=Pu+Qu^2+\mathcal O(u^3),
\qquad P=\frac{2}{3x_\ast^3},\quad Q=-\frac{1}{x_\ast^4},$$

integration from $0$ to $\Sigma$ gives closed forms for $\zeta_1$ and $\zeta_2$
(printed in full by the script).

## 4. The growing-mode-dominated limit and the result

With $W\equiv e^{\alpha\Sigma}$ (the total growth of $\lvert H\rvert^\alpha$ between
Hubble exit and the end of contraction), $\zeta_1\propto W$ and $\zeta_2\propto W^2$,
so as $W\to\infty$

$$\frac{\zeta_2}{\zeta_1^2}=\frac{3\epsilon}{8}-\frac{21}{8}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{\;f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}(\epsilon)=\frac53\,\frac{\zeta_2}{\zeta_1^2}=\frac{5\epsilon-35}{8}\;}$$

$$f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}(\epsilon=3/2)=-\frac{55}{16}=-3.4375 .$$

**Why the $W\to\infty$ limit makes this robust.** Every contamination one would
worry about enters through the *linear* response and therefore grows only as $W^1$,
suppressed by $1/W$ relative to the $W^2$ term that fixes the ratio:

- non-Gaussianity already present in the initial patch data (i.e. $u_i$ being a
  nonlinear function of the Gaussian field perturbation on the flat slice);
- intrinsic sub-Hubble / horizon-crossing non-Gaussianity of $\delta\phi$;
- the local time-shift mode $\delta s_i$, which does not grow at all.

Hence in the matter-bounce regime (many e-folds of $\lvert H\rvert$ growth by
construction) $\zeta_2/\zeta_1^2$ is a pure number, independent of $\Sigma$, of
$s_i$, and of the field-to-$u_i$ map. **This is the reason the number is
meaningful at all**, and it is the one structural claim the whole result rests on.

**Numerical validation (independent of the symbolic path).** The script integrates
the exact nonlinear system $(u,\zeta)$ with `mpmath` at 40 digits for $\pm u_i$ and
splits $\zeta$ into odd/even parts:

| quantity | numeric ($\Sigma=12$, $u_i=10^{-9}$) | exact |
|---|---|---|
| $\zeta_2/\zeta_1^2$ | $-2.062506556$ | $-33/16=-2.0625$ |
| $f_{\rm NL}$ | $-3.437510927$ | $-55/16=-3.4375$ |

relative agreement $3.2\times10^{-6}$, consistent with the expected $\mathcal O(1/W)$
finite-$W$ residual.

## 7. What this does and does not settle

**Settles.**
- The two published values are in the same convention; the discrepancy is not
  definitional (§5).
- The discrepancy is an exact overall factor of $2$, so it lives in the in-in
  bookkeeping (commutator factor $-i\langle[\zeta^3,H]\rangle=+2\,{\rm Im}\langle\zeta^3H\rangle$,
  Wick multiplicity, or index-sum orbit multiplicity), not in one vertex.
- Cai's printed Eq. (37) does not reduce to $-35/8$ under either index-sum
  reading; the distinct-monomial reading gives exactly $-35/16$.
- The local non-Gaussianity of the matter bounce is generated by the non-attractor
  growing mode: with $n_s=1$ the Maldacena consistency relation would give
  $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm sq}=\tfrac{5}{12}(1-n_s)=0$, and the actual value is $\mathcal O(1)$
  and negative. Had the separate-universe integration returned zero, it would have
  meant we had followed the conserved $C$-mode instead of the growing $D$-mode; it
  did not.

**Does not settle.**
- The factor of two itself. $-55/16$ is not a rational multiple of either
  candidate.
- Strict line-by-line comparability. The separate-universe result is the
  **zeroth-order-in-gradients** super-Hubble generation. The in-in calculations
  additionally carry the gradient ($k^2$ / non-local $\Pi k^2$) structure of the
  shape function and a specific vacuum initial state. The residual difference
  ($-55/16$ vs $-35/16$ or $-35/8$) is of exactly the size of the terms the
  gradient expansion drops at leading order, so the two classes of computation
  are not identical objects.

**Observation, explicitly not a claim.** The derived closed form
$f_{\rm NL}(\epsilon)=-35/8+(5/8)\epsilon$ has Cai's published $-35/8$ as its
$\epsilon\to0$ limit. This is recorded because it is striking; **no mechanism is
claimed**, and nothing in this document argues that Cai *et al.* dropped an
$\epsilon$-linear piece. Treat it as a lead, not a resolution.
