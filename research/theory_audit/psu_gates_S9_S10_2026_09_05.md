## S9 — the uniform-density slice at second order

### S9.1 Setup (what is continued, and how)

The 2026-09-04 threading note gives the fluid-congruence e-fold number as a **field**,
$N_c(t,x)=\bar N(t)+\delta N_c(t,x)$, $\delta N_c(t,x)=\zeta(t,x)-\tfrac13\int_{-\infty}^{t}\partial_iN^i\,dt'$ along the
fluid worldline through $(t,x)$ — the uniform-$\phi$ value is $N_c$ read on the coordinate slice $t=t_f$. The uniform-density
value is the *same scalar field* read on the surface $\rho(t,x)=\bar\rho(t_f)$, i.e. at $t=t_f+\delta t(x)$:
$$
\delta N_{c,\rho}(x_f)=N_c\big(t_f+\delta t(x_f),x_f\big)-\bar N(t_f)
=\delta N_c(t_f,x_f)+H\,\delta t-\tfrac{\epsilon}{2}H^2\delta t^2+\partial_t\delta N^{(1)}_c\,\delta t+O(3),\qquad(\text{S9.3})
$$
with $\partial_t\delta N^{(1)}_c=\dot\zeta-\tfrac13\partial_iN^i=\lambda\dot\zeta$ on super-Hubble scales. No label ambiguity
enters (S9.3): $N_c$ is a scalar and $\delta t$ is a surface. The initial-position label is applied afterwards exactly as in the
note (rigid translation by $\Delta_{\rm init}$ of the *full* linear $\rho$-field $\lambda'\zeta_S$, so the translation pieces scale by
$\lambda'/\lambda$; the second-order change of the endpoint, $N_L\delta t_S$, acts on the vanishing background gradient).

**The surface to second order.** In comoving gauge ($\delta\phi=0$, no field gradients) the fluid-frame density is exact,
$\rho=\dot\phi^2/(2N^2)+V$, so $\delta\rho=-\dot\phi^2\alpha+\tfrac32\dot\phi^2\alpha^2$ with $\alpha=\alpha_1+\alpha_2$,
$\alpha_2=A_2(\tau)\,\zeta_L\zeta_S$ the $L\times S$ lapse of the constraint solve. Solving $\rho(t_f+\delta t,x)=\bar\rho(t_f)$
order by order ($\bar\rho=3H^2$, $\dot{\bar\rho}=-6\epsilon H^3$):
$$
\delta t^{(1)}=-\frac{\delta\rho^{(1)}}{\dot{\bar\rho}}=-\frac{\dot\zeta}{3H^2},\qquad
\delta t^{(2)}_{LS}=-\frac{1}{\dot{\bar\rho}}\Big[-\dot\phi^2A_2+3\dot\phi^2\alpha_L\alpha_S+\delta\dot\rho_L\delta t_S+\delta\dot\rho_S\delta t_L
+\ddot{\bar\rho}\,\delta t_L\delta t_S\Big].\qquad(\text{S9.4})
$$
(S9.1) of the previous note is the first of these (asserted). The $\rho$-slice kernel is then
$M_\rho=M_\phi+M_{\rm extra}$, $M_{\rm extra}\zeta_L\zeta_S=\big[H\delta t^{(2)}-\epsilon H^2\delta t_L\delta t_S+\lambda(\dot\zeta_L\delta t_S+\dot\zeta_S\delta t_L)\big]_{t_f}$,
and the bispectrum is assembled with the linear factor of the new variable, $\lambda'\equiv\delta N_{c,\rho}/\zeta_\phi=2\lambda=2(1-\epsilon/3)$
(asserted from the same objects: $\lambda'=\lambda+H\delta t^{(1)}_S/\zeta_S$), $f^\rho=f^{\rm in\text{-}in}/\lambda'+f^\rho_{\rm map}$,
$f^\rho_{\rm map}=\tfrac{5}{12}\lambda'^{-2}[M_\rho(k_L,q)P(q)+M_\rho(k_L,p)P(p)]/P_S$.
