## S6 — Did the second-order map use any in-in / δN input? Eq. (4) assertion (RESOLVED)

**Question (audit):** "Verify (not assert) that the second-order map used no in-in/δN input; add the printed-Eq. (4)
assertion to the sympy gate."

**Static check (script §S6a).** In `threading_map_second_order_2026_09_04.py` every map piece (`D_grad`, `D_zlap`,
`Pg`, `M_fin`, `M_init`) is assembled from the ADM constraint solve + the threading identity on lines ≤ 250; the first
line that mentions the in-in polynomial is 252 ("comparison with the in-in result (read only now)"), and the in-in
coefficients `c0i, c2i` used in the comparison are defined on line 254. Asserted: `max(map_lines) < first_inin`.

**Dynamic check (sentinel).** A scratch copy of the threading script with the in-in coefficients perturbed
(`c0i → c0i + 7`, `c2i → c2i + 11`, `f_inin → f_inin + 7`) was re-run from scratch (~100 s). Result: the
in-in-dependent comparison assertions trip (so the perturbation does propagate), while `map_fNL_pieces`,
`kernels_growing_mode` and `linear_threading_factor` are byte-identical to the committed JSON — receipt
`psu_gates_S6_sentinel_threading_2026_09_04.json`. The only other key that differs is a printing variant of one
exponent in `second_order_constraint_solution` (`τ^((2ε+2)/ε)` vs `τ^(2(ε+1)/ε)`).

**Eq. (4) assertion.** With the frozen pieces,
$$
f_{\rm map}^{\rm fin}-f_{\rm map}^{\rm init}=-\frac{5\epsilon}{4(3-\epsilon)}\,(1-3\mu^2)\qquad(\text{S6.1})
$$
residual $0$ with the minus sign; with the printed plus sign the residual is $\tfrac{5\epsilon(1-3\mu^2)}{2(\epsilon-3)}\neq0$.
Rows: dust $T=-\tfrac{5}{4}(1-3\mu^2)$; attractor and USR ($\epsilon\to0$) $T=0$.

**VERDICT: RESOLVED** — identity, not fit: the map is closed before the in-in enters, and (S6.1) is now a hard assertion.

## S7 — The Cai et al. 2009 factor 2 (NOT — literature step named)

**Question (audit):** "either an equation-level appendix locating the slip, or downgrade to 'differs from [18]'; must
engage Li, Quintin, Wang & Cai 2017, which reproduces Cai's polynomial."

**What is checkable symbolically (script §S7).** This lab's in-in shape at $\epsilon=3/2$ is $-\tfrac{35}{16}+\tfrac{15}{16}\mu^2$
(isoceles $-35/16$, monopole $-15/8$). Cai's $-35/8$ divided by the isoceles value is exactly $2$; divided by the monopole it
is $7/3$. So $-35/8$ is consistent with a **uniform overall factor 2 on the full shape** and inconsistent with any
$\mu$-dependent or $\epsilon$-dependent slip. That narrows the candidate loci to overall normalisations
(literature, **not verified here**): Im vs 2 Im in $\langle\zeta^3\rangle=i\!\int\!\langle[H,\zeta^3]\rangle$; the $f_{\rm NL}$
convention (3 vs 2 permutations in the squeezed formula); a doubled boundary/field-redefinition vertex. The $\zeta$–$\Phi$
factor $3/5$ is excluded (gives $5/3$ or $3/5$).

**Missing step.** Locating the slip requires the published equations of Cai, Xue, Brandenberger & Zhang 2009
(arXiv:0903.0631) and Li, Quintin, Wang & Cai 2017 (arXiv:1612.02036) side by side with the lab's vertex-by-vertex
table (adjudication §4); that is a literature read, not a derivation. Until done, the honest sentence is the
downgrade: "differs from [18] by a uniform factor 2 in every shape coefficient."

**VERDICT: NOT** (symbolic content exhausted; equation-level comparison pending).

## S8 — USR row as a real validation: exact numerical δN(φ,π) at finite ε_s (RESOLVED)

**Question (audit):** "exact numerical δN(φ,π) at finite ε_s (e.g. ε_s=10⁻², ε_f=10⁻⁶, λ−1≈−3×10⁻⁵)".

**Setup (script §S8).** Exact USR ($V=V_0$, $M_p=1$): $u\equiv\dot\phi$, $du/dN=-3u$, $3H^2=V_0+u^2/2$,
$\epsilon=3u^2/(2V_0+u^2)$. Closed-form e-fold function to the uniform-$\phi$ surface,
$N(\phi,u)=-\tfrac13\ln(u_e/u)$, $u_e=\sqrt{2V_0}\sinh\!\big[\operatorname{asinh}(u/\sqrt{2V_0})-\sqrt{3/2}\,(\phi_e-\phi)\big]$;
to the uniform-$\rho$ surface $N=-\tfrac13\ln(u_e^{\rho}/u)$. Exact $k\to0$ linear theory: $\zeta=C_1+C_2\!\int\!dt/(a^3\epsilon)$;
flat-gauge $\delta\phi=-(u/H)\zeta$, lapse $\alpha=u\,\delta\phi/2H$; local data $\phi_{\rm loc}=\phi_s+\delta\phi$,
$\pi_{\rm loc}=(u_s+\delta\dot\phi)(1-\alpha)$. Linear response by symmetric difference ($A=10^{-10}$, 40 digits).

**Result** ($\epsilon_s=10^{-2},\epsilon_f=10^{-6}$, growing mode): $I=1.00109\times10^{-4}$, $\lambda_{\rm pred}-1=-3.337\times10^{-5}$;
$$
\frac{\delta N_{\rm SU}(\phi,\pi)}{\zeta_f}=0.999966630\ldots,\qquad
\frac{\delta N_{\rm SU}}{\zeta_f(1-I/3)}=1+1\times10^{-12}.\qquad(\text{S8.1})
$$
Same to $10^{-12}$ for $(\epsilon_s,\epsilon_f)=(10^{-2},10^{-4})$ ($I=1.0009\times10^{-3}$) and for the mixed mode $C_1=C_2=1$;
the constant mode gives $I=0$ and ratio $1$ exactly; $(10^{-3},10^{-9})$ agrees to $10^{-8}$ (finite-difference floor at
$I\sim10^{-6}$). On the uniform-$\rho$ final surface the same identity holds,
$\delta N_{\rm SU}^{\rho}=\zeta_{\rho,f}-I\zeta_f/3$ to $10^{-12}$, while $\delta N^{\rho}_{\rm SU}/\zeta_{\rho,f}=1.0033$.

So the isotropic $N(\phi,\pi)$ formula — fed the flat-slice local data including the lapse — computes exactly
$\zeta_f(1-I/3)$, the normal-congruence e-fold count of the threading identity, **not** $\zeta_f$; the criterion's
$I$ is the measured offset at finite $\epsilon_s$, not an $O(\epsilon)$ estimate.

**VERDICT: RESOLVED** (the USR row is now a 12-digit numerical validation of $\delta N_c=\zeta_f(1-I/3)$).
