## Why this archive exists

Between late February and mid-March 2026, a structured derivation program tested four
minimal first-principles routes to deriving late-time dark energy ($w = -1$) from the
Einstein-Cartan-Holst microscopic action. All four routes closed with clean negative
results:

1. **Track B** (NJL condensate): S/P channel repulsive at gamma = 0.274
2. **Branch G v1** (strict one-loop): fermion determinant is gamma-independent
3. **Route T1** (dynamical Immirzi): reduces to standard ALP after torsion elimination
4. **Route S1** (parity CMB): no photon coupling; generic ALP birefringence only

These closures established that the minimal ECH+Dirac model does not generate the
late-time vacuum term from first principles at the tested approximation orders, and
does not produce distinctive observational signatures beyond standard ALP models.

Paper 1.01 already contained honest epistemological framing (w=-1 flagged as assumption,
birefringence as consistency check, etc.), but its overall architecture was still
organized around the minimal model as the central foundation. Paper 1.2 repositions
the project based on what the closures taught us structurally.
