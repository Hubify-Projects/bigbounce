Executive Summary. This research program investigated whether Einstein-Cartan spin-torsion gravity can produce observable cosmological signatures—dark energy, cosmic birefringence, and bouncing-cosmology imprints. After 7 foundation studies and 17+ branches testing every viable theoretical route, the core finding is a rigorous negative result: 13 structural barriers close all minimal routes from bounce to dark energy.

Two genuinely positive results survive: (1) a spectator ALP birefringence prediction (β = 0.27°) matching observed data (0.35 ± 0.09°) at 1σ, and (2) a matter-bounce phenomenology framework (Branch V) with a parameter-free prediction fNL = 5/12 testable by SPHEREx. The program also produced a validated MCMC infrastructure with 236,000+ posterior samples.

The honest conclusion: the ECH framework cannot derive dark energy, but it can motivate well-tested phenomenological models and may provide an explicit nonsingular bounce mechanism with testable predictions.
