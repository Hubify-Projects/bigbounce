## Detailed Analysis of "MAYBE" and "YES" Claims

### Claim 5 (PTA/NANOGrav) — MAYBE

**What we have:** The ECH bounce background (modified Friedmann equation). Classical perturbation equations give n_s = 1 for scalars and a blue tensor spectrum.

**What we'd need:**
- The tensor spectrum amplitude at PTA frequencies (~nHz). This requires the tensor transfer function through the bounce, which for our classical ECH case transmits the contraction-phase spectrum faithfully (T = 1)
- The matter bounce tensor spectrum at nHz depends on the contraction dynamics, not the bounce itself
- To avoid the Quintin et al. no-go (large r ↔ large f_NL), need either multi-field or non-dust contraction

**How close are we?** Not very. The PTA fit papers (Papanikolaou 2025) use the generic matter bounce spectrum, not ECH-specific. We could repeat their analysis with the ECH label, but it would add nothing new.

**Honest assessment:** The ECH bounce does not modify the tensor spectrum at PTA frequencies. Claiming ECH explains NANOGrav would be claiming that a generic matter bounce explains NANOGrav — with our label attached. This is not honest novelty.

**Verdict: NOT worth pursuing as an ECH paper.** Could be discussed as context in a review/comparison paper.

### Claim 10 (f_NL = −35/8) — MAYBE

**What we have:** The prediction is already known (Cai et al. 2009). It is a property of matter-dominated contraction, not of any specific bounce mechanism.

**What we'd need:** A way to present this as part of a broader ECH prediction suite, not as a standalone claim.

**How close are we?** The prediction exists. The question is whether we can add value beyond citing it.

**Honest assessment:** We cannot add anything to the f_NL = −35/8 prediction. It is model-independent within the matter bounce class. The ECH framework does not modify it (perturbation transparency).

**Verdict: Include in any comparison/review paper, but NOT as an ECH-specific result.** It is a generic matter bounce prediction.

### Claim 12 (Birefringence) — YES (but bounce-independent)

**What we have:** A specific prediction β = 0.27–0.35° from the ALP (Barbero-Immirzi pseudoscalar) with m ~ 10⁻³³ eV. This matches observations at 3.6σ.

**What it means for the bounce:** Nothing. The birefringence prediction stands regardless of whether there was a bounce, inflation, or anything else. The ALP mass and coupling determine β, and these have nothing to do with bounce dynamics.

**Honest assessment:** This is the strongest positive result in the entire program. But it is an ALP prediction, not a bounce prediction. The ECH framework motivates the ALP's existence (as the promoted Barbero-Immirzi field), but the bounce plays no role in the birefringence mechanism.

**Verdict: CONTINUE developing the birefringence prediction, but do NOT conflate it with bounce evidence.**
