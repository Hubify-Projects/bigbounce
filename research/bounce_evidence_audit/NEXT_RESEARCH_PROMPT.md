You are working in my cosmology research repository. Read CLAUDE.md and check memory before proceeding.

Our goal: find meaningful scientific evidence that bounce cosmology provides a better fit to observations than ΛCDM + inflation.

We just completed a full bounce evidence audit (research/bounce_evidence_audit/). The audit found that the single most concrete, testable, near-term falsifiable prediction of the matter bounce is:

**f_NL^local = -35/8 ≈ -4.375**

This is parameter-free, generated during matter-dominated contraction (Cai et al. 2009), and next-generation LSS surveys (SPHEREx, MegaMapper) could detect it at up to 8σ. It is the sharpest discriminator between bounce and inflation (inflation predicts f_NL ≈ 0).

However, the matter bounce has KNOWN PROBLEMS:
- n_s = 1 (excluded at 8.3σ by Planck)
- The Quintin et al. (2015) no-go: single-field matter bounce cannot have small r AND small |f_NL| simultaneously
- BKL instability during contraction

The audit also found that the ECH-specific program is perturbation-transparent (no novel ECH corrections), so we must work at the level of GENERAL bouncing cosmology, not ECH-only.

If YES → this model makes sharp, testable predictions for CMB-S4, SPHEREx, LiteBIRD

2. QUINTIN NO-GO ANALYSIS
   File: 02_quintin_nogo_evasion.md

   The Quintin et al. (2015) no-go states: in single-field matter bounce, r ∝ |f_NL|^{4/3}.
   Large |f_NL| (which we want, ≈ 4.4) implies large r (which Planck excludes).

   Analyze:
   - What exactly does the no-go assume? (single field? specific EOS? specific bounce?)
   - Which assumptions can be relaxed?
   - Does adding a curvaton field evade the no-go? (curvaton amplifies ζ without amplifying h → r decreases while f_NL is set by contraction)
   - Does modifying w away from 0 help or hurt?
   - What does the viable parameter space look like after evasion?

3. TILT MECHANISM VIABILITY
   File: 03_tilt_mechanism_viability.md

   For each tilt mechanism:
