# 04: Top-Priority Live Channels

**Created:** 2026-03-17
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Selection Criteria

A channel qualifies as "live" only if:
1. It has at least MODERATE literature evidence
2. It is not already killed by one of our 14 structural barriers
3. It has a plausible connection to our framework OR is worth pursuing on its own merits
4. It can be tested or falsified within ~10 years

## Channel 1: Cosmic Birefringence (ALP)

**Why it remains alive:**
- 3.6σ detection (β = 0.342° ± 0.094°) — strongest data contact
- Our prediction (0.27–0.35°) matches within 1σ
- ACT, SPT, LiteBIRD, CMB-S4 will all measure this to higher precision

**Model class:** ALP cosmology with m ~ 10⁻³³ eV, coupling g_aγγ ~ 10⁻¹³ GeV⁻¹

**Distance from our framework:** ZERO — the ALP is the Barbero-Immirzi pseudoscalar promoted to a dynamical field. This is the most natural ECH-derived ingredient.

**Minimum extra ingredient needed:** None beyond what we already have.

**Theory-heavy or data-heavy?** Data-heavy. The theory is done. The next steps are:
- Sharpening the prediction (frequency-dependent birefringence β(ν))
- Comparing with LiteBIRD projections
- Testing for frequency dependence (which would distinguish ALP from miscalibration)

**Quick-kill test:** If LiteBIRD or CMB-S4 resolve the miscalibration degeneracy and confirm β > 0 at ≥ 5σ → strongly supports ALP. If β is shown to be frequency-dependent → may kill the simplest ALP model. Current status: 2.4–3.6σ depending on analysis, with unresolved α vs β separation.

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** This is NOT bounce evidence. The birefringence has nothing to do with whether there was a bounce.

## Channel 2: f_NL = −35/8 (Matter Bounce)

**Why it remains alive:**
- Parameter-free prediction: f_NL^local = −4.375
- Current bound: f_NL = −0.9 ± 5.1 (compatible at 0.7σ)
- CMB-S4 + SPHEREx + Euclid may reach σ(f_NL) ~ 1–2

**Model class:** Matter bounce (dust contraction phase)

**Distance from our framework:** LOW for the prediction itself — ECH provides the bounce, dust contraction gives f_NL = −35/8. However: ECH adds nothing to the f_NL value (perturbation transparency).

**Minimum extra ingredient needed:**
- Must address the Quintin et al. (2015) no-go: single-field matter bounce with f_NL = −35/8 implies r ~ O(1), which is excluded. Need multi-field or modified contraction to decouple r from f_NL.
- Must address n_s = 1 (8.3σ excluded)

**Theory-heavy or data-heavy?** Both. Theory: need to show a consistent model with f_NL = −35/8 AND n_s ≈ 0.965 AND r < 0.036. Data: wait for future f_NL measurements.

**f_NL detection prospects (updated from literature):**
- Planck: σ(f_NL) ~ 5 (current, insufficient)
- CMB-S4 bispectrum: σ(f_NL) ~ 2.5 (factor ~2× improvement)
- SPHEREx scale-dependent bias: σ(f_NL) ~ 1–2 (would give 2–4σ)
- MegaMapper/SPHEREx multi-tracer: σ(f_NL) ~ 0.5 (would give **8σ detection**)

**Quick-kill test:** If future measurements give f_NL = 0 ± 1 → kills matter bounce f_NL prediction.

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** Even if f_NL = −35/8 is eventually measured, it confirms generic matter bounce, NOT ECH specifically. Any matter bounce produces the same value. Also, note Choudhury et al. (2025): the negative f_NL from matter bounce actually helps PBH formation by mitigating overproduction — a constructive role in the PTA+PBH parameter space.

## Channel 3: PTA/NANOGrav Tensor Background

**Why it remains alive:**
- NANOGrav 15yr data confirmed a stochastic signal
- Matter bounce tensor spectrum fits the data
- Bayesian model comparison slightly favors bounce over some conventional sources

**Model class:** Matter bounce (blue-tilted tensor spectrum)

**Distance from our framework:** MODERATE — ECH provides the bounce background, but the tensor spectrum is from the contraction phase. The tensor amplitude depends on contraction dynamics, not ECH.

**Minimum extra ingredient needed:**
- Need to show the tensor amplitude at nHz is consistent with the CMB tensor bound (r < 0.036)
- Must address Quintin no-go (large tensor at PTA → large tensor at CMB → large r → large f_NL)

**Theory-heavy or data-heavy?** Both. Theory: tensor spectrum consistency. Data: PTA spectral characterization.

**Quick-kill test:** If PTA spectral index is measured precisely and favors n_T consistent with SMBH binaries (Ω ~ f^{2/3}) rather than bounce (Ω ~ f^{n_T} with n_T ~ 2) → kills bounce explanation.

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** The Bayesian preference is marginal and model-dependent. SMBH binary explanation is not ruled out.

## Channel 4: CMB Large-Angle Anomalies (LQC-Specific)

**Why it remains alive:**
- Agullo et al. (2021) showed ONE model explains THREE anomalies (low-ℓ deficit, parity, hemispherical asymmetry)
- Each anomaly is 2–3σ; jointly they may be more significant
- Future measurements (LiteBIRD, CMB-S4) may clarify

**Model class:** LQC bounce + inflation (NOT pure bounce, NOT ECH)

**Distance from our framework:** LARGE. This requires:
1. LQC dressed-metric perturbation corrections (ECH doesn't have these)
2. Post-bounce inflation (undermines the "bounce replaces inflation" program)
3. Specific LQC initial conditions

**Minimum extra ingredient needed:** Abandon ECH; switch to LQC; add inflation.

**Theory-heavy or data-heavy?** Mostly data-heavy (the LQC theory already exists).

**Quick-kill test:** If any of the three anomalies goes away with better data or is explained by systematics → weakens the multi-anomaly argument.

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** This channel requires abandoning ECH for LQC. It is relevant to bouncing cosmology broadly but NOT to our specific project.

## Channel 5: GW Echoes from Bounce (Emerging)

**Why it remains alive:**
- Zhu & Cai (2026) is very recent; the idea is novel
- Bounce echoes would be a genuine smoking-gun signature
- Could apply to any bounce model

**Model class:** Bounce-generic

**Distance from our framework:** LOW in principle (ECH bounce has finite duration), but KILLED by frequency gate for ECH-scale bounce (echoes at GHz, inaccessible).

**Minimum extra ingredient needed:** A sub-Planckian bounce energy to bring echoes to observable frequencies — but this loses the ECH motivation.

**Theory-heavy or data-heavy?** Theory-heavy (need to assess whether echo amplitude is detectable for any reasonable bounce energy).

**Quick-kill test:** Same frequency-amplitude scaling as chiral GWs: Ω_GW ∝ f⁸. If this scaling applies to echoes too → DEAD for all Planck-scale bounces.

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** Almost certainly killed by the same frequency gate that killed chiral GWs.

## The uncomfortable conclusion

The top-priority channel (birefringence) is not bounce evidence.
The next three channels (f_NL, PTA, CMB anomalies) are bounce-generic, not ECH-specific.
The fifth channel is likely killed by the frequency gate.

**There is no live channel that is simultaneously:**
1. Supported by data
2. Specific to bouncing cosmology (not just "compatible with")
3. Connected to our ECH framework
4. Achievable with our existing assets

This is not a failure of imagination — it is a structural consequence of the ECH bounce being perturbation-transparent.
