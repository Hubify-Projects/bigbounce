# 01: Claim Registry — "Bounce Helps Explain X"

**Created:** 2026-03-17
**Status:** COMPLETE

---

## How to read this registry

Each claim is classified by:
- **Scope:** bounce-generic / matter-bounce-specific / LQC-specific / ECH-specific / bounce-independent
- **Source:** internal (our work) / external (literature)
- **Data contact:** does the claim touch real data, or is it theoretical only?

---

## Claim 1: Low-ℓ CMB Power Deficit

**Statement:** A pre-inflationary bounce suppresses large-angle (ℓ < 30) CMB power, explaining the observed low-ℓ deficit.

**Sources:**
- Agullo, Kranas & Sreenath (2021), PRL 126, 141301 — LQC bounce + inflation
- Ashtekar & Gupt (2017), PRL 119, 211301 — LQC pre-inflationary dynamics
- Contaldi et al. (2003) — general pre-inflationary suppression (not bounce-specific)

**Scope:** LQC-specific (requires LQC bounce + subsequent inflation with specific initial conditions)

**Data contact:** Yes — fit to Planck 2018 angular power spectrum

## Claim 2: CMB Parity Asymmetry

**Statement:** A pre-inflationary bounce that lasts a finite duration introduces parity-violating correlations in the CMB, explaining the observed odd-parity excess at ℓ < 30.

**Sources:**
- Agullo, Kranas & Sreenath (2021), PRL 126, 141301 — same LQC bounce model
- Planck 2018 VI — parity anomaly is 2.5–3σ depending on estimator

**Scope:** LQC-specific (requires dressed-metric perturbation corrections + inflation)

**Data contact:** Yes — comparison with Planck parity estimator

**Sources:**

**Data contact:** Partial — the mechanism is qualitative, not a precise fit

## Claim 8: Blue Tensor Tilt / Detectable GW Background

**Statement:** Bounce cosmology predicts a blue-tilted tensor spectrum (n_T > 0), potentially detectable by next-generation GW experiments.

**Sources:**
- Brandenberger et al. (2014) — matter bounce tensor spectrum
- Cai & Wilson-Ewing (2015) — LQC tensor perturbations
- Quintin et al. (2015) — no-go: single-field matter bounce cannot have small r AND small f_NL simultaneously

**Scope:** Matter-bounce-specific

**Data contact:** No — predictions are theoretical; r ~ 10⁻⁴ (LQC) or r ~ 0.1–1 (classical matter bounce, but then f_NL too large). No detector sensitivity at relevant frequencies yet.

## Claim 9: Tensor-to-Scalar Ratio Predictions

**Statement:** The ECH/LQC bounce gives specific predictions for r that differ from inflation.

**Sources:**
- Wilson-Ewing (2013) — r ~ 10⁻⁴ in LQC matter bounce
- Our Phase 1a: r ~ 10⁻⁵⁵ (classical perturbation on modified background)
- LQC dressed-metric: r ~ 10⁻⁴ (quantum-geometry corrections suppress but not fully)

**Scope:** LQC-specific (dressed-metric corrections required for non-trivial r)

**Data contact:** No — r < 0.036 (BICEP/Keck 2022). Both predictions are compatible with current upper bounds but not testable.

## Claim 10: Non-Gaussianity f_NL = −35/8

**Statement:** The matter bounce predicts f_NL^local = −35/8 ≈ −4.375, a parameter-free prediction distinguishable from single-field slow-roll inflation (f_NL ≈ 0).

**Sources:**
- Cai et al. (2009), JCAP — original calculation
- Quintin et al. (2015) — no-go: single-field matter bounce has f_NL vs r tension

**Scope:** Matter-bounce-specific (from dust contraction, bounce-independent at transfer level)

**Data contact:** Partial — Planck bound f_NL = −0.9 ± 5.1. Current 1σ error is 5.1, prediction is −4.375. Distinguishable at ~1σ now; future CMB-S4 / LSS surveys could reach 2–3σ.

---

## Claim 11: Galaxy Chirality / Large-Scale Parity Violation

**Statement:** Bounce cosmology with parity violation produces large-scale parity-odd correlations, potentially explaining observed galaxy handedness signals.

**Sources:**
- Hou et al. (2023) — 4-point parity-odd signal in BOSS at 7σ (controversial)
- No bounce-specific mechanism in literature directly targeting this

**Scope:** SPECULATIVE — no clear mechanism connecting bounce parity violation to galaxy-scale signatures. The parity-odd 4-point signal, if real, would need a parity-violating primordial bispectrum or trispectrum. Bounce models have not been shown to produce this specific signature.

**Data contact:** Weak — the Hou et al. signal is contested (potential systematics, fiber collisions)

---

## Claim 12: Cosmic Birefringence

**Statement:** A pseudoscalar ALP with specific mass and coupling produces cosmic birefringence β ≈ 0.30–0.35°, matching observations.

**Sources:**
- Minami & Komatsu (2020) — first detection β = 0.35° ± 0.14° (2.4σ)
- ACT DR6 (Namikawa et al. 2024) — independent confirmation
- Our Paper 1: β = 0.27–0.35° from ALP with m ~ 10⁻³³ eV

**Scope:** **BOUNCE-INDEPENDENT.** The ALP mass and coupling determine birefringence. The bounce provides no input to this prediction. The same ALP model works in standard ΛCDM + inflation.

**Data contact:** Yes — β = 0.342° ± 0.094° (combined, 3.6σ)
