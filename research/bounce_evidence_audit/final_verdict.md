## 3. Does any path remain to argue bounce cosmology fits reality better than ΛCDM + inflation?

**Not for the ECH framework specifically.** The ECH bounce:
- Has no perturbation-level fingerprint (Branch Vb result: perturbation transparency)
- Produces n_s = 1 with dust contraction (8.3σ excluded)
- Has no natural tilt mechanism (Branch W result: ALP mass incompatibility)
- Cannot produce signals at observable GW frequencies (chiral GW frequency gate: FAILED)
- Is identical to LQC at the background level, but strictly LESS predictive at the perturbation level (no dressed-metric corrections)

**For generic bouncing cosmology:** A weak case exists. The matter bounce has:
- f_NL = −35/8 (testable in ~10 years)
- A blue tensor spectrum (qualitative discriminator, but untestable)
- Compatibility with PTA data (but not uniquely)
- CMB anomaly explanations via LQC (but requires inflation after bounce)

**The honest answer:** Bounce cosmology does NOT currently fit observations BETTER than ΛCDM + inflation. It fits observations COMPARABLY for some channels and WORSE for others (n_s, r). The anomalies that bounce explains (CMB low-ℓ, parity) are individually 2–3σ and may be statistical flukes or systematics. No single observation REQUIRES a bounce.

The strongest argument for bouncing cosmology is NOT observational — it is THEORETICAL: bounces resolve the singularity, avoid the trans-Planckian problem, and (in slow contraction models) resolve BKL instability. These are virtues of theoretical economy, not data fits.
