## 1. The Operator and Its Requirements

The one-loop VVA triangle generates:

```
L_eff = C_eff * J^5_mu * K^mu_{CS}
```

with C_eff = (3 alpha_{EM} kappa) / (2 pi) * C(gamma) * (sum_f Q_f^2)

For birefringence, this requires:
- A nonzero chiral density <J^5_0> = n_5 at some point along the
  photon's path from the last scattering surface (z ~ 1100) to today

This section systematically evaluates every possible source of n_5
in the cosmological history.

## 2. Source-by-Source Evaluation

### 2.1 The Early Universe (T > T_EW ~ 160 GeV)

At temperatures above the electroweak scale:
- All fermions are (approximately) massless
- Chirality is a good quantum number
- Electroweak sphalerons are active
- The baryon asymmetry eta_B ~ 6 * 10^{-10} is present

The chiral asymmetry is related to the baryon asymmetry through
sphaleron equilibrium conditions:

```
n_5 ~ n_B ~ eta_B * n_gamma ~ 6e-10 * (2 zeta(3)/pi^2) T^3
```

At T ~ 100 GeV: n_5 ~ 6e-10 * 0.24 * (100 GeV)^3 ~ 1.5 * 10^{-4} GeV^3

But CMB photons were NOT present at T > 160 GeV. CMB photons are
generated at recombination (T ~ 0.3 eV) and their polarization is
set then. The early-universe chiral asymmetry is irrelevant for CMB
birefringence.

**VERDICT: Irrelevant (wrong epoch).**

### 2.2 The QCD Phase Transition (T ~ 150 MeV)

During the QCD phase transition:
- Light quarks (u, d, s) acquire effective constituent masses ~ 300 MeV
- The chiral condensate <psi-bar psi> != 0 breaks chiral symmetry
- BUT: the chiral CONDENSATE is a scalar, not a pseudoscalar
- <J^5_0> is related to the chiral ASYMMETRY, not the chiral condensate

The QCD chiral asymmetry is washed out by strong sphaleron processes
(which violate chirality). In equilibrium:

```
n_5^{QCD} = 0  (in thermal equilibrium)
```

Out of equilibrium during the phase transition: possible transient
n_5, but:
1. Duration is very short (~ 10^{-5} seconds)
2. CMB photons are not sensitive to this epoch
3. The asymmetry is in quarks, not leptons — and quarks are confined

**VERDICT: Irrelevant (wrong epoch, n_5 = 0 in equilibrium).**

### 2.3 The Electroweak Phase Transition (T ~ 160 GeV)

Sphalerons shut off below T_EW. Any chiral asymmetry generated by
sphalerons is FROZEN at T < T_EW.

However, the frozen chiral asymmetry is rapidly ERASED by fermion
mass effects. For a fermion of mass m, the chirality relaxation rate is:

```
Gamma_flip ~ alpha_EM * m^2 / T  (from spin-flip scattering)
```

For electrons at T ~ 160 GeV: Gamma_flip ~ (1/137) * (0.5 MeV)^2 / 160 GeV
~ 10^{-14} GeV ~ 10^{11} s^{-1}. The Hubble rate at T ~ 160 GeV is
H ~ T^2/M_Pl ~ 10^{-14} GeV.

So Gamma_flip / H ~ 1 for electrons at T ~ T_EW. The electron chirality
is marginal at the EW scale and washed out rapidly below it.

For heavier fermions (quarks, tau, muon): Gamma_flip >> H. Their chirality
is washed out immediately.

**By T ~ 10 GeV: all chiral asymmetries are erased for charged fermions.**

**VERDICT: n_5 = 0 for all charged fermions well before recombination.**

### 2.4 Recombination (T ~ 0.3 eV, z ~ 1100)

At recombination:
- Electrons and protons are non-relativistic (T << m_e = 0.5 MeV)
- For non-relativistic fermions: chirality is not a good quantum number
- The expectation value of J^5_0 for a non-relativistic fermion at rest:

```
<J^5_0> = <psi-bar gamma_0 gamma_5 psi> = <psi-dagger gamma_5 psi>
        ~ (p/m) * n_f  (suppressed by v/c)
```

For electrons at recombination: v/c ~ sqrt(T/m_e) ~ sqrt(0.3/5e5) ~ 10^{-3}

```
n_5 ~ (v/c) * n_e ~ 10^{-3} * 300 cm^{-3} ~ 0.3 cm^{-3}
```

This is MUCH smaller than the already-tiny estimate in File 05. The
birefringence from this source is even more negligible (additional
factor of 10^{-3} from v/c).

**VERDICT: n_5 ~ 10^{-3} * n_e. Negligible (adds another 3 orders
of magnitude suppression to the already-impossible 28-order gap).**

### 2.5 Post-Recombination (z < 1100)

After recombination:
- The universe is predominantly neutral (hydrogen and helium atoms)
- Free electron fraction drops to ~ 10^{-4}
- Free electrons are non-relativistic
- There are no free quarks

The chiral density is doubly suppressed: by the low free electron
fraction AND by the non-relativistic velocity.

```
n_5 ~ (v/c) * x_e * n_baryon ~ 10^{-3} * 10^{-4} * 200 cm^{-3}
     ~ 0.02 cm^{-3}
```

This is cosmologically negligible.

After reionization (z ~ 6-10): x_e -> 1 but n_baryon ~ 10^{-7} cm^{-3}
(diluted by expansion). And v/c is still small for non-relativistic
electrons.

**VERDICT: n_5 effectively zero throughout post-recombination history.**

### 2.6 Neutrinos

Neutrinos are electrically neutral (Q = 0). They do not appear in the
VVA triangle (no QED vertex). Even if neutrinos have a nonzero
<J^5_0>, it does not couple to photons through this operator.

(Neutrinos could contribute through gravitational anomaly terms, but
those are R R-tilde which vanishes on FRW.)

**VERDICT: Neutrinos do not contribute.**

### 2.7 Hypothetical Dark Sector Fermions

If there exist dark sector fermions with:
- Non-zero electromagnetic charge (millicharged particles)
- A macroscopic chiral asymmetry

Then they could source birefringence through the ECH operator. However:
1. This requires physics BEYOND the Standard Model
2. Millicharged particles are severely constrained (Q < 10^{-7} e)
3. The coupling would be suppressed by Q^2 in addition to 1/M_Pl^2
4. This would be a generic millicharged-particle effect, not ECH-specific

**VERDICT: Requires BSM physics. Not ECH-specific.**

## 4. Can Anything Save This?

### 4.1 Enhanced chiral asymmetry from new physics?

If there were a mechanism to generate n_5 >> n_baryon (e.g.,
chiral magnetic effect, axionic domain walls, etc.), the gap
could be reduced. But:
- Such a mechanism would require BSM physics
- The large n_5 would have other observable consequences
  (e.g., large scale magnetic fields from chiral magnetic effect)
- These consequences are not observed
- Even with n_5 ~ n_gamma (the theoretical maximum): the Planck
  suppression still gives beta ~ 10^{-20} deg (still 20 orders
  too small)

### 4.2 Resonant enhancement?

If the photon frequency matches some torsion mass scale, there could
be a resonant enhancement. But torsion is non-propagating in ECH
(no mass pole, no resonance). There is no resonance.

### 4.3 Secular accumulation?

The birefringence DOES accumulate over the photon path (it is an
integral along the line of sight). But we already included this
in the estimate (integrating over the Hubble time). There is no
additional enhancement.

### 4.4 Non-perturbative effects?

Could instantons or other non-perturbative effects generate a large
effective theta-term for the photon? In QED, there are no instantons
(the gauge group is U(1), which has trivial topology in 4D). In the
gravitational sector, gravitational instantons exist but do not
directly couple to photons in the minimal model.

### 4.5 Promote the Immirzi parameter to a field?

If gamma -> gamma(x) (dynamical Immirzi field), then the gradient
d(gamma) acts as a rolling pseudoscalar and CAN source birefringence.
But this was already explored and closed in Foundation B (topological-
shift duality: you cannot have both mass protection and geometric
content simultaneously).

## 6. The Structural Reason for Failure

The birefringence formula in a generic ALP model is:

```
beta = (Delta phi) / (2 f)
```

where Delta phi is the field excursion and f is the decay constant.
For beta ~ 0.3 deg ~ 5 * 10^{-3} rad: need Delta phi / f ~ 10^{-2}.

In the ECH model, the analogous quantity is:

```
beta ~ (C_eff * n_5 * t_H) ~ (alpha/pi) * (n_5/M_Pl^2) * t_H
```

The ratio n_5/M_Pl^2 is the killer: even with n_5 ~ T_{CMB}^3 ~ (eV)^3:

```
n_5/M_Pl^2 ~ (eV)^3 / (10^{18} GeV)^2 ~ 10^{-45}
```

This is the same Planck suppression that kills ALL low-energy
observables of ECH gravity. It is Barrier 4 (Planck suppression)

## 7. FRW-Specific Constraints

Even setting aside the magnitude problem, there are additional
FRW-specific constraints:

1. **Isotropy:** <J^5_i> = 0. Only the temporal component contributes.

2. **Homogeneity:** n_5(x) = n_5(t). No spatial gradients. The
   birefringence is the same in all directions (isotropic rotation).
   This is consistent with the observed signal (which IS isotropic).

3. **Time dependence:** n_5(t) evolves with the expansion. For
   non-relativistic fermions: n_5 ~ a^{-3} (dilution). For
   relativistic fermions: n_5 ~ a^{-3} (dilution) but also
   chirality-flip processes wash it out.

4. **R R-tilde = 0 on FRW:** The gravitational contribution to the
   axial anomaly vanishes. No gravitational generation of n_5 on
   FRW backgrounds.

All of these are consistent with (and reinforce) the conclusion that
the ECH birefringence is cosmologically dead.
