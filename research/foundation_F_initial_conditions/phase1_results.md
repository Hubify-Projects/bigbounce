## The Question

Can bounce physics determine or constrain the initial conditions
of a late-time dark-energy field, producing a predictive relation
between bounce parameters and DE parameters?

## The Answer

**No.** Two independent obstacles prevent a bounce-DE connection
through initial conditions:

1. **Attractor washout:** Quintessence models with late-time
   attractors erase all memory of initial conditions. The DE state
   depends on the POTENTIAL PARAMETERS, not on φ_i.

2. **Generic Planck displacement:** The bounce provides initial
   conditions φ_i ~ O(M_Pl) or φ_i ~ 0. These are generic outcomes
   of any Planck-scale epoch (inflation, bounce, or otherwise). No
   specific, predictive displacement is produced.

A third obstacle underlies both:

3. **The 10¹²² gap:** The bounce operates at M_Pl. Dark energy
   operates at 10⁻³⁰ M_Pl. No mechanism bridges 122 orders of
   magnitude without introducing at least one additional tuned
   parameter. This is the cosmological constant problem itself,
   which Foundation F does not solve.

## Strongest Candidate

**Candidate B: pNGB misalignment** is the least problematic.

It works: a pNGB with Λ ~ 10⁻³ eV, f ~ M_Pl, and θ_i ~ O(f)
produces ρ_DE ~ (10⁻³ eV)⁴ with w ≈ -1. The bounce naturally
provides θ_i ~ O(f) because any Planck-scale displacement gives
O(1) misalignment.

But it is NOT bounce-specific:
- θ_i ~ O(f) is produced by ANY high-energy mechanism (inflation,
  thermal fluctuations, random initial conditions)
- Λ and f must be tuned independently of the bounce
- The factor (1 - cos(θ_i/f)) varies only between 0 and 2 — no
  prediction is possible from knowing θ_i more precisely

**The pNGB model is bounce-COMPATIBLE but bounce-GENERIC.** The
bounce provides one of many equivalent ways to set generic O(1)
initial conditions.

## The Attractor-Sensitivity Dilemma

Foundation F reveals a structural dilemma for initial-condition
mechanisms:

```
IF the model has an attractor:
  → Initial conditions are erased
  → Bounce information is lost
  → DE depends on potential parameters (tuned)

IF the model lacks an attractor:
  → Initial conditions matter
  → BUT they must be fine-tuned (sensitive dependence)
  → Bounce provides only generic M_Pl-scale conditions
  → Required precision (10⁻³⁰ or worse) is unachievable
```

**There is no regime where initial conditions are both:**
1. **Meaningful** (not washed out by attractors)
2. **Non-fine-tuned** (achievable from generic bounce physics)

This is a structural impossibility, not a model-specific failure.

## What Remains

### The cosmological constant problem

Every foundation ultimately encounters the same obstacle: the
122-order-of-magnitude gap between M_Pl⁴ and ρ_DE. This gap is
the cosmological constant problem. No mechanism tested in A–F
bridges it from spin-torsion physics.

### The honest conclusion

The spin-torsion bounce cosmology is a valid, well-motivated
early-universe mechanism. But it has NO SPECIAL CONNECTION to
late-time dark energy. The two phenomena occur at scales separated
by 10⁶⁰ in energy (10¹²² in energy density), and every attempt
to bridge this gap encounters a structural barrier.

Dark energy, in the context of this program, must be treated as
an INDEPENDENT problem. The bounce neither causes, constrains,
nor predicts dark energy.
