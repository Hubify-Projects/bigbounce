A matter-dominated contracting phase gives a local-type non-Gaussian amplitude f_NL^local = -35/16 = -2.1875 before the nonsingular transition. We derive this value by re-summing all four cubic vertices at epsilon = 3/2, re-expand the result in an ordered symmetric basis, and obtain the unique coefficients (3, 1, -9, 5, -33, 9); independent checks use Cai et al.'s order-grouped expressions and Li et al.'s general-c_s formula. The result corrects the unreproduced printed -35/8 literature value. Conditional on faithful cubic-order transmission through a specified bounce completion, we recast the published Heinrich et al. SPHEREx multi-tracer bispectrum sensitivity through an explicit template map. The exact shape gives flat-grid amplitude recovery r = 0.8354 and shape cosine r_cos = 0.9817, yielding a 2.63-sigma template-corrected recast before additional nuisance marginalization. A surrogate-covariance nuisance check spans 3.47 sigma with nuisances fixed, 3.14 sigma after marginalizing the relativistic-projection amplitude A_GR, 2.32 sigma with an explicit 30% theory prior on the PNG bias-response coefficient b_phi, and 0.42 sigma when b_phi is free. These are conditional sensitivity checks, not a new joint-covariance forecast. A prior-volume calculation is retained only to illustrate dependence on prior choices. The primary contribution is therefore the amplitude derivation and its conditional observational recast, not a unified forecast.
