Dear Prof. Cai and Prof. Brandenberger,

I am writing as a courtesy ahead of posting a manuscript to arXiv, because it
bears directly on a result of yours and I would value your comment before it is
public.

In studying the local-type non-Gaussianity of the matter bounce, I re-derived
the squeezed-limit amplitude starting from the four cubic-action vertex
contributions given in Cai, Xue, Brandenberger & Zhang (2009,
arXiv:0903.0631) — the field-redefinition term, the ζζ̇² term, the
ζ̇ ∂ζ ∂χ term, and the ζ(∂ᵢ∂ⱼχ)² term, evaluated at ε = 3/2. Summing those
four contributions exactly (symbolic fractions) and taking the squeezed limit
k₁ ≪ k₂ = k₃, I obtain

    f_NL^local = −35/16 = −2.1875  (with an O(k₁²/k²) correction),

rather than the −35/8 quoted in the final printed shape polynomial (your
Eq. 37). I traced the difference to a single spurious +(99/128) Σᵢ kᵢ³
local-shaped term that appears in the final combined polynomial but is absent
from every intermediate ε-order-grouped expression in the same paper; the
per-vertex physics itself gives −35/16. The two values differ by exactly a
factor of two in the local amplitude.

This −35/16 also matches the independent general-c_s result of Li, Quintin,
Wang & Cai (2017, JCAP 03:031, arXiv:1612.02036), whose Eq. (5.1),
−165/16 + 65/(8c_s²), reduces to −35/16 at c_s = 1. Both works use the identical
f_NL = 10A/(3 Σ kᵢ³) normalization, so this appears to be an arithmetic slip in
the final combination step of the 2009 paper rather than a convention difference
— and, since Li et al. (with you as a coauthor) already carry −35/16, it may be
that the correction was effectively made in the later paper without a note.

I want to be careful and collegial here: I have checked this three independent
ways (the vertex re-sum, your own ε-order-grouped intermediates, and the Li et
al. general-c_s formula), but you and your collaborators know this calculation
far better than I do, and I would genuinely welcome any correction if I have
misread a definition or dropped a term. If you agree the correct squeezed
amplitude is −35/16, I would be glad to say so plainly and to cite any note,
erratum, or comment you would like referenced. If you see an error in my
reasoning, I would much rather learn it now than post something incorrect.

The manuscript is a SPHEREx/MegaMapper sensitivity forecast for the matter
bounce; the −35/16 resolution is presented as an appendix result with the full
derivation shown, so it is fully checkable. I am happy to share the current
draft or the symbolic derivation notebook if that would be useful, and to hold
the posting briefly if you would like to look first.

Thank you for the original work — it is the foundation this builds on — and for
any time you can spare to weigh in.
