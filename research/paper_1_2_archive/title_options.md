## Recommendation

**Keep the current title (#7).** It is the most accurate, clearest, and most professionally calibrated. The subtitle "Requirements for Completion" signals that the paper identifies what is missing (positive framing of negative results) without overclaiming.

**Runner-up (#10)** is the strongest alternative if you want to foreground the mass-coupling lock. It names the specific result and communicates the paper's unusual structure (negative results as lessons). Risk: "Five Closed Derivation Routes" might read as too negative for some editors.

**Avoid #5** ("Why Propagating Torsion Cannot Drive Dark Energy") — it overclaims by implying a universal negative, which the paper explicitly does not assert.
