## 1. What is Paper 1.2?

Paper 1.2 is a **scientifically honest reframing** of the spin-torsion
dark-energy project. It is not a defense of the old model. It is not a
presentation of a completed new model. It is a paper that:

1. Presents the ECH phenomenological framework as a motivated starting
   point (not as a sufficient foundation),
2. Reports the systematic closure of four minimal derivation routes as a
   real scientific contribution,
3. Extracts the structural lessons from those closures,
4. Identifies the requirements that any next-generation foundation must
   satisfy,
5. Surveys three candidate foundation directions without claiming any is
   solved.

**Paper type:** Model reconsideration + upgraded foundations paper.

It is a hybrid: partly retrospective (what we learned), partly prospective
(what comes next). This is an unusual paper format but an honest one. The
alternative — pretending the old model is fine, or pretending a new model
is already solved — is worse.

## 2. Proposed Title Options

1. **"Geometric Dark Energy from Spin-Torsion Cosmology: Lessons from
   Systematic Model Testing and Directions for Next-Generation
   Foundations"**
   — Most descriptive; explicitly signals the retrospective+prospective
   structure.

2. **"What Minimal Spin-Torsion Cosmology Cannot Do: Systematic Closure
   of First-Principles Dark Energy Routes and Requirements for
   Next-Generation Models"**
   — Leads with the negative result; strongest scientifically but may be
   less appealing.

3. **"Beyond Minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst Gravity: Toward Geometric
   Foundations for Late-Time Dark Energy"**
   — Forward-looking; positions the closures as motivation rather than
   main result.

4. **"Dark Energy from Geometry: A Phenomenological Framework, Its
   Structural Limits, and the Path Forward"**
   — Clean and honest; presents all three components of the paper.

**Recommended:** Option 4 or Option 1, depending on intended audience.

## 3. Central Thesis Options

### Thesis A (Strongest version)
"Minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity provides a consistent
phenomenological dark-energy framework but cannot derive late-time dark
energy from first principles. Systematic testing of four minimal routes
identifies the structural reasons for failure and establishes concrete
requirements for next-generation geometric foundations."

### Thesis B (Forward-looking version)
"We present a geometric dark-energy framework rooted in spin-torsion
cosmology, identify its structural limitations through systematic model
testing, and propose requirements and candidate directions for
next-generation foundations that address those limitations."

### Thesis C (Conservative version)
"A phenomenological dark-energy framework inspired by Einstein-Cartan-Holst
gravity is shown to be observationally consistent but theoretically
incomplete. We report the systematic closure of four first-principles
routes and extract the structural requirements for any viable geometric
dark-energy mechanism."

**Recommended:** Thesis A — it is the most precise and honest.

## 4. Section-by-Section Structure

### Part I: Framework and Phenomenology

**Section 1: Introduction**
- Dark energy problem; geometric approaches; spin-torsion motivation
- Explicitly state: this paper presents a framework, tests it against its
  own derivation program, and identifies what comes next
- Do NOT overclaim. No "we derive dark energy from geometry" language.

**Section 2: Einstein-Cartan-Holst Framework**
- ECH action, Holst term, Barbero-Immirzi parameter
- Torsion equation of motion (algebraic)
- Torsion elimination and reduced action
- Four-fermion interaction (perfect-square structure)
- **New framing:** Present this as the minimal starting point, explicitly
  noting that torsion is non-propagating and all Holst content reduces
  to the four-fermion term.

**Section 3: Phenomenological Dark-Energy Ansatz**
- The scaling rho_Lambda = Xi * M_Pl^4
- Inflationary dilution factor
- **Explicitly label as ansatz, not derivation**
- w = -1 as assumption
- Fine-tuning reduction (10^{120} to 10^5) as phenomenological observation

**Section 4: Observational Consistency**
- MCMC fits (H_0, sigma_8, S_8) — reframed as standard LCDM-extension
  analysis, not as "evidence for the framework"
- Delta N_eff: consistent with zero, not a signal
- Birefringence: consistency check only (no derived photon coupling)
- Galaxy spins: empirical, large gap, not a prediction of the framework
- **Key change:** These are consistency checks, not confirmations

### Part II: Systematic Model Testing

**Section 5: The Derivation Program**
- Central question: does the minimal model derive w = -1?
- Methodology: canonical problem statements, gates, failure modes
- Summary of four routes tested

**Section 6: Structural Lessons from Closure**
- The five structural lessons from the reconsideration memo
- Why the minimal model fails as a foundation (not just individual routes)
- What remains open

**Section 7: Closure as Scientific Contribution**
- The negative results map the landscape
- Comparison with other approaches in the literature
- What this tells us about the requirements for any viable geometric DE
  mechanism

### Part III: Directions Forward

**Section 8: Requirements for Next-Generation Foundations**
- The four decision rules (DR1-DR4)
- Why each is necessary (tied to specific closure)

**Section 9: Candidate Foundation Directions**
- Foundation A: Propagating torsion / metric-affine IR sector
- Foundation B: Symmetry-protected geometric pseudoscalar
- Foundation C: Vacuum relaxation / sequestering
- Each with: motivation, structural argument, biggest risk, open questions

**Section 10: Discussion**
- Honest assessment of the project's current status
- What Paper 1.2 achieves vs. what remains open
- Comparison with other geometric dark-energy programs

**Section 11: Conclusion**

### Appendices

- Appendix A: Notation and conventions (from Paper 1.01, minimal edits)
- Appendix B: Parameter summary (from Paper 1.01, relabeled as
  phenomenological)
- Appendix C: Four-route closure details (or cite supplement directly)
- Appendix D: Ghost-freedom conditions for propagating torsion (new, brief)
- Appendix E: Claims classification (updated from Paper 1.01)

## 6. Which Old Sections Need Total Rewrite

1. **Introduction** — Cannot open with "we derive dark energy from
   spin-torsion cosmology." Must open with the problem, the approach,
   the test, and the result.

2. **Sec 4 (Enhanced Theoretical Derivations)** — This section presented
   the condensate/one-loop directions as forward-looking possibilities.
   They are now closed routes. This section should be retired and replaced
   by the closure narrative.

3. **Discussion** — Must reflect the honest state: phenomenological
   framework survives; derivation program produced clean negative results;
   next-generation foundations identified but not yet solved.

4. **Conclusion** — Must not conclude with "our framework derives dark
   energy." Must conclude with what we learned and where we go next.

### Claims that are no longer allowed

- "The framework derives dark energy from geometry" — No. It motivates a
  phenomenological ansatz.
- "The birefringence signal supports the framework" — No. The framework
  has no derived photon coupling.
- "Delta N_eff provides a testable signature" — No. It is a free parameter,
  and observations are consistent with zero.
- "The Barbero-Immirzi parameter drives late-time physics" — No. It enters
  only UV-scale contact interactions.
- "The condensate/one-loop mechanism can close the gap" — No. Both are
  closed routes.

## 10. Paper 1.2 Identity

Paper 1.2 is not:
- A cosmetic revision of Paper 1.01
- A presentation of a completed new model
- A defense of the old framework

Paper 1.2 is:
- An honest scientific document that presents a phenomenological framework,
  reports the systematic failure of its minimal derivation program, extracts
  structural lessons, and identifies requirements and directions for
  next-generation foundations
- A contribution to the literature on geometric approaches to dark energy
  that maps the landscape of what does and does not work
- A demonstration of disciplined theoretical methodology applied to a hard
  problem

The paper should be publishable in its own right, independent of whether
any next-generation foundation is ultimately successful.
