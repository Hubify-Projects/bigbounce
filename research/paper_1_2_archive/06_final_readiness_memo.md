## 2. What is the strongest contribution of the paper now?

**The mass-coupling lock (Lesson 6 / DR5) as a new structural
constraint on geometric dark-energy theories.**

This is a concrete, previously unrecognized result: in the ghost-free
PGT 0- axial torsion mode, the canonically normalized matter coupling
scales as g_eff ~ 1/(M_Pl sqrt(|t_3|)), locked to the mass parameter.
Making the mode cosmologically light (|t_3| ~ 10^58) suppresses the
coupling to 10^29 times below gravitational strength. This is not a
tuning annoyance — it is a structural feature of how the kinetic
normalization, mass, and coupling are determined by the same parameter.

The mass-coupling lock:
- Has not been stated in the PGT literature as a constraint on DE programs
- Generalizes beyond PGT to any theory where mass and coupling share
  a common kinetic-normalization origin
- Leads directly to DR5, a testable requirement for any future
  geometric DE proposal
- Gives the paper an original theoretical contribution beyond survey
  or phenomenology

The second strongest contribution is the systematic closure methodology
(predefined gates, kill criteria, honest negative reporting) applied
to five routes.

## 3. What is the single biggest remaining weakness?

**The phenomenological Part I is standard Lambda CDM + Delta N_eff
analysis with a scaling ansatz that is not derived.**

Part I would not be published on its own — it is a standard MCMC
extension analysis with a theoretical motivation story. The fine-tuning
reduction from 10^120 to 10^5 is a real parametric observation, but
without a mechanism that produces w = -1, it is a scaling argument,
not a result.

This weakness is managed, not fixed: the paper's honest framing makes
clear that Part I is phenomenological, and the scientific contribution
comes from Parts II and III. But a referee could reasonably ask: "Why
should I care about a framework that doesn't derive its own central
assumption?"

The answer is: because the systematic closure of the derivation routes
and the identification of structural requirements (especially the
mass-coupling lock) are informative for the field. The framework is the
concrete setting in which these lessons were discovered. Without it,
the lessons are abstractions.
