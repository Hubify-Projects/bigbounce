# Circulation Cover Note — Paper 1.2

**Title:** Geometric Dark Energy: Phenomenological Viability, Systematic Closures, and Requirements for Completion

**Author:** Houston Golden

**Date:** March 2026

## Summary

This paper presents a phenomenological dark-energy framework based on Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity that reduces fine-tuning from 10^120 to ~10^5, then reports the systematic failure of five first-principles routes to derive its central assumption (late-time w = -1). From these closures, we extract six structural lessons and five concrete requirements — most notably a *mass-coupling lock* in which the same parameter that makes a geometric torsion mode cosmologically light simultaneously suppresses its matter coupling to irrelevance — that constrain the space of viable geometric dark-energy theories.

## Key contribution

The paper's central new result is the **mass-coupling lock** (Section 6.6, Decision Rule DR5): in any geometric theory where a single parameter controls both kinetic normalization and mass, making the field cosmologically light automatically decouples it from observable sectors. This is demonstrated concretely in Poincaré gauge theory (coupling suppressed 10^29× below gravitational strength at meV mass) and shown to be a general pattern via canonical normalization analysis. The lock is a previously unrecognized structural constraint on geometric dark-energy programs.
