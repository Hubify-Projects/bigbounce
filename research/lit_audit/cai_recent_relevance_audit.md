## Answers to the Four Audit Questions

### Q1: Did Cai publish (2024–2026) on matter bounce bispectrum normalization, commutator normalization, or survey detectability?

**NO.** Zero papers. The f_NL = -35/8 prediction has not been revisited since 2009. The factor-of-2 between Cai (2009) and Li+ (2017) remains unresolved in the literature. Our normalization audit and template projection are genuinely novel.

### Q2: Did Cai publish (2024–2026) on axion/ALP birefringence overlapping our interpretation?

**NO.** Cai has no birefringence papers. The Nakagawa+ (2503.18924) paper is by a different group. Our birefringence work correctly acknowledges its limitations (missing photon-torsion coupling), and the dossier rates it N2 (derivative), so this is not a novelty risk.

### Q3: Did Cai's DESI papers affect our site framing?

**MINOR.** Cai's quintom papers interpret DESI as w(z) crossing -1, which is a different dark-energy narrative than our ΛCDM null result. Our site correctly presents our MCMC as finding standard ΛCDM values. No conflict, but we should be aware that the DESI interpretation landscape has evolved significantly (quintom-B behavior at 3+ sigma from DR2).

### Q4: Are there newer birefringence papers with miscalibration marginalization?

**YES, partially.** The birefringence field is active (Nakagawa+, Yin+, and others), but these focus on ALP model-building, not on EB estimator systematics. The miscalibration-marginalized likelihoods (Diego-Palazuelos+ 2022, Eskilt+ 2022) remain the relevant references for our beta measurement context. No new estimator-level work found that changes our citations.
