# Branch U: Bounce Connection Map

**Date:** 2026-03-17

---

## Question

Does the ECH bounce add anything to a two-field ALP model that standard (non-bouncing) cosmology doesn't?

---

## Possible Bounce Contributions

### 1. Initial Conditions for ALPs

**Claim:** The bounce could set θ_{i,1} and θ_{i,2} through pre-bounce dynamics.

**Assessment:** Branch J (state selection) CLOSED this route. The bounce is a Hamiltonian scattering event at H = 0 → rotates phase space, cannot contract it (Liouville theorem). The post-bounce ALP state depends on the pre-bounce state, which is unknown. The bounce **passes through** the ALP initial conditions; it does not **set** them.

**Verdict:** CLOSED by Branch J (barrier #9).

---

### 2. Mass Generation

**Claim:** The bounce could generate ALP masses through non-perturbative effects at ρ ~ ρ_Pl.

**Assessment:** At ρ_crit ~ 0.27 ρ_Pl, non-perturbative instanton effects are maximally strong. These generate masses at the Planck scale (m ~ M_Pl), not at the DE scale (m ~ H_0). Scale separation (barrier #5) prevents any Planck-era process from naturally generating a mass 60 orders of magnitude below M_Pl.

**Verdict:** CLOSED by barrier #5 (scale separation).

---

### 3. Coupling Generation

**Claim:** The bounce could enhance or set the ALP-photon coupling.

**Assessment:** The ALP-photon coupling g_{aγ} = Cα/(2πf_a) is determined by the anomaly coefficient C and the decay constant f_a. Both are UV quantities set by the particle spectrum and the compactification geometry, not by cosmological dynamics. The bounce occurs at a specific ρ but does not alter the gauge coupling or the number of charged species.

**Verdict:** CLOSED. Coupling is UV-determined.

---

### 4. Symmetry Breaking During Bounce

**Claim:** The bounce could trigger a phase transition that breaks a PQ-like symmetry, creating ALP masses.

**Assessment:** Branch O (hidden-sector phase transition) CLOSED this route. The bounce determines WHETHER a phase transition occurs (by reaching high T), not WHAT vacuum results. Any hot Big Bang cosmology at the same T produces the same phase transitions. The bounce is not special.

**Verdict:** CLOSED by Branch O (barrier #13).

---

### 5. Isocurvature Generation

**Claim:** The bounce could imprint ALP fluctuations as isocurvature modes.

**Assessment:** Branch K (scalar perturbations) showed T(k) = 1 for k << k_b by time-reversal symmetry. Pre-bounce isocurvature modes pass through unchanged. Post-bounce isocurvature modes are generated only at k ~ k_b ~ GHz (unobservable; k_CMB/k_b ~ 10^{-28}). No bounce-specific isocurvature at CMB scales.

**Verdict:** CLOSED by Branch K (generic transfer).

---

### 6. Alignment Mechanism

**Claim:** The bounce could dynamically align multiple ALP potentials (à la Kim-Nilles-Peloso).

**Assessment:** Alignment requires specific relationships between potential parameters (n, f_1, f_2). These are set by the UV completion, not by cosmological dynamics. The bounce has no mechanism to adjust potential parameters during evolution — the Lagrangian is fixed.

**Verdict:** CLOSED. Potential parameters are Lagrangian-level, not dynamical.

---

## Summary

| Mechanism | Barrier | Status |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Initial conditions | #9 (Liouville) | CLOSED |
| Mass generation | #5 (scale separation) | CLOSED |
| Coupling generation | UV-determined | CLOSED |
| Symmetry breaking | #13 (bounce-vacuum decoupling) | CLOSED |
| Isocurvature | Branch K (T=1) | CLOSED |
| Alignment | Lagrangian-level | CLOSED |

**The bounce adds nothing to any two-field ALP model.** Every possible contribution is blocked by one of the thirteen barriers established in Branches A–O. This is not surprising — the barriers were derived precisely to close all bounce → late-time connections.

---

## Implication for Branch U

The two-field ALP + DE model exists in standard (non-bouncing) cosmology just as well as in ECH cosmology. The bounce is background scenery, not an active ingredient. If we adopt a two-field model, we are doing axiverse cosmology with ECH wallpaper.

This does not invalidate the spectator ALP result — the ALP prediction (β ~ 0.27°) is equally valid with or without the bounce. The bounce is one possible UV motivation for f_a ~ M_Pl, but the prediction does not depend on the bounce happening.
