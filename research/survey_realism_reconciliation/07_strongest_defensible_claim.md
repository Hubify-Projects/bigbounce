# 07: Strongest Defensible Claim

## The Claim

A quasi-dust matter bounce with standard GR perturbation theory and Bunch-Davies vacuum produces a parameter-free prediction: f_NL^local = -35/8 = -4.375, with negative sign and local squeezed-limit shape.

This prediction is:
- **Mechanism-independent** (holds for ECH, LQC, or any nonsingular bounce with matter-dominated contraction)
- **Parameter-free** (no tuning, no free parameters beyond the equation of state w ≈ 0)
- **Testable** by near-future galaxy surveys via the scale-dependent bias effect and the galaxy bispectrum

## What Is Robust

1. The theoretical prediction f_NL = -35/8 (algebraically verified, mechanism-independent)
2. The signal is EXACTLY local-type for LSS scale-dependent bias estimators (squeezed-limit match is exact)
3. SPHEREx can test this at σ(f_NL) ~ 0.7 via the galaxy bispectrum (dedicated forecast, arXiv:2311.13082)
4. The signal is 300× larger than standard single-field inflation's f_NL and opposite in sign

## What Is Conditional

1. **SPHEREx at σ = 0.5** requires combining bispectrum + power spectrum (the bispectrum-only σ = 0.7 is more conservative)
2. **MegaMapper at σ = 0.5** requires: (a) the survey being built, (b) accurate GR projection correction, (c) adequate b_φ priors, (d) multi-tracer performance
3. **The viable model (Wilson-Ewing quasi-dust)** requires LQC to suppress r from O(1) to 10⁻⁴; without this, the model has a tensor-to-scalar ratio tension

## What Is Still Uncertain

1. The residual GR projection effect after correction for MegaMapper LBGs: probably 0.3-1.0σ but model-dependent
2. The PNG bias parameter b_φ for high-z galaxy samples: universality relation overpredicts by Δb_φ ~ 3, need simulation-calibrated priors
3. Whether MegaMapper will be funded and built

## The Single Biggest Caveat

**The detection significance depends on survey methodology, not just survey sensitivity.** The raw statistical power of next-generation surveys is sufficient to detect f_NL = -4.375 at high significance. But the PRACTICAL significance depends on how well ultra-large-scale systematics (GR projection effects, PNG bias calibration, foreground subtraction) are handled. Published forecasts assume these are handled perfectly; reality will be messier.

## The Cleanest Defensible Statement

"The matter-bounce scenario makes a specific, parameter-free, falsifiable prediction: f_NL^local = -35/8. The SPHEREx mission, launching in 2025, is forecast to test this prediction at 6σ significance through the galaxy bispectrum (σ(f_NL) ~ 0.7), with this estimate based on a dedicated multi-tracer forecast that accounts for photometric redshift effects. A null result from SPHEREx (f_NL consistent with zero at 2σ level) would strongly disfavor the quasi-dust matter bounce. A detection of f_NL ≈ -4 would constitute the first observational evidence for a pre-Big-Bang contracting phase."
