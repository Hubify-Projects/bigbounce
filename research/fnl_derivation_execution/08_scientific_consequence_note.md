# 08: Scientific Consequence Note

## If the Effective Local Signal Is Around -4

(f_NL^eff = -3.5 to -4.5)

This is the **strong success** scenario. The flagship prediction survives fully.

- **Competitiveness:** The matter bounce becomes the ONLY alternative-to-inflation model with a parameter-free, O(1), negative f_NL prediction. Standard single-field inflation predicts |f_NL| < 0.02. The matter bounce predicts f_NL = -4.4. The separation is 200x larger than the uncertainty.

- **Viability:** The model is definitively testable. MegaMapper at sigma = 0.5 gives 7-9 sigma detection (or exclusion).

- **Forecasts:**
  - MegaMapper (2030s): 7-9 sigma detection. Definitive.
  - SPHEREx (2025+): sigma ~ 2-3 for local f_NL. Would see 1-2 sigma hint.
  - CMB-S4 (2030s): Limited by cosmic variance on large scales. Marginal.
  - Planck (existing): f_NL = -0.9 ± 5.1. Our prediction at 0.7 sigma — consistent but unconstraining.

- **Hard-to-mimic:** No simple inflationary model produces f_NL = -4. Multi-field inflation CAN produce large negative f_NL, but it requires fine-tuning. The matter bounce produces it generically from epsilon = 3/2.

---

## If the Effective Local Signal Is Around -2

(f_NL^eff = -1.5 to -2.5)

This is the **weakened but usable** scenario.

- **Competitiveness:** Still a distinctive prediction. |f_NL| = 2 is still 100x larger than single-field inflation.

- **Viability:** Testable at MegaMapper: 3-5 sigma. Strong evidence but not definitive.

- **Forecasts:**
  - MegaMapper: 3-5 sigma. Significant detection.
  - SPHEREx: < 1 sigma. No useful constraint.
  - CMB-S4: Marginal.

- **Hard-to-mimic:** Still hard. f_NL = -2 is distinctive. But some multi-field inflation models produce this range, so the discrimination is less clean.

- **Action:** The program survives, but the "single definitive test" claim is weakened to "strong evidence." Would need to combine with other observables (shape analysis, running of f_NL) for a conclusive verdict.

---

## If the Effective Local Signal Is Around -1

(f_NL^eff = -0.5 to -1.5)

This is the **marginal** scenario.

- **Competitiveness:** Still predicted to be negative and O(1), which is distinctive. But the signal is small enough that systematics (foregrounds, shot noise, mode coupling) may be comparable.

- **Viability:** MegaMapper: 1-3 sigma. Suggestive but not conclusive.

- **Forecasts:**
  - MegaMapper: Marginal detection. 1-3 sigma.
  - SPHEREx: Useless for this signal level.
  - No single experiment is decisive.

- **Hard-to-mimic:** Partially. f_NL = -1 is within the range of various models (including some inflationary constructions with turns in field space).

- **Action:** The flagship is severely weakened. Would need to supplement with shape analysis (the full bispectrum shape, not just the local template), running of f_NL, and potentially the trispectrum. The "single clean test" narrative collapses.

---

## If the Effective Local Signal Is Near Zero

(|f_NL^eff| < 0.5)

This is the **fatal** scenario.

- **Competitiveness:** No distinctive signature survives. The matter bounce produces the same observables as LCDM + inflation to within measurement precision.

- **Viability:** The model is untestable with any planned experiment. It becomes a metaphysical preference, not a scientific alternative.

- **Forecasts:** Nothing useful. All signals are below detection thresholds.

- **Hard-to-mimic:** N/A — there is nothing to mimic.

- **Action:** The positive program for matter bounce cosmology is CLOSED at the phenomenological level. Remaining options:
  1. Search for other discriminators (trispectrum, non-adiabatic modes, specific departure from scale-invariance)
  2. Pivot to foundational theory (prove the bounce is mathematically necessary from first principles)
  3. Accept that the matter bounce is observationally indistinguishable from inflation at current/near-future sensitivity
