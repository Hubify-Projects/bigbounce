## The Test

Can cyclic spin-torsion bounce cosmology constrain the sequestering
scale μ⁴ through finite four-volume and bounce matching conditions?

## The Answer

**No. Three independent obstacles prevent it.**

---

## Obstacle 1: Cyclic Cosmology Is Incompatible With Λ_obs

A closed (k = +1) FRW universe with spin-torsion bounce AND
Λ ~ Λ_obs requires:

- a_min ~ √(3/Λ) ~ 10²⁶ m (comparable to current Hubble radius)
- ρ_bounce = ρ_crit ~ M_Pl⁴ at this enormous scale factor
- Total matter content ~ 10²⁰⁰ × observed universe

This is physically absurd. A Planck-density bounce at a ~ 10²⁶ m
requires 10²⁰⁰ times more matter than exists.

For flat (k = 0): no turnaround with Λ > 0. No cycle possible.

**Cyclic cosmology with Λ ~ Λ_obs does not exist in spin-torsion
gravity.** The sequestering framework requires cycles, but cycles
require either Λ = 0 or absurd matter content.

## Obstacle 2: μ⁴ Is Structurally Immune to Bounce Matching

Bounce matching conditions constrain LOCAL quantities at the bounce
surface: a_min, ρ_b, Ḣ_b, matter content.

The sequestering scale μ₁ is an ACTION PARAMETER, not a dynamical
variable. It is not subject to equations of motion, boundary
conditions, or matching conditions.

**Boundary conditions constrain solutions, not action parameters.**
No matching condition at the bounce can determine μ₁, because μ₁
enters the action, not the field equations. This is a structural
impossibility, not a computational difficulty.

## Obstacle 3: The Self-Consistency Equation Does Not Help

The equation Λ = μ⁴/V₄(Λ) has a UNIQUE solution for each μ⁴.
The solution family is CONTINUOUS and parameterized by μ⁴. No
discreteness emerges. The CC problem is repackaged as: why is
μ⁴ ~ 10¹²²?

The bounce contributes negligibly to V₄ (scale separation barrier,
confirmed: V₄^bounce/V₄^cycle ~ 10⁻²⁸⁰). The four-volume is
determined entirely by the turnaround era.

## Program Conclusion

### What has been tested

Seven classes of mechanism connecting the spin-torsion bounce to
late-time dark energy:

| Class | Foundation | Representative mechanism |
|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Local propagating field | A, B | Torsion modes, geometric ALPs |
| Curvature-dependent mass | C | Conformal coupling, environmental mass |
| Effective metric / coupling | D | Disformal, connection coupling |
| Global spacetime integral | E | Sequestering, curvature constraint |
| Initial conditions | F | Quintessence, pNGB misalignment |
| Vacuum selection / branching | G | Cyclic sequestering, matching conditions |

**All seven classes are closed.**

### What this means

The spin-torsion bounce cosmology is a valid early-universe
mechanism. The structural lessons (mass-coupling lock, topological-
shift duality, Planck suppression theorem, etc.) are genuine
theoretical contributions.

But the bounce has NO CONNECTION to late-time dark energy.

The connection fails at every level:
- Local fields are locked, generic, or suppressed (A–D)
- Global integrals are dominated by late-time evolution (E)
- Initial conditions are washed out or non-predictive (F)
- Vacuum selection cannot constrain action parameters (G)

The UNDERLYING reason is the 10⁶⁰ gap between the Planck energy
scale (where the bounce operates) and the Hubble scale (where dark
energy operates). This gap manifests differently in each foundation
but is ultimately the same physical fact: the two phenomena occur
at incommensurable scales with no known bridge.
