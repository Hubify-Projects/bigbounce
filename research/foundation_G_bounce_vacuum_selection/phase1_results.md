# Foundation G — Phase 1 Results

**Date:** 2026-03-15

---

## Verdict: FOUNDATION_G_WEAK_BUT_WORTH_ONE_TEST

---

## Summary

Five candidate mechanisms were screened for bounce-conditioned
vacuum selection. Two collapsed immediately (C: regularity is a
tautology; D: topological invariants are trivial or already closed
by Foundation B). One is bounce-generic (B: any hot epoch provides
the same selection). One produces the wrong answer (E/Toy 3:
matching condition gives Λ ~ M_Pl⁴).

One candidate — **Candidate A: Bounce-Conditioned Sequestering
on Cyclic Cosmology** — remains viable but with significant caveats.

---

## Candidate-by-Candidate Assessment

### Candidate A: Bounce-Conditioned Sequestering — SURVIVES (with caveats)

**Test G1 (bounce relevance): PASSES.**
Removing the bounce → no finite V₄ per cycle → sequestering
undefined or requires future boundary. The bounce is ESSENTIAL
for finite-V₄ cyclic sequestering.

**Test G3 (generic collapse): MARGINAL.**
The mechanism IS sequestering — a known framework. The bounce adds
the specific ingredient of finite V₄ per cycle, which is not
available from other cosmologies. But the mathematical content
(global Lagrange multipliers, vacuum energy cancellation) is
identical to plain sequestering.

Honest assessment: the bounce makes sequestering WELL-DEFINED but
does not make it NEW. It provides infrastructure, not content.

**Test G2 (hidden tuning): LIKELY FAILS.**
Λ_residual = μ⁴/V₄. The scale μ⁴ is a free parameter NOT
determined by the bounce. Changing μ⁴ changes Λ_residual.
The bounce constrains V₄ but not μ⁴. One free parameter remains.

HOWEVER: this is BETTER than plain Λ tuning. Standard Λ must be
tuned to 10⁻¹²² precision. Sequestering reduces this to tuning
μ⁴ — which determines Λ_residual INDEPENDENTLY of vacuum energy
loop corrections. The tuning is reduced from ∞ parameters (each
loop order) to 1 parameter (μ⁴).

**Test G5 (predictive narrowing): MARGINAL.**
Λ is narrowed in the sense that vacuum energy shifts are absorbed.
But the absolute value of Λ depends on μ⁴. No narrowing of the
allowed range of Λ from M_Pl⁴ to smaller values.

**Overall: SURVIVES_PHASE1 (barely).** The bounce is essential for
infrastructure (finite V₄) but not for content (μ⁴ still tuned).
Worth one detailed test to determine whether cyclic matching
conditions constrain μ⁴.

### Candidate B: 4-Form Flux at Bounce — FAIL_BOUNCE_IRRELEVANT

The bounce provides a high-temperature epoch for flux transitions.
But ANY high-temperature epoch (reheating after inflation, Hagedorn
phase, pre-Big-Bang) provides the same. The selection is thermal,
not geometric. Also requires a landscape.

### Candidate C: Regularity Boundary Condition — FAIL_NO_NARROWING

The H = 0 condition is a tautological evaluation of the Friedmann
equation. It either gives Λ = 0 (wrong) or is automatically
satisfied for any Λ by adjusting ρ_bounce.

### Candidate D: Topological Sector Selection — FAIL_BOUNCE_IRRELEVANT

RR̃ = 0 on FRW. Nieh-Yan is topological in EC or breaks shift
symmetry in MAG (Foundation B). Gauge θ-vacua are unrelated to the
gravitational bounce.

### Candidate E / Toy 3: Matching Condition — FAIL_HIDDEN_TUNING

Matching at the bounce IS deterministic (fixes Λ with no free
parameter). But it gives Λ ~ M_Pl⁴ — the natural Planck scale,
not the observed DE scale. The 10¹²² hierarchy is not produced.

## Strongest Candidate Class

**Candidate A: Bounce-Conditioned Sequestering on Cyclic Cosmology.**

Strengths:
- Bounce is genuinely essential (finite V₄)
- Sequestering framework is well-developed
- Vacuum energy loop corrections are absorbed
- Self-consistency equation Λ = μ⁴/V₄(Λ) may have interesting
  structure

Weaknesses:
- μ⁴ is free (hidden tuning, albeit reduced)
- Requires cyclic cosmology (unproven)
- The mechanism IS sequestering — the bounce adds infrastructure,
  not new physics
- The "bounce as decorative" risk is real: the bounce is essential
  for V₄ finiteness but may not contribute to the VALUE of Λ

## Weakest Candidate Class

**Candidate C: Regularity Boundary Condition.** It is a tautology
disguised as a boundary condition. The Friedmann equation at H = 0
does not constrain Λ — it defines the bounce density for a given Λ.

## Is Foundation G Genuinely More Promising Than Generic Sequestering?

**Marginally, at best.**

The honest assessment:

1. Generic sequestering has a V₄ → ∞ problem. The bounce solves it.
   This is a genuine contribution.

2. But the bounce does not DETERMINE Λ. It makes the sequestering
   framework WELL-DEFINED but does not fix the residual Λ. The scale
   μ⁴ remains free.

3. The bounce-sequestering combination is better than plain Λ tuning
   (reduces ∞-parameter tuning to 1-parameter tuning) but this
   improvement is from SEQUESTERING, not from the bounce.

4. The question "does cyclic matching constrain μ⁴?" is the ONLY
   remaining hope for the bounce to add genuine content. If the
   answer is no, Foundation G = "sequestering is nice, and the
   bounce provides the finite V₄ it needs."

**Foundation G is NOT clearly more promising than generic sequestering.
It is generic sequestering WITH a natural resolution of the V₄
problem. The bounce contributes INFRASTRUCTURE, not CONTENT.**

## The Brutal Question

**Is "the bounce provides finite V₄ for sequestering" a genuine
research result, or is it obvious and not worth a foundation?**

Arguments for genuine result:
- Most CC mechanisms suffer from the infinite-V₄ problem
- The bounce is one of very few known nonsingular cosmologies
- The finite-V₄ connection is specific and calculable

Arguments for "obvious and decorative":
- ANY cyclic cosmology provides finite V₄
- The bounce is not the only source of finite V₄ (compact spatial
  topology also works)
- Sequestering with finite V₄ still has μ⁴ as a free parameter

**My assessment: this is a MODEST observation, not a breakthrough.**
It is worth recording and worth one detailed test (to check whether
cyclic matching constrains μ⁴). But the prior probability of
Foundation G producing a genuine bounce-Λ connection is LOW.

## Recommended Immediate Next Test

**Phase 2 test for Candidate A:**

1. Write the Kaloper-Padilla sequestering action on a cyclic
   spin-torsion bounce cosmology (one complete cycle: bounce →
   expansion → turnaround → contraction → bounce).

2. Compute V₄ per cycle as a function of Λ and matter content.

3. Write the self-consistency equation Λ = μ⁴/V₄(Λ).

4. Determine whether the self-consistency equation has discrete
   solutions or a continuous family (parametrized by μ⁴).

5. Check whether the cyclic matching conditions (continuity of
   metric and extrinsic curvature across the bounce) impose any
   constraint on μ⁴.

**If μ⁴ is constrained by the matching: FOUNDATION_G_PROMISING.**
**If μ⁴ remains free: FOUNDATION_G_CLOSED.** The mechanism is
generic sequestering with bounce-provided finite V₄ — a modest
observation, not a foundation-level result.
