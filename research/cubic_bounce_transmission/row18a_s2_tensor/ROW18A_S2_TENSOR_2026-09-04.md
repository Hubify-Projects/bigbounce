## 0. Question and plan (written before any number exists)

Lane 9b-2 (`research/cubic_bounce_transmission/lane9b2_s2_rawadm/`) found that the **scalar** linear transmission
through the Quintin-type bounce is scheme-dependent: S1 (geometric continuation, `z = a`) amplifies the growing mode by
`|lambda_zeta^S1| = 2/(1-rho_B) = 6.06`, while S2 (effective-fluid MS variable, `z^2 = 2 a^2 eps / c_s^2`, exact modes)
transmits it essentially unchanged, `|lambda_zeta^S2| = 0.9696 / 0.9678 / 0.9647` at `k eta_B = 1e-3 / 3e-3 / 1e-2`.
Row 10 (`research/track_a3_multichannel/row10_r_ns/`) computed the **tensor** transfer on the poly and LQC backgrounds
and found `T_h = T_zeta` to 1e-9, hence `r_after = r_before = 24`. But row 10's `T_zeta` is the **S1** scalar transfer.
So the paper's `r_after = 24` is an S1-only statement. **The R7 audit's question:** is `lambda_T = 6.06` vs
`lambda_zeta = 0.97` a like-for-like comparison, or must `T_h` be recomputed in S2?

Steps and gates (each a commit):
1. **Analytic claim to test.** `h_ij` obeys `h'' + 2 (a'/a) h' + k^2 h = 0` — equivalently `mu_T = a h`,
   `mu_T'' + (k^2 - a''/a) mu_T = 0`. This equation contains **only `a`**: no `H` in a denominator, no `eps`, no `c_s`,
   no scalar constraint variables `N_1`, `psi`. The S1/S2 ambiguity is entirely a choice of *scalar* variable
   (which `z` continues through `H = 0`). Prediction: `lambda_T` is **scheme-independent**, and because S1's scalar
   window equation is literally `zeta_ddot + 3 H zeta_dot + (k^2/a^2) zeta = 0` (lane 9b-2 `window_series(scheme="S1")`),
   `lambda_T = lambda_zeta^S1 = 2/(1-rho_B) = 6.06` **identically**, not coincidentally.
2. **Numerics.** Integrate the tensor mode on the Quintin-type background from the pre-bounce adiabatic vacuum through
   the bounce to `eta_* = 50 eta_B`, by an independent code path written from the tensor equation (not by relabelling
   the scalar solver), at `k eta_B` in {1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2} matching lane 9b-2's grid. Gate: `lambda_T` agrees with
   `2/(1-rho_B)` and with the row-10 poly/LQC tensor result to the stated precision.
3. **`r_after` per scheme.** `r_after = r_before (lambda_T / lambda_zeta^scheme)^2` with `r_before = 24` (row 10,

   construction. The S2 scalar carries `c_s` in `z^2` (a constant prefactor that cancels in a transmission *ratio*)

   `c_s` in {0.44, 0.6, 0.888} and report `lambda_zeta^S2(c_s)`.
5. **Which variable is physical after the bounce**, and therefore what A3M may state.

Backgrounds: the Quintin-type piecewise background only for the S2 comparison — lane 9b-2 assumption (A1) is that

## 1. Why the tensor transfer is scheme-independent (derivation)

The transverse-traceless perturbation `h_ij` of `ds^2 = -dt^2 + a^2 (delta_ij + h_ij) dx^i dx^j` obeys, for any
`P(X, phi)` / k-essence / GR-with-a-fluid bounce (no Horndeski `G_4(X)`, `G_5`),

    hdd + 3 H hd + (k^2/a^2) h = 0        <=>        mu_T'' + (k^2 - a''/a) mu_T = 0,   mu_T = a h / 2,

with `c_T = 1`. Three properties matter here:

* **No `1/H`.** The scalar S1/S2 ambiguity arises because the comoving curvature perturbation is defined by a
  *choice of `z`* — `z = a` (S1, geometric continuation) or `z^2 = 2 a^2 eps / c_s^2` (S2, effective-fluid MS
  variable) — and `eps = -Hdot/H^2` blows up at `H = 0`, so the two continuations of `zeta` through the bounce
  differ. The tensor equation contains no `eps`, no `H` in a denominator, and none of the constraint variables
  `N_1 = zetadot/H`, `psi`, whose `H = 0` behaviour is exactly what lane 9b/9b-2 had to regulate. `h_ij` is a
  metric component, gauge-invariant at linear order with no scalar-sector redefinition available. **There is only
  one tensor variable**, therefore only one `lambda_T`.
* **No `c_s`.** `c_T = 1` identically; the sound speed appears only in the scalar gradient term `c_s^2 k^2` and in
  the scalar normalisation `z^2 propto 1/c_s^2`. So `lambda_T` cannot depend on `c_s` (checked numerically anyway).
* **It IS the S1 scalar equation.** With `z = a`, `zeta_S1 = mu_S/z` obeys `zeta_dd + 3 H zeta_d + (k^2/a^2) zeta = 0`
  — literally the tensor equation with `h -> zeta`. This is why lane 9b-2's `window_series(scheme="S1")` is the
  tensor window equation, and why row 10 found `T_h = T_zeta` to `8.5e-9` on poly and LQC: not a coincidence, an
  identity. Junction conditions agree too: at `|t| = tm` the Quintin-type background has `a`, `adot` continuous and
  only `Hdot` jumping, so `h` and `a^3 hdot` are continuous, exactly as `zeta_S1` and `a^3 eps_eff zeta_dot` are
  (with `eps_eff = 1/2` constant across the junction, unlike S2's `eps: +3/2 -> -3/2` sign flip).

**Conclusion (analytic, then verified):** `lambda_T = lambda_zeta^{S1}` exactly, for every `k` and every background.
`lambda_T = 6.06` is therefore the transfer of `h` in **all** schemes; what changes between schemes is the *scalar*
denominator in `r = P_T/P_zeta`.

## 2. Numerical verification (computed, `row18a_s2_tensor.py` -> `results.json`)
