`lambda_zeta = 0.97` a like-for-like comparison, or must `T_h` be recomputed in S2?

## 1. Why the tensor transfer is scheme-independent (derivation)

The transverse-traceless perturbation `h_ij` of `ds^2 = -dt^2 + a^2 (delta_ij + h_ij) dx^i dx^j` obeys, for any
`P(X, phi)` / k-essence / GR-with-a-fluid bounce (no Horndeski `G_4(X)`, `G_5`),

    hdd + 3 H hd + (k^2/a^2) h = 0        <=>        mu_T'' + (k^2 - a''/a) mu_T = 0,   mu_T = a h / 2,

with `c_T = 1`. Three properties matter here:

* **No `1/H`.** The scalar S1/S2 ambiguity arises because the comoving curvature perturbation is defined by a
  *choice of `z`* — `z = a` (S1, geometric continuation) or `z^2 = 2 a^2 eps / c_s^2` (S2, effective-fluid MS
  variable) — and `eps = -Hdot/H^2` blows up at `H = 0`, so the two continuations of `zeta` through the bounce
  differ. The tensor equation contains no `eps`, no `H` in a denominator, and none of the constraint variables
  `N_1 = zetadot/H`, `psi`, whose `H = 0` behaviour is exactly what lane 9b/9b-2 had to regulate. `h_ij` is a
  metric component, gauge-invariant at linear order with no scalar-sector redefinition available. **There is only
  one tensor variable**, therefore only one `lambda_T`.
* **No `c_s`.** `c_T = 1` identically; the sound speed appears only in the scalar gradient term `c_s^2 k^2` and in
  the scalar normalisation `z^2 propto 1/c_s^2`. So `lambda_T` cannot depend on `c_s` (checked numerically anyway).
* **It IS the S1 scalar equation.** With `z = a`, `zeta_S1 = mu_S/z` obeys `zeta_dd + 3 H zeta_d + (k^2/a^2) zeta = 0`
  — literally the tensor equation with `h -> zeta`. This is why lane 9b-2's `window_series(scheme="S1")` is the
  tensor window equation, and why row 10 found `T_h = T_zeta` to `8.5e-9` on poly and LQC: not a coincidence, an
  identity. Junction conditions agree too: at `|t| = tm` the Quintin-type background has `a`, `adot` continuous and
  only `Hdot` jumping, so `h` and `a^3 hdot` are continuous, exactly as `zeta_S1` and `a^3 eps_eff zeta_dot` are
  (with `eps_eff = 1/2` constant across the junction, unlike S2's `eps: +3/2 -> -3/2` sign flip).

**Conclusion (analytic, then verified):** `lambda_T = lambda_zeta^{S1}` exactly, for every `k` and every background.
`lambda_T = 6.06` is therefore the transfer of `h` in **all** schemes; what changes between schemes is the *scalar*
denominator in `r = P_T/P_zeta`.

## 3. `c_s` dependence (computed; `k eta_B = 1e-3`)

`c_T = 1`, so `c_s` does not appear in the tensor equation at all and `lambda_T` is `c_s`-independent **by
construction**, not numerically. For the scalar, a *constant* `c_s` enters the S2 problem only through the gradient
term `c_s^2 k^2`: the normalisation `z^2 = 2 a^2 eps / c_s^2` and the junction quantity `a^3 eps zeta_dot / c_s^2`
carry `c_s` as a constant prefactor that cancels in a transmission *ratio*, and the adiabatic-vacuum seed is
`v(c_s k eta)`. So the minimal exact extension of lane 9b-2's integrator is the substitution `k -> c_s k`; no new
solver was needed and none was written (`scalar_transmission(..., c_s=)` in the script).

**Reading.** The whole `c_s` effect on the *transfer* is a `1.9e-4` relative spread in `lambda_zeta^S2` (and `2e-4`

result: at `k eta_B <= 1e-2` the gradient term is negligible through the bounce, so rescaling it by `c_s^2` barely
moves the growing mode. **`r_after` is `c_s`-insensitive; the scheme choice, not `c_s`, is the `~39x` lever.**
(Row 18 item (b), `Delta f_NL^bounce(c_s)`, is a separate cubic-order question and is not answered here.)

## 4. Which scalar variable is physical, and what A3M may state

*Post*-bounce (`H != 0`, `eps = 3/2` again) the two variables coincide up to the constant linear map
`zeta_S2 = zeta_S1 / sqrt(2 eps_c) x c_s` — i.e. they differ by normalisation only, and both give the same
`P_zeta` once normalised to the same pre-bounce adiabatic vacuum. The entire difference is generated **at the
matching through `H = 0`**: `eps -> infinity` there, so `z_S2^2 = 2 a^2 eps / c_s^2` and `z_S1^2 = a^2` are not
related by a regular map across the bounce, and the growing mode picks up `6.06` in one continuation and `0.97` in
the other. Which one is physical is a **model-level** question — it is fixed by what the microphysical bounce
actually is (which stress tensor / which effective action supplies the NEC violation, and hence which quantity is
conserved through `H = 0`), and it is *not* fixed by anything computable inside the effective description used
here. This is exactly the scheme question the A3M paper already flags (lane 9b-2 §5, A2 linear finding); this lane
does not resolve it and does not claim to.

What is new and *is* settled: **the ambiguity is one-sided.** It lives entirely in the scalar denominator of
`r = P_T/P_zeta`; the numerator is common to both schemes. Therefore:

* `r_after^{S1} = 24.00` — identical to `r_before`, because `lambda_T = lambda_zeta^{S1}` identically.
* `r_after^{S2} = 9.37e2` — the bounce *amplifies* `r` by `(6.06/0.970)^2 = 39.0`, because the tensor grows by 6.06
  through the bounce while the S2 scalar does not.
* Observationally, `r < 0.036` (BK18+Planck, 95%). `r_after^{S1}` exceeds it by `6.7e2x`; `r_after^{S2}` by
  `2.6e4x`. **The conclusion is scheme-independent: the matter-bounce tensor problem is not a scheme artefact,
  and no choice of scalar continuation rescues it — the scheme choice only changes the exclusion from ~670x to
  ~26000x.** A3M may state `r_after` with a scheme label and quote the weaker (S1) number as the conservative one.

## 5. Assumptions and limits

(L1) Quintin-type piecewise background only for the S2 comparison. **No S2 `r_after` is quoted for LQC or poly:**
lane 9b-2 assumption (A1) is that their `Hdot = 0` crossings put `z_S2^2 = 0` and give the S2 `zeta` a logarithmic
point, so exact S2 modes were never constructed there and are not constructed here. The *tensor* result holds on all
three (row 10 covers poly and LQC).
(L2) `r_before = 24` is row 10's `24(1+w)` at pure dust `w = 0`; the Planck-anchored `w = -0.0029` gives 23.93,
a 0.3% shift that does not touch any conclusion.
(L3) The `k eta_B = 10^{-2}` row uses `eta_* = 50 eta_B` for all three `k`, so `k eta_* = 0.5` there and the
super-horizon-at-`eta_*` condition lane 9b-2 imposed (`k eta_* < 0.3`) is violated; only the two smaller `k` are
quotable. The identity `lambda_T = lambda_zeta^{S1}` holds at all three regardless (it is exact, not asymptotic).
(L4) `c_T = 1` assumes no Horndeski `G_4(X)`/`G_5` sector. A model with `c_T != 1` would make the tensor transfer
model-dependent (it would still not make it *scheme*-dependent) and is out of scope here.
(L5) `lambda_zeta^{S2}` at `c_s != 1` uses the exact statement that a *constant* `c_s` enters only as `k -> c_s k`;
a time-dependent `c_s(t)` through the bounce is not covered.
(L6) Nothing here resolves which of `zeta_S1`, `zeta_S2` is physical; that remains the open model-level question.
