1. **Foundation E Phase 1** (1–2 weeks): Screen sequestering and global

2. **Foundation E Phase 2** (2–3 weeks): If screening identifies a

   immediately after E closure, screen bounce-DE link candidates.

   of the above. Lower priority because it abandons DE as the main

## What the Program Concedes at Each Stage

### Moving to E
Concedes: Local geometric FRW dark-energy derivations are structurally
blocked. Dark energy, if connected to geometry at all, requires a
global or nonlocal mechanism.

### Moving to F
Concedes (additionally): Global vacuum mechanisms may not work either.
The bounce may influence but not determine dark energy. The DE sector
is likely generic EFT.

### Moving to G
Concedes (additionally): The bounce-DE connection may be non-perturbative
and possibly landscape-dependent. Calculability may be limited.

### Moving to H
Concedes: Dark energy may not be the main observable consequence of
spin-torsion geometry. The geometric signature may live in
perturbations, propagation, or dense environments instead.

### All fail
Concedes: Spin-torsion bounce cosmology is a valid early-universe
mechanism, but has no direct connection to the dark-energy problem.
The two problems are independent. This is a legitimate scientific
conclusion, not a failure.
