# 00: Live Model Lock

## Canonical Benchmark Model: Wilson-Ewing LCDM Quasi-Dust Bounce

### Definition

A nonsingular bouncing cosmology where:
1. The contracting phase is dominated by pressureless matter (dust) with a small cosmological constant Λ
2. The effective equation of state is w = -ε ≈ -0.003 (quasi-dust, Λ-driven)
3. The bounce is resolved by LQC quantum-geometry corrections at ρ_crit
4. Perturbations originate as Bunch-Davies vacuum fluctuations in the deep contracting phase
5. Standard GR perturbation theory applies during contraction (Maldacena cubic action)

### Ingredient Breakdown

| Ingredient | Source | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Matter contraction (w ≈ 0) | Generic | Generates scale-invariant scalar spectrum + f_NL |
| Λ (cosmological constant) | Already in ΛCDM | Provides w < 0, hence red tilt |
| ε = 0.003 | One free parameter | Fitted to n_s = 0.964 via n_s = 1 - 12ε |
| LQC quantum bounce | Framework-specific | Resolves singularity, suppresses r to ~10⁻⁴ |
| Bunch-Davies vacuum | Standard assumption | Fixes mode function initial conditions |

### Observables: Fixed vs Adjustable

| Observable | Value | Status |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| f_NL (local/squeezed) | -35/8 = -4.375 | **FIXED** (parameter-free, from ε=3/2 cubic action) |
| n_s | 1 - 12ε = 0.964 | **ADJUSTABLE** (ε is the one free parameter) |
| r | ~10⁻⁴ | **FIXED by LQC** (set by quantum-geometry corrections, not tunable) |
| α_s (running) | ~+10⁻⁴ | Fixed but unmeasurably small |
| n_T (tensor tilt) | >0 (blue) | Fixed but unmeasurable (r too small) |
| Shape (equil/fold ratios) | -255/64, -9/4 | **FIXED** (from shape function polynomial) |

### What Counts As This Model

The model IS the Wilson-Ewing quasi-dust bounce if:
- The contracting phase has w ≈ 0 (pressureless or near-pressureless matter)
- The bounce mechanism is nonsingular (ECH, LQC, or equivalent UV completion)
- Perturbations are standard GR during contraction
- The scalar bispectrum is dominated by the growing-mode mechanism (not bounce-generated NG)

The model is NOT defined by:
- The specific bounce mechanism (ECH vs LQC vs other)
- The UV completion details
- The exact form of quantum corrections at the bounce
