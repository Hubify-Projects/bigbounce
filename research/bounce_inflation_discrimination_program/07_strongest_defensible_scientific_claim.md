# 07: Strongest Defensible Scientific Claim

## What Has Actually Been Established

1. **A nonsingular bounce in a matter-dominated contracting universe produces a specific, parameter-free bispectrum with f_NL = -35/8 in the local squeezed limit.** This result (Cai et al. 2009) has been algebraically verified by independent evaluation of the shape function at three distinct momentum configurations, all matching exactly.

2. **This prediction is mechanism-independent:** it does not depend on whether the bounce is realized by ECH, LQC, or any other UV completion. It depends only on: (a) matter-dominated contraction (w ≈ 0), (b) standard GR perturbation theory during contraction, and (c) Bunch-Davies vacuum initial conditions.

3. **Minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity is perturbation-transparent:** the Holst term is topological (Pontryagin density) when torsion vanishes, and torsion vanishes identically for scalar field matter. The Barbero-Immirzi parameter γ is invisible in all scalar and tensor perturbation equations. This was verified through systematic audits of both scalar and tensor sectors.

4. **A viable observational package exists:** the Wilson-Ewing quasi-dust model with LQC quantum corrections achieves n_s = 0.964 (fitted via one parameter), r ~ 10⁻⁴ (from LQC), and f_NL = -35/8 (parameter-free), with no current observational tension.

5. **The f_NL prediction is very difficult for inflation to mimic:** standard single-field slow-roll gives f_NL ≈ 0 (wrong by factor 300 and wrong sign). Multi-field models can produce negative local f_NL but only through engineered constructions that introduce free parameters.

## What Remains Conditional

1. **The universe actually underwent a bounce.** This is the hypothesis being tested, not an established fact.

2. **Perturbation transfer through the bounce preserves the contracting-phase bispectrum.** This is the standard assumption in bounce cosmology but has only been rigorously verified in specific LQC models.

3. **The contraction was matter-dominated when CMB-scale modes exited the Hubble radius.** If a radiation-dominated or ekpyrotic epoch preceded the matter phase, the predictions change.

4. **Bunch-Davies vacuum is the correct initial state.** Pre-contraction dynamics or quantum gravity effects could modify the initial conditions.

5. **The MegaMapper experiment achieves σ(f_NL) ≤ 1.** The definitive test requires next-generation LSS surveys with multi-tracer capability.

## Why It Matters

If MegaMapper measures f_NL ≈ -4 in the local squeezed limit:

- It would be the first observational evidence that the early universe underwent a contracting phase before the Big Bang
- It would establish bouncing cosmology as a serious empirical alternative to inflation, not just a theoretical possibility
- The parameter-free nature of the prediction makes it sharply falsifiable: if f_NL ≈ 0, the matter bounce is excluded

If MegaMapper measures f_NL ≈ 0:

- The matter-bounce scenario with Bunch-Davies vacuum in a matter-dominated contraction is excluded
- The bounce paradigm would need to invoke non-standard initial conditions, non-matter contraction, or significantly modified perturbation transfer — all of which weaken its predictive power

## How Close to a Compelling Science Case?

**Close, but contingent on future data.** The theoretical framework is clean, the prediction is sharp, and the test is well-defined. What's missing is the measurement. This puts the matter-bounce program in a healthy scientific position: it has made a specific, falsifiable prediction and identified the experiment that will test it. This is exactly how science should work.

The program is NOT at the stage of claiming evidence for a bounce. It IS at the stage of having a concrete, testable prediction that distinguishes it from the dominant paradigm.
