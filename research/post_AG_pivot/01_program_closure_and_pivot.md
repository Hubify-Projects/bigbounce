## 2. The Direct Bounce→Dark-Energy Program Is Closed

**Statement:** Within the tested classes — local propagating fields,
global spacetime integrals, initial-condition transfer, and vacuum
selection mechanisms — no route connects spin-torsion bounce physics
to late-time dark energy in a predictive, non-fine-tuned manner.

This is not a failure of effort or ingenuity. It is a structural
result about the relationship between Planck-scale physics and the
cosmological constant problem.

The underlying reason is scale separation: the bounce operates at
E ~ M_Pl, dark energy operates at E ~ 10⁻³⁰ M_Pl. The 10⁶⁰ gap
in energy (10¹²² in energy density) cannot be bridged by any
mechanism that propagates, averages, or matches quantities across
this gap.

## 3. Why Continuing to Tweak Would Be Unscientific

The seven barriers cover ALL standard mechanism classes:

- **Local dynamics:** A, B, C, D cover propagating scalars,
  pseudoscalars, curvature-coupled fields, and effective metrics.
  Every local field route is blocked.

- **Global constraints:** E covers volume-weighted integrals and
  spacetime averages. The bounce contributes negligibly to all of
  them.

- **Initial conditions:** F covers all scalar-field evolution from
  bounce-set data. Attractors erase or fine-tuning is required.

- **Vacuum selection:** G covers discrete branching, matching
  conditions, and cyclic sequestering. Action parameters are immune.

Opening Foundation H₂ (another local field), E₂ (another global
integral), or F₂ (another initial-condition model) would test a
mechanism that falls within an already-closed class. The barriers
are CLASS-LEVEL results, not model-specific failures.

The only scientifically honest move is to accept the negative result
and pivot.

## 4. The New Split

### Branch H: Bounce-First Early-Universe Program

Treat the spin-torsion bounce as a standalone early-universe theory.
Study its predictions, observational signatures, and consistency
constraints WITHOUT requiring it to produce dark energy.

The bounce remains physically motivated:
- Avoids the Big Bang singularity
- Derives from well-established EC gravity + Dirac fermions
- Has a concrete mechanism (spin-torsion repulsion at ρ_crit)
- Makes predictions independent of dark energy

### Branch I: Independent DE Sectors Compatible With Bounce

Treat dark energy as an independent sector. Ask which DE models are
compatible with (not derived from) a spin-torsion bounce cosmology.
Test whether the bounce constrains, informs, or restricts the DE
sector in any nontrivial way.

This is NOT a derivation program. The bounce does not determine Λ.
But the bounce may restrict which DE sectors are viable, or may
constrain their parameters through consistency requirements.
