**Date:** 2026-03-15

## Closure Statement

Seven foundations (A–G) tested every standard mechanism class for
connecting the spin-torsion bounce to late-time dark energy:

| Foundation | Class | Barrier |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| A | Propagating torsion | Mass-coupling lock |
| B | Geometric pseudoscalar | Topological-shift duality |
| C | Curvature-dependent mass | Scalar-tensor universality |
| D | Disformal coupling | Planck suppression |
| E | Global vacuum integrals | Scale separation |
| F | Initial conditions | Attractor-sensitivity dilemma |
| G | Vacuum selection | Parameter immunity |

**All seven are closed.** The barriers are structural, not
model-specific. The underlying reason is scale separation:
10⁶⁰ in energy between the bounce (M_Pl) and dark energy
(10⁻³⁰ M_Pl).

The direct bounce → dark energy program is closed.

## The New Program

Treat the bounce as a standalone theory. Compute its predictions:
- Tensor perturbation spectrum (blue tilt)
- Parity violation from spin-torsion
- Full scalar perturbation theory

### Branch I: Bounce-Compatible DE
Test which DE models are compatible with the bounce:
- Horndeski stability at Planck curvature
- K-essence sound speed at bounce
- Massive gravity ghost structure

## Priority Recommendation

**Branch H first, Branch I second.**

Rationale:
- Branch H produces POSITIVE results (predictions, not just
  constraints)
- The tensor spectrum computation is well-defined and tractable
- A blue-tilted GW spectrum would be a distinctive, testable
  prediction
- Branch I produces NEGATIVE results (exclusions) which are less
  compelling without a positive result to anchor them
