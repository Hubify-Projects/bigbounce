## 1. Key references and their findings

### Shie, Nester, Yo (2008) — "Torsion Cosmology and the Accelerating Universe"

**Established fact:** This paper studies PGT cosmology with a homogeneous
isotropic torsion configuration. They use the torsion TRACE vector T_mu (not
the axial vector S_mu) as the dynamical field on FRW. The trace vector time
component T_0(t) is parity-EVEN and naturally nonzero on FRW.

**Key result:** The trace mode can drive late-time acceleration (dark energy).
This is the scalar (spin-0+) sector, NOT the pseudoscalar (spin-0-) sector.

**Relevance to our analysis:** This paper uses a DIFFERENT torsion mode. The
trace vector T_0 is parity-even and does not face the symmetry obstruction
we identified for S_0. This is consistent with our File 01 analysis: parity-even
modes are naturally populated on FRW; parity-odd modes are not.

### Chen, Ho, Nester, Wang, Yo (2009) — "Cosmological dynamics with propagating Lorentz connection modes of spin zero"

**Established fact:** Extends the Shie/Nester/Yo analysis. Studies both
spin-0+ and spin-0- modes. For the spin-0- (pseudoscalar) mode, they note
that it couples to the AXIAL part of torsion.

**Key finding (relevant):** Their cosmological analysis focuses primarily on
the spin-0+ mode for dark energy. The spin-0- mode is mentioned but its
cosmological role is not extensively developed in the FRW context.

**Our interpretation:** The literature does not appear to have a detailed
analysis of whether the spin-0- mode is populated at a bounce. The focus
has been on late-time cosmology with the spin-0+ mode.

### Baekler, Hehl, Nester (2011) — "Poincaré gauge theory of gravity: Friedman cosmology with even and odd parity modes"

**Established fact:** This is one of the most complete analyses of PGT on
FRW backgrounds. They include both even and odd parity torsion modes.

**Key result:** On FRW, the odd-parity (pseudoscalar) sector enters through
the totally antisymmetric torsion S_mu. They find that S_0 CAN be nonzero
on FRW as a dynamical variable, but it requires specific initial conditions.

**Critical point:** Their analysis is consistent with ours — S_0 is ALLOWED
but not FORCED. The field equations admit S_0 = 0 as a solution. A nonzero
S_0 represents spontaneous parity violation.

**Our interpretation:** The Baekler/Hehl/Nester paper confirms our File 01
finding: the mode exists but is not required by FRW consistency. The question
of population mechanism is not addressed in their paper.

### Li, Sun, Xi (2009) — "Bouncing FRW cosmologies from Einstein–Cartan–Sciama–Kibble theory"

**Established fact:** Studies bouncing cosmology in Einstein-Cartan theory
(not full PGT). The bounce comes from the spin-density squared contact
interaction, which gives the rho^2 correction to the Friedmann equation.

**Key result:** The bounce mechanism does NOT require propagating torsion.
It works with purely algebraic (non-propagating) torsion determined by the
spin density constraint.

**Relevance:** This confirms our File 03 analysis: the bounce is driven by
the contact interaction (spin-density)^2, NOT by the propagating mode.
The propagating mode is a spectator.

### Blagojevic and Hehl (2013) — "Gauge Theories of Gravitation" (book)

**Established fact:** Comprehensive reference on PGT. Chapter on cosmology
discusses torsion on FRW backgrounds. The irreducible decomposition and
symmetry properties are standard.

**Key point:** The book confirms that the trace vector T_mu is the natural
dynamical variable on FRW (parity-even), while the axial vector S_mu involves
parity-odd configurations.

### Yo and Nester (various) — Ghost-free PGT parameter space

**Established fact:** The ghost-free conditions for Sector II (spin-0-) are
well-established: t_2 = -2t_1, t_3 < 0. The mass formula m_T = M_Pl/(2sqrt|t_3|)
is standard.

## 3. Literature gap

**The specific question "does the bounce populate the propagating pseudoscalar
torsion mode?" does not appear to have been addressed in the PGT literature.**

The PGT cosmology literature has focused on:
1. Late-time acceleration from the scalar (spin-0+) trace mode
2. Ghost-free parameter space classification
3. General Friedmann equations with torsion
4. Exact solutions with specific symmetries

The question of relic production from a bounce event is not a focus of the
existing literature, likely because:
- Most PGT cosmology papers focus on late-time (dark energy) applications
- The bounce literature (EC-based) uses non-propagating torsion
- The intersection (PGT bounce + propagating torsion relics) is narrow

**Our analysis fills this gap, and the answer is negative: the bounce does not
populate the pseudoscalar mode.**

## 4. Potential counterarguments from literature

### "But Baekler et al. have nonzero S_0 solutions!"

Yes, but these are solutions with S_0 != 0 as an INITIAL CONDITION, not
solutions where S_0 is dynamically generated. The question is not "can S_0
be nonzero?" but "must S_0 be nonzero?" The answer is no.

### "But the trace vector T_0 IS nonzero — why not S_0?"

Because T_0 is parity-EVEN. It naturally appears in the energy-momentum
tensor of the FRW background. S_0 is parity-ODD. A nonzero VEV would
spontaneously break a symmetry of the background. Symmetry-breaking requires
either an instability (tachyonic mass) or an external source (chiral matter).
Neither is present in the minimal Sector II model on FRW.

### "What about quantum effects at the bounce?"

Quantum particle production (Bogoliubov coefficients) creates PAIRS, not
a condensate. The k=0 homogeneous mode is not populated by pair production.
A condensate would require a phase transition or instability, which is
absent here.
