# Final Verdict: Branch R Novelty Audit

**Date:** 2026-03-17

---

## 1. Is Branch R novel enough?

**As a standalone ALP birefringence result: NO.**

Fujita, Murai, Nakatsuka & Tsujikawa (PRD 103, 043509, 2021) already showed that a Planck-scale spectator ALP fits the birefringence data using MCMC. Nakagawa, Nakai, Qiu & Yamada (2025) show c_γ ~ O(1) is natural in a subdominant axion ΛCDM model. Namikawa, Murai & Naokawa (PRD, 2025) provide far superior ALP mass constraints using the full Planck EB spectrum. Our data analysis (single Gaussian likelihood) is the simplest and least informative in the literature.

**As part of a comprehensive ECH closure assessment: YES.**

No paper in the literature systematically investigates 15 routes through a specific quantum gravity cosmology, identifies 13 structural barriers spanning 5 failure modes, and then presents the sole surviving testable prediction with MCMC constraints. The closure assessment itself is substantial original work. The ALP birefringence gains novelty from its context — it is not "another ALP fit" but "the last prediction standing."
