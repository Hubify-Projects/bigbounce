# Literature Search: ALP Cosmic Birefringence

**Date:** 2026-03-17
**Purpose:** Identify all prior work close to our Branch R spectator ALP birefringence result.

---

## Foundational Papers

### F1. Minami & Komatsu (2020) — The Detection
- **arXiv:** 2011.11254
- **Result:** First detection of isotropic cosmic birefringence β = 0.35 ± 0.14° from Planck 2018 PR3 data using the novel EB self-calibration method.
- **Model:** None (measurement only).
- **Relevance:** This is the observational result that launched the entire ALP-birefringence cottage industry. Our β_obs = 0.342 ± 0.094° (PR4+ACT) is an update of this.

### F2. Fujita, Murai, Nakatsuka & Tsujikawa (2021) — The ALP Interpretation
- **arXiv:** 2011.11894
- **Published:** PRD 103, 043509 (2021)
- **Model:** ALP with quadratic and cosine potentials, both as DE and as spectator.
- **f_a:** Considers f_a = M_Pl explicitly as reference case.
- **Mass:** Constrains mass ranges for different regimes (spectator, DE, EDE).
- **β ~ 0.3?** Yes — fits the Minami & Komatsu measurement.
- **MCMC?** Yes — uses MCMC to constrain ALP parameters from Planck data.
- **Free params:** g (coupling), m (mass), with f_a as derived.
- **Spectator?** Both spectator AND DE cases studied.
- **Key result:** For f = M_Pl, constrains coupling to 10^{-19} ≲ g ≲ 10^{-16} GeV^{-1}.
- **CRITICAL:** This paper already does much of what we do. It considers f_a = M_Pl, fits β, does MCMC, studies both spectator and DE regimes.

### F3. Choi, Lin, Visinelli & Yanagida (2021) — Electroweak Axion DE
- **arXiv:** 2106.12602
- **Published:** PRD 104, L101302 (2021)
- **Model:** Electroweak axion as quintessence DE.
- **f_a:** Not M_Pl; uses electroweak instanton mass generation.
- **β ~ 0.3?** Uses β = 0.35 ± 0.14° as motivation.
- **MCMC?** No.
- **Spectator?** No — axion IS dark energy.
- **Key result:** Predicts current w_DE from birefringence angle.

### F4. Lin & Yanagida (2023) — String-Inspired Electroweak Axion
- **arXiv:** 2208.06843
- **Published:** PRD 107, L021302 (2023)
- **Model:** Electroweak axion with f_A ≈ 10^{16} GeV (string-inspired).
- **β ~ 0.3?** Yes — "naturally explained" by this f_A.
- **MCMC?** No.
- **Spectator?** Not explicitly clear; appears to be DE-like.
- **Key result:** Shows birefringence is natural for GUT-scale f_a with O(1) anomaly coefficient.

## Key ALP-Birefringence Papers (2021–2026)

### P1. Nakagawa, Takahashi & Yamada (2021) — DM-Triggered Birefringence
- **arXiv:** 2103.08153
- **Published:** PRL 127, 181103 (2021)
- **Model:** ALP coupled to DM density, acquires effective mass after matter-radiation equality.
- **f_a:** GUT scale.
- **Mass:** m ≲ 10^{-28} eV.
- **Spectator?** The ALP is dynamical, not a simple spectator.
- **MCMC?** No.

### P2. Obata (2022) — Implications for Axion DM Search
- **arXiv:** 2108.02150
- **Model:** Two-axion alignment mechanism. One field = DM, one = DE (flat direction).
- **f_a:** Sub-Planckian for individual fields; effective super-Planckian via alignment.
- **β formula:** Uses β = g Δφ / 2, not the C α θ / (4π) form directly.
- **MCMC?** No.
- **Key result:** Connects birefringence constraints to axion DM parameter space.

### P3. Gasparotto & Obata (2022) — Monodromic Axion DE
- **arXiv:** 2203.09409
- **Published:** JCAP 08, 025 (2022)
- **Model:** String-theory motivated monodromy potential axion as DE.
- **f_a:** ≲ 10^{16} GeV.
- **β ~ 0.3?** Yes — explains the measurement.
- **MCMC?** No.
- **Key result:** Monodromy provides large field excursion at sub-Planckian f_a.

### P4. Eskilt, Herold, Komatsu, Murai, Namikawa & Naokawa (2023) — EDE + Birefringence
- **arXiv:** 2303.15369
- **Published:** PRL 131, 121001 (2023)
- **Model:** EDE axion with V ∝ [1 - cos(φ/f)]³, coupled to photons.
- **f_a:** Not M_Pl; EDE scale.
- **MCMC?** Yes — fits EB spectrum shape to Planck data.
- **Key result:** Constrains photon-axion coupling g = (0.04 ± 0.16) M_Pl^{-1}. First use of EB spectrum SHAPE (not just amplitude) to constrain ALP mass.
- **Mass exclusions:** Specific masses excluded at > 2σ.
- **NOT spectator:** ALP is EDE component.

### P5. Gasparotto & Sfakianakis (2023) — Axiverse Birefringence
- **arXiv:** 2306.16355
- **Published:** JCAP (2023)
- **Model:** Multiple axion fields drawn from probability distributions.
- **f_a:** Range 10^{15}–10^{17} GeV; finds f_a/c ≤ 10^{-2} M_Pl.
- **MCMC?** No — probabilistic/statistical analysis of axiverse PDFs.
- **Key result:** Generic upper bound on f_a from combined birefringence + abundance constraints.
- **Notably:** f_a = M_Pl is DISFAVORED by their analysis when combined with abundance constraints.

### P6. Murai (2024) — Oscillating ALP Birefringence
- **arXiv:** 2407.14162
- **Model:** Rapidly oscillating ALP during recombination with asymmetric potential.
- **Mass:** m ~ 10^{-26} eV (much heavier than H_0).
- **Spectator?** Yes — energy density negligible.
- **MCMC?** No.
- **β ~ 0.3?** Yes — demonstrates mechanism produces correct amplitude.

### P7. Nakagawa, Nakai, Qiu & Yamada (2025) — Evolving Axion in ΛCDM + DESI
- **arXiv:** 2503.18924
- **Model:** Subdominant axion (aΛCDM) — axion + cosmological constant.
- **f_a:** ~10^{17} GeV (sub-Planckian, string scale).
- **Mass:** 2H_0 ≲ m ≲ 7H_0.
- **β ~ 0.3?** Yes — matches with c_γ ~ O(1).
- **MCMC?** No — parameter space scan.
- **Spectator?** Subdominant but contributes to expansion (not pure spectator).
- **Key result:** Shows c_γ ~ O(1) is sufficient. Mass window 2–7 H_0. Explicitly fits DESI w_0-w_a contours.
- **CRITICAL:** Very close to our setup conceptually (natural c_γ, mass near H_0, β ~ 0.3°). But uses f_a ~ 10^{17} GeV, not M_Pl; and does NOT do MCMC.

### P8. Namikawa, Murai & Naokawa (2025) — Planck EB Spectrum Constraints
- **arXiv:** 2506.20824
- **Published:** PRD (2025)
- **Model:** General ALP with Planck EB spectrum.
- **MCMC?** Yes — multi-frequency likelihood on Planck data.
- **Key result:** Excludes specific ALP masses at > 2σ using EB spectrum shape. This is the state-of-the-art mass constraint.
- **Not spectator-specific:** General ALP framework.

### P9. Yin, Du, Li & Zhang (2026) — Joint EDE + Birefringence
- **arXiv:** 2601.13624
- **Model:** EDE with Chern-Simons coupling.
- **MCMC?** Yes — Cobaya, Planck + ACT + DESI + Pantheon+.
- **Key result:** H_0 = 76.9 km/s/Mpc, f_EDE = 0.23. Joint fit.
- **NOT spectator:** EDE.

## Torsion/EC-Specific Papers

### T1. Castillo-Felisola et al. (2015) — Axions in Gravity with Torsion
- **arXiv:** 1502.03694
- **Published:** PRD 91, 085017 (2015)
- **Model:** Kalb-Ramond and Barbero-Immirzi axions in EC gravity.
- **Key result:** These torsion-descended axions are equivalent in effective theory. Phenomenology determined entirely by Planck scale — very light, very weakly coupled.
- **Birefringence?** Not discussed.
- **CRITICAL:** Establishes the theoretical basis for "torsion → axion" but does NOT connect to cosmic birefringence.

### T2. Mavromatos et al. (2024) — Torsion-Induced Axions
- **arXiv:** 2404.18741
- **Model:** String-theory torsion-induced axions coupled to gravitational CS terms.
- **Birefringence?** Not discussed explicitly.
- **Focus:** Inflation and cosmological tensions, not birefringence.

### T3. Gao & Garcia de Andrade (2024/2025)
- **arXiv:** 2412.05982 and EPJ C (2025)
- **Model:** EC portal to dark-photon-axion-torsion mixing.
- **Birefringence?** Not discussed.
- **BI parameter:** Constrained to ~10^{-31} (very different from γ = 0.274).

## Key Gap Identified

**No paper in the literature does ALL of the following simultaneously:**
1. Fixes f_a = M_Pl AND C = 8 (SM) — making the model 2-parameter (θ_i, m)
2. Runs MCMC to obtain posteriors on (θ_i, m)
3. Explicitly compares the ALP model against a free-β baseline
4. Demonstrates that the ALP is statistically equivalent to free β
5. Frames the ALP result within a systematic closure of a broader theory program
6. Connects to ECH/torsion as UV motivation

**However:** The individual components are all well-established in the literature. The birefringence formula, the spectator ALP regime, f_a = M_Pl, and β ~ 0.3° have all been studied separately.
