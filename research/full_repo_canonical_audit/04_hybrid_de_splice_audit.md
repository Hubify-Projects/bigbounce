## Was It Concept-Only or Data-Tested?

**CONCEPT-ONLY.** All 236,622+ MCMC samples used fixed w = -1 (cosmological constant). No w₀, wₐ, or w(z) was ever varied as a free parameter. The loophole was explored theoretically across 7 disguised forms but never implemented computationally.

## How It Would Have Helped Paper 1

Adding w₀wₐ freedom would have:
- Improved χ² fits to Planck + BAO + SN data
- Potentially shifted H₀ slightly toward higher values
- Introduced effective dark-energy dynamics mimicking the hoped-for bounce→DE connection

But: the improvement would be IDENTICAL to adding w₀wₐ to ΛCDM — no additional theoretical content from the bounce.

## Why We Rejected It

From the formal rejection statement (02_rejected_loophole_statement.md):
"Adding w₀wₐ to a bounce model produces the same fit improvement as adding w₀wₐ to ΛCDM, with no additional theoretical content. We therefore restrict our claims to observables that the bounce physics genuinely controls."
