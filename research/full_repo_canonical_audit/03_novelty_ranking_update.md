# 03: Novelty Ranking Update

## Novelty Scale
- N0: Not novel (prior art, verification only)
- N1: Weakly novel (synthesis / review / compilation)
- N2: Moderately novel (new framing / new application / new quantitative result)
- N3: Strongly novel (new theorem / new method / new observable mapping)
- N4: Breakthrough (new physics / new prediction / paradigm shift)

## Results Assessment

## Honest Assessment

The program's novelty is primarily in **systematic framework construction and quantitative verification**, not in discovering new fundamental physics. The strongest original contribution is the ECH perturbation-transparency result (N3). The focused PNG paper's novelty is in the **quantitative discrimination analysis** (Bayesian comparison, GR-aware hardening, survey hierarchy) applied to a known prediction.
