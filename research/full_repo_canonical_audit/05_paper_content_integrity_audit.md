### Should the Hybrid-DE Loophole Be Mentioned?

**YES — one sentence in the Discussion (§8.3).** This is relevant because a referee might ask: "Why not just add dynamical DE to improve the bounce model?" The answer belongs as a brief caveat, not a full section.

**Recommended addition to §8.3 (Caveats):**

"We also note that appending late-time dynamical-dark-energy freedom (e.g., CPL parametrization) to a bounce model can improve cosmological fits at the parameter level, as explored in recent bounce + dark-energy literature. However, such phenomenological freedom does not derive from the bounce physics itself and does not constitute first-principles evidence for a contracting phase. Our analysis restricts attention to the parameter-free prediction $\fnl = -35/8$, which is controlled by the contraction dynamics."
