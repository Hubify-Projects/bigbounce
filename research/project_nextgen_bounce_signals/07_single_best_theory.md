## What exact observable makes it worth pursuing?

**The circular polarization fraction of the stochastic gravitational wave background:**

$$
\Delta_h(f) = \frac{P_+(f) - P_-(f)}{P_+(f) + P_-(f)}
$$

This is the GW analog of CMB polarization V-mode (circular). It measures the degree to which the GW background is chirally asymmetric.

Why this observable matters:
1. **It is parity-odd.** Standard GR with standard matter produces Δ_h = 0. Any nonzero detection requires parity violation in the gravitational sector.
2. **It cannot be faked by the contraction phase.** The dust contraction with a scalar field is parity-symmetric. Chirality must be generated at or near the bounce, where torsion is active and curvature is maximal.
3. **It is detectable in principle.** LISA can measure circular polarization through cross-correlation of different detector channels. Third-generation detectors (Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer) have proposed chirality search programs. PTA circular polarization searches are underway (Gair et al. 2015 formalism, NANOGrav sensitivity studies).
4. **It satisfies the non-transparency requirement.** The bounce actively generates the chirality — it is not transmitted passively from the contraction phase.

## Why is it still close enough to the bounce idea to matter?

The chirality is generated BY the bounce:

1. **Before the bounce (contraction):** ρ ≪ ρ_c, torsion negligible, parity coupling inactive or weak. Tensor modes evolve symmetrically (P_+ = P_-).

2. **At the bounce (ρ = ρ_c):** Curvature is Planckian. Torsion is maximal. The parity coupling is strongest. The σ̇ (time derivative of the pseudoscalar) is large because the ALP evolves rapidly near the curvature peak. The differential equation for h_+ and h_- differs maximally.

3. **After the bounce (expansion):** Torsion decays, parity coupling turns off. The chirality generated at the bounce is frozen into the tensor spectrum.

**The bounce is the chirality generator.** Without the bounce, there is no moment of maximal torsion/curvature to activate the parity coupling. This is the cleanest example of a bounce that is NOT transparent: it actively processes the tensor modes and leaves a distinctive mark.

This has no analog in inflation (no torsion) or in LQC (no torsion at the perturbation level — same as our finding in Branch Vb, but here it's the tensor sector that matters because the Nieh-Yan term involves T explicitly).

1. **λ_CS ~ 1/(γ M_Pl) and the chirality is confined to k ~ k_b ~ M_Pl.** Then Δ_h at LISA/PTA frequencies is exponentially suppressed. Dead.

2. **The ALP σ is frozen (σ̇ = 0) during the bounce.** Then λ_CS = 0 and no chirality is generated. Dead.
