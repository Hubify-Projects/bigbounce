## What Minimal ECH Taught Us

### Positive results (real)
1. **The ECH bounce is clean.** Modified Friedmann H² = (ρ/3M²)(1 − ρ/ρ_c) with ρ_c = 0.21 M_Pl⁴ from γ = 0.274. Regular, parameter-free (up to γ), singularity-free. Background-level physics works.
2. **The bounce is transparent.** Transfer function T(k) = 1 for super-Hubble modes. The bounce faithfully transmits whatever spectrum the contraction phase produces. This is a feature and a bug.
3. **Cosmic birefringence from the ALP is a surviving independent prediction.** Not bounce-dependent.

### What classes are closed

- **Minimal ECH dark energy:** All routes A–G closed. Seven independent barriers.
- **ECH perturbation novelty:** Torsion drops out for scalar fields at all perturbative orders. Classical Mukhanov-Sasaki equation. Identical to LQC at background level, strictly less informative at perturbation level.
- **Matter bounce scalar spectrum:** n_s = 1, known since 2002. f_NL = −35/8, known since 2009. No ECH-specific modification.
- **ALP curvaton tilt:** Birefringence ALP too light by 75 orders. Generic curvaton already done (Cai & Brandenberger 2011).
- **Transparent bounce observables:** Any bounce that is transparent to super-Hubble modes is observationally silent. The spectrum is set entirely by the contraction phase, where ECH corrections are negligible (ρ ≪ ρ_c).

## Design Requirements for a Successful Next-Generation Theory

Converting the no-go results into positive design requirements:

### Requirement 1: NON-TRANSPARENCY
The theory must include a mechanism that makes the bounce non-transparent: modes must evolve non-trivially during or near the bounce, so the output spectrum differs from the input spectrum in a measurable way.

### Requirement 2: SIGNAL-TRANSMISSION CHANNEL
The theory must have an actual physical channel through which bounce-scale physics gets imprinted on observables. This cannot be just "the background a(t) is modified" — it must be a perturbation-level or nonlinear-level effect.

### Requirement 3: OBSERVABLE DISTINCTION FROM CONTRACTION
The signal must not be degenerate with contraction-phase physics. If the same observable could be produced by modifying the contraction EOS (w ≈ 0.003 instead of w = 0), the bounce isn't contributing anything distinctive.

### Requirement 4: NOT ALREADY SATURATED IN THE LITERATURE
The prediction must not be a known result under a new label. The matter-bounce scalar spectrum, the standard curvaton mechanism, and the transparent-bounce transfer function are already fully explored.

### Requirement 5: MINIMAL NEW INGREDIENTS
The theory should add the minimum necessary new physics beyond the bounce background. Each new field or coupling is a theoretical debt that must be justified.

### Requirement 6: TESTABILITY
The prediction must target an observable that is either currently measured or will be measured within ~10 years. Theoretical novelty without observational relevance is not a flagship.
