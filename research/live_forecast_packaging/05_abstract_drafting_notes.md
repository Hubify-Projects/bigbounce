# 05: Abstract Drafting Notes

## Opening Sentence Options

A. "The matter bounce — a nonsingular cosmological scenario in which perturbations originate during a matter-dominated contracting phase — predicts a distinctive, parameter-free primordial non-Gaussianity with f_NL^local = -35/8."

B. "We present forecasts for testing the generic matter-bounce prediction f_NL^local = -35/8 with next-generation galaxy surveys."

C. "A matter-dominated contracting phase preceding a nonsingular bounce produces scale-invariant scalar perturbations with a specific non-Gaussian signature: f_NL^local = -35/8, negative and ~300 times larger than standard inflationary predictions."

**Preferred: C** — gives the physics, the number, and the contrast with inflation immediately.

## Strongest One-Paragraph Story

A matter-dominated contracting phase produces a parameter-free prediction for local-type non-Gaussianity: f_NL = -35/8 = -4.375 (Cai et al. 2009). This prediction is mechanism-independent — it holds regardless of whether the bounce is realized by loop quantum cosmology, Einstein-Cartan gravity, or other nonsingular completions. We present forecasts for testing this prediction with SPHEREx and MegaMapper. The SPHEREx multi-tracer galaxy bispectrum achieves σ(f_NL) ~ 0.7, giving ~6σ sensitivity. MegaMapper's scale-dependent bias gives σ(f_NL) ~ 0.5 under ideal conditions but is more sensitive to ultra-large-scale systematics. We identify the dominant forecast fragilities: relativistic projection effects, PNG bias (b_φ) uncertainty, and ultra-large-scale mode access. A robust detection of f_NL ≈ -4 would strongly favor a contracting/bounce origin over standard single-field inflation; a null result would disfavor the quasi-dust matter bounce at >4σ significance.

## Numbers That MUST Appear

- f_NL = -35/8 = -4.375
- SPHEREx σ(f_NL) = 0.7 (bispectrum), 0.5 (combined)
- SPHEREx significance: ~6σ
- MegaMapper σ(f_NL) = 0.5 (ideal)
- MegaMapper significance: 3-7σ (realistic range)
- Standard inflation: f_NL ≈ 0

## Caveats That MUST Appear

- "mechanism-independent" (not tied to any specific bounce theory)
- "forecasts" (not actual measurement)
- MegaMapper significance is "conditional on" ultra-large-scale systematics
- "strongly favor" (not "prove" or "uniquely demonstrate")

## Overclaims to AVOID

- "first proof of a pre-Big-Bang contracting phase"
- "rules out inflation"
- "we derived f_NL = -35/8" (we verified it; Cai derived it)
- "definitive test" (MegaMapper is conditional; SPHEREx is strong but not 5σ-guaranteed)
- Any suggestion that the bounce is the ONLY explanation for f_NL ≈ -4
