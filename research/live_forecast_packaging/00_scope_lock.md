# 00: Scope Lock

## Title Candidates

1. "Testing the Matter Bounce with Primordial Non-Gaussianity: Forecasts for SPHEREx and MegaMapper"
2. "f_NL = -35/8 as a Falsification Target for Bouncing Cosmology"
3. "Parameter-Free Non-Gaussianity from Matter Contraction: Observational Prospects"

**Preferred: Option 1** — clear, specific, survey-targeted.

## Central Claim

The generic matter-bounce scenario makes a specific, parameter-free prediction (f_NL^local = -35/8 = -4.375) that is testable at ~6σ significance by SPHEREx via the galaxy bispectrum, and at 3-7σ by MegaMapper via scale-dependent bias — providing a sharp observational test to distinguish bouncing cosmology from standard single-field inflation.

## IN Scope

- The matter-bounce f_NL = -35/8 prediction and its derivation context
- Shape function properties (local-type in squeezed limit, specific equilateral/folded ratios)
- SPHEREx bispectrum forecast (primary near-term test)
- MegaMapper SDB forecast (secondary longer-term test)
- Systematics: GR projection effects, b_φ uncertainty, k_min dependence
- Fisher robustness analysis
- Inflation comparison (why this is hard to mimic)
- Decision thresholds (confirm/weaken/kill)

## OUT of Scope

- Full ECH perturbation closure derivation (mention briefly as motivation for generic approach)
- Dark-energy no-go barriers (not relevant to the forecast)
- ALP birefringence (separate track, mention at most in passing)
- LQC-specific tensor calculations (r suppression is supporting context only)
- Detailed numerical bispectrum reproduction (algebraic verification suffices)
- MCMC results from the ΔN_eff program (different observable)

## Minimal ECH Context Needed

ONE paragraph in the introduction stating: "In minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity, the Holst term is dynamically inert for scalar perturbations (it reduces to a topological invariant when torsion vanishes for canonical scalar field matter). This perturbation-transparency result means the observable predictions of a matter bounce are independent of the specific bounce mechanism, motivating a mechanism-robust approach to observational testing."

That's it. No derivation, no 13-barrier map, no tensor-sector audit.

## Why Focused > Giant Synthesis

- SPHEREx launches soon (~2025-2026, data ~2028) — timeliness matters
- The PNG community reads forecast papers, not cosmology manifestos
- A focused paper is reviewable in 2 months; a grand synthesis takes 6+
- The ECH closure and barrier map can be a SEPARATE publication
- The forecast result stands on its own without the full theoretical backstory
