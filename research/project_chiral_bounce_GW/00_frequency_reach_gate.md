## What the proposed chiral signal is

The Einstein-Cartan-Holst bounce involves a parity-odd gravitational structure (the Holst term, proportional to 1/γ where γ = 0.274 is the Barbero-Immirzi parameter). When the Barbero-Immirzi parameter is promoted to a dynamical pseudoscalar field σ, its coupling to curvature (Chern-Simons: σRR̃) or torsion (Nieh-Yan: σNY) produces differential amplification of left- vs right-handed gravitational wave polarizations.

The bounce is the moment when:
- Curvature is maximal (~Planckian)
- Torsion is maximal (fermion density is highest)
- The parity-violating coupling is strongest

This generates a chirally asymmetric tensor spectrum: P_+(k) ≠ P_-(k), quantified by the circular polarization fraction Δ_h(k).

## Why frequency reach is the first kill test

The chiral signal is generated at the bounce, when the universe is at its smallest and densest. The characteristic wavenumber of the signal is set by the bounce scale k_b. After the bounce, the universe expands by an enormous factor before reaching us today. The question is whether this expansion redshifts k_b into a frequency band where detectors can observe it.

If f_0 (the signal frequency today) is in the GHz range or above, no current or planned detector can measure it. The program would be dead regardless of how strong the chirality is.

If f_0 falls in the PTA (nHz), LISA (mHz), or ground-based (Hz–kHz) band, the signal is in principle detectable.

**This is the single most important question. Everything else (coupling strength, spectral shape, stability) is irrelevant if the frequency is wrong.**

## Pass/fail criteria

**PASS:** There exists a physically reasonable parameter region (bounce energy scale, post-bounce expansion history) where f_0 lands in any of:
- PTA band: 1–100 nHz
- LISA band: 0.1–100 mHz
- Ground-based band: 1–10⁴ Hz

"Physically reasonable" means: no fine-tuning of unrelated parameters, no contrived post-bounce history, no appeal to speculative GHz detector concepts.

**MARGINAL:** f_0 can reach observable bands only with specific assumptions about the post-bounce expansion (e.g., an extended matter-dominated phase, a specific reheating temperature). The assumptions are not absurd but are additional model commitments.

**FAIL:** f_0 > 10⁶ Hz for all physically reasonable scenarios. Or f_0 reaches observable bands only with assumptions that contradict the rest of the framework (e.g., requiring ρ_bounce ≪ ρ_Planck, which removes the ECH motivation).
