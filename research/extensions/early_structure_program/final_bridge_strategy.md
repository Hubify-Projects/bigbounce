## 1. The ONLY Remaining Legitimate Bridge

The sole physically defensible connection from the spin-torsion bounce framework to early-structure observables is:

**Bounce → modified initial conditions for inflation → primordial power spectrum feature P(k) → enhanced small-scale density perturbations → either PBH formation or enhanced early halo formation**

This is a chain with ONE critical missing link: the perturbation spectrum through the spin-torsion bounce has not been calculated. Everything upstream (the bounce dynamics) and downstream (PBH formation, halo formation from enhanced P(k)) is either derived or standard astrophysics. The bridge parameter — the P(k) feature — is currently a free phenomenological proxy.

---

## 2. Why Direct Torsion-Density Effects Fail

The four-fermion contact interaction from Einstein-Cartan theory:

L_int = −(3πG/2) × [γ²/(γ²+1)] × J^μ_A J_{Aμ}

has strength proportional to Gρ. At astrophysical densities:

| Environment | ρ (g/cm³) | Gρ/c² | Torsion effect |
|-------------|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Planck density | 10^93 | O(1) | Active |
| Neutron star core | 10^15 | 10^{-78} | Negligible |
| Galaxy cluster | 10^{-27} | 10^{-120} | Zero |
| SMBH accretion disk | 10^{-5} | 10^{-98} | Zero |

**Conclusion:** Torsion effects vanish at every density relevant to SMBH formation, accretion, or halo dynamics. There is no direct torsion → early structure pathway. The suppression is not marginal — it is 78+ orders of magnitude.

## 3. Why Direct PBH Production from the Framework Currently Fails

The framework's bounce mechanism creates baby universes inside black holes, not primordial black holes in our universe. The distinction:

- **Baby universe (framework):** The interior of a collapsing region undergoes a quantum bounce at ρ_crit, creating a new expanding region disconnected from our Hubble volume. This is the cosmological bounce that replaces the Big Bang singularity.

- **PBH (needed for early structure):** An overdense region in the early universe collapses to form a black hole that remains in our Hubble volume as a compact object. This requires enhanced density perturbations at horizon re-entry during radiation domination.

The framework does not contain a mechanism for producing enhanced density perturbations. It modifies the Friedmann equation at Planck densities, which affects perturbation evolution DURING the bounce — but the resulting P(k) has not been calculated.

**No production mechanism → no PBH prediction → no early structure prediction.**

## 4. Why the P(k) Feature Bridge Is the Only Viable Route

The modified Friedmann equation H² = (8πG/3)ρ[1 − ρ/ρ_crit] is active during the bounce. Perturbation modes evolving through the bounce feel this modification. The bounce-to-inflation transition sets initial conditions for inflation that may differ from the standard Bunch-Davies vacuum.

This is established in the LQC literature:
- Agullo, Ashtekar, Nelson (2012-2013): P(k) features from LQC bounce
- Wilson-Ewing (2013): Scale-invariant P(k) from matter bounce
- Zhu, Cleaver, Ashtekar (2017): Pre-inflationary imprints on P(k)

What IS established:
- The bounce modifies perturbation evolution (generic to all bounce models)
- The modification imprints features at comoving scales set by the bounce-to-inflation transition
- The comoving scale of the feature depends on the number of pre-observable e-folds

What is NOT established:
- Whether the spin-torsion variant produces enhancement or suppression
- At what amplitude
- Whether the four-fermion interaction and parity-odd operator change the LQC results qualitatively

**The bridge is legitimate in principle but empty in practice.** The analysis below treats the P(k) feature as a phenomenological proxy to determine whether any observational window exists for the framework to target.
