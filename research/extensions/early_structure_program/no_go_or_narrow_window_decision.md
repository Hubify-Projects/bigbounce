# No-Go or Narrow Window Decision

**Date:** 2026-03-13
**Program:** Early Structure from Bounce Cosmology
**Decision type:** Final classification of the early-structure direction

---

## Classification: EFFECTIVE NO-GO (with a narrow phenomenological window irrelevant to the framework)

---

## 1. The Three Possible Outcomes and What We Found

### ~~NO-GO: no allowed region exists~~
Not quite. A phenomenological allowed region does exist.

### **NARROW WINDOW: only a very fine-tuned narrow region exists** ← THIS
A window of ~0.4 decades in P_R exists at SMBH-relevant scales (k ~ 10^{5.7} Mpc⁻¹). But this window:
- Is not at the framework-predicted scale (mismatch of 10⁹ in k)
- Requires P_R ~ 10^{-1.6} to 10^{-1.2} (only factor of 2.3 in amplitude)
- Would require abandoning the dark energy constraint (N_tot = 92 → ~70) to place the feature there

### ~~PLAUSIBLE WINDOW: a nontrivial allowed region exists~~
No. The window is narrow AND disconnected from the framework.

---

## 2. Why This Is an Effective No-Go for the Current Paper

The result has three levels of failure:

### Level 1: Scale mismatch
The framework predicts k_feature ≈ 6 × 10¹⁴ Mpc⁻¹. SMBH seeds need k ~ 10⁴–10⁶ Mpc⁻¹. The mismatch is 10⁹ in wavenumber, 10¹⁸ in mass. This cannot be resolved by:
- Theoretical uncertainties in N_pivot (changes k by ~10²)
- Feature broadening (would need Δ ~ 20 e-folds, unphysical)
- Modified bounce dynamics (changes A_bump, not k_*)

### Level 2: N_tot tension
Placing features at SMBH scales requires N_tot ≈ 70. The dark energy constraint requires N_tot = 92. Changing N_tot breaks the paper's central result (ρ_Λ ≈ (2.3 meV)⁴).

### Level 3: Window narrowness
Even in the phenomenological parameter space (ignoring the framework), the allowed window is only ~0.4 decades in P_R. This means any P(k)-based SMBH seed model requires fine-tuning at the factor-of-2 level. This is not catastrophic — many physical parameters are known to factor-of-2 — but it means the feature amplitude must be quite precisely tuned.

---

## 3. What This Means for the Current Paper

**Nothing from this analysis should enter the current paper as a result, a subsection, or an appendix.**

The current paper's claims are:
- Modified Friedmann equation → dark energy from inflationary dilution
- N_tot = 92 from ρ_Λ fit
- Cosmological parameters consistent with observations
- Parity-odd operator → cosmic birefringence (consistency check)

Including an SMBH/PBH phenomenology section would:
- Highlight that N_tot = 92 pushes bounce features to irrelevant scales
- Draw attention to a direction that doesn't work for the framework
- Require heavy caveating that would weaken the paper's narrative

### Acceptable inclusion: 1–2 sentences in Future Directions

A brief mention that the bounce-to-inflation transition may imprint P(k) features, with a note that their location depends on N_tot, is honest and appropriate. See Phase 5 draft text.

## 4. What This Means for a Follow-Up Paper

### As a standalone phenomenology paper (no framework connection):
"Observational Windows for Primordial Power Spectrum Features: SMBH Seeds vs PBH Constraints"
— This is publishable but has been done by others (Kohri, Yokoyama, many groups). Our contribution would be the window analysis methodology, which is incremental.

### As a framework-connected paper:
Not viable until:
1. The perturbation calculation through the spin-torsion bounce is completed
2. The resulting P(k) is found to have features at interesting scales
3. Given N_tot = 92, "interesting scales" means the asteroid-mass PBH window

### As a PBH dark matter paper:
The framework DOES predict features at k ~ 10¹⁵ Mpc⁻¹ (M ~ 10⁻¹⁶ M_☉), which is in the asteroid-mass window where PBH constraints are weakest. If the perturbation calculation shows P_R enhancement at this scale, there could be a PBH dark matter story. This is speculative but at least at the right scale.

---

## 5. Whether the Framework Currently Predicts This or Only Motivates It

**The framework currently predicts NOTHING about the P(k) feature.**

- It predicts the SCALE where a feature would appear (k ~ 10¹⁵, from N_tot = 92)
- It does NOT predict the AMPLITUDE (requires perturbation calculation)
- It does NOT predict the SHAPE (requires perturbation calculation)
- It does NOT predict WHETHER a feature exists at all (the Bunch-Davies vacuum might be recovered)

The entire early-structure direction is currently MOTIVATION, not PREDICTION.
