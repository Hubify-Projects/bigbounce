## Key Results

### 1. The (J^5)^2 interaction conserves B and L

The spin-torsion four-fermion interaction (J^5_mu)^2 is a
chirality-sensitive scattering operator, but it does NOT violate
baryon number or lepton number. It CANNOT generate a matter-antimatter
asymmetry on its own. Any baryogenesis mechanism must rely on
EXTERNAL B/L-violating physics (sphalerons, Majorana masses, GUT
operators), with torsion providing at most a perturbative correction.

### 2. Gravitational democracy at T ~ M_Pl

At the Planck temperature, ALL gravitational-strength interactions
have comparable rates: Gamma ~ G^2 T^5 ~ H. The torsion (J^5)^2
interaction is ONE of approximately g_* ~ 100 such channels. It
cannot dominate any relic abundance or asymmetry because it is
outnumbered by generic gravitational scattering channels of equal
strength.

### 3. No initial condition anchor

The most promising candidate (axial mu_5 baryogenesis) produces
eta_B ~ 10^{-9} for maximal initial chirality, tantalizingly close
to observation. But the initial chirality n_5 is a FREE PARAMETER
that the bounce does not set. The gravitational chiral anomaly
(which could set n_5) vanishes because R-tilde R = 0 on FRW
(established in Branch H). Without a mechanism to determine n_5,
the result is tunable storytelling.

### 4. Axion irrelevance is the cleanest kill

The bounce changes the axion misalignment angle by Delta_theta ~
10^{-96} radians. This is the most extreme null result in the
program -- 96 orders of magnitude below relevance. The axion does
not know the bounce happened.

### 5. PBHs from the bounce evaporate instantly

Planck-mass PBHs (ECH) evaporate in one Planck time. Even in PGT,
PBHs from the bounce scale evaporate before BBN for all m_T > 0.3 GeV.
Surviving PBHs require m_T ~ 0.3 GeV AND fine-tuned pre-bounce
perturbation amplitudes that the model does not predict.

## Is the Route Genuinely Better Than the DE Program?

**Initial hope:** The early universe has no scale-separation problem
(the bounce IS at high energies where relics form). This should be
a better match than bounce-to-DE.

**Actual result:** The scale-separation problem is replaced by the
GRAVITATIONAL DEMOCRACY problem. At T ~ M_Pl, torsion is not special
-- it is one gravitational interaction among many. The "advantage"
of operating at high energies is precisely the DISADVANTAGE: at
Planck temperatures, everything gravitational is in play, and torsion
cannot dominate.

**Assessment: The relic route is NOT more positive than the DE
program.** Both fail for structural reasons, but different ones:
- DE: scale separation (too far from the bounce)
- Relics: gravitational democracy (too close to the Planck scale)

The spin-torsion bounce is trapped: it is too high-energy for
late-time observables and too generic at its own energy scale for
early-time observables.

## Strongest Candidate Mechanism

Candidate A (axial chemical potential baryogenesis) is the strongest
by elimination:
- Produces the right ORDER OF MAGNITUDE (eta_B ~ 10^{-9})
- Uses the genuine (J^5)^2 interaction
- Has a transparent physical mechanism

But it fails on:
- Predictive yield (n_5 is free)
- Torsion dominance (~1% of gravitational effect)
- Already studied in literature (no novelty)

**Not strong enough for Phase 2.**
