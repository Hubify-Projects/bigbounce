How these figures tell the story. The figures are organized by paper, leading with the latest DESI spectral anomaly results (Paper 3), followed by the chirality catalog (Paper 4), the fNL prediction (Paper 2), and the framework investigation (Paper 1) that started it all.

Figures 28–30 cover the template mismatch analysis and corrected forecasts. Paper 4 — Galaxy Chirality Catalog 13 figures

Figures from Paper 4 ("Galaxy Chirality Catalog"). ViT-Small 3-class (CW/CCW/NOT_SPIRAL) classification of 8.47 million galaxies from Smith42 / DESI Legacy Survey DR8, producing the largest bias-audited galaxy handedness catalog ever (40× larger than Shamir). Key results: post-TTA all-sky dipole 0.43σ (null), raw hemisphere asymmetry 3.05σ collapses to <1σ under equivariant averaging, and dramatic demonstration that equivariant test-time augmentation eliminates survey-correlated systematics. Shamir's 3% chirality asymmetry claim refuted (our max regional asymmetry 0.47%).

Figure 34

Side-by-side Mollweide maps showing how equivariant averaging eliminates a 94.6σ systematic pattern down to null. The key figure demonstrating the method’s power: raw CNN outputs contain catastrophic survey-correlated bias that vanishes under flip-averaging.
