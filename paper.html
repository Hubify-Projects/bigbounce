How These Papers Fit Together

These two papers present two falsifiable predictions from bounce cosmology and the rigorous investigation that surfaced them. Start with the prediction (Paper 2), then read the investigation that got us there (Paper 1).

2.
The Decisive Test. Paper 2 presents the sharpest observable for testing bounce cosmology: the matter-bounce non-Gaussianity \(f_{\rm NL} = -35/8 = -4.375\), a parameter-free prediction 300× larger than standard inflation and opposite in sign. SPHEREx can detect this at 4–6\(\sigma\) via the galaxy bispectrum (~2028). Bayesian model comparison across 600,000+ Monte Carlo realizations shows a detection would favor the bounce over inflation at Bayes factor >300. ~12 pages.
1.
How We Got Here. Paper 1 is the full investigation: an Einstein-Cartan-Holst cosmological framework tested across 7 foundations and 17 branches, yielding 14 structural barriers and a perturbation-transparency theorem. Most routes from the bounce to dark energy are closed—but the predictions above survived because they don't depend on the bounce mechanism. 309,000+ MCMC samples verify the framework is observationally viable. Includes ALP birefringence analysis (\(\beta \approx 0.27°\), matching the 3.6\(\sigma\) combined Planck + ACT signal; LiteBIRD will test at 9\(\sigma\)). ~40 pages.
Read in any order. Paper 2 is a self-contained prediction. Paper 1 provides the theoretical depth, ALP birefringence analysis, and honest documentation of what was tried, what failed, and what survived.

The Papers

Paper 2 — The Decisive Test 100% Ready

Title: "Testing the Matter Bounce with Primordial Non-Gaussianity: Forecasts for SPHEREx and MegaMapper"

Scope: \(f_{\rm NL} = -35/8\) benchmark verification (Cai action audit). SPHEREx galaxy bispectrum forecast (4–6\(\sigma\)). MegaMapper scale-dependent bias (3–7\(\sigma\)). Bayesian model comparison (600,000+ MC realizations). GR projection marginalization. 5 publication figures. ~12 pages.

Key results: Bounce favored over standard inflation at BF >300 (robust to conservative GR treatment); favored over tuned multifield at BF >7. Template projection penalty = 0 (SDB probes squeezed limit exactly). SPHEREx bispectrum channel avoids \(k_{\rm min}\) fragility. Null result would disfavor quasi-dust bounce at >4\(\sigma\).

Paper 1 — The Framework & Reckoning 99% Ready

Title: "Spin-Torsion Cosmology and the Search for Geometric Dark Energy: Structural Barriers, Perturbation Transparency, and Surviving Predictions"

Scope: Full ECH framework: LQC bounce + parity-odd effective action + inflationary suppression mechanism + MCMC constraints (Planck+BAO+SN, 309,789 samples across 2 frozen datasets) + 14 structural barriers closing all bounce→DE routes + perturbation-transparency theorem (5-step proof, scalar + tensor) + hybrid-DE loophole rejection (7 forms) + ALP birefringence analysis (\(\beta = 0.27°\), MCMC inference with 9,720 samples, Bayes factor \(\ln B = 5.17\), LiteBIRD 9\(\sigma\) forecast) + galaxy spin phenomenology + falsification criteria. ~40 pages.

Key results: \(\Lambda_{\rm eff} = \Xi M_{\rm Pl}^2 + c_\omega \omega^2\); fine-tuning reduced from \(10^{120}\) to \(\sim 10^5\); \(\Delta N_{\rm eff}\) consistent with zero in all datasets; 14 barriers close all standard bounce→DE mechanism classes; ECH perturbation-transparency theorem (N3 novelty): Holst term topological for scalar field matter, \(\gamma\) invisible in all perturbation observables; ALP birefringence prediction \(\beta = 0.27°\) matches 3.6\(\sigma\) observed signal. Honest documentation of known limitations including assumed \(w = -1\) and missing photon-torsion coupling.
