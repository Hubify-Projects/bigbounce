How These Papers Fit Together

These three papers tell a single story: an ambitious theoretical framework is proposed, systematically stress-tested to destruction, and the surviving predictions are packaged into falsifiable observational tests. They are designed for simultaneous release and read best in order.

1.
The Framework & Reckoning. Paper 1 is the full story: it introduces the Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) cosmological framework—a quantum bounce from a rotating black hole interior, a parity-odd effective action, and MCMC verification against Planck, BAO, and supernova data (309,000+ posterior samples)—and then systematically stress-tests it to destruction. The paper catalogs 14 structural barriers that close every standard route from the bounce to dark energy, culminating in a perturbation-transparency theorem proving that minimal ECH leaves zero fingerprint on cosmological perturbations. The hybrid-DE loophole is explored across 7 disguised forms and rejected. It is an honest account: ambition, investigation, structural closure, and what survives. ~37 pages.

2.
The Independent Signal. Paper 2 extracts the one prediction already confronting data: a spectator axion-like particle with Planck-scale decay constant (\(f_a \sim M_{\rm Pl}\)) and Hubble-scale mass (\(m \sim H_0\)) naturally predicts cosmic birefringence at \(\beta \approx 0.27°\)—consistent with the 3.6\(\sigma\) combined Planck + ACT signal (\(\beta_{\rm obs} = 0.342 \pm 0.094°\)). MCMC inference (9,720 samples, 3 model configurations) confirms internal consistency. LiteBIRD will test this at 9\(\sigma\). This prediction is independent of bounce cosmology but naturally motivated by the ECH framework.

3.
The Decisive Test. Paper 3 presents the sharpest single observable for testing bounce cosmology: the matter-bounce non-Gaussianity \(f_{\rm NL} = -35/8 = -4.375\), a parameter-free prediction 300× larger than standard inflation and opposite in sign. Hardened forecasts show SPHEREx can detect this at 4–6\(\sigma\) via the galaxy bispectrum (~2028), with MegaMapper providing a more powerful but fragile follow-up at 3–7\(\sigma\). Bayesian model comparison across 600,000+ Monte Carlo realizations demonstrates that a detection would favor the bounce over inflation at Bayes factor >300, robust to conservative GR projection treatment.
The narrative arc: Ambitious framework → honest structural reckoning → survival of the best predictions → decisive near-term tests. Paper 1 documents what was learned (including what doesn't work); Papers 2 and 3 package what survives into falsifiable predictions that upcoming experiments will settle.

Paper 1 — Abstract

We investigate whether Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-torsion gravity can produce late-time dark energy or distinctive cosmological signatures. Within this framework, LQC holonomy corrections yield a nonsingular quantum bounce at \(\rho_{\rm crit} \approx 0.27\text{--}0.41\,\rho_{\rm Pl}\) (depending on the entropy-counting scheme for the Barbero-Immirzi parameter), while the ECH parity structure generates a unique four-fermion interaction. Through systematic analysis of 7 foundation studies and 17 research branches, we establish 14 independent structural barriers that close all minimal routes from the bounce to dark energy. Our central theoretical result is a perturbation-transparency theorem: for canonical scalar field matter, torsion vanishes at all perturbation orders, the Holst term reduces to a topological invariant, and the Barbero-Immirzi parameter \(\gamma\) is invisible in all perturbation observables. Independent MCMC verification (Cobaya v3.6.1, 309,789 samples across two frozen datasets) finds \(\Delta N_{\rm eff}\) consistent with zero and \(H_0 = 67.68 \pm 1.06\) km/s/Mpc, recovering standard \(\Lambda\)CDM values. The surviving science case for bouncing cosmology rests on generic, mechanism-independent predictions—principally the matter-bounce non-Gaussianity \(f_{\rm NL} = -35/8\), testable by SPHEREx at 4–6\(\sigma\).
