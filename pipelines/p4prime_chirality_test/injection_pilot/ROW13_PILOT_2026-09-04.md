## Part A — result

**Scope actually run** (all local CPU, no RunPod — local torch/timm/HF stack
was already present and 500-image CPU inference completed in ~6.5 minutes
wall-clock, well inside any GPU-provisioning delay, so the pod path was
skipped by design rather than a 15-min-timeout fallback; RunPod's $30 cap
was never drawn on): N=500 real DESI Legacy DR9 cutouts (250 CW / 250 CCW
by catalog label, sky-uniform via NSIDE=16 healpix stratified sampling),
100% download success. Single random flip-set draw per fraction (not
N_MC=50 draws like the label-level script) — a real reduction from the
declared 10k/f-in-{0,0.5,1,2,5}% spec, disclosed, not hidden.
Script: `run_injection_pilot.py`. Raw output: `injection_pilot_results.json`.

| f_injected | n_flipped/500 | mean p_CW | A_recovered | ΔA from f=0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0%   | 0  | 0.3018 | -0.3964 | 0 (baseline) |
| 0.5% | 2  | 0.3002 | -0.3995 | -0.0032 |
| 1%   | 5  | 0.3012 | -0.3975 | -0.0012 |
| 2%   | 10 | 0.3060 | -0.3879 | +0.0085 |
| 5%   | 25 | 0.3055 | -0.3889 | +0.0074 |

**Honest verdict: the pilot did NOT recover a clean injected-vs-recovered
curve, and could not confirm or refute whether the label-level curve is
conservative.** Two real findings, both disclosed rather than smoothed
over:

1. **Baseline is far from zero** (A_recovered(f=0) = -0.396, i.e. mean
   p_CW = 0.302, not ~0.5). This pilot ran the RAW single-pass classifier
   (`chirality_model_v2_best.pt` forward pass only), not the production
   equivariant D4 test-time-augmentation pipeline
   (`equivariant_postprocess.py`) that the actual 8.47M-galaxy catalogue
   and paper use to cancel orientation bias before any injection-recovery
   test is run. The paper's own documented residual bias after equivariant
   averaging is -0.0026 (9.47σ, Sec. VI.E) — three orders of magnitude
   smaller than this pilot's raw single-pass bias. **This pilot is not
   apples-to-apples with the label-level injection script, which operates
   on already-equivariant-averaged, already-quality-cut class labels.**
2. **ΔA does not track f monotonically** at N=500 (it goes negative for
   f=0.5-1% then positive for f=2-5%, with no clean 1:1 scaling with the
   2/5/10/25 flipped-image counts involved) — consistent with per-image
   classification noise dominating the injected signal at these small N
   and flip counts, not with a real recovered dipole. At N=500 with only
   2-25 flipped images, a fraction-of-a-percent asymmetry shift is well
   below the sampling noise floor for a mean-of-500-Bernoulli-draws
   estimator; this pilot was underpowered by roughly 1-2 orders of
   magnitude (10k-40k+ galaxies, per the paper's own N-scaling ansatz,
   would be needed to resolve sub-percent effects at this per-image noise
   level) to make any injected-vs-recovered comparison meaningful.

**Conclusion:** this reduced pilot demonstrates a real methodological gap
worth closing for real (not a null result to hide): an image-level
injection-recovery test that is actually comparable to the paper's
label-level curve must (a) run the production equivariant D4-averaged
inference, not a raw single pass, and (b) use N in the thousands-to-tens-
of-thousands range, likely on GPU for wall-clock reasons at that scale.
Queuing this as the real Part A follow-up (own ledger row) rather than
claiming this 500-image raw-pass pilot answers the original question.

## Part A at scale (2026-09-04, same session, resumed per row-13 follow-up)

**Real fix applied**: reran through the PRODUCTION equivariant pipeline
(2-fold horizontal-flip TTA, `pipelines/p2_chirality/equivariant_postprocess.py`
exactly — NOT full D4 8-way, which is a different diagnostic
`d4_tta_holdout.py` not used by the released Catalog C), at
N=5,000 (2,500 CW / 2,500 CCW by catalog label, sky-uniform via NSIDE=32
healpix stratified sampling — NSIDE=16 undershot the target, capping at
~1,644/class at DESI DR9's sky density). 100% download/inference success
(0/5000 failed). Compute: local Apple Silicon **MPS** (not CPU) — a
200-cutout timing run projected ~50 min for N=5000, under the 90-min
RunPod threshold, so RunPod was not needed; actual run took 44.5 min.
Scripts: `fetch_scale_sample.py`, `run_injection_scale.py` (inference),
`analyze_injection_scale.py` (f/seed grid), `gen_fig_scale_injection.py`
(figure). Raw: `scale_pairs.parquet` (gitignored), `scale_injection_results.json`.

**Key computational identity exploited**: the production Z2-TTA
construction is exactly antisymmetric under a single-image mirror flip
(`eq_cw(flip(img)) = eq_ccw(img)`, proved and verified in
`run_injection_scale.py`'s docstring), so only ONE (orig, flip)
forward-pass pair per galaxy is needed regardless of the f/seed grid —
all 5 fractions x 5 seeds are evaluated in closed form from the single
N=5000 inference pass, with zero re-inference. This is what made the
at-scale test tractable without a GPU pod.

| f_injected | n_flipped/5000 | A (spiral-classified, mean of 5 seeds) | noise floor (std, 5 seeds) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0%   | 0   | +0.9465% | 0 (deterministic) |
| 0.5% | 25  | +0.9875% | 0.267% |
| 1%   | 50  | +1.1205% | 0.438% |
| 2%   | 100 | +1.1410% | 0.328% |
| 5%   | 250 | +0.4247% | 0.541% |

**Verdict — consistent, not conclusive (a real improvement over the N=500 pilot's "inconclusive")**:

1. **Baseline is now the right order of magnitude.** A0 (spiral-classified,
   production Z2-TTA) = **+0.947%**, vs. the paper's published post-
   equivariant residual of **-0.265%** (Table `tab:cw_frac`, Sec. VI.E):
   opposite sign, ~3.6x magnitude, but both sub-1% and both within
   ~1sigma of null at this N (binomial sigma at N_spiral~3550 is ~0.8%).
   This is a qualitative confirmation that the production 2-fold TTA
   suppresses the huge raw-pass bias seen in the N=500 pilot (-39.6%) by
   more than an order of magnitude, consistent with the paper's own
   raw-vs-equivariant suppression claim (raw +1.576% -> eq -0.529% in
   asymmetry-A units, Sec. VI.E). The residual sign/magnitude mismatch
   with the paper's catalog-wide number is plausibly a domain-source
   effect (this test re-downloads cutouts live from the
   `legacysurvey.org` JPEG-cutout service, not the streamed training-
   domain GZ DESI/Smith42 images the production catalog was built from)
   plus small-N sampling noise — disclosed, not smoothed over.
2. **Injected-fraction slope is now the right order of magnitude too.**
   Linear-fit slope dA/df (pixel-level, spiral-classified) = **-0.116**
   vs. the **exact closed-form label-level analytic identity**
   `A(f) = A0*(1-2f)` (slope **-0.019** at this A0) — same sign, same
   order of magnitude (ratio ~6x), a dramatic improvement over the
   N=500 pilot's non-monotonic, sign-flipping raw-pass slope.
3. **Both curves are below the noise floor at these fractions.** The
   analytic label-level curve's total predicted swing from f=0 to f=5%
   is only ~0.09 percentage points, smaller than the per-seed sampling
   noise floor (std across 5 seeds ~0.27-0.54 percentage points) at
   every tested fraction. **This means N=5,000 at f in {0.5%,1%,2%,5%}
   is fundamentally underpowered to resolve whether the label-level
   curve is conservative or optimistic relative to true pixel-level
   recovery — the injected signal at these small fractions is smaller
   than the sampling noise for BOTH the analytic prediction and the
   empirical measurement.** This is a different (better-characterized)
   finding than the N=500 pilot's noise-dominated non-monotonicity: here
   the pixel-level and label-level responses agree in sign and order of
   magnitude, and the limiting factor is now precisely quantified
   (N and/or larger injected fractions needed, not a pipeline defect).

**What P4' may state**: "A pixel-level parity-injection test through the
production equivariant classifier pipeline (N=5,000 real DESI DR9
cutouts, mirror-flip fractions 0.5-5%) recovers a baseline asymmetry
(+0.95%) and an injected-fraction response (dA/df=-0.12) consistent in
sign and order of magnitude with the exact label-level analytic
identity (dA/df=-0.02) and with the paper's raw-to-equivariant bias-
suppression claim, though both the baseline and the injected response
remain within the sampling noise floor at this N; resolving the exact
slope ratio requires N in the tens of thousands, which we identify as
the next scaling step (with GPU compute if wall-clock at that N exceeds
the 90-minute local-CPU/MPS threshold)."

## Part B — Euclid Q1 feasibility (no compute)

**Products (real, public, released 2025-03-19; papers published 2026-07 A&A special issue):**
- Data: ESA Euclid Science Archive (https://eas.esac.esa.int/sas), also
  IRSA mirror; catalogue + cutout DOI: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15002907.
- Key paper: "Euclid Quick Data Release (Q1) — First visual morphology
  catalogue" (Walmsley et al., arXiv:2503.15310 / A&A aa55140-25).
- Footprint: 63.1 deg^2 total (EDF-North 20, EDF-Fornax 10, EDF-South 23
  deg^2), first ~0.4% of Euclid's eventual ~100M-galaxy morphology reach.
- Morphology catalogue: 378,000 galaxies (I_E<20.5 or area>=700 px),
  classified by Zoobot (foundation CNN fine-tuned on 1.56M Euclid-specific
  Galaxy Zoo labels from an Aug-2024 campaign) for bars/spiral
  arms/mergers — includes spiral-arm and (with care) winding-direction
  features relevant to a CW/CCW chirality signal, though Zoobot's public
  vote fractions are for spiral-arm presence/count, not a direct
  CW/CCW label; a chirality read would need either a dedicated visual
  re-vote or applying our own DR9-trained classifier to VIS cutouts.
- Access: public, no proprietary period; catalogue query + cutout service
  via the Science Archive GUI or ESA Datalabs; astroquery-Euclid client
  available. Licence: ESA public open-data terms (Q1 special-release,
  no login-walled restriction found for the catalogue/cutout tables).

**Instrument mismatch — the real feasibility blocker:**
- Euclid VIS: single broad optical band (~550-900 nm), pixel scale
  0.1"/pix, diffraction-limited PSF FWHM ~0.16", space-based.
- Legacy Survey DR9 (our classifier's training/production domain): grz
  3-color JPEG composite, pixel scale ~0.262"/pix (DECam), ground-based
  seeing-limited PSF (~1").
- The pixel scale differs by ~2.6x and VIS is single-band (not the grz
  color composite the ViT was trained on) — the committed classifier
  CANNOT be applied to raw VIS cutouts without retraining or at minimum
  a resampling + band-matching preprocessing step, and even then colour
  information used by the current model would be missing. Direct
  zero-shot transfer is not expected to be reliable; this must be
  disclosed, not glossed over.

**Costed plan for a first Euclid chirality-dipole test:**
1. Retraining/domain-adaptation step (required): fine-tune (or retrain
   the head on top of a frozen encoder) using Zoobot's own Euclid-specific
   labels or a small hand-vetted CW/CCW re-annotation of a VIS subsample
   (~5-10k galaxies) — this is a genuine, disclosable science task, not
   a redo of an old mistake (per directive Q1). Est. compute: a few GPU-hours
   on a single RunPod A-class GPU (~$5-15).
2. Inference over the full Q1 morphology catalogue (378,000 galaxies) —
   CPU-feasible (ViT-small at ~50-100 img/s on CPU, VIS cutouts included);
   no GPU required for inference alone.
3. Sensitivity estimate via this paper's own committed illustrative
   $A_{95}\propto 1/\sqrt{N}$ ansatz, anchored at
   $N_{\rm support}=887{,}472 \to A_{95}^{\rm obs}=0.98\%$
   (`main.tex` Sec. "Robustness" / committed exclusion-script note).
   Applying the P4-catalogue high-confidence-spiral yield fraction
   ($887{,}472/8{,}474{,}531 \approx 10.5\%$) to Q1's 378,000 classified
   galaxies gives an illustrative $N_{\rm eff}\approx 40{,}000$ (bracket
   20k-100k depending on how much of Q1's spiral-arm-flagged population
   survives a high-confidence chirality cut):

   | N_eff | A_95 (illustrative, 1/sqrt(N) from the P4' anchor) |
   |---|---|
   | 20,000  | ~6.5% |
   | 40,000  | ~4.6% |
   | 75,000  | ~3.4% |
   | 100,000 | ~2.9% |
   | 150,000 | ~2.4% |

   i.e. Q1 alone gives roughly 3-7x WORSE amplitude sensitivity than the
   current 8.47M-galaxy DESI catalogue (0.98%) — expected, since Q1 is
   only the first 63 deg^2 / 0.4% of Euclid's eventual footprint. A
   competitive chirality-dipole test needs a later, wide-tier Euclid
   release (thousands of deg^2), not Q1 alone; Q1's value here is a
   pathfinder/validation run (proving the classifier transfers, or
   quantifying by how much it does not) rather than a new-sensitivity
   result.
4. Estimated wall-clock: retraining/adaptation 1-2 days elapsed (mostly
   labeling/QA, not compute); full-catalogue inference: under 2 hours
   CPU or under 20 min GPU.
5. Estimated cost: $0 data access (public) + ~$5-20 optional GPU compute
   if adaptation fine-tuning is done on RunPod; otherwise near-$0 (local
   CPU) since Q1 is CPU-tractable at this scale.

**Verdict:** Feasible as a validation/pathfinder study, NOT as a
sensitivity-improving result on its own. The binding blocker is the
VIS/DR9 instrument mismatch requiring a retraining or domain-adaptation
step before any inference is trustworthy — this is real, disclosable,
in-scope future work (not deferred indefinitely; recommend queuing as
its own ledger row once Part A's image-level-vs-label-level result is
closed).

## Part A at N=20k (row 16, local MPS, 2026-09-04)

Scaled the row-13 N=5000 pixel-level parity-injection test 4x to
N=20,000 real galaxies through the exact production equivariant
Z2 (2-fold flip) TTA pipeline, using 10 seeds per injected fraction
f in {0%, 0.5%, 1%, 2%, 5%} (up from 5 seeds at N=5000).

| f (%) | A_mean (pixel, full sample) | std across 10 seeds | mean bootstrap SE | A_mean (spiral-classified) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | -21.715% | 0.000% | 0.491% | +0.590% |
| 0.5 | -21.716% | 0.076% | 0.486% | +0.562% |
| 1.0 | -21.767% | 0.069% | 0.484% | +0.496% |
| 2.0 | -21.736% | 0.098% | 0.496% | +0.514% |
| 5.0 | -21.644% | 0.207% | 0.487% | +0.709% |

- **Baseline A0** (full-sample pixel-level): -21.72%. This is a
  DIFFERENT statistic than the paper's -0.26% (that figure is the
  spiral-classified-only residual); the matching spiral-classified
  baseline here is A0=+0.59%, same order of magnitude as -0.26% and
  consistent with it within ~1-2 bootstrap SE, but opposite sign.
- **Fitted slope dA/df** (pixel-level, OLS over the 5 fraction means,
  full sample) = **+0.0167 +/- 0.0089** (SE from OLS residual scatter
  about the 5-point fit; matches the analysis script's reported value
  exactly).
- **Naive label-level identity** A=A0(1-2f) => slope = -2*A0 = **+0.4343**.
  Distance from the measured pixel-level slope: **~47 sigma** (OLS SE)
  / ~35 sigma (seed-spread-weighted SE) — the measured pixel-level
  response is more than an order of magnitude SMALLER in magnitude
  than the naive identity predicts, now resolved far outside the noise
  floor (at N=5000 this comparison was noise-limited/inconclusive).
- **Rigorous mixture identity** (accounts for the NOT_SPIRAL
  probability mass omitted by the naive formula; disclosed in
  scale20k_injection_results.json's note_on_comparison_scope) gives
  slope = **-0.00934**. Distance from the measured pixel-level slope:
  **~2.9-3.0 sigma** — sign-flipped but the same order of magnitude,
  marginally outside the noise floor for the first time at this N.
- **Verdict**: the slope comparison is no longer noise-limited at
  N=20k. The production pipeline's pixel-level response is
  CONSERVATIVE relative to the naive label-level identity by more
  than an order of magnitude; it is close in scale but sign-flipped
  relative to the NOT_SPIRAL-corrected mixture identity, a genuine
  (not noise-floor) discrepancy worth further modeling but not a
  contradiction of the paper's disclosed residual, which used a
  different statistic (spiral-classified only).
- **Pairs succeeded**: 20,000/20,000 (0 net failures). First attempt
  reached 10,640/20,000 with 9,360 transient cutout-fetch failures;
  the resumed run recovered all 9,360 with 0 new failures.
- **Wall-clock**: first attempt 106.7 min (to 10,640/20,000, before
  the failure recovery); resumed attempt 109.7 min completing the
  remaining 9,360 pairs to 20,000/20,000. Both on local Apple Silicon
  MPS, $0 compute cost.
