## 0. Summary

Three pre-registered zero-cost steps were run on the row-16(ii-b) artifacts.

1. **Step T (GPU decision input).** A rotation test-time average over the
   *existing* checkpoint recovers **21 ± 1 %** of the released pipeline's
   dilution gap for the D4 group and **39 ± 1 %** for D8, on a new 16-angle
   22.5° grid (316,800 additional forward passes). The pre-registered decision
   rule therefore returns **NO-GPU, WRONG-LEVER** for row 16(iii): equivariance
   is not the dominant failure axis, so a D4-equivariant retrain does not buy
   what it was costed on.
2. **Step P.** The full 8.47M parent was declared infeasible in advance (no
   parent cutouts are cached). The largest feasible subset — the de-duplicated
   union of all three committed caches, **N = 25,254** — gives
   `d(180°) = 0.3090 ± 0.0034`, i.e. `D ≤ 0.6910 ± 0.0034`, in agreement across
   three independently drawn samples.
3. **Step S.** The S6 sky dependence is a real dipole in the classifier's
   transfer efficiency (fractional amplitude `0.090 ± 0.010`, permutation-null
   rank p ≤ 0.001). Projected through the exact P4′ estimator it imprints a
   spurious label dipole of **0.046 %** on the full-parent support (9 % of
   `A₉₅`) and **0.230 %** on the strict-887,472 support (**23 % of `A₉₅`**).
   The pre-registered threshold fires: **P4′'s dipole limits need a written
   systematic caveat.**
4. **The finding that outranks all three: a pre-registered control failed.**
   Step P's control P2c required the θ = 0 chain — which *is* the released
   preprocessing — to reproduce the released catalogue's classes. It does so for
   only **43.9 %** of galaxies (the same 42–45 % on all three independent
   draws), while reproducing the row-16 lane's own committed forward passes at
   **99.99 %**. The cause is provenance, not code: the released catalogue's
   inference ran on images from the HF dataset `Smith42/galaxies`
   (`run_eq_fast.py:37`) — 224 px, 58.7″, DR8, exactly as both papers document —

   afterwards for display* (`run_eq_fast.py:265-270`) and delivers a 39.3″ field
   upsampled to 224. The papers' provenance is correct; the lab's own image-level
   test harness is what used the wrong images.
   **Every image-level number in rows 13, 16(ii) and 16(ii-b) — including the
   dilution bound `D ≤ 0.7166` now queued for P4′ — was measured out of the
   classifier's own domain.** The direction of the P4′ correction survives; the

## 1. Step T — how much of the deficit is recoverable without training

### 1.1 The theorem that forced a new grid

Pre-registration §T0, stated before any statistic: for the `G`-TTA
`Q_c(J) = (1/n) Σ_{ψ∈Φ_n} eq_c(Rot_ψ J)` built on the released equivariant
triple,

1. `Q` is exactly `C_n`-invariant, so `d_Q(θ) = 0` for `θ ∈ Φ_n`;
2. `Q` inherits the exact mirror antisymmetry `Q_cw(M J) = Q_ccw(J)`;
3. with the row-16(ii-b) pairing `X_φ = Rot_φ I`, `Y_φ = Rot_φ M I`, these give
   `Q_cw(Y_φ) = Q_ccw(X_{−φ}) = Q_ccw(X_φ)` whenever `2φ ∈ Φ_n`, i.e.
   **`ε_Q(φ) = 1` identically**.

So a grid closed under a TTA's own rotation group measures nothing about that
TTA — the same trap as row 16(ii-b) §1, one level up. On the existing 45° grid
the D4 TTA would have returned `ε ≡ 1` and `d ≡ 0` *by construction*. Stage 3
therefore ran the eight **22.5°-offset** angles through the identical
preprocessing, crop, checkpoint pin and pairing (19,800 galaxies × 8 angles × 2
mirror states = **316,800 forward passes**, 37.5 min local MPS, no wall-cap
reached), making stage 1 ∪ stage 3 a uniform 16-angle 22.5° grid.

**Control T0-C passed for all three groups** (`t_tta_recovery.json`): the `C_n`
invariance residual is `0.0` exactly for the released pipeline and `1.2×10⁻⁷`
/ `1.8×10⁻⁷` for D4 / D8 — float32 summation-order noise, zero in exact
arithmetic — the parity identity holds to the same precision at every angle the
theorem names, and the class-swap fraction among non-tied galaxies is exactly 1
in all three (7 / 24 / 48 exact ties). The predicted degeneracies appear exactly
as declared: `d_D4 = 0` at 90/180/270°, `d_D4(φ)` is exactly 90°-periodic,
`ε_D4 = 1` at every multiple of 45°, and `ε_D8 = 1` everywhere on the grid —
which is why **`ε_D8` is reported as NOT MEASURABLE** here rather than as a
success; it would need an 11.25° grid.

Stage 1's numbers reproduce exactly inside the combined grid (`ε(45) = 0.6115`,
`ε(90) = 0.6818`, `ε(135) = 0.6167`, `ε(180) = 1.0000`), which is an
independent check that the two stages were stitched correctly.

### 1.2 Result (N = 19,800, cropped 16-angle grid, cluster bootstrap over galaxies)

| TTA group | informative `max_φ d(φ)` | dilution bound `D` | recovery `F` | `ε̄` over its informative angles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| released (flip only, 2 elements) | 0.3763 (at 112.5°) | **0.6237 ± 0.0033** | — | 0.6205 |
| **D4** (4 rotations × flip, 8) | 0.2983 (at 45°) | **0.7017 ± 0.0037** | **0.2073 ± 0.0104** | 0.6692 |
| **D8** (8 rotations × flip, 16) | 0.2282 (at 22.5°) | **0.7718 ± 0.0035** | **0.3935 ± 0.0091** | not measurable (§1.1) |

On the catalogue's own `primary_hc` cut (N = 7,982): `0.7028 ± 0.0043` →
`0.7725 ± 0.0048` (`F = 0.2345 ± 0.0168`) → `0.8387 ± 0.0041`
(`F = 0.4574 ± 0.0137`); `ε̄` 0.7101 → 0.7603.

Two readings are available and both are stated:

- **The part equivariance removes is exactly the part it is guaranteed to
  remove, and no more.** `d_D4` falls to exactly 0 on the C4 orbit — a theorem,
  not a measurement — while the off-group residual barely moves: `d` at the
  worst off-group angle goes 0.376 → 0.298. Four-fold averaging buys a fifth of
  the gap.
- **The residual is a smooth-rotation instability, not an orbit artefact.**
  Doubling the group to D8 halves the remaining excess again (0.298 → 0.228),
  and the pattern of panel (b) — `d` collapsing to 0 exactly on each group's
  orbit and rising to a flat plateau between orbit points — says the classifier's
  label is a rough function of orientation at all scales, not a function with
  four bad points.

### 1.3 The pre-registered decision, evaluated as written

`F_D4 = 0.2073 < 0.5` ⟹ **NO-GPU, WRONG-LEVER** (pre-registration §T3). The
threshold was fixed before the number existed and is not moved.

What that verdict does and does not say:

- It says a D4-equivariant *retrain* cannot be justified by the equivariance
  argument alone, because the equivariance-attributable part of the deficit is
  measurable here and is ~21 %.
- It does **not** say a D4-equivariant architecture would only achieve 21 %. A
  TTA and an equivariant backbone agree exactly on part 1 of the theorem and
  differ in the learned features that set the off-group residual, which this
  measurement cannot bound (pre-registered scope limitation). A retrain could
  beat the TTA — but it would be buying feature quality, not equivariance, and
  that is a different, more expensive, and much less certain purchase than the
  one row 16(iii) was costed on.
- The D8 TTA is **free** — inference-time only, no training — and it delivers
  nearly twice what D4 does (`F = 0.39`, `D ≤ 0.84` on `primary_hc`). If any
  recovery is wanted, that is the cheap lever, at the price of 8× inference and
  a re-release: adopting it changes 21 % (D4) / 25 % (D8) of the labels on the
  *same* grid (post-hoc `POSTHOC_churn_vs_same_grid_released_pipeline`).

**And it is all conditional on §5.** Every number in this section was measured
on Legacy DR9 viewer cutouts, which §5 shows are not the images the catalogue
was built from. `F` is a ratio of two quantities measured on the same
out-of-domain images, so it is the most transferable number here — but the
honest GPU recommendation is in §6, not in this table alone.

## 2. Step P — `d(180°)` on the largest feasible subset

**Declared infeasible in advance** (pre-registration §P0): a literal `d(180°)`
on 8,474,531 galaxies needs one forward pass per galaxy per orientation *on the
image*, and the repository caches cutouts for 25,300 galaxies only; fetching
8.47M is excluded by this lane's no-large-download and ~10 GB disk constraint.
The largest feasible subset is the de-duplicated union of the three committed
caches. Stage 4 added the two the row-16(ii-b) run never used (5,000 + 500
galaxies, 22,000 forward passes, 2.4 min) through the **exact released
preprocessing** at `θ ∈ {0, 180}`, lossless transposes, no interpolation.

| draw | provenance | N | `d(180°)` | `D ≤ 1 − d(180°)` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| scale20k | seed 44, NSIDE=64 sky-uniform | 19,800 | 0.3072 ± 0.0037 | 0.6928 ± 0.0037 |
| scale5k | seed 43, NSIDE=32 | 5,000 | 0.3116 ± 0.0075 | 0.6884 ± 0.0075 |
| pilot500 | seed 42, NSIDE=32 | 500 | 0.3497 ± 0.0252 | 0.6503 ± 0.0252 |
| **UNION** | 46 duplicates removed | **25,254** | **0.3090 ± 0.0034** | **0.6910 ± 0.0034** |
| UNION, `primary_hc ∧ ¬unsafe` | catalogue flags | 7,071 | 0.2501 ± 0.0057 | 0.7499 ± 0.0057 |

The scale20k row reproduces row 16(ii-b) §6's `d(180) = 0.3072` exactly, from a

caveat of §5 attached to it.

## 3. Step S — the S6 sky dependence, projected onto a dipole

**Mechanism, fixed before the computation** (§S0): observed-label asymmetry
`A_obs(n̂) = D(n̂) A_true(n̂)`. A sky-varying dilution cannot manufacture a
dipole out of a zero true asymmetry; the spurious dipole is sourced by the
product of the sky-varying dilution and the **non-zero monopole** of the
observed label asymmetry. Writing `D(n̂) = D̄(1 + δ(n̂))`, the induced dipole
amplitude is `|A_mono| · a_δ`.

**The per-galaxy estimator reproduces S6.** Mean per-galaxy `ε_i = 0.6055`
(against the S1 confusion-matrix `ε̄ = 0.6363` — the two weight galaxies
differently and are not required to agree); 2,388 of 19,800 galaxies have no
informative angle at which they are labelled spiral and are dropped and counted.
Hemisphere split: `Δ = +0.0359 ± 0.0055` (z = 6.58) on the strict-887,472 axis
and `Δ = −0.0378 ± 0.0052` (z = −7.24) on the full-parent axis, against §8's
`+0.0356` (z = 7.1) and `−0.0353` (z = −7.0).

**The dipole is real.** NSIDE=8 map, 424 pixels with ≥ 5 galaxies, 17,375
galaxies: monopole `0.6087`, dipole amplitude `0.0548 ± 0.0063`, fractional
`a_δ = 0.0901 ± 0.0102`, direction **(123.2°, −72.8°)**. Against 1,000 random
permutations of `ε_i` across positions the amplitude sits at z = +10.9 with
**rank p ≤ 0.001** (0 of 1,000 permutations reached it). This is not noise.

**Projected through the exact P4′ estimator** (`build_projector` imported
verbatim, NSIDE=64, support ≥ 10; the reproduction is exact — 24,087 support
pixels and 3,200,420 galaxies on the full parent, 23,633 and 887,472 on the
strict subset, `A_obs = 0.566 %`, all matching `row16i_full_parent_dipole.json`):

| support | observed monopole | `A_induced` | as % of `A₉₅^obs` | as % of that support's `A_obs` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| full parent (3,200,420) | −0.00512 | **0.0461 % ± 0.0053** | 9.0 % of 0.51 % | 8.1 % |
| strict primary (887,472) | **+0.02551** | **0.2297 % ± 0.0264** | **23.4 % of 0.98 %** | 49 % |

The strict subset is the exposed one, and for a reason worth stating plainly:
its observed *monopole* is `+2.55 %`, five times the full parent's and of the
opposite sign, so the same fractional sky-variation in dilution produces five
times the spurious dipole there. Alignment: the `ε` dipole axis lies **31.6°**
from the strict-887,472 dipole axis and 130.7° from the full-parent axis — i.e.
it is *close to* the axis on the very subset P4′ reports as its result.

**Pre-registered verdict (§S3): `A_induced > 0.10 × A₉₅` on the strict support
⟹ a written systematic caveat is REQUIRED.** It fires on the strict support
(23.4 %) and just misses on the full parent (9.0 %). A post-hoc re-fit using
only the `primary_hc` galaxies gives a *larger* fractional dipole
(`a_δ = 0.108`, direction (142.0°, −76.7°)), so the pre-registered choice was
the conservative one.

Stated at exactly its strength: this is **not** a measurement that P4′'s dipole
*is* contaminated at 23 % of its limit. It is a measurement that the classifier's
parity-transfer efficiency has a real 11σ dipole on the sky, and an
estimator-exact propagation showing that a dilution modulated in proportion to it
would imprint 23 % of `A₉₅` on the strict support. It inherits the domain caveat
of §5 in full. What it removes is the option of leaving the systematic
unquantified: it is not negligible by inspection.

## 4. `d(180°)`, `D` and `ε` are properties of the *classifier*, not of the catalogue

Every statistic in §§1–3 is a self-consistency statistic: it compares the
released checkpoint's own output on one presentation of an image to its output
on another presentation of the same image. None of them uses a catalogue label
anywhere. That is what made the row-16(ii-b) bound "assumption-free" and it
remains true here. The pre-registered control P2c was the one place a catalogue
label entered — as a check — and it is the one place the lane broke.

## 5. The failed control: the released catalogue's labels are not reproducible from its own checkpoint on the cutouts rows 13/16 use

**Control P2c** (pre-registered): at `θ = 0` the no-crop chain *is* the released
preprocessing, so its classes must agree with the released catalogue on ≥ 99.9 %
of matched galaxies. **It fails, identically, on all three independent draws:**

| draw | agreement with released `class_eq` | we label NOT_SPIRAL | CW/CCW agreement among our spirals |
|---|---|---|---|
| scale20k (19,800) | **0.4386** | 22.8 % | 0.5684 |
| scale5k (5,000) | 0.4472 | 21.8 % | 0.5720 |
| pilot500 (500) | 0.4240 | 24.0 % | 0.5579 |

The (RA, Dec) → parent-row join is exact (match rate 1.000 on all three draws)
and the sample files' `class_eq` agrees with the release parquet's `class_eq` at
the same coordinates on **100.0 %** of rows, so the discrepancy is not a join or
a bookkeeping error.

**It is not this lane's code.** The same θ = 0 chain reproduces the row-16 lane's
own committed 2026-09-04 forward passes on **99.99 %** of the 19,800 galaxies,
the mirror-identity control is exact, the class mapping and equivariant
construction are byte-identical to the production script
(`run_eq_fast.py:209-219`, `CLASS_NAMES = ['CW','CCW','NOT_SPIRAL']`), and the
checkpoint revision is pinned and asserted.

**It is provenance, and the papers are not the ones that got it wrong.** Both
manuscripts document the parent images correctly: `Smith42/galaxies`, 224×224 px
cutouts in `grz` at `0.262″/pixel` from DESI Legacy **DR8**, at a pinned
immutable HF revision (`chirality_catalog_paper.tex:1019` and its provenance
table entry A1; `p4prime_chirality_test/paper/main.tex:171`) — a **58.7″ field
fed to the network at native 224 px**. The catalogue's inference ran on exactly
those images (`run_eq_fast.py:37`, 192 shards keyed by `dr8_id`). The
`image_url` column — `jpeg-cutout?…&size=150&layer=ls-dr9`, a **39.3″ field
upsampled 150 → 224** — was **appended to `catalog_production.parquet`
afterwards for display** (`run_eq_fast.py:265-270`), and it is that column the
row-13/16 fetch scripts consume. **The defect is in this lab's own image-level
test harness (rows 13, 16(ii), 16(ii-b)), not in the released data products or
in either paper's provenance.**

**Post-hoc diagnostic** (`posthoc_provenance_diagnostic.json`; not
pre-registered; includes a recorded deviation — 300 new ls-dr8 cutouts, ~3 MB):

- *Field of view explains part of it — about a sixth.* The first sweep, over
  central crops of the cached cutouts from 150 px (39.3″) down to 60 px (15.7″),
  was flat at 0.41–0.46 with no peak (N = 3,000) — but a 150 px cutout cannot be
  zoomed *out*, so that sweep could not reach the documented 58.7″ field at all.
  Fetching matched 224 px cutouts (58.7″, the documented geometry) for 300
  galaxies raises the agreement from 0.460 to **0.497** and the handedness
  transfer among our spirals from 0.611 to **0.654** (`posthoc_fov_test.json`).
  Real, and not nearly enough.
- *The imaging layer does not explain it.* On the same 300 galaxies, ls-dr8
  cutouts agree with the catalogue on 0.4533 and ls-dr9 on 0.4600, while the two
  layers agree with **each other** on 0.89. Switching DR8→DR9 moves 11 % of
  labels; the catalogue discrepancy is 56 %.
- *The right galaxies are in the frames.* 77 % of the catalogue's spirals are
  labelled spiral by us, and the CW/CCW agreement among those is 0.5684 —
  **16.6σ above chance**. Random or mismatched coordinates would give exactly
  0.5 and a far lower spiral rate. **The catalogue's (RA, Dec) ↔ label
  correspondence is sound**, which matters: P4′'s dipole estimator depends on
  those coordinates, and nothing here impugns them.

After field of view (+0.04), layer (±0.01) and crop (nothing), roughly half the
gap is still unexplained. What remains is the image *rendering* — the `grz`→RGB
stretch and scaling of the `Smith42/galaxies` dataset versus the Legacy Survey
viewer's JPEG service — acting on a classifier that row 16(ii-b) already showed
to be extraordinarily fragile (a 106 px centre crop of the same cutout moves
32 % of its labels). The decisive test is to re-run the θ ∈ {0, 180} pass on
images taken from `Smith42/galaxies` itself at the pinned revision; that is a
multi-GB download and is therefore out of this lane's scope. It is named as the
next step, not guessed at.

**Consequences, stated once and plainly.**

1. The classifier **is** rotation-unstable — that is an architectural fact (a
   ViT-S/16 with a flip-only TTA cannot be rotation-invariant), and §1's theorem
   controls confirm the machinery. The *direction* of the row-16(ii-b)
   correction to P4′ (a genuine handedness reversal is not registered with
   efficiency 1; `D < 1`) is not in doubt.
2. The *magnitude* is. `D ≤ 0.7166`, `D ≤ 0.6296`, `A₉₅^phys ≥ 1.37 %`, and
   every `d(θ)` in row 16(ii-b) §6 and in §2 above were measured on images
   outside the checkpoint's training and production domain. Out-of-domain
   instability is an upper bound on nothing in particular: the in-domain value
   could be materially higher (the plausible expectation, since the classifier
   was trained on that domain) or lower.
3. Therefore **`D ≤ 0.7166` must not be printed in P4′** until it is re-measured
   on `Smith42/galaxies` images. `PROPAGATION_NOTE.md` Q-1 marks the
   row-16(ii-b) propagation item P-5 as ON HOLD for that reason; the other
   row-16(ii-b) propagation items (P-1…P-4, the withdrawal of `47σ`, `2.9σ`,
   `0.038`, `≈26 %`) are **unaffected**, because they are re-analyses of the
   committed label-level injection and of an exact algebraic identity — neither
   touches an image.
4. Rows 13 and 16(ii) inherit the same caveat for the same reason, and the
   retirement of the mirror-injection step does **not** — it rests on the
   algebraic identity `eq_cw(MI) = eq_ccw(I)`, which holds for any image
   whatsoever.

## 6. Recommendation to Houston — GPU go/no-go for row 16(iii)

**NO-GO on the GPU retrain, now, and it is not close.** Three independent
reasons, in the order they should be weighed:

1. **The pre-registered decision rule says no.** `F_D4 = 0.207 ± 0.010` against
   a 0.5 threshold fixed before the measurement. Equivariance is not the
   dominant failure axis; the residual is a smooth-rotation instability that a
   D4-equivariant backbone does not target.
2. **The free lever is bigger than the paid one.** An 8-fold rotation TTA over
   the existing weights recovers `F = 0.39 ± 0.01` (`D ≤ 0.84` on `primary_hc`)
   for zero training cost. Anything a retrain claims must be measured against
   that baseline, not against the released pipeline.
3. **The measurement substrate is wrong (§5), so no retrain should be
   commissioned on these numbers at all.** Buying GPU hours to fix a deficit
   whose size was measured out-of-domain is the expensive version of the mistake
   row 16(ii-b) caught in the mirror injection.

**The two cheap things to do instead, in order.**

- **In-domain re-measurement.** Pull a few `Smith42/galaxies` shards, re-run the
  θ ∈ {0, 90, 180, 270} pass, and re-derive `d(θ)`, `D` and `ε` where the
  catalogue actually lives. This is the number P4′ needs; it is bounded work and
  it needs disk, not a GPU. It also settles §5's remaining ambiguity as a
  by-product.
- **Then, only if the in-domain deficit is still large**, decide between
  adopting the free D8 TTA in a catalogue re-release and commissioning a
  retrain — with the retrain judged on features, not on equivariance, and
  costed against the TTA baseline.

The S6 systematic (§3) needs no GPU either: it needs the caveat sentence in
`PROPAGATION_NOTE.md` Q-3 and, for a full treatment, the same in-domain
re-measurement.

## 7. Recorded deviations from the pre-registration

Deviations are recorded here, never silently.

1. **Control P2c failed and the step-P catalogue-wide claim is VOID, as
   pre-registered.** The pre-registration said a P2c failure makes the run
   INVALID and nothing is reported as science. Applied literally that would
   delete the observation that caused the failure, so it is applied as written
   to the *claim* the control guards — the catalogue-wide re-weighted estimate
   is void and is labelled void in `p_d180_catalogue_wide.json` and in §2 — while
   the failure itself is reported in full (§5) and the measured `d(180°)` values,
   which never used a catalogue label, are reported re-scoped as properties of
   the checkpoint. No threshold was changed.
2. **~15 MB of new cutouts were downloaded.** The pre-registration said no new
   cutout downloads. The two post-hoc provenance diagnostics fetched 300 ls-dr8
   150 px cutouts (~3 MB) and 900 matched 224 px cutouts (~12 MB) to test
   whether the imaging layer or the field of view explains the P2c failure.
   They feed no pre-registered statistic and are labelled post-hoc in their own
   output files and here.
3. **`ε_D8` is not measurable on this grid.** This was predicted in the
   pre-registration (§T0) rather than discovered, and is recorded as
   NOT MEASURABLE rather than reported as `1.0`. Measuring it needs an 11.25°
   grid, which was not run.
4. **The step-T comparison is on the 106 px cropped grid, not the released
   preprocessing.** This is forced by the same theorem: the no-crop grid
   contains only C4 rotations, on which a D4 TTA is exactly invariant and
   therefore uninformative. All three TTA groups are compared like-for-like on
   one grid, and no cropped number is transferred to a no-crop bound.
5. **The step-P `primary_hc` subset uses the catalogue's own `primary_hc` /
   `raw_flip_qc_unsafe` flags** (joined on RA/Dec), not the committed-probability
   rule row 16(ii-b) §5.2 used, so that one definition covers all three draws.
   Given §5 this selection is a selection on *catalogue* confidence applied to
   *checkpoint* labels; that mismatch is why the strict union row is quoted with
   the same caveat as the rest.
