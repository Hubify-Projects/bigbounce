\section{Introduction}
[To be drafted.] Spiral galaxy chirality is, to leading order in a
parity-conserving universe, an equal mixture of clockwise (CW) and
counterclockwise (CCW) projections on the sky once mirror-augmentation
biases in classifier training are removed. \textit{Paper IV} establishes
the global mixture in the post-test-time-augmentation equivariant
classifier as CW fraction $0.4974 \pm 0.000279$, consistent with parity
at $\sim$1$\sigma$. That null is a \emph{global} statement: it does not
constrain whether handedness is independent of environment.

The present paper is a focused, environment-conditional null test. We
cross-match the canonical chirality catalog with DESI Data Release 1 to
ask whether handedness correlates with redshift, projected density, or
cosmic-web context after controlling for the dominant selection and
imaging-systematic effects. The test is bounce-model agnostic: a positive
detection of environmental dependence would be a novel observational
constraint, and a clean null tightens the limits of correlated handedness
on cosmologically relevant scales.

\section{Relation to Paper IV}
\textit{Paper IV} \cite{golden_chirality_2026} produces a flat
catalog of 8,474,531 galaxies classified into \{CW, CCW, NS\} by an
equivariant ViT-Small classifier with test-time augmentation. The
canonical labels live in the column \texttt{class\_eq} of
\artifact{pipelines/p2\_chirality/outputs/canonical\_provenance/global\_cw\_fraction.json},
mirrored to HuggingFace
(\href{https://huggingface.co/datasets/bamfai/galaxy-chirality-catalog}{bamfai/galaxy-chirality-catalog}). The catalog inherits sky positions from
DESI Legacy DR8 Tractor and is not redshift-resolved beyond Tractor
photo-$z$; this paper supplies spectroscopic redshifts and environment
context by joining to DESI Data Release 1.

\section{Data}
\subsection{Chirality catalog (Paper IV)}
We use the immutable HF revision \texttt{paper4-v1.0.90}
(\artifact{pipelines/p2\_chirality/outputs/canonical\_provenance/global\_cw\_fraction.json}),
filtered to a configurable confidence floor (default: no cut). Imaging-leg
provenance is retained for systematics: BASS+MzLS, DECaLS, DES.

\subsection{DESI Data Release 1}
We use the canonical \texttt{zall-pix-iron.fits} HEALPix-coadded redshift
catalog from DESI Data Release 1, restricted to
\texttt{ZWARN == 0}, \texttt{SPECTYPE} $\in$ \{GALAXY, QSO\}, and
$0 \le z \le 4$.

\subsection{Cross-match method}
We compute nearest-neighbour angular separations on the celestial sphere
using \textsc{astropy} \texttt{SkyCoord.match\_to\_catalog\_sky}. The
primary acceptance radius is 1.0$''$ (DESI fiber positioning tolerance);
sensitivity is reported at \{0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0\}$''$. Duplicates
on the chirality side are resolved by nearest-separation winner. The full
crossmatch driver is
\artifact{pipelines/p5\_desi\_chirality/scripts/03\_crossmatch.py}.

\section{Statistical methods}
For each binned analysis we report observed CW fraction, exact binomial
95\% credible interval, and the signed $\sigma$ deviation from 0.5. For
hypothesis tests we run two complementary nulls: (i) a label-shuffle
permutation that preserves sky/redshift but destroys any handedness
signal; (ii) a position-shuffle that preserves labels but scrambles
positions. Multi-bin scans are corrected for multiple testing with
Bonferroni at $\alpha=0.01$.

Reported significances should be read as upper bounds on environmental
dependence; we use the phrasing ``no evidence for'' rather than ``proof
of independence''.

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item The cross-match is selection-limited: the 8.47M chirality catalog is
flux-limited at $r \le 17.8$ in the Galaxy Zoo regime; DESI spectroscopic
targets extend much fainter. The matched sample is therefore not a
random subsample of either catalogue.
\item Projected density is a coarse proxy for 3D density and cannot
distinguish chance line-of-sight associations from true overdensities
without spectroscopic confirmation across the neighbourhood.
\item Environmental classification (void/filament/cluster) is pending an
external VAC; until that ingest lands, the cosmic-web analysis is
contractual.
\item Imaging-leg systematics in chirality classification are tracked in
\textit{Paper IV} (per-leg dipole $\sigma < 2$); we propagate the
per-leg split here but do not re-derive the underlying bias.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Rubin/LSST extension}
Rubin/LSST DP1 is early commissioning data and not a complete LSST galaxy
survey release; we treat Rubin as a future validation/scaling dataset
once full LSST releases mature. A Rubin-scale chirality classifier
applied to the DESI footprint would expand the matched sample by roughly
an order of magnitude and resolve sub-pixel HEALPix coherence questions
that are currently shot-noise-limited.
