## Two Major Discoveries from the Deep Dive

### Discovery 1: f_NL = -35/8 regulates PBH production (Choudhury+ 2025)

Choudhury, Dey, Karde, Panda, Sami (2025, EPJC 85:472, arXiv:2409.18983) explicitly studied f_NL = -35/8 as a PBH production parameter and found:

**f_NL = -35/8 (matter bounce value) PREVENTS PBH overproduction while still allowing sizeable PBH abundance (10^{-3} < f_PBH < 1).**

This is a natural regulator: inflationary models with positive f_NL tend to overproduce PBHs (violating constraints), requiring fine-tuning. The matter bounce's NEGATIVE f_NL naturally prevents this. The same number that SPHEREx will measure via galaxy bispectrum also regulates PBH abundance — a cross-channel consistency test.

### Discovery 2: NANOGrav data is consistent with matter bounce (Papanikolaou 2025)

Papanikolaou (April 2025, arXiv:2504.11641) showed that the induced GW spectrum from the matter bounce has universal f^2 infrared scaling, giving spectral index gamma = 3. NANOGrav 15-year data measures gamma = 3.2 ± 0.6. **The matter bounce prediction is within 1 sigma of current PTA data.**

The matter bounce f_NL = -35/8 is not just a number to measure. It simultaneously:
