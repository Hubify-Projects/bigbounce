# VISION.md — the lab's guiding question and how it is protected (2026-09-02)

## The question
Was the Big Bang the beginning? The lab's guiding scientific bet is that a
nonsingular bounce is a more defensible origin than inflation from a
singularity, and its job is to make that bet **testable with data that exists
now** and falsifiable by data that is coming.

Houston's stated motivation (verbatim in `prompt-history.md`, 2026-09-02): a
conviction that the universe is beginningless and endless. That motivation is
recorded openly so it can never operate as a hidden thumb on the scale. The
rule that keeps it honest: **every claim is stated at exactly the strength its
evidence supports; a null or negative result is published as such; the
evidence decides, the motivation only chooses what to test.**

## The three routes to the question (all on-vision)
1. **Bounce vs inflation signatures** — the exact matter-contraction f_NL and
   its transmission through a bounce; multi-channel consistency (PTA γ, PBH,
   SPHEREx-era bispectrum). *Flagship line (Track A).*
2. **The black-hole-cosmology test** — Einstein–Cartan torsion is the bounce
   mechanism in Popławski's black-hole-universe (spin–spin contact repulsion
   — the same contact term P1A/P1C derive); its observable claim (a preferred
   galaxy-spin axis, Shamir/JWST) is what the 8.47M-galaxy chirality catalog
   tests. The ECH Note explains what minimal ECH does for the bounce and
   cannot do for dark energy. *(Tracks B and C1 are on-vision as a test with
   a null outcome so far.)*
3. **The early-universe anomaly map** — anomalies that strain single-field
   inflation (over-massive z>10 galaxies, PNG in clustering, isolated early
   SMBHs, large-scale asymmetries/voids), measured in public data (DESI DR1,
   JWST public catalogs) and connected to explicit bounce-vs-inflation
   discriminators. *(Track C2, redirected.)*

## What derailed it once (2026-03 → 07) and the standing protections
Facts: the lineup grew by split and rescue; review convergence became the
product; the ranked "what to do next" science of March 2026 was not pursued.
Protections, now directive **R** in `CLAUDE.md`:
- `project-context/NEXT_SCIENCE_LEDGER.md` is the ranked open-science list.
  Every session begins by reading it and ends by updating it. A session that
  runs review rounds while the top ledger item is untouched must record why.
- Review convergence is a **gate**, never a deliverable: no more than two
  consecutive review rounds on a paper without a science or scope decision
  in between; rounds stop when the remaining findings are genre/length.
- Every narrowing, split, merge, or retirement is a recorded decision with
  the original claim beside the new claim (`PAPER_LINEAGE`).
- A drift audit (this document vs. the live lineup) is re-run at least every
  30 days or at any lineup change, and its result is committed.
- Hubify inherits these as platform rules (idea → research → formalization
  → publication must carry vision, next-science ledger, and convergence
  controls as first-class objects), see `HUBIFY_RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE_2026-09-02.md`.

## Publication policy
Publish continuously, never all at once, never for its own sake: each work
goes out when it is a genuine contribution in its correct framing. Near-term
milestone: the first one to three genuinely valuable papers out soon.
