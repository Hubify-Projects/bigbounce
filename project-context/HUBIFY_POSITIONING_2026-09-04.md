# Hubify positioning — Houston, 2026-09-04 (durable; source: prompt-history 2026-09-04)

**Core line:** *Anyone with a question can now become a scientist.*
**Tagline candidate:** *What if your next question could lead to a groundbreaking discovery?*

**What it means (Houston's words, condensed):** a reproducible, highly capable AI research
agent you start with a single question. Months later, with the human guiding a bit here and
there — approving spend on compute and GPUs, pushing back, supplying creative ideas and
the next questions — that one question has become "how can we best try to answer it?",
branching into many lanes of research. Hubify is the platform where an uncredentialed human
with curiosity, inspiration and the desire to learn and contribute can ask scientific
questions and contribute to research, discovery, development and progress.

**Proof case:** BigBounce — one question (was the Big Bang the beginning?) → a mission and
ranked science ledger → 15 ledger rows worked, nulls published as nulls, two papers at
readiness 95, a data release, a reproducibility manifest for every experiment (98 + 3 programs),
and a lab site that shows all of it.

**Site consequence (bigbounce):** the homepage and /research carry a short "started from one
question" band that links to the ledger and the timeline; VISION.md's guiding question stays
the first sentence. Hubify's own site carries the positioning; BigBounce is its exhibit.
