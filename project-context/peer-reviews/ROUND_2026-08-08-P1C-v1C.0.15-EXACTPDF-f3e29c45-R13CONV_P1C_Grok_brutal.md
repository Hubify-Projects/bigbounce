**Referee Report (CQG standards — adversarial first-read evaluation)**

**P1C-E1** (Abstract, p. 1; also Sec. IV, pp. 6–8)  
The abstract states “roughly sixty orders of magnitude (conservatively ≥58)” suppression for Route 2 and “61–67 orders” for Route 3. These numbers are not recomputed or derived in the present manuscript; they are imported from the companion paper and from an external one-loop result (Benedetti–Speziale) whose fixed-point structure is explicitly stated to be absent in the Route-2 flow. The abstract therefore presents a quantitative claim whose supporting calculation is not contained in the submitted document.  
**Required fix:** Either move the full one-loop integration and the chiral-count estimate into the present manuscript (making it self-contained) or remove the specific numerical suppression factors from the abstract.

**P1C-E2** (Abstract, p. 1; Sec. V, pp. 12–15)  
The abstract asserts that the six-member operator list “spans the rule-admitted operator space” and is “a generating set.” The only verification offered is an external Python script whose output is not reproduced in the paper. No explicit 6×6 rank calculation or null-space dimension appears in the text.  
**Required fix:** Insert the explicit linear-algebra verification (or the full 4×4 Dirac-matrix construction) inside the manuscript.

**P1C-E3** (Sec. I, p. 2; multiple later references)  
The manuscript repeatedly states that results “are not restated” from the companion and that the division of content is “as follows.” A standalone reader cannot reconstruct the torsion-elimination step, the mean-field NJL check, or the zero-spin transparency theorem without opening the companion. This violates the journal requirement that a submission be independently intelligible.  
**Required fix:** All load-bearing intermediate results must be reproduced or the paper must be merged with its companion.

**P1C-M1** (Table II, p. 13; Sec. III, pp. 4–5)  
Five of the fourteen catalog entries (B5, B6, B7, B10, B13) are explicitly labeled “general naturalness or classification arguments” rather than ECH-specific derivations. Their inclusion inflates the headline count of “14 distinct mechanism-class constraints” while the actual sharp, first-principles no-go theorems remain only two (B1 and B14). The abstract does not qualify this distinction.  
**Required fix:** Either remove the general-naturalness entries from the headline count or rewrite the abstract and Table I caption to state that only two entries are rigorous ECH-specific theorems.

**P1C-M2** (Fig. 1 caption, p. 5; Sec. III, p. 4)  
The diagram and caption claim that B8 and B14 are “logically independent.” The text later concedes that both ultimately rest on the same observable channel (primordial GW chirality / tensor parity violation). The logical-independence claim is therefore at best an ansatz-level distinction, not a demonstrated separation of physical failure modes.  
**Required fix:** Downgrade the independence statement or supply an explicit argument that the two constraints cannot be simultaneously evaded by any single extension inside the minimal ECH field content.

**P1C-M3** (Sec. IV A, pp. 6–8; Eq. (3))

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The manuscript is a lengthy catalog whose two genuinely rigorous, self-contained results (the algebraic torsion-elimination identity and the zero-spin transparency theorem) are already contained in the companion paper. The remaining twelve entries are either imported calculations, general naturalness arguments, or heuristic closure conditions. These defects are structural and cannot be repaired by minor revision.
