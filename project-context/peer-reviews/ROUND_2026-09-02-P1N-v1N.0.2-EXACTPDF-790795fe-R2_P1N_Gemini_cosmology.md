Here is a comprehensive referee report for the manuscript "What Minimal Einstein–Cartan Torsion Does for the Bounce and Cannot Do for Dark Energy," submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.

### Referee Report

**General Comments:**
The manuscript presents a highly detailed, rigorous, and structurally sound analysis of the minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) action, specifically evaluating whether the spin-spin contact term responsible for the Popławski bounce can simultaneously source late-time dark energy. The author systematically closes four distinct dark-energy channels using a 14-barrier catalog, proves a perturbation-transparency theorem for the zero-spin branch, and provides a complete operator-basis adjudication for dimension-four parity-odd densities. The algebraic derivations are generally excellent, and the statistical/observational nuance (e.g., explicitly noting that WMAP+Planck and ACT DR6 birefringence significances are not directly comparable) is commendable. 

However, the manuscript contains a critical notation collision that introduces a dimensional error in a main operator equation, relies on an unstated quantitative scaling relation for one of its primary numerical bounds, and includes several internal version-control artifacts that must be removed prior to publication. 

Below is an itemized list of findings that must be addressed.

### ESSENTIAL (Must Fix for Acceptance)

**P1N-E1**
*   **Location:** Section VI, Page 7, Equation (13)
*   **Specific Problem:** There is a notation collision regarding the Planck mass that leads to a missing factor of $8\pi$ in the evaluation of $O_4^{[4]}$. In Section II (Eq. 1), the author explicitly defines $\kappa = 8\pi G = 8\pi/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$, which defines $M_{\text{Pl}}$ as the *non-reduced* Planck mass ($M_{\text{Pl}}^2 = 8\pi/\kappa$). However, in Eq. (13), the evaluation yields:
    $O_4^{[4]} = -24 M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \alpha \beta (J^5 \cdot J^5)$. 
    Substituting $\alpha = \kappa\gamma^2/[2(1+\gamma^2)]$ and $\beta = \kappa\gamma/[4(1+\gamma^2)]$ gives $\alpha\beta = \kappa^2\gamma^3/[8(1+\gamma^2)^2]$. 
    Thus, $O_4^{[4]} = -3 M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \kappa^2 \frac{\gamma^3}{(1+\gamma^2)^2} (J^5 \cdot J^5)$. 
    To arrive at the text's stated result of $-\frac{3\kappa\gamma^3}{(1+\gamma^2)^2} (J^5 \cdot J^5)$, one must assume $M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \kappa = 1$, which corresponds to the *reduced* Planck mass. Using the paper's own definition ($M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \kappa = 8\pi$), the correct coefficient is $-24\pi\kappa$. This missing factor of $8\pi$ also propagates to the ratio $O_4^{[4]}/O_5^{[4]}$ stated below Eq. (14), which should be $8\pi\gamma/(1+\gamma^2) \approx 5.6$, meaning the trace-vector cross term is actually larger than the axial term, not smaller.
*   **Required Fix:** Either redefine $M_{\text{Pl}}$ as the reduced Planck mass globally (and update the numerical benchmark in Sec II.A accordingly), or correct the coefficient in Eq. (13) to include the $8\pi$ factor and update the subsequent ratio discussion.

### ADDITIONAL FINDINGS

**P1N-M2**
*   **Location:** Section VI, Page 7, discussion of $O_5^{[4]}$ and parity.
*   **Specific Problem:** The manuscript contains a parity-classification contradiction regarding the operator $O_5^{[4]} = \epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} T^I_{\mu\nu} e_{I\rho} J_{5\sigma}$. The text introduces the set $\{O_1^{[4]} \dots O_6^{[4]}\}$ as a "spanning list of dimension-four parity-odd local densities" (Page 1 and Page 6). On Page 7, the author notes that the on-shell reduction of $O_5^{[4]}$ yields $(J^5 \cdot J^5)$, which is parity-even, and explains this by stating: *"This density is parity-even off shell... the parity-odd label belongs to the pre-reduction $\epsilon$-contracted density, not to its on-shell value."* 
    However, the pre-reduction density $O_5^{[4]}$ is *also* parity-even off-shell. Under a parity transformation (spatial inversion in both spacetime and the local Lorentz frame), the components transform as follows: the torsion tensor $T^I_{\mu\nu}$ is a polar tensor (P-even), the tetrad $e_{I\rho}$ is a polar vector (P-even), and the axial current $J_{5\sigma}$ is an axial vector (P-odd). The Levi-Civita symbol $\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ acts as a pseudoscalar density (P-odd). The product of these four objects (Odd $\times$ Even $\times$ Even $\times$ Odd) yields a parity-even scalar density. 
    Because $O_5^{[4]}$ is parity-even both off-shell and on-shell, it does not belong in a spanning list of strictly "parity-odd" densities. Its inclusion does not break the algebraic rank-four proof (Eq. 11), but the text's explicit claim that the pre-reduction operator is parity-odd is mathematically incorrect.
*   **Required Fix:** Correct the text on Page 7 to acknowledge that $O_5^{[4]}$ is parity-even off-shell as well, or clarify the specific discrete symmetry definitions being used if a non-standard parity transformation is intended. If $O_5^{[4]}$ is indeed parity-even, adjust the description of the $\{O_1^{[4]} \dots O_6^{[4]}\}$ list to reflect that it contains a mix of parity-odd and parity-even terms generated by the $\epsilon$-contraction rule, rather than being a strictly parity-odd list.
