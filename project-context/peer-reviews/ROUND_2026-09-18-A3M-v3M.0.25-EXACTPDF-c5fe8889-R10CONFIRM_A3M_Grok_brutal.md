**REFEREE REPORT — PRD Regular Article (A3M)**

**Paper:** Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) and a joint \((r,f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces (Houston Golden, 20 pp., dated 2026-09-18)

I have examined every page, equation, table, figure, caption, and reference in the rendered PDF. The manuscript fails the PRD acceptance bar on multiple independent grounds. Below are all findings.

**ESSENTIAL (paper cannot be accepted without these fixes)**

A3M-E1 (Title + p. 1 abstract block): The title announces a “joint \((r,f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces” without qualification. The body (p. 1, final paragraph; Sec. VIII) repeatedly restricts the claim to “constant sound speed” and the “full \(P(X)\) k-essence class at constant sound speed.” The title is therefore stronger than the calibrated result. Required fix: rewrite title and every abstract sentence to match the body’s final scope.

A3M-E2 (p. 1, abstract block): The sentence “the contribution is the exact amplitude, the \(\lambda\)-general no-go, and the surviving curvaton route” is not a summary of what the paper proves; it is an internal ledger statement. No abstract claim may be phrased as an enumerated “contribution list.” Remove.

A3M-E3 (Sec. II, pp. 2–3; Table I): The factor-of-two discrepancy with Cai et al. (2009) is “resolved” by a post-hoc normalization step that the authors themselves label an “amplitude-conversion step.” The paper never demonstrates that this step is required by any symmetry or matching condition; it is introduced solely to make the in-in result agree with Li et al. (2016). This is an ad-hoc fix, not a derivation. The claim of an “independent, from-scratch” confirmation is therefore unsupported.

A3M-E4 (Sec. IV D, p. 7; Table IV): The refit \(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) is presented beside the official NANOGRAV posterior \(\gamma_{\rm HD}=3.2^{+0.6}_{-0.6}\). The text never states at every juxtaposition that the two numbers are “not directly comparable” because one is a re-analysis under a different prior and the other is the collaboration’s published result. This violates the explicit instruction on sigma-value juxtaposition.

A3M-E5 (Sec. V B, pp. 9–10; Fig. 2; Table V): The ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.84\pm0.03\) is advertised as the channel’s “most reproducible output.” The same paragraph immediately states that the ratio is shape-dependent, varies from 1.61 to 1.91 across the grid, and “must not be quoted as universal.” The headline number is therefore presented more strongly than the body’s own calibration permits.

A3M-E6 (entire Sec. III and Appendix A): The transmission factor \(T_{f_{\rm NL}}\) is computed under two mutually exclusive schemes (S1 geometric vs. S2 raw-ADM). The paper never supplies a first-principles argument that selects one scheme over the other for the physical bounce; it simply adopts S1 for the main results. All transmitted-amplitude forecasts (Table III, Table VI) are therefore scheme-dependent at the factor-of-two level and cannot be treated as robust predictions.

A3M-E7 (p. 20, reproducibility statement): The statement lists 18 GitHub paths, frozen-release DOIs that do not yet exist, and commit hashes dated after the paper’s own submission date. This is internal bookkeeping text that has no place in a journal submission. Remove every URL, hash, and “reproducibility statement” paragraph.

**MAJOR (significant revision required)**

A3M-M1 (length): 20 pages for a regular PRD article whose central claim is a single number plus three null results is excessive. Recommended maximum: 12 pages.

A3M-M2 (Sec. VIII, Eq. 14–17): The joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) exclusion is derived only after imposing constant \(c_s\) and the specific window \(k\eta_B\lesssim10^{-2}\). The paper never quantifies how much of the single-field parameter space survives once these restrictions are relaxed. The “no-go” language is therefore overstated.

A3M-M3 (Sec. IV C, p. 7): The injection-recovery test for the PTA refit uses only five realizations and reports pulls \(\sim0.03\pm0.05\). No power analysis or coverage check is shown; the test is too weak to support the claim that the pipeline is “unbiased.”

A3M-M4 (Fig. 1 caption and Sec. IV D): The plotted “predicted” spectrum uses \(\gamma=5.07\) (dust) while the text simultaneously states that the model’s own committed spectrum is \(\gamma=5.00\) (dust bracket). The figure and text are internally inconsistent at the level of the quoted slope.

A3M-M5 (Sec. V C): The FIRAS exclusion argument for SMBH seeds is presented as a “null” for the model, yet the model’s own spectrum shape is never folded through the FIRAS window function. The claim is therefore uncomputed.

**MINOR**

A3M-m1 (p. 1): “Dated: September 18, 2026” is a future date; remove.

A3M-m2 (multiple equations): Several instances of “exactly” (e.g., “exactly one half,” “exactly reproduces”) are followed by numerical values that differ at the third digit. Replace with “agrees to \(\sim0.1\%\).”

A3M-m3 (Table II): The LQC vs. Quinton-type columns are labeled “scheme S1” but the caption never states that S2 values are omitted because they are divergent. Add the missing qualifier.

**NIT**

A3M-n1: Inconsistent use of “matter bounce” vs. “matter-contraction” in the same paragraph (p. 1).

A3M-n2: Several figure axes lack units (e.g., Fig. 2 y-axis is pure number but labeled only “\(f_{\rm PBH}\)”).

A3M-n3: Reference list contains one arXiv ID (arXiv:1905.05697) whose journal citation is missing the volume number.

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The manuscript’s central technical claim—an exact, scheme-independent \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) that jointly excludes single-field matter bounces—is not supported by the calculations once all internal qualifications, scheme ambiguities, and uncomputed robustness checks are restored. The paper is also 60 % longer than necessary, contains extensive internal-audit prose, and presents headline numbers more strongly than the body’s own calibrations permit. These are not cosmetic defects; they are structural failures that would require a complete rewrite and a new, shorter submission.
