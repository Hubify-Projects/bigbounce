Here is the referee report for the manuscript submitted to ApJS.

### General Comments
This paper presents an 8.47-million-galaxy chirality catalog derived from DESI Legacy DR8 imaging and uses it to test the rotating-black-hole-universe prediction of a preferred galaxy-spin axis. The statistical methodology is generally rigorous, the sample size is unprecedented for this specific question, and the distinction between detection-power floors and Neyman confidence limits is handled with care. The mathematical execution of the null tests and the derivation of the exclusion limits are correct and well-reasoned.

However, the manuscript suffers from a critical structural flaw: it is written as a summary of an external, unreviewed Zenodo deposit rather than as a self-contained catalog paper suitable for the Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series (ApJS). Core components required for a catalog release—architecture, calibration, and systematics—are outsourced to non-peer-reviewed companion PDFs. Furthermore, the manuscript contains residual review-log prose and version-control terminology that must be cleaned up prior to publication. 

### Specific Findings

**ESSENTIAL: Standalone-Reader Test and Venue Scope**
*   **ID:** P4P-E1
*   **Section:** 2.1 (Page 2, lines 123-126) & Section 4 (Page 6, lines 418-421) & Section 4.1 (Page 7, lines 447-449)
*   **Specific problem:** The manuscript explicitly outsources the core components of a catalog paper to unreviewed external documents. Section 2.1 states: *"Full architecture, per-region calibration, and the complete systematics appendix are described in the archived release paper (15) and are not reproduced here..."* Section 4 states: *"author-constructed VoidFinder and the Tempel and ASTRA web classifications are retained as further secondary diagnostics in the archived companion study and are not repeated here."* Reference 16 is cited as a PDF hosted on a personal/project website (`hubify.app`) with no DOI.
*   **Required fix:** ApJS catalog papers must be self-contained. You cannot publish a catalog release in a peer-reviewed journal while leaving the actual architecture, calibration, and systematics in an unreviewed Zenodo upload or a personal website. The manuscript must be expanded to integrate the core contents of References 15 and 16 directly into this paper (likely as appendices or expanded methodology sections). At 11 pages, the current manuscript is unusually short for an ApJS catalog paper; it has ample room to include this necessary material.

**ESSENTIAL: Version-History and Review-Log Prose**

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The statistical analysis, sample size, and theoretical confrontation are excellent and mathematically sound. However, the manuscript currently acts as a summary document that outsources the core requirements of an ApJS catalog paper (architecture, calibration, systematics, and secondary diagnostics) to unreviewed Zenodo deposits and personal websites. The paper must be substantially expanded to integrate these critical components directly into the peer-reviewed text, and the residual review-log prose and version-control terminology must be removed.
