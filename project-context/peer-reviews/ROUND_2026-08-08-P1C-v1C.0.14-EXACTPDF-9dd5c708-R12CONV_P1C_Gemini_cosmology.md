This is a referee report for the manuscript "A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology" submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity*. 

The manuscript presents a comprehensive, 14-entry barrier catalog evaluating whether the minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) framework can source late-time dark energy. The author systematically closes four enumerated routes using a combination of amplitude budgets, naturalness arguments, and an explicit operator-list spanning argument. 

The paper is exceptionally rigorous. The author demonstrates a firm command of effective field theory (EFT) power counting in gravity, carefully distinguishing between on-shell phenomenological ansätze and genuine off-shell dimension-4 operators. The explicit evidentiary classification (Tier I, II, III) is a breath of fresh air that prevents the overstatement of bounds, and the strict separation of parity-odd Lagrangian terms from parity-violating background effects (Appendix B) is handled correctly. The mathematical derivations in the appendices (Fierz identities, Cartan torsion elimination) are flawless and render the paper satisfyingly self-contained. Furthermore, the statistical rigor is commendable; the author correctly flags that the WMAP+Planck and ACT DR6 birefringence significances are derived from distinct null procedures and are not directly comparable as statistical weights.

I have only a few minor and cosmetic findings to address before publication.
