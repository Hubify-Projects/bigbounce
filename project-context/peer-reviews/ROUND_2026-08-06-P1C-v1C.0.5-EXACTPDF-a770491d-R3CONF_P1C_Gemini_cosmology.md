This is a referee report for the manuscript "A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology" submitted to Classical and Quantum Gravity. 

The manuscript presents a comprehensive, structured catalog of 13 mechanism-class constraints on minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source of late-time dark energy. The paper is well-organized, the scope is clearly bounded to minimal ECH, and the distinction between rigorous theorems, structural arguments, and ansatz-level estimates is handled with commendable transparency. The operator-basis completeness argument is a particularly strong addition to the literature. 

However, a detailed audit of the manuscript reveals two critical mathematical/definitional errors—a direct algebraic contradiction in the definition of the Planck mass, and a typo in the Fierz matrix that breaks its claimed involution property—along with several clarity issues in the order-of-magnitude arithmetic and tables. These must be corrected before the paper can be accepted.

Below is the detailed itemization of findings.

### ESSENTIAL (Must Fix for Acceptance)

**ID: P1C-E1**
*   **Location:** Section I (Page 1) and Section II (Page 2)
*   **Problem:** There is a direct algebraic contradiction in the definition of the Planck mass. On Page 1, the text defines "$\kappa = 8\pi G = M_{\text{Pl}}^{-2}$". This definition implies that $M_{\text{Pl}}$ is the *reduced* Planck mass ($M_P = 1/\sqrt{8\pi G} \approx 2.44 \times 10^{18}$ GeV). However, on Page 2, the text explicitly states: "the order-of-magnitude numerics in this survey use the full Planck mass $M_{\text{Pl}} \simeq 1.22 \times 10^{19}$ GeV; the reduced-versus-full distinction ($2.44 \times 10^{18}$ vs $1.22 \times 10^{19}$ GeV) is immaterial at the $\ge 58$-order margins quoted." 
If $M_{\text{Pl}}$ is the full Planck mass ($G^{-1/2}$), then $M_{\text{Pl}}^{-2} = G$. Substituting this into the first definition yields $8\pi G = G$, which is mathematically false. While the factor of $8\pi$ is indeed immaterial to the 58-order-of-magnitude conclusion, a theoretical physics paper cannot contain a contradictory definition of fundamental constants.
*   **Required Fix:** Choose one convention and stick to it. Either define $\kappa = 8\pi G \equiv M_P^{-2}$ and use the reduced Planck mass $M_P$ throughout, or define $\kappa = 8\pi G = 8\pi M_{\text{Pl}}^{-2}$ if you insist on using the full Planck mass $M_{\text{Pl}}$. 

**ID: P1C-E2**
*   **Location:** Appendix C (Page 16)
*   **Problem:** The printed Fierz matrix $F_c$ in Eq. (C1) contains a typo that breaks the claimed identity $F_c^2 = 1$. The element $(F_c)_{13}$ (first row, third column) is printed as $1$. If we compute the dot product of Row 1 with Column 1 using the printed matrix, we get: $\frac{1}{16}[1(1) + 1(4) + 1(12) + (-1)(-4) + 1(1)] = 22/16 \neq 1$. To satisfy $F_c^2 = 1$ and to match standard Fierz rearrangement coefficients for the $(S, V, T, A, P)$ basis, the element $(F_c)_{13}$ must be $1/2$. (With $1/2$, the dot product is $\frac{1}{16}[1 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 1] = 16/16 = 1$). 
*   **Required Fix:** Correct the matrix element $(F_c)_{13}$ in Eq. (C1) from $1$ to $1/2$.

### MAJOR (Significant Revisions Required)

**ID: P1C-M1**
*   **Location:** Section IV A (Page 6)
*   **Problem:** Inconsistent numerical substitution in the Route-2 amplitude budget. The text explicitly defines "$\alpha_{\text{em}}/(4\pi) \approx 5 \times 10^{-4}$". However, in the immediate next sentence, the explicit arithmetic substitutes $10^{-3}$ for this value: "the dimensionless ratio is $\Delta\theta_{\text{one-loop}}/\Delta\theta_{\text{obs}} \sim 10^{-3} \cdot 10^{-61} / (10^{-2} \cdot 6 \times 10^{-3}) \approx 10^{-60}$". Later in the paragraph, the text again uses $10^{-3}$: "Contracting directly against the observed angle alone gives $(\alpha_{\text{em}}/4\pi)(H_0/M_{\text{Pl}})/\beta_{\text{obs}} \approx 10^{-3} \cdot 10^{-61} / (6 \times 10^{-3}) \approx 2 \times 10^{-62}$" (if $5 \times 10^{-4}$ were used, this would be $\approx 8 \times 10^{-63}$). While this is an order-of-magnitude estimate, substituting $10^{-3}$ for a value explicitly defined as $5 \times 10^{-4}$ in the same paragraph is confusing and looks like an arithmetic error to a reader following the math.
*   **Required Fix:** Either explicitly state that you are rounding $\alpha_{\text{em}}/(4\pi)$ to $10^{-3}$ for the sake of the order-of-magnitude bookkeeping, or use $5 \times 10^{-4}$ consistently in the arithmetic expressions.

**ID: P1C-M2**
*   **Location:** Appendix A 1, Table III (Page 15)
*   **Problem:** The "Fate" column for operator O4 reads "$\to \kappa^2(J^5 \cdot J^5)$", whereas for O5 it reads "$\to \kappa(J^5 \cdot J^5)$". A reader scanning the table will conclude that O4 is suppressed by $M_{\text{Pl}}^{-4}$ while O5 is suppressed by $M_{\text{Pl}}^{-2}$. The table caption technically explains this discrepancy by stating that the Fate column records the reduction of the *bare* invariant (prior to multiplication by the $M_{\text{Pl}}^2$ prefactor). However, this presentation directly undermines the core physical conclusion emphasized in the main text (Page 14): "Restoring each prefactor, the two genuine dimension-4 densities land on the same member of the Fierz basis at the same power: $\mathcal{O}_4^{[4]} = M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \cdot \kappa^2 (J^5 \cdot J^5) = \kappa (J^5 \cdot J^5) \dots = \mathcal{O}_5^{[4]}$". 
*   **Required Fix:** Modify Table III so that it clearly communicates the final suppression of the *genuine dimension-4 operator*, not just the bare invariant. You can do this by either changing the Fate column to reflect the final operator (i.e., both O4 and O5 $\to \kappa(J^5 \cdot J^5)$) or by adding a new column that shows the final physical suppression after the prefactor is applied.

### MINOR (Address for Completeness)

**ID: P1C-N1**
*   **Location:** Section IV C (Page 8)
*   **Problem:** Violation of the standalone reader test. The text states: "Fitting this coupling to the observed WMAP+Planck birefringence signal ... gives the anchor value $\alpha/M \sim 10^{-21} \text{ GeV}^{-1}$ used above as a numerical input to the Route-2 amplitude estimate." The derivation of this anchor value is entirely deferred to companion papers [1, 13]. Because this value is a load-bearing numerical input for the Route-2 closure, the reader cannot verify the Route-2 bound without trusting an imported, uncomputed claim.
*   **Required Fix:** Provide a 1-2 sentence summary of the basic algebraic relation used to extract this fit (e.g., $\Delta\theta \sim (\alpha/M) \Delta\phi \sim \dots$) so the reader can see the physical origin of the $10^{-21} \text{ GeV}^{-1}$ scale without needing to open the companion paper.

### NIT (Cosmetic / Formatting)

**ID: P1C-N2**
*   **Location:** Section IV A (Page 5)
*   **Problem:** Sloppy dimensional phrasing. The text states: "the integrand $[\beta(\gamma)/M_{\text{Pl}}] \partial_\mu \vartheta_{\text{NY}} J^{5\mu}$ carries dimension $-1 + 2 + 3 = +4$". Strictly speaking, the integrand of Eq. (1) is $\partial_\mu \vartheta_{\text{NY}} J^{5\mu}$, which has mass dimension +5. It is the *entire term in the action* (including the prefactor outside the integral) that has dimension +4. 
*   **Required Fix:** Rephrase to clarify that the dimension +4 applies to the operator including its dimensionful prefactor, not the integrand alone.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The manuscript provides a highly valuable, systematic catalog of constraints on ECH dark energy routes, and the operator-basis completeness argument is a rigorous and welcome addition. However, the paper currently contains a direct algebraic contradiction in its definition of the Planck mass (which propagates into the definition of the gravitational coupling) and a mathematical typo in the Fierz matrix that breaks the explicitly claimed involution property. These are objective mathematical errors that must be corrected. Additionally, the presentation of the Route-2 arithmetic and Table III require clarification to prevent reader confusion. Once these specific issues are addressed, the paper will easily meet the high standards of Classical and Quantum Gravity.
