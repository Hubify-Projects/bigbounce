### ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without fixes)

**P1A-E1**  
Section I (p. 3) and abstract-level summary (p. 1): “the four enumerated routes … are not proven to be a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis”.  
The title and opening claim “Channel-Level Closure of Four Minimal … Routes”. This is a direct mismatch between title framing and the explicit scope limitation stated in the text.  
**Required fix:** Retitle to reflect that only four enumerated phenomenological channels are examined under stated ansätze; remove any implication of operator-basis completeness.

**P1A-E2**  
Throughout (e.g., p. 1, Sec. IV, Sec. IX, Table II): 13 “logically independent” barriers are asserted, yet the paper repeatedly cites “companion papers in preparation” (Paper I(b), II, III, IV, VI) for MCMC verification, Fisher forecasts, galaxy-spin catalog, and ALP fitting. No standalone numerical result or data product is provided.  
**Required fix:** All load-bearing numerical claims (f_NL = −35/8, β = 0.27°, N_tot ≈ 92, ΔN_eff bounds) must be reproducible from material contained in this manuscript or already-published references. Remove or clearly flag every dependence on unpublished work.

**P1A-E3**  
Appendix B (p. 19) and Sec. II C (p. 6): The parity-odd operator (Eq. 6) is assigned off-shell mass dimension +1 (explicitly “not the +4 required for a local Lagrangian density”). The mapping ρ_Λ ∼ (α/M)M_P1^5 is labeled a “phenomenological on-shell scaling ansatz, not a controlled EFT result”.  
**Required fix:** Either derive the operator at dimension +4 or remove all claims that the construction is inside a consistent EFT framework.

**P1A-E4**  
Sec. X (p. 14) “Perturbation-Transparency Result”: the five-step argument that the Holst term reduces to a total derivative on a torsion-free connection is shown only for canonical scalar matter. The paper simultaneously states that fermions are present in the bounce (Sec. II B) and that the parity-odd sector is generated by fermion bilinears. No demonstration is given that the transparency survives once the axial current is restored.  
**Required fix:** Provide an explicit all-order proof including the fermion sector or restrict the theorem statement to pure scalar gravity.

### MAJOR findings (significant revision required)

**P1A-M1**  
Table I and Fig. 1 (p. 4): the “surviving prediction” f_NL = −35/8 is listed as a class-level property of any w = 0 matter-bounce, yet the text (Sec. XIII) acknowledges it is erased once N_tot ≳ 60. The 3–5σ claim therefore rests on an auxiliary assumption about the precise post-bounce e-fold count that is not independently justified inside this paper.  
**Required fix:** Either drop the σ claim or supply a first-principles calculation of N_tot that does not rely on the ansatz in Eq. (B2).

**Required fix:** Demonstrate that the amplitude-level conclusions are stable under at least one other regularization scheme.
