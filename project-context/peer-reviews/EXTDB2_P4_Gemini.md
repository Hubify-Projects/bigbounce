Peer Review Report
Manuscript Title: Survey-Scale Galaxy Chirality with Equivariant TTA: A Null Real-Space Chirality Dipole, a Quantifiable Monopole-Mask Leakage Channel, and Diagnostic Evidence for a Depth/Morphology-Correlated Canonical-Mask Residual on 8.47 Million DESI Legacy Galaxies
Author: Houston Golden (Independent Researcher)
Date of Review: June 28, 2026

1. Recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

Justification: This paper presents an impressively scaled, meticulously audited analysis of spiral galaxy chirality. The core conclusion—that previously reported large-scale cosmological chirality dipoles are most likely survey and classifier artifacts—is an essential service to the astrophysics community. However, major revisions are required because of a fundamental tension in how the paper treats and presents its statistics, alongside an unresolved architectural data leak in its training pipeline. The author explicitly highlights that the harmonic-channel MASTER ℓ=1 excess (+7.28σ or +7.93σ) is a "systematics diagnostic" rather than an independent cosmological null. Yet, by relying extensively on CE-ResNet pseudo-labels for 66.5% of the training set, the author employs a null-randomization procedure (label shuffling) that randomizes the model's own outputs. As admitted on Page 2, this means the primary null tests cannot detect large-scale survey-correlated structures inherited from the pseudo-labels themselves. This creates a logical vulnerability: the systematic errors under evaluation may be self-generated by the training data inheritance, making the "eight-anchor systematic battery" highly descriptive but fundamentally circular regarding the origin of the bias.

2. BLOCKERS
None. The data and code repositories are openly provided and well-documented.

3. MAJOR REVISIONS

M1. Circularity in Systematics Diagnostics vs. Pseudo-Label Inheritance. The author notes that 66.5% of the training labels derive from CE-ResNet predictions. They explicitly admit that because the label-shuffle and per-pixel permutation nulls randomize the model's own outputs, they do not test independence from large-scale survey-correlated structures inherited via the CE-ResNet pseudo-labels. This limitation severely undercuts the independence of the "eight-anchor systematic analysis" of the canonical-mask residual. If the classifier inherited a large-scale, depth-correlated bias from CE-ResNet, the real-space dipole fit on the high-confidence sample might artificially collapse to null because the model's uncalibrated overconfidence suppresses genuine signal into the low-confidence tail. Requirement: The author must implement an independent verification step—such as training a sub-model exclusively on the 6,637 human-verified GZ1 labels—and evaluate whether the resulting real-space dipole and MASTER ℓ=1 residuals remain statistically consistent with the main production model (Catalog C).

M2. Inconsistent Representation of Statistical Significance Across Estimators. The abstract headlines the primary real-space dipole fit as "+0.410 (moment-z... empirical-rank p=0.31)". However, throughout the text, the harmonic-channel residuals are interchangeably quoted using different null-run sizes (+3.64σ vs. +7.93σ vs. +7.28σ), which the author explicitly notes are not directly comparable. Headlining multiple non-comparable σ values in the same paragraphs creates severe legibility and calibration issues. Requirement: The text, particularly Section III.A and Table I, must be refactored to establish a singular, unified baseline for quoting standard deviations, or explicitly move the non-comparable diagnostic σ values to an appendix table.

4. MINOR REVISIONS

m1. Softmax Overconfidence and Monotone Threshold Cuts: The catalog-wide mean confidence is 0.951, whereas the independent GZ1 three-class accuracy is only 58.7%. The model is strongly overconfident. Because the peq>0.6 cut is a monotone sample selection threshold and not a true frequentist probability, the text needs to clarify how this extreme overconfidence affects the injection-recovery curves. Add caveat to Section VI.A.

m2. Metadata Leakage Test (T5) and Circular Coordinates: Appendix B uses a linear Pearson correlation against the raw RA coordinate, which is circular (0°≡360°). This can severely understate azimuthal coupling. The author should replace or supplement the linear Pearson correlation in Table VIII with a circular-linear correlation coefficient (e.g., Mardia's cross-correlation) to ensure the reported metadata leakage result (<0.04) is mathematically sound.

m3. Edge-on Galaxy Triage and Sample Dilution: 65.7% of visually identified edge-on systems (b/a<0.3) leak into CW or CCW classifications rather than being triaged into NOT_SPIRAL. This contamination penalty causes an estimated 5–8% sensitivity inflation in the Fisher floor. The author states this estimate is "qualitative pending the axis-ratio cross-match." Given that this is a final catalog release, this cross-match should be performed now rather than deferred, providing a definitive quantitative f_edge reduction factor in Section VI.A.

5. Strengths

1. Survey Scale: At 8.47 million total galaxies and 3.2 million spirals, this represents a massive, order-of-magnitude catalog expansion (roughly 1.6× the scale of CE-ResNet and 25× the scale of classic SDSS samples).

2. Methodological Rigor via Test-Time Equivariance: The implementation of a 2-fold flip-TTA pipeline directly eliminates single-pass classifier orientation bias, successfully collapsing a spurious 2.31σ dipole down to a null-consistent 0.41σ.

3. Exposing the Leakage Channel: The paper provides a highly valuable diagnostic service by proving that a minor uniform classifier monopole couples to patchy survey-mask geometry, generating a false pre-MASTER dipole-class artifact that accounts for 99.32% of previously reported raw power.
