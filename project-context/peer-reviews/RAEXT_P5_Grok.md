Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS
This is a technically strong, transparent, and reproducible null-result paper on an interesting topic (parity/environment coupling in the cosmic web). It is suitable for a top journal (MNRAS/PRD/JCAP) after modest polishing. The core claim — no detectable environment dependence of spiral chirality beyond the known catalog-wide classifier monopole at current DESI DR1 sensitivity — is robustly supported across multiple independent methods. The work is honest about limitations (post-hoc primary path, redshift-space nature, selection effects, counting-statistics floors) and sets a high bar for reproducibility via committed artifacts, provenance, and a frozen analysis tag.
No blockers to publication. The science is sound, the statistics are appropriate and well-controlled, and the null is useful as an empirical upper bound for theory.
BLOCKERS
None.
MAJORS (2)
Post-hoc primary-path declaration and garden-of-forking-paths framing (severity: moderate)
The authors are admirably candid: “the primary designation is declared post-hoc — no single a priori preregistered plan was filed” (§V B, abstract). They mitigate well by (a) showing all five DESIVAST estimators (three sphere-PIS + two catalog-native GALZONE) are uniformly null under a Bonferroni-5 family (|zΔ| ≤ 1.25, pΔ ≥ 0.21; Table XI and §VIII), (b) demonstrating the T-Web Phase-2 sweep, Tempel FoF, ASTRA EDR overlap, redshift/density/HEALPix, and program-split paths are also null, and (c) explicitly arguing the post-hoc choice does not drive the result.
For a high-bar journal this still requires tightening. The current text already does most of the work; strengthen the framing so the reader sees the entire analysis tree (primary + secondary + descriptive) as converging on the same null, rather than elevating one post-hoc path. Suggested minimal fixes:
In abstract and §V B / §VI A, lead with “the null is robust across the full analysis tree; we designate the best-powered DESIVAST-anchored void contrast as primary for clarity.”
Add one sentence noting that future work in this program will use pre-registered analysis plans.
This is not a fatal flaw — the mitigation is already strong — but it is the single largest presentational/methodological item that could invite referee or reader concern.
Redshift-space nature of the headline result (severity: moderate)
The paper is explicit that the T-Web classification, DESIVAST cross-check, and all null tests are performed in redshift space and inherit RSD effects (§XIII, abstract, conclusions). The RSD discussion itself is excellent: it distinguishes anisotropic eigenvalue deformation (dominant) from isotropic scalar displacement, supplies an order-of-magnitude boundary-crossing estimate (~3–5% of cells near thresholds at σv/(aH) ~5 Mpc/h), and defers full Zel’dovich/BAO-reconstructed re-classification to future work.
The headline claim (“no detectable dependence… within DESI Data Release 1”) and abstract should more prominently foreground the redshift-space caveat and the quantitative sub-percent contamination bound. A one-sentence elevation in the abstract conclusions paragraph and a short bolded sentence in §XIII would suffice. This is already handled better than most papers; it simply needs slightly higher visibility given the precision cosmology audience.
MINORS (selection; none rise to major)
Readability / sentence density: A few very long sentences with nested parentheticals (especially abstract, §V B primary/secondary declaration, and §XIII RSD paragraphs) slow the expert reader. Minor tightening or strategic breaking would improve flow without loss of precision.
Paper IV dependence: The analysis rests heavily on per-galaxy labels and the monopole from the companion Paper IV (in preparation / coordinated submission). Table I already summarizes the consumed inputs well. A short explicit note in §II or the abstract that “Paper IV is submitted concurrently and supplies only the per-galaxy equivariant labels plus a catalog-wide monopole that is independently re-measured in the P5 matched sample (fP5CW = 0.49719)” would help readers who encounter P5 first.
RSD quantification visibility: The order-of-magnitude boundary-crossing estimate and scalar-displacement heuristic are useful; consider promoting the ~0.2 pp contamination scale to a short dedicated sentence or small table in §XIII so it is not lost in the detailed discussion.
Future-work / power section: §XIV (LSST) is brief. One additional sentence on expected gains with DESI DR2 (larger void samples) or full real-space re-analysis would be welcome but is not required.
Toy EFT appendix: Appendix A is a nice theorist-facing addition. The caveats (rotational invariance resolved by using local ∇ρ direction; gauge invariance not fully addressed; schematic only) are already stated clearly. No change needed beyond ensuring the “guide, not derived constraint” language remains prominent.
Minor typographic / extraction artifacts in the supplied PDF text (e.g., occasional formatting of subscripts or Greek) are not present in the actual rendered PDF; no action required.
STRENGTHS (≥3; actually many)
Exceptional transparency and reproducibility standards. Explicit primary/secondary declaration with Bonferroni families, committed GitHub repo at frozen manuscript tag v0.1.92-2026-06-29, per-output provenance JSON, deterministic seeds, full analysis drivers, and a reproducibility checklist. This is a model for how large-scale null-result papers should be presented. Artifact hyperlinks and Appendix D make every number independently recomputable.
Multi-method robustness of the null. The primary DESIVAST result (nvoid = 56,981, ΔfCW = +0.0007, |zΔ| ≤ 1.12 across three algorithms + catalog-native definitions) is buttressed by: (i) T-Web canonical + 9-cell (Rs, λth) Phase-2 sweep (all monopole-subtracted residuals ≤ 1.64σ in resolved cells; look-elsewhere p ≥ 0.13), (ii) Tempel+2014 FoF cross-validation on SDSS DR10 overlap (filament-like concordance 0.29 pp, ~0.5σ), (iii) ASTRA EDR probabilistic per-object cross-check on 25k-galaxy overlap (same null despite strong per-galaxy label disagreement), (iv) redshift, projected-density, HEALPix sky-position, and tracer-program splits, all with label-shuffle permutation LEE corrections (NMC = 1,000). Every path converges on the catalog-monopole-only model.
Quantitative, honest treatment of systematics and power. Classifier monopole is independently re-measured in the P5 matched sample and propagated explicitly (σpred formula, monopole-referenced residuals, internal consistency with Paper IV). Bright/dark program residuals are diagnosed and attributed to known imaging-leg effects. Counting-statistics floors are stated plainly (T-Web void bin n = 428 is sample-size limited; DESIVAST primary is properly powered). The ~2σ filament bright/dark sign-flip is flagged as a diagnostic for future data rather than over-interpreted. Limitations section (§XIII) is candid and detailed.
Appropriate statistical toolkit for a null claim. Jeffreys binomial credible intervals, two-sample contrasts, 4×2 homogeneity χ² tests (both row-level and unique-spiral), label-shuffle permutations, empirical max-stat LEE, sensitivity sweeps, and within-class density/redshift decompositions. The authors correctly emphasize that raw |σfrom half| values are not comparable across bins of different n and focus inference on monopole-subtracted residuals and two-sample ΔfCW.
Scientific utility. Provides a clean, well-characterized observational upper bound on any future bounce-chirality or parity-violating model that predicts environment dependence at ≳25 Mpc/h smoothing scales. Complements the Paper IV global-dipole null. The toy EFT mapping in Appendix A, while schematic, is a helpful bridge to theorists. The paper does not over-claim (explicitly does not adjudicate Shamir 2022 global signal; notes neither bounce nor inflation currently predicts an environment-dependent signature at this scale).
Scope and caveats handled correctly. The result is framed as a redshift-space statement with quantitative RSD bounds; selection function, projected-density proxy limitations, and absence of a full-DR1 cosmic-web VAC are all noted. The post-hoc primary choice is declared rather than hidden.
Summary
This is a high-quality, publication-ready paper whose scientific conclusions will stand. The two major items are presentational/framing adjustments that do not require new analysis or data. After those minor revisions the manuscript will meet the high bar for MNRAS/PRD/JCAP.
