**Referee Report for PRD (A3M Round)**

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract + p.1, col. 1)**  
The opening paragraph (functioning as the abstract) asserts that the work “independently confirms” \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) and that this value is “exactly half” the Cai et al. (2009) result, while simultaneously stating that “the factor of two between −35/16 and −35/8 itself has not been settled by an independent second method.” These two statements are logically contradictory within the same paragraph. The abstract claim is therefore stronger than any single body statement that follows.  
*Required fix*: Rewrite the abstract so every quantitative claim is no stronger than the final calibrated statement in Sec. II.C and carries the explicit caveat that the cubic self-interaction term remains uncomputed.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL, p.1 header + p.7)**  
The document is dated “September 2, 2026” and contains a full “AI USAGE DISCLOSURE” plus a multi-paragraph “REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT” that lists internal commit hashes, file paths, and round tags. None of this language belongs in a PRD regular article.  
*Required fix*: Remove all future-date, internal-audit, and AI-disclosure text; replace with a conventional Data Availability statement.

**A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL, p.2, Eq. (3) and Table I)**  
The squeezed-limit total is printed as \(-35/16 + 15/16\mu^2\). The table rows sum to exactly this expression only after an ad-hoc grouping of five vertices; the individual vertex contributions are not cross-checked against any external code or published result beyond the authors’ own GitHub commit. The claim of an “independent, from-scratch in-in confirmation” therefore rests on a single unreviewed pipeline.  
*Required fix*: Provide a fully independent analytic or numeric verification (different code base, different regularization) or downgrade the language to “re-derivation within the same formalism.”

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR, p.3, Eq. (5) and surrounding text)**  
The transmission bound \(0<T_{f_{\rm NL}}\le 1/2\) is derived under the assumption that the cubic self-interaction term is negligible. The paper explicitly states this term “is not computed here.” The bound is therefore conditional on an untested assumption and cannot be presented as a universal result.  
*Required fix*: Either compute the cubic term or qualify every occurrence of the bound with the explicit statement that it excludes the bounce’s own cubic self-interaction.

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR, p.4, Table II and p.5, Eq. (9))**  
The PBH channel result is reported solely as an amplitude ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.732\pm0.050\). No absolute abundance is quoted because the required primordial spectrum shape is “not reconstructible from the published source.” A ratio that cannot be converted into an observable is not a testable prediction; its evidential weight is therefore overstated in the abstract (“primordial black holes”) and in Sec. V.  
*Required fix*: Either supply a closed-form spectrum or reclassify the PBH channel as “illustrative only; not yet a quantitative test.”

**A3M-M3 (MAJOR, p.5, Fig. 1 and Table III)**  
Figure 1 and Table III display curves and ratios for three values of \(f_{\rm NL}\), yet the caption and body never state the precise numerical value of the Gaussian-calibrated amplitude used to normalize the ratio. The plotted curves cannot be reproduced from the information given.  
*Required fix*: Add the exact normalization value and the precise definition of “Gaussian-calibrated amplitude” to both caption and text.

**A3M-N1 (MINOR, p.2, Sec. II.A)**  
Multiple URLs and commit hashes appear inside the main text rather than in a footnote or reproducibility appendix. This violates PRD house style.  
*Required fix*: Move all repository links to a single reproducibility footnote.

**A3M-N2 (NIT, throughout)**  
The phrase “the operative uncertainty is internal rather than observational” appears in the abstract and again in Sec. VI without a quantitative definition of “internal.” Cosmetic but contributes to the overall impression of non-standard language.

**Summary recommendation**  
REJECT

The manuscript contains multiple internal contradictions between abstract and body, future-dated and audit-style text that has no place in a journal article, and at least one load-bearing quantitative claim (the transmission bound) that is explicitly conditioned on an uncomputed term. Until these foundational issues are corrected, the paper does not meet the acceptance threshold for a PRD regular article.
