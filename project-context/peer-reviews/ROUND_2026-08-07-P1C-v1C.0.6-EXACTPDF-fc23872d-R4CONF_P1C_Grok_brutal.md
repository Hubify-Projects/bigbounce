**P1C-E2** (ESSENTIAL)  
Abstract (p. 1) and Sec. IV (p. 5–7).  
Abstract states “roughly sixty orders of magnitude (conservatively ≥58) of suppression margin” for Route 2 and “61–67 orders” for Route 3. These numbers are never recomputed from first principles in the present manuscript; they rest on imported one-loop coefficients and the companion’s β-function. No explicit trace of the arithmetic appears.  
Fix: Provide the full numerical derivation (including every prefactor, loop factor, and Planck-mass convention) inside this paper.

**P1C-E3** (ESSENTIAL)  
Abstract, Sec. III, Table I, Fig. 1 (p. 1, 3–4).  
The abstract claims “thirteen distinct mechanism-class constraints”. Table I lists 14 entries with the explicit note that B8 is “subsumed by B14”. The counting is therefore inconsistent on the page.  
Fix: Correct the abstract and all summary statements to the actual number of independent constraints.

**P1C-M1** (MAJOR)  
Sec. V and Appendix A (p. 9–11).  
The operator-basis argument asserts completeness “from the construction rules, not proved by exhaustive symbolic enumeration”. The released script is cited but not reproduced; the six-member basis {O1–O6} is declared closed by fiat. No independent verification of the Fierz projection or Bianchi identities is supplied in the text.  
Fix: Either perform the enumeration inside the paper or downgrade the claim to “conjectured finite basis under stated assumptions”.

**P1C-M2** (MAJOR)  
Sec. IV A–B (p. 5–7) and abstract.  
Route-2 and Route-3 closures are labeled “historical candidate mappings” that the companion “does not retain as results”. The present paper then treats the same mappings as “bounded amplitude budgets”. This is circular: the no-go is asserted by refusing to recompute the stress-tensor matching that would be required to promote the bounds to theorems.  
Fix: Either supply the missing stress-tensor derivation or reclassify both routes as “exploratory upper bounds, not closures”.
