ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. II C (assumption (d) and explicit load-bearing caveat on p. 1): The entire f_NL = −35/8 prediction and all downstream significances/Bayes factors are conditional on faithful cubic-order bispectrum transmission through the bounce. This is verified only at linear order [1] and supported at cubic order solely by a superhorizon scaling estimate (δf_NL ∼ 10^{-3}, order-of-magnitude, not a derived bound). No explicit in-in computation with bounce-modified mode functions is performed; the paper itself identifies this as the single weakest link and #1 required follow-up. Headline claims of a “test” therefore rest on an unverified theoretical input rather than a derived result.

[MAJOR] Sec. III B (template projection, Eq. 5) and Sec. IV (SPHEREx headline 5.2–5.5σ optimistic / 2.6–5.5σ realistic): The Heinrich et al. [6] σ(f_local_NL) ≈ 0.7 is rescaled post-hoc by a shape-overlap factor r ≈ 0.84 (noise-weighted central value from Fisher/ℓ-space/MC/null-space methods). However, the additional variance from the non-local tails of the bounce bispectrum in the full multi-tracer estimator covariance is neither recomputed nor included (explicitly stated as out of scope and absorbed into the systematic envelope). Combined with the additive-quadrature heuristic for the full budget (Sec. VII, no joint multi-tracer Fisher for the bispectrum channel), the quoted ranges are scoping sensitivity envelopes, not derived measurement precisions, yet are presented as the primary forecast results.

[MINOR] Sec. VI (Bayesian comparison, Table II and closed-form Eq. 9): Bayes factors (headline BF ≈ 9–14 after r-rebooking) are computed exactly under chosen priors (σ_theory = 1.0 Gaussian bounce; broad [−15, +15] vs. narrow [−5, +5] competitors) and validated with 3 × 10^5 MC realizations. However, the factors inherit the full theoretical uncertainty in the bounce prediction itself (ϵ-correction 0.6–8%, null-space r scatter ±0.13, assumption (d)); the text correctly labels them “illustrative,” but the prominent abstract/table presentation does not sufficiently caveat that broader theoretical priors on f_NL would monotonically reduce BF.

[MINOR] Sec. II A–B and null-space analysis (10,000-sample scan, r = 0.85 ± 0.13): The underdetermined 6-coefficient polynomial representation (3 benchmark constraints) produces a 3-dimensional null space whose scatter is quantified and shown not to shift the central r materially. The 16th-percentile tail (r ≈ 0.70) maps to noticeably lower pre-systematic significance (~4.4σ); while the headline uses the noise-weighted central value, the distributional robustness bound is not propagated into the final quoted ranges or abstract.

The central claim—that SPHEREx (and MegaMapper) can furnish a meaningful test of the matter-bounce f_NL = −35/8 prediction capable of discriminating it from single-field slow-roll and tuned multifield inflation—is conditionally supported inside the stated assumptions, recast methodology, and heuristic systematic budget, but the supporting evidence remains limited by the unverified cubic transmission assumption and the approximate (non-recomputed) covariance treatment.
