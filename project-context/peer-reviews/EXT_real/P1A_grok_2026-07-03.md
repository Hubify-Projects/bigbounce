## VERBATIM RESPONSE

VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. II C, Eq. (10), Appendix B, and all R4/DE claims: The mapping from the parity-odd operator (Eq. 6) to late-time ρ_Λ rests entirely on a phenomenological on-shell scaling ansatz that assigns the observed dark-energy density via [(α/M) M_Pl] D_inf. The operator has off-shell mass dimension +1 (explicitly acknowledged), not +4; every quantitative claim about Route 4 producing ρ_Λ and the associated N_tot ≈ 92 bookkeeping is therefore conditional on this ansatz rather than derived from the ECH action. This materially weakens the "closure" language.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV D (Route 2) and Sec. IV E (Route 3): The amplitude-suppression budgets that close these routes (Eqs. 15–18 and the Δθ_one-loop/Δθ_obs ∼ 10^−60 and Δγ/γ ∼ 0.3 estimates) are constructed from explicitly labeled EFT scaling ansatze and conservative upper-bound coefficients. The text states these are "not a result extracted from" Mercuri, Shapiro–Teixeira, or Benedetti–Speziale. A channel-level no-go at the claimed strength requires either a controlled derivation of the coefficients or a demonstration that even order-of-magnitude inflation of the prefactors still leaves the routes excluded; neither is supplied.

[MAJOR] Sec. IX and Table IV (13/14 barriers): The catalog mixes two Tier-I results (R1 torsion-elimination derivation; B14 perturbation transparency) with a majority of Tier-II naturalness/structural arguments and at least one explicitly heuristic closure (B9 Liouville conservation, conditional on closed Hamiltonian evolution, no particle production, no entropy injection). Several barriers (B5, B6, B7, B10, B13) are general to broad classes of bounce or modified-gravity models rather than ECH-specific calculations. Labeling them uniformly as "13 distinct mechanism-class constraints" that "collectively constrain the enumerated channels" overstates both their individual rigor and their distinctness.

[MAJOR] Title, Abstract, Sec. IV "Scope" paragraph, and Sec. IV G: The paper repeatedly frames the result as a "Four-Route No-Go" and "channel-level amplitude closure" while correctly noting that (a) the four routes are an illustrative enumeration, not a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis, (b) the Jackiw–Pi term and parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner were initially omitted and are closed only in Sec. IV B–C, and (c) the complete dimension-6 parity-odd basis plus projection lemma is deferred to a follow-up. The title and abstract emphasis on "closure" therefore exceeds what the scoped, ansatz-conditional argument actually establishes.

[MINOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result): The five-step argument (zero spin density for canonical scalars → T=0 exactly via Cartan equation → Levi-Civita connection → algebraic Bianchi identity R_μ[νρσ]=0 forces the Holst dual contraction to vanish pointwise) is correct and cleanly executed within its stated scope. However, the core fact that torsion vanishes identically for spinless matter is standard Einstein–Cartan theory (Hehl et al. 1976 and subsequent literature); the added value is the explicit all-orders statement for the Holst sector and the clean separation from the Pontryagin density. The result should be positioned as a useful but incremental clarification rather than a primary novel theorem.

[MINOR] Sec. V, VII, and multiple observational citations: The galaxy-spin null result, SPHEREx f_NL forecast significances, and several numerical inputs (e.g., γ_PTA, β benchmarks) are imported from companion papers "in preparation." While the text correctly states these numbers are non-load-bearing for the structural closures, their presence reduces self-containment for a standalone PRD submission.

One sentence on the central claim: The scoped claim that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are amplitude- or naturalness-constrained under the stated phenomenological on-shell scaling ansatz and the listed mechanism-class barriers is supported by the explicit torsion-elimination derivation for R1, the Bianchi-identity argument for perturbation transparency, and the order-of-magnitude budgets and naturalness arguments for R2–R4, but only within the heavy qualifications the paper itself repeatedly emphasizes.
