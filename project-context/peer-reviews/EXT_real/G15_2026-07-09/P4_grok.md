Referee Report for Physical Review D

Manuscript: "A Null Chirality Dipole in 8.5 Million DESI Galaxies from Equivariant Deep Learning" by Houston Golden

This is a large-scale, methodologically careful observational cosmology paper. It delivers the largest chirality-labeled spiral catalog to date (8.47 million galaxies, 3.20 million spirals) using a flip-equivariant ViT-Small classifier with test-time averaging, performs a real-space dipole measurement on a pre-specified high-confidence subsample, and quantifies sensitivity via empirical injection-recovery. The primary conclusion — a null real-space chirality dipole at sub-percent amplitude on the high-confidence sample — is robustly supported by the declared primary estimator (+0.41σ moment-z, rank-p = 0.31 against isotropic pixel-permutation null) and by the block-bootstrap WLS template fit that strongly disfavors a clean 1.7% cosmological dipole. The harmonic-channel residuals are correctly treated as systematics diagnostics rather than independent cosmological probes, with an eight-anchor battery (Appendix D) and forward modeling that attributes the bulk to survey effects. Limitations are stated explicitly, reproducibility artifacts (catalog, model weights, scripts, provenance JSONs) are extensive and public, and the comparison to prior Shamir claims is appropriately caveated as amplitude-level tension pending matched reanalysis.

The work meets PRD standards for rigor and transparency in observational cosmology. I have identified only minor issues that should be addressed for clarity and to forestall misinterpretation; none rise to the level of blocking publication.

Minor Concerns (do not block publication but should be addressed)

Statistical interpretation and presentation of the block-bootstrap WLS template-fit exclusion (z ≈ −18)
The bootstrap procedure (NSIDE = 8 super-pixels, Nboot = 1000, validated across NSIDE ∈ {4, 8, 16} yielding stable |z| ≥ 17) and its scope are well documented in Appendix D, including the explicit statement that it is a “model-dependent template-disfavor statistic under the adopted NSIDE = 8 block-bootstrap spatial covariance, not a calibrated frequentist exclusion significance.” The text also correctly notes that classifier-label noise is folded into the empirical injection-recovery floors (making the disfavor conservative) and that the joint nuisance bound on depth/morphology/confidence channels is obtained via coupled diagnostics rather than a single simultaneous likelihood. However, the abstract and Table I (row P2) present the z ≈ −18 result prominently as one of the two “primary” claims (“A clean cosmological 1.7% dipole is disfavored”). A short, explicit reiteration in the main text near Table I or in Sec. IV C — e.g., “This z quantifies tension with a clean 1.7% template under the spatial error model validated in Appendix D and is conservative with respect to classifier dilution; it is not a calibrated frequentist exclusion” — would eliminate any risk of readers treating it as an 18σ frequentist statement.
Classification: MINOR. Cite: Abstract; Sec. IV C (block-bootstrap WLS paragraph and Table I); Appendix D (block-bootstrap scope, caveat, and NSIDE-sensitivity paragraphs; Table XIV).
Unmodelled fraction (~47%) of the post-MASTER canonical-mask ℓ = 1 residual amplitude
The forward model (imaging-leg fractions + pixel density + density² + real DR8 morphology templates) accounts for ~53% of the observed |a₁| = 6.95 × 10⁻³ (Ap ≈ 0.695%) residual in the correct direction (cos θ ≈ +0.83–0.84). The paper correctly bounds even the full residual below the primary real-space estimator’s empirical 50%-recovery threshold (A₅₀ ≈ 0.75%, A₉₅ ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%]) and notes that the real-space +0.41σ null is independent of the harmonic channel. The remainder is attributed to the classifier’s confidence-vs-depth response at production scale (a per-galaxy, morphology-correlated selection effect not captured by pixel-level templates) and is deferred to future GPU-scale mapping. Because the harmonic channel is used to support the systematic attribution in the eight-anchor battery (Table VII), a one-sentence quantitative closure in Sec. IV D would be helpful: any cosmological contribution to the unmodelled remainder must lie below A₅₀ and is therefore consistent with the observed real-space null. The current text already contains the necessary ingredients; a single explicit sentence would make the logic airtight for readers focused on the diagnostic channel.
Classification: MINOR. Cite: Sec. IV D (“Quantitative forward model of the residual amplitude” and “Statistical upper limit on the cosmological content of the unmodelled remainder” paragraphs); Table VII (synthesis row); Appendix D (anchors b, c, d, g, i and forward-model results).
Contextualization of the Shamir et al. amplitude comparison
The paper correctly reports an amplitude-level tension of ~7–18× (under the present ViT/TTA pipeline) while explicitly stating that this is not a frequentist exclusion of Shamir’s distinct Ganalyzer estimator and that a matched-footprint reanalysis is required (Sec. V A and Introduction). This conservative stance is appropriate. A minor polish addition in Sec. V A would strengthen the methodological framing without altering the claim: briefly note that the monopole-mask leakage channel (Sec. IV D, 99.32% pre-MASTER reproduction) and the depth-coupled classifier bias demonstrated here (global monopole artifact, confidence-cut sweep, leg-proxy cross-power) supply plausible mechanisms by which a pipeline lacking equivalent equivariant TTA and an explicit NOT_SPIRAL class could generate or amplify spurious low-ℓ power on a patchy footprint. This would provide positive context for why the present null is expected under improved methodology, while still calling for the matched reanalysis.
Classification: MINOR. Cite: Sec. V A (Shamir comparison paragraph); Introduction (paragraphs discussing Shamir 2012/2020/2022 claims); Secs. IV B–D (monopole artifact, confidence-cut sweep, leakage null).

No other issues were identified. The declared analysis hierarchy (Table I), pre-specified peq > 0.6 cut with commit-hash provenance, full confidence-cut sweep showing stability in the high-confidence regime, model-independent GZ1-human-only dipole cross-check (z = −0.54σ), empirical injection-recovery calibration, edge-on contamination audit (Appendix E; dilution only, no directional bias due to equivariance), and comprehensive reproducibility package (public catalog with QC flags, model checkpoint, GitHub scripts, provenance JSONs, Zenodo deposit plan) are exemplary. The explicit handling of the global CW-fraction monopole as a classifier artifact (spatially uniform, does not source a dipole) and the warning to downstream users about local monopole renormalization for precision parity tests are particularly responsible.
