Summary of contribution.
The manuscript systematically examines whether minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst (ECH) gravity, in the presence of a quantum bounce (LQC holonomy corrections), can source late-time dark energy through four enumerated channels (R1: NJL-type four-fermion contact; R2: one-loop graviton corrections to the Holst sector; R3: quantum running of the Barbero–Immirzi parameter; R4: parity-odd coupling to a spectator ALP or neutrino current). It concludes that all four are closed at the channel-amplitude level under explicitly stated assumptions: R1–R3 by amplitude suppression (Planck-scale or loop factors), R4 by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection that relocates rather than solves the cosmological-constant problem.
