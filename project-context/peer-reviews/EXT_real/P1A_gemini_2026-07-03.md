ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section I and Table II (Dependence on Unpublished Work): The manuscript heavily imports quantitative observational inputs—such as Markov Chain Monte Carlo posteriors, Fisher forecast significances, and galaxy spin pipeline results—from companion papers (Papers I(b), II, III, and IV) that are currently "in preparation". While the author asserts these imported numerical values are non-load-bearing for the analytical theorems , contextualizing the broader observational programme and evaluating the phenomenological viability of the framework is impossible for a reviewer without access to the full methodology, data, and coordinated submissions of these companions.

[MAJOR] Section II.A.2 and Appendix B (Dimensional Scaling Ansatz): The proposed mechanism connecting the Einstein-Cartan-Holst parity-odd operator to a dark energy density rests entirely on a phenomenological on-shell scaling ansatz where an off-shell mass dimension +1 operator is promoted to +4. Although the author explicitly acknowledges this dimensional mismatch and treats it as an assumption rather than a derivation , bridging a three-unit dimensional gap with an ad-hoc on-shell scaling rather than a controlled effective field theory calculation fundamentally limits the theoretical rigor of the dark energy mapping.
PDF

[MAJOR] Sections IV.D and IV.E (Heuristic Upper Bounds for Routes 2 and 3): The amplitude closures for Route 2 (one-loop graviton corrections) and Route 3 (quantum running of the Barbero-Immirzi parameter) are based on "conservative upper bounds derived from a chiral-count EFT scaling ansatz". Because these are not precise, first-principles extractions from the underlying theory, the closure of these routes remains an exploratory estimate—honest to an order of magnitude—rather than a definitive, structural no-go result.
PDF

[MINOR] Section IX (Heterogeneous Nature of the Constraints): The 13 distinct mechanism-class constraints present a mix of rigorous first-principles derivations (e.g., the perturbation transparency theorem of Barrier 14) and qualitative philosophical or heuristic arguments (e.g., Barrier 13's "Gravitational Democracy" or Barrier 9's "Liouville Conservation"). Grouping these disparate evidentiary tiers together as a unified catalog of barriers dilutes the impact of the analytically rigorous components.

This redundancy heavily impedes readability and should be condensed into a single, sharply defined scope section.
