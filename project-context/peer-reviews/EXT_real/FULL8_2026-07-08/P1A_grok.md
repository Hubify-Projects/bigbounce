ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Title, abstract, and Sec. IV opening paragraph vs. Sec. IV F: The title and abstract frame the work as delivering “Channel-Level Amplitude Closure” of four routes, yet the body explicitly states that R4 is closed only by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection (relocating the CC problem via m_θ ∼ H_0 tuning) rather than any amplitude mismatch or suppression; this mismatch between title framing and actual R4 result must be corrected.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV (Scope paragraph) and App. C: The claim that the four enumerated routes are “basis-complete at the M_Pl-power-counting level within minimal ECH” (with the Jackiw–Pi term and parity-odd four-fermion partner now “closed explicitly”) rests on the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma of App. C plus NDA monotonicity, but the main text does not demonstrate that every diffeomorphism-invariant operator up to mass dimension 6 built from the minimal ECH field content (tetrad, algebraic torsion via Cartan constraint, Holst term, minimal fermion coupling) is exhausted; without an explicit operator inventory or proof ruling out additional structures (e.g., derivative corrections on contorsion consistent with minimal coupling), the completeness assertion is not substantiated.

[MAJOR] Sec. X (proof of perturbation transparency): The argument that the Holst dual contraction ϵ^{μνρσ} R_μνρσ vanishes identically by the algebraic Bianchi identity R_μ[νρσ] = 0 on the torsion-free Levi-Civita connection, implying decoupling from scalar/tensor perturbation EOM, is formally correct for a fixed torsion-free background; however, the paper does not explicitly expand the full ECH + scalar-matter action to quadratic order, substitute the algebraic Cartan solution for torsion at linear order, and verify that no first-order torsion is sourced by scalar perturbations or that the Holst sector contributes zero to the linearized metric/scalar equations after back-substitution. The stated caveats (excluding fermion spin density, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings) are noted but the explicit verification for the “canonical scalar matter” case central to the claim is missing.

[MINOR] Sec. IV D and E: Routes 2 and 3 closures rely on phenomenological operators (Eq. 15) and ansätze (Eq. 17) motivated by but not directly extracted from the cited one-loop calculations (Shapiro & Teixeira; Benedetti & Speziale); while the resulting suppression margins (∼60 orders) render the qualitative conclusions robust to O(1) coefficient variations, the precise coefficient mapping, validity of the EFT operator at cosmological scales, and treatment of the non-perturbative RG flow (no fixed point for R2) require clearer justification or additional matching calculations.

[MINOR] Sec. IX (14-barrier catalog): Several barriers are hierarchically related or restate overlapping mechanisms (B8 is explicitly subsumed by B14; multiple “universality/dilemma” entries probe similar decoupling or naturalness issues); the classification into “mechanism-class constraints” is asserted but not accompanied by an explicit independence or minimality argument, reducing the catalog’s utility as a structured constraint map.

The central claim that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at channel-amplitude granularity (R1–R3 by explicit suppression under labeled scaling assumptions, R4 by naturalness) under single-scale EFT power counting, together with the perturbation-transparency result for canonical scalar matter, is supported by the per-route derivations in Sec. IV, the dimensional no-go of App. B, and the Bianchi-identity decoupling argument of Sec. X within the paper’s explicitly stated scopes and caveats.
