VERDICT: REJECT

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Appendix A / central correction claim: the claimed resolution of the Cai–Li factor-of-two discrepancy is internally inconsistent. The manuscript says Cai’s printed polynomial differs from the vertex sum by a spurious +(99/128)∑i k_i^3 term, admits that this isolated term has the wrong sign and size to explain −35/16 → −35/8, yet still presents it as the algebraic origin of the discrepancy; it also states that Li’s total polynomial reduces to the same polynomial as Cai’s while supposedly yielding a different squeezed-limit amplitude under the same normalization. These statements cannot all be true.

[MAJOR] Secs. II–III / template-overlap forecast: the paper uses Cai et al.’s printed monomial shape for the overlap r while simultaneously claiming that the printed polynomial is arithmetically wrong. If the correction is an additive local-shaped term, it changes the configuration-dependent BNL shape and therefore r, not merely the overall amplitude. The SPHEREx significance cannot be based on an overlap computed from the allegedly erroneous shape.

[MAJOR] Sec. II C / cubic-order bounce transmission: the load-bearing assumption that the third-order bispectrum transmits through the bounce with only O((kηbounce)^2) ≈ 10^−4 corrections is not demonstrated. Invoking single-clock superhorizon ζ conservation is insufficient without an explicit proof that the LQC bounce satisfies the nonlinear theorem’s hypotheses through the NEC-violating/high-curvature transition and without computing the cubic interaction terms or matching conditions.

[MAJOR] Secs. IV and VII / forecast methodology: the headline 1.3–2.75σ range is not a controlled forecast. It mixes a scalar local-template rescaling, flat/noise-weighted shape overlaps, an independent Fisher result with reff ≈ 0.99, additive-quadrature nuisance budgets, and a GR-correlation proxy imported from a different SDB Fisher channel. This is not a self-consistent multi-tracer bispectrum Fisher analysis.

[MAJOR] Sec. VI / Bayesian comparison: the Bayes factors are prior-dominated and conditional on a mock measurement centered exactly on the proposed prediction. The point-prior-versus-uniform-prior calculation does not establish real model-selection power, and the competitor priors [−5,+5] and [−15,+15] are not physically derived or marginalized over a defensible model space.

[MAJOR] Data and Code Availability / reproducibility: many quantitative claims rest on named scripts and JSON artifacts, but the manuscript itself does not provide enough derivation or tabulated intermediate results to audit the factor-of-two correction, template-overlap values, independent Fisher matrix, or GR-degeneracy estimates. “DOI inserted at submission” is not an acceptable reproducibility statement for review.

[MINOR] Presentation / PRD suitability: the manuscript is excessively redundant and advocacy-oriented, repeatedly answering anticipated referee objections inside the text rather than presenting a compact derivation. Several sections read as internal audit notes rather than a journal article.

[MINOR] Figures and wording: Fig. 6 and Sec. IX still refer to fNL ≈ −4 in places despite the corrected central value being −35/16 ≈ −2.19, and several figure captions conflate SPHEREx-only, MegaMapper, optimistic, conservative, and post-systematic ranges in ways that make the plotted significance bars difficult to interpret.

The central claim is not supported by the evidence presented, because the key theoretical correction and the sensitivity forecast both rely on internally inconsistent algebra and non-self-consistent forecast bookkeeping.
