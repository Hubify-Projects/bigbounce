ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Title, Abstract, §I, §III (opening): The manuscript repeatedly headlines a “268,519 Validated Sources” / “validated catalog-grade subset” while the body explicitly defines this count as a “process-volume figure” of “anomaly candidates surviving per-survey validation gates … not a count of confirmed physical detections.” The title and early claims therefore misrepresent what the validation actually certifies (reconstruction-error outlier status for the broad/continuum class only).
[MAJOR] §III E: The eROSITA production score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible as a matter of provenance—an undocumented post-hoc rescaling that cannot be recovered from any of 16 monotone transforms or 3 IsolationForest retrains on the committed raw scores; the tier is excised from every headline and inclusive count (including 377,482) yet still released as a top-298 membership list. This constitutes a serious reproducibility failure for a multi-survey catalog.
[MAJOR] §III G: The Gaia DR3 tier was synthetic placeholder data (sequential fake source_ids of the form ⌊5×10¹⁸ + i⌋, duplicate IDs, unphysical G magnitudes) generated by the fallback path in gaia_expanded.py; it was removed only after internal audit. Inclusion of synthetic data in the production pipeline until that point indicates inadequate provenance controls.
[MAJOR] §III D, §VI A, Table I: LAMOST native retrain produces a 98 % blue-excess training-bias artifact with only 5.8 % injection-recovery at 5σ (gate FAIL); the tier is retained solely in the inclusive 377,482 total as a “methodological lesson.” This demonstrates that the Path-C native-retrain protocol does not deliver uniform detector performance and undermines the claim of a coherently validated multi-survey product.
[MAJOR] §III A, Table II, §VI D (i): ~98.7 % of the 195,829 DESI anomalies (the dominant contributor) lie on non-science-target spectra (sky fibers, fillers, calibration exposures); only 2,468 (1.3 %) match primary science-target bitmasks. The like-for-like benchmark versus Liang et al. is therefore ≈0.92× (2,468 vs 2,685), not the ~73× or 141× multipliers emphasized for process scale. The headline numbers materially overstate immediate utility for rare-object or cosmology-tracer science.
[MINOR] §II B: Tabular-survey feature scalers (eROSITA, NEOWISE) are fit on the full sample rather than the training split; the bounded robustness check shows ~15–17 % extreme-tail churn (top-298 Jaccard 0.76). While ranking robustness is argued, this introduces avoidable validation-set leakage into the normalization constants used for the canonical S score.
[MINOR] Abstract, §VI D (i): DESI injection-recovery certifies the broad/extended anomaly class at 99–100 % for 5σ per-spectrum strength but explicitly limits narrow single-pixel lines to ≥15σ (the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer sensitivity floor). This critical limitation is not stated in the abstract or headline results.

The central claim that the work delivers a validated multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 sources is only partially supported: per-survey gates (5-fold Jaccard, OOD, injection-recovery) demonstrate reliable flagging of reconstruction outliers for the included tiers (DESI, SDSS, Planck, NEOWISE) and the deduplication script is reproducible, but the aggregate “validated catalog-grade” label does not extend to uniform physical confirmation, uniform detector sensitivity across surveys, or science-target rarity at the headline scale.
