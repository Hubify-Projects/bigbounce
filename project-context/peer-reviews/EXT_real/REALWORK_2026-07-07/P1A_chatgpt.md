## RAW VERBATIM

(2) ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. II A / Eq. (1): The starting "Einstein–Cartan–Holst action" is not a well-defined fundamental variational action, because it inserts a torsion-squared term described as an "on-shell Hehl–Datta shorthand" inside the action while also claiming to vary only the original first-order fields and then integrate torsion out; an on-shell back-substituted contact term cannot simultaneously be part of the off-shell action without double counting or changing the Cartan equation.
[MAJOR] Sec. II A 2 / Eqs. (5)–(7) and Appendix B: The parity-odd "dark-energy" operator has admitted off-shell mass dimension +1, so it is not a legitimate four-dimensional local Lagrangian density as written; the subsequent "single-scale NDA no-go" obtained by inserting missing powers of M_Pl is not a derivation of a physical vacuum energy and cannot establish a channel closure.
[MAJOR] Secs. II C, XII A, Appendix B / Eqs. (10), (11), (26): The manuscript treats a torsion/axial-current quantity that dilutes as a^{-3} as if it can become a w=-1 cosmological-constant contribution; this is internally inconsistent unless a separate mechanism converts the redshifting density into vacuum energy, and no such mechanism is derived.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV "Scope" and Appendix C: The claimed "basis-complete at M_Pl-power-counting level" closure is not established by the Fierz lemma, which only rearranges a restricted four-fermion bilinear basis and does not prove completeness of the diffeomorphism-invariant ECH effective action, boundary/topological sectors, higher-derivative operators, non-minimal matter couplings, or dynamical pseudoscalar completions.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV D / Eqs. (15)–(16): Route 2 is not derived from the cited one-loop Holst literature; the operator ∂μϑ_NY J_5^μ, the matching to photon birefringence, and the comparison ratio to β_obs are introduced as an EFT ansatz, so the claimed many-orders-of-magnitude closure is not a calculation of the channel.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV E / Eqs. (17)–(18): Route 3 mixes an admitted schematic chiral-count beta function with a different Benedetti–Speziale running result, then propagates Δγ/γ to dark energy through an unexplained (H_0/M_Pl) suppression; the key amplitude map from Immirzi running to ρ_Λ is asserted rather than derived.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F: Route 4 is explicitly not closed by amplitude and the manuscript admits that a free-coupling spectator ALP can fit both birefringence and ρ_Λ; relabeling the remaining issue as "naturalness" does not support the stated "four-route closure" except in the weak sense that minimal ECH does not predict the ALP parameters.
[MAJOR] Sec. X: The perturbation-transparency result for canonical scalar matter is essentially the standard statement that scalar matter sources no torsion and that the Holst term is inert on the torsion-free branch; it is correct within its narrow assumptions but too limited and too standard to support the broader dark-energy-channel claims, especially after excluding fermions, dynamical Immirzi fields, propagating torsion, and non-minimal sectors.
[MAJOR] Sec. IX / Table IV: The "13 mechanism-class constraints" are overcounted and heterogeneous: the manuscript itself labels several as heuristic, naturalness-based, shared assumptions, or philosophical observations, so they cannot be aggregated as independent evidence for a no-go theorem.
[MAJOR] Secs. I B, III, V, VII, XIII, XV: Many numerical and observational claims rely on unpublished companion papers, internal MCMC chains, future/concurrent submissions, or repository artifacts not reproduced in the manuscript; these cannot be used to support a Physical Review D submission's scientific claims without the relevant derivations and validation being present.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C 1: The reheating "thermal reset" argument is conditional on uncomputed rates Γ_wash>H, mixes several processes with different temperature domains, and is later used rhetorically as a barrier despite being explicitly left to future work.
[MAJOR] Sec. XIV D: The claimed structural incompatibility between N_tot≃92 and the matter-bounce f_NL signal is a qualitative mode-history argument without a transfer-function calculation, yet it is used as a strong conclusion about erasure at SPHEREx scales.
