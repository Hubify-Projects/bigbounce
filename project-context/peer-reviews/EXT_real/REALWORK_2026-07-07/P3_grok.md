## RAW VERBATIM

VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract, §I (Introduction), and §III (catalog structure): The headline claim of a "validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies" (and the title's framing of 268,519 validated sources) is presented as the primary deliverable, yet the text explicitly defines this count as a process-volume figure from the full instrument stream. Only ~1.3% (2,468) of deduplicated DESI anomaly clusters match validated science-target spectra (BGS/LRG/ELG/QSO/MWS bitmask), ≈0.92× the prior Liang et al. single-survey benchmark on a like-for-like basis; ~98.7% fall on sky-fiber/filler spectra. Validation (5-fold Jaccard J̄_CV=0.862, OOD J=0.732, injection-recovery) certifies sensitivity only to the broad/continuum-dominated class; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ (a stated sensitivity floor). This scope mismatch between title/abstract framing and the carefully caveated body text risks overstating immediate discovery power.
[MAJOR] §III E (eROSITA) and production provenance (§II B, §III G): The eROSITA tier (298 objects) is excised from every count (including the inclusive 377,482) because its production score axis (threshold 0.259) cannot be reproduced from any committed artifact (fails all 16 monotone rescalings tested and 3 IsolationForest retrains; raw scores are non-monotone, Spearman ρ=−0.10). The initial Gaia tier was entirely synthetic placeholder output. While the paper supplies a committed membership-list reproduction script and excises both tiers, these production-run provenance failures indicate gaps in end-to-end pipeline integrity and documentation that must be exhaustively audited, with explicit mitigation steps and release artifacts demonstrating that the retained (DESI/SDSS/Planck/NEOWISE) chains are free of analogous undocumented steps.
[MAJOR] §V (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL analysis on the 5,384 QSO-candidate sample returns α_jk=0.19±0.65 (0.29σ from null) with no improvement over the single-tracer baseline (σ(fNL) envelope [3.92, 8.98] after noise-driven shift); the NANOGrav 15-yr free-spectrum MCMC yields γ=2.567±0.382 (matter-bounce prediction at +1.13σ; decisive only vs. idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference). Both are correctly labeled non-detections and "secondary methodological demonstrations." Their presence in the main text as catalog "applications" adds no new constraint and dilutes focus; they should be moved to an appendix or condensed to a single paragraph noting consistency checks only.
[MINOR] §II B (Training and Scoring) and §III: Feature scalers for tabular surveys (eROSITA 47 features, NEOWISE 15 features) are fit on the full sample rather than the training split. The eROSITA robustness check (top-298 Jaccard 0.76) is within model-retrain churn, but this remains non-standard practice that can allow tail information into normalization constants. The paper should either refit strictly on the training split for the released pipelines or provide a stronger quantitative bound showing no material re-ranking of the extreme tail used for catalog selection.
[MINOR] §IV A (SIMBAD cross-match and novelty): The 17.8% (178/1,000; Wilson 68% CI ±1.2%) genuine novelty fraction is quantified only for the DESI top-1,000 via CDS X-Match against 18 catalogs. No equivalent rate is given for the full 268k validated set or other surveys. SIMBAD-unmatched fractions (up to ~99% for DESI top-10k) substantially overstate novelty because SIMBAD does not individually index most wide-field photometric detections (100% archival-ID rate recovered in NED+VizieR for tested SDSS/eROSITA/NEOWISE subsets). The paper must either supply a full-catalog novelty estimate or explicitly limit novelty claims to the quantified DESI top stratum and state the database-coverage caveat in every summary table/figure.
[MINOR] §III D and Table I (LAMOST handling): LAMOST native retrain is retained in the inclusive 377,482 grand total (and flagged as exploratory) despite 5.8% injection-recovery at 5σ (gate FAIL) and the documented 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact in the cross-transfer scan. While the validated headline (268,519) correctly excludes it, the contribution to the grand total and all summary statistics must be separated even more explicitly (e.g., a dedicated "exploratory-only" column or footnote) to prevent inadvertent inclusion by readers.

One sentence on the central claim: The central claim that a multi-survey autoencoder anomaly catalog of 268,519 unique sources has been produced and validated (via per-survey native retrains, 5-fold/OOD Jaccard gates, and injection-recovery for the broad anomaly class) with committed reproducibility scripts for the headline deduplication is supported by the detailed per-survey results, held-out tests, and excision of irreproducible tiers, once the process-volume framing and scope limitations stated in the text are read precisely.
