Gemini said Recommendation: MAJOR REVISIONSOverviewThis manuscript presents a highly ambitious, pioneering effort to execute unsupervised anomaly detection at a multi-survey scale, scanning 37.3 million astronomical sources and CMB map patches using the BIGAE (BigBounce Integrated Galaxy Autoencoder) framework. The author provides a commendable level of structural transparency, explicitly detailing major pipeline failure modes—such as the 98% blue-excess training bias in the LAMOST DR10 tier and the unrecoverable provenance of the eROSITA production score axis—rather than sweeping them under the rug. Furthermore, the candor regarding the use of an agentic AI research pipeline sets an excellent, honest precedent for future AI-assisted data science publications.However, the paper currently contains outstanding technical contradictions and incomplete analyses that must be resolved before it can be accepted for publication in a premier journal. Most notably, a core robustness check is left explicitly "queued," and the definition of the primary "validated" catalog count artificially bundles a rigorous detector-sensitivity check with a geometric masking gate that passes by construction.StrengthsUnprecedented Scale and Ambition: The work successfully scales unsupervised outlier detection across six distinct major archives (DESI, SDSS, LAMOST, eROSITA, Planck, and NEOWISE), moving past the sub-million scale of prior single-survey studies.Exemplary Reproducibility Standards: The explicit inclusion of file hashes, specific script paths within the repository (e.g., reproduce_headline_dedup.py), and a public Hugging Face release manifest provides an ironclad audit trail that makes the catalog genuinely recomputable.Rigorous Cosmological Scoping: The author resists the urge to claim false cosmological discoveries. The treatment of the convex mapping in the fNL​ Fisher forecast correctly prioritizes the broad 1σ envelope over an optimistic central value, and the NANOGrav spectral-index interpretation carefully addresses environmental flattening variables for supermassive black hole binaries (SMBHBs).BLOCKERS (Must Fix Before Publication)1.
