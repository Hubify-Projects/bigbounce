Thought for 47sReferee Report: MNRAS / Phys. Rev. D / JCAP
Paper: Environmental Dependence of Spiral Chirality: A DESIVAST Three-Algorithm Void Null Test on 56,981 DESI DR1 Spirals
Author: Houston Golden
Version: v0.1.110 (2026-07-09), 40 pages
Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS
Summary
This is a clean, high-quality null-result paper testing whether spiral-galaxy chirality (CW/CCW) shows environment dependence in DESI DR1, using the public DESIVAST void catalog (three algorithms + catalog-native zones) as the primary anchor and a T-Web tidal-tensor classification as a secondary diagnostic. The headline result is a well-powered, definition-family null on the void/non-void contrast at the ≈0.5–0.6 pp sensitivity level (DESIVAST VoidFinder primary: ∆f_CW = +0.0007, z_∆ = +0.31, p_∆ = 0.76 on n_void = 56,981). All five Bonferroni-5 DESIVAST estimators are null, the T-Web secondary path is null after monopole subtraction, and an extensive suite of robustness checks (Phase-2 (R_s, λ_th) sweep, redshift/density/sky-position scans, program splits, cross-survey Tempel/ASTRA overlays, fixed-geometry RSD Monte Carlo) consistently support the conclusion. The paper is exceptionally transparent about post-hoc primary-path designation, garden-of-forking-paths exposure (analysis-tree table), fixed-redshift-space nature of the bound, and the fact that the quoted envelope is an approximate sensitivity figure (counting statistics + dominant membership systematic), not a complete systematic budget. Reproducibility infrastructure (committed deterministic pipeline at tag v0.1.110-2026-07-09, per-output provenance JSON, verification drivers, public GitHub + pending DOI snapshot) is outstanding. The companion Paper IV labels are public (CC-BY-4.0) and the headline ∆f_CW contrast is algebraically invariant to the catalog monopole. No positive environmental signal is claimed; the result is presented as a controlled-sample upper bound.
BLOCKERS (must fix before publication)
None.
MAJORS (should fix)
Abstract and §I (headline sensitivity language) — The ≈0.5–0.6 pp figure is described as an “approximate DR1 sensitivity envelope (not a complete systematic budget)” in the body, but the abstract and opening paragraphs of §I could be tightened by one sentence to make explicit that this is counting statistics + the empirically bounded fixed-void-geometry membership term only, and that other systematics (footprint, match radius, classifier confidence) have been shown sub-dominant but are not folded into a single quadrature envelope. Proposed fix: add a parenthetical clause in the abstract headline result sentence and in the first paragraph of §I, e.g. “(≈0.5–0.6 pp approximate sensitivity envelope after folding the dominant fixed-void-geometry membership systematic; see §VIII for full breakdown)”. This removes any residual risk that a reader treats the number as a formal combined systematic uncertainty.
§V B (primary-path declaration and garden-of-forking-paths) — The post-hoc nature of the DESIVAST primary designation and the few-dozen-trial analysis tree are already disclosed with exemplary clarity and the Bonferroni-5 family null is correctly identified as the robust quotable statement. However, one additional short sentence would further insulate the paper: explicitly state that any single most-favorable row (e.g., the VoidFinder k=20 result) should not be quoted in isolation and that the family-wise null across all five estimators is the controlled claim. Proposed fix: one sentence at the end of the primary-path paragraph in §V B.
These are the only two items that rise above minor polish; both are straightforward wording clarifications that align the front matter even more tightly with the already-excellent scoping language in the body.

In Appendix B (toy EFT mapping), the scoping language (“speculative guide … not a derived empirical result”) is already strong; consider adding one extra italicized sentence at the very top of the appendix reiterating that the operator form is heuristic and outside the empirical scope of the paper. Strengths
Transparency and scoping discipline are at the highest level seen in recent DESI cosmology papers. The post-hoc primary designation, analysis-tree disclosure (Table III), fixed-redshift-space caveat, and explicit statement that the 0.5–0.6 pp figure is an approximate sensitivity envelope (not a complete budget) are model examples of how to handle garden-of-forking-paths and systematic-floor issues honestly.
Reproducibility infrastructure is exceptional: deterministic seed (20260515), committed pipeline at a frozen GitHub tag, per-output provenance JSON, verification recompute drivers, and a pending DOI-minted Zenodo snapshot. Every quoted number is independently regenerable by a reader. This sets a high bar for the field.
Statistical and robustness framework is rigorous and appropriately conservative: Jeffreys binomial intervals, label-shuffle permutation look-elsewhere corrections (with both free and imaging-leg×program stratified variants), monopole-referenced residuals (σ_vs monopole), Bonferroni family control on the primary DESIVAST estimators, Phase-2 hyperparameter sweep, and multiple orthogonal cross-checks (sky-position stratification by maximal-void density, program splits, cross-survey Tempel/ASTRA overlays). The primary DESIVAST result is properly powered (n_void = 56,981) and internally cross-validated across three independent void-finding algorithms plus two catalog-native zone definitions.
The paper correctly anchors the headline claim on the volume-limited DESIVAST BGS path (where target-program mixing is minimized) rather than the survey-edge-contaminated T-Web void bin, and it explicitly flags the residual bright/dark sign-flip in the secondary T-Web filament class as a diagnostic for future DR2 + Rubin work rather than over-interpreting it.
Overall assessment: This is a carefully executed, transparently reported null result that meets the standards of MNRAS / Phys. Rev. D / JCAP. The two major items above are minor wording tightenings that will make an already strong manuscript even tighter; they do not affect any scientific conclusion. With those addressed, I recommend acceptance. The community will benefit from both the scientific bound and the methodological example this paper sets for controlled null tests on public large-scale-structure catalogs.
