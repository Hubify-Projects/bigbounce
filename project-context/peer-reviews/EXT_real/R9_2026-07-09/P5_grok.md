CENTRAL CLAIM: Spiral galaxy chirality exhibits no detectable void/non-void environmental dependence in DESI DR1, with the primary DESIVAST-anchored test (56,981 void spirals) returning a void-vs-non-void CW-fraction contrast of ∆f_CW = +0.0007 (z_∆ ≈ +0.31, p_∆ = 0.76) that is statistically indistinguishable from parity and consistent across all five DESIVAST void definitions (|∆f_CW| ≤ 0.004, |z_∆| ≤ 1.25); this constitutes a bounded null (effective |∆f_CW| ≲ 0.5–0.6 pp at ~2σ after counting statistics plus fixed-geometry membership systematic) with no evidence for environment-dependent handedness at current sensitivity. The claim is supported by the multi-algorithm cross-checks, Phase-2 (R_s, λ_th) sweeps, label-shuffle permutation nulls with look-elsewhere correction, monopole-invariant contrast statistic, explicit post-hoc disclosure, GZ1 validation of labels, and extensive robustness tests; the paper does not overclaim independence but correctly frames the result as an empirical upper bound on any future bounce-chirality coupling models at the probed scale.

ISSUES:

Post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored path as the single primary estimand (§V B, Table III). The paper explicitly flags that “no timestamped plan predates the data” and supplies three result-independent a priori justifications (largest powered public peer-reviewed DR1 VAC; three void-finding algorithms built in; volume-limited low-z BGS anchor that removes target-program mixing). It further shows the Bonferroni-5 family null holds uniformly across all five estimators and that the full analysis tree (~dozen+ descriptive tests) yields no significant signals after correction. This disclosure and uniform-null argument are adequate and transparent for PRD. However, in a null-result paper whose headline bound is tied to one chosen path, even principled post-hoc selection still leaves a residual garden-of-forking-paths concern. A short additional sentence stating how a timestamped plan would be implemented for DR2 follow-up (or explicitly presenting the tightest bound as representative of the family rather than uniquely privileged) would eliminate any lingering perception of selective emphasis.
Quoted effective bound is not a complete systematic error budget (acknowledged by the authors). The text correctly states that “the tabulated CI is not a full systematic error budget” and folds only counting statistics (SE(∆) ≈ 0.0022) plus the ±0.34–0.37 pp fixed-void-geometry membership systematic from the σ_v/(aH) = 5 Mpc/h finger-of-god Monte Carlo. Other plausible contributions (void-center/R_eff uncertainties beyond the point-in-sphere vs. maximal-sphere test already performed, residual 1″ matching or photo-z effects, or any subtle environment-correlated classifier-confidence variations) are either checked at the “mild sensitivity” level or argued to be sub-dominant but are not propagated into a consolidated budget. For a precision bounded-null claim this is a minor but genuine gap. A brief supplementary paragraph or small table quantifying that the next-leading systematics are << 0.3 pp (or an explicit statement that they do not alter the 0.5–0.6 pp effective 2σ envelope) would close the issue without requiring new heavy computation.
Residual bright/dark target-program sign-flip (~2.1σ in the filament class) and its propagation into the primary result. The paper honestly discloses this structure (§VI D), supports the BGS-selection-function / imaging-leg systematic interpretation with the contingency test (χ² = 4933, Cramér’s V = 0.078), logistic regression (T-Web coefficients stable after program control), and the fact that the catalog-wide monopole is bright-program dominated. It also correctly notes that the primary DESIVAST path (volume-limited BGS z ≤ 0.24) is constructed to be far less sensitive to this residual than the full T-Web run. The disclosure and future-data call (DR2 + Rubin) are adequate. However, without a quantitative end-to-end propagation test (injecting the known per-leg bias into mocks and re-measuring the void contrast), a small leakage into the primary ∆f_CW at a level comparable to the quoted bound cannot be entirely ruled out. Adding even a short mock-based validation paragraph would remove the “residual ambiguity” caveat and strengthen reader confidence that the primary null is robust to this known systematic.

The paper is otherwise rigorous, well-scoped, and exemplary in reproducibility (Appendix D commits the exact manuscript tag v0.1.107-2026-07-07, lists all artifacts, provides a reproducibility checklist, and supplies contingency tables for independent χ² recomputation). The speculative toy EFT mapping in Appendix B is clearly labeled as non-empirical and outside the scope of the observational claims. These three issues are the only substantive points that require minor attention; once addressed, the manuscript will meet PRD standards for a clean, bounded null result in large-scale structure.
