CENTRAL CLAIM: The large-scale projected chirality dipole of spiral galaxies is consistent with null in the largest bias-hardened catalog to date (8.47 M DESI Legacy DR8 galaxies, 3.20 M spirals classified by a flip-equivariant ViT-Small with dedicated NOT_SPIRAL class). The primary real-space dipole estimator on the pre-specified high-confidence subsample (N ≈ 9.5 × 10^5 spirals, p_eq > 0.6) yields +0.41σ (empirical rank p = 0.31 vs. isotropic pixel-permutation null), while a block-bootstrap WLS template fit on the full canonical-mask field disfavors a clean cosmological dipole at Shamir’s lower reference amplitude (1.7% in f_CW units, A_ref = 0.034 in A_p units) at z ≈ −18 under the adopted spatial covariance; the MASTER ℓ = 1 pseudo-C_ℓ residuals (+3.64σ canonical) are attributed to survey systematics (monopole–mask leakage + coherent depth/morphology) via an eight-anchor battery and forward modeling (~52–54% of the residual amplitude reproduced in the correct direction), not interpreted as a cosmological detection. This is supported by the evidence for the two primary estimators; the harmonic channel is transparently treated as diagnostic only.

The paper is a careful, reproducible null-result analysis that meets PRD standards for observational isotropy/parity tests. It provides the community with the largest publicly released chirality-labeled catalog (three tiers on HuggingFace with model weights, full provenance artifacts, and explicit QC flags), demonstrates the necessity of equivariant post-processing (raw Catalog A produces a spurious 2.31σ real-space dipole from only 0.79% classifier bias modulated by survey depth; equivariant TTA collapses it to 0.41σ), and includes a decisive model-free GZ1-human-label cross-check (z = −0.54σ). Limitations are disclosed rather than minimized (MASTER is not an independent cosmological null; σ values derive from distinct null procedures and are not directly comparable; ~47% of the canonical ℓ = 1 residual amplitude remains unmodeled but is bounded below the real-space sensitivity floor; no frequentist exclusion of Shamir’s distinct Ganalyzer estimator is claimed). The work advances the field by tightening the methodological bar for future chirality-dipole searches.

ISSUES:

Statistical phrasing of the block-bootstrap template disfavor (most important for precision and to avoid misreading by readers in the isotropy/parity literature). The z ≈ −18 value (Appendix D g, Table XIV, Fig. 10) is a template-model disfavor statistic under the NSIDE = 8 block-bootstrap spatial covariance (N_boot = 1000; σ_boot inflated 14.7× over naive WLS), not a calibrated frequentist exclusion significance. The paper correctly caveats this language (“disfavors,” not “excludes at 18σ”; naive WLS z = −264 is explicitly superseded) and anchors the primary cosmological claim on the real-space HC dipole (+0.41σ) plus the empirical injection-recovery falsification boundary (A_50 ≈ 0.75%, A_95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%]; Sec. VI B). However, for PRD standards in this subfield, the distinction should be stated more explicitly in the abstract, Sec. IV C (primary results), and Conclusions b: e.g., one additional sentence noting that a formal joint likelihood exclusion would require a Gaussian-process spatial model with simultaneous per-galaxy classifier-noise propagation (flagged as future work in Sec. VII). The injection-recovery A_95 bracket already supplies the clean frequentist falsification criterion for the primary real-space estimator and should be cross-referenced when the z ≈ −18 number is quoted. This is a clarity/presentation issue, not a flaw in the analysis itself.
Unmodeled remainder in the canonical-mask ℓ = 1 residual (~47% of |a_1| = 6.95 × 10^{-3}). The eight-anchor battery (Appendix D: apodized-mask robustness, ℓ = 2 > ℓ = 1 broadband structure, quality-quartile washout with all |σ| < 1 and no monotonic trend, leg-proxy cross-power ~25%, density-stratified null, boundary-distance uniformity, WLS template disfavor, and direct cross-spectrum r_ℓ=2 = −0.65 at −2.89σ) plus the imaging + per-galaxy DR8 morphology forward model (reproducing ~52–54% of the amplitude in the correct direction, cos θ ≈ +0.83–0.84) convincingly disfavor interpretation (i) — a clean primordial dipole. The paper is explicit that even the full residual maps to an equivalent real-space amplitude (A_p ≈ 0.695%) below A_50 and far below A_95, and that the direct real-space HC dipole is null; the bounding argument is therefore conservative and correct. The physical origin of the remaining ~47% (per-galaxy classifier confidence-vs-depth response beyond pixel-level templates) is identified as the leading candidate and left as a “genuine open item” for a future full per-galaxy DR8 morphology pull. This disclosure is adequate and honest; a single tightening sentence in Sec. IV D or Appendix D j quantifying why this channel is not expected to coherently source a dipole exceeding the already-bounded remainder would make the systematics section fully self-contained for PRD readers without requiring new computation.
Scope of the amplitude-level tension with Shamir (minor clarification to preempt misreading). The paper correctly reports an amplitude-level tension of ~7–18× (in consistent A_p units) under the present ViT/TTA pipeline and explicitly states that this is not a frequentist exclusion of Shamir’s distinct Ganalyzer estimator on a matched footprint (Sec. V A, Conclusions b; calls for the required reanalysis). This is fair and non-overreaching. A brief parenthetical in Sec. V A (or the abstract/introduction when the tension factor is first quoted) noting that differences in classifier architecture/training labels/footprint may contribute (consistent with the Iye et al. 2021 critique already cited) would further insulate the claim from potential misinterpretation by readers focused on the ongoing Shamir controversy. No change to the scientific conclusion is needed.

Additional minor notes (all addressed or disclosed in the manuscript):

The MASTER channel is correctly scoped as diagnostic only (Sec. III B, IV D, Appendix A); the primary cosmological statements rest on the real-space HC dipole and block-bootstrap WLS fit.
Pseudo-label independence (66.5% CE-ResNet) is addressed by the decisive GZ1-human-only cross-check (z = −0.54σ on N ≈ 4.6 × 10^4 confident spirals); the shuffle-null limitation is explicitly bounded.
Edge-on contamination (empirically 15.8% at b/a < 0.3) produces dilution, not directional bias, under flip-equivariant TTA; the argmax tie-break coherence on borderline galaxies is bounded and leg-selective (depth systematic signature), not isotropic.
Reproducibility is exemplary (committed artifacts with checksums, explicit QC flag for the 2.9% flip-identity violators, clear usage notes on the 0.26% spatially uniform monopole residual). The pending Zenodo DOI at submission is standard; the live commit hash already serves as an interim citable handle.
No unsupported claims, statistical errors in the primary estimators, or missing reproducibility elements.

The manuscript is ready for PRD after the three clarifications above. It sets a high methodological bar for future chirality/isotropy analyses and supplies a valuable public resource.
