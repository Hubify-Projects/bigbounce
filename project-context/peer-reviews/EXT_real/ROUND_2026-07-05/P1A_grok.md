VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and Sec. I (Introduction), claim of “channel-level amplitude closure” of four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes: The abstract and opening paragraphs frame the result as a substantive no-go constraining the minimal-ECH route to late-time dark energy, yet the body (Scope paragraphs in Sec. IV and IV G) repeatedly qualifies that this is only an illustrative enumeration of four commonly discussed channels, not a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis, and that R2–R3 closures rest on explicitly labeled phenomenological upper-bound EFT ansätze rather than derivations extracted from the cited works (Mercuri 2009; Shapiro & Teixeira 2010; Benedetti & Speziale 2009/2010). This mismatch between framing and actual logical strength requires explicit rewriting.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV D (Route 2) and Sec. IV E (Route 3), operator construction and coefficient treatment: The parity-odd one-loop operator in Eq. (15) and the chiral running ansatz in Eq. (17) are introduced as “phenomenological upper-bound EFT ansatz” or “schematically motivated by but not literally derived from” the referenced literature. The subsequent amplitude budgets (∼60 orders of suppression) therefore close only the specific ansatz chosen, not the physical route itself. The paper must either (a) derive the precise coefficients from the cited papers or (b) re-label the closures as conditional on the stated ansätze throughout the abstract, introduction, and conclusions.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F (Route 4) and Sec. II C, treatment of R4 as “naturalness objection rather than amplitude no-go”: The argument correctly shows that matching both β_obs and ρ_Λ simultaneously requires m_θ ∼ H_0 (or an equivalent tuning of α/M). However, the text presents this as closing the route while simultaneously noting that a free-coupling spectator-ALP fit can reproduce both observables. The logical status is therefore an explanatory-deficit / fine-tuning objection internal to the ECH+ALP identification, not an amplitude exclusion of the operator. This distinction must be stated unambiguously in the abstract and Sec. IV G.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result), scope and completeness of proof: The result (torsion vanishes and Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor EOM for canonical scalar matter via the algebraic Bianchi identity) is correctly restricted to the torsion-free branch, excluding propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, fermion spin density, and non-minimal couplings. The provided text gives only a high-level statement, scalar-sector sketch, and tensor extension; the full algebraic verification that the Holst dual contraction vanishes identically on the Levi-Civita connection at all perturbation orders must be expanded with explicit metric + scalar perturbation expansions (or a clear reference to a self-contained appendix) so that a referee can audit the claim without external material.
[MAJOR] Sec. IX (Structural Constraints / 14-barrier catalog) and cross-references to “Foundations A–G” and “Branches H, J, L, M, N, O”: The catalog is presented as a central result, yet the excerpt provides only one-sentence titles for each barrier. If the detailed derivations of Barriers 1–14 reside in un-provided foundation studies or companion manuscripts, the paper is not self-contained for the “13 distinct mechanism-class constraints” claim. Either the key steps for each barrier must be included or the catalog must be explicitly labeled as a summary whose individual entries are audited elsewhere.
[MINOR] Sec. I B, II, and throughout, dependence on in-preparation companions for all numerical values (Table II, MCMC posteriors, f_NL forecasts, β benchmark): While the paper correctly states that core closure logic does not load-bear on these numbers, the repeated importation of specific values (H_0 = 67.68 ± 1.06, ΔN_eff = −0.020 ± 0.169, SPHEREx forecasts, etc.) and the long justificatory Table II create the appearance that the observational program is integral. All such numbers should be moved to a clearly marked “illustrative context only” subsection or removed if not required for the structural claims.
[MINOR] Sec. XIV D (Structural Tension: Dark Energy vs. Bounce f_NL): The e-fold erasure argument (N_tot ≈ 92 pushing matter-bounce modes deep into the inflationary sub-horizon regime) is logically sound under the paper’s own NDA bookkeeping, but f_NL = −35/8 itself is stated to be a class-level result derived in a companion with no ECH input. The tension is therefore between the paper’s fitted N_tot (required to match ρ_Λ via the dimensional ansatz) and a separate class prediction, not an internal ECH inconsistency. The wording must be tightened to avoid implying that ECH itself predicts or erases f_NL.
[MINOR] Overall length, repetition, and readability (37 pages): The manuscript contains extensive self-referential “Scope” paragraphs, repeated caveats, and long executive-summary tables. While the caveats are intellectually honest, they render the central logical thread difficult to follow. Substantial condensation (especially Secs. IV, IX, and XIV) is required for PRD standards.

One sentence on whether the central claim is supported by the evidence presented: The channel-level amplitude assessments for the four enumerated routes are internally consistent within the explicitly stated assumptions and ansätze (R1 by standard torsion elimination, R2/R3 by large-margin suppression under the chosen EFT operators, R4 by naturalness), and the perturbation-transparency result follows directly from the algebraic Bianchi identity on the torsion-free Levi-Civita connection for canonical scalar matter; however, the paper does not establish a complete operator-basis no-go or a first-principles derivation of observed-scale dark energy from minimal ECH dynamics.
