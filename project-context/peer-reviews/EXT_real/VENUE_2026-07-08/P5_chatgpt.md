(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] §XIII / Appendix A / Paper IV dependency: the analysis depends on per-galaxy chirality labels and classifier validation from a concurrently submitted companion paper with arXiv/DOI placeholders; MNRAS acceptance should require Paper IV to be available, citable, and co-reviewed, or the present paper must reproduce enough classifier validation and catalog provenance to stand alone.

[MAJOR] §V B / primary-path designation: the DESIVAST analysis is explicitly post-hoc, and the manuscript admits a few-dozen-trial analysis tree; the Bonferroni-5 treatment of only five DESIVAST rows does not fully account for the broader path selection that led to declaring DESIVAST primary after inspecting multiple failed/diagnostic environment classifiers.

[MAJOR] §VIII B–E / DESIVAST void membership: the primary VoidFinder membership uses an “any hole sphere” point-in-sphere proxy rather than a unique catalog-native galaxy membership definition; the maximal-sphere and GALZONE checks help, but the quoted ∼0.5–0.6 pp bound is still not a clean official DESIVAST void-membership constraint.

[MAJOR] §IV, §VI, §IX A, §XIII / T-Web analysis: the T-Web classifier is heavily compromised by radial selection, redshift-space distortions, boundary conditions, and survey-shell geometry; it should be demoted further or removed from the headline narrative rather than repeatedly presented as a secondary “concurrence.”

[MAJOR] §VIII / systematic error budget: the reported counting CI is clear, but the full uncertainty model for void membership, classifier label dilution, environment-dependent label confusion, cross-match purity, RSD, and selection-function residuals is not propagated into a single defensible bound; the claimed effective 2σ limit is therefore under-justified.

[MAJOR] Appendix A / classifier labels: the quoted ∼70% binary accuracy and 21.4% borderline D4 instability raise a serious dilution and possible environment-dependent misclassification concern; no environment-stratified human-label confusion matrix is provided, so the null could partly reflect classifier noise rather than astrophysical absence.

[MINOR] Abstract and §XV: the manuscript is overlong and overqualified for a null result; the headline should be simplified to the DESIVAST void/non-void contrast, with T-Web, Tempel, ASTRA, and toy-EFT material moved to appendices or substantially shortened.

[MINOR] Appendix B: the toy EFT mapping is speculative, weakly connected to the data, and risks distracting from the observational result; it should be removed or clearly labelled as non-refereed speculation.

[MINOR] References / data availability: placeholder arXiv identifiers, pending Zenodo DOI language, and internal artifact IDs are not publication-ready; final immutable links and exact scripts/data products are required before acceptance.

(3) The central DESIVAST void/non-void null is broadly supported as a non-detection at current sensitivity, but the precision of the quoted bound and its independence from classifier and membership systematics are not yet established strongly enough for acceptance.
