Quick Executive Summary (ready to copy for arXiv/X/abstract)

Headline: Spiral galaxy chirality (CW/CCW fraction from the public Paper IV equivariant ViT catalog) shows no statistically significant dependence on large-scale environment in DESI DR1.

Primary result (DESIVAST-anchored, declared upfront): On 56,981 VoidFinder void spirals (volume-limited low-z BGS, n = 678,945 parent), the void/non-void contrast is ∆f_CW = +0.0007 (SE ≈ 0.0022 counting-only; z∆ = +0.31, p∆ = 0.76). Secondary T-Web (full-DR1 tidal-tensor on 14.6M galaxies): Also null after monopole subtraction (|σ_vs monopole| < 1.15 across all four classes {void, wall, filament, cluster}; omnibus χ² p = 0.31–0.39); small void bin (n=428) is explicitly power-limited and survey-edge dominated at low-z. Phase-2 sensitivity sweep (9 cells in Rs/λth), redshift/density/sky-position scans, Tempel FoF, ASTRA EDR, bright/dark program splits, and HEALPix all return consistent nulls within look-elsewhere corrections.

The contrast is algebraically invariant to the Paper IV classifier monopole (–0.26 pp, independently recovered internally), uses only public labels/data for the headline, and is presented as a controlled-sample upper bound (redshift-space statement) rather than a positive claim of perfect independence. All artifacts, deterministic seeds, pipeline tag (v0.1.107-2026-07-07), and self-contained Paper IV provenance summary are there — reproducibility is nailed.
