ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Dependency on Unpublished Labels (Section II, Appendix A): The entire analysis, including the baseline -0.26% classifier-monopole offset, relies on galaxy chirality labels from a concurrently submitted manuscript ("Paper IV"). While the author transparently states that acceptance should be conditional on Paper IV, the core label validity and equivariance cannot be fully refereed without that foundational manuscript being accepted and peer-reviewed.

[MAJOR] Redshift-Space Distortions in T-Web (Section IV, XIII): The T-Web cosmic-web classification is performed in observed redshift space rather than real space. While the DESIVAST primary path bounds this sensitivity via a fixed-void-geometry check , the T-Web eigenvalues are highly susceptible to anisotropic eigenvalue deformation from finger-of-god and Kaiser effects. A real-space reconstruction is necessary to robustly validate the T-Web class boundaries.

[MAJOR] Target-Program Mixing Ambiguity (Section VI.D): The filament class exhibits a 2.1 standard deviation sign-flip between BGS-bright and dark targets. The author acknowledges this as a residual structure that current data cannot cleanly partition between a selection-function-only origin and a true astrophysical signal. A quantitative selection-function mock is required to confirm this is purely an imaging systematic.
