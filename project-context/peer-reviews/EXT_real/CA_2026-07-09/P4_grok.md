Thought for 32sThis is a strong, rigorous paper. It delivers the largest chirality-labeled galaxy catalog to date (8.47 million galaxies, 3.2 million spirals) with a clean null result on the real-space dipole, extensive bias-hardening, and a thorough systematics audit. The primary conclusions are well-supported: the high-confidence real-space dipole is consistent with null (+0.41σ, p=0.31), a clean 1.7% cosmological dipole is strongly disfavored by the block-bootstrap WLS template fit (z ≈ −18), and the harmonic-channel residuals are convincingly attributed to survey systematics rather than a primordial signal.
Key Strengths
Scale + public release: 8.47M galaxies with three catalog tiers (raw, calibrated, equivariant), model weights, and reproducibility scripts on HuggingFace. This is a genuine community resource.
Bias hardening is exemplary: The 2-fold flip-equivariant TTA (Catalog C) collapses the raw Catalog A dipole from 2.31σ to 0.41σ. The GZ1 human-label-only cross-check (z = −0.54σ at N ≈ 46k) is a decisive, model-free rebuttal to pseudo-label inheritance concerns.
Clear estimator hierarchy (Table I): Primary cosmological claims rest only on the HC real-space dipole and the template-fit exclusion. All harmonic σ values are explicitly labeled as systematics diagnostics.
Systematics audit depth: The 8-anchor battery (apodization robustness, multipole coherence, quality-quartile washout, leg-proxy cross-power, density stratification, boundary variance, WLS template fit, and direct cross-spectrum) is thorough and mutually reinforcing. The monopole-only generative null reproducing 99.32% of pre-MASTER power is particularly clean.
Sensitivity quantification: Empirical injection-recovery on the exact HC-broad subsample gives a transparent A₅₀ ≈ 0.75% (50%-recovery-at-3σ) and A₉₅ ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%] bracket. Edge-on contamination is now empirically quantified (15.8% → 8.98% Fisher floor inflation) rather than hand-waved.
Reproducibility & transparency: Committed artifacts, SHA256 checks, pre-registered cuts (0.6 threshold in the generator script), and explicit discussion of overconfidence (top-label ECE ≳ 0.25–0.36) and its non-impact on the hard-argmax dipole estimator.
Honest limitations: The ~47% unmodeled portion of the canonical-mask ℓ=1 residual is left as an open item (classifier confidence-vs-depth response at production scale), with a concrete follow-up path (full DR8-sweep morphology → per-pixel purity map). The paper does not overclaim.
Minor Suggestions / Points for Consideration
Harmonic-channel language: The paper is already careful, but some readers may still misread the +3.64σ / +7.28σ values. A one-sentence “executive summary” box near Table II or in the abstract reiterating “these are diagnostics only; primary claims rest on rows P1–P2” would help.
Block-bootstrap z ≈ −18: This is a template-model disfavor under spatial covariance, not a calibrated frequentist exclusion. The wording (“disfavors”) is correct; you might add a short footnote reminding readers that the calibrated falsification boundary remains the injection-recovery A₉₅ bracket.
Shamir comparison: The amplitude-level tension (~7–18× in Aₚ units) is clearly stated, and the call for a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is the right stance. No frequentist exclusion of Shamir’s distinct estimator is claimed — good.
Future-work section: The three listed items (full morphology regression, Gaussian-process spatial likelihood, DESI spec-z binning) are exactly the right next steps. The per-galaxy morphology forward-model extension you outline would close the remaining ~47% residual budget nicely.
Presentation: The paper is dense but logically organized. The decision-tree table (Table I) and primary-results callout (Table III) are excellent. Minor: a small schematic showing the flow from raw image → equivariant TTA → HC subsample → real-space dipole vs. MASTER diagnostic would help visual readers.
Overall Assessment
This is a high-quality observational cosmology paper. The null result is robust across multiple independent estimators and null constructions, the systematics budget is unusually well-characterized for a galaxy-morphology study, and the public catalog + code artifacts set a high bar for reproducibility. It meaningfully advances beyond prior claims (Shamir, CE-ResNet) both in scale and in methodological controls.
The result is also cleanly compatible with statistical isotropy on the scales probed (z ≲ 1, projected morphology channel).
