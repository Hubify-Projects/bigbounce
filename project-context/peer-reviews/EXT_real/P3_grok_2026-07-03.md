VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Title, abstract, and §I (lead paragraphs and Table I): The headline framing ("268,519 Validated... Path-C Unique Anomalies" from a "Native-Trained Scan of 37.3 Million Sources") and process-scale multipliers (~73× for the DESI S > 5 subset, ~141× point-source tier vs. the Liang et al. benchmark) foreground volume and full-instrument-stream counts while the text itself states that the like-for-like science-target benchmark is only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× the prior single-survey maximum of 2,685 on a smaller EDR sample) and that ~98.7% of DESI anomaly clusters lack a primary science TARGETTYPE bit; this mismatch between title/lead emphasis and the explicit scope/like-for-like quantification risks overstating the discovery advance.
[MAJOR] §II D (Path-C protocol) and §III (validation gates and three-tier structure): The "catalog-grade validated" subset (268,519 unique after 5″ dedup of DESI + SDSS + Planck + NEOWISE) is defined by per-survey gate compliance, yet NEOWISE contributes via a masking-geometry QA that "passes by construction" rather than a blind detector-sensitivity injection-recovery test, while the paper correctly excludes eROSITA (1.2% recovery at 5σ) and LAMOST (5.8% recovery, 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact); the scope of "validated" (explicitly limited to the broad/continuum-dominated class, with narrow single-pixel lines recovering only at ≥15σ) requires still more prominent, repeated qualification in the abstract, §I, §III opening, and Table I caption to prevent misreading by downstream users.
[MAJOR] §V (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL forecast yields a central value σ(fNL) = 8.14 whose 9.4% shift from the single-tracer baseline (8.98) lies inside the reported 1σ envelope [3.92, 8.98], producing "no statistically significant improvement"; the NANOGrav γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 is only +1.13σ from the matter-bounce prediction and the Savage-Dickey factor is decisive solely against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference—these sections function as consistency/null demonstrations rather than positive support for the motivating fNL = −35/8 quasi-matter-bounce prediction, weakening the physics case for a PRD submission.
[MINOR] §III A and Table II (DESI science-class recount): The positional bitmask match shows only 2,468 science-target clusters at 1″ (0.012% rate on the 20.3 M-row denominator) versus the full-stream 195,829; while disclosed, the implications for catalog utility (host-galaxy association, follow-up feasibility, contamination by sky-fiber/filler spectra) are not quantified in a dedicated use-case subsection or limitations paragraph.
[MINOR] §IV A (archival cross-match and novelty): The 17.8% (178/1,000) genuine novelty fraction on the DESI top-1,000 via CDS X-Match against 18 catalogs is a substantive result, but the 178 objects receive no further characterization (redshifts, spectral features, multi-wavelength SEDs, or even a small gallery); without this the claim that they "represent a previously unexploited high-bias tracer reservoir" remains a count rather than an illustrated population.
[MINOR] Reproducibility statements throughout (§II B, §III, and figure/table captions): Extensive references to committed scripts (reproduce_headline_dedup.py, desi_injrec_CORRECTED.json, erosita_membership_reproduce.py, etc.) and artifacts are present, but a formal Data Availability Statement with permanent, versioned archives (e.g., Zenodo DOIs for code, model weights, and per-object tables) is required before publication.
The central claim of a large-scale, multi-survey anomaly catalog produced via native-trained autoencoders with documented per-survey validation gates for the included components is supported by the processing volume, 5-fold/OOD Jaccard and injection-recovery results for DESI/SDSS/Planck, the reproducible deduplication script, and the 17.8% novelty fraction, although the effective science-target yield is comparable to prior single-survey work, the "validated" label is qualified, and the cosmological applications return no significant new constraints.
