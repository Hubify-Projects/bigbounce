Manuscript: "A Null Chirality Dipole in 8.5 Million DESI Galaxies from Equivariant Deep Learning" by Houston Golden (Dated: July 9, 2026)
Overall Assessment
This is a high-quality, rigorously executed observational cosmology paper. It delivers the largest chirality-labeled galaxy catalog to date (8.47M galaxies, 3.20M spirals) using a flip-equivariant ViT-Small classifier with test-time averaging (TTA), accompanied by an exhaustive bias-hardening audit. The central result—a clean null real-space chirality dipole on the high-confidence subsample—is robustly supported by two independent primary estimators, careful separation of diagnostics from cosmological claims, and explicit falsification thresholds. The work directly addresses (and places in amplitude-level tension with) prior Shamir claims while providing a public catalog and full reproducibility artifacts. Statistical methodology is strong, systematics are handled transparently via an eight-anchor battery plus forward modeling, and limitations are stated without overclaim. Novelty and significance are high for the galaxy spin/isotropy literature.
The paper is suitable for PRD with only minor revisions. No concerns rise to the level of blocking publication.
Specific Concerns (Classified MAJOR/MINOR)
1. Forward modeling of the post-MASTER ℓ=1 residual (MINOR — Sec. IV D; Appendix D; ~53% accounted)
The imaging + morphology forward model (brick-level templates for legs, density, PSF FWHM, depth, E(B−V)) accounts for only ≈52–54% of the canonical-mask post-MASTER |a₁| = 6.95×10⁻³ residual amplitude (aligned at cos θ ≈ +0.83). The remaining ≳47% is explicitly left as an "open item" (primarily classifier confidence-vs-depth response at production scale). While the authors correctly bound the entire residual below the real-space A₅₀ ≈ 0.75% and A₉₅ ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%] floors (so it cannot affect the primary null or exclusion statements) and show the primary estimators are independent of this channel, a fuller per-galaxy DR8-sweep morphology → purity map would close the budget and strengthen the systematics attribution. This is already flagged by the authors as future work (Sec. VII) and does not undermine current conclusions.
2. Pseudo-label inheritance and shuffle-null limitations (MINOR — Sec. VI A; Introduction; Appendix B)
66.5% of training labels derive from CE-ResNet predictions, so per-galaxy label-shuffle and pixel-permutation nulls randomize the model's own outputs and cannot by themselves test independence from survey-correlated structure inherited via pseudo-labels. The authors explicitly acknowledge this limitation and bound it via: (i) a fully model-free GZ1-human-only dipole test on the confident overlap (N ≈ 4.60×10⁴, z = −0.54σ under the identical real-space estimator), (ii) template-regression/cross-spectrum anchors in Appendix D that are independent of shuffling, and (iii) quality-quartile washout (all |σ| < 1, no monotonic trend) inconsistent with a diluted primordial signal. The primary real-space null (+0.41σ) is conservative against any inherited bias (which would push away from null). The limitation is discussed transparently and does not create circularity in the headline result.
3. Edge-on contamination and argmax tie-break (MINOR — Appendix E)
Empirical DR8 morphology cross-match shows f_edge ≈ 15.8% (b/a < 0.3) of classified spirals are edge-on systems mislabeled CW/CCW rather than not_spiral, producing an ≈9% sensitivity dilution (Fisher scaling). Equivariance correctly symmetrizes this into pure dilution with zero directional bias (⟨p_eq^CW⟩ = ⟨p_eq^CCW⟩ for flip-symmetric morphologies). The borderline (p_eq ∈ [0.5, 0.6]) argmax tie-break coherence is leg-selective (strongest in DECaLS) and already bounded by the primary real-space null; isolating edge-on systems removes the leg coherence. A one-sentence main-text summary of this point would improve accessibility, but the analysis is already rigorous and the primary conclusions unaffected.
No other concerns. The pre-specified p_eq > 0.6 cut (committed script, immutable commit hash), confidence-cut sweep stability, block-bootstrap spatial covariance (NSIDE=8 choice justified and stable across scales), injection-recovery axis-averaging protocol, and explicit decision tree (Table I) separating primary cosmological estimators from diagnostics are all best-practice. Multiple-testing and look-elsewhere handling (direct MC max-statistic for hemispheres) is correct. Harmonic vs. real-space conventions are never interchanged. The Shamir comparison is carefully framed as amplitude-level tension under this pipeline (not a frequentist exclusion of Ganalyzer) with an explicit call for matched-footprint reanalysis.
Clarity and Completeness
Excellent. The decision tree (Table I), estimator hierarchy (Sec. III B), provenance tables (App. B), mask/weight audits (App. A, Tables X/XV), and injection tables (VIII/IX) make the logical chain transparent. Appendices are self-contained. Minor density in technical appendices is appropriate for a methods-heavy PRD paper; all quantitative claims are traceable to committed artifacts with checksums and commit hashes.
Novelty and Significance
High. This is the largest and most rigorously bias-audited chirality catalog to date, with sub-percent sensitivity on a survey-scale footprint. It provides a clean methodological demonstration that modest classifier bias + patchy coverage can produce spurious dipoles (Catalog A 2.31σ real-space / +6.48σ pre-MASTER → Catalog C null after TTA + MASTER), and it places prior claims in quantitative tension while supplying the community resource needed for follow-up. The work is a significant contribution to the galaxy spin/isotropy and parity-violation literature.
Recommendation
ACCEPT
(The three MINOR points above are already acknowledged by the authors as limitations or future work; addressing them in revision would further strengthen the paper but is not required for publication.
