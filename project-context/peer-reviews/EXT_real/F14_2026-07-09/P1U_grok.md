Manuscript: "Channel-Level Constraints on Four Enumerated Minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst Dark-Energy Routes Under Stated Assumptions (Amplitude Closure for R1–R3, Naturalness Closure for R4), and Perturbation Transparency for Scalar Matter" by Houston Golden.
Summary.
This 61-page theoretical cosmology paper assesses whether minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst (ECH) gravity with spin-torsion can source the observed late-time dark energy (DE) scale through four enumerated channels (R1: NJL four-fermion contact; R2: one-loop graviton corrections to the Holst sector; R3: quantum running of the Barbero–Immirzi parameter; R4: parity-odd CMB coupling via spectator ALP or neutrino current). It concludes that R1–R3 are amplitude-suppressed (by Planck factors, loop factors, or power-law running) under explicitly labeled scaling assumptions, while R4 is closed by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection (the coupling needed for the observed birefringence angle βobs  \beta_\text{obs}  βobs​ forces an ultralight-mass tuning mθ∼H0  m_\theta \sim H_0  mθ​∼H0​ that merely relocates the cosmological-constant problem).
A central supporting result is the perturbation-transparency theorem (Sec. X): for canonical scalar matter, torsion vanishes identically at all orders around the torsion-free branch (via the algebraic Cartan constraint with zero spin density), the Levi-Civita connection is recovered, and the Holst dual contraction vanishes pointwise by the first (algebraic) Bianchi identity Rμ[νρσ]=0  R_{\mu[\nu\rho\sigma]}=0  Rμ[νρσ]​=0. Consequently the Holst sector decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion. The paper also catalogs 13 distinct mechanism-class structural barriers (14 historical entries, with B8 subsumed by B14) spanning foundations and observational branches, identifies a structural tension between the Ntot≈92  N_\text{tot}\approx 92  Ntot​≈92 e-folds required for DE dilution and the survival of the matter-bounce fNL=−35/16  f_{NL}=-35/16  fNL​=−35/16 signature at SPHEREx scales, and presents supporting MCMC consistency checks (Λ  \Lambda  ΛCDM + ΔNeff  \Delta N_\text{eff}  ΔNeff​ proxy) and a Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma establishing basis-completeness of the four-fermion sector at MPl  M_\text{Pl}  MPl​-power-counting order within minimal ECH.
The main claims are framed as channel-level and assumption-conditional (explicitly not a full operator-level no-go or first-principles positive-amplitude derivation). Observational numbers (e.g., from companion Papers II–V or archived artifacts) are stated to be non-load-bearing for the closures.
Assessment
(1) Scientific validity and correctness of central claims and derivations.
The core derivations are correct and rigorously scoped.
The perturbation-transparency result (Sec. X) is solid. Steps 1–2 establish T=0  T=0  T=0 exactly (not perturbatively) because canonical scalars carry zero spin density and the Cartan constraint is algebraic. Step 4 then shows the Holst dual contraction vanishes identically on any torsion-free connection by the algebraic Bianchi identity—an exact pointwise tensor identity that holds order-by-order on the perturbed curvature without requiring a separate linearized expansion. The explicit term-by-term verification in Sec. X D–E (perturbed tetrad, connection, and curvature orders) and the differential-form decomposition confirming it is distinct from the Pontryagin density are clean. Scope exclusions (propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, fermion loops, non-minimal matter) are stated clearly and repeatedly.
The four-route closure (Sec. IV) is well-supported at the stated granularity. R1 follows the standard Hehl–Datta torsion-elimination map to a parity-even, MPl−2  M_\text{Pl}^{-2}  MPl−2​-suppressed four-fermion operator whose mean-field amplitude at cosmologically relevant densities is ∼70  \sim 70  ∼70 orders of magnitude below ρΛ  \rho_\Lambda  ρΛ​ (explicit numerical estimate on p. 15). R2 and R3 use motivated but explicitly ansatz-level operators whose suppression (loop factor + H0/MPl  H_0/M_\text{Pl}  H0​/MPl​ or (μ/MPl)2  (\mu/M_\text{Pl})^2  (μ/MPl​)2 running) is robust even after allowing generous O(1)–O(1010  ^{10}  10) coefficient uncertainty. R4 is correctly diagnosed as a naturalness (not amplitude) closure.
The Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (App. C) is a genuine technical contribution. The explicit 5×  \times  ×5 Fierz matrix on the Clifford basis, verification that F2=1  F^2 = 1  F2=1, and demonstration that both (J5⋅J5)  (J^5 \cdot J^5)  (J5⋅J5) and the parity-odd Holst partner (J⋅J5)  (J \cdot J^5)  (J⋅J5) rearrange into the closed set {SS,VV,AA,PP}  \{SS, VV, AA, PP\}  {SS,VV,AA,PP} (tensor channel absent) with rational coefficients that preserve the single κ=MPl−2  \kappa = M_\text{Pl}^{-2}  κ=MPl−2​ prefactor are correct and properly scoped to minimal ECH (single-species, minimal coupling, algebraic torsion). The companion symbolic check script is noted.
The dimension-4 parity-odd operator basis enumeration (App. B 1, Table VII) and single-scale NDA no-go are consistent: every admissible local dimension-4 parity-odd density is topological (total derivative, zero EOM/vacuum energy), Fierz-reducible to the suppressed four-fermion sector, or Bianchi-vanishing. No escape without a new light scale or exact cancellation—the tuning the mechanism was meant to explain.
The bespoke ECH-sector ΔNeff  \Delta N_\text{eff}  ΔNeff​ estimate in App. E 2a (ρtor∼GNT6  \rho_\text{tor} \sim G_N T^6  ρtor​∼GN​T6, ΔNeff∼(T/MPl)2≈10−43  \Delta N_\text{eff} \sim (T/M_\text{Pl})^2 \approx 10^{-43}  ΔNeff​∼(T/MPl​)2≈10−43 at BBN and 10−56  10^{-56}  10−56 at recombination) is a correct leading parametric result and correctly frames the stock-CAMB proxy run as a conservative observational envelope rather than a direct theory test.
No factual or calculational errors were found in the central derivations. The scoping language ("channel-level", "under stated assumptions", "not a full operator-level theorem") is consistent and repeated appropriately.
(2) Statistical methodology and handling of uncertainties/systematics.
The MCMC analysis (App. E) is a stock-CAMB Λ  \Lambda  ΛCDM + ΔNeff  \Delta N_\text{eff}  ΔNeff​ proxy (no custom torsion Boltzmann module). It uses large samples (309 189 raw accepted across two frozen chains; ∼  \sim  ∼216 432 post-burn-in at a conservative uniform 30 % cut), standard Cobaya/CAMB configuration with BBN-consistent YHe  Y_\text{He}  YHe​, flat prior on ΔNeff  \Delta N_\text{eff}  ΔNeff​, and proper reporting of both two-sided means and one-sided 95 % upper limits after physical-boundary renormalization. Convergence diagnostics and release-pairing robustness checks are provided. The bespoke ECH ΔNeff  \Delta N_\text{eff}  ΔNeff​ calculation supplies the correct theoretical context, showing the proxy is an envelope many orders of magnitude above the actual ECH prediction. Uncertainties and systematics (likelihood pairing, burn-in definition, effective-sample weighting) are handled transparently with explicit footnotes reconciling GetDist outputs. No over-claiming: the run is presented as a consistency/bounce-class check, not a direct spin-torsion test or tension resolver. Model-comparison statistics are appropriately deferred.
(3) Whether conclusions are supported by the evidence.
Yes, within the carefully delimited scope. The amplitude closures for R1–R3 follow directly from the suppression factors and the Fierz lemma; the naturalness closure for R4 follows from the requirement that a single coupling source both βobs  \beta_\text{obs}  βobs​ and ρΛ  \rho_\Lambda  ρΛ​. The perturbation-transparency theorem is proven exactly (algebraic identities, not truncation). The 13/14-barrier catalog is a useful organizing device that maps distinct physical failure modes. The structural tension between Ntot≈92  N_\text{tot} \approx 92  Ntot​≈92 (DE dilution) and survival of matter-bounce fNL  f_{NL}  fNL​ at SPHEREx scales (Sec. XIV D) is a logical consequence of the scale mapping and is correctly flagged as ECH-independent class information. All load-bearing claims are independent of the companion papers and archived artifacts.
(4) Novelty and significance.
The work is novel in its systematic channel-level audit of the four standard minimal-ECH DE routes at amplitude granularity, the explicit Fierz projection lemma establishing four-fermion basis-completeness, and the clean all-orders perturbation-transparency result for the scalar sector. It synthesizes LQC, ECH, and observational constraints into a coherent set of mechanism-class barriers. Significance lies in (i) sharply constraining a motivated class of spin-torsion models as DE sources, (ii) cleanly separating perturbative observables (identical to GR) from non-perturbative parity channels, and (iii) highlighting an incompatibility between a viable ECH DE mechanism and a testable matter-bounce fNL  f_{NL}  fNL​ signature under prolonged post-bounce inflation. The paper is honest about scope and limitations.
(5) Clarity and completeness.
The paper is dense and technically complete but suffers from presentation issues that impede readability. The title is excessively long. Prose contains many long sentences, nested parentheticals, and cross-references. Some technical justifications (e.g., the (Treh/MGUT)3/2  (T_\text{reh}/M_\text{GUT})^{3/2}  (Treh​/MGUT​)3/2 phase-space factor in Dinf  D_\text{inf}  Dinf​) are acknowledged as order-of-magnitude but could be streamlined. Heavy reliance on coordinated sibling papers and archived artifacts for context, while explicitly non-load-bearing for the main results, still burdens a standalone reader.

MINOR (should be addressed but does not block publication)
Presentation and readability (throughout, especially title, Sec. II C 1, Sec. X). The title is overly long and complex. The dense style with frequent parentheticals and footnotes makes some arguments harder to follow than necessary. The phenomenological status of the (Treh/MGUT)3/2  (T_\text{reh}/M_\text{GUT})^{3/2}  (Treh​/MGUT​)3/2 factor in the inflationary suppression (Sec. II C 1) is correctly flagged but could be condensed. These are fixable with editing and do not affect scientific content.
Companion/sibling dependency and standalone readability (Sec. I b, Table II, Sec. XIII, App. E). While the paper repeatedly and correctly states that the central closures (Secs. IV, IX, X) and lemmas are self-contained and that imported numbers are illustrative/non-load-bearing, the volume of references to coordinated submissions (Papers II–V) and archived pipelines may inconvenience readers. A short consolidated table summarizing the status of each external input would help.
Scope remark on fermions in realistic cosmology (Sec. X A, F). The transparency result is correctly restricted to canonical scalar matter and explicitly excludes fermion sectors. In a full SM cosmology, perturbed axial currents could source small δT  \delta T  δT at linear order. A one-sentence estimate of the size of such corrections in the radiation/matter era (or confirmation they remain negligible for the DE/bounce focus) would strengthen applicability without changing the theorem.
MCMC chain-processing footnotes (App. E 3 a). The detailed reconciliation of burn-in cuts, GetDist outputs, and sample counts is correct and conservative but meta-detail that could be condensed into a single reproducibility paragraph or supplementary note.
No MAJOR concerns were identified. No claims are overstated beyond the explicit scoping language, no calculational errors were found in the central derivations or lemmas, and the statistical analysis is appropriate and transparent for a theory paper.
Recommendation
MINOR REVISIONS
The manuscript presents a substantive, carefully scoped, and technically sound contribution.
