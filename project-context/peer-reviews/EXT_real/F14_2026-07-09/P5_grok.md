Manuscript: Redshift-Space Environmental Dependence of Spiral Chirality: A DESIVAST Three-Algorithm Void Null Test on 56,981 DESI DR1 Spirals (Houston Golden)
Summary. This is a companion paper to the concurrently submitted catalog paper (Paper IV). It performs a focused null test of whether spiral galaxy handedness (CW/CCW fraction from an equivariant ViT classifier on DESI Legacy imaging) depends on large-scale environment, using DESI DR1 spectroscopic redshifts cross-matched to the public DESIVAST DR1 void catalog (three algorithms: VoidFinder sphere-growing + V2-REVOLVER/V2-VIDE watershed). The designated primary analysis (post-hoc, explicitly flagged as exploratory) is the DESIVAST-anchored void vs. non-void contrast on the volume-limited z ≤ 0.24 BGS sample (n_void = 56,981 for VoidFinder point-in-sphere). Secondary paths include a T-Web tidal-tensor classifier on the full DR1 spectro sample, Phase-2 (R_s, λ_th) hyperparameter sweep, Tempel+2014 FoF cross-check on SDSS overlap, ASTRA EDR probabilistic cross-check, redshift/density/HEALPix stratifications, and multiple membership/geometry variants. All tests are performed in fixed redshift space (no real-space reconstruction).
Headline result (supported). The void vs. non-void ∆f_CW = +0.0007 (z_∆ = +0.31, p_∆ = 0.76) on the primary DESIVAST VoidFinder contrast, with |∆f_CW| ≤ 0.004 and |z_∆| ≤ 1.25 across all five Bonferroni-5 DESIVAST estimators (three sphere-PIS + two catalog-native GALZONE). The family-wise null holds. An honest quadrature systematic envelope of ≈0.9 pp (max single term 0.60 pp from any-hole vs. maximal-sphere geometry) is reported; the T-Web secondary void bin (n=428) is acknowledged as sample-size-limited and survey-shell contaminated. The result is robust to membership definition, footprint restriction, FoG-scale Monte Carlo membership perturbation, imaging-leg/program splits, and classifier confidence cuts. The per-galaxy labels are public (CC-BY-4.0); the headline contrast is algebraically invariant to the global classifier monopole.
Assessment
1. Scientific validity and correctness of central claims and derivations.
The central claim (no detectable environment dependence of classifier-labelled CW fraction at current sensitivity) is correct and rigorously supported. All quantitative results (binomial σ_from_half, two-sample z_∆, Jeffreys intervals, permutation p_LEE, χ² homogeneity tests) are internally consistent and match standard formulas. T-Web implementation follows Hahn et al. (2007)/Cautun et al. (2014) conventions exactly (sign, CIC deposition, Poisson solve in k-space, eigenvalue counting). DESIVAST point-in-sphere membership, k=20 KDTree vs. exact unbounded rerun (0.18% membership shift), and maximal-sphere subset are correctly implemented and cross-checked. Monopole cancellation in ∆f_CW is algebraically exact and properly exploited. RSD treatment is honest: fixed-void-geometry FoG MC bounds membership flips at the 0.34–0.37 pp level; the dominant anisotropic eigenvalue deformation channel is explicitly unquantified (limitation, not error). No derivation errors or unsupported extrapolations.
2. Statistical methodology and handling of uncertainties/systematics.
Excellent. Look-elsewhere effects are controlled via both parametric Bonferroni (per-family thresholds |σ| ≈ 2.58 for Bonferroni-5, 2.77 for Bonferroni-9) and empirical max-stat label-shuffle permutation nulls (N_MC=1000, stream-independent). Systematic budget (Table XI) consolidates every reported term (counting CI 0.44 pp, membership FoG MC 0.34–0.37 pp, geometry 0.60 pp, etc.) and folds them in quadrature with explicit disclosure that terms are comparable in magnitude and that the envelope is a sensitivity figure, not a hard exclusion. Monopole subtraction is correctly applied only to σ_pred/σ_vs_monopole diagnostics (headline ∆f_CW is invariant). Small-n regimes (T-Web void n=428; n=6 illustrative overlap) are properly caveated and not over-interpreted. No hidden systematics or under-estimated uncertainties.
3. Whether conclusions are supported by the evidence.
Yes. The family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across five DESIVAST estimators is directly supported and is the robustly quotable result. The ≈0.9 pp envelope follows from the tabulated terms. All secondary paths (T-Web Phase-2 sweep with max |σ_obs – σ_pred| ≤ 1.64 in resolved cells; Tempel like-for-like filament concordance 0.29 pp; ASTRA EDR consistency despite per-galaxy label disagreement; HEALPix sky-position nulls p ≥ 0.135; redshift/density scans) concur. Conclusions are appropriately scoped: fixed-redshift-space bound on classifier-labelled f_CW (not physical chirality; attenuation caveat via GZ1 accuracy floor κ=0.40 and lack of environment-stratified confusion matrix is stated); exploratory/post-hoc primary path (explicitly flagged); no claim of real-space immunity or model discrimination (no published bounce/inflation model predicts a signal at this scale/sensitivity). The result is a clean controlled-sample non-detection providing an empirical upper bound for future theory.
4. Novelty and significance.
Moderately novel and useful. This is the first large-scale (tens of thousands of void spirals), multi-algorithm (three independent void finders + catalog-native zones), public-DR1 test of chirality vs. cosmic-web environment. It complements Paper IV’s global dipole null with a conditional test at ≳25 Mpc/h (T-Web) / void scales. The bound is relevant for parity-violating gravity or bounce-chirality coupling models (even if none currently predict a detectable signal here). It is an incremental but clean negative result that narrows model space and demonstrates the power of DESI + DESIVAST for such tests. Not high-impact standalone, but solid and citable in the chirality/LSS parity-violation literature.
5. Clarity and completeness.
High technical clarity with excellent navigation aids (reader’s guide to six recurring concerns, analysis-tree Table IV, statistics glossary Table III, systematic budget Table XI). Reproducibility is outstanding (deterministic seed 20260515, per-output provenance JSON, committed drivers, public artifacts, GZ1 human-vote validation). Caveats (RSD, labelled vs. physical, post-hoc primary, co-submission with Paper IV, no environment-stratified confusion matrix) are prominent and honest. Minor presentational issues only: abstract and headline paragraphs could foreground the family-wise Bonferroni-5 statement more prominently relative to any single-row ∆f_CW; the labelled-vs-physical attenuation caveat could be stated even more explicitly in the abstract’s result paragraph; Appendix B (toy EFT mapping) is properly scoped as speculative/non-empirical but could be shortened or moved to supplementary material to reduce any risk of misreading.
Explicit concerns (classified)
MINOR (should be addressed; do not block publication)
Post-hoc primary designation and exploratory framing (Abstract; §V B; Table IV; §XV). The analysis tree, Bonferroni-5 family null, and explicit “exploratory/post-hoc… no timestamped plan predates the data” language are already present and correct. Minor tightening: make the abstract’s “headline result” paragraph lead with the family-wise null as the strictly quotable statement and the single-row ∆f_CW as illustrative/sensitivity check. This prevents any misreading of the ≈0.9 pp envelope as pre-registered.
Labelled vs. physical distinction and de-attenuation (Abstract; §XIII; Appendix A). The caveat that the bound applies to classifier-labelled f_CW (attenuated proxy at GZ1 accuracy floor 69.91%, κ=0.40) and that a physical-chirality interpretation requires the (unavailable) environment-dependent relabeling matrix is stated, but it could be made even more prominent in the abstract’s result sentence and conclusions. No error; just emphasis.
RSD treatment (Abstract; §VIII; §XIII). The fixed-geometry FoG MC and scalar-displacement heuristic are correctly presented as indicative; the dominant anisotropic eigenvalue deformation channel is explicitly unquantified. Minor: ensure language consistently says “RSD-bounded at the level of the quoted envelope” rather than implying stronger immunity. Already largely correct.
Appendix B (toy EFT operator). Properly labelled “speculative guide… not a derived empirical result… outside the empirical scope.” Minor presentational preference: consider shortening or moving to supplementary material. No scientific issue.
Minor repetition in robustness sections (§VI–X). Thorough cross-checks are a strength, but some overlap (monopole subtraction, small-n caveats) could be streamlined without loss of clarity or completeness.
No MAJOR concerns. No statistical errors, unsupported claims, hidden systematics, over-interpretation of small-n results, or failure to disclose limitations. The analysis is self-adversarial and transparent. The null is genuinely supported by the data and multiple independent paths.
Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS
The manuscript is scientifically sound, statistically rigorous, reproducible, and appropriately caveated. It reports a clean, useful null result with a conservatively presented sensitivity bound. Minor revisions are limited to presentational tightening (primarily abstract framing of the family-wise null and labelled-vs-physical distinction, plus optional Appendix B adjustment). These do not affect validity, conclusions, or evidential support. Coordinated review with the companion Paper IV (as the authors explicitly request) is appropriate and already anticipated in the text.
