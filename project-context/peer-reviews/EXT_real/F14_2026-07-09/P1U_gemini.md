The author frames the decoupling of the Holst sector from scalar/tensor observables for canonical scalar matter as a "central result" and a "rigorous result". However, the proof provided in Section X.B relies on the fact that a canonical scalar field has zero spin density, which algebraically enforces zero torsion via the Cartan constraint. Consequently, the connection reduces to the Levi-Civita connection , and the Holst term vanishes identically due to the first algebraic Bianchi identity Rμ[νρσ]​=0. This is not a novel theorem; it is a textbook identity of Riemann-Cartan geometry. Presenting basic geometric identities as "Barrier 14"  inflates the theoretical contribution of the manuscript.
