Implications

This result strengthens the empirical case for statistical isotropy in the late-universe (z ≲ 1), projected, morphology-channel observable at the sub-percent level. The ℓ=1 dipole is parity-even (isotropy-breaking axial-vector); it is not a direct parity-violation test (the parity-odd channel is ℓ=0 monopole + even multipoles). The documented classifier monopole artifact implies that future ℓ=0 parity searches on this or similar catalogs should use locally monopole-renormalized labels.

For your broader Big Bounce / spin-torsion program (P1–P5 series), this provides a clean, high-sensitivity null on one isotropy-breaking channel. It does not directly constrain primordial chiral-tensor transfer functions (that mapping is left to theory follow-up), but it sets a concrete late-universe morphology dipole floor (A ≳ 0.75% at 50% power) that any such model must accommodate or predict below current reach / in other observables (e.g., higher-z spectroscopic bins).
