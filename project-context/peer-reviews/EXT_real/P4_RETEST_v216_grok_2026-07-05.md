[MINOR] Sec. IV.D (Monopole+Mask Leakage Generative Null) and Appendix D (eight-anchor battery): The imaging+DR8-morphology forward model reproduces only 52–54% of the observed canonical-mask |a_1|≈6.95×10^{-3} residual amplitude (cosθ≈+0.83 alignment). The remaining ≳46% is attributed to per-galaxy classifier confidence-vs-depth response not captured by pixel-averaged templates, but no explicit upper limit on a possible coherent cosmological dipole component within that remainder (beyond the already-null real-space bound) is derived from the forward-model residual itself; the "diagnostic evidence for a depth/morphology-correlated canonical-mask residual" claim in the title and abstract is therefore only partially substantiated.

[MINOR] Sec. V.A (Comparison with Previous Work) and abstract: The statement that the present result is "inconsistent in amplitude with Shamir's claimed ∼3% signal by a factor of ∼7–18" (after conversion to A_p units) is presented as a direct comparison, yet no matched-footprint reanalysis with Ganalyzer (or explicit quantification of differences in spiral selection, magnitude limit, or footprint masking) is performed. The text correctly caveats that this is "an amplitude-level tension under our pipeline, not a frequentist exclusion," but the phrasing in the abstract and opening paragraphs still risks being read as a stronger statistical confrontation than the evidence supports.

[MINOR] Sec. IV.C (Dipole Analysis) and Sec. VI.B (Sensitivity Floor): The primary peq>0.6 cut is stated to have been pre-specified in the generator script (commit 94113e5), which is good practice; however, the injection-recovery thresholds (A_50≈0.75%, A_95 bracketed in (1.0%,1.5%]) are derived from only N_inj=100 realizations per amplitude point, yielding binomial standard errors ≲0.05 on the recovery probabilities. These thresholds are therefore known only to the tested-grid precision and should be explicitly labeled "approximate to grid resolution" rather than presented as sharp falsification boundaries.

[MINOR] Throughout (significance reporting) and Table I/II: The repeated, correct emphasis that σ values from distinct null procedures (pixel-permutation, label-shuffle, block-bootstrap, max-statistic MC, monopole-only generative) are incommensurable is scientifically sound but creates a high cognitive load; a single consolidated "Primary vs. Diagnostic" decision table that also lists the exact null hypothesis, sample, and estimator for each quoted number would materially reduce the risk of misinterpretation.

The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole on the pre-specified high-confidence (peq>0.6) subsample is consistent with null (+0.41σ, rank-p=0.31) while a clean 1.7% cosmological dipole is strongly disfavored (block-bootstrap WLS z≈−18)—is supported by the primary estimators, the injection-recovery calibration, the model-independent GZ1 human-label cross-check, and the explicit separation of diagnostic harmonic residuals from cosmological conclusions.
