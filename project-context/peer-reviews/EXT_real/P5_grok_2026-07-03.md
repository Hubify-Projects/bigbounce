## RAW RESPONSE (verbatim)

VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sections I–II and Appendix A: The headline Δf_CW result is presented as independent of the in-preparation Paper IV manuscript because it uses only public per-galaxy class_eq labels and is algebraically invariant to any monopole shift. However, the parity-equivariance validation, training details, test-time augmentation procedure, and physical origin of the ~9σ catalog monopole (treated as the dominant systematic) are only summarized in Appendix A. This summary is insufficient for a standalone PRD referee to verify the classifier systematics without the companion paper; the public HuggingFace catalog alone does not resolve this.
[MINOR] Section V B and Table III: The DESIVAST-anchored path is explicitly designated primary post-hoc. While a priori grounds are enumerated and the result is shown to be robust across all five Bonferroni-5 estimators (|z_Δ| ≤ 1.25), the full analysis tree (5 primary + 9 Phase-2 secondary + ~30–40 descriptive stratifications) is not accompanied by a quantitative effective-trials or false-discovery assessment beyond per-family Bonferroni thresholds. This leaves a residual garden-of-forking-paths exposure that should be bounded more explicitly.
[MINOR] Section VIII and abstract: The primary quantitative result is the DESIVAST void/non-void contrast Δf_CW = +0.0007 (SE_counting = 0.0022) on n = 56,981 spirals, with an effective 2σ bound of ~0.5–0.6 pp after the fixed-void-geometry systematic. This is correctly described as a bounded upper limit set by the void sample size, but the abstract and §VIII foreground the raw p-value rather than elevating the minimum-detectable-effect statement and the FoG Monte-Carlo distribution of Δf_CW shifts as the headline constraint.
[MINOR] Section VI A and the bright/dark split discussion: The ~2.1σ filament-class bright-vs-dark sign-flip (and catalog-level |z| ≈ 1.95 on unique galaxies) is interpreted as a BGS-selection-function residual (consistent with Paper IV imaging-leg trends) rather than an astrophysical signal. Given the strong T-Web × target-program non-orthogonality (χ² p ≪ 10^{-300}, Cramér’s V = 0.078), this ~2σ structure is the largest post-monopole residual in the paper and should be flagged more prominently in the abstract/conclusions as a diagnostic for Rubin + DESI DR2, even though it does not affect the DESIVAST primary null.
[MINOR] Introduction and §I: The claim that the null “supplies an empirical upper bound on any future model in the bounce-chirality coupling class” is formally correct as a model-agnostic statement at the ≳25 Mpc/h smoothing scale. However, no specific bounce-model coupling is cited or mapped to a predicted |Δf_CW|; the language should be revised to “provides a new empirical constraint that any such model must satisfy” to match the actual calculation performed.

The central claim—that spiral chirality shows no environment dependence beyond the catalog monopole at the sensitivity of DESI DR1—is supported by the evidence: the primary DESIVAST contrast is statistically indistinguishable from zero across five independent void definitions, the T-Web Phase-2 sweep yields monopole-subtracted residuals ≤1.64σ with label-shuffle p ≥ 0.13 in all cells, and all secondary null tests (redshift, density, sky position, tracer program) are consistent with the counting-statistics-plus-monopole floor.
