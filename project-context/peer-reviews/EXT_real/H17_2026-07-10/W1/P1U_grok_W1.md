VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I (Introduction, Scope paragraph): The repeated emphasis that the work is a “channel-level assessment, not an operator-level theorem” is appropriate and correctly qualified, but the subsequent claim of “basis-complete within minimal ECH at the MPl-power-counting level” (via F1/F2 structural facts + NDA monotonicity + Fierz-by-Fierz lemma) creates a tension that should be resolved in one concise paragraph early in the introduction or at the start of Sec. IV. Explicitly state that completeness holds only inside the stated minimal-ECH field content and single-scale NDA; do not let the wording suggest a broader diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis.

[MINOR] Sec. IV A–B (R1 and parity-odd Holst partner): The amplitude bounds use nψ ∼ O(10²) cm⁻³ as a “deliberately conservative high-density upper bound.” Justify this choice quantitatively (compare to cosmic-mean baryon density ∼2×10⁻⁷ cm⁻³ today and to bounce-era densities) and state whether the bound remains robust if the mean-field NJL condensate analysis (explicitly flagged as out-of-scope) were performed. The ∼70-order suppression is insensitive to O(1) factors, but the text should say so explicitly.

[MINOR] Sec. IV D (Route 2 one-loop estimate): The canonical evaluation yields ∼10⁻⁶⁰ suppression (or ≥10⁻⁵⁸ allowing two orders for higher-loop corrections). The margin note correctly notes that even a 10¹⁰ inflation of the O(1) β(γ) prefactor leaves ≳48 orders of margin. Move this robustness statement into the main text of Sec. IV D rather than a margin note; it is load-bearing for the closure claim.

[MINOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result): The result is conceptually clean and correctly restricted to canonical scalar matter (excluding fermions, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings). However, the proof sketch in subsections B–E is too terse for a central claim labeled “rigorous.” Either expand the key algebraic steps (especially the explicit perturbed-tetrad expansion and the Bianchi-identity vanishing of the Holst dual) in the main text or add a short dedicated appendix containing the term-by-term verification. The current level is acceptable for an internal note but marginal for PRD.

[MINOR] Sec. IX (Structural Constraints catalog): The 13/14 barriers are usefully classified by mechanism class, but the section would benefit from a compact summary table (one row per barrier: Foundation/Branch label, mechanism class, primary route(s) constrained, and one-sentence physical content). This would improve readability without lengthening the paper.

[MINOR] Appendix B (Dimensional status of the parity-odd operator): The distinction between the on-shell curvature-dressing route (promoting to M⁵_Pl) and the local-operator-promotion route (absorbing powers into the coupling) is clearly drawn, but the text should explicitly state that both routes yield the same Ntot ≈ 92 at order-of-magnitude level and that all subsequent barriers test the on-shell amplitude budget, rendering the off-shell bookkeeping choice immaterial to the closures. Add this one-sentence reconciliation.

[MINOR] General presentation: The manuscript is self-contained for its primary claims (perturbation transparency, four-route closures, 14-barrier catalog), correctly flags that illustrative MCMC/NaMaster/spectator-ALP numbers come from the folded appendices and do not enter the no-go logic, and archives reproducibility artifacts. Minor typographic/notation clean-up (consistent use of κ vs. 8πG, explicit statement that the displayed ¼T^{abc}T_{abc} is on-shell shorthand only) would improve polish but does not affect scientific content.

All issues are addressable by targeted textual revisions, clarifications, and one modest table/appendix addition. No new calculations or data are required. The central results (channel-level amplitude closures under stated assumptions and the perturbation-transparency theorem for canonical scalars) appear technically sound once the above points are tightened.
