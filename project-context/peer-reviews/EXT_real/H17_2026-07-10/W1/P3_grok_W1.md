VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §I (also §III.A): The phrasing “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” and the headline emphasis on this number as the primary deliverable overstate the evidential strength; the text correctly qualifies it as a process-volume figure of candidates surviving per-survey gates (broad/continuum class only for DESI at 99–100% recovery; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ; NEOWISE is geometry-QA only), but the abstract and opening paragraphs do not foreground these scope limitations with equal prominence, risking misreading by readers expecting confirmed physical detections or uniform validation.
[MAJOR] §III.E (and cross-referenced provenance claims in §II.D, §VI.D): The production eROSITA DR1 score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible from any committed artifact (fails on 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains; raw scores non-monotone, Spearman ρ ≈ −0.10); although the tier is correctly excised from all counts and replaced by a reproducible top-298 membership list, the existence of an undocumented post-hoc rescaling step in a production run raises a material question about the end-to-end provenance and documentation standards of the overall Path-C pipeline, even for the retained tiers.
[MINOR] §V.A–B (and motivation in §I): The multi-tracer fNL and NANOGrav sections are presented as secondary demonstrations, yet the introduction foregrounds testing the quasi-matter-bounce prediction fNL = −4.375 as a key science driver; the actual results (α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65; no multi-tracer improvement; γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively inconsistent only with an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB) are null/consistent with no gain, so the sections should be explicitly scoped as “illustrative null results at current S/N” or moved to an appendix to avoid any appearance that the catalog delivers cosmological constraining power.
[MINOR] Table II, §II.B, §III.C–D (and associated footnotes ♡, ♠, ‡, ∥): The headline numbers rely on a heterogeneous set of thresholds (absolute S > 5 for DESI; fixed-size continuity slice S ≥ 0.1060 for SDSS; native top-1% for LAMOST/Planck/NEOWISE; membership list for excised eROSITA) whose rationale is scattered across many long footnotes; this complexity obscures the exact selection that produces the released 268,519 and 377,482 figures and makes independent verification or re-use harder than necessary.
[MINOR] §IV.A: The archival cross-match novelty fraction (178/1,000 ≈ 17.8% for DESI top-1,000) is reported as a single-sample point estimate with Wilson CI but without an explicit control (e.g., random or magnitude/redshift-matched non-anomaly sample processed through the identical CDS X-Match pipeline); adding such a control would strengthen the claim that the observed unmatched rate reflects genuine novelty rather than database incompleteness or matching systematics.

The central claim—that the Path-C native-retrain protocol applied to 37.3 million spectra/patches has produced a large, per-survey-validated anomaly-candidate catalog (268,519 objects after deduplication and excision of irreproducible tiers) together with public reproducibility artifacts—is supported by the documented methodology, injection-recovery results for the retained components (DESI broad class, SDSS continuum-dip, Planck), explicit failure-mode handling, and committed scripts.
