ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. IV A and Appendix D: The central exclusion of a vacuum scalar condensate in Route 1 rests on the Fierz projection yielding a repulsive scalar-channel coupling (G_scalar = −3/64 κ < 0) plus a sub-critical |G_eff|/G_crit ratio; the manuscript states these results but does not display the explicit projection coefficients (Eq. (C2) reference only) or the regulated gap-equation solution in the main text or a self-contained excerpt, rendering the key “repulsive + sub-critical” step non-transparent without external scripts.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV D–E and abstract: Routes 2 and 3 are declared amplitude-closed only “under explicitly-labeled scaling ansätze”; these ansätze (and the “mass-dimension lock” for R3) are not derived from first-principles EFT power counting or standard renormalization-group flow in the provided text, so the closures remain conditional on unmotivated or ad-hoc assumptions that must be either justified rigorously or removed from the headline claim.
[MAJOR] Sec. IX and abstract: The catalog of “13 distinct mechanism-class barriers (14 historical entries, B8 subsumed by B14)” is presented as collectively constraining the channels, yet most entries receive only one-sentence descriptions referencing unnamed “Foundation A–G” or “Branch H/J/L/M/N/O” studies; without self-contained derivations or explicit forward references showing independence and non-overlap, the catalog functions as a list rather than a demonstrated set of distinct physical failure modes.
[MINOR] Sec. II C, App. B and abstract: The single-scale NDA no-go for the parity-odd operator (off-shell dimension +1 forcing ρ_ECH_Λ ∼ M_Pl^4) and the N_tot ≈ 92 bookkeeping are caveated as phenomenological, but the distinction between the on-shell curvature-dressing ansatz and the off-shell operator-promotion route is not illustrated with a short side-by-side equation comparison; this creates unnecessary ambiguity for readers tracing how the +1 → +4 gap is closed.
[MINOR] Sec. X and abstract: The perturbation-transparency theorem (Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor EOM via the algebraic Bianchi identity on the torsion-free branch) is a clean Tier-I result, but the restrictive scope (“canonical scalar matter only; excludes fermions, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings”) is not stated in the section heading or opening paragraph, risking over-generalization by casual readers.
[MINOR] General presentation: The 63-page manuscript contains extensive internal cross-references, repeated caveats about “channel-level only,” and non-load-bearing citations to companion papers and external GitHub scripts; while technically self-contained for the core claims, the density and length hinder readability and would benefit from condensation of Sec. IX, tighter equation numbering, and a one-page “key results at a glance” box.
