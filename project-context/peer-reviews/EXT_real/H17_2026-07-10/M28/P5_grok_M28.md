(1) VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section V B (and abstract): The designated-primary DESIVAST-anchored estimand and family-wise Bonferroni-5 null are explicitly post-hoc (exploratory, no timestamped pre-registered plan); while the paper discusses garden-of-forking-paths and commits to DR2 pre-registration, the framing of a single “headline” contrast and its systematic envelope as the strictly quotable result requires clearer separation from the multi-path exploratory tree (Table IV) to avoid over-claiming confirmatory status for the bound.
[MAJOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment and Table XI): The ≈0.9 pp effective 2σ systematic envelope folds geometry (0.60 pp co-dominant), footprint, confidence, and match-radius terms in quadrature after fixed-void-geometry MC and first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction; however, full nonlinear void-catalog reconstruction under RSD is not performed, finger-of-god residuals are only indirectly bounded, and the envelope is presented as “honest” without a dedicated sensitivity table showing how each term was validated against the primary any-hole vs. maximal-sphere contrast— this understates a limitation on the de-attenuated physical bound quoted in the abstract.
[MAJOR] Abstract and §I (de-attenuation claim): The labelled-fraction bound of ≈0.9 pp is scaled by the GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %, attenuation factor ≃0.40) to obtain a “physical-chirality bound ≈2.26 pp”; this assumes classifier errors are environment-independent and that the contrast attenuation is strictly linear in (2a−1), but no explicit test (e.g., environment-stratified accuracy or mock injection) is shown to confirm the factor applies to the void/non-void Δf_CW rather than only to the global monopole— the step needs justification or removal from the headline claim.
[MINOR] Section VI A and §XI (T-Web bright/dark sign-flip): The ∼2.1σ filament bright-vs-dark residual is correctly flagged as a survey-selection diagnostic, yet the paper still presents the full T-Web 4-class table and omnibus test as a “secondary diagnostic path” without a quantitative propagation showing that the known per-leg/per-program monopole leakage (0.81 pp catalog-level) cannot produce the observed sign-flip amplitude under the measured T-Web × program correlation (χ²=4933); a short end-to-end mock or analytic bound would strengthen the claim that it does not affect the primary DESIVAST null.
[MINOR] Throughout (companion Paper IV dependence): Key inputs (GZ1 accuracy floor, global monopole Δf_P4_CW = −0.0026, classifier architecture) are summarized in Table I and Appendix A but remain tied to a concurrently submitted placeholder arXiv; the manuscript should either embed the minimal self-contained derivation of the attenuation factor and monopole uncertainty propagation or add an explicit “inputs from Paper IV” validation subsection so referees can assess the present analysis independently.

(3) The central claim—that spiral chirality shows no significant environment dependence in DESI DR1 at the ≳25 Mpc/h scale, with a robust ≈0.9 pp (labelled) / ≈2.26 pp (physical) upper bound from the primary DESIVAST void/non-void contrast—is supported by the data, statistics, and extensive robustness checks presented.
