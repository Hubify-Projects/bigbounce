VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. I, and Sec. VII: The headline sensitivity envelope ∼1.3–2.75σ (and the ∼0.8σ unresolved-GR edge) is obtained by transferring a proxy correlation ρ ≈ −0.868 from the power-spectrum scale-dependent-bias Fisher channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance CovB of Heinrich et al. is not public; while the paper later computes a channel-native surrogate covariance yielding ρ ≈ −0.42 and a higher marginalized floor σ_marg(f_NL^bounce) ≈ 0.94 (∼2.3σ), it retains and quotes the more conservative proxy floor as the “conservative 1.3σ floor” without demonstrating that the SDB proxy is the appropriate or more robust choice for the bispectrum estimator.
[MAJOR] Sec. II A and Appendix A (referenced throughout): The central claim that the literature value f_NL = −35/8 is erroneous and the correct squeezed-limit amplitude from the four cubic-action vertices at ε = 3/2 is exactly −35/16 (with the discrepancy traced to a −(99/128) ∑_i k_i^3 term in the transcribed polynomial) is the foundation for doubling all quoted significances and Bayes factors; the appendix must explicitly display the individual vertex contributions (L_redef, L_ζζ̇², L_ζ̇∂ζ∂χ, L_ζ(∂_i∂_jχ)²), their in-in symmetrized sum, the resulting degree-9 polynomial coefficients in a reproducible monomial basis, and the direct squeezed-limit evaluation so that the factor-of-two correction can be independently verified.
[MINOR] Sec. V: The MegaMapper outlook is explicitly labeled an “uncalibrated projection” whose GR-projection and b_ϕ budgets are transferred unchanged from the SPHEREx analysis, yet the abstract and introduction present 1.5–3.5σ (and the ideal 3.7–3.85σ) ranges without a quantitative statement of the additional high-z (z = 2–5) relativistic-projection uncertainty that is known to grow steeply; this risks overstating the robustness of the Stage-V forecast.
[MINOR] Sec. II C and Sec. VI: The six assumptions (a)–(f) and the Bayesian model-comparison section are carefully caveated, but the repeated phrasing “UV-completion-independent within the Wilson-Ewing class” and “robust across the bounce class” (conditional on faithful cubic-order transmission verified only at linear order) can be misread as broader model independence; a single consolidated paragraph restating the precise scope of the claim would eliminate any appearance of overstatement.
[MINOR] Overall length and presentation: At 37 pages the manuscript contains valuable technical detail (null-space sampling of 10 000 coefficient sets, multi-radius convergence tests, three Monte-Carlo validations of the closed-form Bayes factor), yet several subsections could be condensed or moved to appendices/supplemental material (e.g., the full 23 098-triangle grid description, the SVD of the 3 × 6 constraint matrix) without loss of rigor, improving readability for Phys. Rev. D.

The central claim that the corrected matter-bounce prediction f_NL = −35/16 yields a realistic SPHEREx detection significance of order 2σ (after template mismatch r ≈ 0.84 and a disclosed systematic budget) with Bayes-factor discrimination BF ≈ 9–14 against tuned multifield competitors is supported by the multi-way verification of the amplitude, the independent tree-level multi-tracer Fisher that reproduces the Heinrich baseline to 2–11 %, the explicit noise-weighted overlap calculation, and the closed-form Bayes-factor formula cross-checked by Monte-Carlo ensembles, subject to acceptance of the proxy-covariance assumptions that define the lower edge of the quoted range.
