VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV D (Route 2) and Sec. IV E (Route 3): Closures rest on explicitly labeled phenomenological ansatze for the parity-odd operator (Eq. 17, motivated by but "not literally derived" from Shapiro & Teixeira) and the running ansatz (Eq. 19). Amplitude budgets demonstrate suppression only for these specific choices; no demonstration is given that they exhaust the channel or that other operators at the same mass-dimension/power-counting class are forbidden from contributing larger amplitudes.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (overall four-route closure) and App. B (dimensional status): The single-scale NDA no-go and completeness argument rely on the off-shell dim-+1 schematic (Eq. 6) being promoted via either on-shell curvature dressing or the genuine dim-4 basis (Eq. 8). The collapse of the latter to topological/Fierz-reducible/Bianchi-vanishing operators is asserted via the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma and symbolic script in App. C, but neither the lemma nor the explicit enumeration of admissible O_n^[4] is proven or even sketched in the main text; different promotion schemes already yield inconsistent N_tot values (~92 vs. ~94), exposing sensitivity to an unverified completion choice.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result): For canonical scalar matter the Cartan equation is algebraic (T = κS) with S = 0 identically, so T = 0 holds exactly (not order-by-order in perturbations). The subsequent claim that the Holst dual vanishes by the algebraic Bianchi identity and therefore decouples from all scalar/tensor EOM at every classical order is standard Palatini geometry and adds no new content once the source term vanishes; the "transparency" statement therefore reduces to a triviality outside the excluded sectors (fermions, propagating torsion, non-minimal couplings) already flagged in the scope paragraph.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C and Sec. XII A (dark-energy mapping and inflationary-suppression factor): The parameterization Λ_eff = Ξ M_Pl² + … with D_inf = exp(−3N_tot) … is introduced as a phenomenological ansatz required because "the proposed link from ECH to a late-time vacuum energy requires a phenomenological dimensional ansatz beyond the minimal framework." All subsequent quantitative claims (N_tot ≈ 92, structural tension with f_NL = −35/16, dilution bookkeeping) therefore follow from this external ansatz rather than from any derived ECH dynamics; the "closure" of the dark-energy route is then tautological once the ansatz is inserted.
[MINOR] Sec. I, abstract, and conclusions: Repeated scope qualifiers ("channel-level … under stated assumptions … not an operator-level theorem") are correct but are placed after strong titular and abstract language ("Channel-Level Constraints … Amplitude Closure for R1–R3, Naturalness Closure for R4"). The mismatch between prominent claims and the narrow, assumption-dependent scope is repeated throughout and obscures the actual reach of the results.
[MINOR] Sec. IX (14-barrier catalog): The 14 entries are presented as "distinct mechanism-class constraints" obtained from 7 Foundations + 6 Branches, yet several (e.g., Planck suppression, topological-shift duality, parameter immunity) are standard EFT or LQC statements repackaged; no explicit cross-check is supplied showing that they are independent once the perturbation-transparency result (B14) is imposed, nor is a falsifiability criterion given for each beyond the generic single-scale NDA assumption already used in App. B.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH routes are closed at channel-amplitude granularity (R1–R3 by explicit suppression, R4 by naturalness) under single-scale EFT assumptions, together with a perturbation-transparency result for canonical scalars—is supported only conditionally on the unproven completeness of the channel enumeration, the representativeness of the adopted R2/R3 operators, and the validity of the external phenomenological dark-energy ansatz; the evidence presented does not establish these premises at the level required for an unconditional no-go.
