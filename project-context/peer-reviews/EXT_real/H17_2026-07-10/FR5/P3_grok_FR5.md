VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §I (Introduction/Reader’s guide): The headline deliverable is presented as a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” from a “full-instrument-stream scan,” yet the text explicitly qualifies the count as a process-volume figure (not confirmed physical detections), notes that ~98.7% of DESI anomalies fall on sky/filler fibers, and states the like-for-like science-target yield (2,468 clusters) is only ≈0.92× the prior single-survey benchmark of Liang et al. [11]; this framing mismatch inflates perceived impact.
[MAJOR] §III E (eROSITA DR1) and §II D (Path-C protocol): The production score axis (0.259 threshold) cannot be reproduced from any committed artifact (fails all 16 tested monotone rescalings plus IsolationForest retrains; raw scores are non-monotone), forcing excision of the tier from every count (including the inclusive 377,482); while transparently disclosed, this constitutes a critical, unrecoverable provenance failure that selectively undermines the reproducibility claims applied to the rest of the pipeline.
[MAJOR] §V (Cosmological applications): Both the multi-tracer fNL measurement (α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65, no improvement on single-tracer σ(fNL)) and the NANOGrav γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 consistency check return null results with no new constraints or detections; these are labeled “secondary demonstrations” but provide insufficient physics motivation or impact for a Physical Review D submission.
[MINOR] §II B (Training and Scoring) and §III (survey-by-survey): Preprocessing choices (full-sample scaler fits for tabular data) receive only partial bounded robustness checks (eROSITA only); analogous checks for NEOWISE are queued, some raw score parquets reside on exited pods, and the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer’s documented sensitivity floor (narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ) is not propagated into catalog completeness claims.
[MINOR] Throughout (Tables I–II, §III, length): The manuscript is excessively long (~37 pages) with dense footnotes, exhaustive failed-tier diagnostics (LAMOST 98% blue-excess artifact, ACT quarantine, synthetic Gaia excision), and process-volume bookkeeping that could be condensed or moved to appendices without loss of scientific content.

The central claim that the work delivers a validated multi-survey anomaly catalog with demonstrated detector sensitivity via native-trained autoencoders is only partially supported, as validation gates are survey-specific and mixed (strong for DESI broad-class/Planck/SDSS continuum-dip but geometry-only for NEOWISE and failed/excised for others), the large headline count is dominated by non-science-target spectra, and novelty remains modest.
