VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. X (and cross-references in Sec. IV, IX): The central perturbation-transparency result (Holst sector decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation EOM for canonical scalar matter) is presented as a Tier-I theorem with "explicit verification" and "term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion," yet the main text provides only a high-level outline without the key expanded equations, variation steps, or cancellation identities; this prevents independent assessment of a strong decoupling claim that underpins multiple barriers (including B14).
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (Scope paragraph, completeness argument) and App. C: The claim that the four routes are "basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes" (with Jackiw–Pi R∧˜R and parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner now "closed explicitly") rests on the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma and a finite enumeration, but the main text summarizes rather than reproduces the projection matrix, symbolic verification steps, or explicit operator list from the released script; without this, the completeness statement (central to ruling out omitted channels) cannot be verified at the required rigor for a no-go result.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F and abstract: Route 4 is closed only by a "naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection" (relocating the cosmological-constant problem rather than excluding amplitude), yet it is grouped with the amplitude-suppressed closures of R1–R3 under the single phrase "four-route no-go" and "channel-level closure"; this conflates qualitatively distinct arguments and overstates the uniformity of the result.
[MINOR] Sec. II C, App. B, and Sec. XII A: The single-scale NDA dimensional no-go (off-shell dimension +1 forcing ρ_ECH_Λ ∼ M_Pl^4) is logically sound under the stated assumptions, but the two admissible completions (on-shell curvature dressing vs. local-operator promotion) are treated as equivalent at order-of-magnitude level without demonstrating that both yield identical N_tot ≈ 92 bookkeeping and identical barrier constraints; a short explicit comparison table would remove ambiguity.
[MINOR] General (Secs. I, IV, IX): Repeated emphasis that results are "channel-level, assumption-conditional" and "not an operator-level theorem" is appropriate, but the manuscript still frames the outcome as a broad structural constraint on "minimal ECH dark-energy routes"; tightening the abstract and introduction wording to match the precise scope (explicitly enumerated channels + power-counting completeness under single-scale NDA) would eliminate any appearance of overclaim.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are each constrained at channel/amplitude level (R1–R3 by suppression or gap-equation exclusion; R4 by naturalness) under the stated assumptions, with perturbation transparency for canonical scalars—is supported by the dimensional power-counting, explicit route-by-route budgets, and structural arguments given in the manuscript.
