VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV D & Appendix D: The post-MASTER canonical-mask ℓ=1 residual (+3.64σ) is only ~53% reproduced by the imaging+morphology forward model, leaving an explicit ~47% unmodeled open item (amplitude ~Ap=0.32%). While the paper correctly bounds the entire residual below the real-space A50=0.75% floor, this incomplete systematics closure on a coherent low-ℓ structure that survives multiple nulls weakens the attribution to pure survey systematics and requires either full per-pixel morphology-purity modeling or a stronger demonstration that it cannot contaminate the primary estimator.
[MAJOR] Sec. II B & VI A: 66.5% of training labels are CE-ResNet pseudo-labels. The model-free GZ1-human-only cross-check (N≈46k, z=−0.54σ) is correctly presented as supportive but has ~4.5× coarser sensitivity (A50≈3.4%, A95≈4.5–6.8%); it therefore cannot exclude sub-percent survey-correlated inherited biases at the headline sensitivity level. The template-regression anchors mitigate but do not fully close this channel for the primary HC sample.
[MINOR] Sec. III B & IV C: The p_eq>0.6 primary-sample cut is stated as pre-registered via a specific git commit, which is good practice, but the manuscript should explicitly document the blinding status (was the dipole evaluated before the cut was frozen?) and whether any post-unblinding adjustments occurred.
[MINOR] Sec. IV C & V A: The block-bootstrap WLS template-fit exclusion (z≈−7.6 vs. Aref=0.017) and the amplitude-level tension with Shamir are useful, but both are model-dependent; the text correctly notes that a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is still required for a likelihood-level comparison— this caveat should be elevated in the abstract and conclusions.
[MINOR] Throughout (esp. Figs. 4–8, Tables I–VII): The manuscript is extremely dense with internal pipeline/artifact paths and json references. While reproducibility is excellent, key quantitative anchors (exact A95 interval, null distributions, reproduction fractions) should be made fully self-contained in the main text/figures so that a reader does not need the public repo to evaluate the central claims.

The central claim that the real-space chirality dipole on the pre-specified high-confidence subsample is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity (and that a genuine Shamir-scale signal would have been recovered) is supported by the primary estimator, injection-recovery calibration, and multiple robustness tests.
