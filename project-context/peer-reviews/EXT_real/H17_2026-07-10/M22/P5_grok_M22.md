VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] §V.B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths and pre-registration caveat): The post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast as the “designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered)” estimand, even while correctly declaring the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five void definitions as the “strictly quotable headline,” introduces unnecessary garden-of-forking-paths ambiguity for an upper-bound claim; the paper should remove the privileged framing and present the five estimators on fully equal footing in the abstract and conclusions.
[MINOR] §VIII (RSD treatment for DESIVAST and first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction): The fixed-void-geometry Monte Carlo and linear reconstruction bound the shift in Δf_CW to ≲0.37 pp (well inside the ≈0.9 pp envelope), but these tests are performed on the unrestricted or variant samples rather than the exact footprint-restricted primary estimand; a direct statement confirming membership stability (or quantifying any residual bias) under the same perturbations for the quoted n_void = 57,081 sample is required.
[MINOR] Abstract and §I (de-attenuated physical-chirality bound): The conversion of the ≈0.9 pp labelled-fraction envelope to the ≈2.26 pp physical-chirality bound via the factor (2a−1) ≃ 0.40 relies on the GZ1 accuracy floor reported in the companion Paper IV; a one-sentence self-contained summary of how this attenuation factor (and its uncertainty) is derived must be added so the key model constraint is interpretable without the companion manuscript.
[MINOR] §VI.A (T-Web secondary diagnostic): The T-Web void class contains only n = 428 spirals (n_void ranging 363–853 across the Phase-2 sweep), producing wide credible intervals that add negligible constraining power; the text should explicitly state that this path has low statistical power relative to DESIVAST, is retained solely for consistency checking, and does not contribute to the headline bound.
[MINOR] §II and §VIII.F (global monopole treatment and internal verification): While the catalog-wide Δf_CW ≈ −0.0026 offset is correctly treated as a classifier systematic and internally corroborated by the matched-sample f_CW^P5 = 0.49719, the paper should add a short explicit test (or citation to an artifact) confirming that this monopole shows no residual correlation with T-Web or DESIVAST environment class beyond the σ_pred subtraction already performed.

The central claim of no detectable environment dependence of spiral chirality (null Δf_CW consistent with zero within the reported systematic envelope across multiple void definitions) is supported by the data, statistical framework, and robustness checks.
