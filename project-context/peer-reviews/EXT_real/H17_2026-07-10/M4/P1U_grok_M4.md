VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract + Sec. IV (Scope paragraph) + Sec. IV b: The paper repeatedly and correctly states that the four-route assessment is a channel-level enumeration under single-scale NDA power counting and explicit ansätze, not a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator-level theorem. However, the title, opening abstract sentence, and executive summary (Table I) frame the result as a tight “channel-level closure” that “exhausts the fourth minimal-ECH dark-energy channel,” which risks overstating the result for readers who stop at the abstract; the completeness lemma (Fierz-by-Fierz projection + Bianchi vanishing + topological reduction) is only fully articulated in Sec. IV b and App. C and should be elevated to a numbered subsection in the main text with a one-paragraph self-contained summary.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F (Route 4) + abstract (“Naturalness Closure for R4”): The R4 argument is an explanatory-deficit / fine-tuning objection (single coupling forced to produce both β_obs and ρ_Λ requires m_θ ∼ H_0, relocating rather than solving the CC problem). This is conceptually valid but is not on the same logical footing as the amplitude-suppression arguments for R1–R3; it does not exclude the mechanism. The paper should re-label it consistently as a “naturalness objection” or “failure to solve the CC problem” rather than “closure,” and the title/abstract should reflect the heterogeneous character of the four constraints.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result) + footnote a: The central claim that the Holst dual contraction vanishes identically on the Levi-Civita connection by the algebraic Bianchi identity R_μ[νρσ] = 0 (hence decouples from scalar/tensor EOM for canonical scalar matter) is correct and interesting. However, the proof is presented at high level; the explicit term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion promised in Sec. X E is not shown in the provided text, and the precise statement of which perturbation orders and which matter sectors are covered is scattered across footnotes and caveats. The key Bianchi step and the Cartan-equation slaving for T = 0 should be written out with one or two explicit linearized equations in the main text.
[MINOR] Sec. II C, App. B, and multiple footnotes on mass-dimension bookkeeping: The on-shell (+1 → +4) versus local-operator-promotion readings of the parity-odd operator are discussed at length and stated to agree at order-of-magnitude level for N_tot ≈ 92. The two readings are nevertheless presented with different compensating powers of M_Pl; a single consolidated paragraph (or a small table in App. B) that shows both routes side-by-side with the identical numerical outcome would remove any appearance of ambiguity.
[MINOR] Overall presentation: The manuscript is 63 pages, footnote-dense, and contains extensive self-referential language (“this paper’s appendices,” “companion [2],” archived artifacts). While the main theoretical claims are asserted to be self-contained, the observational appendices (F–I) and the reliance on external sibling papers for even illustrative numbers reduce standalone readability. Condense repetitive passages (especially the repeated scope disclaimers) and move purely technical scripts/details to a clear “Supplementary Material” statement.

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are channel-level constrained (amplitude suppression for R1–R3 under stated single-scale NDA and scaling ansätze; naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection for R4) and that the Holst sector is perturbation-transparent for canonical scalar matter—is supported within the explicitly delimited scope by the dimensional power-counting argument, the Fierz projection lemma, the Bianchi-identity decoupling, and the regulated NJL gap-equation analysis.
