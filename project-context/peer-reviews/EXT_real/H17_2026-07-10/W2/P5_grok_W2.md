VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and §V B: The abstract leads with the specific post-hoc “primary” DESIVAST same-footprint estimand (∆fCW = +0.0018) and designates it for reporting, while explicitly stating in §V B/Table IV that this choice is exploratory/post-hoc with no timestamped pre-registration; the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five estimators is the only strictly quotable result, yet the abstract framing risks readers citing the single most-favorable row as the headline constraint.
[MINOR] Abstract, §I, and §XIII: The de-attenuated physical-chirality bound (~2.26 pp) is quoted for model-builders, but the additional unquantified uncertainty from the lack of an environment-stratified confusion matrix is noted only qualitatively; a sensitivity test or explicit inflation of the bound under plausible differential relabeling would make the model constraint more usable.
[MINOR] §II and abstract: The analysis is algebraically invariant to the Paper IV monopole and uses only public class_eq labels, but the full parity-equivariance validation, training details, and GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %) reside exclusively in the concurrent companion (arXiv placeholder); the manuscript requests co-review, which is appropriate, yet the placeholder and citation timing should be flagged more explicitly as a coordinated-submission caveat.
[MINOR] §IV A step 4 and §IX A: The T-Web void volume fraction and class assignments are shown to be sensitive to deduplication of repeat coadds and to the survey selection function (collapsing by ~23× under BGS-randoms weighting); while the DESIVAST primary path avoids this, the secondary T-Web results would benefit from a short quantitative statement of the residual selection-induced class migration after the re-projection.
[MINOR] §VIII and Table XI: The honest ~0.9 pp systematic envelope is obtained by quadrature of selected terms (membership, geometry, counting, footprint, etc.); the any-hole-vs-maximal-sphere geometry term (0.60 pp) is co-dominant, yet no covariance between membership and geometry is propagated, and the envelope is labeled a “sensitivity figure, not a firm systematic exclusion”—a brief Monte-Carlo propagation of the two dominant correlated terms would tighten the presentation.

The central claim that spiral-galaxy chirality shows no detectable void/non-void environmental dependence in DESI DR1 (family-wise Bonferroni-5 null |∆fCW| ≤ 0.004 across all five DESIVAST definitions) is supported by the multi-algorithm cross-check, explicit multiplicity control, and extensive robustness tests.
