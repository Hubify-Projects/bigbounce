VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. IV (esp. IV F), and title: R4 is closed only by a “naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection” (the same coupling that fits β_obs forces m_θ ∼ H_0, “relocating the CC problem”), while R1–R3 are closed by explicit amplitude suppression. These are not equivalent; naturalness is a subjective preference, not a mathematical or amplitude-level no-go. Equating them in the title and abstract overstates the strength of the R4 result and misleads readers on what “channel-level closure” actually means for that route.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C, App. B (and repeated in IV, IX, XIV): The single-scale NDA “dimensional no-go” for the parity-odd operator (off-shell mass dimension +1 forces ρ_ECH_Λ ∼ M_Pl^4, never (meV)^4) is correct as power counting but assumes precisely what must be shown—no new light scale μ ≪ M_Pl and no exact cancellation. This restates the cosmological constant problem rather than deriving a channel-specific obstruction; the argument is not circular only because no positive amplitude is claimed, but it therefore provides no new evidence against the enumerated routes beyond the standard EFT observation that minimal single-scale gravity cannot produce observed vacuum energy without tuning.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV “Scope” paragraph and completeness discussion: The paper asserts the four routes plus the two previously omitted parity-odd operators (Jackiw–Pi R∧R̃ and the γ_BI/(γ_BI²+1)·8πG vector–axial four-fermion partner) are now “basis-complete at the M_Pl-power-counting level” via the Fierz-by-Fierz lemma (App. C) and structural facts F1/F2. However, it simultaneously states that the four routes “are not proven to be a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis.” This internal tension leaves the central “four-route closure” claim ambiguous: if the enumeration is only illustrative, the no-go is weaker than advertised; if it is exhaustive within minimal ECH, the paper must demonstrate (not merely enumerate) that no other M_Pl-suppressed operator evades the NDA bound.
[MINOR] Sec. X (perturbation transparency): The result that torsion vanishes and the Holst dual contraction ϵ^μνρσ R_μνρσ ≡ 0 on the torsion-free branch for canonical scalar matter (via algebraic Bianchi identity) is technically plausible and explicitly verified term-by-term in the perturbed tetrad expansion. However, the scoping (“excluding propagating-torsion, dynamical-Immirzi, fermion-loop, and non-minimal-matter sectors”) is so restrictive that the result adds limited new insight beyond the well-known non-dynamical character of torsion in Einstein–Cartan theory; the paper should state whether this decoupling survives at second order or in the presence of the very parity-odd operators discussed in Sec. IV.
[MINOR] Overall structure and length (60 pp.): The manuscript is excessively repetitive (scope caveats, “channel-level not operator-level,” “under stated assumptions,” and the 14-barrier catalog are restated in abstract, introduction, Sec. IV, IX, XIV, and conclusions). Many of the 13/14 “distinct mechanism-class constraints” overlap (e.g., Planck suppression, scale separation, and perturbation transparency all trace to the same algebraic Cartan equation and M_Pl power counting). PRD expects concise presentation; the current length obscures the genuinely novel technical elements (explicit Fierz lemma, term-by-term perturbation verification, and the N_tot ≈ 92 vs. f_NL erasure tension in Sec. XIV D).

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are tightly constrained at channel-amplitude granularity (R1–R3 by explicit suppression, R4 by naturalness) and that the Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor perturbations for canonical matter—is only partially supported: the amplitude arguments for R1–R3 and the Bianchi-identity decoupling are technically sound under the stated assumptions, but the R4 “naturalness closure,” the asserted basis-completeness, and the single-scale NDA no-go do not constitute rigorous channel-level exclusions beyond restating known EFT and CC-problem limitations.
