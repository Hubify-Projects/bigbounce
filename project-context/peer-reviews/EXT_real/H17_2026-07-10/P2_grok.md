VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. II.A and Appendix A (referenced throughout): The central claim that Cai et al. [1] contains a spurious +(99/128) ∑_i k_i^3 term in the final polynomial collapse (their Eq. 37) that doubles the squeezed-limit amplitude from the correct vertex sum −35/16 to the printed −35/8 is asserted via re-summation of the four cubic vertices at ε=3/2, but the manuscript provides only a high-level summary (“certified vertex-by-vertex in Table VII”, “Eq. A5”, “Cai’s own ϵ-order-grouped intermediates”) without reproducing the explicit contributions of L_redef, L_ζζ̇², L_ζ̇∂ζ∂χ and L_ζ(∂i∂jχ)², their individual squeezed-limit reductions, or the precise algebraic step at which the extra local-shaped term appears. This is insufficient to substantiate a factor-of-two correction to the published literature.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (headline significance) and Sec. VII (systematic budget): The quoted 1.3–2.75σ range is assembled from qualitatively heterogeneous endpoints (CMB-Fisher signal-only r=0.876 vs. noise-weighted r≈0.84; additive-quadrature combination of template mismatch, ϵ-correction, photo-z, b_ϕ marginalization, GR-projection bracket with ρ≈0.95, and null-space scatter) without a joint multi-parameter Fisher matrix or covariance propagation. The paper correctly labels the result a “scoping sensitivity envelope” yet presents the range as the primary headline; the individual endpoints are not statistically comparable and the procedure understates the true joint uncertainty.
[MINOR] Sec. II.C (assumption (d) and load-bearing caveat ⋆): The assertion that cubic-order bispectrum transmission equals the contraction-phase value to O((k η_bounce)^2)∼10^{-4} (δf_NL≲10^{-3}) is derived from single-clock nonlinear superhorizon conservation plus a scaling estimate. While the single-clock argument (no new scalar dof, ˙ζ→0) is physically coherent for the Wilson-Ewing dressed-metric LQC model, the coefficient of the leading gradient correction is never computed; the bound remains an order-of-magnitude statement whose explicit evaluation at third order is stated to be “not required” but is in fact needed to close the largest model-dependence caveat.
[MINOR] Sec. III.B and independent Fisher validation (c13_independent_bounce_fisher.py): The reconciliation between the flat-weight shape cosine r=0.84 and the survey-optimal recovery reff≈0.99 is correctly attributed to squeezed-configuration weighting, but the tree-level Fisher limitations (Gaussian diagonal-triangle covariance only, b2/bs2 held fixed at fiducial, linear k_max, no fingers-of-God) are acknowledged yet not propagated into any uncertainty band on reff or on the headline recast. The claim that “the ratio reff is far more robust” is plausible but unquantified.
[MINOR] Overall presentation: The 38-page manuscript is excessively dense, with repeated parenthetical caveats, long self-referential justifications, and cross-references that fragment the logical thread (e.g., the r=0.84 derivation, the Bayes-factor formula, and the GR bracket appear in multiple places with slightly varying numerical endpoints). Key quantitative results are difficult to extract without reading the entire text; the manuscript would benefit from substantial streamlining and relocation of technical appendices.

The central claim that the corrected f_NL=−35/16 yields a SPHEREx detection significance of ∼1.3–2.75σ (post-systematics) and Bayes factor ∼9–14 against multifield competitors is supported by the explicit template-overlap integration, the independent multi-tracer Fisher validation (reff≈0.99), and the closed-form Bayes-factor formula cross-checked against three Monte Carlo ensembles, all conditional on the listed assumptions and on the discrepancy resolution whose explicit algebra is only summarized.
