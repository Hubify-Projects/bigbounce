VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section IV C (primary real-space dipole) and VI B (injection-recovery): The text states that the GZ1-measured chirality accuracy of 69.91% (g = 2a − 1 dilution) “is folded into the empirical injection-recovery floors,” yet the generative model is described as p_CW( n̂_p ) = p_global + (A/2) cos θ with sampling around the already-classified catalog; it is not explicit whether the quoted A_50 ≈ 0.75% and A_95 ∈ (1.0 %, 1.5 %] thresholds refer to injected amplitude at the observed-label level or the true physical amplitude after dilution. This ambiguity affects how directly readers can map the sensitivity claim (“a genuine Shamir-scale dipole would have been detected”) to the reported 1.7 %–4.0 % range.
[MINOR] Section IV D and Appendix D (monopole+mask leakage and eight-anchor battery): While correctly concluded that the unexplained ≈47 % of the post-MASTER ℓ = 1 residual lies below the real-space recovery threshold and does not alter the primary null, the forward-modeling result (|a_sys_1| = 3.75 × 10^{-3}, ≈53–54 % of observed) is presented without a concise quantitative bound on the maximum cosmological (coherent dipole) fraction still allowed by the unmodeled remainder; adding one sentence tying the remainder explicitly to < A_50 even under the most conservative assumption would tighten the systematics attribution.
[MINOR] Overall manuscript structure and presentation (Secs. III–IV and appendices): The paper is extremely dense, with the primary cosmological claims (real-space HC dipole +0.41σ and block-bootstrap WLS exclusion z ≈ −7.6) embedded among many secondary diagnostics, pre-registered cuts, artifact file references, and an eight-anchor table whose individual entries are only summarized in the main text. Several robustness panels and the full per-quartile/leg cross-power numbers could be moved to supplementary material or condensed without loss of rigor, improving readability for a PRD audience while preserving the explicit decision tree in Table I.

The central claim—that the large-scale chirality dipole is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity under the adopted pipeline and that a clean 1.7 % Shamir-scale signal is disfavored—is supported by the pre-specified primary real-space estimator, the injection-recovery calibration, the model-independent GZ1 cross-check, and the template-fit exclusion.
