VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section VIII B/E, “primary footprint-restricted estimand”: the control footprint is constructed from the union of DESIVAST hole-sphere angular discs and their radial span, not from the published BGS/DESIVAST completeness mask or DESI random catalogs; the manuscript explicitly acknowledges that it does not match fiber completeness, imaging depth, or radial selection, but later calls it a “same-selection-function” estimator. The resulting comparison is between void galaxies and a data-dependent, void-proximate subset of non-void galaxies, not a demonstrably selection-matched void/non-void sample. The primary contrast must be recomputed with the actual angular/radial mask and randoms, or with non-void controls matched or weighted in redshift, sky position, magnitude, size, morphology, confidence, imaging leg, and target program.

[MAJOR] Section V B and Tables IV/XIV, post-hoc primary designation and multiplicity: the exact footprint-restricted VoidFinder estimator used in the abstract is not the VoidFinder member tabulated in the stated Bonferroni-5 family, which instead uses the approximate k=20, unrestricted control. The multiplicity family also omits the footprint choice, exact-versus-k=20 membership, maximal-sphere alternative, and the prior selection among T-Web, DESIVAST, ASTRA, and Tempel paths. Bonferroni correction over five variants selected after inspecting a much larger analysis tree does not yield the advertised family-wise inference for the chosen primary bound. The result should be labeled exploratory throughout unless confirmed on independent data or analyzed with a complete, pre-specified estimator family.

These quantities are neither independent Gaussian nuisance errors nor consistently defined estimates of the same parameter, so their root-sum-square has no valid 95% or 2σ interpretation. [MAJOR] Abstract, Sections V B/XII/XV, numerical upper bound: even accepting the manuscript’s quadrature value, the approximately 0.94 percentage-point half-width must be centered on the measured +0.18 percentage-point contrast, giving approximately [−0.76,+1.12] percentage points and hence an absolute upper limit near 1.12 percentage points, not 0.9. This agrees with the manuscript’s own least-constraining simultaneous Bonferroni interval. Under the stated attenuation factor 0.3982, that would map to roughly 2.8, not 2.26, percentage points before accounting for the other methodological deficiencies.

[MAJOR] Sections V and VIII, uncertainty model: the primary two-proportion z-test assumes independent Bernoulli observations, although galaxies share voids, HEALPix regions, imaging conditions, target-selection fields, and classifier systematics. The large galaxy count does not remove this spatial and group-level dependence. The primary uncertainty and upper bound require a void-level and sky-block bootstrap, cluster-robust model, or spatially stratified permutation test; the full-sample HEALPix diagnostics do not substitute for a direct uncertainty estimate on the DESIVAST contrast.

[MAJOR] Appendix A and Sections VIII F/XII B, classifier monopole and de-attenuation: a spatially constant classifier monopole cancels algebraically, but environment-dependent sensitivity or specificity does not. The void-stratified human-label validation contains only 933 void objects and has an error-asymmetry interval of several percentage points, explicitly too broad to exclude differential misclassification at the claimed sub-percent scale. Moreover, the 69.91% “accuracy floor” is not a calibrated accuracy for the primary sample, and dividing by 2a−1 requires known, symmetric, non-differential errors. The manuscript therefore cannot convert its classifier-label result into a quantitative physical-chirality bound without an environment-stratified measurement-error model or substantially stronger human-label validation.

[MAJOR] Sections VIII and XIII, RSD treatment: randomly perturbing memberships while holding the observed null labels fixed mainly demonstrates that random reassignment preserves an existing null; it does not quantify attenuation of a possible true environmental signal. The purported Zel’dovich check displaces galaxies and published holes without rerunning VoidFinder on a reconstructed tracer field, and it is applied to the unrestricted rather than the primary estimator. Consequently, the quoted 0.024-percentage-point RSD systematic is not a validated uncertainty on the headline measurement. A meaningful bound requires injection–recovery of known chirality signals through a reconstructed catalog and a rerun of the void finder, or the quantitative RSD claim should be removed.

[MAJOR] Sections IV, VI, VII, and IX A, T-Web analysis: the canonical field uses a global mean density in a highly non-uniform, irregular survey volume without the necessary selection-function correction. The random-weighted rebuild changes most galaxy class assignments and alters the void volume fraction by over an order of magnitude, demonstrating that the canonical labels predominantly encode survey selection and boundaries rather than a stable cosmic-web classification. Obtaining the same chirality null after radically repartitioning an already near-parity label set is not independent validation. The T-Web analysis should either be rebuilt with standard random-catalog weighting and validated mocks over the relevant volume or be removed from the main evidentiary chain and substantially shortened.

[MAJOR] Sections XII B/XV and Appendix B, physical interpretation: the primary DESIVAST estimator has no defined 25Mpc/h smoothing scale, so the manuscript cannot transfer the secondary T-Web smoothing scale to the primary void bound. No cited bounce or inflation model predicts the tested observable, and Appendix B explicitly presents a non-covariant, non-derived toy operator. The claims that model builders should use the quoted number, or that the result constrains a “bounce-chirality coupling class,” are therefore unsupported; the paper should be framed as an empirical catalog-level null unless a concrete model-to-observable calculation is supplied.

[MAJOR] Sections XIII and Appendices A/D/E, companion-paper and reproducibility dependency: the central handedness labels, training provenance, accuracy claims, and monopole characterization depend on an unpublished Paper IV with an arXiv placeholder, while the archival DOI is also pending. A referee cannot fully assess the dominant measurement instrument or reproduce the analysis from a submission that points to mutable repository material and unavailable companion documentation. Coordinated review of Paper IV and a frozen, checksummed archival release must precede acceptance.

[MINOR] Sections VIII A–E and Tables X/XIII/XIV, catalog-definition consistency: the manuscript alternates among 1,489 “interior” VoidFinder voids, 3,765 maximal voids, 101,863 hole spheres, exact and k=20 memberships, and unrestricted and footprint-restricted parents without one authoritative cut-flow table. The exact NGC/SGC quality flags, treatment of edge voids, membership semantics, and integer counts for every primary-family estimator should be consolidated and reconciled.

[MINOR] Overall presentation: the manuscript is highly repetitive, with the same caveats, null statements, and monopole arguments restated across the abstract, main text, limitations, conclusions, and appendices. The main paper should be reduced substantially, with secondary T-Web diagnostics, artifact inventories, speculative EFT material, and extended robustness tables moved to supplemental material; statements that the “null holds” should be replaced by confidence-interval or equivalence-language appropriate to a post-hoc non-detection.

CENTRAL CLAIM: The data support only an exploratory non-detection of a difference in the classifier-labelled CW fraction within the analyzed redshift-space samples; they do not support the quoted 0.9-percentage-point/2.26-percentage-point physical bound or the claimed bounce/inflation-model constraint.
