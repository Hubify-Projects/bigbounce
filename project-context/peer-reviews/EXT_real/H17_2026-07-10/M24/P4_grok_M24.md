Review this manuscript to the standard of a real submission. Respond with exactly: (1) VERDICT: ACCEPT / MINOR REVISIONS / MAJOR REVISIONS / REJECT. (2) ISSUES: numbered, each prefixed [MAJOR] or [MINOR], naming the specific section/claim and concrete problem. (3) One sentence: is the central claim supported?Thought for 32sVERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. VI A (and cross-referenced Sec. II B, Appendix D): The 66.5% CE-ResNet pseudo-label fraction in training creates a potential channel for inherited large-scale survey-correlated bias in the production Catalog C labels. The fully model-independent GZ1-human-label dipole test (N≈4.6×10^4, z=−0.54σ) is correctly performed and returns null, but its sensitivity floor (A95≈4.5–6.8%) is ~3–4× coarser than the headline HC sample (A95∈[1.0%,1.5%]); it therefore cannot independently bound sub-percent inherited structure at the precision required to support the central null claim. The eight-anchor template-regression/cross-spectrum battery constrains only the components aligned with the chosen imaging/morphology basis; any orthogonal inherited pattern would still have to be bounded by the block-bootstrap WLS and injection-recovery, but the paper does not provide an explicit quantitative ceiling on the maximum undetected inherited dipole amplitude consistent with all anchors simultaneously.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D (and Appendix D): The imaging+morphology forward model reproduces only ≈52–54% of the observed post-MASTER canonical-mask ℓ=1 residual amplitude (with correct sign and direction, cosθ≈+0.83–0.84); the remaining ≳47% is left as an explicit open item. While the paper correctly shows that even the full residual amplitude lies below the primary real-space estimator’s A50=0.75% and A95 recovery thresholds (and therefore does not affect the null conclusion), a concise quantitative statement of the maximum cosmological fraction still allowed in the unmodelled remainder, plus a short description of the planned per-galaxy confidence-vs-depth purity map follow-up, would strengthen the systematics closure.
[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I: The pre-registration of the p_eq>0.6 cut (fixed in the generator script before any dipole evaluation, commit 94113e5) is stated but buried; elevating this fact and the explicit confidence-cut sweep stability (|z|<1.2 for p_eq≥0.6) into the abstract or a dedicated “Analysis hierarchy and pre-registration” paragraph would make the robustness of the primary sample definition more immediately transparent to readers.
[MINOR] Sec. V A and Conclusions: The amplitude-level tension with Shamir (and the explicit statement that a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is still required for a likelihood-level exclusion of that specific estimator) is appropriately caveated in the body, but the abstract’s phrasing (“disfavors a clean Aref=0.017 dipole”) could be read as a stronger exclusion than intended; repeating the “clean dipole under this pipeline” qualifier in the abstract and final paragraph would eliminate any risk of over-interpretation.
[MINOR] General (Methods/Results presentation): The manuscript is exceptionally thorough and the estimator decision tree (Table I) plus hierarchy declaration are exemplary, yet the extreme density and heavy reliance on appendix/artifact-file cross-references make it difficult for a reader to reconstruct the full logical chain without the supplementary material. A short “key methodological choices at a glance” box or expanded one-paragraph summary of the injection generative model and block-bootstrap implementation in the main text would improve accessibility without lengthening the paper.
