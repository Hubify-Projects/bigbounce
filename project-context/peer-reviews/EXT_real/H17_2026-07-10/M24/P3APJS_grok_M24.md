Review this manuscript to the standard of a real submission. Respond with exactly: (1) VERDICT: ACCEPT / MINOR REVISIONS / MAJOR REVISIONS / REJECT. (2) ISSUES: numbered, each prefixed [MAJOR] or [MINOR], naming the specific section/claim and concrete problem. (3) One sentence: is the central claim supported?Thought for 29sVERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract, §1, and §3 (three-tier structure): The primary deliverable is framed as a single “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies”; however, this combined number includes the NEOWISE component (419 objects) that passes only a post-hoc geometry-QA mask and explicitly fails the detector-sensitivity injection-recovery gate applied to DESI, SDSS, and Planck. The uniform “validated” label is therefore unsupported for the aggregate catalog.
[MAJOR] §2.4 (Path-C protocol), §3.5 (eROSITA), and §3.7 (Gaia): The manuscript admits that the eROSITA production score axis (0.259 threshold) is irreproducible from any committed artifact (fails 16 monotone rescalings + IsolationForest retrains) and that the original Gaia tier was synthetic placeholder data; while both are excised from counts, the length of discussion and the need for a separate “membership-list-only” release reveal fundamental provenance failures that undermine the reproducibility claims made for the overall pipeline.
[MAJOR] §3.1 and Table 3: The DESI component (195,829 objects, the dominant term in the 268k headline) is shown by positional cross-match to consist of only ~1.3% (2,468) objects on validated science-target spectra; the remaining ~98.7% lie on sky fibers/fillers. The “catalog-grade” and “like-for-like benchmark” language therefore overstates the science-ready yield relative to the Liang et al. (2023) comparison.
[MAJOR] §2.2 and §6.4(i): Injection-recovery validation is reported only for the broad/continuum-dominated class (99–100% at 5σ for DESI); narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, a sensitivity floor that is disclosed but not propagated into the per-object validity flags or the headline “validated” definition. This renders the sensitivity claim incomplete for the full anomaly population.
[MINOR] Throughout (especially §1, §3, Table 2 footnotes): The text is excessively defensive and repetitive (“process-volume figure”, “read this before Table 2”, repeated caveats on multipliers, “not a detection”). This obscures the actual results and violates ApJS expectations for concise, unambiguous catalog papers.
[MINOR] §5: The multi-tracer fNL and NANOGrav sections are explicitly labeled “secondary demonstrations” that return null results; they add no new cosmological constraint and dilute the catalog focus. They should be removed or relegated to a short appendix.
