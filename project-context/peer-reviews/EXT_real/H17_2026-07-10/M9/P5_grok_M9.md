VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section V.B (and abstract, Table IV): Primary analysis path (DESIVAST-anchored void vs. non-void Δf_CW) is explicitly designated post-hoc after inspecting multiple correlated estimators (VoidFinder/V2-REVOLVER/V2-VIDE + GALZONE variants); while Bonferroni-5 family null is reported, this introduces unavoidable selection among estimators that tightens the quoted simultaneous bound, violating standard pre-registration expectations for a null-result claim in PRD.
[MAJOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment) and §XIII: Fixed-geometry membership + first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction bounds coherent outflow shift at only 0.024 pp, but full nonlinear iterative re-derivation of the void catalog (VoidFinder rerun on reconstructed density field) is not performed; the disclosed Monte-Carlo (fixed centers/radii) therefore underestimates boundary-flip systematics for galaxies near Reff ≳ 10 Mpc/h edges.
[MINOR] Section VI.A and §VI.D: T-Web classes are strongly non-orthogonal to DESI target program (χ² = 4933, p ≪ 10^{-300}, Cramér’s V = 0.078); the ~2.1σ bright/dark filament sign-flip is flagged as diagnostic, yet no end-to-end selection-function mock is shown to demonstrate that the observed amplitude is fully reproduced by known imaging-leg residuals, leaving a residual ambiguity that could leak into secondary claims.
[MINOR] Section II and VIII.F (monopole handling + de-attenuation): Physical-chirality bound of ≈2.26 pp assumes environment-independent classifier misclassification (attenuation factor 2a−1 ≃ 0.40 from GZ1 κ = 0.40); no explicit test (e.g., per-environment accuracy or confusion-matrix stratification) is provided to justify this assumption for the void vs. non-void contrast.
[MINOR] Section VIII and Table XI: The ≈0.9 pp effective 2σ systematic envelope is presented as quadrature sum with geometry term co-dominant (0.60 pp), but the explicit per-term breakdown, covariance assumptions, and propagation of the 1″ match-radius/footprint/confidence contributions are not tabulated in the main text or a self-contained appendix, impairing immediate referee reproducibility.

The central claim (no significant environment-conditional chirality signature at ≳25 Mpc/h with |Δf_CW| ≲ 0.9 pp labelled / ≲ 2.3 pp physical) is supported by the multi-algorithm DESIVAST null, permutation tests, and RSD-bounded systematics, subject to the post-hoc and reconstruction limitations disclosed.
