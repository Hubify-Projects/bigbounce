VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Sec. IV D and Appendix D: The imaging+morphology forward model reproduces only ~52–54% of the observed post-MASTER canonical-mask ℓ=1 residual amplitude (|a1| = 6.95 × 10^{-3}), leaving an explicit ~47% open item. While the authors correctly bound even the full residual below the real-space A50/A95 recovery thresholds and note that the primary HC real-space estimator returns null, a quantitative propagation test (e.g., injecting the residual template into the HC p_eq > 0.6 subsample and re-running the dipole fit) would close this loop and strengthen the claim that unmodeled structure “does not affect the primary null.”
[MINOR] Sec. VI A and Abstract: The model-independent GZ1-human-only dipole test (z = −0.54σ) is presented as important corroboration, but its sensitivity floor (A95 ≈ 4.5–6.8%) is ~4–5× coarser than the headline HC result. The text should state more explicitly that this test rules out large inherited biases but does not independently constrain sub-percent coherent structure that could be inherited through the 66.5% CE-ResNet pseudo-labels; the template-regression anchors remain the operative defense.
[MINOR] Abstract, Sec. IV C, and Table I: The block-bootstrap WLS template-fit result (z ≈ −7.6 against Aref = 0.017) is given prominent weight as a “primary” exclusion, yet the paper itself qualifies it as a model-dependent template-disfavor statistic under NSIDE=8 spatial covariance, not a calibrated frequentist exclusion. This distinction should be stated in the abstract and reinforced in the decision-tree table to prevent readers from interpreting it as a direct 7.6σ upper limit on Shamir-scale signals.
[MINOR] Sec. IV C and VI B: The pre-registered p_eq > 0.6 cut and the full confidence-cut sweep are excellent, but the manuscript should add a short explicit statement that the HC subsample selection function is not propagated into the block-bootstrap covariance matrix (as already noted in passing), and confirm that the injection-recovery floor was recomputed on the exact HC selection used for the primary result.
[MINOR] Throughout (esp. Sec. IV D, Appendix D, and Data Availability): The paper is admirably thorough and reproducible (public catalog, weights, scripts, committed artifacts), but the proliferation of distinct null procedures, σ conventions, and MC sizes risks reader confusion. A single consolidated “null-procedure decision tree” table (expanding Table I) placed early in Sec. III or IV, plus a short “how to interpret the different σ values” paragraph, would improve clarity without lengthening the manuscript.

One sentence: The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole on the pre-specified high-confidence (p_eq > 0.6) subsample of 949,584 spirals is consistent with null at +0.41σ while the pipeline has demonstrated sensitivity to recover a genuine Shamir-scale (A ≈ 1.7%) dipole—is supported by the primary estimator, the injection-recovery calibration, the pre-registered cut, and the extensive systematics battery.
