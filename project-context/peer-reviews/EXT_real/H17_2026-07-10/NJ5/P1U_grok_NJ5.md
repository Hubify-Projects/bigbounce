VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV and abstract: The "four-route channel-level closure" is presented as exhaustive at the M_Pl-power-counting level within minimal ECH, but the supporting argument (structural facts F1–F2 plus NDA monotonicity + Fierz lemma in App. C) only closes the listed operators; it does not rigorously demonstrate that no other dimension-6 or higher parity-odd operators (e.g., additional curvature–torsion or non-minimal completions) can evade the single-scale NDA bound without introducing a new light scale. The paper itself repeatedly qualifies this as "not a full operator-level theorem.
[MAJOR] Sec. IX (Barriers 1–14) and abstract: The catalog of "13 distinct mechanism-class constraints (14 historical entries)" largely repackages the core amplitude-suppression and perturbation-transparency arguments already derived in Secs. IV and X, plus standard EFT naturalness considerations. Several barriers (e.g., B4 Planck suppression, B14 perturbation transparency, B1 mass-coupling lock) are not independent derivations but restatements or consequences of the same calculations, inflating the count without adding new physical content.
[MAJOR] Sec. X and abstract: The "perturbation-transparency result" (torsion vanishes and Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor perturbations around the torsion-free branch for canonical scalar matter) follows directly from the algebraic Cartan constraint + first Bianchi identity R^μ_[νρσ]=0 and is a standard feature of Einstein–Cartan theory in the torsion-free limit. Its presentation as a central novel theorem, with extensions to "Tier I–III" and discrimination among bouncing cosmologies, overstates its originality and scope (explicitly excludes fermions, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings).
[MAJOR] Throughout (esp. Secs. II C, IV F, XII A, App. B): The mapping from the parity-odd operator (naive mass dimension +1) to ρ_Λ ~ (meV)^4 via single-scale NDA and N_tot ≈ 92 dilution is a phenomenological ansatz, not derived from the ECH action. The paper correctly identifies this as relocating (not solving) the CC problem for R4, but the repeated emphasis on "channel-level amplitude closure" and "no positive amplitude derived" creates an impression of a stronger no-go than the dimensional bookkeeping actually provides.
[MINOR] Presentation and length: The 63-page manuscript (with 6+ appendices containing MCMC details, NaMaster validation, and reproducibility manifests stated to be "non-load-bearing") violates PRD expectations for conciseness. Core claims (four-route closure + transparency theorem) could be stated rigorously in ≤15–20 pages; the proliferation of Foundations A–G, Branches H/J/L/M/N/O, and self-references to unpublished companion papers and an internal GitHub reproducibility tree obscures the logic.
[MINOR] Sec. IV A and App. D: The regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion of a vacuum scalar condensate relies on the Fierz projection lemma (App. C) showing a repulsive scalar channel (G_scalar = −3/64 κ < 0). While the sign and sub-criticality appear internally consistent under the mean-field framework used, the result is sensitive to the precise Fierz rearrangement and cutoff scheme; a brief explicit tabulation of all four channels (SS, PP, VV, AA) with numerical coefficients would strengthen reproducibility.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed under the stated assumptions (amplitude suppression for R1–R3 via Planck/M_Pl factors and scaling ansätze; naturalness/explanatory-deficit closure for R4 via m_θ ~ H_0 tuning)—is supported by the torsion-elimination derivation, Bianchi-identity decoupling, Fierz projection, and single-scale NDA power counting.
