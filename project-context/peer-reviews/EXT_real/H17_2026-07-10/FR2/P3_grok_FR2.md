VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, title, and §I: The primary deliverable is framed as a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” with large process-volume multipliers (~141×, ~73×), yet the text explicitly states that validation is mixed (NEOWISE passes only a masking-geometry QA gate, not detector-sensitivity injection-recovery), ~98.7% of DESI anomalies lie on non-primary science-target spectra (sky/filler fibers), and the like-for-like science-target yield is only 2,468 clusters (~0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark on a comparable target selection). This framing risks overstating the catalog’s immediate scientific utility relative to prior single-survey work.
[MAJOR] §III E and §II D (Path-C protocol): The eROSITA production score axis is irreproducible (0.259 recovered on none of 16 monotone rescalings or 3 IsolationForest retrains; non-monotone in the committed raw-score artifact; Spearman ρ = −0.10 on top entries), prompting post-hoc excision of the tier from all counts. While transparently disclosed, this provenance failure raises questions about end-to-end documentation and reproducibility standards applied to the retained tiers (DESI, SDSS, Planck, NEOWISE).
[MAJOR] §II D, §III, and §VI D(i) (validation gates): The six-step Path-C protocol applies heterogeneous criteria—DESI/SDSS/Planck clear detector-sensitivity injection-recovery (broad class only for DESI; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ), Planck is accepted despite val loss = 0.4437 > 0.30, NEOWISE uses geometry QA only (passing by construction), and LAMOST/eROSITA fail and are reclassified exploratory/excised. The 5-fold and OOD checks for DESI use deliberately short-trained proxy models (val loss > 0.30), so they are correlated stability probes rather than independent confirmations; the single production-ensemble gate therefore carries disproportionate weight for the headline count.
[MINOR] §V (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL measurement returns α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65 (0.29σ from null) and a noise-driven 9.4% central shift in σ(fNL) that lies inside the 1σ envelope of the single-tracer baseline; the NANOGrav γ result is only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively rules out only an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference. These sections read as methodological illustrations of a null result rather than substantive cosmological constraints and could be condensed.
[MINOR] §IV A and Table II (novelty and cross-match statistics): The 17.8% genuine-novelty fraction (Wilson CI ±1.2%) is reported for the DESI top-1,000 only; the much higher SIMBAD-unmatched fractions (~99% DESI, 90% SDSS, ~50% LAMOST) are repeatedly used as proxies while the text correctly notes they largely reflect database coverage rather than astrophysical novelty. Downstream users could misinterpret unmatched rates without stronger per-object caveats or flags.

The central claim—that a large multi-survey anomaly-candidate catalog has been produced by native-trained autoencoders, subjected to per-survey validation gates, and reduced to a deduplicated 268,519-object validated subset with explicit limitations—is supported by the committed scripts, injection-recovery artifacts, and unusually candid self-auditing, but requires clearer reframing of “validated catalog-grade” status, uniform validation strength, and the distinction between process-volume and science-target yield to meet PRD standards.
