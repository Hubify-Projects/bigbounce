VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract (and page 1): The abstract is excessively long, dense, and contains a proliferation of incommensurable numerical results (multiple distinct σ/z values from pixel-permutation, label-shuffle, block-bootstrap, and MASTER channels), pre-specified cuts, and detailed limitations; this violates PRD norms for concise abstracts that foreground the primary scientific result and method.

[MINOR] Sec. IV C and IV D (primary real-space estimator and confidence-cut sweep): The p_eq > 0.6 cut is stated as pre-specified in the generator script, but the full paper (including the HC subsample definition for the primary +0.41σ result) must explicitly document the immutable commit hash/date of that script in the main text (not only Data Availability) and confirm that no post-unblinding adjustments were made to the exact threshold or mask after inspecting dipole outcomes.

[MINOR] Sec. IV D and Appendix D (monopole+mask leakage and eight-anchor battery): The imaging+morphology forward model reproduces only ≈53% of the post-MASTER canonical ℓ=1 residual amplitude (|a1| = 6.95 × 10^{-3}), leaving an explicit ~47% remainder whose origin is unresolved; although the paper correctly bounds this remainder below the real-space A95 and states it does not affect primary conclusions, the text must add a quantitative demonstration (e.g., via the existing template-regression machinery) that any spatially coherent component of this remainder cannot leak into the uniform-weight real-space dipole fit on the HC subsample at a level that would alter the +0.41σ or A95 statements.

[MINOR] Sec. V A (comparison to Shamir): The amplitude-level tension (~7–18×) with Shamir’s reported 1.7%–4.0% range is presented prominently, but the paper must state more explicitly in the abstract/introduction (beyond the current parenthetical) that this is not a statistical exclusion of Shamir’s distinct Ganalyzer estimator and that a matched-footprint reanalysis remains required; the current phrasing risks overstating the discrepancy as a direct refutation.

[MINOR] Sec. III A and Table I: While Table I is a useful decision tree, the proliferation of σ values from distinct null procedures (isotropic pixel-permutation, per-galaxy label-shuffle, block-bootstrap, MASTER label-shuffle) throughout Results requires a single, prominent “reader’s note” box immediately after the significance conventions subsection that reiterates only rows P1–P2 carry cosmological weight and that all other σ/z are incommensurable diagnostics.

[MINOR] Sec. VI B (injection-recovery): The A50 ≈ 0.75% and A95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%] falsification thresholds are derived from NMC,inj = 100 per amplitude on the HC-broad sample; the paper must add a short statement confirming that the axis-averaged P(σ > 3) recovery fractions are stable under both area-uniform (primary) and fixed-axis injections, and that the quoted A95 bracket already incorporates the dilution factor g = 2a − 1 from the external GZ1 chirality accuracy floor.

One sentence on the central claim: The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole on the high-confidence (p_eq > 0.6) subsample of 949,584 spirals is consistent with null (+0.41σ vs. the isotropic pixel-permutation null, empirical rank p = 0.31) and that a clean 1.7% cosmological dipole is disfavored—is supported by the primary estimator, the independent GZ1-human-label cross-check (z = −0.54σ), and the injection-recovery calibration, with the harmonic-channel residuals appropriately classified as non-cosmological diagnostics.
