[MAJOR] Section X: the “perturbation-transparency theorem” is correct only in a much narrower and essentially standard sense. For a classical constant-γ ECH action with canonical scalar matter, zero spin density implies zero torsion, after which the Holst contraction vanishes by the algebraic Bianchi identity. This does not establish GR perturbation equations for the LQC holonomy-corrected bounce invoked elsewhere, nor for fermion loops, a dynamical Immirzi field, propagating torsion, boundaries, or nonminimal matter. Those sectors include most of the mechanisms discussed in Routes 2–4 and in the bounce phenomenology. The result should be presented as a limited classical lemma, not used to subsume other barriers or to support a model-wide closure.
