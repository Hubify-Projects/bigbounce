VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. IV (entire), and App. B/C: The central “four-route channel-level closure” framing and the repeated claim of “basis-complete within minimal ECH at the MPl-power-counting level” rest on the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (App. C) and the explicit dim-4 parity-odd operator enumeration plus two tensor identities (App. B 1). While the paper states these are proven/verified symbolically and supplies the released script, the main text provides no sketch of the 6-operator basis, the key Fierz matrix, or the Bianchi-vanishing step; readers cannot assess the completeness argument without the appendices. This is a load-bearing claim for the “exhausts the fourth minimal-ECH channel” language and must be made self-contained or the scope language tightened.

[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result) and claim in abstract/Sec. I: The result is advertised as “the central result” and “Tier I”. The main-text statement is only a one-paragraph sketch plus “explicit verification” and “term-by-term” bullets; the actual expansion of the perturbed tetrad, the sourcing of torsion perturbations by scalar-matter stress-energy at each order, and the demonstration that the Holst dual contraction vanishes identically on the Levi-Civita connection are not shown. Because the result is used to subsume Barrier B8 into B14 and to argue decoupling from all scalar/tensor EOM, the key algebraic steps must appear in the main text (or a short dedicated subsection) with the explicit perturbed Cartan equation.

[MAJOR] Sec. II C, Eq. (12), App. B, and Sec. XII A: The mapping from the off-shell dimension-+1 parity-odd operator (Eq. 6) to a late-time ρΛ ∼ (meV)4 requires a phenomenological on-shell curvature dressing (or local-operator promotion) that inserts MPl factors and yields the Ntot ≈ 92 bookkeeping. The paper correctly flags this as “phenomenological dimensional ansatz beyond the minimal framework” and notes it relocates the CC problem, but the two alternative readings (on-shell dressing vs. off-shell promotion) are not shown to give numerically consistent order-of-magnitude Ntot; the dilution factor Dinf and the precise power counting that produces ΔNtot ≈ 4 must be derived explicitly once, not asserted.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV F (R4) and abstract: R4 is included as one of the “four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes” and closed only by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection (same coupling that fits βobs forces mθ ∼ H0, relocating the CC tuning). However, a spectator ALP (or neutrino current) is an additional field; the paper itself states that R4 is “not amplitude-suppressed but … closed by this naturalness … route” and that the parity-odd CMB coupling is “via spectator ALP”. Labeling it a “minimal-ECH” channel while simultaneously treating the ALP as external blurs the boundary the paper otherwise maintains for R1–R3. Either reclassify R4 explicitly as an extension or remove it from the “four-route” count and state that only three internal minimal-ECH channels are amplitude-closed.

[MINOR] Sec. IX (14-barrier catalog) and Sec. I: The catalog of 7 Foundations + 6 Branches producing 13 distinct mechanism-class constraints (with B8 subsumed by B14) is presented as a major organizational result, yet several barriers (e.g., thermal-reset argument supporting B14, attractor-sensitivity, UV→IR specificity) are conditional, order-of-magnitude, or rest on the same underlying algebraic facts already used for the four-route closures. The catalog adds length and repetition without introducing independent quantitative exclusions; condensing to a single summary table with explicit cross-references to the four-route arguments would improve focus.

[MINOR] Title, abstract, and repeated scope disclaimers: The title is 28 words long and the abstract contains multiple parenthetical qualifications (“under stated assumptions”, “channel-level assessment, not an operator-level theorem”, “R4 is not amplitude-suppressed but …”). While the disclaimers are intellectually honest, their cumulative effect makes the headline claim (“Channel-Level Constraints … Amplitude Closure for R1–R3, Naturalness Closure for R4”) difficult to parse on first reading. A shorter title and a single crisp scope paragraph early in the introduction would strengthen presentation.

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at the channel-amplitude (or naturalness) level under the stated single-scale NDA and scaling assumptions, and that the Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor perturbations for canonical scalar matter—is supported by the dimensional power-counting, the explicit Fierz projection and Bianchi-identity arguments, the regulated NJL gap-equation analysis, and the perturbation expansion presented, once the technical lemmas are made verifiable in the main text and the scope of “minimal ECH” versus extensions is stated unambiguously.
