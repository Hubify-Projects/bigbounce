VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §1 (Introduction), and §3 (three-tier structure): The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” (with “Validation establishes that the 268,519 subset is real”) is not uniformly supported; NEOWISE (419 objects) passes only a masking-geometry QA gate by construction, not detector-sensitivity injection-recovery, while LAMOST (~113k, 98% blue-excess artifact) and eROSITA (298, irreproducible production score axis) are either exploratory or excised from every count, undermining the “catalog-grade” framing for a journal data product.
[MAJOR] §3.1 and Table 3: The like-for-like science-target yield on validated DESI spectra is only 2,468 anomaly clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark), yet the abstract, title, and process-volume multipliers (~73×–141×) prominently feature the full-stream 195,829 DESI count where ~98.7% of clusters fall on sky/filler fibers (non-primary science targets); this creates a material mismatch between advertised scale and actual science-ready content.
[MAJOR] §2.4 (Path-C rebuild) and §3.5–3.7: Detailed exposition of LAMOST training-bias failure, eROSITA provenance-irreproducible axis (non-monotone on 16 rescalings + IsolationForest), and Gaia synthetic-placeholder tier (excised after audit) in the main text of a catalog paper signals systemic pipeline robustness issues; these belong in a concise methods caveat or appendix, not as core narrative.
[MINOR] §2.2 (Tabular feature preprocessing): Full-sample (not training-split) scaling for eROSITA/NEOWISE is used in production; the bounded robustness check (Jaccard 0.76 on top-298) is provided only for eROSITA and is absent for NEOWISE, leaving a quantifiable but unaddressed source of potential tail reordering.
[MINOR] Throughout (esp. §2.4, §3, Table 2 footnotes): Excessive defensive scaffolding (“read once”, “read this before Table 2”, repeated “process-volume figure—not confirmed physical detections”, 20+ footnotes per section) and self-referential pipeline paths make the manuscript difficult to evaluate on its own merits; ApJS catalog papers require streamlined, self-contained presentation of selection function, purity/completeness, and released products.
[MINOR] §5 (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL and NANOGrav sections return null results (central shift within 1σ envelope; no detection) and are explicitly labeled secondary/methodological demonstrations; they add little standalone value and dilute focus on the primary catalog deliverable.

The central claim of delivering a validated multi-survey anomaly-candidate catalog of 268,519 sources is only partially supported: the committed scripts reproduce the headline number and injection-recovery gates pass for the DESI/SDSS/Planck majority components on the broad/continuum class, but the validation is non-uniform, the effective science-target yield is far smaller than advertised, and significant tiers were excised for reproducibility failures.
