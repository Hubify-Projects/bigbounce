VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] §V B, §VIII B, Tables X and XIV — The declared primary estimator and the Bonferroni-5 family are not consistently defined. The abstract and §XV designate the exact, k-unbounded, footprint-restricted VoidFinder contrast as primary, whereas Table XIV uses the approximate k=20, unrestricted VoidFinder contrast; the other family members also use different parent samples, with the sphere-PIS rows drawn from 678,945 objects and the GALZONE rows from 145,789. The quoted family-wise p-values and simultaneous bound therefore do not apply to one clearly specified five-estimator family or one common estimand. All five tests must be recomputed on a common valid footprint and parent, with the exact primary estimator explicitly included.

[MAJOR] §VIII B and §VIII E — The “footprint-restricted” control is not a DESIVAST/BGS selection-function-matched control. It is constructed from the union of void-hole angular discs intersected with a global radial span, rather than from the published angular mask, vetoes, fiber-assignment completeness, or DESI random catalogs. The manuscript acknowledges this limitation but later states that the restriction “matches the void and control selection functions directly.” That statement is incorrect. The primary analysis requires the official mask/randoms and either redshift-sky-magnitude-morphology-confidence matching, inverse-probability weighting, or a covariate-adjusted regression.

[MAJOR] §VIII B, Table XI, Eq. (4), and §V B — The advertised 0.9 percentage-point “effective 2σ” envelope has no valid confidence interpretation. Equation (4) combines a two-sided statistical confidence half-width with maximum excursions from correlated alternative geometries, different populations, different membership definitions, and different estimands, treating them as independent Gaussian errors. Several terms plausibly double-count the same membership/geometry uncertainty. Moreover, §V B obtains a family-wise simultaneous absolute bound of approximately 1.12 percentage points, already larger than the 0.9-point number promoted in the abstract and conclusions. The authors must construct a defined nuisance-parameter likelihood or simulation-calibrated interval, or report the sensitivity variations separately without calling their quadrature a 2σ bound.

Likewise, displacing galaxies and already-identified holes together without reconstructing the tracer field and rerunning VoidFinder cannot bound changes in void topology, centers, radii, or survey-edge classifications. Injection-recovery tests on realistic mocks, including an environment-confusion matrix and a rerun of the void finder after reconstruction, are required before the 0.024-point RSD systematic or the claimed “40×” margin can be used.

[MAJOR] §V and Tables X–XIV — The primary standard errors treat individual galaxies as independent Bernoulli observations. Galaxies within the same void complex, imaging region, spectroscopic tile, and Legacy Survey data set share environment and classification systematics, so the effective sample size need not equal the galaxy count. Per-galaxy binomial intervals and unrestricted label permutations can consequently underestimate uncertainty. The primary inference should use void-level and angular-block bootstrap or jackknife errors, with block-preserving permutations and an explicit covariance treatment across the five correlated estimators.

[MAJOR] Appendix A, §XII B, and §XIII — The 2.26-percentage-point “physical-chirality bound” is not established by dividing the label-space envelope by 2a−1. Overall accuracy alone does not determine attenuation; the relevant quantity is the environment-specific sensitivity plus specificity minus one, with its uncertainty. The symmetry evidence comes mainly from a higher-accuracy GZ1 subset, while the directly relevant void arm has a directional-error-asymmetry uncertainty of approximately ±3.7 percentage points, much wider than the claimed label-space bound. The result should remain explicitly a bound on the chosen classifier labels unless a calibrated latent-label or hierarchical misclassification analysis is supplied.

[MAJOR] §V B — The primary path was selected after examining several environment classifiers, membership constructions, masks, thresholds, control definitions, and numerous stratifications. Bonferroni correction over five DESIVAST variants does not correct for selecting DESIVAST, the footprint definition, or the preferred estimator after examining the data. This matters especially for an upper limit, because post-hoc selection can favor an unusually tight or reassuring null. The current bounds must be labeled exploratory rather than exclusion limits, unless the complete analysis tree is incorporated into selective inference or the result is confirmed on an independent preregistered data release.

[MAJOR] §IV, §IX A, §XII, and §XV — The canonical T-Web classification is not demonstrated to represent the physical cosmic web. The random-catalog-weighted rebuild changes approximately 73% of matched-galaxy class labels and reduces the inferred void volume fraction by a factor of about 23; additionally, the canonical 25.9Mpc/h grid cell is comparable to the adopted R

The T-Web analysis should either be rebuilt with the full survey selection function, convergence and RSD tests, or removed as physical robustness evidence; it cannot presently justify the claimed universal ≳25Mpc/h model scale for the DESIVAST result.

[MAJOR] §XII B, §XV, and Appendix B — The theoretical conclusion is broader than the measurement. The primary statistic tests one void definition against a heterogeneous aggregate of walls, filaments, and clusters, so opposite-sign or population-specific effects within the non-void sample could cancel. No bounce or inflation model is actually mapped to the measured likelihood, and Appendix B explicitly introduces an ad hoc, non-covariant operator rather than deriving a prediction. The manuscript should be framed as an empirical classifier-label null for specified DESIVAST partitions, not as a general constraint on environment-dependent chirality or on bounce/inflation models.

[MINOR] §III C, §XI, and Appendix D — Before publication, the authors should quantify false cross-matches with shifted-coordinate or mutual-nearest-neighbor tests, report the number and influence of SPECTYPE=QSO objects in the primary low-redshift sample, replace all arXiv and DOI placeholders, and provide an immutable archive containing the exact labels, masks, random catalogs, code, configurations, and intermediate membership tables used for the submitted version.

[MINOR] Throughout — The manuscript is excessively long and repetitive, repeatedly calls secondary analyses “headline” analyses, alternates among incompatible notions of a bound, and uses evaluative terms such as “honest” where a statistical definition is needed; substantial condensation and consistent terminology are necessary.

The central claim is supported only as an exploratory statement that the selected classifier labels show no statistically significant difference for the chosen DESIVAST partitions; the 0.9-percentage-point classifier bound, the 2.26-percentage-point physical bound, and the broader environment-independence claim are not presently supported.
