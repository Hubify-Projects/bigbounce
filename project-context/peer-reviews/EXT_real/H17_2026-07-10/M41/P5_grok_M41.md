VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section V B (and abstract): Post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored path as “designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered)” is transparently declared with Bonferroni-5 family control and a DR2 pre-registration commitment, but the choice among five correlated void definitions still constitutes a data-dependent framing decision that requires stronger a-priori justification or explicit sensitivity tests showing the family null is insensitive to any single definition.
[MINOR] Section VIII and Table XI: The “honest effective 2σ systematic envelope ≈0.9 pp” is obtained by folding counting CI, fixed-void-geometry membership (0.60 pp), footprint, confidence, and match-radius terms in quadrature, but the table does not tabulate the individual contributions, their correlations, or the exact quadrature formula, preventing independent reproduction of the envelope.
[MINOR] Section VI A, XI, and abstract: The ∼2.1σ (row-level) / 1.95σ (unique-galaxy) T-Web filament bright-vs-dark sign-flip is correctly flagged as a survey-selection diagnostic, yet the quantitative claim that BGS/imaging-leg systematics fully explain it rests on a contingency test (χ²=4933) and logistic regression without an end-to-end injection-recovery mock on the current data; the residual ambiguity disclosure is appropriate but should be elevated to a numbered limitation with a numerical upper bound on leakage into the primary DESIVAST contrast.
[MINOR] Abstract, Section II, and Appendix A (referenced): The de-attenuation from classifier-labelled bound (0.9 pp) to physical-chirality bound (≈2.26 pp) uses attenuation factor 2a−1≃0.3982 derived from the 69.91% (κ=0.40) GZ1 accuracy floor; while referenced to Paper IV, the present manuscript must explicitly verify that this factor applies to the equivariant ViT-Small + Z₂ TTA labels used here and state the uncertainty on a.
[MINOR] References and throughout: Multiple citations to the companion Paper IV appear as “arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX” (posted concurrently); these must be replaced with the actual identifier before acceptance, and the coordinated-submission status should be stated in a footnote.
