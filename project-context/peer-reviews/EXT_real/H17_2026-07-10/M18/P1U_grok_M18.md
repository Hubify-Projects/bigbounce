VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract, Sec. I (Introduction), and Sec. IV (opening Scope paragraph): The language of a “four-route no-go” and “channel closes” is imprecise and risks overstating the result. R1–R3 closures are amplitude bounds under explicitly labeled scaling/ansatz assumptions (single-scale NDA, specific one-loop or chiral-count estimates); R4 is closed only by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit argument that relocates rather than solves the cosmological-constant problem. The abstract and introduction must state this distinction in the first paragraph and replace “no-go” with “channel-level amplitude/naturalness constraints under stated assumptions.”

[MINOR] Sec. IV (Scope and completeness argument) and Sec. IX (barrier catalog): The claim that the four routes are “basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes” is supported by the explicit dim-4 operator enumeration (App. B, O1–O6), Bianchi identity, and Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (App. C), but the paper must add one explicit sentence: the only logical evasions are non-minimal completions that introduce a new light scale μ ≪ M_Pl or an exact protected cancellation—precisely the tuning the mechanism was intended to explain. Without this sentence the completeness language can be misread as stronger than intended.

[MINOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result, especially F “What Would Break the Transparency” and G): The all-orders algebraic proof (zero spin density → T=0 via Cartan → Levi-Civita connection → Holst term vanishes identically by the first Bianchi identity R_μ[νρσ]=0 at every perturbative order) is clean and correctly distinguished from the Pontryagin density. However, the quantum-scope limitation paragraph is too brief; it must explicitly note that one-loop graviton or matter corrections could source effective Holst contributions (consistent with the R2 amplitude closure) and that the theorem is strictly classical for canonical scalar matter.

[MINOR] Sec. XIV D (Structural Tension) and Table I: The derived incompatibility between the N_tot ≈ 92 e-folds required for dark-energy dilution and an observable matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 at SPHEREx scales is a genuine and interesting structural result. It must be stated more prominently that this tension is independent of the ECH-specific closures and applies to any bounce model that requires large post-bounce inflation to produce a small late-time vacuum energy.

[MINOR] Sec. IX (barrier list, especially B9, B10, B13) and Table I: Several barriers are explicitly labeled heuristic, philosophical, or ansatz-level (Liouville conservation, UV–IR specificity dilemma, gravitational democracy). These should be moved to a short subsection “Heuristic and naturalness considerations” or clearly flagged in the table so that the 13/14 “mechanism-class structural constraints” maintain uniform rigor; otherwise the catalog appears to mix derived theorems with interpretive arguments.

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are amplitude-constrained (R1–R3, under explicit scaling assumptions) or naturalness-closed (R4), with a rigorous all-orders perturbation-transparency theorem for canonical scalar matter—is supported by the explicit dim-4 operator enumeration and Bianchi/Fierz identities in App. B–C, the regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion in App. D, and the algebraic decoupling proof in Sec. X.
