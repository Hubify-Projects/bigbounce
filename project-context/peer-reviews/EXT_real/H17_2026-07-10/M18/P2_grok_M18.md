VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. II A (and Appendix A): The central correction of the literature value from Cai et al. (2009) f_NL = −35/8 to −35/16 is asserted via a traced −(99/128)∑k_i^3 discrepancy in the transcribed degree-9 polynomial; while four independent certifications are cited (vertex re-summation at ϵ=3/2, ϵ-ordered intermediates, three-benchmark matching, and Li et al. cs=1 formula), the explicit term-by-term cancellation in the squeezed limit and the precise mapping between the single-time-ordering integrand and the symmetrized monomial basis must be shown in full in the appendix (or a dedicated subsection) with every intermediate polynomial written out, so that the error claim can be verified without external code execution.
[MINOR] Sec. I and IV: The headline sensitivity ranges (∼2.6–2.75σ optimistic → ∼1.3–2.75σ post-systematics) conflate qualitatively distinct procedures (CMB-Fisher signal-only weighting, LSS noise weighting, proxy ρ=−0.868 transferred from SDB, and the new channel-native ρ≈−0.42 GR-marginalized floor from the in-house tree-level Fisher); a single consolidated table that separates the template-mismatch recast, the GR/b_ϕ budget, and the channel-native vs. proxy floors, with explicit statements of which numbers are used for which claims, is required for clarity.
[MINOR] Sec. IV: The independent multi-tracer bispectrum Fisher validation reproduces the Heinrich et al. local baseline only to 2–11% (σ≈0.63–0.69 vs. 0.7); the sources of the residual difference (Gaussian covariance approximation, linear k_max, fixed b_2/bs_2, real-space vs. redshift-space kernels, absence of FoG) must be itemized quantitatively so readers can judge whether the reff≈0.99 recovery factor is robust or carries an unquantified systematic floor.
[MINOR] Sec. VI C: The Bayes-factor grid (and the recommended σ_theory=1.0 baseline) is presented as “illustrative” and prior-sensitive, yet the abstract quotes BF≈9–14 without a corresponding one-sentence caveat that these values assume the broad [−15,+15] multifield competitor prior and the delta-function (or σ_theory=1) bounce prior; the text must explicitly state that BF drops to ∼4 under the curvaton-natural [−5,+5] competitor prior.
[MINOR] Throughout (notation and scope): The multiple r-like quantities (r=0.84 noise-weighted, reff≈0.99 survey-optimal, r_cos>0.97, r=0.85±0.13 null-space) are defined once but never summarized; a compact table (or boxed list) of their definitions, weighting schemes, and which enters the headline σ_eff is needed to prevent mis-citation.

The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce f_NL=−35/16 produces a marginal but non-negligible SPHEREx detection significance (∼1.3–2.75σ after template mismatch and systematics) that retains qualitative discriminatory power against single-field inflation—is supported by the explicit r-weighted recast, the in-house Fisher cross-check, the multi-way amplitude certification, and the transparent disclosure of the proxy-based conservative floor.
