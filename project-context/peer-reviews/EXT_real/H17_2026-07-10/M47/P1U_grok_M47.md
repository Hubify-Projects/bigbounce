VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. I (Scope paragraph), and Sec. IV: The central “four-route channel-level closure” is repeatedly and explicitly qualified as assumption-conditional, non-operator-level, and limited to M_Pl-power-counting classes (with non-minimal extensions or new light scales evading it by construction). This framing makes the no-go a targeted constraint audit rather than a general theorem; the manuscript should either strengthen the result to a broader operator basis (using the already-provided Fierz lemma and completeness argument) or reframe the title/abstract/claims to match the limited scope without overstating generality.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result): The decoupling proof is restricted to canonical scalar matter only; fermion spin density, propagating torsion (Poincaré gauge theory), dynamical Immirzi, and non-minimal couplings are explicitly excluded. Since ECH torsion is fundamentally sourced by fermionic spin and realistic cosmologies contain fermions, the result’s domain of applicability is narrow; the paper must explicitly delineate when/why the transparency holds or breaks in the presence of the very spin-torsion physics that motivates the ECH framework.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV D–E (Routes 2–3) and Sec. II C: The one-loop parity-odd operator (Eq. 17) and Immirzi-running ansatz (Eq. 19) are presented as “motivated by” or “phenomenological” constructions citing Shapiro & Teixeira and Benedetti & Speziale, yet used to close the routes via amplitude budgets. The manuscript must clearly separate strictly derived results from illustrative upper-bound ansätze and justify why the chosen normalizations constitute fair closure bounds rather than tunable choices.
[MINOR] Sec. II C, XII A, and App. B: The dark-energy mapping is a single-scale NDA dimensional no-go (off-shell dimension +1 forcing ρ_ECH^Λ ∼ M_Pl^4) that relocates the CC problem to a fitted N_tot ≈ 92 (or ΔN_tot ≈ 4) initial-condition tuning. While correctly identified as non-solution, the paper should more sharply contrast this relocation against standard CC literature and clarify whether any positive amplitude is ever derived or only excluded.
[MINOR] Throughout (esp. Sec. I, III, XIII and App. F–G): Heavy reliance on concurrently posted companion papers (II–IV) for SPHEREx f_NL forecasts, galaxy-spin catalogs, and pipeline validations—even when labeled “non-load-bearing illustrative”—renders the submission difficult to assess standalone. Key numerical anchors and validations should be summarized self-containedly or moved to truly supplemental material.
[MINOR] App. D and Sec. IV A: The regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion of the vacuum condensate relies on the Fierz-by-Fierz projection (App. C) showing a repulsive scalar channel (G_scalar = −3/64 κ). The argument is technically sound but must explicitly confirm exhaustiveness across all Fierz channels and cutoffs, and state that no sub-critical attractive channel permits a (meV)^4 condensate at the Planck-suppressed coupling.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes close at channel/amplitude level under the stated assumptions, with Holst-sector perturbation transparency for canonical scalars and a structural DE–f_NL tension—is supported by the explicit power-counting, Fierz lemma, gap-equation analysis, and decoupling proof within the narrowly scoped framework.
