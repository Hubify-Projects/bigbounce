VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. IV D (and Appendix D): The post-MASTER canonical ℓ=1 residual (+3.64σ) is correctly labeled a non-primary systematics diagnostic, and the paper demonstrates that even the full residual amplitude (Ap ≈ 0.695%) lies below the real-space A50/A95 recovery thresholds. However, the forward model (imaging + morphology templates) accounts for only ~53% of the amplitude, leaving an explicit ~47% "open item." While the eight-anchor battery and primary-estimator independence resolve the cosmological interpretation for the headline result, a single consolidated paragraph quantifying the maximum allowed cosmological dipole fraction in the unmodeled remainder (consistent with all anchors and the block-bootstrap covariance) would eliminate any residual ambiguity for readers.
[MINOR] Sec. V A and abstract: The amplitude-level tension with Shamir (1.7–4% range) is stated clearly, and the text correctly caveats that this is not a frequentist exclusion of Shamir’s distinct Ganalyzer estimator/pipeline and that a matched-footprint reanalysis would be required. The phrasing “∼3.7–8.8× tension” and “disfavors a clean Aref = 0.017 dipole at z ≈ −7.6” could still be misread as a direct statistical confrontation; recommend tightening the language to emphasize “amplitude comparison under the present equivariant pipeline” versus “statistical exclusion of prior claims.”
[MINOR] Sec. III B and Data Availability: The pre-specification of the peq > 0.6 primary sample via git commit hash is excellent practice and the analysis hierarchy (Table I) is exemplary. For a journal archive, however, the manuscript should add an explicit statement that the exact commit (or a frozen snapshot) used for all quoted numbers, masks, and null distributions is permanently archived alongside the public catalog release; this removes any ambiguity for future reproducibility checks.
[MINOR] General presentation (throughout Results and Discussion): The paper is exceptionally rigorous and dense, with many cross-references to artifacts and appendices. A short “reader’s guide” paragraph immediately after the abstract (or as a boxed note) that states in plain language “the cosmological conclusion rests solely on the two primary estimators in rows P1–P2 of Table I; all harmonic-channel σ values are systematics diagnostics” would improve accessibility without lengthening the paper.

The central claim that the large-scale chirality dipole is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity (with prior claims likely systematics-driven under improved methodology) is robustly supported.
