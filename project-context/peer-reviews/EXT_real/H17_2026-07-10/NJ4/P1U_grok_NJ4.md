VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV (and Scope paragraph) + App. B1/C: The four-route “channel-level closure” is repeatedly qualified as non-exhaustive and assumption-dependent, yet the title, abstract, and Sec. IX present it as a comprehensive constraint on “the enumerated channels of the minimal-ECH route”; the explicit operator enumeration and Fierz-by-Fierz lemma are deferred to a released script and appendices without sufficient inline derivation of the six dimension-4 parity-odd densities or the symbolic verification of the two tensor identities (εR=0 and Lorentzian εε collapse), making independent referee verification of basis-completeness at the M_Pl-power-counting level impossible from the manuscript alone.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result) + Sec. X E: The central claim that “torsion vanishes at all classical metric/scalar perturbation orders around the torsion-free branch” and that the Holst sector “decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion” is asserted via the algebraic Bianchi identity and Cartan constraint, but the term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion is only sketched; no explicit linearized EOMs (scalar or tensor sectors) are displayed showing the cancellation of every Holst contribution order-by-order, nor is the extension beyond canonical scalar matter (fermion spin density, non-minimal couplings) quantified with counter-term estimates.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F + Sec. II C + App. B: Route 4 is closed only by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit argument (the same coupling that fits β_obs forces m_θ ∼ H_0, “relocating the CC problem”); this is logically distinct from the amplitude-suppression arguments used for R1–R3, yet is counted equally in the “four-route closure” and the 14-barrier catalog, without a clear separation of mechanism classes or a demonstration that the m_θ ∼ H_0 tuning is parametrically worse than the tuning it replaces.
[MINOR] Sec. IX (Barriers 1–14 catalog) + Foundations A–G: The 13/14 “mechanism-class structural constraints” are enumerated with short descriptive titles but lack self-contained derivations or cross-references to explicit equations; several (e.g., Barrier 14 subsuming B8, thermal-reset conditional on Γ_wash(T_reh) > H(T_reh)) are stated as supporting results without the supporting calculation or rate comparison being performed or cited inside the manuscript.
[MINOR] Overall structure + observational appendices (F–I): The manuscript interleaves a theoretical channel-closure argument with full MCMC pipelines, NaMaster validation, spectator-ALP fits, and reproducibility manifests; while reproducibility is commendable, the core no-go logic (Secs. IV, IX–X) is diluted and the paper exceeds typical PRD length without a clear partition between load-bearing analytic results and illustrative numerical context (the latter explicitly declared non-load-bearing).

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at the channel-amplitude level (R1–R3 by explicit suppression, R4 by naturalness) under the stated assumptions, reinforced by a perturbation-transparency result and a 13/14-barrier catalog—is supported within its narrowly scoped, assumption-conditional framework by dimensional power-counting, the algebraic Bianchi identity, and the Fierz projection, but does not establish a general operator-level no-go or remove the need for a new light scale or exact cancellation.
