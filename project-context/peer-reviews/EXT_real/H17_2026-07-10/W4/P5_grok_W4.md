VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §V B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths / pre-registration caveat): The DESIVAST-anchored path is designated "primary for reporting" explicitly post-hoc with no timestamped pre-registration plan predating the data; although the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five estimators (|z_Δ| ≤ 1.25) is uniformly null, the garden-of-forking-paths exposure and data-dependent choice of which single estimand to foreground as "the" primary weakens the strength of the bound-setting claim despite the disclosed analysis tree and DR2 pre-registration commitment.
[MINOR] Abstract and §I (headline result): The opening claim that "spiral galaxy chirality shows no void/non-void environment dependence" and the quoted |Δf_CW| ≤ 0.004 bound refer strictly to the classifier-labelled statistic at the 69.91% (κ ≈ 0.40) GZ1 accuracy floor; the de-attenuated physical-chirality implication (~2.26 pp) and the explicit statement that this is an attenuated proxy (not a direct physical handedness measurement) are not stated in the abstract, risking misinterpretation by model-builders seeking parity-violation constraints.
[MINOR] Title, §I, and §XIII (redshift-space limitation): All T-Web, DESIVAST, and null-test analyses are performed in fixed redshift space without real-space reconstruction and inherit RSD effects (including the unquantified anisotropic tidal-tensor eigenvalue channel); while the limitation is correctly noted for model-builders, the title and introduction do not prominently caveat that the reported environmental-independence statement is not a pure real-space constraint.
[MINOR] §II and abstract (companion Paper IV): Multiple references to the concurrently posted companion catalog paper use the placeholder "arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX"; the algebraic invariance of the void/non-void Δf_CW contrast under monopole shifts is correctly emphasized, but the placeholder should be resolved or explicitly flagged as a coordinated concurrent submission to enable fully independent refereeing from public GZ1/DESI/DESIVAST data alone.
[MINOR] §VIII D and Table XIV (DESIVAST family): The five DESIVAST estimators comprise correlated pairs (three sphere-PIS constructions plus two catalog-native GALZONE definitions on the same watershed algorithms); while the Bonferroni-5 family null is reported and the correlation is acknowledged, no sensitivity check replacing the family threshold with an effective-K adjustment or a single joint estimator is provided to confirm robustness of the |z_Δ| ≤ 1.25 claim.

The central claim of no significant void/non-void dependence in the classifier-labelled CW fraction (family-wise Bonferroni-5 null with |z_Δ| ≤ 1.25 and ≈0.9 pp systematic envelope) is supported by the presented cross-matched analysis, label-shuffle permutations, Jeffreys intervals, and systematic budget, subject to the acknowledged post-hoc and redshift-space limitations.
