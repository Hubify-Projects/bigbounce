VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract + §V B (primary-path designation) + Table IV: The DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast is explicitly elevated as the “designated primary for reporting” and “exploratory primary” after the data were examined, with the explicit admission that “no timestamped plan predates the data.” While the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five estimators is correctly identified as the strictly quotable result, the narrative framing and section ordering still center a single post-hoc estimand; this undermines the claimed protection against garden-of-forking-paths concerns even though the analysis tree and disclosure are provided.
[MAJOR] Abstract + §I + §VIII + implied §XIII (de-attenuation): The quoted de-attenuated physical-chirality bound of ≈2.26 pp is derived from the classifier-label bound under the symmetric-error approximation (attenuation factor 2a−1 ≃ 0.40). The text acknowledges that “no environment-stratified confusion matrix is available” and that the de-attenuation “carries additional uncertainty from environment-dependent relabeling,” yet still presents the 2.26 pp figure as the quantity model-builders should use. This renders the physical bound materially weaker and less robust than stated.
[MINOR] §VI A + §IX C (T-Web void bin): The secondary T-Web void sample (n = 428) is repeatedly tabulated, plotted, and discussed at length despite the paper’s own demonstration that it is dominated by survey-shell selection artifacts at z ≲ 0.24 and has negligible overlap with DESIVAST voids. Although labeled non-load-bearing, its prominence risks reader misinterpretation of its statistical weight.
[MINOR] §I (Scope) + §IV + §VIII + §XIII: All environment classifications, void memberships, and Δf_CW contrasts are performed exclusively in redshift space with no real-space reconstruction and no quantitative estimate (even via mocks) of RSD-induced bias on either class membership or the chirality contrast. The text correctly warns that the results are redshift-space statements, but the absence of any RSD impact assessment limits their utility for constraining theoretical models that predict real-space environmental effects.
[MINOR] Reproducibility (implied §D/Appendix E + data sections): The manuscript references a committed pipeline tag (v0.1.113-2026-07-10), DOI-minted archival snapshot, and companion data repository but lacks a standard Data Availability Statement that explicitly lists the exact public DESI DR1 zall-pix-iron.fits version/tag, the precise DESIVAST VAC release used, the Hugging Face catalog version, and a persistent link or DOI to the full analysis code and derived parquet files.

The central claim—that spiral-galaxy chirality shows no void/non-void dependence at the level |Δf_CW| ≲ 0.004 (family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across the five DESIVAST estimators) on the classifier labels—is supported by the two-sample z_Δ statistics, Jeffreys intervals, label-shuffle permutation tests, and cross-algorithm robustness checks performed on the large DESIVAST void sample.
