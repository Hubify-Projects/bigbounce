VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section III.B (Declared Analysis Hierarchy) and abstract: Language is internally inconsistent on estimator status—the text explicitly declares the HC (p_eq > 0.6) subsample as “the single primary science sample for all primary cosmological statements” and calls the block-bootstrap WLS fit a “diagnostic template-disfavor cross-check,” yet the hierarchy bullet and Table I both label the WLS exclusion (z ≈ −7.6 on the full Catalog C) as a primary cosmological estimator; the selection function of the HC cut is also not propagated into the NSIDE=8 block-bootstrap covariance.
[MINOR] Section IV.D and Appendix D (Monopole+Mask Leakage and eight-anchor battery): The imaging + per-galaxy morphology forward model reproduces only ≈52–54% of the canonical-mask post-MASTER ℓ=1 residual amplitude (|a1| = 6.95 × 10^{-3}), leaving an explicit ≈47% open item whose origin is unresolved; while the paper correctly bounds any cosmological contribution below the real-space A50 floor, this incomplete attribution should be stated more prominently as a limitation of the harmonic-channel systematics interpretation.
[MINOR] Section V.A (Comparison with Shamir): The claimed amplitude-level tension with Shamir’s 1.7–4% range is correctly caveated, but the text should more explicitly and repeatedly state that a matched-footprint reanalysis with Shamir’s Ganalyzer estimator (and identical cuts) remains required before the present pipeline can be read as a direct statistical exclusion rather than an amplitude comparison under a different classifier and mask treatment.
[MINOR] Sections IV.C and VI.B (confidence-cut sweep and injection-recovery): The p_eq > 0.6 threshold is described as pre-registered via a specific git commit; while verifiable, a short explicit statement that the threshold was frozen before any dipole evaluation (or that the full sweep was pre-specified) would eliminate any residual forking-paths concern for readers.

The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole on the high-confidence subsample is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity and that a clean Shamir-scale (A ≈ 1.7%) dipole is disfavored—is supported by the primary +0.41σ (p = 0.31) isotropic-permutation result, the empirical injection-recovery calibration (A50 ≈ 0.75%, A95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%]), the block-bootstrap template disfavor, and the independent GZ1-human-label cross-check.
