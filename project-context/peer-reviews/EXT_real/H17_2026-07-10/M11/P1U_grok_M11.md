VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I (Introduction, "Scope and limitations" paragraph): The phrasing "four enumerated minimal-ECH ... routes ... each constrained" and "basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes" risks being read more strongly than the explicit qualifications ("channel-level, assumption-conditional statement, not an operator-level theorem"). Add one explicit sentence in the abstract and at the end of Sec. I stating the precise scope boundaries (four enumerated channels under labeled assumptions; NDA monotonicity + Fierz/Bianchi collapse for the minimal-ECH power-counting tower only).
[MINOR] Sec. IV D (Route 2) and Sec. IV E (Route 3): The effective operator Eq. (17) and running ansatz Eq. (19) are introduced as "motivated by (but not literally derived in)" the cited works, with explicit statements that the budgets are illustrative upper bounds. While the large suppression margins (∼60 orders) make the qualitative closures robust, the section headings and closure sentences should be rephrased to "closed under the adopted one-loop/EFT ansatz" to match the paper's own careful caveats and avoid any appearance of stronger derivation.
[MINOR] Sec. X (The Perturbation-Transparency Result): The result is a Tier-I structural claim and the paper states its scope clearly (canonical scalar matter only; fermion spin density, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings outside scope). However, the main-text sketch (B–E) is high-level; the explicit term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion and verification that the Holst sector drops from all scalar/tensor EOMs should be summarized with one key intermediate equation in the main text (or flagged as "detailed in Appendix X") so a reader can follow the decoupling without jumping to supplemental material.
[MINOR] Sec. IV A (Route 1) and Appendix D: The regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion (repulsive scalar channel, sub-critical |G_eff|/G_crit) is a load-bearing step for the vacuum-condensate closure. The main text cites the script and Fierz lemma but does not reproduce the two-line key result (sign of G_scalar = −3/64 κ < 0 and the numerical |G_eff|/G_crit ratio). Add a one-sentence summary of the regulated gap-equation outcome in Sec. IV A so the vacuum exclusion stands independently of the appendix.
[MINOR] General (length and structure): The manuscript is 62 pages with extensive self-referential appendices and reproducibility claims. While the separation of load-bearing analytic results from illustrative MCMC numbers is clearly stated, a modest tightening (e.g., moving the full Fierz matrix or one-loop RG integration details to a compact supplemental file while retaining one summary equation in the main text) would improve readability without loss of rigor.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at the channel/amplitude-budget level under the explicitly stated assumptions, via single-scale NDA, explicit operator closures (Fierz + Bianchi), and the perturbation-transparency decoupling—is supported by the dimensional bookkeeping, the completeness argument at M_Pl-power-counting level, and the logical structure of the barriers.
