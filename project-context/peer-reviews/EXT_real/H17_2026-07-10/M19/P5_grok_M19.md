VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Title: The title specifies a "Three-Algorithm Void Null Test," but the abstract, Section V B, and Section VIII explicitly frame the primary result and family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across five DESIVAST void definitions (VoidFinder + V2-REVOLVER + V2-VIDE sphere-growing/watershed algorithms plus the two catalog-native GALZONE/ZONEVOID membership variants). Update the title to "Multi-Algorithm (or Five-Definition) Void Null Test" for precision and consistency with the declared primary path.
[MINOR] Abstract and Section V B (primary/secondary designation): The manuscript transparently labels the DESIVAST-anchored path as "designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered)" and provides garden-of-forking-paths accounting plus Bonferroni-5 control; however, it should add one explicit sentence in the abstract stating that the choice of primary estimand (and the decision to anchor on the volume-limited BGS DESIVAST catalog) was made after inspecting results from the T-Web, Tempel FoF, and ASTRA paths, to fully preempt reader concerns about post-hoc selection even though the uniform null across all five estimators holds.
[MINOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment and systematic envelope, Table XI): The first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction (0.024 pp shift) and fixed-geometry Monte Carlo (membership perturbation shifting ΔfCW by ≤0.37 pp) are strong; the quadrature folding of counting-statistics CI with geometry (0.60 pp co-dominant), footprint, confidence, and match-radius terms into an "honest effective 2σ systematic envelope of ≈0.9 pp" is reasonable but requires one additional paragraph justifying independence of terms and why quadrature (rather than a conservative linear sum) is adopted, plus explicit confirmation that the envelope applies to the observed (classifier-labelled) ΔfCW before de-attenuation.

The central claim that spiral-galaxy chirality exhibits no significant large-scale environmental dependence (void vs. non-void ΔfCW null at |ΔfCW| ≲ 0.004 with de-attenuated physical bound ≲ 2.3 pp) is supported by the multi-algorithm DESIVAST primary tests, permutation/LEE statistics, RSD bounds, and consistency across secondary diagnostics.
