ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. V A: The phrasing “places our amplitude in ∼3.7–8.8× tension with Shamir’s claimed ∼2–4% signal” and the z ≈ −7.6 template-fit statement are presented as strong disfavor; however, both are amplitude-level or model-dependent comparisons (explicitly noted as requiring a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis for likelihood-level exclusion). This risks reader misinterpretation as a direct statistical exclusion of Shamir’s result. Add a single clarifying sentence in the abstract and a parenthetical in Sec. V A.
[MINOR] Sec. II B and VI A: 66.5% of training labels derive from CE-ResNet pseudo-labels. While the fully model-free GZ1-human-only dipole test (z = −0.54σ) and eight-anchor battery are provided, the per-galaxy label-shuffle/per-pixel permutation nulls remain limited by construction; the quantitative ceiling on any inherited coherent power (|A_inh| ≲ 1.5% at 95% recovery) is stated only in the discussion. Move or expand this bound to Sec. II B or early in VI A for immediate visibility.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D and Appendix D: The forward-modeling accounts for only ∼52–54% of the post-MASTER canonical ℓ=1 residual amplitude, leaving an explicit ∼47% open item (below A95 but still +3.64σ). The physical interpretation (depth/PSF/morphology selection vs. residual signal) is well-argued but would benefit from a one-paragraph summary of the planned per-galaxy morphology-purity map follow-up and a brief statement that this residual does not enter the primary real-space or template-fit estimators.
[MINOR] Sec. III B and Table I: The declared analysis hierarchy and Table I are excellent, but the block-bootstrap WLS template fit (primary row P2) operates on the full Catalog C without propagating the p_eq > 0.6 selection function used for the HC real-space estimator (row P1). A short explicit note that the two primary estimators are therefore on overlapping but non-identical samples would eliminate any ambiguity.

The central claim that the large-scale chirality dipole is consistent with null (real-space +0.41σ on the pre-specified HC subsample, with genuine Shamir-scale signals above the calibrated A95 floor excluded) is supported.
