(1) VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

(2) ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract, §1, and §3 (three-tier catalog structure): The headline claim of a single “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is not supported; validation is explicitly heterogeneous (DESI/SDSS/Planck pass broad-class 5σ injection-recovery; NEOWISE passes only a post-hoc geometry-QA mask; eROSITA is excised for 1.2% recovery + irreproducible production score axis; LAMOST is retained only as a documented failure mode with 5.8% recovery and 98% blue-excess artifact), yet all are aggregated under one “validated” label with per-object flags that readers must consult to interpret.
[MAJOR] §3.1 and Table 3: The DESI component (the dominant contributor) yields only 2,468 anomaly clusters on validated science-target spectra (≈0.92× the Liang et al. benchmark), while 98.7% of the 195,829 raw detections fall on sky-fiber/filler spectra with no primary TARGETTYPE; the ~73× and ~141× multipliers repeatedly cited are therefore process-volume figures, not like-for-like catalog increases, and the paper’s own “reader’s guide” and recount artifact confirm this mismatch between headline numbers and science-usable content.
[MAJOR] §2.2, §3.5, and Table 5: The eROSITA tier’s production score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible on the committed raw reconstruction scores (non-monotone, fails all 16 tested monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains); this provenance failure forces excision from every count (including the inclusive 377,482) yet the tier is still presented in detail, undermining the reproducibility standard the paper itself invokes for Gaia and the overall catalog.
[MAJOR] §2.4 and §6.4(i): The single production-ensemble sensitivity gate for DESI (broad/extended class recovers 99–100% at 5σ) is supported only by two correlated fold-stability checks computed on deliberately short-trained proxy models that fail the paper’s own val-loss ≤0.30 retain gate; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, so the “catalog-grade” label for the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer is narrower than the title and abstract imply.
[MINOR] Title, abstract, and §1: The framing foregrounds “37.3 million” and large multipliers without sufficient upfront qualification that these are rounded process-volume totals (actual read/scored 36.93 M) and that the released 268,519 subset excludes two tiers for reproducibility/sensitivity failures; this risks mis-citation by downstream users.
[MINOR] §2.2 and §3.6: Full-sample feature scaling for tabular surveys (eROSITA, NEOWISE) and in-sample scoring + train/score overlap for the Planck native bank introduce acknowledged leakage; the bounded robustness checks (Jaccard 0.76, tail enrichment p=5.5×10^{-4}) are reported but do not fully mitigate the concern for a catalog intended for community re-use.
[MINOR] §4 and §5: The external cross-match novelty fraction (178/1,000 ≈17.8% for DESI top-1,000) and the two cosmological demonstrations (fNL shift inside 1σ envelope; NANOGrav γ consistent with matter-bounce but decisive only against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB) are correctly caveated as non-detections, yet their inclusion adds length without strengthening the central catalog claim.

(3) The central claim that the work delivers a validated, catalog-grade multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 sources whose robustness is established by injection-recovery and reproducibility protocols is only partially supported by the presented evidence, because validation is heterogeneous across components, the large majority of candidates lie on non-science-target spectra, and multiple tiers were excluded precisely for failures in sensitivity or provenance.
