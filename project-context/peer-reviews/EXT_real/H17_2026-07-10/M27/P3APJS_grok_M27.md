VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §1, and §3 (three-tier structure): The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is inconsistent with the paper’s own repeated framing that this is a process-volume figure of candidates surviving heterogeneous per-survey gates (not confirmed physical detections), with ~98.7% of DESI anomalies on non-science-target (sky/filler) spectra and only 2,468 on validated science targets.
[MAJOR] §3.5 (eROSITA) and §3.7 (Gaia): The eROSITA production score axis is explicitly irreproducible (undocumented post-hoc rescaling; fails all 16 monotone rescalings + IsolationForest retrains) and the Gaia tier is synthetic placeholder data; retaining detailed discussion and a “separately released” membership list while excising both from every count violates the reproducibility standard the paper itself invokes and should be removed entirely from the manuscript.
[MAJOR] §2.2, §2.4, §6.4(i) (reproducibility): Full per-object re-inference of the released 22.5 M DESI catalog is blocked because raw native score parquets reside on an exited pod and are absent from the committed tree/HF release; a catalog paper in ApJS requires end-to-end reproducibility of the delivered catalog entries, not merely headline deduplication scripts.
[MAJOR] §2.4 (Path-C validation protocol) and §3 (Table 2): Validation is not uniform—DESI/SDSS/Planck pass detector-sensitivity injection-recovery while NEOWISE passes only a geometry-QA mask by construction—yet all four are aggregated into the single “validated catalog-grade” headline; the paper must either apply one consistent gate or present four separately labeled tiers.
[MINOR] §3.1 (DESI science-target recount) and Table 3: The like-for-like claim “≈0.92× the largest published single-survey catalog” (Liang et al. 2,685 on ~250 k EDR spectra) is presented without explicit comparison of science-target sample sizes, selection functions, or redshift-quality cuts between the ~6.5 M validated-TARGETTYPE DR1 subset and the EDR benchmark; the numerical similarity alone does not establish equivalence.
[MINOR] §5 (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL and NANOGrav sections are explicitly “secondary demonstrations” that return null results; they add length without strengthening the catalog claim and should be moved to an appendix or removed to focus the ApJS data paper on the anomaly catalog itself.
[MINOR] Overall manuscript: Heavy dependence on internal pipeline paths (e.g., pipelines/p3 anomaly engine/…), committed JSON artifacts, and pod-specific provenance notes makes the text read as an internal technical report rather than a self-contained ApJS submission; core validation results and catalog statistics must be presented without requiring external repository consultation.

The central claim that the work delivers a validated 268,519-source multi-survey anomaly catalog supported by injection-recovery tests is only partially supported, because validation standards are heterogeneous, two tiers were excised for irreproducibility, and full per-object reproducibility of the dominant DESI component remains blocked by missing pipeline artifacts.
