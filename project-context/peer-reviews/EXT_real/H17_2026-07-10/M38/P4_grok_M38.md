VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I (primary claims): The phrasing that the block-bootstrap WLS fit "disfavors a clean Aref=0.017 dipole at z≈−7.6" risks being read as a calibrated frequentist exclusion; the text correctly qualifies this in Sec. IV C and Appendix D as a template-disfavor statistic under the adopted NSIDE=8 spatial covariance (not a p-value with coverage), but the abstract and introduction summary should add the same explicit qualifier to prevent misinterpretation.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D (monopole+mask leakage and residual attribution): The imaging+morphology forward model reproduces only ≈52–54% of the observed canonical-mask post-MASTER ℓ=1 amplitude (with the remainder ≈47% left explicitly open); while correctly bounded below the real-space A95 threshold and shown not to affect the primary null, the paper should either (a) strengthen the quantitative upper limit on any cosmological dipole component in the unmodeled fraction or (b) flag the per-galaxy morphology-purity map computation as a required follow-up to close the budget.
[MINOR] Sec. VI A (pseudo-label independence): The fully model-independent GZ1-human-only dipole test (z=−0.54σ) is a valuable cross-check, but its sensitivity floor (A95≈4.5–6.8%) is stated only in the appendix; the main-text discussion should explicitly note that this test corroborates the null at the headline sample's sensitivity level but does not independently constrain the sub-percent regime.
[MINOR] Sec. III B and Table I (analysis hierarchy): The declared primary/secondary distinction and the non-comparability of σ values across null procedures are correctly emphasized, but a concise one-paragraph "estimator roadmap" early in Sec. IV would help readers map which rows carry cosmological weight versus systematics diagnostics without having to cross-reference multiple tables and appendices.

The central claim—that the chirality dipole is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity and that a genuine Shamir-scale signal would have been recovered—is supported by the pre-specified high-confidence real-space estimator, the injection-recovery calibration, and the model-independent GZ1 cross-check.
