VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §1 (Introduction), and §3 (Survey-by-Survey Results, including the three-tier catalog structure and Table 2): The primary deliverable is framed as a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” with large process-volume multipliers (~141×, ~73×), yet the text immediately qualifies it as a non-detection “process-volume figure” of reconstruction outliers surviving per-survey gates (not confirmed physical detections), with ~98.7% of DESI clusters on sky-fiber/filler spectra and a science-target yield of only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark on a comparable EDR science-target sample). This creates a systematic mismatch between headline claims and the actual evidence presented.
[MAJOR] §3.5 (eROSITA DR1): The production score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible from any committed artifact (fails on 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains; raw scores are non-monotone, Spearman ρ = −0.10); the tier is excised from every count (including the inclusive 377,482) and released only as a top-298 membership list. This constitutes a fundamental provenance failure for one of the six archives and undermines the multi-survey pipeline’s uniformity, even though the excision itself is disclosed.
[MAJOR] §3.1 (DESI DR1) and Table 3: The anomaly catalog is scored on the full 22.5 M spectra stream, but only 2,468 deduplicated clusters (1.3%) match primary science-target bitmasks within 1″ (Redrock-confirmed GALAXY/QSO/STAR); 98.7% fall on non-primary targets. The paper’s own like-for-like benchmark shows no increase over prior single-survey work, yet the abstract and introduction continue to emphasize the full-stream volume and multipliers without foregrounding this scope limitation for downstream science use (e.g., high-z QSO candidate selection).
[MAJOR] §2.4 (Path-C Rebuild) and §3 (overall tier structure): Three of the six archives are either excised (eROSITA, synthetic Gaia), relabeled exploratory failure modes (LAMOST: 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact, 5.8% injection-recovery), or limited to geometry QA rather than detector-sensitivity injection-recovery (NEOWISE). The “validated” label therefore applies to a heterogeneous and reduced subset (DESI + SDSS native + Planck native + NEOWISE), weakening the multi-survey claim while the title and abstract retain the six-archive framing.
[MINOR] §2.2 (Tabular-survey feature preprocessing) and §6.4: Feature scalers for eROSITA and NEOWISE were fit on the full sample rather than the training split, introducing potential validation-set leakage into normalization constants (though a bounded eROSITA robustness check shows ranking stability). The paper correctly flags this as non-ideal but retains the production state; absolute MSE values and any downstream threshold interpretations carry this unquantified caveat for the photometric tiers.
[MINOR] §5 (Cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL (§5) and NANOGrav γ analyses are presented as “secondary demonstrations” yet return explicitly null results (α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65, no improvement on σ(fNL); γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively inconsistent only with an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference). These sections add no new constraints and dilute focus on the catalog; they should be moved to an appendix or removed.

The central claim of a validated 268,519-anomaly multi-survey catalog is only partially supported: the evidence demonstrates reproducible native-retrain outlier detection and injection-recovery for the broad/continuum class in the retained tiers (DESI/SDSS/Planck/NEOWISE), with transparent documentation of exclusions and failures, but does not establish uniform detector-sensitivity validation, science-target purity, or provenance integrity across the full claimed scope.
