VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Sec. II A (and Appendix A): The central correction of the literature value from f_NL = −35/8 to the vertex-sum result −35/16 is substantiated by four cross-checks (re-summation of Cai et al.’s four cubic vertices at ϵ = 3/2, their ϵ-grouped intermediates, three-benchmark matching, and Li et al.’s general-c_s formula at c_s = 1) and the explicit identification of a −(99/128) ∑_i k_i^3 term discrepancy in the transcribed polynomial. However, the appendix should present a side-by-side tabulation (or direct equation comparison) of Cai et al.’s printed monomial coefficients versus the exact vertex-sum coefficients so readers can verify the discrepancy at a glance without reconstructing the full in-in calculation.
[MINOR] Sec. IV and VII: The headline sensitivity range adopts the conservative proxy ρ ≈ −0.868 transferred from the power-spectrum SDB channel for the GR–f_NL covariance (because Heinrich et al.’s per-triangle Cov_B is not public). The authors’ own channel-native tree-level multi-tracer Fisher (which reproduces the Heinrich local baseline to 2–11 % and yields σ_marg(f_NL) ≈ 0.94 or ∼2.3σ) is presented only as a consistency check. This native result should be given equal or primary weight in the abstract, Table V, and conclusions, with the proxy floor retained explicitly as the more conservative cross-check.
[MINOR] Sec. I, III B, and notation throughout: The manuscript carefully defines multiple r-like quantities (bare r = 0.84 ± 0.02 noise-weighted recovery, r_eff ≈ 0.99 survey-optimal Fisher recovery, r_cos shape cosine, ρ Fisher correlation, etc.). The density of similar symbols still creates unnecessary cognitive load; a compact notation table (or consistent subscripting such as r_shape, r_Fisher, ρ_GR) would eliminate any residual risk of misreading.
[MINOR] Sec. V and abstract: The MegaMapper projections are correctly labeled “illustrative” and “uncalibrated” (transferred GR/b_ϕ budget, no dedicated high-z kernel). The abstract and final paragraph should nevertheless restate in one explicit sentence that these numbers are scoping envelopes only and not equivalent in status to the SPHEREx recast.

One sentence: The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce prediction f_NL = −35/16 (with template overlap r ≈ 0.84) yields a SPHEREx sensitivity of ∼2.6–2.75σ before systematics that still preserves qualitative discriminatory power against single-field inflation (|f_NL^infl| ≈ 0.015, opposite sign)—is supported by the explicit shape-overlap integration, the independent multi-tracer Fisher validation that recovers r_eff ≈ 0.99, the closed-form Bayes-factor calculation cross-checked by three Monte-Carlo ensembles, and the transparent (if conservative) systematic budget.
