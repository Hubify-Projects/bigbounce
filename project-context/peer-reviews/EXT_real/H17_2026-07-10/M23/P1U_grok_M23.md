VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract + Sec. I + title: The title and abstract are excessively long, acronym-heavy, and bury the scoped central claims ("channel-level... under stated assumptions... not an operator-level theorem"); this violates PRD standards for concise, self-contained front matter and risks reader misinterpretation of the no-go strength.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (esp. scope paragraph + R1–R3 closures) + Sec. IX (14-constraint catalog) + Sec. X: Core technical results (Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma, regulated NJL gap equation with explicit repulsive scalar channel, term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion proving decoupling) are relegated to appendices without sufficient self-contained key steps, intermediate equations, or verification summaries in the main text; a referee/reader cannot audit the amplitude closures or transparency theorem without the full appendices.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C 1 (Inflationary Suppression + reheating thermal-reset paragraph): The supporting argument for Barrier 14 (coherent axial-current washout at T_reh) is conditional on an unquantified inequality Γ_wash(T_reh) > H(T_reh) with only order-of-magnitude SM channel estimates and no Boltzmann integration or explicit rate comparison; this is presented as strengthening the perturbation-transparency result but remains heuristic and weakens the claimed support for B14.
[MINOR] Sec. IV completeness argument + App. B/C: While the M_Pl-power-counting basis-completeness (via F1/F2 structural facts + NDA monotonicity + explicit closure of Jackiw–Pi and parity-odd four-fermion partner) is carefully scoped, the repeated phrasing of "basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes" without a single consolidated operator table in the main text invites over-reading as a full diffeomorphism-invariant no-go.
[MINOR] Sec. IX + Table I + observational appendices (F–H): The 14-barrier catalog is load-bearing for the structural claims, yet its justifications are scattered; simultaneously, the lengthy MCMC/NaMaster/spectator-ALP material (explicitly declared non-load-bearing and illustrative) inflates the manuscript to 62 pages and distracts from the analytical no-go.
[MINOR] Sec. X (perturbation transparency) + Sec. XIV D (structural tension): The mutual-exclusivity claim between ECH-sourced DE (requiring N_tot ≈ 92) and observable matter-bounce f_NL is logically derived but rests on the specific dilution bookkeeping and SPHEREx scale mapping; the coherence-window argument (N_coh ~ O(few)) needs an explicit numerical window or plot to be fully reproducible from the main text alone.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes close at the stated channel/amplitude/naturalness level under the paper’s explicit assumptions, that the Holst sector decouples from canonical scalar/tensor perturbations, and that a structural tension exists with the matter-bounce f_NL signature—is supported within the narrow, repeatedly qualified scope by the dimensional no-go, algebraic identities, Fierz lemma, gap-equation analysis, and one-loop estimates.
