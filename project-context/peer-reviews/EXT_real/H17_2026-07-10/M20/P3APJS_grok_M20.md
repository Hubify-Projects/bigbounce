VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §3 (three-tier catalog structure), and §3.1: The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is not uniformly supported; NEOWISE (419 objects) clears only a masking-geometry QA gate “by construction (not a detector-sensitivity test)”, while DESI/SDSS/Planck clear the injection-recovery gate for the broad/continuum class. The paper’s own text states the validation label “is mixed-validation, not uniform” and that narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, yet the abstract and title present 268,519 as the primary validated deliverable without this qualification in the lead sentence.
[MAJOR] Title, abstract, and §1/§3.1: The 268,519 / 377,482 counts are repeatedly framed as process-volume figures of “anomaly candidates surviving per-survey validation gates across a full-instrument-stream scan, not confirmed physical detections”, with ~98.7% of raw DESI anomalies on sky-fiber/filler spectra and only 2,468 on validated science-target spectra (≈0.92× the Liang et al. benchmark). Leading with the large multipliers (~141×, ~73×) while burying the science-target yield and non-detection framing creates a misleading impression of catalog scale and readiness for downstream science use.
[MAJOR] §3.5 and §3.7: The eROSITA tier (298 objects) is excised from every count (including the inclusive 377,482) because its production score axis (0.259) is irreproducible (non-monotone in committed raw scores; fails all 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains), and the former Gaia tier is removed as synthetic placeholder output. Releasing them only as separate membership lists while still discussing them at length undermines the reproducibility standard the paper itself invokes for ApJS catalog papers; a catalog submission cannot credibly present excised/irreproducible components as part of the core deliverable.
[MAJOR] §2.4, §3.4, and §6.1: LAMOST DR10 is retained in the inclusive 377,482 total (~113k objects) despite the explicit finding that the cross-transfer tier is a 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact and the native retrain yields only 5.8% injection-recovery at 5σ (gate FAIL). Labeling it a “methodological lesson” while still folding it into the grand total (and the 5″ dedup) is inconsistent with the three-tier validation framework the paper defines.
[MINOR] §2.2 (tabular feature preprocessing) and §6.4: Full-sample (not train-split-only) feature scaling is used for eROSITA/NEOWISE (and inferred for Gaia), with only a post-hoc bounded robustness check for eROSITA (Jaccard ~0.76 on top-298). This introduces potential validation-set leakage into the normalization constants that define the anomaly axis; the paper acknowledges the assumption but does not demonstrate that rank-ordering of the extreme tail is unaffected for all surveys.
[MINOR] §5: The multi-tracer fNL (§5) and NANOGrav (§5.1) sections are presented as “secondary demonstrations” yet occupy substantial space; both return null results (α_jk consistent with null at 0.29σ; γ consistent with matter-bounce but decisive only against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference). These add little to a catalog paper and risk appearing as overreach given the stated “no cosmological detection” conclusion and the large forecast uncertainties.
[MINOR] §3.1 and Table 3: The science-target recount (2,468 clusters) is a valuable clarification, but the paper’s own positional join shows that only ~1.3% of deduplicated DESI anomaly clusters match primary science-target bitmasks; the dominant population (sky fibers, fillers) is not representative of the science cases motivating the work (rare high-z QSOs, multi-tracer cosmology). This scope choice should be stated more prominently in the abstract and introduction.

One sentence: The central claim—that a reproducible, validated multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 sources can be produced at this scale via native-trained BigAE models with documented injection-recovery and deduplication—is only partially supported, because the validation is explicitly mixed, two tiers had to be excised for irreproducibility/synthetic content, one tier fails its own gate, and the large majority of the DESI component lies on non-science-target spectra.
