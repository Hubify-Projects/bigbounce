VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. II A and Appendix A (the f_NL = −35/16 correction): The claim that Cai et al.’s printed −35/8 is an erroneous transcribed-polynomial artifact (specifically a −(99/128)∑k_i³ discrepancy) and that the vertex-sum re-derivation yields exactly −35/16 is central to every downstream number, yet the explicit intermediate polynomials, the discrepant term, and the four-way cross-check (vertex re-summation, ϵ-grouped intermediates, three-benchmark matching, Li et al. general-c_s formula) are not reproduced in sufficient algebraic detail in the main text or a self-contained appendix for a referee to verify without the author’s private code; this must be expanded with the key collapsed polynomials shown side-by-side.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV, VII and the headline sensitivity ranges: The conservative 1.3σ floor (and the 0.8σ GR-bracket edge) rests on a transferred proxy correlation ρ ≈ −0.868 taken from the power-spectrum SDB channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance Cov_B of Heinrich et al. is not public; while the author transparently discloses this and supplies an in-house tree-level Gaussian multi-tracer surrogate that yields a higher channel-native floor (~2.3σ), the published headline range mixes proxy and surrogate procedures without a single, fully native marginalized Fisher matrix, rendering the lower edge heuristic rather than rigorous.
[MINOR] Sec. I and overall length (37 pp.): The manuscript is framed as “a sensitivity re-cast of a single external forecast,” yet devotes extensive space to in-house code validation, 10 000-sample null-space Monte Carlo, and coefficient-set scans whose ±0.13 scatter is ultimately sub-dominant to the noise-weighted r = 0.84 ± 0.02; the technical bulk (null-space SVD, monomial-basis sampling, c9i epsilon ratio checks, etc.) should be condensed or moved to supplemental material to meet Phys. Rev. D length and readability standards.
[MINOR] Sec. II C (assumption (d) and cubic-order transmission): The closure of faithful bispectrum transmission through the bounce to δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3} is argued via single-clock super-horizon ζ-conservation plus an O((k η_bounce)^2) gradient estimate; while physically motivated, it remains a scaling argument rather than a direct numerical integration of the Maldacena cubic action with bounce-modified mode functions, and should be flagged more prominently as the largest remaining model-dependence.
[MINOR] Sec. VI (Bayesian comparison): The BF ≈ 9–14 range is presented as “illustrative” and prior-sensitive (delta vs. σ_theory = 1.0 Gaussian bounce prior; [−5,+5] vs. [−15,+15] competitor); the closed-form formula and Monte-Carlo cross-checks are correct in structure, but the headline must state explicitly that BF values are not robust model-selection evidence and depend on the chosen competitor prior width.

The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 produces a template-corrected SPHEREx detection significance of ~2.6–2.75σ (optimistic) that remains qualitatively discriminatory against single-field inflation after systematics—is supported by the explicit overlap calculation (r = 0.84 ± 0.02), the independent in-house Fisher validation (reff ≈ 0.99), and the recast of the Heinrich et al. baseline, conditional on the listed assumptions and with the proxy-based conservative floor clearly caveated.
