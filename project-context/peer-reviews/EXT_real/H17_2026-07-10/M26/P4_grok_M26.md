VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I (also Sec. VI B): The claim that “a genuine Shamir-scale dipole would have been detected” (A95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%]) is not fully supported without explicit qualification for dilution. The GZ1 cross-match gives chirality accuracy 69.91% (g = 2a − 1 ≈ 0.40); the empirical injection-recovery (performed at catalog level via binomial sampling of the observed Ap field) therefore measures recoverable amplitude in the classified labels. A true-sky A_true = 1.7% would appear as A_obs ≈ 0.68% (near A50), yielding only ~50% power for a ≥3σ detection; the lower end of Shamir’s range is therefore only marginally testable under the stated pipeline.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D and Appendix D: The post-MASTER canonical ℓ = 1 residual (+3.64σ) has ~47% of its amplitude left as an “explicit open item” after the imaging + morphology forward model (52–54% reproduced, aligned at cos θ ≈ +0.83). While correctly argued to lie below the real-space A95 and therefore not to affect the primary null, a quantitative propagation of this remainder through the real-space dipole estimator (or an explicit upper limit on any cosmological fraction) would close the systematics budget.
[MINOR] Sec. III B and Table I: The declared hierarchy correctly identifies the HC (peq > 0.6) real-space dipole as primary, but the peq > 0.6 threshold—while pre-specified in the generator script and shown robust in the confidence-cut sweep—is justified post hoc by the depth-correlated tail identified in Sec. IV C. A short blinded-analysis statement or frozen pre-registration artifact (beyond the commit hash) would eliminate any residual forking-path concern.
[MINOR] Sec. II B and VI A: The 66.5% CE-ResNet pseudo-label fraction is openly acknowledged and bounded by the model-free GZ1-human-only dipole test (z = −0.54σ). However, the per-galaxy label-shuffle and pixel-permutation nulls still randomize the model’s own outputs; the template-regression and cross-spectrum anchors constrain inherited structure only to the extent it projects onto the tested templates. A one-paragraph statement quantifying the maximum undetected inherited dipole amplitude consistent with both the block-bootstrap exclusion and the GZ1 ceiling would make the independence argument airtight.

The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole on the high-confidence subsample is consistent with null (z = +0.41, rank-p = 0.31) at a sensitivity that disfavors a clean 1.7% observed dipole—is supported by the primary estimator, the declared hierarchy, the injection-recovery calibration, and the extensive systematics battery.
