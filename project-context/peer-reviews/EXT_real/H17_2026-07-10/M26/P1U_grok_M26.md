VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV (Scope paragraph and completeness lemma): The assertion that the four routes are basis-complete at the M_Pl-power-counting level within minimal ECH (via structural facts F1/F2, NDA monotonicity, and explicit closure of the Jackiw–Pi term plus parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner) relies on the collapse of the dimension-4 operator set {O1–O6} to topological/Fierz/Bianchi-vanishing sectors; while Appendix C states the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma and a released script performs the enumeration, the manuscript does not reproduce the explicit algebraic verification or matrix in the main text or a self-contained appendix subsection, preventing independent audit of the “no unenumerated channel” claim.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result, subsections B–E): The Tier-I claim that the Holst sector decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion for canonical scalar matter is central and strong; the text outlines the strategy (explicit perturbed-tetrad expansion, term-by-term cancellation) but does not display the key cancellations in the linearized Einstein or Holst equations of motion, so the decoupling must be shown explicitly (or the full algebra placed in a dedicated appendix) to support the result at PRD standards.
[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. IV F (Route 4 closure): The naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection for R4 is correctly distinguished from the amplitude closures of R1–R3, but the abstract’s phrasing (“closed not by amplitude mismatch but by a naturalness / explanatory-deficit objection, relocating rather than solving the cosmological-constant problem”) risks being misread as a uniform no-go; a one-sentence clarification that R4 is closed only by relocating the tuning (not by amplitude exclusion) would eliminate any perception of overclaim.
[MINOR] Sec. II C 1, Appendix B (dimensional bookkeeping): The off-shell dimension-+1 operator (Eq. 6) versus the genuine dimension-4 basis (Eqs. 7–8) is addressed by two admissible completions (on-shell curvature dressing vs. local-operator promotion), which produce slightly offset N_tot values (~92 vs. ~94); the manuscript should adopt one uniform convention and propagate its effect on D_inf and all amplitude budgets for internal consistency.
[MAJOR] Sec. XIV D (structural tension): The mutual-exclusivity claim between ECH-sourced dark energy (N_tot ≈92) and an observable matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 at SPHEREx scales rests on the differential e-fold mapping (~32) exceeding the coherence window N_coh ∼ O(few); this quantitative erasure argument is stated without an explicit derivation or reference to the mode evolution equations, so the “definitively erased” conclusion requires a short self-contained calculation to be rigorous.

The central claim—that under the stated assumptions the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes close at channel level (amplitude for R1–R3, naturalness for R4), the Holst sector is perturbation-transparent for canonical scalar matter, and ECH dark energy is structurally incompatible with an observable matter-bounce f_NL—is supported by the amplitude budgets, operator enumerations, and decoupling arguments, subject to the NDA/ansatz qualifications the authors themselves emphasize.
