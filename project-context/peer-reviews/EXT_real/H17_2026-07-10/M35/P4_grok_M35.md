VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. IV C (primary estimator): The headline real-space dipole (+0.41σ, rank-p=0.31) is reported exclusively on the pre-specified high-confidence subsample (p_eq > 0.6, N≈9.5×10^5), yet the full Catalog C exhibits a larger excess (z≈4.2–4.4) localized to the p_eq ≤0.6 tail; while the confidence-cut sweep demonstrates stability above 0.6, the main-text justification for adopting 0.6 as the single primary threshold (rather than a purity-completeness optimization or GZ1-validated cut) remains implicit and should be stated explicitly with the pre-registration record.

[MINOR] Sec. IV D and Appendix D (monopole+mask leakage and eight-anchor battery): The post-MASTER canonical ℓ=1 residual (+3.64σ) is attributed to survey systematics after the imaging+morphology forward model accounts for only ≈52–54% of the amplitude (leaving an explicit ~47% open item); although the authors correctly bound even the full residual amplitude below the real-space A_50≈0.75% floor and note that the primary claim does not consume the harmonic channel, this incomplete closure of the diagnostic residual should be caveated more prominently as a limitation on the harmonic systematics budget.

[MINOR] Sec. VI A (pseudo-label independence): The model-independent GZ1-human-label cross-check (z=−0.54σ) is presented as corroboration against CE-ResNet inheritance, but its sensitivity floor (A_95≈4.5–6.8%) is ~4–5× coarser than the headline HC sample; this should be stated quantitatively so readers do not over-interpret the GZ1-only null as constraining sub-percent inherited structure at the level of the primary result.

[MINOR] Sec. V A and abstract (comparison to Shamir): The block-bootstrap WLS template fit is reported to disfavor a clean 1.7% dipole at z≈−7.6, but the text correctly notes that a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is still required for a likelihood-level exclusion of Shamir’s specific estimator; the abstract and conclusions should avoid phrasing that could be misread as a direct statistical exclusion of Shamir’s published results rather than a pipeline-level amplitude comparison.

[MINOR] Overall presentation (Secs. III–IV and Table I): The analysis hierarchy and “only P1–P2 carry cosmological weight” framing are declared, but the proliferation of distinct null procedures, non-commensurable σ values, and heavy appendix cross-references makes the logical chain difficult to follow; a single consolidated flowchart or executive summary of which estimators support which claims would improve readability without altering content.

One sentence: The central claim—that the large-scale chirality dipole is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity under the declared primary real-space estimator and is robust to the tested systematics—is supported by the +0.41σ HC dipole, the block-bootstrap template exclusion, and the injection-recovery calibration, provided the minor clarifications above are addressed.
