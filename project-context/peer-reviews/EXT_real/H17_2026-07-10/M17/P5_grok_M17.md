VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section V.B (and abstract): The DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast is explicitly designated post-hoc as the “exploratory, not pre-registered” primary estimand after data inspection, with the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across five correlated void definitions offered as the strictly quotable headline. This garden-of-forking-paths exposure (multiple classifiers, stratifications, and hyperparameter choices enumerated in Table IV) requires a more prominent, front-loaded caveat that the quoted ≈0.9 pp systematic envelope and de-attenuated ≈2.26 pp physical bound represent the tightest among several post-hoc estimators rather than a pre-specified constraint; the promised DR2 pre-registration is noted but does not retroactively strengthen the DR1 claim.
[MINOR] Abstract and Section VIII: The RSD robustness argument (fixed-geometry Monte Carlo + first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction shifting ∆fCW by only 0.024 pp) is technically sound for the dominant coherent outflow term, but the disclosure that the published DESIVAST void catalog itself is not re-derived under a full nonlinear reconstruction (small-scale FoG residuals on membership remain) should be stated explicitly in the abstract and summary; the current phrasing risks implying a stronger immunity than the bounded sensitivity test actually demonstrates.
[MINOR] Abstract and Section II (relation to Paper IV): The de-attenuated physical-chirality bound (≈2.26 pp) is derived from the labelled-fraction envelope divided by the GZ1 accuracy attenuation factor (2a−1 ≃ 0.40 at 69.91 % floor). While the global monopole attenuation is justified in the companion catalog paper, the manuscript should briefly confirm (or reference) that the same linear attenuation applies to a potential environment-conditional signal before presenting the de-attenuated number as the quantity “model-builders should use.”
[MINOR] Section VI.A and VII (secondary T-Web path): The ∼2.1σ bright-vs-dark filament sign-flip is correctly flagged as a survey-selection diagnostic rather than an astrophysical signal, and logistic regression plus contingency tests support a BGS imaging-leg origin. However, the residual ambiguity disclosure (no end-to-end injection-recovery mock is shown) should be cross-referenced more explicitly when the T-Web results are introduced, so readers do not mis-weight the secondary path relative to the DESIVAST primary null.

The central claim of no detectable environment dependence of spiral chirality (null ∆fCW consistent with parity across DESIVAST definitions, with a budgeted upper bound) is supported by the data, multiplicity-controlled tests, and systematics envelope.
