VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §1, and §3 (three-tier structure): The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is presented as the primary deliverable, yet the text simultaneously defines it as a process-volume figure (not confirmed physical detections), excludes the ~113k LAMOST exploratory tier (98% blue-excess artifact), excises the eROSITA 298-object tier for irreproducible production score axis, and removes the synthetic Gaia tier; the like-for-like science-target yield on validated DESI spectra is only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark), rendering the large multipliers (~141×, ~73×) misleading despite the caveats.
[MAJOR] §2.4 (Path-C rebuild) and §3 (survey-by-survey): The native-retrain validation protocol is uneven and incompletely passed—LAMOST recovers only 5.8% at 5σ (explicit FAIL), eROSITA 1.2% (FAIL + axis irreproducibility), Planck passes injection-recovery but fails the val-loss ≤0.30 gate (0.4437), and NEOWISE passes only a geometry-QA mask (not detector-sensitivity); the “validated” label therefore rests on non-uniform, survey-specific gates rather than a consistent standard.
[MAJOR] §3.5 (eROSITA): The production score axis (threshold 0.259) cannot be reproduced from any committed artifact (fails all 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains; raw scores are non-monotone, Spearman ρ = −0.10); excising the tier from every count while still publishing a detailed “membership addendum” and Table 5 creates a reproducibility inconsistency that undermines the catalog’s claim to catalog-grade status.
[MAJOR] §6.4(i) and DESI injection-recovery results: The single production-ensemble sensitivity gate certifies only the broad/continuum-dominated class at 99–100% recovery for 5σ per-spectrum strength; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ (explicit sensitivity floor of the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer), yet the catalog is released and framed without a clear, prominent statement that emission-line completeness is not certified at the advertised grade.
[MINOR] §2.2, §3.3, and Table 2: Per-survey anomaly scores S are normalized on each survey’s own validation pool (or cross-transfer scale for SDSS/LAMOST), making cross-survey rate or rank comparisons invalid; the paper repeatedly juxtaposes them anyway, and the SDSS “headline” 77,905 is a fixed-size continuity slice (not a native threshold), further complicating interpretation.
[MINOR] Overall presentation and §5: The manuscript is excessively dense with internal pipeline/script/JSON artifact citations that are required to verify core numbers; the two cosmological applications are explicitly null results (no fNL improvement, NANOGrav γ only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively inconsistent only with an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference) and add little value as “secondary demonstrations.”

The central claim of a rigorously validated, catalog-grade multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 sources is only partially supported, because validation is limited to broad/continuum anomalies on a subset of surveys, key tiers are excised or fail reproducibility gates, and like-for-like science-target yields are comparable to (not substantially larger than) existing single-survey benchmarks.
