VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §I (headline counts), and §III (three-tier structure): The core claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” (with 268,319 point sources) is not supported by uniform, detector-sensitivity validation across components. DESI, SDSS, and Planck pass injection-recovery for the broad/continuum class, but NEOWISE passes only a geometry-QA mask (explicitly “not a detector-sensitivity test”), LAMOST is relabeled exploratory (98% blue-excess training-bias artifact, 5.8% recovery FAIL), eROSITA is excised entirely (1.2% recovery + irreproducible production score axis), and the synthetic Gaia tier was removed post-audit. The like-for-like science-target yield is only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× prior single-survey benchmark [11]), while the large multipliers (∼73×–141×) are explicitly process-volume figures over the full instrument stream (∼98.7% non-primary targets). The manuscript repeatedly caveats that 268,519 “is a process-volume figure… not a count of confirmed physical detections,” yet foregrounds it as the primary deliverable.
[MAJOR] §III E (eROSITA) and §II B (feature scaling/provenance): The eROSITA production score axis (threshold 0.259) cannot be recovered from any committed artifact; 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains all fail, and production values are non-monotone in the raw score (Spearman ρ = −0.10). Excising the tier and releasing only a top-298 membership list is the correct action, but the extensive discussion, Table V, and admission of an “undocumented post-hoc rescaling step whose code was never committed” demonstrate a fundamental reproducibility failure in a core pipeline component. The same standard was applied to remove the synthetic Gaia tier, yet the manuscript retains lengthy provenance audits that undermine overall catalog integrity.
[MAJOR] §II D (Path-C rebuild), §VI D (limitations), and reproducibility artifacts: While a standalone deduplication script (reproduce_headline_dedup.py) and some injection-recovery JSONs are committed, critical elements remain pod-side or unrecoverable (raw native score parquets, full 22.5 M DESI re-inference, Planck held-out re-score, NEOWISE feature tables). The 5-fold stability checks use deliberately short-trained proxy models that fail the paper’s own val-loss ≤0.30 gate; the DESI robustness claim therefore rests on a single production-ensemble broad-class injection-recovery test. Full end-to-end reproducibility of the released 268,519 catalog is not demonstrated at the standard required for a catalog data product in Phys. Rev. D.
[MINOR] §V (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL measurement returns a noise-driven central shift (9.4%) lying inside the 1σ envelope of the single-tracer baseline, and the NANOGrav γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 result is only “decisive” against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference while remaining consistent with environmentally modified models. These sections add substantial length and complexity with no claimed detection or improvement on existing bounds; they read as post-hoc demonstrations rather than motivated science drivers for the catalog.
[MINOR] Overall structure and presentation: The manuscript is footnote-heavy, uses three overlapping tier definitions, repeatedly re-explains “process-volume vs. like-for-like,” and interleaves validation caveats with headline numbers. This makes it difficult for a reader to extract a crisp, self-contained statement of exactly which objects meet which validation standard and what downstream analyses are licensed. A single consolidated validation-summary table and stricter separation of the validated catalog from exploratory/process-volume material would materially improve clarity.

The central claim of delivering a uniformly validated 268k multi-survey anomaly catalog is not supported; the actual science-target yield is comparable to prior single-survey work, validation is heterogeneous, and reproducibility failures required excision of multiple tiers.
