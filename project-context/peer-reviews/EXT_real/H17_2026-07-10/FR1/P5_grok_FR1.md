VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] §V B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths) and abstract: The DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast is explicitly designated "primary" and "exploratory/post-hoc" with the statement that "no timestamped plan predates the data" and an enumerated ~dozen-trial analysis tree; while the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null (|z_Δ| ≤ 1.25 across all five estimators) is transparently reported and the DR2 pre-registration commitment is noted, the "headline result" and "designated primary" framing still risks appearing data-dependent and requires stronger, repeated caveats that the strictly quotable claim is the uniform family-wise null rather than any single estimator.
[MINOR] Abstract, §VIII, and §XIII: All results (T-Web, DESIVAST point-in-sphere/GALZONE, membership) are performed in fixed redshift space without real-space reconstruction; the finger-of-god Monte Carlo stability (<0.4 pp) is cited but no end-to-end RSD mock quantifying net bias on Δf_CW from void geometry distortion, Kaiser effect, or survey-edge leakage is provided, leaving the model-constraint interpretation (especially the de-attenuated ~2.26 pp physical bound) as a redshift-space statement whose translation to real-space parity-violation amplitudes is unquantified.
[MINOR] §II, Appendix A, and Table I: The per-galaxy class_eq labels and monopole are taken from the concurrently submitted companion Paper IV (arXiv placeholder); although the contrast is algebraically monopole-invariant and labels are public (CC-BY-4.0), the self-contained summary of parity-equivariance validation, GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91%, κ=0.40), and training details is abbreviated; key validation metrics or tables should be reproduced or expanded so the environmental null stands fully independently of the companion.
[MINOR] Headline result, §VIII C, and §XIII: The de-attenuated physical-chirality bound (~2.26 pp) assumes symmetric classifier error across void/non-void strata; the reported asymmetry test (difference −0.018, z=−0.89, p=0.37) is consistent with zero but relies on an illustrative n=6 per-galaxy disagreement check in the DESIVAST z≤0.24 overlap, which is under-powered and explicitly flagged as non-formal, so the additional uncertainty from possible environment-dependent relabeling should be stated more prominently as a limitation on the physical interpretation.

The central claim of no significant void/non-void chirality dependence (family-wise Bonferroni-5 null with |Δf_CW| ≤ 0.004 and |z_Δ| ≤ 1.25 across five DESIVAST definitions on 57,081 exact-footprint void spirals, bounded by an ~0.9 pp systematic envelope) is supported by the data, public labels, multiple void algorithms, permutation tests, and disclosed analysis tree.
