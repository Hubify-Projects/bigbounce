VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. IV (Scope paragraph, p. 14) and abstract: The assertion that the four enumerated routes exhaust minimal-ECH dark-energy channels at the M_Pl-power-counting level (via structural facts F1/F2, NDA monotonicity, and the Fierz-by-Fierz lemma of App. C) is not supported by an exhaustive operator classification; the symmetry argument is plausible but does not rule out additional diffeomorphism-invariant operators (e.g., higher-derivative or boundary terms) that could evade the single-scale NDA ceiling without introducing a new light scale.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result, pp. 26–32): The central technical claim that torsion vanishes identically at all classical metric/scalar perturbation orders around the torsion-free branch (and that the Holst sector therefore decouples) rests on a Bianchi-identity sketch for the dual contraction plus a statement of explicit verification; the provided text does not display the full perturbed-tetrad expansion or term-by-term cancellation for all torsion irreps, making independent verification impossible.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F (pp. 20–22) and title: Route R4 is closed only by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit argument (the same coupling that fits β_obs forces an ultralight m_θ ∼ H_0 tuning that merely relocates the CC problem); this is qualitatively different from the amplitude-suppression closures of R1–R3 and does not justify grouping all four under a uniform “channel-level constraints” headline or the parenthetical “Amplitude Closure for R1–R3, Naturalness Closure for R4.”
[MINOR] Sec. II C and App. B (pp. 11–12, 37–39): The phenomenological mapping ρ_Λ = Ξ M_Pl^4 with N_tot ≈ 92 and the single-scale NDA no-go are presented as robust, yet the text acknowledges that any genuine dimension-4 parity-odd completion or exact cancellation evades the bound; the paper does not quantify the measure of such evasions or demonstrate that they are excluded by the 14-barrier catalog once non-minimal matter couplings are admitted.
[MINOR] Sec. XIV D (p. 35): The claimed structural tension (N_tot ≈ 92 erases the matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 signature at SPHEREx-accessible k) depends on a specific e-fold differential ∼32 and an assumed coherence window N_coh ∼ O(few); the mapping from comoving k_SPHEREx to physical bounce-era scales is stated but not derived with explicit formulas or references, weakening the mutual-exclusivity conclusion.
[MINOR] General (throughout, esp. Secs. IV, IX, X): The manuscript is 60 pages of dense cross-references to appendices, “stated assumptions,” and companion papers; while the core analytic claims are asserted to be self-contained, the absence of inline key intermediate steps (e.g., the explicit symbolic Bianchi and εε identities, the full Fierz matrix) impairs readability and referee verification.

The central claim is internally consistent within its narrowly delimited scope and assumptions but only partially supported, as the operator-basis completeness, full perturbation decoupling, and uniform treatment of R4 rest on symmetry arguments and sketches rather than exhaustive, explicitly displayed derivations.
