VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. VII (and abstract): The conservative 1.3σ floor (and ~0.8σ GR-bracket edge) relies on a transferred proxy correlation ρ ≈ −0.868 from the power-spectrum SDB Fisher channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance Cov_B of Heinrich et al. is not public; this proxy does not rigorously capture the full multi-tracer bispectrum covariance structure (including off-diagonal triangle correlations and cross-bin mode coupling), so the quoted floor is an estimate rather than a channel-native marginalization.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C (assumption (d)) and abstract: Faithful cubic-order bispectrum transmission through the bounce is closed only via single-clock nonlinear superhorizon ζ-conservation plus an O((k η_bounce)^2) ∼ 10^{-4} scaling estimate giving δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3}; no explicit numerical evaluation of the four Maldacena cubic integrals with bounce-modified mode functions is performed, leaving the largest model-dependence caveat under-quantified relative to the headline sensitivity claim.
[MINOR] Sec. I, abstract, and Table V: The optimistic 2.6–2.75σ range is presented prominently while the full post-systematics envelope reaches marginal values; the text correctly labels the lower end as “marginal-sensitivity estimates,” but the abstract and figure captions should more explicitly state that even the template-corrected optimistic end remains < 3σ and constitutes a sensitivity target rather than a projected detection.
[MINOR] Sec. II A and Appendix A: The central correction f_NL = −35/16 (vs. the printed −35/8) is verified four ways and traced to a specific −(99/128) ∑ k_i^3 discrepancy in the transcribed polynomial, but the explicit re-summation of Cai et al.’s four cubic-action vertices at ε = 3/2 (or the committed null-space scripts) is not reproduced in the appendix; adding the key intermediate polynomials or a short code excerpt would strengthen reproducibility of the factor-of-two shift.
[MINOR] Sec. V: The MegaMapper projection transfers the SPHEREx-derived GR/b_ϕ systematic budget without dedicated high-z (z = 2–5) calibration of relativistic projection kernels; while labeled “illustrative” and “uncalibrated,” the section should more clearly bound the additional uncertainty from unmodeled Doppler/gravitational-redshift/lensing-magnification terms that grow steeply above z ≈ 2.

The central claim—that the corrected f_NL = −35/16 sets a realistic SPHEREx sensitivity target of ∼1.3–2.75σ (optimistic 2.6–2.75σ) that preserves qualitative discriminatory power against single-field inflation—is supported by the thorough template-overlap calculation (r = 0.84 ± 0.02), independent multi-tracer Fisher validation (r_eff ≈ 0.99), and explicit systematic budget, conditional on the stated assumptions and proxy correlation.
