VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

Excessive length, density, and idiosyncratic internal structure (60 pp with Foundations A–G, Branches H/J/L/M/N/O, 14 barriers, repeated “channel-level under stated assumptions” caveats) severely impair readability and journal suitability; the manuscript requires substantial condensation, a clearer executive roadmap early in the introduction, and reduced reliance on self-referential numbering.
Scope and potential overclaiming: Although the text repeatedly qualifies results as channel-level (not a full operator-basis theorem) and assumption-dependent, the abstract, title, and Sec. I framing risk misinterpretation as a stronger no-go; the limitations (scaling ansätze for R2/R3, naturalness objection for R4, single-scale NDA, phenomenological dimensional ansatz for ρ_Λ) must be foregrounded more explicitly and consistently.
Perturbation-transparency result (Sec. X) is presented as central but the scalar-sector proof and tensor extension are sketched at a level insufficient for independent verification; expand with explicit perturbed-tetrad expansions, term-by-term cancellation steps, and confirmation that the Holst dual ϵ^{μνρσ}R_{μνρσ} vanishes identically on the Levi-Civita connection via the algebraic Bianchi identity (distinct from Pontryagin).
Dimension-4 parity-odd operator completeness (App. B) rests on enumeration plus two symbolically verified tensor identities (εR=0 Bianchi contraction, εε=3!δ collapse); while a verification script is referenced, the main text or appendix must demonstrate that no other admissible local diffeomorphism-invariant dim-4 operators exist outside the topological/Fierz-reducible/Bianchi-vanishing classes.
Route 2 one-loop operator (Eq. 17) is motivated by but not directly taken from Shapiro & Teixeira; clarify the precise status of the absolute normalization β(γ), confirm the ~10^{-60} suppression margin is robust to O(1)–O(10^{10}) prefactor uncertainty, and ensure the dimensionless ratio derivation is free of convention ambiguities (H_0/M_Pl placement, reduced vs. unreduced Planck mass).
Structural tension (Sec. XIV D) between N_tot≈92 (required for dark-energy dilution) and erasure of the matter-bounce f_NL=−35/16 signature is conceptually interesting but quantitatively under-supported; provide explicit mapping of SPHEREx-accessible k~10^{-1} h/Mpc back to bounce-era physical scales (including coherence window N_coh and subhorizon regime transition) and justify why ΔN≈32 definitively erases the contraction-mode bispectrum.
Observational appendices (MCMC ΛCDM+ΔN_eff, NaMaster validation, spectator-ALP fit) occupy substantial space yet are declared non-load-bearing for the core no-go and transparency results; move them to supplemental material or a dedicated reproducibility companion to streamline the main text while retaining only essential illustrative context.
Technical consistency of conventions: Torsion weighting (half- vs. full-weight), Holst prefactors, κ=8πG vs. reduced Planck mass, and on-shell vs. off-shell dimensional bookkeeping must be uniformly applied; cross-check all derivations (especially Cartan equation, four-fermion coefficients, and App. B operator promotion) against a single chosen convention set.
References to coordinated submissions (Papers II–V) and external archives: For a standalone PRD submission, every load-bearing claim, numerical value used in comparisons, and reproducibility artifact must be either self-contained or explicitly supplemental within this manuscript; clarify which quoted posteriors/forecasts are purely contextual.
Related-work placement and novelty framing: The discussion of prior LQC/ECH/ALP literature and how the 14-barrier catalog plus explicit closure of the Jackiw–Pi and parity-odd four-fermion partner operators advance beyond existing no-go arguments is underdeveloped; strengthen Sec. VIII and the introduction to articulate the incremental contribution more sharply.
