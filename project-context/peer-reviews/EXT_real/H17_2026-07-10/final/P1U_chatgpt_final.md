[MAJOR] Quantum consistency of the “minimal” basis: The manuscript invokes fermion and graviton loops for R2–R3 while excluding torsion kinetic terms, higher-curvature terms, and other symmetry-allowed operators as “non-minimal.” A quantum effective theory is not closed under that restriction: the same loops generally require the full set of allowed counterterms. One cannot use selected loop effects while treating other radiatively generated operators as outside the theory.

[MAJOR] Section X, perturbation transparency: The restricted classical statement is correct but essentially immediate from standard ECH: spinless canonical scalar matter has zero Cartan source, the connection is Levi–Civita, and a constant-coefficient Holst term then vanishes by the algebraic Bianchi identity. The “all-orders theorem” adds no substantive perturbative result beyond this identity and excludes fermions, dynamical Immirzi fields, torsion propagation, non-minimal couplings, and loops—the sectors relevant to the other proposed routes. It therefore cannot reinforce their closure, and its novelty is insufficient to carry the paper.
