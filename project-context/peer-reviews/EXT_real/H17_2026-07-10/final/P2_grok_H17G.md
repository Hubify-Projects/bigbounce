VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] The abstract is excessively long and dense, devoting an entire paragraph to the detailed vertex re-summation, the specific −(99/128) term, and the Cai–Li transcription discrepancy before stating the headline sensitivity ranges; condense the technical derivation summary and move the full accounting exclusively to Appendix A (or a dedicated subsection) to improve readability while preserving the central claim that the vertex sum yields the clean −35/16 value matching Li et al. at cs = 1.
[MINOR] The independent tree-level multi-tracer Fisher validation (c13_independent_bounce_fisher.py and redshift-space extension) is a valuable cross-check that recovers reff ≈ 0.99 and reproduces the Heinrich et al. baseline to 2–11%, but its limitations (leading-order Gaussian covariance, tree-level kernels, b2/bs2 held fixed rather than marginalized, no fingers-of-God) are stated only in passing; elevate these caveats to a short dedicated paragraph immediately after the validation numbers so readers do not over-interpret the ~3.2–3.5σ (real-space) or ~4.9–5.2σ (redshift-space) figures relative to the conservative headline recast that uses r = 0.84.
[MINOR] The 10 000-sample null-space scan of the six-monomial coefficients (radius-50 ball, uniform Euclidean measure) is thorough and correctly identifies the 3-dimensional null space arising from the three benchmark constraints, but the quoted r = 0.85 ± 0.13 scatter and the 16th–84th percentile range (0.70–0.99) are explicitly basis- and measure-dependent; add a one-sentence statement that an alternative monomial basis or log-uniform sampling in coefficient space would shift the numerical envelope while leaving the shape-cosine stability (rcos > 0.95–0.97) and the headline noise-weighted central value r = 0.84 ± 0.02 unchanged.
[MINOR] The MegaMapper section is candidly labeled “illustrative,” “uncalibrated projection,” and “design-uncertainty envelope,” yet it still propagates the full SPHEREx-derived GR-marginalization and bϕ budget to z = 2–5 without any high-redshift-specific kernel or reference to the known steep rise of relativistic projection effects at z ≳ 2; either (a) remove the quantitative 1.5–3.5σ envelope or (b) replace it with a purely qualitative statement that a dedicated Stage-V forecast would be required, citing the relevant high-z GR-projection literature.
[MINOR] Occasional strong phrasing (“decisively favors,” “the evidence decisively favors −35/16,” “clean fNL = −35/16”) appears in the abstract, introduction, and summary; replace with neutral language such as “the explicit vertex-level re-summation yields −35/16, in agreement with Li et al. at cs = 1” and “supports adoption of the corrected central value,” consistent with the paper’s own careful hedging on assumption (d) and the ϵ-correction range.
[MINOR] The load-bearing caveat (⋆) on faithful cubic-order transmission (assumption (d)) is well argued via single-clock nonlinear adiabaticity and the Wilson-Ewing escape from the Quintin et al. no-go, but the quantitative bound δfNL ≲ 10−3 rests on the gradient-expansion estimate O((kηbounce)2) ~ 10−4; add a short sentence noting that a full numerical evaluation of the O((kηbounce)2) coefficient in the dressed-metric scheme would tighten (but is not required to establish) the bound, thereby making the model-dependence statement fully explicit.
