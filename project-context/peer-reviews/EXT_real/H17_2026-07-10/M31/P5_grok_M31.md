VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and §I: The statement that “no published bounce or inflation model currently predicts an environment-conditional chirality signature at this scale” is presented without citations to specific models or quantitative predictions in the literature; adding 1–2 targeted references (even confirming the null prediction) would better contextualize the new empirical bound for readers.
[MINOR] §V B and Table IV: The post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored path as “designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered)” is transparently declared with garden-of-forking-paths accounting, but the effective trials budget is described only qualitatively (“order of a few dozen”); an explicit enumeration of all tested stratifications (exact HEALPix NSIDE counts, quintile definitions, program splits, etc.) and their effect on the quoted Bonferroni thresholds would further strengthen statistical transparency.
[MINOR] §VIII (and referenced Table XI): The ≈0.9 pp “honest effective 2σ systematic envelope” is obtained by folding counting-statistics CI with fixed-void-geometry membership, any-hole-vs-maximal-sphere geometry (0.60 pp co-dominant), footprint, confidence, and match-radius terms “in quadrature,” yet the explicit per-term values and arithmetic steps are not shown or tabulated; a short supplementary calculation or expanded Table XI entry would make the envelope fully reproducible.
[MINOR] §VI D and logistic-regression paragraph: The bright/dark target-program contingency (χ² = 4933, Cramér’s V = 0.078) and marginal bright-program coefficient (p ≈ 0.049) are acknowledged, with the claim that leakage into the primary DESIVAST contrast is ≪ 0.01 pp; however, no explicit regression or split of the DESIVAST void/non-void sample by imaging leg (BASS+MzLS / DECaLS / DES) is reported, leaving a small residual ambiguity about leg-dependent classifier bias that a one-sentence check could close.
[MINOR] §II and §VIII F: The physical-chirality de-attenuation (0.9 pp / 0.3982 ≈ 2.26 pp) relies on the global GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %, κ = 0.40) taken from the concurrent companion Paper IV; while the labelled-fraction contrast itself is algebraically monopole-invariant, a one-paragraph self-contained recap of how that accuracy floor was derived (and why it is treated as environment-independent) would make the present manuscript more standalone for referees and readers.

The central claim—that spiral-galaxy chirality shows no significant redshift-space environmental dependence at the ≳25 Mpc/h scale probed by DESIVAST and T-Web, with a robust |∆f_CW| ≲ 0.004 (labelled) / ≲ 2.26 pp (de-attenuated physical) upper bound—is supported by the multi-algorithm family-wise null, systematic envelope, RSD MC/reconstruction tests, and cross-check consistency.
