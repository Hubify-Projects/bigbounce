VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract & Sec. I (Scope and conventions): The headline sensitivity envelope ∼2.6–2.75σ (optimistic) to ∼1.3–2.75σ (post-systematics) and the separate 1.3–1.4σ all-combined floor are explicitly described as conditional envelopes from qualitatively different null procedures (CMB-Fisher vs. LSS noise-weighting; proxy ρ = −0.868 vs. channel-native surrogate covariance); however, the abstract presents them as a single range without reiterating that the endpoints are not directly comparable, which could mislead casual readers.
[MINOR] Sec. II A (The Prediction) & Appendix A (referenced): The factor-of-two correction f_NL = −35/16 (vs. literature −35/8) is certified internally via vertex re-summation, ϵ-ordered intermediates, three-benchmark matching, and Li et al. cross-check, with the discrepancy traced to a −(99/128)∑k_i^3 term in the transcribed polynomial; while the logic is internally consistent and the Table I benchmarks plus Li et al. formula at c_s=1 agree, the explicit corrected monomial coefficients or vertex-by-vertex breakdown should be reproduced in the appendix (or supplemental material) for immediate referee/reader verification without relying on the released code/json artifacts.
[MINOR] Sec. II A & III B (null-space analysis, r = 0.84 ± 0.02): The 10 000-sample null-space scan (r = 0.85 ± 0.13) and the noise-weighted central value are carefully distinguished, with the headline using only the latter; however, the propagation statement that the 16th–84th percentile range maps to 2.2–3.1σ pre-systematics (and ∼1.25σ under GR quadrature) is mentioned only in a footnote and not cross-referenced in the abstract or Sec. IV summary, leaving the reader to track which uncertainty is propagated where.
[MINOR] Sec. IV (SPHEREx forecast) & independent Fisher validation: The in-house tree-level multi-tracer Fisher (real-space σ(f_local_NL) ≈ 0.63–0.69; redshift-space ≈ 0.42–0.45) validates the Heinrich et al. baseline to 2–11% and yields r_eff ≈ 0.99, which is correctly used only as a consistency check; the listed honest limitations (Gaussian covariance, tree-level kernels, b_2/bs_2 held fixed, no FoG) are stated, but a short quantitative estimate of the expected shift if b_2 were marginalized or non-Gaussian covariance included would strengthen the validation claim.
[MINOR] Sec. VII (systematic budget, not fully excerpted but referenced) & notation: The GR-marginalization proxy (ρ = −0.868 transferred from SDB channel) is transparently adopted because Cov_B is not public, and the channel-native surrogate gives a higher floor (σ_marg ≈ 0.94); the text already flags both the 0.8σ and 1.3σ values as marginal-sensitivity estimates. A one-sentence clarification in the abstract that the lower edge remains a proxy-based floor (pending public per-triangle covariance) would eliminate any perception of over-claiming.
[MINOR] Throughout (presentation): The manuscript is commendably thorough (source-level audit, explicit template mismatch, itemized systematics, closed-form BF cross-validated by three MC ensembles), but the density of internal file references (c15_..., phase3_..., null space analysis.py, etc.), long parenthetical asides, and repeated scope disclaimers make it longer than necessary for the core result; minor tightening (e.g., move exhaustive code-commit lists to Data Availability, condense repetitive assumption restatements) would improve readability without loss of rigor.

The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 sets a realistic SPHEREx sensitivity target (∼1.3–2.75σ post-systematics) that preserves qualitative discriminatory power against single-field slow-roll inflation (|f_NL^infl| ≈ 0.015, opposite sign), with illustrative BF ≈ 9–14—is supported by the explicit template-overlap calculation (r = 0.84 ± 0.02), independent Fisher validation, conservative systematic budget, and analytic Bayes-factor formula cross-checked by Monte Carlo ensembles, all conditional on the stated assumptions (a)–(f) and the proxy nature of the GR floor.
