VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I (Scope paragraph) + Sec. IV (opening): The phrasing “shown basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes” and “two previously-omitted parity-odd operators … are closed explicitly” is accurate only after the Fierz-by-Fierz lemma (App. C) and the F1/F2 symmetry counting; the text occasionally elides the qualifier “at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes under algebraic torsion and minimal coupling,” which risks being read as a stronger operator-basis theorem than claimed.
[MINOR] Sec. IV F (Route 4) and Sec. IX (Barrier catalog): Route 4 is closed by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit argument (“relocating rather than solving the cosmological-constant problem”) rather than an amplitude or operator prohibition; this Tier-II objection should be explicitly distinguished in the executive summary (Table I) and in the 14-constraint catalog from the Tier-III amplitude suppressions of R1–R3 to prevent conflation of mathematical closure with naturalness reasoning.
[MINOR] Sec. X (Statement and Proof): The perturbation-transparency result is correctly restricted to “canonical scalar matter,” but the main-text definition of “canonical” (minimal coupling, vanishing spin density, no non-minimal or propagating-torsion terms) is stated only in caveats; a one-sentence definition in Sec. X A and a parenthetical in the abstract would eliminate ambiguity about the precise domain of decoupling.
[MINOR] Sec. IV A (Route 1) + App. D: The regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion (repulsive scalar channel, sub-critical |G_eff|/G_crit) is a strong result, but the main text does not quote the explicit cutoff-independent ratio or the Fierz-projected G_scalar = −3/64 κ; adding the numerical value and the one-line statement that the ratio remains ≲ 0.16 across all scanned cutoffs would make the vacuum-condensate closure self-contained without requiring the appendix.
[MINOR] Sec. II C 1 and Sec. XII A (Inflationary suppression and N_tot ≈ 92): The bookkeeping that converts the single-scale NDA hierarchy into a residual ΔN_tot ≈ 4 sensitivity is clearly labeled as reparameterization rather than solution; however, the text does not explicitly flag that this reparameterization still leaves an O(10^5) tuning in the initial condition for N_tot, which is the same order as the usual inflationary fine-tuning problem and should be stated once for balance.

The central claim—that none of the four enumerated minimal-ECH channels yields an unsuppressed, natural (meV)^4 dark-energy density under the stated assumptions and single-scale power counting—is supported by the explicit dimensional mismatch, the Fierz closure of the four-fermion sector, the regulated gap-equation sign, the one-loop and RG estimates for R2/R3, and the perturbation-decoupling theorem.
