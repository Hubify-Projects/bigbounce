VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. IV (Scope paragraph), and Sec. IX: The assertion that the four enumerated routes are “basis-complete at the level of MPl-power-counting classes” within minimal ECH (with the Jackiw–Pi CS term and parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner now “closed explicitly”) rests on structural facts F1/F2 plus NDA monotonicity and the Fierz-by-Fierz lemma (App. C). However, no exhaustive enumeration or symbolic proof is provided that every admissible local dimension-4/6 parity-odd diffeomorphism-invariant operator built from the minimal ECH field content is captured by the six O[4]n operators or reduced to the three classes (topological total derivatives, Fierz-reducible four-fermion contact, or Bianchi-vanishing single-curvature). The Scope paragraph itself concedes this is not a complete operator-basis theorem; the completeness claim therefore exceeds what is rigorously demonstrated.

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. IV F, and Sec. IX (Barrier catalog): Route 4 is declared “closed” by a “naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection, relocating rather than solving the cosmological-constant problem.” This is interpretive commentary on tuning (mθ ∼ H0 or equivalent single-scale requirement), not a deductive amplitude or operator-level exclusion comparable to the Planck suppression + gap-equation closure of R1 or the loop/Immirzi-running suppression of R2–R3. A mechanism that can still be realized (with the very tuning the CC problem demands) is not closed in the same sense; the wording conflates “does not solve the CC problem” with “route is ruled out at channel level.”

[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result) and Sec. XIV D (structural tension with matter-bounce fNL): The decoupling proof is restricted to canonical scalar matter; the explicit caveats (fermion spin density, propagating torsion/Poincaré gauge theory, dynamical Immirzi, non-minimal couplings, boundary/topological sectors) are stated but the downstream claim that the ECH dark-energy mechanism and an observable SPHEREx-scale matter-bounce fNL = −35/16 signature are “mutually exclusive” assumes the dark-energy route must operate in a regime where those caveats do not apply. No demonstration is given that the transparency survives when the bounce-era matter content includes the fermions or axial currents required by the R1/R2 channels themselves.

[MINOR] Sec. IV D–E (R2 and R3 closures): Both routes are closed using phenomenological one-loop ansätze or upper-bound EFT estimates (“motivated by but not literally derived in” Mercuri/Shapiro–Teixeira or Benedetti–Speziale) rather than coefficients extracted from those references. While the ∼60-order suppression margins render the precise prefactors irrelevant to the qualitative conclusion, a PRD no-go paper should either recompute the relevant β-functions or quote the exact numerical coefficients used, together with a controlled error estimate on the residual O(1)–O(1010) normalization freedom.

[MINOR] Sec. II C 1, App. B, and App. D: The Ntot ≈ 92 dilution bookkeeping, the (Treh/MGUT)3/2 thermal phase-space factor, and the conditional reheating thermal-reset argument (Γwash > H) are order-of-magnitude matching exercises whose quantitative outputs feed the amplitude budgets and the “structural tension” claim. These are labeled approximations in the text but are not varied or bounded; the sensitivity of the 14-constraint catalog and the R1–R3 closures to plausible variations in these inputs should be shown.

[MINOR] Sec. III B, Table I, and references to coordinated-submission siblings (Papers II/IV/V): The galaxy-spin “confirmed null,” SPHEREx fNL forecast significances, and several numerical anchors in Tables I–II are drawn from external companion analyses whose pipelines and catalogs are archived but whose results are not reproduced here. While the paper correctly states these numbers are non-load-bearing for the theoretical closures, their presence in the observational-signatures and executive-summary sections impairs standalone readability and invites referee requests for self-contained data or supplemental material.

One sentence: The central claim that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are amplitude-constrained (R1–R3) or naturalness-constrained (R4) under the stated assumptions, with the Holst sector shown to decouple from canonical scalar/tensor perturbations, is supported at the level of power-counting, explicit operator reductions, the Fierz lemma, and the regulated gap equation, but the strength of the “channel-level closure” conclusion is limited by the interpretive status of the R4 naturalness argument, the ansatz-level treatment of R2/R3 coefficients, and the incomplete demonstration that the enumerated routes exhaust the minimal-ECH parity-odd operator basis at the relevant mass dimensions.
