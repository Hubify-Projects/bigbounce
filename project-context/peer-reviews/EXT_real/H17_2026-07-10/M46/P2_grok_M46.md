ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. I: The phrasing “the result corrects the unreproduced printed −35/8 literature value” overstates the body text (Appendix B explicitly states the work “do[es] not claim a complete reconstruction of how the published −35/8 arose” and only identifies the spurious −(99/128)∑k_i³ term in the transcribed polynomial).
[MINOR] Sec. IV and Table III: The channel-native surrogate significances (3.47σ, 3.14σ, 2.32σ, 0.42σ) and the 2.63σ arithmetic map are computed and displayed with a leading-order Gaussian covariance that is not the published Heinrich et al. per-triangle covariance; the repeated disclaimers that these are “illustrative conditional diagnostics, not an observational headline” do not fully prevent a reader from misinterpreting them as a forecast.
[MINOR] Sec. III.C: The galaxy-bispectrum paragraph consists of a single sentence that adds no new content or calculation; it should be expanded with at least a brief statement of the relevant kernels or removed.
[MINOR] Data/Code Availability and Appendix B: The statement that “an immutable versioned archive and DOI remain a submission-time camera-ready packaging step and are not claimed here as complete” is ambiguous; the public repository scripts are referenced but their exact version/commit hash at submission is not stated, weakening reproducibility claims.
[MINOR] Sec. II.A and Eq. (3): The ordered-symmetric basis re-expansion and the treatment of the (5,2,2) orbit multiplicity are correct but presented without an explicit footnote or short derivation showing how the six-permutation counting produces the coefficient −33; a one-line clarification would aid readers who reconstruct the polynomial.

The central claim that the exact four-vertex sum for the matter-contraction bispectrum yields f_NL^local = −35/16 with the unique ordered-basis coefficients (3,1,−9,5,−33,9) is supported by the explicit per-vertex expressions, the symbolic summation to −35/16 (squeezed) and −255/128 (equilateral), the order-grouped intermediate check, and the independent Li et al. general-c_s formula at c_s=1.
