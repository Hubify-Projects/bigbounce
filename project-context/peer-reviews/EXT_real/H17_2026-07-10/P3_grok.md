VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §I (process-volume framing): The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is internally inconsistent with the paper’s own repeated emphasis that this is a process-volume count of candidates surviving per-survey gates (not confirmed physical detections) and that ~98.7% of DESI anomalies lie on sky/filler fibers rather than science targets; the title and primary deliverable language therefore overstate the scientific yield relative to the evidence.
[MAJOR] §III E (eROSITA) and §III G (Gaia): The eROSITA production score axis is irreproducible (undocumented post-hoc rescaling; fails all 16 monotone rescalings and IsolationForest retrains; non-monotone in committed raw artifact), leading to excision of the tier from every count; combined with the synthetic-placeholder Gaia tier that was also removed, this demonstrates material provenance and implementation failures in at least two of the six surveyed components, undermining in the overall multi-survey pipeline robustness.
[MAJOR] §II D and §VI D (validation protocol): The “validated” label rests on a heterogeneous set of gates (DESI/SDSS/Planck pass detector-sensitivity injection-recovery for the broad/continuum class only; NEOWISE passes only a geometry-QA mask test by construction; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ; LAMOST fails at 5.8%); no uniform, survey-independent sensitivity characterization or end-to-end held-out re-inference of the full released catalog is provided, so the three-gate claim for DESI and the overall catalog-grade designation are not demonstrated at the stated rigor.
[MINOR] §II B and §III (score definitions and thresholds): The canonical anomaly score S is defined per-survey on each survey’s own validation statistics, yet the paper mixes absolute S > 5 cuts (DESI), fixed-size continuity slices (SDSS 77,905), top-1% slices (LAMOST, Planck, NEOWISE), and a non-reproducible score-knee for eROSITA; this heterogeneity, plus the explicit disclosure that full per-object re-inference remains blocked for some tiers, prevents straightforward independent reproduction or cross-survey comparison of the released catalog without external artifacts.
[MINOR] §V (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL measurement yields α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65 (0.29σ from null) with no improvement over the single-tracer baseline and a noise-driven forecast shift; the NANOGrav γ result is only decisive against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference. These null demonstrations are correctly scoped as secondary, but the section adds substantial length while contributing no new constraints, weakening the paper’s focus on its primary catalog deliverable.
[MINOR] Throughout (reproducibility presentation): While the commitment of scripts and JSON artifacts is commendable, the manuscript relies on external repo paths, pod logs, and per-survey training artifacts for core claims (e.g., injection-recovery JSONs, reproduce_headline_dedup.py output, held-out rescore results); key numerical results and selection criteria should be derivable from the text or supplementary material alone to meet PRD standards for a self-contained methods section.

The central claim of a rigorously validated 268,519-object multi-survey anomaly catalog is only partially supported by the presented per-survey gates, cross-validation statistics, and injection-recovery tests, which are qualified by heterogeneous thresholds, excised tiers, and a predominant non-science-target composition.
