(1) VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. I (Introduction), Sec. IV (entire Four-Route No-Go), and Sec. IX (Structural Constraints): The manuscript is excessively long (~62 pages) with highly repetitive, defensive language that repeatedly qualifies scope, assumptions, and “what is not claimed.” This obscures the core arguments and violates PRD standards for concise exposition; the text must be substantially streamlined (e.g., move repeated scope paragraphs, completeness lemmas, and non-load-bearing MCMC details to appendices or supplementary material).
[MAJOR] Sec. IV A–D (Routes 1–3 closures) and App. B, D: The amplitude-suppression arguments for R1–R3 rest on order-of-magnitude estimates, phenomenological ansätze (D_inf dilution with (T_reh/M_GUT)^{3/2} factor), and one-loop coefficients (β(γ), chiral running) that are not derived from the minimal ECH action itself. These constitute assumption-conditional suppression statements rather than rigorous no-go theorems; the regulated NJL gap-equation exclusion (repulsive scalar channel) in App. D must be reproduced explicitly in the main text with full algebra and critical-coupling comparison.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result): This is potentially the strongest novel claim, yet the proof (scalar-sector decoupling, tensor extension, explicit perturbed-tetrad expansion, term-by-term verification) is only sketched. The full equations of motion demonstrating that all Holst-sector contributions vanish identically at linear and higher order for canonical scalar matter must be supplied in detail for independent verification.
[MINOR] Sec. II C, XII A, and XIV D: The single-scale NDA mapping to ρ_Λ and the derived structural tension between the N_tot ≈ 92 dark-energy route and the matter-bounce f_NL signature are presented as bookkeeping that merely relocates the cosmological-constant problem. While honest, this framing weakens the physical motivation; the incompatibility should be stated quantitatively as a conditional result under the specific dark-energy ansatz rather than as a general discriminator among bouncing cosmologies.
[MINOR] Abstract, Sec. I, and Sec. IV (completeness claim): The assertion that the four routes plus the two explicitly closed operators (Jackiw–Pi term and parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner) render the enumeration “basis-complete at the M_Pl-power-counting level” is central to novelty. The Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (App. C) and the symmetry-counting argument (F1–F2) must be summarized quantitatively in the introduction or early in Sec. IV so that exhaustiveness is transparent without requiring the appendix.
[MINOR] Sec. III, App. F–G, and references to companion Papers II/IV: Observational elements (galaxy-spin null, NaMaster EB validation, spectator-ALP MCMC) and numerical values drawn from external siblings or archived artifacts are included while the paper simultaneously claims theoretical self-containment. These should be removed or reduced to brief citations; the present manuscript should stand alone as a theory paper.

(3) The central claim of channel-level amplitude closure for the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes (plus the perturbation-transparency result) under the authors’ explicitly stated assumptions is supported by the dimensional power-counting, Fierz projection, and amplitude budgets in Sec. IV and IX, though the result remains conditional and is not presented as a general operator-level theorem.
