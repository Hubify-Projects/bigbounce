VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract and §I: The headline claim of a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” from a 37.3-million-source scan is presented without sufficient qualification; the text itself defines this number as a process-volume figure of candidates surviving per-survey gates (not confirmed physical detections), with ~98.7% of DESI anomalies on non-science-target (sky/filler) spectra and the science-target yield (2,468 clusters) ≈0.92× the prior single-survey benchmark, providing no like-for-like increase in catalog size or discovery power.

[MAJOR] §III E: The eROSITA production score axis (0.259 threshold) is irreproducible from any committed artifact (fails on all 16 tested monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains; production values are non-monotone in the raw score, Spearman ρ = −0.10); excising the tier for this reason while retaining the rest of the pipeline does not resolve the underlying provenance failure in the core anomaly-scoring workflow.

[MAJOR] §II D and §III (Path-C protocol and survey-by-survey results): Validation is explicitly non-uniform—“validated” encompasses NEOWISE passing only a masking-geometry QA gate (by construction, not a detector-sensitivity test) while DESI, SDSS, and Planck pass injection-recovery only for the broad/continuum-dominated class (narrow single-pixel lines recover at ≥15σ); LAMOST fails the gate (5.8% recovery) and is labeled exploratory, so the uniform “validated catalog-grade” label applied to the deduplicated 268,519 count is not supported by the evidence presented.

[MAJOR] §IV A and cross-match discussion: The extended CDS X-Match of the DESI top-1,000 yields only 178/1,000 ≈17.8% genuine novelty (Wilson 68% CI ±1.2%); this modest fraction, together with the documented dominance of known populations or systematics in other tiers (ultra-cool dwarfs in SDSS cross-transfer, 98% blue-excess training artifact in LAMOST), does not substantiate the claim of systematic discovery of rare or novel astrophysical objects across multiple independent surveys.

[MINOR] §V: The multi-tracer fNL result (α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65, de-biased forecast σ(fNL) = 8.14 with envelope [3.92, 8.98]) shows no improvement over the single-tracer baseline and lies within the 1σ envelope; the NANOGrav γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 is decisive only against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference (Bayes factor 7.14×10^3) and is correctly not claimed as a detection—yet the introduction motivates the work with testable fNL constraints at 2.6–5σ, creating a mismatch between motivation and delivered result.

[MINOR] §II B: Feature scalers for the tabular surveys are fit on the full sample rather than the training split (with only a post-hoc bounded robustness check performed for eROSITA); this introduces potential tail information leakage into the normalization constants used for MSE scoring, even if within-survey ranking is shown to be robust at the Jaccard/Spearman level reported.

The central claim that the BigAE + Path-C framework has produced a reproducible, gate-validated multi-survey anomaly-candidate catalog of 268,519 sources (with detector-sensitivity support for the dominant DESI/SDSS/Planck components) is supported by the committed pipeline execution and per-survey injection-recovery artifacts, but the astrophysical novelty, uniformity of validation, and scientific advance relative to prior single-survey work are not supported at the level asserted in the title, abstract, and framing.
