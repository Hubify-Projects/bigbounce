VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. IV (entire four-route analysis), and Appendix B: The "amplitude closure" for R1–R3 (Planck suppression via torsion elimination for R1; scaling ansätze for R2–R3) and "naturalness/explanatory-deficit closure" for R4 rest on single-scale NDA power counting that explicitly acknowledges its own evasion (new light scale µ ≪ M_Pl or exact cancellation); this relocates rather than resolves the cosmological constant problem and does not constitute a structural no-go derived from ECH dynamics beyond standard dimensional analysis.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result, including subsections B–E): The claim that torsion vanishes at all classical orders and the Holst term decouples from scalar/tensor EOM via the algebraic Bianchi identity R_µ[νρσ]=0 is asserted for canonical scalar matter, but the main-text treatment provides only a high-level sketch; the "Explicit Perturbed-Tetrad Expansion (Term-by-Term)" and back-reaction analysis are not shown in sufficient detail to verify that higher-order metric/scalar perturbations remain torsion-free without sourcing from the excluded sectors (fermions, propagating torsion, non-minimal couplings).
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (Scope paragraph and completeness discussion) together with Appendix C: Repeated disclaimers that the work is "channel-level, not an operator-level theorem" and "not proven to be a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis" sit in direct tension with the assertion that the four routes plus the now-explicitly-closed Jackiw–Pi and parity-odd four-fermion partner "exhaust" minimal-ECH channels at the M_Pl-power-counting level via the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma; this hedging undermines the strength of the claimed closure.
[MINOR] Sec. IX (14-barrier catalog, Foundations A–G, Branches H/J/L/M/N/O) and Sec. XIV D: The idiosyncratic barrier numbering, historical catalog entries, and subsumption (B8 by B14) add organizational overhead without independent physical content; several barriers reduce to restatements of the same NDA/Bianchi/Fierz arguments, and the N_tot ≈ 92 vs. f_NL erasure mapping relies on specific un-derived assumptions (N_exit ∼ 60, coherence window) without explicit bounce-inflation scale matching.
[MINOR] Overall manuscript (60 pp., writing throughout Secs. II–XV and appendices): Excessively dense run-on sentences, nested parentheticals, and heavy self-referential cross-citation (to "this paper’s appendices" and companion papers for all numerical anchors) impair readability and traceability; PRD standards favor concise, self-contained exposition even when reproducibility artifacts are archived.

The central claim that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are constrained under the stated assumptions is supported at the level of power counting plus standard identities (Bianchi vanishing, Fierz rearrangement, algebraic Cartan constraint), but only as a reframing of the cosmological constant problem inside minimal ECH rather than a derivation of new dynamical obstructions.
