VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract and Sec. I (Introduction): The phrasing that the block-bootstrap WLS template fit “disfavors a clean cosmological dipole at the 1.7% reference amplitude … at z ≈−7.6” is not supported by the statistical definition given in the body (Sec. IV C and VI B); the text explicitly states this z is a model-dependent template-disfavor statistic under an adopted NSIDE=8 block-bootstrap covariance and “not a calibrated frequentist exclusion significance.” The primary cosmological claim is the real-space HC dipole (+0.41σ, empirical-rank p=0.31 vs. isotropic pixel-permutation null), while the template fit is labeled a diagnostic cross-check whose selection function is not propagated into the covariance.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV D (Monopole+Mask Leakage Generative Null) and Appendix D (referenced throughout): The imaging+morphology forward model reproduces only ≈53% of the post-MASTER canonical-mask ℓ=1 residual amplitude (|a1| = 6.95×10−3), leaving an explicit ~47% remainder whose physical origin is stated as “unresolved” (survey-systematic attribution favored on independent grounds but not demonstrated). Although the paper bounds the cosmological content of this remainder below the real-space A50≈0.75% floor and notes it does not alter the primary null, the harmonic-channel systematic budget is therefore not closed; the eight-anchor battery (Table VII) is invoked to attribute the residual, but its full quantitative tests, nuisance marginalization, and cross-spectrum results reside in the unreproduced Appendix D.

[MAJOR] Sec. II B (Training Labels) and Sec. VI A (Pseudo-label independence): 66.5% of training labels are CE-ResNet pseudo-labels; the independent GZ1-human-only dipole test (N≈4.60×10^4 confident spirals, no learned model in the label chain) returns a consistent null (z=−0.54σ) but has ~4.5× coarser sensitivity (A50≈3.4%, A95≈4.5–6.8% by Fisher scaling) than the headline HC sample. This test corroborates the null at coarse amplitude but does not independently constrain the sub-percent regime; the moderate GZ1 chirality accuracy (69.91%, κ=0.40) is correctly noted to dilute signals (g=2a−1 folded into injection-recovery), yet the main catalog’s cosmological interpretation still rests on a partially pseudo-labeled pipeline whose inherited large-scale structure is bounded only indirectly via template-regression anchors.

[MINOR] Sec. IV C (Dipole Analysis) and Sec. III B (Declared Analysis Hierarchy): The p_eq>0.6 cut is documented as pre-specified in the generator script (commit hash given) and the confidence-cut sweep shows stability for p_eq∈{0.6,0.7,0.8}. However, the sharp transition from z≈4.0–4.3 (p_eq≤0.5) to z=+0.41 (p_eq>0.6) occurs precisely at the adopted threshold, and the unthresholded full-sample excess (z≈4.2–4.4) is attributed entirely to the discarded low-confidence tail; additional explicit justification is required that the cut choice was independent of the dipole outcome itself and that the HC subsample remains representative for isotropy tests.

[MINOR] Sec. III A, Tables I–II, and Sec. IV C: The paper correctly warns that σ/z values from distinct null procedures (isotropic pixel-permutation, per-galaxy label-shuffle, block-bootstrap, max-statistic MC, monopole-only generative, etc.) are not directly comparable as detection significances. Nevertheless, the proliferation of quoted values (+0.41σ primary, z≈−7.6 template, +3.64σ/+7.28σ harmonic, +3.05σ hemisphere, etc.) across primary and diagnostic rows creates unnecessary reader burden; Table I is the required decision tree but is not sufficiently foregrounded in the Results narrative to prevent misreading of the harmonic-channel σ as competing cosmological constraints.

The central claim that the real-space chirality dipole on the high-confidence (p_eq>0.6) equivariant subsample is consistent with null is supported by the primary estimator (+0.41σ, p=0.31 vs. pixel-permutation null), the injection-recovery calibration (A50≈0.75%, A95∈(1.0%,1.5%]), and the model-independent GZ1-human cross-check, subject to the dilution factor from moderate classifier accuracy.
