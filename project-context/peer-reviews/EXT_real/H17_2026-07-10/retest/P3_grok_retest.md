VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract (p. 1) and §I (Introduction, “Reader’s guide”): The headline deliverable is framed as a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” with process-scale multipliers (~141× full point-source tier, ~73× DESI-only S > 5), yet the text immediately qualifies these as non-like-for-like figures because ~98.7% of raw DESI anomaly clusters lie on sky/filler fibers; the actual science-target yield is only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark of 2,685). This creates a material mismatch between prominent claims and the evidence presented.

[MAJOR] §III E (eROSITA DR1) and §II D (Path-C protocol): The eROSITA production score axis (threshold 0.259, top-298) is irreproducible from any committed artifact (non-monotone in raw reconstruction score; fails reproduction on 16 monotone rescalings and 3 IsolationForest retrains; Spearman ρ = −0.10 on top-5). While the tier is excised from all counts and released only as a membership list, the existence of undocumented post-hoc rescaling in a core production run undermines the reproducibility assertions made for the entire native-retrain + validation pipeline.

[MAJOR] §III D (LAMOST DR10) and §VI D (i) (DESI validation scope): LAMOST native retrain recovers only 5.8% of 5σ continuum-dip-plus-emission-line injections (gate FAIL) and is dominated by a 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact; eROSITA recovers 1.2%. The DESI “validated” claim rests on a single production-ensemble injection-recovery gate (99–100% broad/extended class at 5σ) plus two correlated fold-stability checks computed on deliberately short-trained proxy models that themselves fail the paper’s val-loss ≤0.30 retain gate. Narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, a stated sensitivity floor never elevated to a certified completeness claim.

[MINOR] §III G (Gaia DR3) and §II B (feature preprocessing): The committed Gaia DR3 output table was synthetic placeholder data (source_id = [5×10¹⁸ + i], duplicates, G magnitudes outside physical range). While excised, its initial inclusion reveals inadequate data-ingestion validation in the committed scripts (gaia_expanded.py fallback path) and weakens the overall provenance narrative.

[MINOR] Abstract structure and §V (cosmological applications): The opening text is an extremely long, footnote-dense block containing numerical results, process-volume disclaimers, and gate-status caveats before the formal INTRODUCTION begins. The multi-tracer fNL (α_jk = 0.19 ± 0.65, no improvement on single-tracer baseline) and NANOGrav (γ = 2.567 ± 0.382, consistency only) sections return explicitly null results yet occupy main-text space as “secondary demonstrations.”

[MINOR] §II B and §III C (SDSS native re-score): The SDSS headline count of 77,905 is a fixed-size continuity slice at S ≥ 0.1060 sized to match the cross-transfer baseline; the native top-1% yields only 19,253 and strict S > 5 yields 12 (~6500× compression). The text acknowledges this but still tabulates the continuity slice as the primary SDSS entry without a parallel native-threshold column in Table II, complicating direct comparison to prior single-survey catalogs.

Central claim support: The claim that a detector-sensitivity-validated multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 sources has been produced is supported for the dominant DESI, SDSS, and Planck components by the committed injection-recovery tests (broad-class 99–100% at 5σ for DESI; 64% for SDSS; 100% for Planck) and the reproducible 5″ deduplication script, but is materially qualified by the excision of irreproducible tiers, outright FAIL gates on LAMOST and eROSITA, mixed-validation labeling, and the fact that the science-target DESI yield remains comparable in size to the largest existing single-survey catalog.
