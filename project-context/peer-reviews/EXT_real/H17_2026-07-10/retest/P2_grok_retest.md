ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract, Sec. I, and Appendix A: The resolution of the Cai–Li discrepancy is presented as definitive via vertex re-summation yielding exactly −35/16 (certified “vertex-by-vertex in Table VII” and matching Li et al. Eq. 5.1 at cs=1), with one identified −(99/128)∑k_i³ transcription error in Cai et al. Eq. 37; however, the main text does not tabulate or quote the individual numerical squeezed-limit contributions of the four vertices (field redefinition, ζζ̇², ζ̇∂ζ∂χ, ζ(∂i∂jχ)²) at ϵ=3/2 before summation, forcing readers to consult the full appendix and committed code for independent algebraic verification of the clean −35/16 result (vs. the printed −35/8 or the −305/64 from the transcribed polynomial alone).
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (SPHEREx forecast) and Sec. VII (systematic budget): The headline 1.3–2.75σ range (and the optimistic 2.6–2.75σ bispectrum-only) is obtained by rescaling the imported Heinrich et al. σ(f_local_NL)≈0.7 baseline by the noise-weighted template mismatch factor r=0.84±0.02 (plus ϵ-correction and additive-quadrature systematics); while the independent tree-level multi-tracer Fisher (c13_independent_bounce_fisher.py) validates reff≈0.99 and reproduces the local baseline to 2–11%, the full bounce-template covariance (including non-local tails and joint marginalization over bϕ, GR projections, photo-z) is never recomputed, so the lower edge remains a heuristic envelope rather than a statistically rigorous marginalized constraint.
[MINOR] Sec. II C (assumptions) and Sec. III B (template projection): Assumption (d) (faithful cubic-order transmission) is closed to δf_NL≲10^{-3} via the single-clock nonlinear superhorizon conservation argument plus (kη_bounce)² gradient estimate; however, no explicit numerical evaluation of the four Maldacena cubic integrals with bounce-modified mode functions is provided (only the scaling/gradient-expansion bound), leaving a model-dependent residual that is stated to be negligible but not bounded beyond the O(10^{-4}) heuristic for the specific Wilson-Ewing dressed-metric quantization.
[MINOR] Sec. II A and null-space analysis: The 3×6 underdetermined system for the degree-9 polynomial coefficients (c1…c6) produces a well-quantified r=0.85±0.13 scatter (rcos>0.97) across 10,000 samples, but the basis choice (symmetrized monomial orbits) and uniform Euclidean sampling measure in null-space coordinates are conventional rather than unique; the paper correctly treats this as a representation uncertainty and does not propagate the full tails into σ_eff, yet a brief statement on invariance under linear reparametrization of the monomials would strengthen the robustness claim.
[MINOR] Sec. VI (Bayesian comparison) and Table III: The closed-form Bayes-factor formula (Eq. 9) and three 10^5-realization Monte Carlo ensembles are a strength, but the headline BF≈9–14 (and the curvaton-natural BF≈4–7) mixes delta-prior and σ_theory=1.0 Gaussian bounce priors with broad vs. narrow competitor priors without a single consolidated table showing the exact prior convolution for every cell; the r→1 bookkeeping endpoint is reported separately, but its mapping to the noise-weighted headline r=0.84 is not tabulated per prior combination.

One sentence on the central claim: The central claim—that the vertex-rederived f_local_NL=−35/16 (halving the erroneous −35/8 literature value) sets a realistic SPHEREx sensitivity target of ∼1.3–2.75σ after template mismatch (r≈0.84) and heuristic systematic budget, with discriminatory power against single-field slow-roll—is supported by the explicit match to Li et al.’s general-cs result, the stable null-space shape-cosine and injection-recovery tests, the independent Fisher validation (reff≈0.99), and the transparent (if approximate) propagation of the imported Heinrich et al. baseline.
