VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored path as the designated primary estimand (§V B, Table IV, abstract) without any timestamped pre-analysis plan or pre-registration, despite explicit disclosure of the ~dozen-trial analysis tree and the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five estimators; this weakens the organizational framing of a single “primary” result even though the uniform null (|z_Δ| ≤ 1.25) holds across the family.
[MINOR] The de-attenuated physical-chirality bound of ≈2.26 pp (abstract, §I, Appendix A) is obtained from the ≈0.9 pp classifier-label envelope via the symmetric-error factor 0.9 / 0.3982 using the GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %, κ = 0.40); the text acknowledges additional unquantified uncertainty from environment-dependent relabeling (no stratified confusion matrix) but does not propagate or bound it, so the model-constraining quantity quoted for theorists is overstated in precision.
[MINOR] The consolidated ≈0.9 pp systematic envelope (abstract, Table XI, §VIII) is formed by quadrature of counting-statistics CI, fixed-void-geometry membership (±0.34–0.37 pp), any-hole-vs-maximal-sphere geometry (0.60 pp co-dominant term), confidence, footprint, and match-radius contributions; no explicit justification or covariance assessment for the quadrature assumption is provided, nor is a Monte-Carlo realization of the joint systematic shown.
[MINOR] Full referee evaluation of the classifier provenance, parity-equivariance validation, and monopole origin (Appendix A, Table I) is deferred to the concurrently submitted companion Paper IV (arXiv placeholder); while the headline Δf_CW contrast is algebraically invariant to any global monopole and uses only public class_eq labels, the submission is not fully standalone.
[MINOR] All results (T-Web, DESIVAST, robustness checks) are performed in fixed redshift space with inherited RSD effects (§XIII, abstract); the text correctly flags this but provides only a limited finger-of-god Monte-Carlo test (§VIII) and does not quantify how RSD distorts the void/non-void membership or contrast relative to real-space model predictions that theorists would use.

The central claim of no statistically significant void/non-void dependence in the classifier-labelled CW fraction (family-wise Bonferroni-5 null with |Δf_CW| ≲ 0.004 across the five DESIVAST definitions, consistent with parity after monopole subtraction) is supported by the reported two-sample z_Δ statistics, label-shuffle permutation tests, Phase-2 hyperparameter sweeps, and cross-checks on the public labels.
