VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section V.B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths) and abstract: The DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast is explicitly designated post-hoc as the "designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered) estimand" with no timestamped analysis plan; while the paper is admirably transparent, declares the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null as the strictly quotable headline, and commits to pre-registration for DR2, this weakens the rhetorical framing of a single "primary" path and should be stated more prominently (e.g., in the abstract) to preclude any appearance of selective emphasis even for a clean null.
[MINOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment for DESIVAST) and abstract: The fixed-geometry Monte Carlo and first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction bound the coherent void-outflow shift to |Δf_CW| ≲ 0.03 pp (∼40× below the envelope), but the paper correctly notes that small-scale finger-of-god effects on individual memberships are only bounded (not removed) by the fixed-geometry test; this limitation should be stated more explicitly as applying to the primary result and flagged for DR2 work with group catalogs.
[MINOR] Abstract, Section I, and Section II: The statement that "No published bounce or inflation model currently predicts an environment-conditional chirality signature at this scale" is presented without citations or a brief literature-search qualifier; adding "to the best of our knowledge after reviewing the bounce/inflation-chirality literature" or a short reference list would strengthen the claim that the null supplies a new empirical constraint.
[MINOR] Section VI.D (Tracer-program stratification and bright/dark sign-flip) and Section VIII: The ∼2.1σ filament bright-vs-dark sign-flip is correctly interpreted as a survey-selection diagnostic (imaging-leg systematics propagating through the BGS bright/dark split) and shown to leak negligibly (∼0.001 pp) into the DESIVAST primary contrast; however, the quantitative mapping from Paper IV per-leg biases to the observed amplitude via the program selection function is only sketched—explicit propagation or a short mock would make the dismissal fully rigorous rather than plausible.
[MINOR] Section VIII.F and Table XVI (monopole reconciliation): The internal P5 matched-sample monopole f_CW^P5 = 0.49719 (∼8% larger offset than Paper IV) is stated to be reconciled in §VIII F, but the text provided does not show the explicit numerical reconciliation or covariance with the classifier-monopole uncertainty propagation; confirming this in the final manuscript (or moving the short derivation to an appendix) removes any residual ambiguity in the σ_pred diagnostics.

The central claim—that spiral-galaxy chirality exhibits no detectable large-scale-environment dependence (void vs. non-void Δf_CW consistent with zero at |z_Δ| ≤ 1.25 across five DESIVAST definitions, with an honest ∼0.9 pp observed / ∼2.3 pp de-attenuated systematic envelope)—is supported by the data, multi-algorithm null tests, permutation framework, and systematic controls.
