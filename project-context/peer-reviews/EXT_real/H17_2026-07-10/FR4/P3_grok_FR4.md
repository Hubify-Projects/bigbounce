VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §I, and Table II: The headline deliverable is repeatedly framed as a “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” (268,319 point-source), yet the text itself defines this as a process-volume figure (anomaly candidates surviving per-survey gates across the full instrument stream, “not a count of confirmed physical detections”). The like-for-like science-target benchmark is only 2,468 DESI clusters (≈0.92× the prior single-survey maximum of 2,685), with ~98.7% of raw DESI anomalies on sky/filler fibers; the large multipliers (~141×, ~73×) are explicitly not like-for-like catalog-size gains. This framing is misleading.

[MAJOR] §III E and §II D (Path-C step 5): The eROSITA tier (298 objects) is excised from every count (validated 268,519 and inclusive 377,482) because its production score axis is “irreproducible as a matter of provenance” (non-monotone in committed raw scores; 0.259 fails to reproduce on 16 monotone rescalings or 3 IsolationForest retrains). A full survey component failing basic reproducibility undermines the claimed “Path-C native-retrain” protocol and the overall validation narrative.

[MAJOR] §VI D (i), §III A, and injection-recovery results: DESI validation (the dominant ~73% of the validated count) certifies only the “broad/extended anomaly class” at 99–100% recovery for 5σ per-spectrum strength; narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ (explicit sensitivity floor of the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer). The 5-fold Jaccard (¯J=0.862) and OOD checks are “correlated stability probes, not independent confirmations” computed on deliberately short-trained proxy models that fail the paper’s own val-loss ≤0.30 gate. The “validated” label therefore does not support full line-completeness claims.

[MAJOR] §II D, §III, and Table II: Multiple surveys fail the stated detector-sensitivity gate at 5σ (LAMOST 5.8%, eROSITA 1.2%; cross-transfer CMB FAIL). The final validated subset rests on only four components with mixed gates (detector-sensitivity PASS for DESI/SDSS/Planck; geometry-QA only for NEOWISE). LAMOST is retained solely as a “methodological lesson” (98% blue-excess training-bias artifact). The multi-survey “validated” framing is therefore not uniform.

[MINOR] §II B, Table II footnotes, and reproducibility claims throughout: The manuscript repeatedly cites “committed standalone scripts,” pod-side artifacts, and exact JSON outputs (e.g., reproduce_headline_dedup.py, desi_injrec_CORRECTED.json, r24conf_erosita_axis_sweep.json) that are not provided in the manuscript or supplementary material. Readers cannot recompute or verify the headline 268,519 number or per-survey gates from the text alone; the paper is not self-contained.

[MINOR] §V: The two cosmological applications (multi-tracer fNL and NANOGrav γ) are explicitly “secondary demonstrations, not headline results” and return null outcomes (fNL central shift lies inside the 1σ envelope of the single-tracer baseline; NANOGrav result is decisive only against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference). These sections add substantial length with no new cosmological constraint.

One sentence: The central claim that a rigorously validated multi-survey anomaly catalog of 268,519 objects has been produced from a full-instrument-stream scan is only weakly supported, because validation gates are survey-specific and incomplete, key tiers are excised for irreproducibility or FAIL status, and the like-for-like science-target advance is marginal relative to prior single-survey work.
