(2) ISSUES: numbered, each prefixed [MAJOR] or [MINOR], naming the specific section/claim and concrete problem. (3) One sentence: is the central claim supported?Thought for 34sVERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS
ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. I (Introduction) and Sec. VII (systematics budget): The headline sensitivity range and conservative 1.3σ floor rely on a transferred proxy correlation ρ ≈ −0.868 from the power-spectrum SDB channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance Cov_B from Heinrich et al. is not public; while the paper discloses this, computes a channel-native surrogate Fisher (σ_marg(f_NL) ≈ 0.94), and retains the proxy as conservative, the procedure remains heuristic and cross-channel, weakening the rigor of the lower edge of the quoted sensitivity envelope.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C (Assumptions, point (d)): The claim of faithful cubic-order bispectrum transmission through the bounce (δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3}) is closed via single-clock nonlinear ζ-conservation plus a gradient-expansion scaling argument O((k η_bounce)^2) ∼ 10^{-4}; this is plausible and the paper correctly identifies it as the weakest link, but it is not a full third-order computation with bounce-modified mode functions, leaving a model-dependent caveat that should be more prominently quantified in the abstract and conclusions.
[MINOR] Sec. II A and II B (null-space analysis and UV-independence): The 3-dimensional null space in the symmetrized monomial coefficients (c1…c6) produces r = 0.85 ± 0.13 (range 0.55–1.14) under uniform sampling; while the headline uses the narrower noise-weighted r = 0.84 ± 0.02 and shows r_cos > 0.95 stability, the basis dependence of the scatter and the conditional nature of “UV-completion independence” (explicitly limited to assumptions (a)–(f) and dressed-metric quantization) need a single consolidated paragraph early in Sec. II to avoid reader confusion.
[MINOR] Sec. VI (Bayesian comparison) and abstract: The BF ≈ 9–14 range is correctly labeled “illustrative” and prior-sensitivity is explored, but the four-corner grid and continuous marginalization over σ_theory should be summarized in a compact table in the main text (rather than only in the long derivation) so that the dependence on competitor prior width [−5,+5] vs. [−15,+15] and on bounce prior width is immediately visible to readers.
The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce prediction f_NL = −35/16 (verified by vertex re-summation, Li et al. matching, and benchmark configurations) yields a realistic SPHEREx sensitivity of ∼2.6–2.75σ before systematics that remains qualitatively discriminatory after template mismatch and a transparent (if heuristic) systematic budget—is supported within the stated assumptions and caveats.ExpertAttach to messageDrop here to add files to your message
