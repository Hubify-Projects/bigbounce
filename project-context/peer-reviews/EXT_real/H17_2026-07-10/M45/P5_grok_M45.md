ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §V B: The focal hierarchy (released GALZONE TARGET + OUT=0 + exact VoidFinder hole-union membership as the primary estimand) was selected after venue review and data inspection; although explicitly disclosed as post-hoc/exploratory/not preregistered, this post-selection of the single most null path among the documented analysis tree (Table IV) undermines the evidential priority given to the headline Δf_CW = +0.00125636 result.
[MINOR] §VI A and §VIII: The logistic model (Eq. 4) with B-splines and NSIDE=4 cluster sandwich reports SE = 0.00341274 and p = 0.71277, but sensitivity to alternative specifications (different spline degrees, inclusion of void-edge × redshift interactions, or bootstrap/jackknife covariance at the maximal-void scale) is shown only for one clustering variant; a fuller specification-robustness table is needed.
[MINOR] §VII and Table XI: The Phase-2 T-Web sweep (9 cells) applies per-cell label-shuffle p_LEE and Bonferroni-9, yet the overall multiplicity across the full analysis tree (focal + 5 DESIVAST variants + 9 T-Web cells + redshift/density/HEALPix scans) lacks a global family-wise or false-discovery-rate bound; the claim of “no environmental dependence” therefore rests on per-family rather than experiment-wide control.
[MINOR] §VI B and §IX C: The secondary T-Web void bin (n = 428, f_CW = 0.4836) is correctly flagged as survey-shell contaminated and non-load-bearing, but its retention in the main text and figures still invites misinterpretation; it should be moved to an appendix or removed given its negligible overlap with the focal DESIVAST sample.

The central claim of a catalog-native, classifier-label non-detection (Δf_CW consistent with zero after covariate standardization) is supported by the focal estimate, its interval, and the consistent nulls across sensitivities.
