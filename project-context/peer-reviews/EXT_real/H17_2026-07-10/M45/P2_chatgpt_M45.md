(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Appendix B, “Vertex-by-vertex certification”: the claimed correction of the literature value is based on re-summing already-signed vertex expressions transcribed from Cai et al., rather than deriving Table IV directly from the cubic action and the in-in integral. Because an overall sign, symmetry factor, boundary term, field redefinition, or Hamiltonian-versus-Lagrangian conversion could reside upstream of those imported expressions, the manuscript must provide a self-contained derivation of each Table IV contribution—including normalization and Wick multiplicities—before the result can be regarded as an exact four-vertex derivation.

[MAJOR] Appendix B, Eqs. (B2)–(B5): the manuscript identifies a polynomial discrepancy and demonstrates that the printed polynomial yields −305/64, but it does not establish why both Cai et al.’s separately stated −35/8 and the shared Cai–Li printed polynomial are wrong while Li et al.’s later closed-form expression is correct. The authors should explicitly reconstruct the normalization conventions and intermediate algebra in both references, or narrow the claim to “the quoted source-level expressions sum to −35/16” rather than asserting that the historical amplitude has been definitively corrected.

[MINOR] Sections III–IV, Eqs. (8)–(9): the adopted r=0.84±0.02 is not computed with the actual SPHEREx covariance, yet it is used to quote a specific 2.63σ mapping. The manuscript should either remove the artificial uncertainty envelope and report only the explicitly defined flat-grid diagnostic, or provide the weighting functions and numerical results for every estimator included in the stated interval.

[MINOR] Sections IV and VII: the surrogate Fisher calculations are insufficiently reproducible from the manuscript because the tracer populations, redshift bins, k-cuts, number densities, bias prescriptions, covariance equations, parameter derivatives, and nuisance priors are not tabulated. These details should be supplied in an appendix even though the observational mapping is presented as secondary.

(3) The central claim is algebraically plausible and internally consistent, but it is not yet supported to Physical Review D publication standard without a self-contained derivation of the imported vertex contributions and their normalization.
