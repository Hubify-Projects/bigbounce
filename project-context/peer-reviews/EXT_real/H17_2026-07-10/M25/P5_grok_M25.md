VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section V.B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths) and abstract: The DESIVAST-anchored path is explicitly designated post-hoc/exploratory (no timestamped pre-registration) even though principled a-priori motivations are given (largest powered public catalog, three algorithms + zone definitions, volume-limited BGS minimizing target-program mixing); while the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five estimators is uniform and robust, this designation slightly weakens the strength with which a “new empirical constraint” at ≳25 Mpc/h can be claimed without additional language committing the family-wise result to be independent of the post-hoc framing.
[MINOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment and systematic envelope) and Table XI: The fixed-geometry MC and first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction convincingly bound the dominant coherent outflow term (shift 0.024 pp) and any-hole vs. maximal-sphere geometry term (0.60 pp co-dominant), but the manuscript does not explicitly quantify or bound the residual nonlinear finger-of-god / small-scale stochastic membership flips on the published redshift-space void centers/radii themselves; this leaves the “honest effective 2σ ≈0.9 pp envelope” slightly under-documented as a complete systematic budget.
[MINOR] Section II, VIII.F and abstract (monopole handling): The headline Δf_CW contrast is algebraically invariant to the catalog-wide monopole, and the internal P5 matched-sample value f^P5_CW = 0.49719 cross-checks Paper IV to high precision; however, the propagation of the finite counting uncertainty on the Paper IV monopole (and its ~0.36–0.7σ effect on σ_pred for the large bins) into the final systematic envelope and de-attenuated physical bound is not shown in a single consolidated table or propagated explicitly for the Bonferroni-5 family, making the “model-builders should use ≈2.26 pp” statement harder to audit independently.
[MINOR] Abstract and Section I (de-attenuation): The conversion from labelled-fraction bound (≈0.9 pp) to physical-chirality bound (≈2.26 pp) via attenuation factor 2a−1 ≃ 0.3982 at the 69.91 % (κ = 0.40) GZ1 accuracy floor is correct in principle but appears without a one-sentence definition of “a” or a brief methods pointer in the abstract; this reduces immediate accessibility for readers outside the GZ1/classifier sub-field.

The central claim—that spiral chirality exhibits no significant void vs. non-void environmental dependence in DESI DR1 at the scales probed, with a uniform family-wise null |Δf_CW| ≲ 0.004 and an effective ~0.9 pp systematic envelope on the labelled fraction—is supported by the data, the multi-algorithm DESIVAST tests, permutation nulls, and the documented robustness checks.
