VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract & Sec. I (and Table V / Fig. 2): The quoted sensitivity envelopes (2.6–2.75σ optimistic template-corrected; 1.3–2.75σ post-systematics; 1.3–1.4σ all-combined floor) mix qualitatively different procedures (CMB-Fisher signal-only weighting vs. LSS noise weighting, proxy vs. channel-native GR marginalization, b_ϕ prior widening) without a single consolidated table that explicitly defines each endpoint’s exact assumptions, weighting scheme, and correlation proxy; this obscures direct comparability of the numbers.
[MINOR] Sec. II A & null-space analysis: The 3×6 underdetermined monomial system, SVD, 10 000-sample scan at radius 50, and r = 0.85 ± 0.13 distribution are presented at length; while the headline r = 0.84 ± 0.02 is noise-weighted and robust, the section could be condensed by moving the full radius-convergence tables, per-sample 16th–84th percentile propagation, and basis-measure stress-band details to an appendix or supplementary material without loss of the key conclusion that null-space scatter does not materially affect the forecast.
[MINOR] Sec. IV (independent Fisher validation) & Sec. VII (GR / b_ϕ budget): The in-house tree-level multi-tracer Fisher reproduces Heinrich et al.’s local baseline to 2–11 % and yields reff ≈ 0.99, yet the headline numbers continue to use the published σ(f_local_NL) ≈ 0.7; the text should state explicitly (in one paragraph or footnote) whether substituting the independent σ values changes any quoted significance by more than the existing ±0.02 r uncertainty.
[MINOR] Sec. II C (assumption (d)) & Appendix A (implied): The cubic-order transmission closure via single-clock nonlinear ζ-conservation and the explicit vertex re-summation that corrects Cai et al.’s transcribed polynomial are central; both are asserted with four-way cross-checks, but the main text would benefit from a one-paragraph summary of the leading O((k η_bounce)^2) gradient term and a pointer to the exact side-by-side polynomial comparison (transcribed vs. vertex-summed) so readers can verify the −(99/128) ∑ k_i^3 term without immediately turning to the appendix.
[MINOR] Overall length & focus (37 pp.): The manuscript is thorough and self-auditing, but the combination of a source-level literature correction, detailed null-space Monte Carlo, independent Fisher cross-check, full systematic itemization, and Bayesian illustration makes it longer than necessary for a sensitivity recast; selective condensation of Secs. II A, IV, and VI (while preserving all numerical results and code-release statements) would improve readability for PRD.

The central claim—that the matter-bounce class predicts f_NL = −35/16 (corrected) and that SPHEREx/MegaMapper data can furnish a marginal but qualitatively discriminatory test against single-field slow-roll inflation—is supported within the explicitly stated assumptions, template-overlap quantification, and systematic budget.
