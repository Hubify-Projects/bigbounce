VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Section V B and abstract: The DESIVAST path is explicitly designated "primary" post-hoc with no timestamped pre-registration or analysis plan; while the garden-of-forking-paths exposure is bounded via Bonferroni-5, full disclosure of the ~dozen-trial tree, and the uniform null across all five estimators, the language ("designated primary for reporting") risks implying a privileged status chosen after seeing the data. This should be rephrased to "exploratory primary" or "DESIVAST-anchored headline" throughout, with the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null (not any single row) elevated as the sole strictly quotable result.
[MINOR] Abstract, §VIII, and Appendix A: The de-attenuated physical-chirality bound of ≈2.26 pp (from the 69.91% GZ1 accuracy floor and symmetric-error approximation) is presented as the quantity "model-builders should use," but rests on an under-powered environment-dependent relabeling check (n=6 overlap galaxies, void arm ±3.7 pp) and the assumption that label errors are symmetric and environment-independent. The observed void/non-void error-asymmetry difference (−0.018, z=−0.89) is consistent with zero but cannot exclude a headline-scale bias; the conversion should be labeled "approximate/illustrative" with explicit caveats on the additional uncertainty.
[MINOR] §IV, §VIII, and §XIII: All results (T-Web, DESIVAST void membership, and the headline Δf_CW) are performed in fixed redshift space with no real-space reconstruction. While RSD effects are acknowledged and a finger-of-god Monte Carlo shows membership stability to <0.4 pp, there is no quantitative estimate of how redshift-space void geometry distortions (finger-of-god elongation, Kaiser effect on walls/filaments) propagate into the any-hole-vs-maximal-sphere geometry term or the overall ≈0.9 pp systematic envelope. A short dedicated paragraph or supplementary figure quantifying this for the DESIVAST sphere-growing and watershed algorithms would strengthen the interpretation for bounce/inflation models.
[MINOR] §VI A, Table V, and §IX C: The secondary T-Web void bin (n=428) is dominated by survey-shell/edge artifacts at z≲0.24 and carries negligible overlap with the DESIVAST z≤0.24 sample; its use as a "diagnostic stress test" is appropriate, but the text should more explicitly state that it contributes zero weight to the headline null and that the controlling constraint comes entirely from the DESIVAST re-projection (n≈57k).
[MINOR] Overall presentation: The manuscript is commendably transparent and thorough (analysis-tree table, Phase-2 sweep, label-shuffle LEE, monopole-invariance algebra, duplicate-row checks), but the extreme length, repeated caveats, and defensive tone ("strictly quotable result," "not a positive-signal claim") make the central null harder to extract. Minor condensation of §VI–VII (T-Web diagnostics) and clearer separation of "headline family-wise result" from "supporting cross-checks" would improve readability for PRD without loss of rigor.

One sentence: The central claim—that spiral-galaxy chirality exhibits no statistically significant void/non-void environmental dependence at the ≈0.9 pp (classifier-label) / ≈2.3 pp (de-attenuated physical) level across five DESIVAST void definitions in DESI DR1—is supported by the data, with the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null, same-footprint retabulation, and comprehensive cross-checks providing a robust bounded null within the explicitly stated redshift-space and classifier-proxy limitations.
