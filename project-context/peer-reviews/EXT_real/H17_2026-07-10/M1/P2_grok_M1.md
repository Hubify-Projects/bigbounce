(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sec. I (Scope) and Sec. VII (systematics budget): The headline conservative floor (~1.3σ) and GR-marginalized range rely on a transferred proxy correlation ρ = −0.868 from the power-spectrum SDB channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance Cov_B from Heinrich et al. is not public; while the authors' own tree-level surrogate Fisher reproduces the local baseline to 2–11% and yields a milder native ρ ≈ −0.42 with σ_marg(f_NL) ≈ 0.94 (~2.3σ), the proxy transferability across channels is not quantitatively justified and renders the quoted 1.3σ endpoint a marginal estimate rather than a channel-native result.
[MAJOR] Sec. II.A (The Prediction) and Appendix A (f_NL correction): The central claim that Cai et al. [9] contains a transcription error producing the erroneous printed f_NL = −35/8 (vs. correct vertex-sum value −35/16) rests on identifying a specific −(99/128)∑k_i^3 discrepancy in the final monomial polynomial; this foundational correction (certified four ways) must be demonstrated with explicit side-by-side polynomials, vertex contributions, and squeezed-limit reductions in the main text or a prominent early appendix subsection rather than relegated to Appendix A, as it underpins every subsequent significance and Bayes factor.
[MINOR] Sec. II.C (Assumptions, point d): Faithful cubic-order bispectrum transmission across the bounce is closed only by an O((k η_bounce)^2) ~ 10^{-4} scaling estimate plus single-clock nonlinear ζ-conservation (conditional on dressed-metric quantization with c_s^2 = 1); while the authors correctly flag this as the weakest link and bound δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3}, the absence of an explicit numerical integration of the full Maldacena cubic action with bounce-modified mode functions leaves the "UV-completion-independent within the Wilson-Ewing class" statement resting on a motivated but non-rigorous closure.
[MINOR] Sec. VI (Bayesian comparison) and abstract: The reported BF ≈ 9–14 is explicitly illustrative and prior-dependent (varying from ~4 to ~17 across competitor widths [−5,+5] vs. [−15,+15] and bounce priors delta vs. σ_theory = 1.0 Gaussian); the abstract presents the interval without immediately qualifying its sensitivity to prior choice and the fact that it is not robust model-selection evidence, which risks overstating discriminatory power.
[MINOR] Overall manuscript (length and focus): The 37-page scope is justified by the source-level audit, explicit template map, itemized budget, and three-way MC validation of the closed-form BF, but the dense digressions on null-space SVD sampling, multi-radius convergence tests, and committed JSON artifacts dilute readability; tighter editing or relocation of technical validation details to a supplemental file would better serve PRD readers while preserving reproducibility.

(3) The central claim that the matter bounce yields a corrected f_NL = −35/16 distinguishable from single-field inflation and testable by SPHEREx at marginal-to-moderate significance (~1.3–2.75σ after template mismatch r ≈ 0.84 and systematics) is supported by the internal multi-method verification of the amplitude, the explicit Fisher-weighted overlap calculation, the in-house multi-tracer validation Fisher, and the transparent (if proxy-dependent) systematic budget, subject to the stated assumptions.
