VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract (and §I, §V.B): The abstract calls the DESIVAST void-vs-non-void contrast “the primary, pre-declared estimand,” yet §V.B explicitly states that the DESIVAST path was designated post-hoc as the “exploratory primary” because “no timestamped analysis plan predates the data” and that the result must be read as the tightest of several correlated estimators within a uniform null family. This direct contradiction on analysis status undermines the headline claim and requires immediate correction (remove “pre-declared” or supply timestamped plan evidence).
[MAJOR] §II, Appendix A, and Table I: The entire environmental null rests on per-galaxy class_eq labels and the catalog monopole (−0.0026) supplied by the concurrent companion Paper IV (arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX). While Appendix A summarizes architecture and parity tests, the referee cannot independently verify training details, Z2 TTA implementation, GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %, κ = 0.40), or monopole origin without the companion manuscript; any residual label bias propagates undiluted into ∆fCW and the de-attenuated bound.
[MAJOR] §VIII, Table XI, and §VIII E: The “honest effective 2σ systematic envelope of ≈0.9 pp” is formed by quadrature addition of individually estimated terms (geometry 0.60 pp dominant, footprint, confidence, match radius). No unified end-to-end Monte Carlo or covariance propagation of the full pipeline (including residual RSD boundary flips at the fixed-void-geometry level and BGS selection-function leakage) is presented; the envelope therefore remains an informed estimate rather than a validated total uncertainty.
[MINOR] §VI.A, §VII, and §IX.C: The secondary T-Web void bin (n = 428) is severely underpowered and survey-shell contaminated at z ≲ 0.24, while the bright/dark ~2.1σ sign-flip (and its partial orthogonality to T-Web class, χ² = 4933) is explained via selection function but lacks the quantitative injection-recovery mock the text itself flags as necessary for DR2. These diagnostics are correctly subordinated, yet their residual ambiguity should be more explicitly bounded before the paper is finalized.
[MINOR] Reproducibility and presentation: The text repeatedly invokes unprovided artifacts [A1]–[A34], committed pipeline drivers, and a companion data repository. For a PRD submission the core membership test, T-Web grid construction, and DESIVAST point-in-sphere cross-match must be fully reproducible from the public DESI DR1 zall catalog + DESIVAST VAC alone, or a self-contained methods supplement must be added.

One sentence: The central claim of environment-independent spiral chirality (null ∆fCW to within an effective ~0.9 pp labeled / ~2.3 pp de-attenuated bound at the ≳25 Mpc/h scale) is supported by the DESIVAST primary contrast, the uniform Bonferroni-5 family null, and the consistency suite, once the post-hoc designation contradiction and systematic-envelope validation are resolved.
