VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (and abstract/title): The “four-route channel-level closure” and claim that the enumerated routes “exhaust” the minimal-ECH dark-energy channels are presented as a substantive no-go, yet the text repeatedly and explicitly caveats that this is not an operator-level theorem, not a proven complete diffeomorphism-invariant basis, and is only “channel-level… under stated assumptions”; the completeness argument (F1 algebraic torsion + F2 totally antisymmetric spin current + NDA monotonicity) is asserted via symmetry counting but is not shown to rule out all admissible higher-dimension or boundary operators within the minimal field content, weakening the headline result.

[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result) and Sec. XIV D (structural tension): The central claim that “torsion vanishes at all classical metric/scalar perturbation orders around the torsion-free branch” (and that the Holst sector therefore decouples) is stated for canonical scalar matter only, with explicit exclusions for fermion spin density, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, and non-minimal couplings; however, the proof sketches in X B–E do not demonstrate that the algebraic Cartan constraint plus first Bianchi identity suffice to all perturbative orders (as opposed to linear order), nor do they quantify the size of the neglected sectors, so the claimed “Tier I” status and the N_tot ≈ 92 vs. f_NL = −35/16 erasure argument rest on an incompletely justified extrapolation.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV A, App. D (Route 1 NJL closure) and App. C (Fierz-by-Fierz lemma): The regulated gap-equation exclusion of a vacuum scalar condensate and the claim that the four-fermion basis closes onto a finite, uniformly M_Pl^{-2}-suppressed set are central to amplitude closure of R1 (and its parity-odd partner); these rest on external scripts and appendices whose explicit algebra, cutoff independence, and sign/magnitude results are not reproduced or cross-checked in the main text, making independent verification impossible without the supplementary material.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV F and App. B (R4 naturalness closure + single-scale NDA no-go): The argument that R4 merely “relocates the CC problem” via an ultralight m_θ ∼ H_0 tuning is conceptually sound, but the quantitative mapping ρ_Λ = Ξ M_Pl^4 with N_tot ≈ 92, the on-shell vs. off-shell dimension bookkeeping (+1 vs. +4), and the dilution factor D_inf are presented as derived results while depending on phenomenological ansätze (bounce-curvature insertion, thermal phase-space factor (T_reh/M_GUT)^{3/2}, etc.) whose first-principles justification is acknowledged as incomplete; the claim that “no amplitude is derived, so the argument is not circular” is formally true but does not rescue the predictive power of the no-go when the only evasion is the tuning the mechanism was meant to explain.

[MINOR] Throughout (especially Intro, Sec. IV Scope paragraphs, Sec. IX): The manuscript is written in an extremely dense, footnote-heavy style with repeated hedging (“under stated assumptions,” “channel-level not operator-level,” “exhausts the fourth channel”) that forces the reader to parse multiple layers of qualification before reaching any concrete claim; this obscures the precise logical status of the 13/14 barriers and makes the paper unnecessarily difficult to referee or cite.

[MINOR] Sec. II C, XII A (inflationary suppression and reheating thermal-reset barrier): The conditional thermal-washout argument (Γ_wash(T_reh) > H(T_reh) erasing coherent ⟨J_5⟩) is offered as supporting B14, yet it is left as an inequality whose satisfaction is asserted rather than computed across the relevant SM channels (top Yukawa, sphalerons, neutrinos); this is a plausible additional erasure channel but is not load-bearing and should be clearly labeled as such.

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are each constrained (amplitude-suppressed for R1–R3; naturalness/explanatory-deficit for R4) under the paper’s explicit single-scale power-counting, algebraic-torsion, and perturbation-transparency assumptions—is supported by the dimensional no-go, torsion-elimination derivations, and Bianchi-identity decoupling arguments once the carefully scoped “channel-level” (rather than operator-complete) nature of the result is accepted, but the strength of that claim is limited by the acknowledged incompleteness of the route enumeration and the external-script/appendix dependence of several key technical steps.
