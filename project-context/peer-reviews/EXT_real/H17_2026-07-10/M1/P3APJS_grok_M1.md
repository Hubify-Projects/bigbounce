VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract / §1 / §3 (three-tier catalog structure) / §6.4: The repeated claim that “Validation establishes that the 268,519 subset is real” (and that the catalog is “catalog-grade”) is unsupported; the validation consists of per-survey injection-recovery tests that certify detector sensitivity only for the broad/continuum-dominated anomaly class (99–100% recovery at 5σ for DESI broad class; 64% for SDSS continuum-dip; 100% for Planck), with narrow single-pixel lines recovering only at ≥15σ, while NEOWISE uses only a geometry-QA mask (not a sensitivity test) and the bulk of the 268,519 objects (especially the 195,829 DESI anomalies) lie on sky/filler fibers with no independent purity or false-positive-rate assessment.
[MAJOR] §3.5 / Table 5 / §2.2: The eROSITA DR1 tier (298 objects) has a production score axis (threshold 0.259) that is irreproducible on the committed raw reconstruction scores, on any of 16 monotone rescalings, or on retrained IsolationForest axes (Spearman ρ = −0.10 on top-5; non-monotone); the tier is correctly excised from all counts but its inclusion in the pipeline provenance and the need for a post-hoc “membership-list-is-canonical” workaround expose a fundamental reproducibility failure in at least one survey component.
[MAJOR] §2.2 / §2.4 / §6.4(i): Feature scalers for tabular surveys (eROSITA, NEOWISE) were fit on the full sample rather than the training split, introducing validation-set leakage into normalization constants; while a bounded robustness check exists for eROSITA (top-298 Jaccard 0.76), equivalent checks for NEOWISE are “queued” and the full 22.5 M-spectrum DESI catalog re-inference is blocked because native score parquets reside on an exited pod, so key reproducibility claims rest on incomplete or proxy evidence.
[MAJOR] §3.1 / Table 3 / Abstract: The headline 268,519 count and the ~73× / ~141× multipliers are process-volume figures dominated by non-science-target spectra (~98.7% of DESI anomaly clusters match no primary science-class TARGETTYPE bit; only 2,468 science-target clusters remain, ≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark); the paper’s own framing acknowledges this, yet the title, abstract, and introductory multipliers still risk overstating the catalog’s like-for-like advance over prior single-survey science-target searches.
[MINOR] §2.4 / §3.4 / §6.1: The LAMOST DR10 tier (~113,000 objects after dedup) is retained in the inclusive 377,482 total as a “methodological lesson” despite 98% blue-excess training-bias artifact and 5.8% injection-recovery at 5σ (gate FAIL); its explicit exclusion from the validated 268,519 headline is correct but the decision to carry it in the grand total while labeling it a failure mode creates unnecessary reader confusion.
[MINOR] §5 / Abstract: The two cosmological applications (multi-tracer fNL yielding no improvement beyond the single-tracer baseline; NANOGrav γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively ruling out only an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB) are presented as “secondary demonstrations” that return null results; their inclusion adds length without demonstrating unique scientific utility of the anomaly catalog beyond what existing tracers already provide.
[MINOR] §2.2 / §3.7: The Gaia DR3 tier was a synthetic-placeholder fallback (exact source IDs of the form ⌊5×10¹⁸ + i⌋, duplicates, unphysical magnitudes) that was correctly excised, but its initial presence in the committed pipeline outputs and the deferral of a genuine Gaia tier indicate incomplete data-audit hygiene at the time of the production run.

The central claim that a validated multi-survey anomaly-candidate catalog of 268,519 objects has been produced from a 37.3-million-source scan is only partially supported: the injection-recovery and reproducibility-script evidence substantiates sensitivity for the broad anomaly class on DESI/SDSS/Planck and supplies a transparent (if heavily qualified) process-volume catalog, but is materially weakened by the irreproducible eROSITA score axis, incomplete held-out re-inference, predominance of unverified non-science-target detections, and absence of a catalog-wide purity or contamination estimate.
