VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section V.B (Primary vs. secondary analysis paths): Explicit post-hoc designation of the DESIVAST-anchored void/non-void contrast as the "designated-primary (exploratory, not pre-registered)" estimand after inspecting results violates standard practice for claiming a focused primary result, even though the family-wise Bonferroni-5 null across all five void definitions is transparently robust; reframe the DESIVAST family null as the sole quotable headline without elevating one post-hoc row.
[MINOR] Sections II–III and throughout (Reliance on companion Paper IV): All per-galaxy class_eq labels, the catalog-wide monopole Δf_P4_CW = −0.0026, and the GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91 %, κ = 0.40) used for de-attenuation are imported from the concurrent but unpublished companion catalog paper; the present null and physical-chirality bound are conditional on Paper IV's validity, yet no joint reproducibility statement or sensitivity to monopole uncertainty beyond the quoted ±0.000279 is provided.
[MINOR] Section VIII (RSD treatment and systematic envelope): The first-order Zel’dovich reconstruction shifts the unrestricted Δf_CW by only 0.024 pp and the fixed-geometry MC bounds membership flips at ≤0.37 pp, but the analysis remains strictly in redshift space; the quoted ≈0.9 pp (labelled) / ≈2.26 pp (de-attenuated) envelope does not explicitly propagate residual anisotropic tidal or finger-of-god model uncertainty, nor does it show the quadrature table (Table XI) with term-by-term justification and covariance assumptions.
[MINOR] Section VI.A and XI (T-Web bright/dark sign-flip and non-orthogonality): The ~2.1σ filament bright-vs-dark Δf_CW flip is correctly flagged as a selection diagnostic, but the documented T-Web × program non-independence (χ² = 4933, Cramér’s V = 0.078) and absence of an end-to-end injection-recovery mock leave a small unquantified leakage pathway into the primary DESIVAST null; strengthen the explicit propagation calculation (currently ~0.001 pp) or defer the claim to DR2.
[MINOR] Sections IV and VII (T-Web Phase-2 sweep and grid caveats): The Rs = 10 Mpc/h cells are retained despite being below the 25.9 Mpc/h grid scale (explicitly excluded from the robustness claim), and the mask-dilation iteration count varies per cell; document that all physical conclusions survive the six resolved cells only and add a converged-grid test at N_grid = 384³ for the canonical Rs = 25 Mpc/h run.

The central claim—that spiral chirality shows no detectable environment dependence at the ≳25 Mpc/h scale in DESI DR1, with a well-bounded null Δf_CW consistent with parity—is supported by the data and the multi-algorithm cross-checks once the post-hoc framing and companion-paper dependence are acknowledged.
