VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §3 (Three-Tier Catalog Structure), and §3.1: The headline claim of a single “validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies” is unsupported by the paper’s own tier definitions. Validation is explicitly non-uniform: only DESI (broad/extended class), SDSS (continuum-dip), and Planck pass detector-sensitivity injection-recovery at 5σ; NEOWISE passes only a geometry-QA mask (not a sensitivity test); eROSITA and the synthetic Gaia tier are excised entirely; LAMOST is retained solely as an exploratory failure mode (98% blue-excess artifact, 5.8% recovery). The 268,519 figure therefore aggregates tiers of demonstrably unequal validity.

[MAJOR] §3.1, Table 3, and the positional recount against zall-pix: 98.7% of the 195,829 DESI anomalies (the dominant component) lie on sky-fiber, filler, or non-primary TARGETTYPE spectra. The science-target yield is only 2,468 clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark on a comparable target selection). The large multipliers quoted (~73× DESI-only S>5, ~141× full point-source) are process-volume figures comparing a full-instrument stream against a science-target benchmark; they do not represent a like-for-like catalog-size increase and are presented in a manner that invites misreading.

[MAJOR] §3.5 and §3.7: The production pipeline exhibits fatal provenance failures. The eROSITA score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible on the committed raw reconstruction scores, on any of 16 tested monotone rescalings, or on retrained IsolationForest axes; the production values are non-monotone in the raw artifact. The Gaia DR3 tier is entirely synthetic placeholder data (source IDs exactly ⌊5×10¹⁸ + i⌋, duplicates, unphysical magnitudes). Both required post-hoc excision after the fact, demonstrating that the native-retrain/Path-C protocol did not guarantee integrity or reproducibility of the production outputs.

[MAJOR] §6.4(i) and the candid-scope note on the 5-fold checks: The DESI robustness claim rests on a single production-ensemble injection-recovery test (broad class recovers 99–100% at 5σ on real SPARCL spectra) plus two correlated stability diagnostics (Jaccard and tail-preservation) computed from the same short-trained proxy-model score vectors. Those proxy models fail the paper’s own val-loss ≤0.30 retain gate and are explicitly not independent confirmations. No full per-object held-out re-inference of the released 22.5 M catalog exists (raw native scores reside on an exited pod). Narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, establishing a hard sensitivity floor for the 496-bin mean-reconstruction scorer that is not reflected in the “catalog-grade” label.

[MINOR] §2.2 (Tabular preprocessing) and the bounded-robustness paragraph: Feature scalers for eROSITA, NEOWISE, and Gaia were fit on the full sample rather than the training split alone. While a post-hoc check for eROSITA shows acceptable top-298 Jaccard (0.76), the equivalent verification for NEOWISE is stated as “queued” and the assumption that within-survey rankings are invariant is unverified for the tiers that remain in the validated count.

[MINOR] Table 2, §2.4, and the multiplicity of footnotes: The paper requires an elaborate three-tier taxonomy, cross-transfer vs. native distinctions, continuity-slice vs. native-threshold distinctions, and repeated “read once” warnings to prevent misinterpretation of every headline number. This level of internal qualification makes the central deliverable difficult to evaluate or use without constant cross-reference to caveats that are not summarized in a single, prominent limitations section.

[MINOR] §5: The two cosmological applications are presented as secondary demonstrations yet occupy substantial space while returning null results (no multi-tracer fNL improvement; NANOGrav γ consistent with matter-bounce but decisive only against an idealized circular-orbit SMBHB reference that the authors themselves note is not realistic). They add no new scientific constraint and dilute focus on the catalog.

One sentence on the central claim: The central claim that a validated, catalog-grade sample of 268,519 multi-survey anomalies has been produced from a 37.3-million-source scan is only weakly supported by the evidence, because the validation is partial and tier-dependent, the dominant DESI contribution is overwhelmingly non-science-target spectra, multiple production components had to be excised for irreproducibility or synthetic content, and the single robust sensitivity gate applies only to the broad anomaly class at a stated sensitivity floor that excludes narrow-line recovery.
