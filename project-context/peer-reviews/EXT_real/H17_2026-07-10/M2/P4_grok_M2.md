(2) ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract (and Sec. IV C, Table I): The claim that the block-bootstrap WLS template fit "disfavors a clean Aref = 0.017 dipole ... at z ≈−7.6" is presented without the explicit qualifier (present in the body text) that this is a model-dependent template-disfavor statistic under the specific NSIDE=8 block-bootstrap covariance; it is not a calibrated frequentist exclusion or upper limit, and the primary sensitivity statement rests on the injection-recovery A95 bracket instead.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D (and Appendix D, Table VII): The forward-modeling of the post-MASTER canonical ℓ=1 residual accounts for only ~53% of the amplitude with imaging+morphology templates (leaving ~47% as an explicit open item); while the paper correctly bounds even the full residual amplitude below the real-space A95, it should include a short quantitative demonstration (e.g., diluted-dipole injection into the residual map) that no plausible cosmological component consistent with the observed harmonic structure could evade detection in the primary HC real-space estimator.
[MINOR] Sec. VI A (and Sec. II B): The fully model-independent GZ1-human-only dipole test returns a clean null (z = −0.54σ), but its sensitivity (A95 ≈4.5–6.8%) is ~4–5× coarser than the headline HC sample; the text should state more explicitly that this cross-check corroborates absence of large inherited bias but does not itself constrain sub-percent pseudo-label inheritance (that limit is carried by the template-regression and block-bootstrap anchors).
[MINOR] Sec. III D and Appendix B: The 2-fold flip TTA enforces output equivariance by construction and the flip-swap correlation = 1.000 is reported, but the D4 hold-out stability test (|Δ⟨pCW⟩| < 0.0016) is performed only on small subsamples; a brief statement confirming that the production Catalog C dipole result is unchanged under full D4 TTA on a larger validation subset would remove any residual concern about rotation-induced non-equivariance.

(3) The central claim that the large-scale chirality dipole is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity (with a genuine Shamir-scale signal recoverable) is supported by the primary real-space HC estimator, injection-recovery calibration, and cross-checks.
