(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section VIII B/E, primary “footprint-restricted” contrast: the adopted footprint—the union of angular projections of detected hole spheres intersected with their radial span—is not the published DESIVAST/BGS completeness mask or a random-catalog selection function. The manuscript acknowledges this but repeatedly describes the estimator as “same-selection-function.” Void and control galaxies may therefore differ in radial selection, imaging depth, fiber completeness, magnitude, angular size, inclination, and morphology. The primary result must be recomputed using the official survey mask/randoms and either per-void matched controls, inverse-probability weighting, or a covariate-adjusted model with explicit balance diagnostics.

[MAJOR] Sections II and XIII and Appendix A, classifier-bias cancellation and physical de-attenuation: a spatially constant classifier monopole cancels algebraically, but environment-dependent sensitivity, specificity, confidence selection, and leakage into the excluded NS class do not. The manuscript’s own void-stratified GZ1 validation has an error-asymmetry confidence interval of approximately ±3.7 percentage points, far wider than the advertised 0.9-point bound. Global accuracy of 69.91% and approximate global error symmetry therefore cannot justify the claimed 2.26-point physical-chirality bound. Until an environment-calibrated confusion matrix or an end-to-end measurement-error model is supplied, the conclusion must be restricted to the fraction of objects assigned class_eq CW among those assigned CW/CCW.

[MAJOR] Section VIII B, Table XI, and Eq. (4), “honest effective 2σ systematic envelope”: this is not a statistically defined uncertainty interval. It combines a two-sided statistical confidence half-width with maximum excursions under alternative membership definitions, perturbation experiments, and analysis choices, assumes independence despite substantial overlap among geometry, membership, GALZONE, footprint, and RSD terms, and assigns no probability model to the systematic entries. [MAJOR] Section V B and Table IV, post-hoc primary selection and upper-bound multiplicity: DESIVAST was designated primary after examining T-Web, DESIVAST, Tempel, ASTRA, multiple membership constructions, control volumes, thresholds, and stratifications. Bonferroni correction over five selected DESIVAST rows does not account for selection of that family or of the preferred footprint estimator. This matters especially for an upper-bound claim, because post-hoc selection can favor the tightest or most reassuring interval even when every test is nonsignificant. The DR1 result should be labeled exploratory and stated as “no evidence detected,” not as a confirmatory exclusion; a smallest effect size of interest and equivalence test should be fixed prospectively.

[MAJOR] Section VIII and Tables X–XIV, independence assumption in the primary confidence intervals: the two-sample binomial standard errors treat tens of thousands of galaxies as independent Bernoulli trials. Galaxies are clustered within the same voids and angular observing regions and share imaging and classifier systematics; physical spin correlations may also occur within structures. The relevant effective number of independent units may therefore be closer to the number of voids or sky blocks than to the number of galaxies. The primary interval must be checked with a void-level and angular block bootstrap/jackknife, cluster-robust inference, or a hierarchical model.

[MAJOR] Section VIII and Table XII, missing primary covariate-adjusted analysis: the manuscript explicitly defers the appropriate DESIVAST regression or matched-control estimate. This is not optional at the claimed sub-percent precision because classifier performance and CW/CCW inclusion can depend on redshift, apparent magnitude, size, surface brightness, inclination, imaging leg, and confidence, while these quantities differ environmentally. The dark-program stratum itself shows a roughly 4.7-point void/non-void difference at about 2σ; multiplying the global bright/dark offset by the small difference in dark fractions assumes away a void-by-program interaction and does not resolve this observation. A stratified permutation or multivariable model including interactions is required.

[MAJOR] Abstract, Section V B, Sections XII and XV, inconsistent numerical bound: Section V B identifies approximately 1.1 percentage points as the least-constraining simultaneous family-wise bound, whereas the abstract and conclusions recommend 0.9 points. Furthermore, if approximately 0.9–0.95 points is intended as a half-width around the nonzero primary estimate of +0.18 points, the corresponding maximum absolute contrast is approximately 1.1 points before classifier attenuation, not 0.9. Applying the stated attenuation factor would then give a physical scale near 2.8 points rather than 2.26. One interval, with a clearly defined center, confidence construction, and treatment of classifier uncertainty, must replace these competing numbers.

[MAJOR] Section XII B and Appendix B, bounce/inflation interpretation: no cited bounce or inflation model predicts the measured void/non-void statistic, and no transfer function connects a parity-violating cosmological parameter to the classifier-labelled CW fraction. Appendix B explicitly introduces an ad hoc, non-covariant toy operator, so it cannot turn the observational null into a constraint on an EFT coupling. Moreover, the quoted 25Mpc/h smoothing scale belongs to the secondary T-Web analysis, not to the primary DESIVAST void estimator, whose geometrical scales are heterogeneous. The model-exclusion language should be removed; at most the result is an observational benchmark for future models, and Appendix B should be omitted or moved to non-refereed supplementary discussion.

[MAJOR] Sections II and XIII and Appendices D/E, dependence on Paper IV and unreleased archival products: the chirality labels are the indispensable measurement input, yet Paper IV still has an arXiv placeholder and the Zenodo DOI and final immutable analysis snapshot are pending. A methodological synopsis cannot substitute for coordinated review of the training data, pseudo-label provenance, CW/CCW/NS selection, and final catalog version. Publication should require coordinated review of Paper IV and this manuscript, fixed version identifiers for all consumed labels and weights, and a DOI-archived executable release reproducing the primary tables.

[MINOR] Sections IV and IX A, secondary T-Web analysis: the random-catalog-weighted rebuild changes approximately 73% of matched-galaxy class assignments and changes the void volume fraction by a factor of about 23. This shows that the canonical unweighted T-Web classes are dominated by the DESI selection function and should not be presented as physical corroboration. Either use the completeness-corrected implementation as the canonical secondary analysis or move the unweighted T-Web material to an appendix.

[MINOR] Figures, tables, and sample-definition audit: Figure 6’s upper panel appears to be mislabeled “maximal voids per pixel,” although its 50–734 range is consistent with galaxy counts rather than maximal-void counts; “exact” and k=20 memberships are interchanged across headline and family tables; and SPECTYPE=QSO objects are admitted into a sample described as spirals without a dedicated sensitivity result. These points require correction, including a SPECTYPE=GALAXY-only primary rerun.
