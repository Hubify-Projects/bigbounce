VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract, Sec. IV C, and Sec. VI B: The injection-recovery calibration (A95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%], A50 ≈ 0.75%) and claim that “a genuine Shamir-scale dipole would have been detected” are performed on observed (post-classifier) Ap amplitudes. The ~70% chirality accuracy on the independent GZ1 cross-match (g = 2a − 1 ≈ 0.4 dilution) is acknowledged and stated to render the null “conservative,” but the text does not explicitly propagate g to quote a physical (pre-classifier) amplitude sensitivity or revised exclusion on true sky dipole strength; this blurs the mapping between reported observed limits and physical isotropy constraints.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D and referenced Appendix D: The forward-modeling of the canonical-mask post-MASTER ℓ = 1 residual attributes only ~52–54% of the amplitude to imaging + morphology templates (leaving an explicit ~47% open item). While correctly noted as lying below the real-space A95 threshold and therefore not affecting the primary null, the lack of a closed per-pixel systematics budget for the residual (or a quantitative statement that any cosmological fraction must be ≪ A50) leaves a small but non-zero possibility of partial cosmological contribution in the diagnostic channel.
[MINOR] Sec. II B and Sec. VI A: The model-independent GZ1-human-label-only dipole test (z = −0.54σ) is a valuable cross-check, but its HC spiral sample (N ≈ 4.6 × 10^4) has ~4.5× lower statistical power than the primary HC subsample (N ≈ 9.5 × 10^5). It corroborates the null at coarse sensitivity (A95 ≈ 4.5–6.8%) but does not independently validate the sub-percent regime of the headline result; the text should more explicitly separate “corroboration” from “equivalent-sensitivity confirmation.”
[MINOR] Sec. III B (Declared Analysis Hierarchy) and Table I: The primacy of the real-space HC dipole and block-bootstrap WLS template fit is correctly declared, and harmonic-channel σ values are labeled diagnostic. However, the paper’s length and dense cross-referencing to git artifacts, multiple null constructions, and eight-anchor battery make it easy for readers to misinterpret the +3.64σ / +7.28σ residuals as competing detection significances; a one-paragraph “reader’s guide” summarizing Table I in the main text would improve clarity.
[MINOR] Data Availability and reproducibility: The statement that the catalog, model weights, and scripts are released publicly is present, but the provided excerpt cuts off before the full declaration. PRD requires an explicit, complete statement (including exact URLs/DOIs or permanent archive locations) before acceptance.

One sentence: The central claim—that the real-space chirality dipole in the pre-specified high-confidence subsample is consistent with null (+0.41σ, p = 0.31) while cleanly disfavoring a Shamir-scale (Aref = 0.017) dipole via template regression—is supported by the primary estimator, injection-recovery calibration, and extensive systematics battery.
