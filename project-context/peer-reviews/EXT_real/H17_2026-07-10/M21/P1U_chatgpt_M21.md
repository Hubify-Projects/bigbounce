[MAJOR] Section X: the perturbation-transparency statement is correct only in its narrow classical domain, but it is not the novel theorem claimed. For canonical spin-zero matter and constant γ, the connection equation sets torsion to zero and the Holst contraction then vanishes by the algebraic Bianchi identity; the theory is simply classically equivalent to GR in that sector. This does not constrain fermion loops, dynamical Immirzi fields, propagating torsion, non-minimal matter, boundary sectors or quantum effective operators—all of which the manuscript excludes and several of which are invoked in Routes 2–4. The valid scalar-sector observation therefore cannot support the broader dark-energy closure.
