VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract & Sec. I (Scope paragraph): The assertion that the four routes are “basis-complete at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes” (and that the two previously omitted parity-odd operators are now “closed explicitly”) is stated without a self-contained one-paragraph summary of the completeness argument or the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (App. C). A referee must read the appendix and the script reference to verify exhaustiveness; this should be elevated to the main text or a boxed statement.
[MINOR] Sec. IV D–E (Routes 2 & 3): The amplitude budgets rely on phenomenological one-loop ansätze/upper bounds (Eq. 17, Eq. 19) whose absolute normalizations are not derived from first principles in the cited works (Mercuri, Shapiro–Teixeira, Benedetti–Speziale). While the ~60-order suppression margin makes the qualitative closure robust, the text should explicitly label these as “conservative EFT estimates” and state that the no-go survives O(1)–O(10) coefficient variations.
[MINOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result): The claim that the Holst sector “decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion” for canonical scalar matter is central and novel, yet the main-text sketch (items B–E) omits even one explicit perturbed-tetrad term or the key cancellation in the Einstein or Klein–Gordon equations. The full term-by-term verification is relegated to the appendix; a single illustrative equation (or reference to the precise cancellation) should appear in Sec. X for readability.
[MINOR] Sec. XIV D (Structural Tension): The mutual-exclusivity argument between the ECH dark-energy route (N_tot ≈ 92) and an observable matter-bounce f_NL = −35/16 relies on the differential N_tot − N_exit ≫ N_coh and the mapping k_phys^SPHEREx → bounce-era scales. The numerical factor (~32) and coherence-window estimate are stated but not derived inline; a short equation or one-line derivation should be added so the claim is auditable without external companion papers.
[MINOR] General (readability & self-containment): The manuscript contains >60 forward references to its own appendices, “this paper’s lemma,” and coordinated-submission siblings. While the closures are analytically self-contained (as asserted), a short “Roadmap of Load-Bearing Results” table or paragraph early in Sec. I would materially improve referee and reader navigation.

The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at channel/amplitude level under the stated assumptions, that the Holst sector is perturbation-transparent for canonical scalar matter, and that a structural tension exists with observable bounce f_NL—is supported by the dimensional no-go, explicit operator closures, regulated gap equation, and decoupling argument within the carefully delimited scope.
