VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, §1, and Table 2: The headline “268,519 validated catalog-grade” claim and 37.3 M / 141× process-volume framing are not supported by the actual science-ready yield. Only 2,468 DESI anomalies (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2023 benchmark) lie on validated science-target spectra; ~98.7 % fall on sky/filler fibers. The large multipliers and “validated” label therefore require prominent, quantitative caveats in the title/abstract and §1 rather than being relegated to later footnotes and §6.4.
[MAJOR] §3.5, §3.7, and provenance audit artifacts: eROSITA’s production score axis is irreproducible on any committed monotone rescaling or IsolationForest retrain (explicitly excised), the Gaia DR3 tier was synthetic placeholder data (removed), and LAMOST is 98 % blue-excess training-bias artifact (exploratory only, injection-recovery 5.8 %). These exclusions plus ACT DR6 quarantine and pod-lost raw scores mean the released catalog does not meet ApJS standards for a fully reproducible, provenance-clean data product.
[MAJOR] §2.4, §3.1, §6.4(i), and injection-recovery artifacts: Detector-sensitivity validation is limited to the broad/continuum-dominated class (99–100 % recovery at 5σ for DESI/SDSS/Planck); narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ (stated sensitivity floor of the 496-bin MSE scorer). NEOWISE passes only geometry-QA. The heterogeneous “validated” label therefore applies to a mixed population whose per-object flags do not fully communicate completeness limits for the science cases emphasized in the abstract.
[MINOR] §5 and cosmological applications: The multi-tracer fNL (§5) and NANOGrav γ analyses are null results (no improvement on single-tracer baseline; no cosmological detection) and add negligible scientific value. They should be shortened to a brief methods demonstration or moved to an appendix.
[MINOR] Throughout (especially §2.2, §3, Table 2 footnotes): Over-reliance on internal reproducibility-script paths, pod-side artifacts, and hashed TARGETIDs prevents independent verification of ~86 % of the released DESI rows. While the companion repo is referenced, a catalog paper must allow a reader to reproduce headline numbers and join the catalog to external archives without executing non-public pod scripts.

The central claim of a large, validated multi-survey anomaly catalog is only partially supported: the pipeline was executed and numbers were produced, but the headline count, reproducibility, and sensitivity validation contain material limitations that are disclosed yet not resolved to ApJS catalog standards.
