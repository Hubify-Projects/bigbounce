VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:
[MINOR] Abstract & §I: The companion catalog paper is cited only as “arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX” (placeholder). A real submission requires the actual arXiv identifier (or explicit “submitted concurrently” note with DOI once available) so readers can immediately locate the monopole and classifier details on which the present analysis rests.

[MINOR] §V B (Primary vs. secondary paths) & abstract: The DESIVAST-anchored path is explicitly designated post-hoc (“exploratory, not pre-registered”). Although the authors apply a Bonferroni-5 family null, commit to pre-registration for DR2, and show the headline contrast is stable across all five void definitions, the abstract and conclusions must more prominently label the DR1 result as exploratory to prevent any appearance of selective path reporting; the current phrasing (“designated-primary”) is insufficiently cautious for a null-result claim.

[MINOR] §VIII & Table XI: The ≈0.9 pp “honest effective 2σ systematic envelope” is obtained by folding counting, geometry (0.60 pp co-dominant), footprint, confidence, and match-radius terms in quadrature. The text does not tabulate the individual contributions or show the quadrature sum explicitly; a short supplementary table or equation block is required so referees can verify that no term was omitted and that the envelope is not under-stated.

[MINOR] §II & §VIII F: The internal P5 matched-sample monopole f_CW^P5 = 0.49719 is stated to be consistent with Paper IV’s −0.0026 offset, yet the ~8 % difference in amplitude is only “reconciled in §VIII F.” The reconciliation paragraph is terse; a one-sentence derivation showing how the matched subsample’s selection function produces the observed 0.0002 shift would eliminate any residual reader uncertainty about whether the monopole reference itself carries unpropagated bias into the Δf_CW contrast.

One sentence: The central claim—that spiral-galaxy chirality shows no detectable dependence on large-scale environment at the ≳25 Mpc/h scale probed by DESIVAST and T-Web, with a robust upper bound of ≲2.3 pp on physical chirality after attenuation correction—is supported by the large-sample, multi-algorithm null results and explicit systematic envelope.
