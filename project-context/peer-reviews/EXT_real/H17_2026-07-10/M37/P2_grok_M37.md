VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Sec. I (Introduction) and abstract: The quoted sensitivity envelopes (∼2.6–2.75σ pre-systematics; ∼1.3–2.75σ post-systematics, including the ∼0.8σ GR-bracket edge) combine results from qualitatively different null procedures (CMB-Fisher signal-only weighting vs. LSS noise weighting; proxy ρ = −0.868 transferred from the SDB channel vs. the authors’ own channel-native surrogate-covariance Fisher with ρ ≈ −0.42–0.49). While the distinctions are disclosed in the text, the absence of a compact summary table that explicitly maps each numerical endpoint to its precise assumptions (weighting scheme, GR treatment, bϕ prior width) risks reader misinterpretation of the “conservative floor.”
[MINOR] Sec. II.A (The Prediction) and referenced Appendix A: The central correction f_NL = −35/16 (vs. the published −35/8) rests on identifying a −(99/128)∑k_i^3 discrepancy in one transcribed polynomial. Although four independent certifications are listed (vertex re-summation at ϵ = 3/2, ϵ-ordered intermediates, three-benchmark matching, and Li et al. general-c_s formula), the manuscript should ensure Appendix A displays the explicit side-by-side monomial comparison (Cai et al. printed coefficients vs. exact vertex sum) so readers can verify the algebraic origin of the factor-of-two shift without recomputing the full cubic action.
[MINOR] Sec. II.A and Sec. IV: The 10 000-sample null-space scan yields r = 0.85 ± 0.13 (range 0.55–1.14) and a pre-systematic significance band 2.2–3.1σ; the headline uses only the narrower noise-weighted central value r = 0.84 ± 0.02. While the authors correctly note that the tails do not alter the post-systematic floor, the null-space uncertainty is not propagated as an additional component in the final systematic budget or abstract ranges.
[MINOR] Sec. V (MegaMapper forecast): The section is correctly labeled illustrative and transfers the SPHEREx-derived GR/bϕ budget as a proxy. However, at z = 2–5 the relativistic projection effects (Doppler, gravitational redshift, lensing magnification) grow steeply; the adopted degradation factors may therefore be optimistic. A short quantitative remark or reference to the expected increase in GR contamination relative to z ≈ 1.5 would strengthen the caveat.
[MINOR] Overall presentation and flow: The manuscript is dense with long parenthetical asides, cross-references to specific committed .json/.py files, and technical reproducibility details that interrupt narrative continuity. Relocating a subset of these (detailed Monte Carlo validation statistics, exact coefficient sets, per-configuration overlap tables) to Supplemental Material would improve readability for the broader PRD audience while preserving full technical transparency.

The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce prediction f_NL = −35/16 yields a detectable signal in SPHEREx at the ∼1.3–2.75σ level (after explicit template-mismatch and systematic corrections) while furnishing moderate Bayesian preference (BF ≈ 9–14) over tuned multifield competitors—is supported by the multi-way amplitude verification, noise-weighted overlap calculation, independent tree-level Fisher cross-check (reff ≈ 0.99), and closed-form Bayes-factor evaluation.
