[MINOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result, especially subsections E–G): The explicit term-by-term perturbed-tetrad expansion and the statement of what would break transparency are welcome, but the proof is presented only for canonical scalar matter; insert a short paragraph (or footnote) explaining why the excluded cases (fermion spin density, propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi) are outside the minimal-ECH dark-energy routes under consideration, so the scope limitation is immediately visible to non-expert readers.
[MINOR] Sec. [MINOR] Sec. III B & references to companion Papers IV/V: The galaxy-spin null is presented as consistent with the predicted negligible amplitude, but all quantitative details (sample size, classifier performance, bias-audit suite, dipole significance) are deferred to external siblings; add a one-paragraph self-contained summary of the key controls and the factor by which the claimed asymmetry is excluded, to make the present manuscript standalone for this observational consistency check.
