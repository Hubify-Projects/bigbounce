[MINOR] Sec. III A and VI B (significance conventions and injection-recovery): The distinction between moment-z / empirical-rank p and Gaussian z is correctly emphasized, but the injection-recovery null (per-pixel binomial shuffle) should be noted in one parenthetical as statistically equivalent (for this purpose) to the primary isotropic pixel-permutation null used for the real-space dipole, to make the A50/A95 thresholds directly transferable without cross-reference to artifacts.

The central claim—that the large-scale chirality dipole in the high-confidence DESI spiral subsample is consistent with null at sub-percent sensitivity and that a clean Shamir-scale signal would have been recovered—is supported by the primary real-space estimator, the declared analysis hierarchy, the empirical injection-recovery calibration, and the multi-anchor systematics battery.
