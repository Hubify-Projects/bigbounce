VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and §3 (three-tier catalog structure) + §3.1: The claim that "Validation establishes that the 268,519 subset is real" and the labeling of a "validated catalog-grade subset of 268,519 unique anomalies" is unsupported; the text explicitly defines this count as a "process-volume figure" of candidates surviving per-survey gates (not confirmed physical detections), with validation limited to broad/continuum-dominated class recovery at 5σ (99–100% for DESI re-pulled spectra) while narrow single-pixel lines recover only at ≥15σ, and NEOWISE limited to geometry-QA only.
[MAJOR] §3.5 (eROSITA DR1): The production score axis (threshold 0.259) is irreproducible (fails all 16 monotone rescalings of the committed raw reconstruction score and 3 IsolationForest retrains; non-monotone in raw artifact with Spearman ρ = −0.10); excising the tier from all counts (including 377,482) while releasing only a raw-score top-298 membership list is appropriate, but retaining extensive discussion of a failed, non-reproducible component undermines the multi-survey validated-catalog claim.
[MAJOR] §2.2, §2.4, §6.4(i) and reproducibility statements: Full end-to-end reproduction of the exact per-object anomaly lists is blocked by pod-lost artifacts (raw native score parquets on exited pod; tid column mixes real TARGETIDs with hashes where only ~1.3% are re-pullable via SPARCL; some tabular feature tables pod-side only); the committed reproduce_headline_dedup.py only recomputes the final 5″ deduplication step (274,353 → 268,519), not the upstream per-survey scoring from public archives.
[MINOR] §3.1 and Table 3: The prominent process-scale multipliers (~73× DESI-only S > 5; ~141× full point-source tier) are presented alongside the headline 195,829 / 268,519 counts, yet the like-for-like science-target benchmark is only 2,468 anomaly clusters (≈0.92× the Liang et al. 2,685 benchmark on validated science-target spectra); the text correctly notes ~98.7% fall on sky/filler fibers but the framing still risks misinterpretation.
[MINOR] §5 (cosmological applications): The multi-tracer fNL (§5) and NANOGrav (§5.1) sections are included as "secondary demonstrations" yet return null results (no multi-tracer improvement; γ = 2.567 ± 0.382 only marginally consistent with matter-bounce and decisively inconsistent only with idealized circular-orbit SMBHB); these add length to a catalog paper without strengthening the primary deliverable.
[MINOR] Title, abstract, Table 2 and §3 intro: The three-tier structure (validated / separately-released membership addendum / methodological lesson for LAMOST) plus extensive footnotes, "read once" framing, and "reader's guide" caveats make the headline counts (268,519 vs. 377,482) and per-survey contributions difficult to parse without repeated cross-reference; LAMOST (~113k, 98% blue-excess artifact) and excised tiers further complicate interpretation of the "multi-survey" scope.

The evidence supports release of a large, partially validated set of reconstruction outliers from native-trained autoencoders on four surveys (with explicit gates and a recomputable deduplicated count from released lists) but does not support the headline framing of a uniformly validated 268,519-object multi-survey catalog-grade sample of real anomalies.
