VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Sec. II.C (Assumption (d)) and abstract: The bound δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3} on cubic-order transmission through the bounce is derived from a gradient-expansion + single-clock nonlinear ζ-conservation argument (conditional on dressed-metric quantization and no transient entropy modes). While plausible and transparently caveated as the "weakest link," it is not backed by explicit numerical evaluation of bounce-modified mode functions in the Maldacena cubic integrals; a one-sentence acknowledgment that a full numerical verification remains future work would strengthen the conditional robustness claim without changing the forecast.
[MINOR] Sec. I, IV, VII and abstract/Fig. 2/Table V: The conservative ~1.3σ (and ~0.8σ) floor after GR marginalization uses a transferred proxy correlation (ρ = −0.868) from the power-spectrum SDB channel because the per-triangle bispectrum covariance Cov_B of Heinrich et al. is not public. While the paper explicitly labels these as "marginal-sensitivity estimates, not channel-native measurement precisions" and provides a channel-native surrogate check (~2.3σ), the abstract's headline sensitivity range (∼1.3–2.75σ) and any summary figure/table should reiterate this proxy dependence in one additional clause to prevent misinterpretation of the lower edge.
[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. VI.C (including Table III): The Bayes factor BF ≈ 9–14 is presented as quantifying "discriminatory power." The text correctly calls the factors "illustrative" and maps their strong prior dependence (~4–17 across competitor widths and σ_theory), but the abstract should qualify the quoted range as "prior-dependent illustrative BF ≈ 9–14 (under recommended baseline priors)" to align with the careful Sec. VI language and avoid overstating model-selection weight.
[MINOR] General (length and structure): The 37-page manuscript is thorough, with extensive self-validation (10k-sample null-space scan, independent Fisher reproducing Heinrich et al. to 2–11%, three MC ensembles + closed-form Bayes cross-checks, public committed scripts/JSON artifacts). However, several technical subsections (full SVD of the 3×6 constraint matrix, per-coefficient r scatter distributions, detailed Wick-contraction algebra already in Appendix A) could be condensed or moved to a supplementary appendix/public repository to improve readability of the core sensitivity-recast narrative while preserving reproducibility.
[MINOR] Appendix A (and cross-reference in Sec. II.A): The literature correction f_NL = −35/16 (vs. printed −35/8) is rigorously supported by explicit four-vertex symbolic summation at ε = 3/2, per-vertex tables, exact-fraction intermediates (Eqs. A4–A5), identification of the specific spurious −(99/128)∑k_i^3 term, and cross-checks against Cai's own ε-grouped expressions and Li et al.'s general-c_s formula. The statement that Cai’s separately-published −35/8 "is not reproduced by the transcribed printed coefficients" should add one explicit cross-reference (e.g., "Cai Eq. 37 monomials vs. vertex-sum polynomial (A4)") for immediate verification without external scripts.

One sentence: The central claim—that the corrected matter-bounce prediction f_NL = −35/16 yields a testable (∼1.3–2.75σ post-systematics) signature in SPHEREx/MegaMapper multi-tracer bispectrum forecasts after explicit template-mismatch correction (r = 0.84 ± 0.02), with illustrative prior-dependent Bayes factors favoring it over tuned multifield inflation—is supported by the vertex-level re-derivation, independent Fisher validation to 2–11%, transparent proxy accounting, and public reproducibility artifacts, conditional on the listed assumptions (especially cubic transmission) and the use of transferred correlations for the conservative floor.
