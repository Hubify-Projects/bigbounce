VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract and Sec. IV (entire “Four-Route No-Go”): The uniform phrasing “amplitude closure for R1–R3, naturalness closure for R4” conflates three logically distinct categories of argument (deductive Planck/one-loop suppression for R1, ansatz-dependent EFT budgets for R2–R3, and an interpretive explanatory-deficit objection for R4). This blurs the strength of the central claim and should be replaced by a tiered taxonomy (e.g., Tier I deductive, Tier II interpretive, Tier III ansatz-bounded) with explicit cross-references to the 14-barrier catalog in Sec. IX.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV a–b and App. B–C (completeness lemma): The claim that the six operators O[4]1–O[4]6 plus the Fierz projection exhaust all minimal-ECH parity-odd channels at mass dimension ≤6 rests on structural facts F1 (algebraic torsion) and F2 (totally antisymmetric spin current) plus NDA monotonicity. The text does not explicitly rule out other dimension-6 operators (e.g., derivative corrections to the axial current, curvature-torsion cross terms beyond the listed basis, or non-minimal but still minimal-ECH-allowed fermion bilinears). The released script is cited but the logical gap must be closed in the manuscript itself.

[MAJOR] Sec. X (perturbation-transparency result, especially subsections B–E): The claim that “the Holst sector decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion” for canonical scalar matter is presented as a Tier-I theorem, yet the provided text contains only high-level sketches (“Proof (Scalar Sector)”, “Explicit Perturbed-Tetrad Expansion (Term-by-Term)”). For a result advertised as basis-complete and load-bearing for the entire observational programme, the full linearized Einstein–Cartan–Holst equations (including constraint equations and the precise cancellation of torsion contributions) must appear explicitly in the main text or a self-contained appendix.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV F and Sec. XII (R4 naturalness closure): The argument that a spectator-ALP or neutrino-current parity coupling merely “relocates rather than solves the cosmological-constant problem” is philosophically reasonable but is not a dynamical or amplitude-level closure of the same type as R1–R3. It should be re-labeled as a “naturalness/explanatory-scope objection” and its logical status clearly separated from the amplitude no-gos; otherwise the abstract’s parallel construction misleads readers about the uniformity of the four-route result.

[MINOR] Sec. II C 1 and App. B (inflationary dilution bookkeeping and Ntot ≈ 92): The conversion of the single-scale NDA hierarchy into a sensitivity to ΔNtot ≈ 4 is presented as a reparameterization that “does not solve” the CC problem. While correct, the numerical value Ntot ≈ 92 is obtained from two slightly inconsistent routes (on-shell curvature dressing vs. local-operator promotion); the manuscript must state which convention is used for all subsequent structural-tension claims (Sec. XIV D) and propagate any O(1) uncertainty.

[MINOR] Throughout (especially Sec. I, IV, IX, XIV): Repetitive restatement of scope caveats (“channel-level, assumption-conditional, not operator-level theorem”) is protective but fragments readability. Consolidate into a single, prominently placed “Scope and Limitations” box or subsection that is referenced rather than repeated.

One sentence: The central claim—that the four enumerated minimal-ECH dark-energy routes are closed at the channel level under the stated assumptions, accompanied by a perturbation-transparency theorem for canonical scalars and a structural tension with observable matter-bounce fNL—is supported within its explicitly delimited scope by the dimensional/power-counting arguments, Fierz projection lemma, integrated one-loop running, and decoupling analysis, though the R4 component remains interpretive rather than deductive.
