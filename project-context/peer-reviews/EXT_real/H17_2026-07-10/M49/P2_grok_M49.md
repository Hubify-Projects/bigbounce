[MINOR] Abstract and Sec. [MINOR] Sec. IV, VII and Table III: The in-house surrogate Gaussian multi-tracer Fisher (including the explicit 30% b_ϕ prior row and free-b_ϕ limit) is correctly labeled a sensitivity test, but the text does not sufficiently emphasize that these specific significance values cannot substitute for the external Heinrich et al. per-triangle covariance, non-Gaussian covariance, FoG damping, or higher-order bias marginalization; a one-sentence disclaimer reiterating this in Sec. VII would align expectations.
[MINOR] Data/Code Availability section: The github link is provided and scripts are named, but key symbolic verification artifacts (exact sympy session confirming the vertex sum equals −35/16, the precise +(99/128)∑k_i^3 discrepancy term, and the printed-polynomial squeezed reduction to −305/64) are not deposited as supplementary material or a versioned archive; this reduces immediate reproducibility for a literature-correction claim.

The central claim that the exact four-vertex sum for the stated ϵ = 3/2 cubic action yields the unique ordered-basis coefficients (3, 1, −9, 5, −33, 9) and the contraction-phase amplitude f_NL^local = −35/16 is supported by the explicit per-vertex contributions, their symbolic summation to the degree-9 polynomial, the matching ϵ-order-grouped intermediates, and independent agreement with Li et al.’s general-c_s formula at c_s = 1.
