[MAJOR] Appendix B / central four-vertex derivation: The manuscript does not derive the four vertex contributions from the cubic action and in-in integrals; it imports already-evaluated expressions from the source of Ref. [7] and then performs an algebraic re-summation. Because the paper overturns a long-standing published coefficient, it must show enough intermediate analytic work—including Fourier conventions, symmetry factors, boundary/field-redefinition terms, mode-function normalization, and the time-integral evaluation—to establish that the transcribed vertex expressions and their common normalization are correct rather than merely demonstrating that the quoted expressions sum to −35/16.

[MAJOR] Appendix B, Eqs. (B2)–(B5) / historical factor-of-two claim: The manuscript establishes an inconsistency among the printed total polynomial, the listed vertex contributions, and the separately quoted −35/8 value, but it does not identify which published expression is actually erroneous or reconstruct the origin of the discrepancy. The conclusion that −35/8 should be “replaced” is therefore stronger than the demonstrated result; the authors should compare the journal and arXiv source versions, verify all conventions directly from the original derivation, and either provide a complete correction or narrow the claim to an unresolved inconsistency whose vertex-sum branch yields −35/16.

[MAJOR] Table IV / auditability of the claimed certification: The central source-level formulas are typeset in compressed and ambiguous summation notation, with index structures and multiplicities insufficiently explicit for independent verification. Each vertex should be written as a fully defined symmetric polynomial with unambiguous ordered-versus-distinct sums, and the manuscript should display the exact symbolic sum before and after conversion to the basis of Eq. (3), rather than relying on repository scripts as the principal certification.

[MAJOR] Section II B–D / applicability to a physical bounce model: The result proven in the manuscript is only a pre-bounce contraction-phase coefficient. No third-order transfer through the nonsingular bounce is calculated, and the cited construction is verified only linearly. Although this limitation is repeatedly acknowledged, the title, abstract, and observational sections still associate the coefficient with a matter-bounce prediction too directly; the paper must either supply a nonlinear transfer calculation for a specified completion or consistently frame the result as a contraction-phase bispectrum that may not survive the bounce unchanged.

[MAJOR] Sections III–VII / observational mapping: The SPHEREx discussion combines a scalar uncertainty imported from Ref. [1], a flat-grid template projection, and several unpublished surrogate Fisher calculations with different observables, covariances, and nuisance sets. These quantities are not components of a single forecast and cannot validate one another. The surrogate likelihood, binning, tracer model, survey volume, mode cuts, covariance, derivatives, and priors must be specified sufficiently for reproduction, or the numerical significance ladder should be removed from the main scientific claims.
