(2) ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Abstract and Sec. IV (and Table III): Specific numerical significances (2.63σ arithmetic map; 3.5σ/3.1σ/2.3σ/0.4σ channel-native ladder) are reported from an explicitly surrogate Gaussian multi-tracer covariance that the text itself states "does not replace the unpublished external per-triangle covariance" and is only a "sensitivity test"; placing these headline-style numbers in the abstract and tables risks reader misinterpretation as a realistic forecast or detection claim despite the repeated "illustrative conditional" disclaimers.
[MAJOR] Appendix B and Sec. II A: The claim that the exact four-vertex sum yields f_NL = −35/16 (and that Cai et al.’s −35/8 is "unreproduced") is internally consistent with the provided tables, order-grouped intermediates, and Li et al. cross-check, but the paper must explicitly state whether the original Cai et al. (2009) −35/8 arose from a missed overall factor in the in-in evaluation, a different Wick-convention normalization, or solely from the later polynomial transcription error; without this, the community correction remains incomplete.
[MINOR] Sec. II C and assumption (d): The load-bearing "faithful cubic transmission through the nonsingular transition" is correctly caveated as unverified beyond linear order, but the text should add one sentence quantifying what a minimal third-order numerical check would require (e.g., evolution of the cubic action across the Wilson–Ewing bounce with the same effective sound speed).
[MINOR] Throughout (especially Sec. IV B and Eq. (9)): The adopted r = 0.84 ± 0.02 envelope is justified as a weighting-scheme range, but the flat-grid exact value 0.8354 and the survey-weighted reff ≈ 0.99 are presented without a clear statement that they probe different inner products; readers may conflate the two recovery metrics.
[MINOR] Presentation: Multiple embedded screenshot figures and the long Appendix B walkthrough are useful for verification but should be converted to proper vector graphics and streamlined; the reproducibility note in Appendix A is welcome but belongs in a short Data Availability paragraph rather than a dedicated appendix.
