(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Section VIII C / focal estimand: The manuscript calls the headline analysis “catalog-native,” but only the eligible parent and OUT=0 quality cut are catalog-native; the focal void arm is still defined by an author-computed point-in-sphere union of VoidFinder holes rather than a released per-galaxy VoidFinder membership. This distinction materially affects the interpretation and must be reflected consistently in the title, abstract, methods, and conclusions.

[MAJOR] Sections V B, VI A, and XV / post-hoc hierarchy: The focal estimator was selected after review and after inspection of the results. Although this is disclosed, the paper still gives the selected analysis privileged inferential status and repeatedly contrasts it with a large tree of alternative estimators. The manuscript needs a clearer account of why this particular estimator was selected, a specification-curve or multiverse summary of all defensible parent, membership, adjustment, and covariance choices, and substantially weaker claims about robustness.

[MAJOR] Section VI A / covariance estimation: The headline uncertainty is based on only 50 occupied NSIDE=4 sky clusters while estimating a 78-parameter logistic model. The ordinary finite-sample cluster correction quoted in Eq. (5) is not sufficient to establish reliable coverage with few, highly unequal spatial clusters. A wild cluster bootstrap, randomization-based spatial inference, or a systematic comparison across several physically motivated clustering scales is required before the reported 95% interval can be treated as dependable.

[MAJOR] Section VI A / model specification: The focal logistic standardization uses numerous spline and categorical terms, but no diagnostics are provided for positivity, leverage, separation, influential clusters, functional-form sensitivity, or extrapolation of the average marginal contrast. Overlap weighting balances the measured covariates, but agreement between two analyses using the same measured variables does not establish robustness to model misspecification or residual selection bias.

[MAJOR] Sections VIII B–C / selection function: Neither the focal released-parent analysis nor the author-defined footprint control reconstructs the DESIVAST/BGS angular and radial selection function. The manuscript acknowledges that the VAC mask and randoms are unavailable, yet the environment contrast may remain confounded by imaging depth, targeting completeness, fiber assignment, and redshift-dependent selection. A stronger matched-control construction using available DESI random catalogs, or a quantitatively justified bound on residual imbalance, is needed.

[MAJOR] Appendix A and Section XIII / label validity: The environmental contrast is measured with classifier labels whose conservative binary accuracy is reported as 69.91%. The void-stratified human-label validation has only 933 void objects and admits directional-error differences much larger than the headline contrast. Consequently, the analysis cannot exclude environment-dependent differential misclassification at the scale being measured. This limitation should be elevated to the abstract and conclusions and prevents interpreting the result as a strong null test of physical chirality.

[MAJOR] Appendix C / reproducibility and submission readiness: The manuscript states that the exact release tag, archival DOI, several linked artifacts, and the final Paper IV label/provenance release do not yet exist or require verification. A PRD submission must provide an immutable, publicly accessible snapshot that reproduces the focal row set, integer counts, fitted model, covariance calculation, tables, and figures. The current local commit and pending links are insufficient.

[MAJOR] Section II and Appendix A / dependence on unpublished Paper IV: The per-galaxy labels, classifier construction, training provenance, and key validation claims originate in an unpublished companion manuscript. Reproducing a narrative summary in an appendix does not replace independent peer-reviewable documentation, especially given the use of pseudo-labels and the changing Paper IV harmonic analysis. Paper IV should be publicly available and frozen, or the relevant classifier methods and validations must be incorporated fully into this manuscript.

[MAJOR] Sections IV, VII, IX, and X / secondary T-Web analysis: The canonical T-Web field is severely contaminated by radial and angular selection effects; the randoms-weighted rebuild changes class labels for most matched spirals and alters the void volume fraction by a factor of about 23. This means the original T-Web classes are not a credible physical environment reconstruction. The extensive canonical T-Web results should be removed from the evidentiary chain or replaced by a properly completeness-weighted analysis throughout.

[MINOR] Section VI E / causal language: Statements that boundary “misclassification leakage” or BGS imaging-leg systematics explain particular residuals are stronger than the evidence presented. The manuscript itself concedes that no injection-recovery mock reproduces the observed sign pattern. These passages should be rewritten as hypotheses rather than demonstrated mechanisms.

[MINOR] Section XII C / comparison with Shamir 2022: The assertion that the analysis leaves “no room” for a 2–4 percentage-point environmental signal is not adequately supported because the focal 95% interval is roughly −0.54 to +0.79 percentage points in classifier-label space, label attenuation is substantial, and environment-dependent misclassification is weakly constrained. The comparison should be expressed only in terms of the observed classifier-label estimand.

[MINOR] Presentation throughout: The manuscript is excessively long and repetitive for a narrowly scoped exploratory null test. Many secondary diagnostics, repeated caveats, artifact-level provenance details, and duplicated interpretations should be moved to supplementary material. The main text should center on the focal sample definition, estimand, adjustment, spatial inference, validation, and limitations.

(3) The central claim of a catalog-specific non-detection in classifier-labelled CW fraction is provisionally supported by the reported calculations, but the strength and reliability of that claim remain limited by post-hoc estimator selection, uncertain spatial inference, incomplete selection-function control, classifier-label dependence, and unavailable immutable reproducibility artifacts.
