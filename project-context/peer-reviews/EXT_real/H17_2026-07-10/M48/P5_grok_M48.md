VERDICT: MINOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MINOR] Abstract and §I (Introduction): The focal result is explicitly post-hoc (hierarchy changed after review and data inspection; §V B) and exploratory/not preregistered, yet the abstract presents the specific ∆fCW = +0.00125636, SE, CI, and p = 0.71277 without sufficiently foregrounding that this is a descriptive catalog-native estimate rather than a confirmatory test. This risks overstating interpretability of the p-value.
[MINOR] §V B and §VI A: The switch to the released GALZONE TARGET + OUT=0 parent (N=145,766) as focal (over the earlier author-constructed any-hole VoidFinder sample) is disclosed but lacks a clear, even retrospective, justification tied to estimand definition or robustness; the sensitivity variants in Table IV and §VIII are numerous but not pre-specified, weakening the claim that the focal path is privileged.
[MINOR] §VI B, §VII (Phase 2 sweep), and §VIII: The T-Web void bin (n=428) is acknowledged as noise-dominated and survey-shell contaminated, and the n=6 low-z purity cross-check vs. DESIVAST is too small to be informative (one-sided 95% upper bound ~39%). These sections contain excessive secondary decompositions (density quartiles, redshift quartiles, tracer-program splits) whose null verdicts are correctly reported but dilute focus on the headline DESIVAST-anchored result.
[MINOR] §II, §V, and §VIII G: Propagation of the Paper IV monopole uncertainty into σpred and σvs monopole is done correctly and the internal P5 matched-sample fP5CW = 0.49719 corroborates it, but the paper does not quantify the resulting loss of power to detect a hypothetical environment-dependent signal of size comparable to the observed SE (~0.0034); a short power statement would strengthen the non-detection claim.
[MINOR] Overall manuscript: The paper is thorough and transparent (permutation LEE corrections, cluster-sandwich covariance, fixed-geometry RSD perturbations, etc.), but its length (~39 pp) and proliferation of sensitivity tables/figures for a clean null result (p=0.71 on the focal contrast) reduce readability; several Phase-2 and within-class stratifications could be condensed to appendix without loss of rigor.

The central claim—that classifier-labelled spiral chirality shows no statistically significant difference between the released DESIVAST void and non-void environments in the focal catalog-native sample after covariate standardization—is supported by the reported contrast, interval, p-value, and consistent sensitivities across paths.
