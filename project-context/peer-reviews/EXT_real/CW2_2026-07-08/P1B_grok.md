## RAW RESPONSE

VERDICT: MAJOR REVISIONS

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Abstract, Sec. I (Introduction), and Sec. VII (Conclusions): The manuscript repeatedly and explicitly frames its contribution as a "reproducibility and null-consistency note" whose "scope, stated up front" is that "none of the three analyses below implements or tests a torsion-modified Boltzmann/theory module, and none verifies the ECH spin-torsion sector." While this scoping is scrupulously honest, it directly undermines the case for a standalone PRD research article: the MCMC is a stock-CAMB generic-radiation proxy with no new theory module, the NaMaster exercise is a synthetic-sky pipeline validation without galactic foregrounds or the β–α degeneracy, and the ALP section is a standard GR+ALP parameter scan on a published summary likelihood that the text itself states is "not a distinctive ECH prediction." PRD criteria require demonstrated advances in physical understanding or broadly applicable methods; this content is better suited as a consolidated appendix or supplementary technical note to Paper I(a) [1] rather than an independent article.
[MAJOR] Sec. VI (Cosmic Birefringence: Spectator ALP Consistency Check): The "prior-predictive accommodation cost" (e.g., 11.6% of draws within 1σ of βobs at fixed Caγ = 8) and the spectator-subset readouts in Table IV rest on a Gaussian summary likelihood centered on the Eskilt & Komatsu published value β = 0.342° ± 0.094° rather than a re-analysis of the underlying EB spectra or the full joint covariance that includes shared calibration systematics. This choice makes the quoted fractions prior-dependent and approximation-dependent; the claim of a "genuine (if not parameter-free) accommodation" therefore overstates the robustness of the result relative to what a full likelihood analysis would support.
[MINOR] Sec. III A: The first-principles derivation of the torsion-induced four-fermion contribution yielding ∆N_eff^(ECH) ∼ 10^{-44} at BBN correctly identifies the leading (T/M_Pl)^2 scaling from the dimension-6 contact operator, but drops unspecified O(1) coefficients in the thermal average ⟨(ψ̄ γ_5 γ^μ ψ)^2⟩_T ∼ n_f^2 and labels a full finite-T NJL computation "outside the scope." For a result presented as "a genuine result of this paper rather than one imported from Paper I(a)," an explicit statement of the dropped prefactors or a direct citation to the standard Hehl–Datta–Mercuri finite-temperature treatment would remove any residual ambiguity without altering the conclusion.
[MINOR] Sec. IV and abstract: The NaMaster validation quantifies a pipeline-recovery bias |∆β̂| ≤ 0.040° on foreground-free synthetic skies and correctly notes that this "is not a real-sky bias bound." However, the text still carries the 0.040° figure forward as "the observed NaMaster pipeline bias" and reports template-fit SNRs (20.32, 25.71) in a manner that requires careful reading of scope notes to avoid conflation with the published 2.7–2.9σ sky detections. A single dedicated paragraph that cleanly separates "synthetic deconvolution validation figure" from "real-sky systematic floor" would prevent misinterpretation.
[MINOR] Sec. V B, Table II, and associated footnotes: The detailed reconciliation of burn-in fractions (20% vs. 30%), raw vs. post-burn-in counts (309,189 raw, 216,432 post-burn-in), GetDist vs. paper conventions, and per-chain stratification is technically conscientious but interrupts the physics narrative with multi-paragraph footnotes. These details belong in a concise "Convergence diagnostics" paragraph in Sec. V or entirely in Appendix A.
[MINOR] Data and Code Availability section: The reproducibility manifest references internal pod paths, specific commit-era directories (reproducibility/p1_namaster_500mc/, etc.), and a Hugging Face dataset without stable DOIs or tagged public releases. Permanent archival (Zenodo or equivalent) with citable identifiers for chains, scripts, and the ALP ODE grid must be completed before acceptance to satisfy PRD
