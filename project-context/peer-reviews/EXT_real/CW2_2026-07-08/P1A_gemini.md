## RAW RESPONSE

VERDICT: REJECT

ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Perturbation Transparency (Sec. X): The central "Tier I" rigorous result states that minimal ECH gravity is perturbation-transparent for canonical scalar matter because the spin density is zero, causing torsion to vanish, which in turn decouples the Holst sector. This is a well-known, trivial consequence of minimally coupled scalar fields in Einstein-Cartan-type theories. Elevating this to a novel "Perturbation Transparency Theorem" significantly overstates the physical significance of a standard literature result.

[MAJOR] Route 4 Closure (Sec. IV F): The manuscript claims to "close" the parity-odd CMB coupling channel (Route 4), yet explicitly admits this closure is not based on an amplitude mismatch or physical exclusion. Instead, it relies entirely on an "explanatory-deficit / cosmological-constant fine-tuning objection", acknowledging that at a fitted coupling, the model successfully reproduces both the observed birefringence angle β_obs and the dark energy density ρ_Λ. A subjective philosophical naturalness argument regarding the parameter m_θ ~ H_0 does not constitute a rigorous structural closure suitable for publication in Physical Review D.

[MAJOR] Dependence on Phenomenological Ansätze and NDA (Sec. IV, Appendix B): The closures of Routes 2 and 3 depend heavily on loosely justified scaling ansätze and order-of-magnitude estimates rather than controlled loop calculations or UV-complete flows. Furthermore, the "dimensional no-go" identifying the parity-odd operator as off-shell dimension +1 and forcing its natural density to M_Pl^4 is a standard application of effective field theory (EFT) naive dimensional analysis (NDA), not a novel theoretical or geometric obstruction.

[MAJOR] Presentation and "Mechanism-Class Constraint" Bloat (Sec. IX): The text is highly defensive, overly verbose, and structurally cluttered with meta-commentary detailing exactly what the paper does and does not claim (e.g., categorizing claims into "Tier I, II, III" evidentiary levels). The presentation of "14 historical catalog entries" of constraints is repetitious and artificially inflates the paper's scope; many "barriers" overlap conceptually, rely on identical scaling ansätze, or are heuristic arguments (e.g., Liouville conservation) rather than distinct, rigorous mathematical field-theoretic results.

[MINOR] Reliance on Unpublished Companions (Sec. I, Table II): While the author states the theoretical closures do not logically depend on the companion papers, an excessive amount of the manuscript (including entire tables and sections, e.g., Sec. III, V, Table II) is dedicated to discussing MCMC values, Fisher forecasts, and galaxy spin classifier pipelines derived in unreviewed, concurrently posted companions. This phenomenological padding distracts from the theoretical core of the paper and should be excised.

The central claim of a structural channel-level closure is insufficiently supported, as it relies primarily on standard dimensional analysis, order-of-magnitude ansätze, and subjective naturalness objections rather than rigorous, novel field-theoretic derivations.
