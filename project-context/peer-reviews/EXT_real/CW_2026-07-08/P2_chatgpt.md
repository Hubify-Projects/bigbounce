(1) VERDICT: REJECT

(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Appendix A / Cai–Li factor-of-two claim: the claimed resolution of the literature discrepancy is not presented as a self-contained derivation sufficient for PRD; the manuscript simultaneously states that the shared printed Cai/Li polynomial yields −35/8 while Li’s closed-form formula gives −35/16, but does not fully reconcile the polynomial, normalization, time-ordering, and vertex-sum algebra in a way that can be verified from the text alone. The key “spurious +(99/128)∑k_i^3” term is also described inconsistently: once as not by itself producing the doubling and elsewhere as exactly what pushes −35/16 to −35/8.

[MAJOR] Sec. II C / faithful cubic-order transmission through the bounce: the paper treats third-order bispectrum transmission as “closed” by single-clock superhorizon conservation and degree-of-freedom counting, but no actual third-order perturbation calculation through the Wilson-Ewing/LQC bounce is performed. Linear conservation plus absence of an extra scalar degree of freedom is not, by itself, a derivation of the cubic transfer function or of the claimed δf_NL ≲ 10^−3 bound.

[MAJOR] Secs. III–IV / SPHEREx forecast: the headline 1.3–2.75σ result is not an independent forecast but a post-hoc recast of a single external local-template Fisher number, with template mismatch, GR projection, b_φ, photo-z, and non-local-tail effects handled by heuristic rescalings rather than a joint multi-tracer bispectrum Fisher. The manuscript acknowledges this, but the headline still presents the result more strongly than the calculation warrants.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV / “independent Fisher” validation: the in-house Fisher calculation does not validate the full headline forecast because it uses simplified ingredients—real-space monopole, leading Gaussian covariance, fixed b₂/b_s², no full RSD multipoles, and no channel-native GR/b_φ marginalization—while the claimed agreement with Heinrich et al. is only at the level of a local-template baseline. It cannot establish the quoted post-systematic significance.

[MAJOR] Sec. VII / GR-projection treatment: the GR degradation is not computed with the SPHEREx bispectrum covariance; instead, the manuscript imports a proxy correlation from a different SDB Fisher and a shape-overlap estimate, then brackets the result. This is insufficient for a quantitative PRD forecast, especially because the lower endpoint of the headline range is controlled by this treatment.

[MAJOR] Sec. VI / Bayesian comparison: the Bayes factors are driven by assumed prior widths and by a mock detection placed at the model prediction. They therefore quantify an illustrative prior-volume effect, not evidence expected from SPHEREx data. The caveats are extensive, but the abstract-level BF ≈ 9–14 headline remains too strong for the calculation performed.

[MAJOR] Sec. II / polynomial-coefficient null space: the manuscript bases template-overlap robustness on an internally chosen symmetrized six-coefficient representation that is explicitly basis- and measure-dependent. Since the coefficient ambiguity is introduced by the author’s recompilation rather than by Cai et al.’s derivation, propagating it as a physical uncertainty is not justified without a derivation of the correct full shape in a fixed convention.

[MINOR] Secs. IV–IX / presentation: the manuscript is excessively long, repetitive, and filled with defensive caveats, scope summaries, and “reviewer response” prose. It should be rewritten as a concise physics paper with derivations, assumptions, and forecast inputs cleanly separated.

[MINOR] Data and Code Availability: many central claims depend on named scripts and JSON artifacts, but the manuscript does not include enough numerical detail for independent verification without external code. For a theory/forecast paper, the decisive algebra and Fisher inputs must be reproducible from the paper or an attached, frozen archive.

[MINOR] Sec. IX / auxiliary birefringence discussion: the cosmic-birefringence paragraph is not used in the forecast and is only qualitatively connected to the matter-bounce prediction; it distracts from the central result and should be removed unless quantitatively integrated.

(3) The central claim is not supported at PRD publication standard because the corrected f_NL value, cubic-order bounce transmission, and SPHEREx significance are all conditional on unverified or heuristic steps rather than a complete derivation and joint forecast.
