(2) ISSUES:

[MAJOR] Sections II, XIII, and Appendix A / dependence on Paper IV labels: the central test rests on per-galaxy CW/CCW labels from a concurrently submitted companion paper with placeholder arXiv metadata, and the manuscript itself states acceptance should be conditional on Paper IV; the appendix summary is not a substitute for a refereed validation of label provenance, and no environment-stratified human-label confusion matrix is provided to show that label errors cannot vary with void/non-void environment. 

cw_P5

[MAJOR] Appendix A and Sections VIII–XII / quoted 0.5–0.6 pp bound: the classifier’s quoted binary accuracy floor is only 69.91%, so any true chirality-environment signal would be diluted by classification noise; the manuscript does not propagate this dilution into the claimed physical upper bound, nor does it demonstrate that CW/CCW error rates are environment-independent. 

cw_P5

[MAJOR] Section V.B / post-hoc primary path: the DESIVAST path is explicitly designated primary after inspecting an analysis tree of several dozen related tests, and a Bonferroni-5 correction over only the five DESIVAST estimators is insufficient to justify the strongest “primary” exclusion bound as though it were prospectively specified. 

cw_P5

[MAJOR] Sections VIII.B–VIII.E / DESIVAST void membership definition: the primary VoidFinder result uses the union of 101,863 hole spheres as a permissive proxy rather than an official DESIVAST galaxy-membership definition, while the stricter maximal-sphere rerun shifts the contrast by about 0.6 pp, comparable to the claimed effective bound; this makes the numerical bound membership-definition-limited, not purely statistics-limited. 

cw_P5

[MAJOR] Sections IV, IX.A, XII, and XIII / T-Web analysis status: the manuscript repeatedly shows that the T-Web environment labels are strongly affected by redshift-shell and survey-selection artifacts, including a 23× collapse of the void volume fraction under a randoms-weighted rebuild and reassignment of most matched galaxies; T-Web should be removed from headline support and presented only as a failed diagnostic, not as convergent cosmic-web evidence. 

cw_P5

[MINOR] Sections XII.B, XV, and Appendix B / theoretical motivation: the manuscript states that no published bounce or inflation model predicts the tested environment-dependent chirality signal, so the speculative EFT mapping and bounce-model language should be sharply de-emphasized or moved out of the main PRD framing. 

cw_P5

[MINOR] Data availability / archival status: several reproducibility claims depend on a future DOI-minted archive and placeholder arXiv identifiers; these must be replaced with immutable, accessible records before publication.
