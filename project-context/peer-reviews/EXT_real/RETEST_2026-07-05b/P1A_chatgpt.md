[MAJOR] Abstract / Sec. I / Sec. IV "Scope": the manuscript is internally contradictory about the scope of the claimed closure. It states both that Jackiw–Pi R∧R̃ and the parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner are "now closed explicitly," and also that these operators are "omitted," "left to a follow-up operator-basis analysis," or that no operator-level no-go is claimed. [MAJOR] Eq. (1), Sec. II A 1: the "fundamental" Einstein–Cartan–Holst action is not a well-defined first-order action as written. It includes a T_abc T^abc term inside the action while later declaring that term to be only an on-shell Hehl–Datta shorthand "not varied independently." A term placed in the action contributes to the variational principle unless the action is rewritten after integrating out torsion; the manuscript cannot simultaneously use it as part of the fundamental action and not vary it.

[MAJOR] Sec. II A 2 / Appendix B, Eq. (6): the parity-odd operator used for the dark-energy mapping is dimensionally invalid as a local Lagrangian density, and the manuscript turns this defect into a "single-scale NDA no-go." A missing M_Pl^2-type gravitational normalization or a properly specified EFT coefficient is not evidence for a no-go; it is evidence that the operator has not been correctly formulated. The subsequent claims ρ_Λ^ECH ∼ M_Pl^4, N_tot ≈ 92, and the "dimension +1 → +4" argument are therefore not derived results.

[MAJOR] Sec. IV D, Route 2: the one-loop Holst-sector amplitude estimate is not derived from the cited literature and does not establish a calculable birefringence amplitude. The operator ∂_μ ϑ_NY J_5^μ contains no photon field; the asserted connection to FF̃ through the anomaly is not worked out with a specified fermion sector, background, matching scale, or line-of-sight calculation. The ratio in Eq. (16) is therefore an order-of-magnitude ansatz, not a closure of a physical route.

That is a naturalness criticism of an added ALP model, not a no-go for minimal ECH.
