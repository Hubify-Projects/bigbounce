(2009) (who found fNLlocal​=−35/8) and Li et al. (2016) (who found fNLlocal​=−35/16). The analysis goes deep into systematic vulnerability, tracking scale-dependent bias fragilities, relativistic projection alignments, and galaxy bias parameter uncertainties (bϕ​).1.
