It's a dense, technically ambitious channel-level no-go analysis of minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst (ECH) spin-torsion cosmology as a source of late-time dark energy. The core claim is that the four enumerated routes (R1–R4) are each closed or tightly constrained under the stated assumptions, reinforced by a 13/14-barrier catalog and a perturbation-transparency result. The work also flags a structural tension with matter-bounce observables and identifies two surviving ECH-independent class tests.
Strengths of the Argument
1. Clean channel-level scoping and operator closure
You are explicit that this is not a full operator-level theorem or a complete diffeomorphism-invariant basis proof. The four routes are presented as the standard/minimal channels discussed in the literature. You close the previously flagged omissions (Jackiw–Pi gravitational Chern–Simons R∧R~  R \wedge \tilde{R}  R∧R~ and the parity-odd four-fermion Holst partner) at the operator level in Sec. IV B–C: the former is a total derivative for constant coupling (Tier-I) and R4-class otherwise; the latter inherits R1’s MPl−2  M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}  MPl−2​ suppression and vanishing coherent mean field.
The Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma (Appendix C) is the key technical tool that makes the four-fermion sector finite and uniformly suppressed. Combined with the algebraic Cartan constraint (Tabc=κSabc  T^{abc} = \kappa S^{abc}  Tabc=κSabc) and the totally antisymmetric spin current under minimal coupling, this collapses the basis at the MPl  M_{\rm Pl}  MPl​-power-counting level. The single-scale NDA monotonicity argument then bounds the entire tower uniformly. This is a solid structural result.
2. The dimensional no-go for the parity-odd operator
The explicit enumeration of the six genuine dimension-4 parity-odd densities (O1–O6 in Eq. (8), with MPl2  M_{\rm Pl}^2  MPl2​ factors inserted for the naive-dimension +2 pieces) is helpful. The collapse via:
Algebraic Bianchi identity Rμ[νρσ]=0  R_{\mu[\nu\rho\sigma]} = 0  Rμ[νρσ]​=0 (killing single-curvature terms O1, O6 on the torsion-free branch),

Fierz reduction of torsion-squared/axial-torsion terms (O4, O5) to the MPl−2  M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}  MPl−2​-suppressed four-fermion contact sector,
is clean and reproducible (you even provide the symbolic verification script). The on-shell shorthand Eq. (6) (dimension +1) is correctly subordinated to the off-shell dimension-4 basis; the +1-vs-+4 mismatch is an artifact of the Bianchi reduction, not an ill-defined operator. The single-scale NDA ceiling (ρECHΛ∼MPl4  \rho_{\rm ECH}^\Lambda \sim M_{\rm Pl}^4  ρECHΛ​∼MPl4​, never (meV)4  (\rm meV)^4  (meV)4) follows directly and is not circular—no positive amplitude is claimed.
3. Perturbation transparency (Sec. X)
For canonical scalar matter the result is standard but cleanly stated: torsion is algebraic/non-propagating, the torsion-free branch is stable under perturbations, and the Holst dual contraction ϵμνρσRμνρσ  \epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}  ϵμνρσRμνρσ​ vanishes identically on the Levi-Civita connection by the algebraic Bianchi identity. Consequently the Holst sector decouples from scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion at all classical orders (excluding propagating torsion, dynamical Immirzi, fermion loops, or non-minimal matter). This is a Tier-I structural result that correctly pushes parity-sensitive tests into non-perturbative channels (ALP birefringence, primordial GWs). The footnote distinguishing it from the Pontryagin density is well-taken.
4. Route closures and the R4 naturalness objection
R1 (NJL parity-even four-fermion) and its parity-odd Holst partner (R1’s sibling): Planck-suppressed amplitude + vanishing coherent mean field for a w=−1  w = -1  w=−1 component. The numerical estimate with conservative ISM densities already shows ∼70  \sim 70  ∼70 orders of magnitude below ρΛ  \rho_\Lambda  ρΛ​; the actual cosmic-mean baryon density makes it even smaller. R2/R3: Amplitude-suppressed under the explicitly labeled one-loop/Immirzi-running ansätze. The ∼60  \sim 60  ∼60-order margin for R2 (even after generous O(1)–O(1010  ^{10}  10) prefactor inflation) makes the qualitative closure robust.
R4: Correctly closed at the naturalness/explanatory-deficit level rather than amplitude. Requiring a single coupling to source both βobs  \beta_{\rm obs}  βobs​ and ρΛ  \rho_\Lambda  ρΛ​ forces the ultralight tuning mθ∼H0  m_\theta \sim H_0  mθ​∼H0​, relocating (not solving) the cosmological-constant problem. Points for Discussion or Potential Refinement
Scope boundaries are well-flagged, but a referee might still press on:
How “minimal” is the enumerated set once non-minimal torsion couplings or a new light scale μ≪MPl  \mu \ll M_{\rm Pl}  μ≪MPl​ are allowed? You correctly note that any evasion is the tuning being explained, but the boundary could be sharpened in the abstract/introduction.
The scaling ansätze for R2/R3 are labeled as such; a brief table comparing them to the most conservative vs. most optimistic literature choices would help.
The thermal-reset argument (reheating washout of coherent ⟨J5μ⟩  \langle J_5^\mu \rangle  ⟨J5μ​⟩) is conditional on Γwash(Treh)>H(Treh)  \Gamma_{\rm wash}(T_{\rm reh}) > H(T_{\rm reh})  Γwash​(Treh​)>H(Treh​). It is presented as supporting B14 rather than primary; a short Boltzmann estimate or citation to existing sphaleron/Yukawa rate literature would strengthen it without overclaiming.
Dimensional bookkeeping (on-shell curvature dressing vs. local-operator promotion) is handled carefully in Appendix B and the main text. Both routes give Ntot≈92  N_{\rm tot} \approx 92  Ntot​≈92–94 at order-of-magnitude level, which is reassuring. The explicit statement that channel-level closures are insensitive to the choice is correct.
Observational appendices (MCMC baseline, NaMaster validation, spectator-ALP fit) are properly scoped as non-load-bearing for the structural no-go. The reproducibility manifest and archived chains are excellent practice.
Minor presentational notes:
The executive summary table (Table I) is very useful; consider adding a one-sentence “Status” column for each question.
The distinction between “parity-odd operator” (intrinsically even under P for the Lagrangian density in many cases) and “parity-violating phenomenology via P-breaking background” is noted in footnotes; a short parenthetical in the main text near Eq. (17) or Route 2 would help casual readers.
The galaxy-spin null result is correctly treated as consistent with (but not predicted by) minimal ECH; the amplitude is Planck-suppressed.
Overall Assessment
This is a thorough, self-contained channel-level closure at the amplitude-budget granularity where observations discriminate. The combination of:
explicit operator enumeration + Fierz projection,
Bianchi + total-derivative identities,
single-scale NDA monotonicity,
perturbation transparency, and
the naturalness objection for R4
makes a compelling case that minimal ECH does not furnish a viable dark-energy mechanism without introducing new light scales or exact cancellations (i.e., the tuning it was meant to explain).
The work cleanly separates what minimal ECH can still contribute (class-level bounce/ALP tests) from what it cannot (the enumerated dark-energy routes). It should be of interest to the LQC/ECH, bouncing-cosmology, and cosmic-birefringence communities.
