This is a detailed theoretical + forecasting paper by independent researcher Houston Golden. It has two main parts:
1. Resolution of the Cai–Li factor-of-two discrepancy (central theoretical result)
A matter-dominated contracting phase (ϵ = 3/2) produces a minimally parameterized local-type primordial non-Gaussianity with squeezed-limit value
f_local_NL = −35/16 = −2.1875 (corrected central value).
This is opposite in sign and ~146× larger in amplitude than the single-field slow-roll consistency-relation value (~ +0.015 at n_s ≈ 0.9649).
Key fix: Cai et al. (their Eq. 37 polynomial) printed −35/8; Li et al. (general-c_s formula at c_s = 1) gave −35/16.
Direct vertex-by-vertex re-summation of Cai et al.’s own four cubic-action operators (field redefinition, ζζ̇², ζ̇∂ζ∂χ, ζ(∂_i∂_jχ)²) at ϵ = 3/2 in the squeezed limit k_1 ≪ k_2 = k_3 recovers exactly −35/16 (plus O(k_1²/k²) correction).
This matches Li et al.’s independent result and Cai et al.’s own ϵ-ordered intermediate expressions.
The printed −35/8 arises from one identifiable spurious +(99/128) Σ k_i³ local-shaped term that appears only in the final polynomial collapse (Eq. 37), not in the vertex sums or intermediates.
The prediction holds in the scalar-only Wilson–Ewing class under six explicit assumptions (exact matter domination during contraction, standard GR, Bunch–Davies vacuum, faithful cubic-order transmission through the bounce, no prolonged post-bounce inflation, negligible fermion-sourced torsion).
Residual model dependence is small: O(ϵ) correction ~0.6–8% (shifts f_NL into [−2.175, −2.01]); underdetermined polynomial coefficients (c_1…c_6) produce ~±13% scatter in template overlap but leave shape cosine r_cos > 0.97 and benchmark values exact.
2. Sensitivity recast for SPHEREx and MegaMapper (observational forecast)
Uses published Heinrich et al. (2024) multi-tracer galaxy-bispectrum forecast baseline σ(f_local_NL) ≈ 0.7 (SPHEREx).
Template mismatch quantified explicitly (bounce shape vs. pure local template):
Noise-weighted overlap r = 0.84 ± 0.02 (range 0.829–0.876 across schemes; CMB-Fisher signal-only r = 0.876).
Validated by ℓ-space Fisher, 200 injection-recovery Monte Carlos, and 10,000-sample null-space scan of polynomial coefficients (r_cos > 0.97).
SPHEREx headline ranges (at corrected central |f_NL| = 2.1875):
Optimistic (bispectrum-only, CMB-Fisher r, σ_GR = 0): 2.6–2.75σ.
Realistic post-systematic-budget (noise-weighted r + additive-quadrature systematics: mismatch, ϵ-correction, photo-z, b_ϕ marginalization, GR projections): 1.3–2.75σ (conservative floor ~1.3–1.4σ when GR + widened b_ϕ included).
Independent in-house tree-level multi-tracer bispectrum Fisher (built from scratch on the same Doré et al. SPHEREx survey table, Scoccimarro kernel, Gaussian covariance) reproduces Heinrich baseline to 2–11% and yields reff ≈ 0.99 for the bounce template (because squeezed configurations that dominate SPHEREx weight are where the templates coincide). This validates the recast and shows r = 0.84 is deliberately conservative.
Bayes factor (closed-form analytic + three independent 10^5-realization Monte Carlo ensembles): detection near −35/16 favors bounce over tuned multifield competitors at BF ≈ 9–14 (recommended σ_theory = 1.0 Gaussian bounce prior; broad [−15, +15] competitor) up to ~10–17 at delta-prior maximum. Curvaton-natural [−5, +5] narrows it to ~4–7. Strongly disfavors single-field slow-roll (parameter-free prediction f_NL ≈ +0.015).
MegaMapper (illustrative, not funded): ideal σ(f_NL) ≈ 0.5 → 1.5–3.5σ envelope (design uncertainty dominant; GR projections larger at z = 2–5).
Load-bearing caveat (⋆): entire forecast conditional on faithful third-order bispectrum transmission through the bounce. Verified at linear order; at cubic order bounded by single-clock nonlinear superhorizon ζ-conservation (Lyth–Malik–Sasaki / Maldacena-δN) to δf_NL ≲ 10^{-3} (negligible vs. σ ≈ 0.7). Wilson–Ewing evades Quintin et al. bounce no-go.
Gauge vs. physical frame: Forecast uses gauge-frame f_NL (what local-template estimators measure). Physical (CFC) frame sends single-field slow-roll f_NL → 0 while bounce value is robust; a non-zero local detection would disfavor single-field attractor (discriminator, not on-sky observable).
Overall takeaway: The corrected −35/16 sets a real, near-term sensitivity target. SPHEREx can reach ~2.6–2.75σ (optimistic) or ~1.3–2.75σ (realistic post-budget) — preserving strong discrimination vs. single-field inflation while the erroneous −35/8 would have produced an overstated ≥5σ headline. The paper is thorough: source-level audit of the f_NL prediction, explicit template-mismatch/null-space quantification, itemized systematic budget, and cross-validated Bayesian comparison.
