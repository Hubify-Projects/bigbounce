[MAJOR] II.B (Training Labels) and III.B (Declared Analysis Hierarchy): 66.5% of the 25,790 source training images are CE-ResNet pseudo-labels. The template-regression and cross-spectrum anchors in Appendix D bound inherited survey-correlated structure but do not fully close the channel for the primary catalog; the result therefore remains partially conditional on the CE-ResNet label distribution.

[MAJOR] IV.D (Monopole+Mask Leakage Generative Null) and Appendix D (eight-anchor battery): Imaging + morphology forward modeling accounts for only ~53% of the observed +3.64σ canonical-mask ℓ = 1 residual amplitude (cos θ ≈ +0.84). The remaining ~47% is explicitly flagged as an open item (possible unmodeled classifier confidence-vs-depth response or higher-order survey structure) without a quantitative propagation to the real-space dipole estimator or a closure plan. While the primary claims rest on real-space estimators, an unexplained coherent residual of this size in the harmonic diagnostic weakens the overall systematics narrative for a high-profile null result.

[MINOR] Abstract, I (Introduction), and V.A (Shamir comparison): The amplitude tension (factor ~4–9) with Shamir’s 1.7–4% range is correctly qualified as pipeline-specific and not a frequentist exclusion of the Ganalyzer estimator. However, this critical caveat is not elevated to the abstract, risking misreading of the “null dipole” headline as a direct statistical confrontation rather than an estimator-specific amplitude comparison.

[MINOR] VI.B (Sensitivity Floor) and VII (Conclusions): The falsification criterion (future ≥5σ real-space detection at A ≳ A95 with A95 ∈ [1.0%, 1.5%]) and the 50%-recovery threshold (A50 ≈ 0.75%) are explicitly tied to the HC (peq > 0.6) real-space dipole estimator on this footprint and cut. The text should state more prominently that other pipelines (including Ganalyzer) require their own injection-recovery calibration before direct tension can be claimed.

[MINOR] III.A, IV.C, Table I–II, and V (significance conventions): The paper correctly notes that σ values from pixel-permutation, label-shuffle, block-bootstrap, generative-monopole, and max-statistic MC nulls are incommensurable.
