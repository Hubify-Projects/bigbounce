ISSUES:
[MAJOR] Sec. IV (entire “Four-Route No-Go”) and Scope paragraph (p. 12–13): The central “channel-level amplitude closure” is explicitly scoped as non-operator-level and dependent on the four enumerated routes being exhaustive only at the level of M_Pl-power-counting classes within minimal ECH; the Fierz-by-Fierz lemma (Appendix C) closes the four-fermion sector but does not establish that no other diffeomorphism-invariant operators at dimension ≤6 can source a coherent w = −1 density, so the claim that the minimal-ECH dark-energy route is closed remains conditional on an unproven completeness assumption.
[MAJOR] Sec. X (Perturbation-Transparency Result) and Sec. X D–E: The statement that “torsion vanishes at all classical metric/scalar perturbation orders around the torsion-free branch” and that the Holst dual contraction vanishes identically by the algebraic Bianchi identity is asserted as the central technical result supporting Barrier 14, yet the explicit verification is only sketched; no expanded derivation of the perturbed Cartan equation, the vanishing of the contorsion perturbation δK_μνρ at linear order, or the decoupling of the Holst term from the scalar and tensor mode equations is supplied, preventing independent verification.
[MAJOR] Sec. II C, Appendix B, and the dark-energy mapping (Eq. 10 and surrounding text): The identification ρ_Λ = Ξ M_Pl^4 with Ξ ≡ [(α/M) M_Pl] D_inf is introduced via a single-scale NDA dimensional no-go on an off-shell dimension-+1 operator; this is standard EFT power counting that relocates (rather than solves) the cosmological-constant problem and does not constitute an ECH-specific amplitude derivation, yet it is presented as the quantitative basis for the N_tot ≈ 92 bookkeeping and the structural tension in Sec. XIV D.
[MAJOR] Sec. IV F (Route 4) and Sec. IX (Barrier catalog): Route 4 is closed solely by a naturalness/explanatory-deficit objection (“relocating the CC problem”) rather than by any amplitude mismatch internal to minimal ECH; because the same coupling that reproduces β_obs requires m_θ ∼ H_0 to also produce ρ_Λ, the argument is logically identical to the standard CC fine-tuning problem and adds no new ECH-specific constraint beyond what is already known for spectator-ALP models.
[MINOR] Sec. I, II, and IX: The 14-barrier catalog (7 Foundations + 6 Branches + Barrier 14) mixes analytic results internal to this manuscript with references to companion papers (MCMC, Fisher forecasts, pipeline validations, galaxy-chirality catalog) whose numerical outputs are repeatedly invoked for context; even though the text states that none are load-bearing for the closures, the presentation blurs the boundary between self-contained analytic claims and externally supplied inputs.
[MINOR] Throughout (especially Sec. IV A–D and Appendix B): Amplitude-suppression statements rely on order-of-magnitude estimates (∼70 orders for R1, ∼60 orders for R2, ∼41–67 orders for R3) with explicit “conservative allowances” for higher-order corrections and uncomputed O(1) normalizations; while qualitatively sufficient, the absence of explicit upper-bound integrals or cited precise one-loop computations for the specific operators weakens the rigor of the “budget” arguments.
[MINOR] Abstract, title, and Sec. XV (Conclusions): The framing that the four-route assessment “tightly constrains” the minimal-ECH route “from the quantum bounce to observable dark energy” and that the two surviving predictions are “ECH-independent class tests” is accurate only under the narrow scoping; the title and abstract do not sufficiently foreground that R4 is closed by a standard naturalness objection and that the f_NL = −35/16 signature is erased precisely when the dark-energy mechanism is realized (Sec. XIV D), risking overstatement of the overall impact.
