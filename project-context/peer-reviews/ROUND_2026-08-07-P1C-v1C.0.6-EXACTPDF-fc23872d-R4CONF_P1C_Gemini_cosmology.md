### General Evaluation
This is an exceptionally rigorous, well-structured, and carefully argued theoretical survey. The author systematically maps the parameter space of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity to evaluate its viability as a source of dark energy and bounce phenomenology. The paper excels in its explicit evidentiary classification (distinguishing rigorous theorems from structural arguments and ansatz-level estimates) and its meticulous dimensional bookkeeping. The handling of the Route 2 amplitude budget—specifically the explicit justification for the conservative normalization choice—and the operator-basis closure argument are physically sound and clearly presented. 

The core physics, dimensional analysis, and Fierz rearrangement derivations are correct. The manuscript meets the high standards of the venue. However, there are a few administrative and provenance-related issues (timeline contradictions in the reproducibility artifacts and residual internal bookkeeping text) that must be addressed before publication.
