### ESSENTIAL findings

**P1A-E1** (Abstract-level claim vs. body, p. 3 col. 1)  
The title and opening paragraphs assert “channel-level closure” of the four enumerated minimal-ECH routes. The text immediately qualifies: “we do not claim a full operator-basis closure” and explicitly omits the Jackiw–Pi term \(R\wedge\tilde{R}\) and the parity-odd four-fermion partner of Route 1. A paper whose title advertises closure but whose first substantive paragraph disclaims operator completeness cannot be published in PRD without a title and abstract rewrite that matches the actual scope.

**P1A-E2** (Dimensional status of the central operator, p. 5–6 and Appendix B)  
Equation (6) is stated to have naïve mass dimension +1. The identification \(\rho_\Lambda=\Xi M_P^4\) is declared an “on-shell scaling ansatz, not a derivation.” An EFT paper whose leading parity-odd operator is three units short of dimension 4 and whose dark-energy scale is inserted by hand cannot claim to have closed a dark-energy route. This is fatal to the central claim.

**P1A-E3** (Perturbation-transparency theorem, Sec. X, p. 15–16)  
The theorem is proved only after setting torsion to zero and reducing the connection to Levi-Civita. The Bianchi-identity step is correct but trivial once \(T=0\). The paper never demonstrates that the same vanishing holds inside the full torsionful theory at any perturbative order when fermions are present. The “transparency” result is therefore conditional on the very assumption (vanishing torsion) that the ECH framework was invented to relax.

**P1A-E4** (Key numerical predictions not derived here)  
\(f_{NL}=-35/8\) and the \(\beta\approx0.27^\circ\) central value are imported from “companion Paper II” and “Paper Ib.” The present manuscript contains no derivation or error budget for either number. A 23-page PRD article whose headline predictions are not computed inside it violates the journal’s requirement that the work be self-contained.

### MAJOR findings

**P1A-M1** (Length vs. actual advance)  
The manuscript is 23 pages. After subtracting the 14 “barriers” (many of which are restatements of known Planck suppression or dimensional mismatch) and the extensive cross-references to six companion works, the net new calculational content is a single no-go argument under restrictive assumptions. PRD does not publish 23-page “we have enumerated four routes and found them closed under our ansatz” papers.

**P1A-M2** (Four-route enumeration is incomplete by the paper’s own scope)  
Routes R1–R4 omit the dimension-6 parity-odd operators that would be generated by integrating out the very fermions that source torsion. The closure is therefore circular: the operators that could source the required amplitude are defined out of the problem.

**P1A-M3** (Fig. 1 and Table I overclaim)  
Figure 1 draws a red “structurally closed (this paper)” dashed line through the ECH box. Table I labels the result “Yes, class-level.” Both are inconsistent with the repeated textual caveats that the result is ansatz-dependent and operator-incomplete.

**P1A-M4** (Uncontrolled \(\alpha/M\) prefactor)  
The one-loop estimate (Eq. 7) yields \(\alpha/M\sim10^{-2}\) only after choosing a specific regularization scheme (\(\gamma_5\)) whose scheme dependence is acknowledged but never quantified. All subsequent amplitude budgets inherit this uncontrolled factor.

### MINOR findings

**P1A-m1** (Citation hygiene)  
Multiple “in preparation” citations (Refs. 2, 6, 23, 46) are used to support quantitative claims. PRD permits this only when the companion manuscripts are already submitted; the present PDF gives no evidence of that status.

**P1A-m2** (Notation collision)  
The symbol \(\Xi\) is used both for the dimensionless prefactor in Eq. (10) and for the energy-density hierarchy in Fig. 2. Minor but avoidable.

**P1A-m3** (Figure axis units)  
Fig. 2 vertical axis is labeled in units of \(\rho_{\rm Pl}\) on the left and “observed \(\Lambda_{\rm obs}\)” on the right without an explicit conversion factor shown on the plot.

### Summary recommendation

**REJECT**

The manuscript advertises a “channel-level closure” whose central operator is three mass dimensions short of the required EFT counting, whose dark-energy scale is inserted by hand, and whose headline numerical predictions are not derived inside the paper. The perturbation-transparency theorem is proved only after torsion is set to zero—the very regime the ECH framework was constructed to transcend. These are not cosmetic shortcomings; they are structural. The work may be publishable as a short “note on limitations of minimal ECH dark-energy routes” (≤6 pages) once the title, abstract, and scope are rewritten to match the actual limited result. In its present form it does not meet Physical Review D standards.
