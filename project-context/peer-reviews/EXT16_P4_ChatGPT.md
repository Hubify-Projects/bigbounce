Strengths retained

The paper remains strong in four respects.

First, it provides a large public chirality-labeled catalog: 8,474,531 DESI Legacy DR8 galaxies and 3,201,160 spirals, with model weights and reproducibility scripts.

Second, the flip-equivariant TTA protocol is an important methodological contribution for this literature. The paper demonstrates that raw classifier chirality bias can masquerade as a sky dipole and that equivariant post-processing collapses the relevant real-space signal to the null.

Third, the estimator hierarchy is unusually explicit: real-space dipole, WLS template fit, MASTER diagnostics, monopole-mask leakage nulls, and injection-recovery thresholds are all kept in separate statistical conventions.

Fourth, the systematic audit is extensive and transparent: confidence-tail behaviour, depth-stratified nulls, weight-map sweeps, monopole-mask leakage, multipole coherence, quality-quartile washout, and cross-spectrum evidence all support the paper’s interpretation.
