Here is the referee report for the manuscript "A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology" submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.

### General Comments
The manuscript presents a comprehensive and structurally rigorous no-go survey of the minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst (ECH) framework as a source of late-time dark energy. The effective field theory (EFT) power-counting arguments, the Fierz-closure lemma for the parity-odd four-fermion sector, and the careful evidentiary tiering (distinguishing rigorous theorems from ansatz-level estimates) are methodologically sound. The length of the manuscript (20 pages) is appropriate for the scope of the 14-barrier catalog and the detailed operator-basis appendices; no reduction in page count is required. 

However, the manuscript contains several strict policy violations regarding statistical reporting, data provenance, self-containment, and manuscript hygiene that must be addressed before publication.

### Findings

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

**Justification**: The theoretical physics content of the manuscript is highly rigorous. The dimensional analysis, the integration of the Benedetti-Speziale $\beta$-function, and the Fierz-by-Fierz projection lemma are mathematically sound and correctly executed. The author's careful distinction between rigorous theorems, structural arguments, and ansatz-level estimates is commendable. However, the manuscript fails several strict editorial policies: it juxtaposes statistical significance metrics from different procedures without explicit qualification, relies on a "planned" future DOI rather than providing a frozen release for review, defers a load-bearing derivation (Route 4 closure) entirely to a companion paper, and contains internal audit paths/version tags. These structural and provenance issues must be corrected in a major revision before the manuscript can be accepted.
