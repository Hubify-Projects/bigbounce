Here is a referee report for the manuscript submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity* (CQG) / *Physical Review D*.

### General Comments
The manuscript presents a comprehensive and structurally rigorous no-go survey of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source for late-time dark energy and bounce phenomenology. The author catalogs 13 mechanism-class constraints and provides a compelling operator-basis completeness argument grounded in single-scale effective field theory (EFT) power counting. The dimensional arguments (particularly the $+1 \to +4$ mass-dimension deficit analysis in Appendix A) are elegant, physically sound, and well-argued. 

However, the manuscript currently suffers from several critical issues that preclude publication in its present form. Most notably, the paper violates the standalone-reader requirement by outsourcing load-bearing proofs to an un-peer-reviewed Zenodo upload, the abstract misrepresents the observable used to constrain one of the primary routes, there is an algebraic inconsistency in the Fierz rearrangement derivation, and the text contains several internal-bookkeeping/audit tags that were not removed prior to submission. 

A major revision is required to address these points.

---

### Specific Findings

**1. ESSENTIAL: Abstract-Last Drift Sweep (Conflation of Observables)**
*   **Location:** Abstract (Page 1)
*   **Problem:** The abstract states: *"we present amplitude-budget closures of the two historical quantum routes: Route 2... and Route 3... each leaves roughly sixty orders of magnitude (conservatively $\ge 58$) of suppression margin against the observed dark-energy density."* 
*   **Fix:** This claim is unsupported by the body text. In Section IV.A (Page 6, Eq. 2), the Route 2 amplitude budget is explicitly computed against the **observed birefringence signal** ($\Delta\theta_{obs}$), yielding a suppression of $\sim 10^{-60}$. Route 2 is never quantitatively compared to the dark-energy density $\rho_\Lambda$ in the text. The abstract must be corrected to accurately reflect that Route 2 is closed against the birefringence amplitude, not the dark-energy density.

**2. ESSENTIAL: Standalone-Reader Test (Reliance on Unpublished Companion)**
*   **Location:** Throughout (e.g., Page 1, Page 8, Page 14) and Bibliography [1]
*   **Problem:** The manuscript relies heavily on a "companion paper" [1] for load-bearing derivations, including the foundational torsion-elimination step, the closure of the Jackiw-Pi term, and the Tier-I perturbation transparency theorem. However, Reference [1] is cited as a Zenodo upload that is explicitly *"not an arXiv preprint and not peer reviewed."* A PRD/CQG submission cannot outsource core theoretical proofs to an inaccessible or un-refereed repository upload. 
*   **Fix:** The author must either (a) include the relevant derivations (torsion elimination, Jackiw-Pi closure, perturbation transparency) in an appendix to make this manuscript self-contained, or (b) ensure the companion paper is simultaneously submitted to the journal and provided to the reviewers, updating the citation to a standard preprint (e.g., arXiv) identifier.

**3. ESSENTIAL: Internal Audit Tags and Version History**
*   **Location:** Page 1, Page 12, Page 14, Page 15
*   **Problem:** The manuscript contains explicit internal-bookkeeping placeholders, version tags, and audit-trail language that are inappropriate for a published journal article. 
    *   Page 1: `(Dated: August 6, 2026 (v1C.0.3))`
    *   Page 14: *"the labeling is historical, retained for artifact-matching — there are no Checks B or C in this survey or in the script."* (Explicit internal placeholder).
    *   Pages 12 & 15: References to internal working paths like `research/theory_audit/fierz_adjudication_2026_08_05.py`.
*   **Fix:** Remove the version tag `v1C.0.3`. Remove the "historical artifact-matching" parenthetical on Page 14 entirely. Clean up the file paths in the Data Availability section to reflect a public release structure rather than an internal audit directory.

**4. ESSENTIAL: Provenance Surfaces (Missing Commit Hashes)**
*   **Location:** Data and Code Availability (Page 12)
*   **Problem:** The code availability section provides a raw GitHub URL (`https://github.com/Hubify-Projects/bigbounce`) and file paths, but fails to provide a frozen commit hash or a Zenodo DOI for the repository itself. Live repositories can change, breaking reproducibility.
*   **Fix:** Provide a specific Git commit hash corresponding to the exact scripts used for this paper, or (preferably) mint a Zenodo DOI for the specific release of the repository.

**5. MAJOR: Algebraic Inconsistency in Fierz Identity**
*   **Location:** Appendix B, Page 15, Equations (B1) and (B2)
*   **Problem:** The stated Fierz rearrangement in Eq. (B2) is algebraically inconsistent with the provided Fierz matrix $F$ (Eq. B1) and the stated Grassmann exchange rule $F_{op} = -F_c$. 
    *   The axial row of the $F_c$ matrix (divided by 4) is $(1, 1/2, 0, -1/2, -1)$.
    *   Applying the stated rule $F_{op} = -F_c$ yields coefficients $(-1, -1/2, 0, 1/2, 1)$ for $(S, V, T, A, P)$.
    *   However, Eq. (B2) claims the result is $SS + \frac{1}{2} VV + \frac{1}{2} AA - PP$, which corresponds to coefficients $(+1, +1/2, 0, +1/2, -1)$. The signs for the $S$, $V$, and $P$ channels are flipped relative to the strict application of $-F_c$.
*   **Fix:** Correct the algebra in Eq. (B2) to match the rigorous derivation, or clarify if a different sign convention/basis definition is being silently applied to the $S, V, P$ channels.

**6. MINOR: Phrasing Consistency Regarding "Tier-I" Results**
*   **Location:** Abstract vs. Section III (Page 3)
*   **Problem:** The abstract states *"only the perturbation-transparency result is a Tier-I rigorous theorem"*. However, Section III states *"The two sharp, first-principles results in the catalog are the Route-1 torsion-elimination derivation... and the perturbation-transparency theorem"*. While Table II clarifies that R1's ultimate *closure* is Tier II+III (making the abstract technically correct about the closure), the phrasing in Section III creates a slight contradiction for the reader.
*   **Fix:** Adjust the phrasing in Section III to perfectly align with the abstract and Table II (e.g., clarify that while the torsion-elimination *derivation* is first-principles, its application to close the route relies on Tier-II/III arguments).

### Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The paper provides a highly valuable, structurally sound, and dimensionally rigorous catalog of constraints on Einstein-Cartan-Holst cosmology. However, it cannot be accepted until the load-bearing proofs are made accessible (resolving the standalone-reader violation), the abstract is corrected to accurately reflect the Route 2 observable, the Fierz algebra is fixed, and all internal audit/version tags are scrubbed. Once these issues are addressed, the manuscript will be a strong candidate for publication.

## PASS 2 — self-critique findings (what initial review missed)

Here are the additional findings from a fresh-eyes re-evaluation of the manuscript, focusing strictly on arithmetic, dimensional consistency, and internal cross-referencing issues missed in the initial review.

**[P13-A1] Arithmetic/Display Mismatch (Stale Numbers)**
In Appendix A (Page 13), the inline equation for the cosmological constant hierarchy is written as:
$M_{Pl}^4 / \rho_\Lambda^{obs} \sim 10^{19 \text{ GeV} \times 4} / (10^{-3} \text{ eV})^4 \sim 10^{122}$.
This is mathematically incorrect as displayed. The left-hand side evaluates to $10^{124}$ (since $10^{76} \text{ GeV}^4 / 10^{-12} \text{ eV}^4 = 10^{112} \text{ eV}^4 / 10^{-12} \text{ eV}^4 = 10^{124}$). The author correctly obtained $\sim 10^{122}$ in their private calculation by using the exact values ($1.22 \times 10^{19}$ GeV and $2.25 \times 10^{-3}$ eV), but displayed the rounded order-of-magnitude exponents in the text, creating a visible arithmetic error for the reader. The displayed equation must be corrected to reflect the actual math.

**[P4-B1] Figure vs. Body-Claim Contradiction**
The text explicitly states that Barrier 14 (Perturbation Transparency, Branch H) applies to all four routes: *"[R1–R4.] For canonical scalar matter, torsion vanishes..."* (Page 4). Table I confirms this scope. However, the flowchart in **Figure 1** only draws an arrow from the Branch H box to the R1 box, entirely omitting the required connections to R2, R3, and R4. The figure must be updated to match the structural claims in the text and Table I.

**[P6-C1] Equation Dimensional Consistency Error**
In the Route 2 amplitude budget (Page 6), the text correctly identifies that the derivative of the Nieh-Yan pseudoscalar, $\partial_\mu \vartheta_{NY}$, has mass dimension +2. However, in the very next sentence, the text states: 
*"Once $\partial_\mu \vartheta_{NY} \sim H \sim 10^{-33} \text{ eV}$ at the present epoch is substituted..."*
This explicitly substitutes a dimension +1 quantity ($H$) into a dimension +2 variable. While the final dimensionless ratio $\Delta\theta_{\text{one-loop}}/\Delta\theta_{\text{obs}} \sim H_0/M_{Pl}$ implies the author actually used $\dot{\vartheta}_{NY} \sim H_0^2$ in the background calculation to get the correct dimensionless cancellation, the inline text explanation is dimensionally broken and must be corrected to $\partial_\mu \vartheta_{NY} \sim H_0^2 \sim 10^{-66} \text{ eV}^2$.
