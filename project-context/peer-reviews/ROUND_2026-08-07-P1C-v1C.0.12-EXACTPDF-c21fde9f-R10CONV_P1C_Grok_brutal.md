**Paper:** P1C v1C.0.12, “A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology,” Houston Golden (22 pp., dated 7 Aug 2026).

**Overall assessment on first read:** The manuscript does not meet CQG standards. It is a long, heavily cross-referenced catalog whose central claims are not self-contained, whose quantitative headline numbers cannot be recomputed from the displayed material without the companion paper, and whose evidentiary tiering is asserted rather than demonstrated at the level required for a “no-go survey.” The internal versioning string in the header, the repeated invocation of an unpublished companion, and the mismatch between abstract claims and standalone derivability constitute immediate grounds for rejection.

**ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without these fixes)**

P1C-E1 Header, p. 1  
Offending text: “(v1C.0.12)”  
Problem: Internal round/version tag appears in the published header.  
Required fix: Remove all version-control strings from the title block.

P1C-E2 Abstract, p. 1 and §I, p. 1  
Offending text: “we catalog thirteen distinct mechanism-class constraints … Route 2 … Route 3 … 61–67 orders … 58 … observed dark-energy density”  
Problem: The numerical closures (58, 61–67) and the precise mapping of the four routes are stated in the abstract but are derived only after importing the companion paper’s one-loop results and the R1 benchmark. A standalone reader cannot recompute them.  
Required fix: Either (a) reproduce the one-loop integrals and the R1 arithmetic inside this manuscript or (b) downgrade the abstract to qualitative statements only.

P1C-E3 Throughout (explicitly flagged in §II, p. 2 and §V, p. 10)  
Offending text: repeated citations to “the companion paper [1]” for the torsion-elimination chain, the Fierz lemma, the zero-spin-branch transparency theorem, and the R1 benchmark arithmetic.  
Problem: The argument is not self-contained. The single-scale NDA bound, the operator-list closure, and the Route-2/3 amplitude budgets all rest on results that are merely cited, not re-derived.  
Required fix: Make every load-bearing identity and numerical input either fully derived here or removed from the abstract and conclusions.

P1C-E4 Abstract + Table II, p. 4  
Offending text: “only the perturbation-transparency result is a Tier-I rigorous theorem”  
Problem: The abstract presents the catalog as a “structural no-go survey,” yet the sole Tier-I result is imported from the companion. The remaining 12 entries are Tier-II/III or naturalness arguments. This is a material over-statement.  
Required fix: Rewrite the abstract to state exactly what is proved at Tier-I level inside this manuscript.

**MAJOR findings (significant revision required)**

P1C-M1 §III, p. 3 and Fig. 1, p. 4  
The 14-entry barrier catalog collapses 13 mechanism-class constraints only after B8 is “subsumed by B14.” The diagram and Table I treat them as distinct for most of the paper. Internal bookkeeping inconsistency.

P1C-M2 §IV A, p. 6–7  
The Route-2 amplitude budget (Eq. 2) is presented as an “illustrative upper-bound” yet is used in the abstract as a quantitative closure. The normalization ambiguity (two possible schemes, factor ~10 difference) is acknowledged only after the headline number is quoted.

P1C-M3 §IV B, p. 8–9  
The Route-3 integrated result (61–67 orders) is obtained from the chiral-count ansatz Eq. 3, which the text itself labels “deliberately pessimistic” and “not a value quoted in Ref. [4].” The abstract nevertheless reports the interval as a firm bound.

P1C-M4 Appendix A, p. 15–16  
The single-scale NDA no-go is derived under the explicit assumption that no new light scale appears. The text concedes that any non-minimal completion evades it. This is a conditional naturalness argument, not a no-go theorem, yet is advertised as closing the channel.

P1C-M5 Length vs. contribution  
22 pages for a catalog whose only Tier-I result is imported and whose remaining entries are either historical or naturalness arguments. CQG’s typical limit for a focused no-go letter is ~10–12 pages. The manuscript reads as an expanded internal review document rather than a journal article.

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

Even after the essential fixes above, the work would remain a long internal survey rather than a CQG-grade no-go paper. A substantially shorter, fully self-contained letter focused solely on the perturbation-transparency result (the only Tier-I item) might be considered; the present 22-page catalog is not.
