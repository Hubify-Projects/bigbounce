## Per-finding detail

### m1 (MINOR) — §IV.D R4 closure: "both directions of the range" is a slight phrasing imprecision
**Location:** lines 681–682, `\subsection{Route 4 ...}` (sec:r4_birefringence).
**Quote:** "the produced $\rho_\theta \propto m_\theta^2$ *overshoots* $\rho_\Lambda$ in both directions of the range".

Because the natural ALP mass range $[10^{-22},\,10^{-15}]$\,eV lies *entirely above* $m_\theta = H_0 \approx 1.5\times 10^{-33}$\,eV, $\rho_\theta(m_\theta)$ is *monotone* increasing across the range. The overshoot therefore occurs at *both endpoints* of a monotone curve, not "in both directions" of a U-shape symmetric around $H_0$. The parenthetical that follows ("because $m_\theta=H_0$ is the only point where $\rho_\theta=\rho_\Lambda$, and the natural ALP range lies entirely *above* that point") immediately corrects the impression, so a careful reader recovers the intended meaning. But the headline phrase still reads as if the natural ALP range straddled $H_0$, which it doesn't.

**Suggested fix:** replace "in both directions of the range" with "across the entire natural range" or "at both endpoints of the range". One word change in a single line; the IIIII closure clause and the 22 / 36 OOM numerics are otherwise correct and arithmetically clean.

**Why MINOR not BLOCKER:** the numbers, the conclusion, and the closure logic are correct. This is a phrasing residue from the IIIII inversion, not a substantive error. A peer reviewer at PRD might circle it; they would not reject over it.

### n1 (NIT) — Tab.~III row "Quintom-B" footnote $^\dagger$ may read as inconsistent with conclusion line on bounce-class observable

**Location:** Table III caption / footnote at line 1065, vs. line 1058 ("Matter bounce (any host; not ECH-specific) ... not tested$^\ddagger$").

The Quintom-B row reports "consistent$^\dagger$" while the matter-bounce row reports "not tested$^\ddagger$" for the same $w_0w_a$ DESI column. The asymmetry is the right one (Quintom-B *theoretically accommodates* the DESI evidence; matter-bounce was *not implemented* in the frozen MCMC), and the footnotes correctly disambiguate, but a hostile reader may take half a second to register that the asymmetry is between *theoretical accommodation* and *fit-quality measurement*, not between two posterior outcomes. Footnote $\ddagger$ is doing a lot of work and lands the distinction; this is fine for PRD but worth flagging once.

**Suggested fix:** none required for ship; if a future revision touches Table III, consider promoting the footnote $\ddagger$ phrase "theoretical accommodation, not of fit quality measured in this program" to a one-word column flag (e.g., "accom." vs "not tested"). NIT.
