ESSENTIAL
---------

P4-E1  
Sec. IV D, pp. 4–5  
Problem: Internal inconsistency between quoted significance and empirical p-value: “+3.64 σ (empirical rank: pMC = 0.030)”. A Gaussian 3.64 σ event corresponds to p ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ (two-sided), whereas p = 0.03 corresponds to 1.88 σ.  
Fix: Recompute either σ or p so that they are mathematically consistent, and state clearly which definition of σ is being used (Δ/σnull or Gaussian tail-probability). If σ is merely Δ/σnull for a non-Gaussian null, drop the “σ” label and quote the empirical percentile instead.

P4-E2  
Sec. IV C & Tables I, III  
Problem: The headline −0.12 σ result and several other key σ values are derived from Monte-Carlo nulls with only N = 500 realisations. The relative uncertainty on σnull is √[2/(N−1)] ≈ 6.3 %, which is larger than the effect they are trying to measure (0.12 σ).  
Fix: Increase each null to ≥10,000 realisations or provide an analytic covariance so that the uncertainty on σnull is ≪0.1 σ. Quote the resulting error bars.

P4-E3  
Sec. III A/Table I  
Problem: The map “weighted” galaxy count (Nmap = 5 547 858) exceeds the catalogue spiral count (Ncatalog = 3 201 160) even though each object should contribute at most unit weight. The weighting scheme is undocumented, making the dipole numbers non-reproducible.  
Fix: Give an explicit formula for the per-galaxy weight, explain why ∑weights > Nobjects, and supply a unit check that shows the dipole estimator is unbiased under that weighting.

P4-E4  
Sec. IV and Appendix A  
Problem: The null distribution for Cℓ is generated by “per-pixel random-label permutation”. This destroys the spatial clustering of the galaxies and therefore ignores cosmic variance, leading to under-estimated error bars on low-ℓ modes.  
Fix: Replace the permutation null by a simulated sky that preserves the observed n(gal)/pixel map (shot noise) and adds isotropic dipole-free fluctuations drawn from ΛCDM (or explicitly justify why clustering is negligible for ℓ = 1 at the survey’s depth).

P4-E5  
Sec. IV D/Table IV  
Problem: The “99.3 % reproduction” claim for monopole-mask leakage is based on the mean of the null realisations only. No confidence interval is given, and the quoted z = +1.68 (≈95 % CL) contradicts the textual “99.3 %”.  
Fix: Provide the full distribution, quote the percentile of the data value inside that distribution, and correct the percentage.

MAJOR
-----

P4-M1  
Throughout (e.g. Abstract, Table I)  
Problem: σ values from different null procedures are listed side-by-side and occasionally compared in prose (“subsample-mask null … canonical-mask residual”) despite the disclaimer that they are “not directly comparable”. This invites mis-interpretation by readers.  
Fix: Segregate results by null type in tables/figures, or quote all significances as p-values instead of σ. Remove prose comparisons that cross null definitions.

P4-M2  
Sec. III A (Declared Analysis Hierarchy)  
Problem: The hierarchy is declared after data inspection; it is therefore not a true pre-registration. The text currently reads like a post-hoc choice.  
Fix: State explicitly which estimator was chosen a-priori (before looking at real data) and which were exploratory. If none were pre-declared, remove language implying pre-registration.

P4-M3  
Appendix A  
Problem: The paper claims “monopole subtraction reduces decoupled C1 by 34 % and increases σ from 1.85 to 3.64”. Units are missing (sr?) and the sign of σ cannot “increase” from +1.85 to +3.64 because the first number is attached to a different mask.  
Fix: Give the exact numeric values with units for both masks and show the arithmetic leading to each σ.

P4-M4  
Sec. VI A  
Problem: Injection–recovery threshold (0.75 %) uses only the 471 k high-confidence subsample, not the 3.2 M full sample used elsewhere. This mis-states the survey sensitivity.  
Fix: Repeat the injection–recovery on the full sample (or weighted map) or justify analytically why the threshold scales trivially with √N.

P4-M5  
Abstract & Conclusions  
Problem: Falsification criterion “σ>5 and amplitude ≳ 0.75 %” is based on the current pipeline errors, not on a model-independent requirement. With upgraded simulations (see E2/E4) the threshold will change.  
Fix: Re-state the criterion in terms of signal-to-noise ratio relative to the improved null, or remove the specific 0.75 % number.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The authors present an ambitious dipole-null analysis on 8.5 M galaxies, but several methodological weaknesses compromise the claimed precision: (i) inconsistent significance reporting (E1, E5); (ii) inadequate Monte-Carlo statistics for the headline 0.12 σ null (E2); (iii) undocumented weighting that inflates the sample size (E3); and (iv) a permutation null that ignores cosmic variance (E4). These issues must be fixed, the null simulations enlarged, and all σ/p cross-checks made self-consistent before the manuscript can be considered for publication.
