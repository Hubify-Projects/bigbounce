## Per-finding detail

### B1 (BLOCKER) — line 1056 — Table II ‡ footnote: cross-reference to "Paper~I(b) §VII.H" does not resolve, AND the asserted "zero free-w0wa samples" is contradicted by Paper 1B's own MCMC inventory

The AAAAA closure of m3 added a ‡ footnote on Table II that reads:

> "The frozen MCMC posteriors hosted in Paper~I(b) contain zero free-$w_0 w_a$ samples (see Paper~I(b) \S VII.H)..."

Two paper-stopping problems:

(a) **§VII.H does not exist in Paper 1B.** Paper 1B has 9 numbered sections (Introduction → Reproducibility appendix). Section VII is `\section{Cross-Paper Verification Status}` at line 451 of `paper1b_mcmc_companion.tex`, and it has no `\subsection` H (in fact, it has no lettered subsections at all). The cross-reference is dangling. A referee following it will land in the wrong place; the editor's compile checker would not flag it because it's prose, not `\ref`.

(b) **The "zero free-w0wa samples" claim is factually wrong against Paper 1B's own canonical inventory.** Paper 1B Table III (line 481) lists explicitly:

> `DESI DR2 w0wa (new) & ~109 accepted & >0.1 & Running`

So Paper 1B has 109 accepted free-w0wa samples (status = Running, $\hat R - 1 > 0.1$, not converged — but they exist on disk). Paper 1A's footnote tells the reader that Paper 1B has zero such samples; Paper 1B contradicts this. If a referee cross-checks, the discrepancy reads as a fabricated or stale claim and undermines the entire ‡ footnote — which is the load-bearing caveat justifying the column-asymmetry that AAAAA introduced specifically to close R46 m3.

This is a BLOCKER not a MAJOR because (i) the failure mode is a referee opening Paper 1B and immediately catching that the cited claim is false, which is the worst possible failure mode for a no-go theorem submission, and (ii) the fix has to thread the needle: either Paper 1B's `Running` row gets relabelled / reported honestly, or Paper 1A's footnote has to be rewritten to say "no *converged / frozen* free-w0wa posterior" rather than "zero samples." Both are surgical edits but they have to be coordinated across both papers in the same commit. Recommended fix:

- (P1A) replace the `\S VII.H` cite with a real section ref and the "zero free-w0wa samples" claim with: "the *frozen* MCMC posteriors contain no free-$w_0 w_a$ samples; the DESI DR2 $w_0 w_a$ chain (Paper~I(b) Table~III) is currently running and not yet converged, so no posterior verdict is reported."
- (P1B) make sure §VII or the Table III caption is the natural anchor, and label-bump if needed.

### M1 (MAJOR) — line 67, 195, 1261 + Table II — "7 observational research branches (Branches H–O)" is internally inconsistent with the table

Abstract (line 67) and Sec. \ref{sec:foundations} (line 195) and Conclusions (line 1261) all assert "7 observational research branches (Branches H–O)". But:

- "H to O" is 8 letters inclusive (H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O), not 7.
- Looking at Table~II actually has source labels: `Branch H`, `Branch J`, `Branch L`, `Branch L/M`, `Branch M`, `Branch N/O`, `ECH Gates`. That's 6 letter-labelled branches plus one "ECH Gates" entry — not 7 H-through-O branches.
- I and K are silently skipped in the catalogue.

Either the count "7 observational research branches" is wrong, or the range "H–O" is wrong, or both — and Barrier 14 sourced from "ECH Gates" is being wedged into the H-O range to make the headline "7 + 7 = 14" work. A referee comparing the abstract count against the Table II `Source` column will catch this in 30 seconds. The structural-closure narrative depends on the catalog being well-defined; an off-by-one in the catalog summary read straight off the table is a real credibility hit.

This is MAJOR not BLOCKER because the underlying *catalog* (the 14 named barriers, individually) appears intact — the bug is in the summary count. Fix recommendation: rewrite the abstract / intro / conclusions count as "7 foundation studies (Foundations A–G) and 6 observational research branches (Branches H, J, L, M, N/O) plus the ECH perturbation gates", or rename Barrier 14's source to a letter (e.g. `Branch P`) and amend the abstract to "Branches H–P (7 branches, with I and K reserved for follow-up)". The latter is cleaner. Either way, the surface count must match the Table II `Source` column on a one-to-one read.

### m1 (MINOR) — lines 587–588 — R2 dimensional ratio: paper says ~10^{-58}, the inputs as written give ~10^{-60}

The new R2 closure (B1 from R46, closed in WWWW) computes the dimensionless ratio
$\Delta\theta_{\rm one\text{-}loop}/\Delta\theta_{\rm obs} \sim (\alpha_{\rm em}/4\pi)\cdot(H_0/M_{\rm Pl}) \,/\, [(\alpha/M)\cdot M_{\rm Pl}\cdot \beta_{\rm obs}]$. Plugging in the values quoted on lines 582–585: $10^{-3}\cdot 10^{-61} / (10^{-2}\cdot 6\times 10^{-3}) = 10^{-64}/6\times 10^{-5} \approx 1.7\times 10^{-60}$. The paper concludes $\sim 10^{-58}$ (line 588). That's two orders of magnitude off the arithmetic of the inputs as written.

Either: (a) one of the inputs is the rounded value and a more careful version gives 10^{-58} (acceptable but should be flagged), or (b) the algebraic form on line 580 is doing the arithmetic differently than the dimensional reduction the prose describes. Either way, the headline "suppressed by ~58 orders of magnitude" should be restated as "~60 orders of magnitude" or the input values should be quoted more precisely. The qualitative conclusion (closure by amplitude suppression) is unaffected; only the headline OOM number changes. NIT-vs-MINOR: borderline; calling it MINOR because R2 dimensional closure was the load-bearing R46 BLOCKER fix and we want the headline number on this closure to be tight.

### m2 (MINOR) — Conclusions line 1249, 1261 vs Abstract line 67–68 — "14 distinct" vs "14 mechanism-class (B8 ⊂ B14)" voice not unified

The AAAAA n1 closure put B8 ⊂ B14 explicit in the abstract: "14 mechanism-class constraints (one of which, B8, is a special case of B14...)". But Conclusions §15 (lines 1249, 1261) still says "14 *distinct* mechanism-class constraints" without the B8⊂B14 caveat. Table II caption (line 826) makes the dependence very explicit. So the paper has three voices on this: abstract (B8⊂B14 explicit), Table II (subsumption acknowledged), Conclusions ("14 distinct"). A referee reading abstract → conclusions sees the framing change.

Fix: replace "14 distinct mechanism-class constraints" in lines 1249 and 1261 with "14 mechanism-class constraints (B8 ⊂ B14, retained for catalog completeness)" or equivalent. Three lines, mechanical edit.

### m3 (MINOR) — line 1296 — repository tag `v2.3.0` does not exist in the git repo

The Data and Code Availability statement pins reproducibility to `tag v2.3.0`. The repository's existing tags are `v0.9.0` and `v1.7.9-paper2` only — there is no `v2.3.0` tag. A referee following the URL to `tree/v2.3.0/reproducibility` gets a 404. This needs to be either (a) tag the repo `v2.3.0` before submission, or (b) point the URL at a commit hash, or (c) point at `tree/main/reproducibility` and accept the unpinned snapshot. Standard arXiv practice is (a) or (b).

### n1 (NIT) — references.bib line 598 — `Heinrich:2023` bibkey but published 2024

The Heinrich/Doré/Krause SPHEREx multi-tracer bispectrum paper has bibkey `Heinrich:2023` (matching the eprint year `2311.13082`) but publishes in JCAP 04 (2024). Citation rendering will show 2024 in the reference list and 2023-style in any inline year cite. Cosmetic; recommend the bibkey stay (don't break inbound references) but add `note = {arXiv:2311.13082; published JCAP 04 (2024) 074}` for unambiguous resolution. This is a NIT not a MINOR.
