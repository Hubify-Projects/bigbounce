This is a referee report for the manuscript "Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction $f_{NL}^{\rm local} = -35/16$ and a joint $(r, f_{NL}^{\rm local})$ no-go for single-field matter bounces at constant sound speed". 

The manuscript presents a highly rigorous, multi-channel cosmological stress-test of the matter-bounce scenario. The author re-derives the local non-Gaussianity amplitude from scratch (resolving a literature discrepancy), propagates it through a non-singular bounce using two different continuation schemes, and tests the resulting amplitude against pulsar timing arrays, primordial black hole abundances, and large-scale structure data. Furthermore, the paper establishes a joint no-go theorem for the tensor-to-scalar ratio and $f_{NL}$ at constant sound speed. The theoretical handling of gauge ambiguities (particularly the $\delta N_c$ vs $\zeta_{\rm Mald}$ mapping in Appendix A) is exemplary, and the observational comparisons are handled with an unusually high degree of statistical care and reproducibility. 

However, the manuscript contains several instances of internal version-history language and review-log prose that must be removed prior to publication. Additionally, a few minor rounding inconsistencies and structural optimizations should be addressed.

### Findings

**1. ESSENTIAL: Version-history and internal-draft language in the text**
*   **Location:** Multiple locations (Abstract, Sec. III, Table III, Sec. IX, Reproducibility Statement).
*   **Problem:** The manuscript contains numerous artifacts of internal drafting, version control, and review-log prose. PRD requires the published text to present the final scientific results directly, without referring to the paper's own internal revision history. Examples include:
    *   Page 1 (Abstract): "(S1's superseded range on all three backgrounds was $[-0.65, -0.50])$"
    *   Page 4 (Sec. II D): "the earlier initial-label figure $5(2\epsilon-15)/24 = -5/2$ came from..."
    *   Page 5 (Sec. III): "(replaced on Quintin-type by the resolved S2 result below)"
    *   Page 6 (Table III caption): "(the superseded S1 values...)"
    *   Page 7 (Sec. III A): "an earlier version of this argument had wrongly treated the continuation as unique"
    *   Page 14 (Sec. VI B): "(the superseded S1 values, retained for comparison, were $\in [-0.65, -0.55]$)"
    *   Page 17 (Sec. IX A): "(the S1 values, $\in [-0.65, -0.55]$, are superseded but retained for comparison)"
    *   Page 18 (Sec. IX B): "the earlier S1 value $-0.50$" and "the earlier lognormal grid"
    *   Page 21 (Reproducibility Statement): "supersedes LEDGER4_RESULT_v3_2026-09-04.md, kept as record"
    *   Page 22 (Reproducibility Statement): "pre-registration committed before the computation"
*   **Required Fix:** Remove all instances of "superseded", "resolved", "replaced", and "earlier version/draft". If the S1 scheme results are retained for pedagogical comparison, refer to them simply as "the alternative S1 scheme" rather than "superseded". Remove internal bookkeeping notes from the reproducibility statement.

**2. MAJOR: Structural density and readability**
*   **Location:** Section VI A (Pages 13-14).
*   **Problem:** The paper is 25 pages long and extremely dense. While the scope justifies a longer manuscript, the detailed validation of the DESI DR1 LRG reproduction pipeline (specifically the paragraph detailing the 5-row imaging-systematics table, EZmock covariance, and WEIGHT_SYS nulls) interrupts the cosmological narrative. 
*   **Required Fix:** Move the detailed DESI DR1 pipeline validation and systematics checks to a new Appendix B. Retain only the top-level conclusion in the main text (that DESI DR1 cannot distinguish the candidate values and that the LRG/QSO channels are internally consistent).

**3. MINOR: Abstract caveat for $\lambda$-independence**
*   **Location:** Page 1 (Abstract).
*   **Problem:** The abstract claims "$r$ and the bounce term are exactly $\lambda$-independent (the cubic coefficient cancels by parity in the bounce window)". However, Section VIII (Page 16) clarifies that this exact parity cancellation is verified specifically "in the S1 in-in integral". Given that S2 is the selected scheme on the Quintin-type background, the abstract's claim is slightly stronger than the body's calibrated statement.
*   **Required Fix:** Add a brief qualifier in the abstract (e.g., "under the S1 continuation") or ensure the body explicitly states that the parity cancellation holds identically in S2's raw-ADM action as well.

**4. NIT: Rounding inconsistency in Table III (poly S1)**
*   **Location:** Page 6 (Table III).
*   **Problem:** The poly (S1) row reports $f_{NL}^{\rm after} = -0.555$. Using the inputs provided in the table ($T_{fNL} = 0.196$, $\Delta f_{NL}^{\rm bounce} = -0.127$, and $f_{NL}^{\rm before} = -2.1875$), the calculation yields $0.196 \times (-2.1875) - 0.127 = -0.55575$, which rounds to $-0.556$.
*   **Required Fix:** Correct the rounding to $-0.556$, or provide an extra decimal place for the $T_{fNL}$ / $\Delta f_{NL}$ inputs if the exact machine-precision calculation yields $-0.555$.

**5. NIT: Bracket calculation in Table III (poly Bardeen)**
*   **Location:** Page 6 (Table III).
*   **Problem:** The poly (Bardeen) row reports the bracket $[-1.27, -0.13]$. The linear transfer is $0.521 \times (-2.1875) = -1.1397$. Using the cubic endpoints $-0.127$ (S1) and $+1.00$ (S2), the upper bound of the bracket should be $-1.1397 + 1.00 = -0.1397$, which rounds to $-0.14$, not $-0.13$.
*   **Required Fix:** Correct the upper bracket bound to $-0.14$.

**6. NIT: Informal phrasing**
*   **Location:** Page 11, Col 2 and Page 24, Table V caption.
*   **Problem:** The phrase "must not be quoted as universal" is slightly informal/prescriptive for a PRD manuscript.
*   **Required Fix:** Rephrase to a standard scientific caveat, e.g., "is highly sensitive to the grid boundaries and does not represent a universal bound."

## Summary recommendation
ACCEPT WITH MINOR CORRECTIONS

The manuscript is an outstanding contribution to theoretical cosmology, combining deep formal rigor (e.g., resolving gauge ambiguities in the bounce continuation) with exhaustive observational cross-checks. The physics is sound, the derivations are meticulously validated, and the conclusions are robustly supported by the data. Once the internal version-history language is scrubbed and the minor rounding/structural nits are addressed, this paper will easily meet the high standards of Physical Review D.

## PASS 2 — self-critique findings (what initial review missed)

Here are the additional findings from a second, deeper pass of the manuscript, focusing on arithmetic verification, cross-references, and internal consistency. 

[A3M-M1] ARITHMETIC / STALE NUMBER: In the caption of Table V (Page 24), the linear slope reconciliation equation is written as "$1.759 + (-0.20)(0.430 - 0.853) = 1.84$". However, the value $1.759$ is undefined and directly contradicts the 27-point grid mean of $1.732$ explicitly listed in the table text just above it ("mean ratio 1.732... n = 27"). If the correct mean of $1.732$ is used, the equation yields $1.732 + (-0.20)(-0.423) = 1.8166 \approx 1.82$, which fails to match the target $1.84$ 144-point mean. This suggests $1.759$ is a stale number from a previous grid iteration that was not updated when the grid parameters changed.

[A3M-m1] ARITHMETIC: In Table IV (Page 9), the Gaussian z-distances for the refit marginal ($\gamma = 2.567 \pm 0.382$) contain minor rounding errors. For the borrowed $\gamma=3$ case, $(3 - 2.567) / 0.382 = 1.1335$, which should round to $1.13\sigma$, not $1.14\sigma$. For the SMBHB $13/3$ case, $(4.3333 - 2.567) / 0.382 = 4.6239$, which should round to $4.62\sigma$, not $4.63\sigma$.
