**Referee Report**

**P1A-E1 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section I, p. 3: “the four enumerated routes … are not proven to be a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis; we acknowledge missing operators … their explicit closure is left to a follow-up operator-level analysis.”  
The title and abstract-level claims assert “Channel-Level Closure,” yet the manuscript explicitly disclaims a complete operator basis and defers the actual closure proof. This is a direct mismatch between title/claim and content. Required fix: either change the title and all closure language to “preliminary amplitude-level survey under incomplete operator set” or supply the missing operator analysis.

**P1A-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Multiple locations (p. 3, p. 12, p. 21, references): repeated citations to “companion Paper I(b)”, “Paper II”, “Paper III”, “Paper IV”, “in preparation”, and “posted concurrently.”  
A PRD manuscript must be self-contained. Dependence on six unpublished works renders the central “14-constraint catalog” and MCMC results unverifiable. Required fix: remove all dependence on unpublished companions or withdraw until they exist.

**P1A-E3 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Appendix B, p. 22 and Eq. (6): parity-odd operator is assigned naïve mass dimension +1 (explicitly “three units short of the required +4”). The text states this is a “scaling ansatz, not a controlled EFT calculation.”  
An EFT operator with incorrect dimension cannot be used to derive late-time dark-energy density or to close channels. The entire amplitude-budget argument collapses. Required fix: either derive a dimension-4 operator or retract all quantitative claims that rest on Eq. (6).

**P1A-E4 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Abstract-level claim (p. 1) and Sec. XIII: \(f_{NL}=-35/8\) is presented as a “surviving” ECH-related prediction.  
The value is taken from the scalar-only matter-bounce class (Paper II, Assumption (f)), not generated by any ECH torsion term. The paper itself states the prediction is “not fully mechanism-independent across the bouncing-cosmology landscape.” Presenting it as an ECH result is false attribution.

**P1A-M1 (MAJOR)**  
Table II and Sec. IX: 13 of the 14 “logically independent structural barriers” are heuristic or philosophical statements (“attractor-sensitivity dilemma,” “parameter immunity,” “gravitational democracy”). Only Barrier 14 (perturbation transparency) contains an explicit calculation.  
A 14-barrier “closure” whose majority are non-quantitative does not constitute a rigorous no-go theorem. Required fix: label the non-calculational items as “heuristic considerations” and reduce the claimed constraint count accordingly.

**P1A-M2 (MAJOR)**  
Sec. X and Eq. (23): the Bianchi-identity argument for Holst-term vanishing is performed only at \(T=0\). The text acknowledges this is “distinct from the Pontryagin density.”  
The transparency theorem therefore holds only in the torsion-free limit; it does not close the theory at finite torsion. The central “perturbation transparency” result is therefore conditional on an assumption that removes the very degree of freedom under study.

**P1A-M3 (MAJOR)**  
Fig. 1 and p. 4: the diagram shows ECH/torsion routes as “structurally closed (this paper)” while the matter-bounce route remains open.  
The figure caption and surrounding text do not state that the closure applies only under the 14 listed assumptions and the incomplete operator set. The visual therefore overstates the result.

**P1A-N1 (MINOR)**  
p. 1: “Dated: June 10, 2026 PDT.”  
Future dating on a submission is irregular; replace with actual receipt date.

**P1A-N2 (NIT)**

**Summary recommendation**  
**REJECT**

The manuscript asserts a “channel-level closure” of four minimal ECH dark-energy routes yet simultaneously (i) disclaims a complete operator basis, (ii) relies on six unpublished companion papers, (iii) employs a parity-odd operator three mass dimensions below the required EFT power counting, and (iv) attributes a matter-bounce \(f_{NL}\) prediction to the ECH framework. The majority of the 14 “barriers” are non-quantitative. These defects are structural, not cosmetic; the paper does not meet Physical Review D standards for rigor, self-containment, or accurate representation of its own results.
