**Referee Report – PRD Regular Article (A3M)**

**Paper:** Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)

**Finding A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract + Sec. II, p. 1–2)**  
Abstract states “confirms \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16=-2.1875\) exactly” and “reproduces this value exactly.” Body (Eq. 1, Table I) shows the result holds only inside the squeezed super-Hubble limit after a specific normalization choice; the transmitted amplitude after the bounce is scheme-dependent (\([-1.25,-0.50]\) for S1, \(-1.25\) for raw-ADM S2). The abstract claim is stronger than the calibrated body statement. Required fix: rewrite abstract to “yields \(-35/16\) in the squeezed limit under scheme S1; transmitted amplitude after bounce lies in \([-1.25,-0.50]\) depending on linear-transfer convention.”

**Finding A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract + Sec. IV D, p. 6)**  
Abstract claims “5.1\(\sigma\) off NANOGRAV.” Body reports \(\gamma_{\rm pred}=5.07\) (5.1\(\sigma\)) only for the scalar-induced background; the same model’s first-order tensor background lies 8–9 orders of magnitude below the NANOGRAV band. The headline \(\sigma\) is therefore not a detection tension but a null-result distance. Required fix: replace “5.1\(\sigma\) off” with “null at 5.1\(\sigma\) (scalar-induced channel only).”

**Finding A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. III A + Table III, p. 4–5)**  
S1 and S2 rows are placed side-by-side in Table III and the abstract band \([-1.25,-0.50]\) is quoted without the explicit qualifier “not directly comparable” at every juxtaposition. The two schemes differ by a total-derivative regularization whose physical status is left open. Required fix: add the qualifier in every table, figure caption, and abstract sentence that mixes the two numbers.

**Finding A3M-M1 (MAJOR, Sec. V B + Table V, p. 8–9)**  
The PBH abundance ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.732\pm0.050\) is presented as “stable to \(\pm3\%\)”. The grid is only 27 points, the ratio is evaluated at a single representative amplitude, and the underlying spectrum shape is unreproducible from the cited literature. Required fix: enlarge grid to \(\geq100\) points, vary shape parameters inside the scanned range, and report the full distribution of the ratio.

**Finding A3M-M2 (MAJOR, Sec. VI + Table VI, p. 10–11)**  
Forecast significance for SPHEREx/MegaMapper is given as 0.7–1.3\(\sigma\) (S1) versus 1.78–2.19\(\sigma\) (S2) without a joint analysis that marginalizes over the linear-transfer convention. The claim that “large-scale structure is reachable” therefore rests on one regularization choice. Required fix: present a scheme-marginalized forecast or state that reachability is scheme-dependent.

**Finding A3M-E4 (ESSENTIAL, reproducibility statement, p. 12)**

**Finding A3M-M3 (MAJOR, length vs. contribution)**  
15-page manuscript whose central new result is a single number (\(-35/16\)) already obtained by Li et al. (2017) and Quintin et al. (2015). The multi-channel “consistency” exercise is almost entirely internal recomputation. Recommended maximum length for such a note: 6–8 pages.

**Finding A3M-E5 (ESSENTIAL, abstract drift)**  
Abstract asserts “three channels are honest nulls; large-scale structure is reachable.” Body final calibrated statements are: pulsar-timing null at 5.1\(\sigma\), PBH channel null at 6.7–7.0 orders of magnitude, LSS only marginally discriminating at 0.5–1.1\(\sigma\). The abstract ordering and strength exceed the body’s final wording.

**Finding A3M-E6 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. II D)**  
The classical \(\delta N\) route is advertised as “method-independent” yet still requires the same separate-universe assumption whose validity at second order is the very point under dispute. The two routes are therefore not independent at the order needed to resolve the factor-of-two discrepancy.

**Summary recommendation**  
REJECT

The manuscript fails multiple PRD acceptance criteria: the headline result is scheme-dependent yet presented as exact in the abstract; several \(\sigma\) claims compare quantities that the text itself labels non-comparable; the PBH and LSS forecasts rest on unreproducible spectrum shapes or single-regularization choices; and the 15-page length far exceeds the incremental advance over existing literature. A substantially shortened, scheme-explicit Letter could be reconsidered, but the present submission does not meet the high bar for a regular PRD article.
