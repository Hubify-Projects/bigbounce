## MAJOR

### M1 — Body sentence anchoring fn:mc_count contradicts footnote (i) on N_MC for the simple-dipole bootstrap [GATING]

**Location:** lines 1070–1073, §sec:dipole, immediately above the rewritten footnote.

**Body text (line 1070–1072):**

> "A dipole is fitted to the asymmetry map, and its significance is assessed via **1,000** bootstrap randomizations in which the pixel asymmetry values are shuffled while preserving the mask."

**Footnote (i) anchored on that exact sentence (line 1073):**

> "(i)~Simple-dipole bootstrap significance ($\sigma_{\rm hemi}=0.43$, $p=0.30$): $N_{\rm MC} = \mathbf{10{,}000}$ isotropic-null realizations on the Catalog~C equivariant spiral subsample (canonical, used in the abstract / Sec.~\ref{sec:dipole})."

The body sentence describes the **simple-dipole bootstrap** procedure — exactly the procedure footnote (i) categorizes — and quotes the obsolete N_MC = 1,000 ensemble that footnote 31 (lines 1085–1090) explicitly labels as "superseded." Six lines below (line 1078) the same paragraph then quotes the canonical "$N_{\rm MC} = 10{,}000$." So in a single paragraph the simple-dipole bootstrap is described as 1,000 in the methodology sentence, 10,000 in the result sentence, with footnote 30 (fn:mc_count) declaring 10,000 canonical and footnote 31 declaring 1,000 superseded. A hostile referee cites this as evidence that the authors do not know which Monte Carlo count produced their headline number.

**Why this is a MAJOR for cross-vendor:** This is the most-quoted sentence in the dipole methodology — it is the anchor of the footnote that exists *specifically to disambiguate the three MC counts*. The footnote cannot disambiguate if its anchor sentence quotes a number the footnote then declares superseded.

**Required fix (one-line edit):** Change "1,000 bootstrap randomizations" → "10,000 bootstrap randomizations" at line 1071. (Confirm the production log actually carries N_MC=10,000 for the simple-dipole bootstrap; if the on-disk log is N_MC=1,000 then the abstract / Sec. dipole / footnote (i) / footnote 31 are *all* wrong and the issue is far worse than M1.)
