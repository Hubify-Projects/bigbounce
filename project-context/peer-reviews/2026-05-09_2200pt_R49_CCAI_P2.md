## R49 findings

### B1. **BLOCKER — §V.B L207, σ_theory=0.5 bullet contains two numerical errors against the stated σ**

The bullet (introduced by Wave 14-JJJJJ to close R48-m2) reads:

> **Gaussian prior, σ_theory = 0.5** (encompassing the central ε-correction window [4–8% systematic shift in f_NL] but *not* the factor-of-two convention ambiguity; **positions f_NL ∈ [-5.4, -3.4] at 1σ**, **which excludes the Li & Brandenberger value -2.1875 at 2.4σ**): ~12

Both bolded numbers are wrong against σ_theory = 0.5:

1. **1σ interval is wrong.** With central value f_NL = -4.375 and σ_theory = 0.5, the 1σ interval is -4.375 ± 0.5 = **[-4.875, -3.875]**, not [-5.4, -3.4]. The stated interval [-5.4, -3.4] corresponds to a half-width of ~1.025, i.e. **σ ≈ 1.0** (the next bullet's σ value), not σ = 0.5. This looks like the σ_theory = 1.0 row's interval was accidentally copied into the σ_theory = 0.5 row.

2. **Tension against -2.1875 is wrong.** With σ_theory = 0.5, |-4.375 − (-2.1875)| / 0.5 = 2.1875 / 0.5 = **4.375σ**, not 2.4σ. The "2.4σ" figure also does not correspond to σ_theory = 1.0 (which would give 2.1875 / 1.0 = 2.19σ ≈ 2.2σ); the origin of "2.4" is not recoverable from any σ_theory in the bullet list. It does not match any of {0.5, 1.0, 2.0}.

**Cross-check against the σ_theory = 2.0 row (L209):** that row correctly states "2.1875 / 2.0 = 1.09σ" — arithmetic is right there. So the only broken bullet is the σ_theory = 0.5 row.

**Required fix.** Two equivalent options:

- **(a) Recompute the bullet at σ_theory = 0.5 honestly:** "positions f_NL ∈ [-4.875, -3.875] at 1σ, which excludes the Li & Brandenberger value -2.1875 at 4.375σ"
- **(b) Re-anchor the bullet to a 2σ interval explicitly:** "positions f_NL ∈ [-5.375, -3.375] at 2σ, which excludes the Li & Brandenberger value -2.1875 at 4.375σ" (note the σ-count to L&B does not change with the interpretation of the interval; it's still 4.375σ at σ_theory = 0.5)

Either fix removes the contradiction. **Option (a) is preferred** because it preserves the "1σ" framing the prose already commits to and uses the σ value that labels the bullet.

**Why this is a BLOCKER, not a minor:** the bullet exists *specifically* to give the σ_theory = 0.5 row a physical-justification clause (the m2 close from JJJJJ). If the justification clause has its arithmetic backwards against the stated σ, the row's physical motivation evaporates and a hostile reviewer will conclude the authors did not check their own justification. Cross-vendor R-round will catch this in <60 seconds and dock the paper for it.
