(2) ISSUES:

1. [MAJOR] The Stage-B \(A_{95}=1.5\%\) claim is statistically unsupported. Abstract and Sec. VI.B, lines 676 and 1187–1218, derive it from only 20 axes with one realization per axis and amplitude. Frozen-script lines 295–325 and 328–364 and the JSON give 16/20 detections at both 0.75% and 1.0%, then 20/20 at 1.5%. For 20/20, the exact one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on recovery probability is only 0.861, not 0.95. Many independent injections per axis with power-curve uncertainties are required before calling this \(A_{95}\).

2. [MAJOR] The primary-sample choice is not demonstrably pre-specified and determines the verdict. Sec. IV.C, line 976, reports \(z\simeq4.0\)–4.3 for cuts \(p_{\rm eq}\le0.5\), followed by \(z=0.41\) at the adopted 0.6 cut. The claimed “pre-registration” is only a commit that simultaneously defines the estimator, with no frozen analysis plan or tag predating evaluation; the stated rationale explicitly selects the first threshold where the excess disappears. Confidence dependence must be modeled jointly or the selection penalty assessed rather than declaring the selected 30% subsample uniquely primary.

3. [MAJOR] The physical chirality interpretation exceeds the demonstrated calibration. Sec. VI.B, lines 1212–1240, concedes that Stage B is classifier-conditional, lacks independent full-catalog truth, and leaves the depth×PSF×morphology-dependent confusion model open; the science-cut differential-error bound is itself approximately 0.6 percentage points, comparable to the reported amplitudes. Nevertheless Sec. VI.C, line 1258, claims the 0.75% floor constrains parity-violating cosmological scenarios. Disclosure does not supply the missing image-to-physical transfer function or spatial confusion likelihood; those physical bounds must be removed or actually calibrated.

4. [MAJOR] The unresolved harmonic residual is load-bearing for the real-space conclusion. Sec. IV.D, line 1071, finds the 0.695% residual above the like-for-like full-sample \(A_{95}=0.63\%\), and injecting it into the HC field changes the fitted real-space amplitude from 0.4597% to 0.6710%—a 46% shift—while explicitly leaving the joint real-space×harmonic covariance open. Calling the harmonic result merely “diagnostic” does not establish that this shared-catalog residual cannot bias or covary with the primary estimator; a joint nuisance/covariance analysis is required.

5. [MINOR] The inherited-power ceiling contains a factor-of-two unit error. Sec. VI.A, line 1121, identifies \(A_{95}\le1.5\%\) as an \(f_{\rm CW}\)-unit ceiling and converts it to \(A_p\le3\times10^{-2}\), whereas Appendix D, line 1514, correctly states that injected full amplitude \(A=A_p\) and the \(f_{\rm CW}\)-deviation amplitude is \(A/2\). Thus 1.5% corresponds to \(A_p=0.015\) and a 0.75% \(f_{\rm CW}\) deviation.

6. [MAJOR] PRD suitability and novelty are not yet established at the claimed physics level. The Introduction, line 693, defines novelty mainly as a 1.6-fold scale increase, an explicit non-spiral class, and bias audits over CE-ResNet-derived labeling; Sec. VI.C, line 1258, admits that no primordial-to-morphology transfer function is derived. This supports a valuable catalog/methodology paper, but not yet a quantitative fundamental-physics constraint suitable for PRD without substantial reframing or a derived cosmological likelihood.

7. [MINOR] The reproducibility record is not immutable in the manuscript. Data Availability, lines 1564–1571, points to a mutable `main` branch and promises that the exact commit hashes, frozen release, and Zenodo DOI will be inserted later. The published version must pin the supplied review commit or an equivalent immutable release and archive every load-bearing script, catalog, and artifact.
