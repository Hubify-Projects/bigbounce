**Referee Report – PRD Regular Article (A3M)**

**Paper**: Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) and a joint \((r,f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces

### ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without fixes)

**A3M-E1** (Abstract, p. 1; Sec. III, p. 4–5)  
The abstract states a scheme-qualified band \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}\in[-0.65,-0.50]\) (S1) and a raw-ADM value \(-1.25\) (S2) as if both are equally legitimate predictions. The body shows these arise from physically inequivalent treatments of the bounce (linear Mukhanov–Sasaki vs. raw ADM continuation) and that only S1 satisfies the validated \(k\eta_B\lesssim10^{-2}\) window. No explicit statement appears that the two numbers are not directly comparable.  
*Required fix*: Remove the joint band from the abstract or add a one-sentence qualifier at every juxtaposition that the S2 value is an artifact outside the controlled regime.

**A3M-E2** (Abstract, p. 1; Sec. IV D, p. 7)  
Abstract claims “pulsar timing \(\gamma_{\rm pred}=5.07\) vs. NANOGrav’s \(3.2\pm0.6\), \(5.1\sigma\) null”. The body (Table IV and surrounding text) shows the \(5.1\sigma\) distance is computed against the official posterior while the author’s own 30-bin refit yields only \(1.14\sigma\). The abstract therefore reports the stronger of two non-equivalent statistics without the required “not directly comparable” tag.  
*Required fix*: Either quote only the refit distance or label both numbers with an explicit non-comparability statement in the abstract.

**A3M-E3** (Sec. V B, p. 9–10; Table V)  
The compaction-function PBH scan reports a “robust” amplitude ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.732\pm0.050\) across 27 grid points. The same table shows that 5 of the 27 points lie in the non-perturbative branch (\(\gamma_{\rm cr}\lesssim0.63\)) where the quadratic map is uncontrolled. The ratio is therefore not robust under the paper’s own perturbativity cut.  
*Required fix*: Restrict the quoted ratio to the perturbatively controlled sub-grid and recompute the uncertainty.

**A3M-E4** (Sec. VIII, p. 12–13; Eq. 14–17)  
The joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) no-go is presented as background-independent. The derivation of the curvature spectator route (Eq. 17) is performed only for the dust case and never shown to survive the LQC or poly backgrounds used elsewhere. The claim is therefore unsupported for the full set of backgrounds advertised in the abstract.

### MAJOR findings

**A3M-M1** (p. 1, length)  
18-page PRD regular article whose core result is a single number (\(-35/16\)) plus three null channels. The contribution is closer to a 6–8 page letter. Excessive length is driven by repeated scheme comparisons and reproducibility boilerplate.

**A3M-M2** (Sec. III, p. 4–5)  
The transmission calculation is performed only inside scheme S1. Scheme S2 is shown to diverge or to require an ad-hoc cutoff, yet the abstract still quotes an S2 number. The methodology is therefore incomplete for any claim that treats both schemes symmetrically.

**A3M-M3** (Sec. VI, p. 11)  
DESI DR1 constraint is re-analysed with the author’s own window matrix and covariance; the recovered central value shifts from the published \(-3.6^{+9.0}_{-9.1}\) to \(-2.2\pm2.5\). No quantitative assessment is given of how much of the shift is due to the different mask versus the different \(f_{\rm NL}\) template. The result is therefore not demonstrably reproducible from the public data release alone.

**A3M-M4** (Fig. 2, p. 9; Table V)  
The PBH abundance curves for the two candidate \(f_{\rm NL}\) values cross the literature band at different amplitudes, yet the paper never states the numerical factor by which the two predictions differ inside the observationally allowed window. Effect-size information is missing.

### Abstract-last drift sweep (pattern-045)

Every load-bearing scalar in the abstract was traced to the body. Two claims fail the test:  
1. The joint \([-0.65,-0.50]\) band is presented without the scheme qualifier that the body itself requires.  
2. The \(5.1\sigma\) pulsar-timing tension is quoted from the official posterior while the body’s own refit is weaker; the abstract therefore over-states the tension relative to the paper’s final calibrated statement.

### Summary recommendation

**REJECT**

The manuscript contains multiple essential internal contradictions (scheme-dependent results presented as a single band, non-comparable \(\sigma\) values juxtaposed without qualification, perturbativity cuts ignored in quoted ratios) that cannot be repaired by minor revision. The core technical result (\(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)) is already in the literature; the added multi-channel analysis is either null or scheme-qualified and does not rise to the level of a full PRD regular article. A substantially shortened letter that restricts itself to the controlled S1 calculation and states its limitations clearly might be reconsiderable, but the present 18-page version does not meet PRD standards.
