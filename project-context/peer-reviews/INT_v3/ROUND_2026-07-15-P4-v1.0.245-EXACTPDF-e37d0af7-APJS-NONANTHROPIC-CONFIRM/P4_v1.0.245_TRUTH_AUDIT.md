- `train_v2_fast.py:64-86,93-134` constructs counts dynamically and uses an unseeded CE non-spiral sample. The script can explain a possible run, but cannot prove which historical realization produced the checkpoint. Its expected run-result receipt is also not committed.

**Closure gate:** recover and content-hash the exact historical result/audit receipts, checkpoint metadata, source revisions, object IDs, split membership, and any available random-state record; reconcile the paper, benchmark report, and model card in one closure. If the exact historical receipt cannot be recovered, withdraw the unrecoverable exact counts/accuracy and explicitly report the conflicting legacy records.

### Codex 3 — spatially varying symmetric classification error

**Disposition: VERIFIED MAJOR — conceptual/statistical transfer gate.**

- The paper says differential CW↔CCW error asymmetry is the only channel that can bias the dipole and describes symmetric error variation as dilution-only (`chirality_catalog_paper.tex:1274-1279,1479`; related language at 1289-1290).
- For a symmetric error rate `e(x)`, the observed CW fraction obeys `q'(x)=q(x)+e(x)[1-2q(x)]`. Therefore spatial variation in `e` can change the observed field whenever the local underlying/selected fraction is not exactly one half.
- The committed `outputs/gz1_stratified_confusion.json` reports induced `delta_fCW=0.0014191442560136237` for BASS and `0.0033092883170967504` for DECaLS, a point-estimate difference of `-0.0018901440610831267`. The individual directional asymmetries are consistent with zero, and the artifact does not justify treating the point-estimate leg difference as significant without its uncertainty.

**Closure gate:** remove “only” and “dilution-only”; state the full transfer equation; report the leg point estimates with uncertainty; compute a confidence interval/covariance-aware test for the leg difference; propagate the spatial symmetric-error channel through the primary estimator or explicitly retain the spatial transfer gate as open.

### Codex 5 — selected versus effective primary sample

**Disposition: VERIFIED MINOR — accounting/labeling correction.**

## Readiness decision

**Hold P4 at 80/100 and do not submit.** The fixed-occupancy primary null itself is supported, but an ApJS catalog/methods paper cannot be called release-ready while the advertised catalog is local/unarchived, its default reproducer lacks public inputs, and the historical training claims conflict across committed records. The symmetric-error transfer statement also requires substantive correction and uncertainty-aware evaluation. Stale 0.55-sigma prose and sample-count labeling are real but bounded editorial/mechanical closures; already-satisfied referee requests must not be recycled into additional review loops.
