## Truth-gated result

The released DESIVAST V2-REVOLVER GALZONE TARGET universe contains 694,642 unique TARGETIDs (604,032 NGC + 90,610 SGC). Its intersection with the frozen P5 parent contains 145,789 rows; 145,766 remain after `OUT=0`. Exact VoidFinder hole-union membership yields 31,937 void rows (15,873 CW) and 113,829 non-void rows (56,741 CW), with crude non-void-minus-void contrast +0.001466.

The accepted Newton--Cholesky logistic standardization converged in 16 iterations, is full rank (78/78), has scaled-Gram condition number 1989.93, and maximum mean score 2.32e-13. The adjusted average marginal contrast is +0.001256:

- coarse NSIDE=4 sky sandwich: SE 0.003413, 95% CI [-0.005432, +0.007945], p=0.713;
- nearest-MAXIMALS sandwich: SE 0.003174, p=0.692;
- overlap weighting: delta +0.001204, coarse-sky SE 0.003403, p=0.723;
- maximum absolute SMD: 0.218623 before weighting, **0.000105635 after weighting**;
- `EDGE=0`: adjusted delta +0.003497, coarse-sky SE 0.003737, p=0.349; post-weight maximum absolute SMD **0.0000457003**.

Correction: an earlier working note carried forward a stale post-weight SMD of 0.0161 from the superseded nonconverged run. It is not an accepted result. The only accepted values are 0.000105635 and 0.0000457003 above.

The public VAC does not provide the exact smoothed-mask/random FITS referenced by its construction. GALZONE membership is therefore described as the strongest released catalog-native selection proxy, not as a reconstruction of the unreleased angular selection function. The later LSS v1.5 BGS_BRIGHT-21.5 sample was not substituted because it overlaps only 15,378 frozen rows and would change the estimand.
