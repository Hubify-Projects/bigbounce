## Finding-by-finding adjudication

Statuses: **VERIFIED-BLOCKER**, **VERIFIED-STANDING**, **FALSIFIED/STALE**, **OPINION**, or **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### TA-01 — primary control is not selection-function matched

**VERIFIED-BLOCKER; standing DP5-06 / DP5-19.** OpenAI and Codex are correct that the load-bearing non-void control is the author-constructed union of hole-sphere angular discs plus radial span, not the official DESIVAST/BGS completeness mask and not a DESI-randoms or covariate-matched control. The manuscript discloses this explicitly, so claims that it is hidden are false; disclosure does not make the unresolved confounding disappear. Bright/dark balance addresses one axis only. A PRD-ready controlled environmental test needs the adjusted/matched analysis in the closure contract, or materially narrower descriptive language. This does not falsify the observed count contrast.

### TA-02 — exact row-level bootstrap provenance and immutable release

**VERIFIED-BLOCKER; standing DP5-10 / DP5-18 / DP5-21.** The local driver is deterministic and the rerun is byte-identical, but Appendix C acknowledges that the current DESI FITS hash differs from the unavailable historical snapshot. Matching aggregate gates cannot prove identical object identities, coordinates, or region assignments—the ingredients that determine covariance. The artifact gives a canonical row-content hash, but the exact derived row/region table is not archived as an immutable public input. In addition, the PDF claims a version tag and pending Zenodo archive; no `v0.1.128-2026-07-14` tag exists locally or on `origin`, and the Paper-IV arXiv identifier remains a placeholder. OpenAI/Codex/Gemini are therefore correct on the publication gate. The numerical SE is reproduced locally, not shown false.

### TA-03 — 57,081 versus 56,981 and primary-family naming

**Mixed: count-seam allegation FALSIFIED/STALE; family semantics VERIFIED-STANDING (DP5-01/-02/-03/-04).** Table X supplies a single explicit reconciliation: 57,081 is the exact `k`-unbounded headline membership and 56,981 is the approximate `k=20` sensitivity row. Boldface is an editorial preference.
