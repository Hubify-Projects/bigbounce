## 1. VERDICT

**MAJOR REVISIONS.**

The manuscript is unusually careful about scope: the three-tier evidentiary table, the
"what is not established" section, the repeated disclaimer that the spanning assertion is not
proved by enumeration, and the explicit branch-scoping of the Tier-I theorem are all done to a
standard well above the norm for this kind of survey. Every numerical claim I could check
reproduced (§3). I found no citation fabrications.

However, one load-bearing structural premise is wrong on the paper's own evidence. The
operator-list argument of Sec. V / App. A 1 — the piece the paper itself calls "the primary
physical foundation of the no-go" (p. 13) — is built on the premise that the minimal-ECH
Cartan torsion is *purely axial*. The paper's own Eq. (E2) (p. 22) exhibits a non-axial
(trace-vector) torsion component generated by the Holst term under *minimal* coupling, which at
the paper's own LQG value γ ≈ 0.24 is roughly **twice** the axial component. Two of the three
disposal classes in the Verdict of App. A 1 therefore do not hold on the Einstein–Cartan–**Holst**
branch: O4 is not identically zero, and O1/O6 are not exact total derivatives. The final physics
conclusion (no (meV)⁴ vacuum energy without a new light scale) survives — all the affected
operators fall into the *other* disposal class the paper already establishes, the
M_Pl^{-2}-suppressed Fierz-closed four-fermion sector — but the printed statements, Table III,
the abstract's trichotomy, and Route 2's dark-energy leg case (i) must be rewritten. This is
MAJOR, not fatal.

The revision is bounded: either (a) declare the operator list scoped to the γ→∞ Einstein–Cartan
torsion branch and say so everywhere the phrase "T = κS branch" appears, or (b) redo the
disposal with the finite-γ ECH torsion, in which case O1, O4, O6 join O5 in disposal class (ii).
Option (b) is the honest one for a paper whose framework is ECH at γ ≈ 0.2375.

## 2. MAJOR FINDINGS

### MAJOR-1 [correctness] — The minimal-ECH Cartan torsion is not purely axial at finite γ; the paper's own Eq. (E2) contradicts the premise that O4 ≡ 0 and that O1/O6 are exact total derivatives on the ECH branch

**Anchors:** Eq. (E2), p. 22 (`main.tex:2826–2831`, label `eq:fmt_contorsion_p1c`);
Sec. II, p. 2 (`main.tex:614`); Sec. V collapse bullets (a)/(b), p. 14–15
(`main.tex:1950–1971`); Sec. V trichotomy, p. 14 (`main.tex:1934`); App. A 1, p. 19
(`main.tex:2406–2422`, `2503–2513`, Verdict `2544–2545`); Table III row O4 and caption, p. 20
(`main.tex:2458`, `2437–2440`); abstract, p. 1 (`main.tex:417–421`); Data & Code Availability,
p. 16 (`main.tex:2164`); App. C scope paragraph, p. 21 (`main.tex:2694`).

**What the paper asserts.** Sec. II states the ECH connection constraint *without qualification*:

> "varying the first-order action with respect to the connection gives an algebraic
> (non-dynamical) constraint fixing the torsion tensor `T^{abc}` in terms of the fermion spin
> current `S^{abc}`, `T^{abc} = κ S^{abc}`." (`main.tex:612–614`)

Sec. V's construction rule admits "the algebraically-fixed torsion `T = κS`"
(`main.tex:1784`), and App. A 1 repeats "algebraic torsion fixed by the Cartan constraint
`T^{abc} = κ S^{abc}`" (`main.tex:2378`). The O4 disposal then reads:

> "The minimal Cartan torsion `T^{abc} = κS^{abc} = (κ/4) ε^{abcd} J^5_d` is *purely axial* —
> its vector and tensor parts vanish identically — so `O_4^{[4]} ≡ 0` on shell … This
> *strengthens* the closure: an operator contributing nothing at all is a stronger disposal than
> one contributing a Planck-suppressed contact term." (`main.tex:2411–2422`, p. 19)

and Sec. V bullet (a):

> "On the `T = κS` branch O1 and O6 are not pointwise zero, but Eq. (11) together with
> `O_4^{[4]} = 0` (below) gives `O_1^{[4]} = O_6^{[4]} = −O_2^{[4]}` exactly, so they are minus
> the Nieh–Yan total derivative and contribute zero to the equations of motion and zero to the
> vacuum energy." (`main.tex:1950–1956`, p. 14)

**The contradicting sibling — the paper's own Eq. (E2).** Re-read at 300 DPI (p. 22), Eq. (E2)
is

    e_I^μ C_{μJK} = 4πG · γ²/(1+γ²) · [ ½ ε_{IJKL} J_5^L  −  (1/γ) η_{I[J} J^5_{K]} ] ,

and the bracket immediately above it says, in the paper's own words:

> "[`S^{IJK} = ¼ ε^{IJKL} J_{5L}`, the Sec. II convention; **in the Einstein–Cartan limit** the
> constraint reads `T^{IJK} = κ S^{IJK} = (κ/4) ε^{IJKL} J_{5L}`]" (`main.tex:2820–2823`,
> emphasis mine)

So App. E 1 knows that `T = κS` is the γ→∞ Einstein–Cartan statement, while Sec. II, Sec. V and
App. A 1 use it as *the* ECH constraint. The second term of Eq. (E2) is the non-axial piece,
present under **minimal** coupling, and it does not vanish for any finite γ.

**Consequences, computed.** Converting Eq. (E2) to torsion
(`T_{abc} = C_{cab} − C_{bac}`, i.e. `T = 2C` for the totally antisymmetric part, the standard
Einstein–Cartan relation) and evaluating in exact symbolic arithmetic with the paper's
conventions (mostly-plus, ε contraction `ε_{abcd}ε^{abce} = −3! δ^e_d`):

1. **Torsion trace vector is nonzero:** `T^a{}_{ac} = 6πG γ/(1+γ²) · J^5_c ≠ 0`. It vanishes only
   as γ→∞ (or γ→0). The torsion therefore carries a nonvanishing *trace-vector* irrep — the very
   irrep the paper says is absent.
2. **The vector-to-axial ratio inside Eq. (E2) is `1/(2γ)`** — normalization-independent, since it
   is a ratio of the two printed terms. At the paper's own LQG value γ = 0.2375 this is **2.1**:
   the non-axial component *dominates* the axial one.
3. **O4 does not vanish.** With `O4 = ε^{μνρσ} T^I{}_{μν} T_{Iρσ}`,

       O4(bare) = −192 π² G² γ³/(1+γ²)² · (J⁵·J⁵)  ≠ 0   for finite nonzero γ,

   → −22.7 G² (J⁵·J⁵) at γ = 0.2375. The same computation returns **exactly 0** for the purely
   axial `T = κS`, confirming both that my machinery is right and that the paper's/script's
   result is right *on the branch they actually evaluated*. Note this is precisely the
   "vector×axial cross terms" channel the paper itself names as the surviving one
   (`main.tex:2409–2411`, `2509–2511`) — the paper identifies the channel correctly and then
   asserts, wrongly for ECH, that the torsion has nothing to put in it.
4. **O1/O6 are then not total derivatives.** Eq. (11)'s `O1 = ½O4 − O2` is an off-shell identity
   (verified independently in `operator_basis_adjudication_2026_08_07.md` §2, null vector
   `[2,2,0,−1,0,0]`), so with `O4 ≠ 0` the step `O4 = 0 ⟹ O1 = O6 = −O2` fails, and with it the
   claim that O1/O6 "contribute zero to the equations of motion and zero to the vacuum energy"
   on the `T = κS` branch.
5. **O5's coefficient also shifts** on the true branch: the `−(3/2)κ(J⁵·J⁵)` reduction
   (`main.tex:1957–1962`) becomes `−3κ γ²/(1+γ²)(J⁵·J⁵)`; the non-axial term drops out of O5 (it
   is annihilated by the ε contraction), so O5's *disposal class* is unaffected — only its
   numerical coefficient.

**A third, cleanly quotable internal contradiction.** App. C's scope paragraph says the lemma
does not enumerate

> "non-minimal torsion irreps (trace-vector and tensor irreps) that appear only when the minimal
> coupling assumption is relaxed." (`main.tex:2693–2695`, p. 21)

and App. A 1 says "no non-minimal (trace/tensor) torsion irreps are admitted, as these lie
outside the stated minimal scope" (`main.tex:2384–2386`). Eq. (E2) generates a trace-vector
torsion irrep under **minimal** coupling, from the Holst term, at every finite γ. These three
statements cannot all be true.

**Impact on the closure.** Not fatal. On the ECH branch, O1, O4, O6 all reduce to
Planck-suppressed four-fermion structures of the same order as O5 (e.g.
`O4^{[4]}/O5^{[4]} = κ M_Pl² γ/(1+γ²)`, an O(0.2)–O(6) number depending on which Planck mass the
prefactor uses). They therefore fall into the paper's disposal class (ii) — the
M_Pl^{-2}-suppressed Fierz-closed sector — which is already bounded by the single-scale NDA
argument. The final "no (meV)⁴ without a new light scale" conclusion is unchanged. What must go
is (i) the claim that O4 ≡ 0 and that this is "a strictly stronger disposal", (ii) the claim that
O1/O6 are exact total derivatives on the `T = κS` branch, (iii) the abstract's trichotomy clause
"the ε-contracted torsion-square under the purely axial Cartan torsion", (iv) the Table III
O4/O1 rows, and (v) the operator-level step in Route 2's dark-energy leg case (i) — see MAJOR-2.

**Requested action.** Either scope the whole operator list explicitly to the γ→∞ Einstein–Cartan
torsion (and then state plainly that the ECH torsion at γ ≈ 0.24 is *not* on that branch, which
weakens the survey's reach), or redo Sec. V (a)/(b), App. A 1 Check D, Table III rows O1/O4/O6,
the abstract trichotomy, and Sec. IV A case (i) with the Eq. (E2) torsion. Option 2 is a
half-page of arithmetic and leaves the no-go intact; I recommend it.

### MAJOR-2 [correctness/scope] — The released artifact cited as verifying "O4 ≡ 0 on shell" evaluated an **Einstein–Cartan** configuration, not an Einstein–Cartan–**Holst** one; the paper reports it as an ECH on-shell verification

**Anchors:** App. A 1, p. 19 (`main.tex:2414–2418`); Data & Code Availability, p. 16
(`main.tex:2158–2166`); artifact
`research/theory_audit/operator_basis_adjudication_2026_08_07.md` §1, §5.

**What the paper claims.**

> "`O_4^{[4]} ≡ 0` on shell, both as an algebraic identity **and when evaluated in an explicitly
> curved on-shell Einstein–Cartan configuration obtained by solving the Cartan equation for ω at
> `T = κS ≠ 0`** (independent operator-basis adjudication script …)" (`main.tex:2414–2418`)

and in Data & Code Availability:

> "evaluates every member on an explicitly curved on-shell Einstein–Cartan configuration —
> establishing in particular that the ε-contracted torsion-square O4 vanishes identically under
> the purely axial Cartan torsion." (`main.tex:2161–2165`)

**What the artifact actually computed.** The adjudication report states its own premise
explicitly:

> "The paper's on-shell Cartan torsion `T^{abc} = κ S^{abc} = (κ/4) ε^{abcd} J^5_d` is verified to
> be **pure axial** — vector part zero, tensor part zero `[L09]`. **This fact turns out to decide
> MAJOR-2.**" (artifact §1)

i.e. the script *imposed* the pure-axial torsion as its input and then confirmed that O4 vanishes
on it. It never solved the Holst-modified connection equation
`Q_γ(e^{[I} ∧ T^{J]}) = J^{IJ}` at finite γ; γ does not enter the computation at all. The
artifact is a correct verification of an Einstein–Cartan statement and is honestly labelled as
such internally. The manuscript, however, promotes it to an ECH on-shell verification, and the
"independent symbolic computation" language in the abstract and Sec. V inherits that promotion.

This is a scope-honesty defect independent of MAJOR-1's math: even if a referee accepted the
pure-axial premise, the paper's cited evidence does not test it against the Holst sector that
defines the framework. I verified the six cited artifact files are byte-identical between the
pinned commit `1130b7c5e3d2` and current `HEAD` (all six SAME), and that
`dim4_parityodd_enumeration.py` contains exactly `[CHECK A]` and `[CHECK D]` and no enumeration
— both provenance claims in Data & Code Availability check out. The defect is in what the
computation is *said to establish*, not in its integrity.

**Requested action.** State that the released adjudication is evaluated on the Einstein–Cartan
(pure-axial) torsion branch, and either extend it to the finite-γ ECH torsion of Eq. (E2) or
restrict the on-shell claims accordingly.

## 3. MINOR FINDINGS

**MINOR-1 [presentation] — "Levi-Civita *symbol*" with a metric-dependent contraction identity.**
Sec. II (p. 2, `main.tex:~578`) reads: "the spacetime Levi-Civita **symbol** is normalized to
`ε^{0123} = +1` (whence `ε_{abcd} ε^{abce} = −3! δ^e_d`, which fixes the −3/8 and −3/2
coefficients of Sec. V and Appendix A 1)". Eq. (9) repeats "Levi-Civita **symbol** with
`ε^{0123} = +1`" (p. 14). For a metric-independent *symbol* with `ε_{0123} = ε^{0123} = +1` the
contraction is `+3! δ^e_d`; the `−3!` is the Lorentzian *tensor* identity (`ε_{0123} = −1`). The
intended convention is unambiguous from the identity, but the sign of two load-bearing
coefficients (−3/8, −3/2) hangs on it, and the released adjudication flags the same ambiguity
("the symbol-vs-tensor choice is a single overall `1/√−g`", artifact §1). Write "Levi-Civita
tensor", or add `ε_{0123} = −1`.

**MINOR-2 [presentation] — the quoted Nieh–Yan identity's coefficients disagree with Eq. (11) and
the stated explanation does not account for the discrepancy.** Sec. V (p. 14, `main.tex:1876–1878`)
quotes `d(e_I ∧ T^I) = T_I ∧ T^I − e_I ∧ e_J ∧ R^{IJ}` (coefficients 1 : 1 : 1) a few lines
before Eq. (11) gives `2·O1 + 2·O2 − O4 = 0` (i.e. 1 : 1 : ½ on O4). The paper attributes this to
"the form-versus-density normalization of the Nieh–Yan entry … a schematic normalization
misstates them by a factor of two" (`main.tex:1866–1870`). But rescaling O2 alone cannot produce
a relative factor 2 between the O4 and O1 terms; the actual source is the wedge combinatorics of
`T∧T` versus `e∧e∧R` in the form→density conversion (the released adjudication records
`d(e_I∧T^I)_dens = ¼·O4 − ½·O1`, §4). Give the conversion in one line so a reader can reproduce
Eq. (11) from the quoted identity.

**MINOR-3 [presentation] — mixed barred/unbarred Planck mass inside one displayed identity.**

`κ = M̄_Pl^{-2}` of Sec. II and an order-of-magnitude identity at the full mass]"
(`main.tex:2422–2425`). The identity is exact only with `M̄_Pl`, which the display writes as
`M_Pl`. Write `M̄_Pl² κ² = κ`. (I confirmed at 300 DPI that Sec. II's reduced-mass definition
