# P4 v1.0.254 exact-board truth audit

## Scope and artifact binding

This audit covers every `MAJOR` and every concrete `MINOR` in the three raw
reviews in this directory. The board is validly bound to repository commit
`96c3a97eaa33a543420c2e26702fb9e279b87461` and the 29-page PDF with SHA-256
`d8d4896d651f172a9fa407951cea12d96ac79e796ff0a88dad2dc2c7fd6533dd`.
The manifest records Grok 4.3 and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview as native-PDF direct
API legs and GPT-5.6-sol (high) as a ChatGPT-subscription Codex CLI leg, with
`openai_api_used=false` and `anthropic_used=false`
([`manifest.jsonl`](manifest.jsonl), records 1--3). The local cached review PDF
was independently SHA-256 checked and `pdfinfo` reports 29 pages.

Disposition labels below mean:

- **NEW-REAL:** a defect newly demonstrated on this exact artifact.
- **STANDING-DISCLOSED:** a real limitation already stated as an open gate; it
  is not evidence of a new regression or an undisclosed claim.
- **FALSE/STALE:** contradicted by the pinned source, committed evidence, or
  rendered PDF.
- **OPINION/VENUE:** a reasonable editorial preference, not a correctness gate.
- **MINOR-PRESENTATION:** a bounded clarity defect or optional robustness
  improvement that does not overturn the central result.

## Codex-subscription findings

### C-M1 — public semantic bootstrap is not independently runnable

**Disposition: NEW-REAL MAJOR. Edit/code fix warranted: yes.**

The public bootstrap downloads only
`build_apjs_release_v1_0_244.py` and
`apjs_release_schema_v1_0_244.json` into `pinned-source`
([bootstrap](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/apjs_release_v1.0.253_semantic_contract/validate_p4_catalog_c_semantics_v1_0_253.py),
lines 78--118; pinned at commit `c546a102484a953ec289fd63c3e3332ff09a47fc`
by `SEMANTIC_CONTRACT.json`). The validator imports
`reproduce_p4_primary_null_v1_0_244` at module load
([validator](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/build_apjs_release_v1_0_244.py),
lines 28--29), while the release inventory is downloaded into a separate
`release` directory. A clean reproduction containing exactly the two declared
pinned-source files fails before argument parsing with
`ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'reproduce_p4_primary_null_v1_0_244'`.
The immutable dataset publication receipt proves that the published bootstrap
is byte-identical (5,355 bytes, SHA-256
`901034ebc8281d4bacec0d2d8841ff3e41a5b62885e1ab326326502acf0ccde5`),
not that it executes
([dataset receipt](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/outputs/canonical_provenance/p4_hf_contract_dataset_receipt_v1_0_253.json)).
The manuscript's “independently runnable” statement is therefore false on the
exact release ([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
line 1650). Fix by publishing all import-time dependencies in the location the
pinned validator expects (and pinning their hashes), or by removing the
validate-only import dependency; then execute the bootstrap from an empty
directory and retain the receipt.

### C-M2 — quarantine membership is not checked row by row

**Disposition: NEW-REAL MAJOR. Edit/code fix warranted: yes.**

The validator fully scans the 8,474,531-row safe catalog for twelve primary
semantic properties, but its quarantine pass reads only `is_primary_hc`,
`unsafe_reason_code`, and `do_not_use_for_science`; it compares aggregate counts
and never compares `object_id` membership or per-object HC flags against the
safe catalog's `raw_flip_qc_unsafe=True` set
([validator](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/build_apjs_release_v1_0_244.py),
lines 269--344 and 347--506). The retained PASS receipt confirms those aggregate
gates and the primary-row gates, but contains no quarantine identity gate
([semantic receipt](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/outputs/canonical_provenance/p4_catalog_c_semantic_validation_v1_0_253.json)).
Thus it does not independently prove the manuscript's claim that *all* 249,066
flagged rows are in quarantine ([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
line 948). Add disk-backed uniqueness and exact set-equality checks on
`object_id`, plus row-level equality between `is_primary_hc` and `primary_hc`;
rerun over the immutable public payload and retain a new receipt.

### C-M4 — historical classifier realization cannot be reconstructed

**Disposition: STANDING-DISCLOSED MAJOR/GATE. New manuscript edit warranted: no.**

The underlying limitation is real: the two committed histories disagree on
26,616 versus 26,626 rows and 93.6878% versus 92.10%, and no stable object/split
manifest or random-state receipt survives. But v1.0.254 already states every
element, explicitly says the gap can harbor training/selection bias, discloses
the unremoved GZ1 overlap, and limits TTA to output-protocol flip equivariance
([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
lines 787--789, 1449--1467, 1502--1503, 1651, 1664). This remains a legitimate
publication-readiness gate that only recovered evidence, a reconstructed model,
or editorial acceptance of the bounded observed-label scope can close. Repeating
the same caveat cannot make the historical realization reproducible.

### C-M5 — “definitive bias mitigation” overstates the demonstrated control

**Disposition: NEW-REAL wording MAJOR (scientifically bounded). Edit warranted: yes.**

The sentence “provides the definitive bias mitigation”
([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
line 1485) conflicts with the immediately preceding statement that the seven
tests are necessary but insufficient and with the manuscript's explicit open
spatial-confusion and joint-covariance gates (lines 1338--1345, 1363--1368).
The demonstrated result is structural flip-equivariance and mitigation of the
measured raw orientation channel, not definitive bias control. Replace
“definitive” with that bounded description. This wording defect does not
invalidate the observed-label null, but leaving it invites a physical-bias
interpretation the manuscript otherwise rejects.

### C-M6 — full-catalog systematics metadata are unavailable

**Disposition: STANDING-DISCLOSED MAJOR/GATE. New manuscript edit warranted: no.**

Redshift, imaging leg, depth, seeing, and PSF are absent from the released
full-catalog product. This limits independent reproduction of spatial-selection
analyses and is a real ApJS utility/reproducibility gate. It is not newly hidden:
Data Availability lists those exact absent fields and calls the metadata sidecar
open ([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
line 1650); the spatial-transfer discussion limits claims to the observed
hard-label field (lines 1310--1345), and the conclusion says no physical bound is
supported (lines 1358--1368). The existing morphology sidecar is useful but does
not close the missing fields. Close by publishing a keyed executable sidecar, or
retain this explicit scope and obtain venue/editorial agreement; do not relabel
this standing gate as a newly discovered regression.

### G-m3 — rerun the HC estimator on the exact FSC support

**Disposition: MINOR-PRESENTATION / optional new robustness computation.
Edit warranted: not without a new computation.**

The 405-pixel arithmetic is correct: HCRI has 23,682 pixels and FSC has 24,087.
The manuscript explicitly defines them as sample-dependent supports and warns
against conflation ([source](../../../../../../pipelines/p2_chirality/chirality_catalog_paper.tex),
lines 810--813, 1025). It does not contain the requested FSC-support HC rerun and
there is no evidence that its change in `z` is `<0.1`; that threshold must not be
asserted. Moreover, forcing HC labels onto a mask defined by full-sample
occupancy changes the primary support rule and may introduce low-HC-occupancy
pixels. An explicitly labeled sensitivity rerun could be useful, but it is not
required to support the predeclared HCRI statistic. If performed, preserve the
primary definition and report the actual result, including any change exceeding
0.1.

## Central-claim and readiness assessment

All three reviewers agree that the narrowly stated primary result is supported:
the high-confidence **observed-label** real-space chirality-dipole statistic is
consistent with its fixed-occupancy random-label null. The Codex leg independently
reports recomputation to `z=0.705317`, `p=0.224678`; the manuscript binds the
selected/support counts and null convention and explicitly disclaims a
true-spin, physical-amplitude, or primordial-parity bound. None of the findings
above falsifies that narrow result.

The exact board is nevertheless **not minor-revisions-only** because C-M1,
C-M2, and C-M3 are genuine public-release contract failures, and C-M5/C-m7 need
bounded wording repair. C-M4/G-m1, C-M6, and C-m8 remain standing publication
gates. Readiness must therefore remain **80 at most**; this audit provides no
basis for an increase and makes no claim of acceptance. After fixing and
republishing the bootstrap, quarantine identity gates, and model card; applying
the two bounded text corrections; and obtaining a fresh exact-PDF three-leg
board, readiness should be reassessed from evidence rather than advanced
automatically.
