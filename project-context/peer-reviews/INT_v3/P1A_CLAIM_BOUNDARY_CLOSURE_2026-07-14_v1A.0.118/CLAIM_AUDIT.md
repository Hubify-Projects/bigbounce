# P1A v1A.0.118 active-claim audit

## Verdict

**Pass.** No active, reader-visible inference remains from
`<J5> = 0` to an absent/vanishing coherent field, a vacuum stress tensor,
`w = -1`, an equation of state, or a claim that the contact operator generally
cannot source late acceleration.

## Audit method

The source was scanned line by line after excluding `%` comments and every
`comment` environment. The active-source search covered `mean field`,
`mean-field`, `coherent`, `w=-1`, `<J5>`/`J^5`, `vanish`, `absence`, `EOS`,
`equation of state`, `does not source`, `late acceleration`, and closure/table
language. The compiled PDF text was independently scanned for the rejected
positive formulations.

The following unsupported active formulations have count zero:

- `coherent mean field` as a section/result title;
- `no coherent` or `vanishing coherent` as a derived result;
- `does not source coherent dark energy`;
- `supply neither late acceleration`;
- any active `w=-1` inference;
- any use of a vanishing axial one-point current as a condensate, vacuum, or
  stress-tensor proof.

## Surviving claims and why they are supported

1. **Finite-density benchmark only.** At the explicitly specified homogeneous
   benchmark `n_psi = 100 cm^-3`, the coefficient-one scale is
   `kappa*n_psi^2 = 9.9542e-80 eV^4 = 3.5571e-69 rho_Lambda`; inclusion of the
   actual `3/16` coefficient gives `6.6695e-70 rho_Lambda`. The manuscript now
   labels this a scale comparison, not a vacuum stress tensor or equation of
   state.
2. **Conditional direct-channel sign result only.** For the declared
   `G_s*(psi_bar psi)^2` normalization, hard Euclidean four-ball cutoff, and
   standard direct-channel mean field, `G_scalar=-3*kappa/64<0` admits no
   nonzero real homogeneous scalar-mass solution. The paper retains every
   regulator, Fierz, exchange, flavor/color, and beyond-mean-field limitation.
3. **Separate scalar-sector identity.** Remaining active `vanish` statements
   concern zero scalar spin density and the exact Bianchi vanishing of the
   constant-Immirzi Holst contraction on the torsion-free canonical-scalar
   branch. They do not use `<J5>=0`.
4. **Boundary statements.** The two remaining reader-visible `<J5>` passages
   explicitly say that no coherent order, vacuum expectation, metric stress,
   or equation of state is inferred. The appendix occurrence is only the
   Fierz operator identity. The closure-table caption explicitly says that it
   tests neither coherent axial order nor a cosmological stress tensor/EOS.
