## Summary

The manuscript re-derives the matter-contraction local amplitude f_NL = -35/16 vertex-by-vertex, localizes the factor of two relative to Cai et al. (2009), derives a scheme-qualified transmission band through three bounce backgrounds, and propagates the result through pulsar-timing, PBH, and LSS channels before stating a joint (r, f_NL) no-go for constant-c_s P(X) matter bounces. I re-ran the committed sympy derivations (byte-identical output), independently evaluated Cai et al.'s printed Eq. (37) from the arXiv source (confirms exactly half of every quoted Cai amplitude), verified every Li/Cai/Quintin equation attribution against the rendered arXiv PDFs, and reproduced the transmission, tensor, window, curvaton, PTA, induced-GW, DESI and survey-reach numbers by hand or from the committed JSONs. The central results stand. Four items must be fixed before publication: Table V's f_PBH columns are wrong at their labeled grid points in 3 of 5 rows; the DBI "best case r_min = 12.6" in the abstract uses an undeclared |f_NL| <= 5.1 criterion different from the Planck-95% criterion of the adjacent number (on a common criterion it is ~10.3-11.2); p. 8 and p. 13 quote incompatible shortfalls (8-9 decades vs 10^6.2) for the same 1.7e-14 first-order tensor amplitude; and the reproducibility statement does not currently resolve on the public branch it names. Recommendation: MAJOR REVISIONS (bounded, mechanical; the science is not in question).
