## Executive adjudication

The central claim is supported by all three reviewers: the equivariant,
high-confidence, hard-label real-space dipole is consistent with its declared
isotropic pixel-permutation null (`z_mom=+0.55`, one-sided empirical-rank
`p=0.265`). The exact v1.0.243 closure independently froze the inclusive-mask
calculation at `N=949,584`, 23,682 pixels, amplitude `0.0045970743`, and the
10,000-draw null. Nothing in this panel falsifies those values.

That support is narrow. It is not evidence for calibrated true-galaxy-spin
sensitivity, a physical upper limit, primordial parity, or a likelihood-level
rebuttal of a different classifier/selection. P4 already says this repeatedly.
The panel does confirm that an ApJS submission still has release and catalog-
utility gates: immutable archive/DOI/commit provenance is prospective; the
reconstructed raw/flip probability mismatch must be resolved or the unsafe
columns must be unmistakably quarantined; and open DP4-15/16/17/21 limitations
must not be converted into physical claims.

No readiness uplift is warranted from this confirmation round. The narrow
science survives; publication packaging and catalog-integrity work remain.
