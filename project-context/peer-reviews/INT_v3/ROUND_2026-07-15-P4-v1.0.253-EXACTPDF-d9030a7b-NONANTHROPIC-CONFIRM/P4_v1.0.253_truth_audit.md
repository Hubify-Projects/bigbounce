## Dispositions

### Grok — MINOR REVISIONS

1. **Conflicting historical training records — STANDING / DISCLOSED REFLAG.**
   The source explicitly preserves the 26,616/26,626-row, 826/846-non-spiral,
   and 93.6878%/92.10%-accuracy conflicts and the absence of the historical
   object/split/random-state record (manuscript lines 784 and 1451–1465). This
   is a real standing training-realization gate, not a new v1.0.253 defect.
2. **No formal preregistration or pre-unblinding record — STANDING / DISCLOSED
   REFLAG.** The limitation is stated directly at lines 808 and 1364. The
   declared estimator hierarchy does not create evidence that was never
   retained, and the paper does not claim that a formal preregistration exists.
3. **Persistent archive and machine-readable manifest — MIXED.** The absent DOI
   is a standing, honestly open archive gate. The narrower request for a direct,
   referee-usable machine-readable manifest URL is a **VERIFIED NEW
   PRESENTATION MINOR**: checksums and release artifacts exist, but the PDF does
   not give readers a direct manifest endpoint.
4. **Finite injection grid allegedly needs a clearer disclaimer — FALSE / STALE.**
   The source repeatedly and explicitly labels the finite grid and Stage-B
   fractions as descriptive pilots, not calibrated completeness, coverage, or
   physical-amplitude limits. No missing scientific qualification was found.

### Gemini — MINOR REVISIONS

1. **Inline hashes, paths, and JSON names — EDITORIAL PREFERENCE.** These
   strings are deliberate audit bindings in a catalog/methods article and are
   already consolidated in Data Availability. Further compression may improve
   readability but is not a scientific or reproducibility defect.
2. **Move the training-record conflict to an appendix — EDITORIAL PREFERENCE.**
   The conflict properly appears in the data description and is expanded in an
   appendix. Moving it would change presentation, not evidence or validity.
3. **Spatial-confusion limitation allegedly lacks a high-level summary — FALSE /
   STALE.** The paper already states at high level that the scalar transfer is
   incomplete, spatially varying confusion is unresolved, and the observed-label
   result is not a true-spin or physical-parity constraint.

### Codex subscription — MAJOR REVISIONS

1. **Catalog B is not reproducibly defined — VERIFIED NEW MAJOR.** The paper
   presents Catalog B as a released Platt-calibrated tier and reports its
   all-row count and handedness fraction, but the public schema defines only
   Catalog C. The retained recalibration artifact is not a valid substitute: it
   reports an incompatible fraction and NaN Brier scores. Catalog B currently
   lacks an exact transform, fitted parameters, payload, and validation receipt.
   It must be released reproducibly or removed as a released catalog tier.
2. **Release validation does not enforce row-level schema semantics — VERIFIED
   REAL, BOUNDED MAJOR.** The validator checks names, counts, selected flags,
   exclusions, and hashes, but does not independently enforce identifier
   uniqueness/nullness, coordinate domains, allowed class values, score bounds
   or simplex behavior, or the declared relations among `class_eq`, `is_spiral`,
   `score_eq_max`, and `primary_hc`. The payload may be correct; the present
   receipt does not prove the advertised machine-readable semantic contract.
3. **v1.0.253 binding remains stamped v1.0.252 — VERIFIED NEW MINOR.** Both
   public cards still identify v1.0.252 as current, and the fail-closed
   source-to-claim validator is hard-coded to v1.0.252 and fails on the current
   manuscript version. Provider revision pins may remain immutable, but the
   current paper/card binding and validator must recognize v1.0.253.
4. **Model-card “leg provenance” claim — VERIFIED NEW MINOR.** The phrase
   contradicts the dataset card and manuscript, which correctly state that
   full-catalog imaging-leg metadata are unavailable. The model card must use
   the same limited provenance scope.
5. **Overcausal attribution of the hemisphere excess — VERIFIED NEW MINOR.**
   “Specifically” attributes the excess to the GZ1-label/depth mechanism even
   though multiple mechanisms remain unresolved. The defensible statement is
   consistency with that mechanism, not established causation.
6. **Unspecified 157 rejected parent objects — VERIFIED NEW MINOR.** The paper
   gives neither rejection criteria and reason counts nor a rejected-object
   ledger. Those items are necessary to make this small part of the catalog
   selection function reviewable.

## Board conclusion

All three reviewers independently support the narrow central claim: the
declared 949,584-object high-confidence observed-label dipole is consistent
with its fixed-occupancy null. This support does **not** convert the result into
a true-spin constraint or a physical/primordial-parity bound.

The board nevertheless identifies two new or newly verified major release
defects: Catalog B is presented without a reproducible public definition, and
the release validator does not prove the row-level semantic contract it claims
to validate. Four additional Codex release/presentation defects and Grok's
direct-manifest-link issue are real minors. The training-realization,
preregistration, spatial-transfer/joint-covariance, and DOI gates remain open;
editorial preferences and stale critiques do not count as new defects.

Readiness therefore remains **80**. There is no acceptance decision and no
basis for claiming minor-revisions-only convergence while the two verified
major defects and the standing scientific gates remain open.

   transform and parameters, schema, and validation receipt, or remove Catalog B
   from the released-tier claims and associated headline table.
2. Extend the release validator with fail-closed row-level semantic checks and
   regression tests for every declared invariant, then generate a fresh receipt

3. Synchronize v1.0.253 card/current-version language and the source-to-claim

4. Correct the model-card provenance scope, weaken the causal sentence to a

5. Compile and visually audit the resulting exact PDF, publish any changed
