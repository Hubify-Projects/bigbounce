### 13. Claude [MAJOR 5] — the null "rests on a monopole-leakage interpretation of several genuinely large raw deviations; this interpretation should be stress-tested, not asserted"; the definitive injection–recovery mock is explicitly declined ("We do not run this mock here"); asks for at least one end-to-end selection-function/injection test **on the focal contrast**
**Verdict: DISCLOSED-RE-FLAG on the premise + declination disclosed with justification; the reviewer's requested forward-injection test is RETAINED as the one GENUINELY-NEW-REAL bounded closure (see below).**

Findings on the two sub-parts the task flagged:
- **(a) Is the declination disclosed with justification?** YES. `tex:2331–2349` is an explicit *"Residual-ambiguity disclosure (honest limitation)"*: it names the definitive end-to-end injection–recovery mock, states *"We do not run this mock here — it requires a DR2-grade selection-function mock and bears only on the secondary T-Web diagnostic, not on the focal contrast — and we flag it explicitly as the DR2 validation step."* Justification (needs DR2-grade external data; scoped to the secondary diagnostic) is given. The declination is a disclosed limitation, not a silent gap.
- **The premise "the null rests on monopole-leakage" is partly FALSIFIED for the FOCAL null.** The focal void-vs-non-void Δf_CW is *algebraically invariant* under any catalog-wide monopole shift — it cancels in the two-sample difference (`tex:4857–4862`). The large raw single-arm deviations that DO receive the monopole-leakage reading (cluster −4.7σ, bright −5.28σ, sky-region −4.75/−5.28σ) are secondary, and the leakage hypothesis is already *quantitatively* tested by σ_pred subtraction: those excursions collapse to `|σ_obs−σ_pred| ≤ 1.55` monopole-subtracted (`tex:1481–1487`), with an independently re-measured spatially-uniform monopole `f_CW^P5 = 0.49719`. So the leakage reading is not merely "asserted a dozen times"; a monopole-subtraction consistency test exists and is committed.
- **(b) Would an injection–recovery mock be a REAL, bounded, computable closure from committed artifacts?** The **full** end-to-end DR2-grade mock is NOT computable from committed artifacts (the paper's own justification: it needs a DR2-grade selection-function mock, external data not yet available) — that remains a legitimately-deferred open-compute item under directive-L (DR2/Rubin future work, `tex:2339–2349`). **However**, a **bounded semi-analytic forward-leakage injection** IS plausibly computable now from already-committed numbers, and it is materially different from the σ_pred *subtraction* the paper already does: rather than subtracting a fitted catalog-wide monopole, it would *forward-propagate* the measured per-imaging-leg and per-target-program classifier biases (per-leg `|σ|<3`, imaging-leg dipole `0.455%`, bright/dark residual `0.81 pp`) through the measured env-class × tracer-program contingency (`χ²=4933`, Cramér's V `0.078`, `tex:2388–2411`) to **predict** each large raw single-arm σ and the filament bright-vs-dark Δf_CW, then report the fraction of each observed deviation the known systematics reproduce. Per the audit's conservative tie-break and the task's explicit steer, this is retained as GENUINELY-NEW-REAL. See closures section for the honest, bounded scope.
