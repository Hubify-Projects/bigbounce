This is a referee report for the manuscript "Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ and a joint $(r, f_{NL}^{local})$ no-go for single-field matter bounces" submitted to Physical Review D. 

The manuscript presents a rigorous, multi-channel consistency test of the matter-bounce scenario, specifically focusing on the parameter-free local non-Gaussianity amplitude $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$. The author performs a from-scratch in-in computation that resolves a known factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature, carefully tracks the amplitude through the non-singular bounce under two different continuation schemes, and propagates the resulting predictions through four observational channels (PTA, PBHs, SMBH seeds, and LSS). The theoretical physics is sound, the distinction between gauge and physical frames is handled correctly (particularly the $\delta N_c$ vs $\zeta_{Mald}$ resolution), and the observational comparisons are appropriately scoped and statistically rigorous. 

However, the manuscript contains pervasive formatting violations—specifically the inclusion of internal audit tags, raw file paths, and version-history language directly in the main text—that fall below PRD publication standards. These must be cleaned up before the paper can be accepted.

Below is the itemized list of findings.

### ESSENTIAL
**A3M-E1: Internal Audit Tags and File Paths in Main Text**
*   **Location:** Throughout the manuscript (Pages 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17).
*   **Problem:** The author has left internal laboratory file paths, script names, and audit tags inline within the physics text and figure/table captions. Examples include:
    *   Page 3: `(research/theory_audit/a2_lapse_monopole_adjudication_2026_09_07.md)`
    *   Page 7: `(outputs/inlab_delta2_zeta_2026-09-03.json)` and `(pta_injection_30bin_2026_09_02.py)`
    *   Page 7 (Table IV): `pta_gamma_reproduce.py`
    *   Page 8 (Fig 1): `outputs/sigw_nhz_from_lab_spectrum_2026_09_04.json`
    *   Page 9 & 10: `outputs/pbh_compaction_fnl.json` and `pbh_compaction_fnl.py`
    *   Page 11: `outputs/survey_reach_fnl.json`
    *   Page 13: `row19_lambda`
    *   Page 15 & 17: `(research/theory_audit/a2_lapse_monopole_adjudication_2026_09_07.md)`
*   **Required Fix:** Remove all raw file paths, script names, and internal markdown/json references from the main text, footnotes, and captions. All such artifacts must be strictly confined to the "Reproducibility Statement" section at the end of the paper, or cited properly as external data releases if minted with a DOI. 

**A3M-E2: Version-History Language in the Body**
*   **Location:** Page 16, Appendix A.2.
*   **Problem:** The text contains explicit internal version-history and drafting notes: "(An earlier draft of this appendix printed $f_{map}^{fin}$ composed with the initial-label total -5, evaluating to $-25/4 + 15/4 \mu^2$ under the initial-label name; both numbers above are correct, the label was mixed.)"
*   **Required Fix:** Remove this parenthetical. Published papers must present the final, correct derivation without narrating the internal drafting history or previous typos of the manuscript.

### MINOR
**A3M-M1: Ambiguous Variable Notation ($p=1.6$)**
*   **Location:** Page 11, Section VI.A.
*   **Problem:** The text states: "...and recover $f_{NL}^{local} = -2.2 \pm 25$ at $p = 1.6$ - $0.06\sigma$ from DESI’s published..." In the context of statistical reporting, "$p=1.6$" immediately reads as an invalid p-value (which cannot exceed 1), especially when juxtaposed directly next to a $\sigma$ distance. While a domain expert will recognize $p$ as the quasar response/merger parameter, this is confusing for a general PRD reader.
*   **Required Fix:** Explicitly define $p$ here to avoid statistical confusion (e.g., "...at a quasar response parameter of $p = 1.6$").

**A3M-M2: Uncomputed Qualitative Claim**
*   **Location:** Page 14, Section VIII.
*   **Problem:** The text states that the curvaton route "only becomes detectable for $r \gtrsim 23$ - essentially the single-field value this section excludes." 
*   **Required Fix:** Replace the qualitative intensifier "essentially" with a precise quantitative statement (e.g., "approaching the excluded single-field value of $r=24$").

### NIT
**A3M-N1: Duplicate Word**
*   **Location:** Page 15, Section IX.C, item (vii).
*   **Problem:** Typographical error: "and and (vii) the consistency relation".
*   **Required Fix:** Delete the duplicate "and".

## Summary recommendation
MINOR REVISIONS

The manuscript is an exceptionally thorough, physically rigorous, and highly reproducible piece of theoretical cosmology. The resolution of the factor-of-two discrepancy in the in-in computation and the careful handling of the $\delta N$ gauge mapping are valuable contributions to the literature. The observational forecasts and null results are derived with commendable statistical care. However, the pervasive inclusion of internal file paths, script names, and drafting-history notes directly in the main text violates PRD formatting standards. Once these stylistic and formatting artifacts are cleaned up and relegated entirely to the Reproducibility Statement, the paper will be ready for publication.
