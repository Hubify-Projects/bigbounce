## Codex subscription addendum

The exact-PDF Codex subscription review (`gpt-5.6-sol`, high) returned **MAJOR
REVISIONS** with three major and two minor findings. Fresh source/artifact
inspection confirms all five. This addendum supersedes the direct-provider-only
statement above that there were zero genuinely new scientific or numerical
findings. It does not change the bounded central conclusion: the observed-label
HC real-space dipole remains null-consistent, but two manuscript claims are
factually unsupported and the primary null should be changed before submission.

| ID | Codex finding | Fresh truth audit | Required closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| COD-1 | The primary pixel-permutation null assumes exchangeable pixel asymmetries despite occupancy-dependent variance. | **Confirmed methodological defect; existing P4-243-03 re-flag, now closure-requiring.** `run_dipole_catalog_c.py` and the retained primary reproducer permute pixel `A_p` values while leaving pixel occupancies/fit weights at their sky locations. That does not preserve the conditional variance of `A_p` when occupancy varies. The committed same-sample per-galaxy shuffle already preserves the fixed footprint and occupancies and still yields a null (`N=949,584`, amplitude `0.004597`, `z=0.696`, rank `p=0.2298`; `c11b_hc_dipole_nulls.json`), versus pixel-permutation `z=0.545`, `p=0.2741`. Thus the qualitative null survives, but the headline exact `p=0.265` is not validated by an occupancy-preserving primary calibration. | Promote the fixed-occupancy per-galaxy CW/CCW randomization (or an equivalent conditional-binomial null) to primary; retain its exact 10,000-draw array, generator, seed, and checksum; recompile all headline values/captions. Keep pixel permutation only as a robustness check. |
| COD-2 | The 25,790-to-26,616 training provenance and reproducibility description contradict production code. | **Confirmed factual/provenance defect.** The manuscript's listed counts sum to 25,790, but `train_v2_fast.py` constructs 26,616 rows before splitting: 6,637 GZ1 + 17,153 CE spiral + **826 CE-selected non-spiral** + 2,000 synthetic non-spiral. Horizontal flips are generated on the fly for the consistency loss and do not add rows. The script also leaves CE non-spiral sampling, synthetic generation, `torch.randperm` splitting, and epoch shuffling unseeded; the committed semantics JSON records final sizes but does not repair exact reproducibility. | Correct the source-count table/text and CE-derived fraction; describe flip consistency without claiming row augmentation; publish the exact object/split manifest and seed every stochastic operation, or explicitly state that the historical training run is not exactly reproducible. |
| COD-3 | The exact reviewed catalog products are local and unavailable. | **Confirmed standing submission gate (P4-243-01 / DP4-21-adjacent release work), not a new numerical error.** Data Availability explicitly says the v1.0.244 Parquets remain local. The exact science payload, quarantine, manifest, validation receipt, immutable tag, and DOI are absent from the public bound release. | Upload the exact content-addressed science and quarantine payloads, publish checksums/manifest/validation receipt, freeze code and paper to an immutable tag, mint the archive DOI, and verify public retrieval before any ApJS submission claim. |
| COD-4 | ECE lower bounds mix catalog-wide mean confidence with GZ1-subset accuracies. | **Confirmed genuinely new statistical error.** Jensen's inequality applies only when confidence and correctness means are over the same evaluation objects. The paper combines catalog-wide `mean(max p_eq)=0.951` with full-GZ1-cross-match accuracies `0.58707698` and `0.69910840`; the committed artifact contains no GZ1-subset mean confidence or binned ECE. The stated `ECE >= 0.36/0.25` bounds therefore do not follow. | Remove those numerical lower bounds, or recompute mean confidence, correctness, bins, ECE, and uncertainty on the identical object-level GZ1 evaluation sample (and define the chirality-only denominator separately). |
| COD-5 | The 69.91% GZ1 metric is called disjoint without object-level exclusion. | **Confirmed factual/provenance defect.** `B20_B21_results.json` reports `0.6991083998` on 117,205 chirality-evaluable rows within the full 240,919 match. It contains no training-ID exclusion. The manuscript obtains 234,282 only by subtracting 6,637, while the training script does not persist a training-object manifest; subtraction cannot establish either disjointness or an unchanged metric. | Reconstruct and publish stable object IDs for the 6,637 training GZ1 rows, anti-join them from the evaluation cross-match, and recompute the full confusion matrix/accuracy/kappa; otherwise remove “disjoint,” “independent,” and the 234,282 claim and describe the metric as overlap-contaminated external-label agreement. |
