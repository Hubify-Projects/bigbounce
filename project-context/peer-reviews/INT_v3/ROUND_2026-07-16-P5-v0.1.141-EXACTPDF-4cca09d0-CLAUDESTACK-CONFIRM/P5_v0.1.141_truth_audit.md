### 4. Grok [MINOR] — §VI.E/Table X: "the semi-analytic forward-leakage calculation … relies on an untested assumption of no program-by-environment interaction; a one-sentence statement that a full DR2 selection-function mock is required to close this loop must be added" (THE finding targeting the new v0.1.141 content)
**Verdict: DISCLOSED-RE-FLAG** (both demanded elements are already in the manuscript — one verbatim inside the very paragraph under review).
This is the only finding this round that attacks the new forward-leakage analysis. Evaluated honestly against the committed `[A47]`/`[A48]` artifacts, it is a re-flag, not a genuinely-new methodological objection:

- **"A full DR2 selection-function mock is required — must be added."** Already present, verbatim, in the same paragraph: *"While the definitive DR2-grade end-to-end mock is deferred above, the committed numbers already permit a bounded forward test…"* and the closing sentence *"This is a semi-analytic bounded surrogate and does not replace the deferred DR2 selection-function mock"* (`tex:2380–2381`, `tex:2416–2418`). The forward-injection explicitly does **not** claim to close the DR2 loop; it names the DR2 mock as the definitive deferred step. The exact sentence Grok asks to be added is the paragraph's own thesis statement.
- **"Untested assumption of no program-by-environment interaction."** The model assigns each arm its program-mixture-weighted monopole (`tex:2394–2398`) — i.e. it assumes the per-program classifier bias is environment-independent. That assumption is (i) made explicit by the model's own stated construction, and (ii) its limitation is disclosed repeatedly and independently of this paragraph: *"does not constrain a program-by-environment interaction"* (`tex:2451`); *"the existing mixture arithmetic cannot tightly bound interaction leakage … provide no robust evidence of a void-by-program interaction, but they also do not rule one out or establish that program leakage is negligible"* (`tex:2452`, `tex:2463–2465`); *"substantial program-by-environment interaction effects are not excluded"* (`tex:4505–4506`); and the interaction fit `[A43]`/`[A44]` is reported with sparse-stratum intervals spanning zero (`tex:3385–3392`). The interaction being untested/unbounded is a standing, disclosed limitation of the paper (SSOT v0.1.136 gate; v0.1.139 banner), not a hidden assumption the forward-injection smuggles in.

Both components are therefore already satisfied. No new in-paper closure is created: what Grok asks to "add" is present, and the substantive caveat it raises is disclosed. Under the conservative tie-break, a one-line cross-reference from the forward-leakage paragraph to the existing interaction-disclosure passages is at most venue-level editorial polish (gate (d)); it reveals no error, no missing analysis, and no unsupported claim. **Not genuinely-new-real.**
