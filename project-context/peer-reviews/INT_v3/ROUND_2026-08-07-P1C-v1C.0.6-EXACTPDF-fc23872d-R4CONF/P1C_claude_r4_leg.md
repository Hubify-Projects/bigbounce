## MINOR findings

**m1 — Table II (R3 row): attribution conflation.** [Anchor: Table II, p. 10, R3 row.] The row calls the Tier-III bound "the chiral-asymmetry beta-function bound (Benedetti–Speziale)." In the body (§IV.B) the chiral-asymmetry bound is Eq. (3), a chiral-count EFT ansatz motivated by Date–Kaul–Sengupta [8]; the Benedetti–Speziale flow is the *distinct* Eq. (4) that yields the derived 1.4×10⁻⁶ primary estimate. The parenthetical attaches the wrong provenance to the pessimistic bound and undersells the derived leg. Reword to, e.g., "the chiral-count ansatz bound (DKS-motivated, Eq. 3); the integrated Benedetti–Speziale flow (Eq. 4) gives the far smaller derived value."

**m2 — Tier-wording misalignment between §V.a and Table II.** [Anchor: §V.a, p. 9 vs Table II, p. 10.] §V.a states "R2–R3 are Tier-III ansatz-level estimates," while Table II records R3 (and R1) as "(II)+(III)" — the mass-dimension lock being structural Tier-II. The table caption says it records "the highest level at which each leg is claimed," under which the §V.a sentence reads as a contradiction on R3. One clarifying clause ("their *amplitude* legs are Tier-III") resolves it.

**m3 — R1 benchmark transcription check.** [Anchor: §II, p. 2 and Table II R1 row, p. 10.] Recomputing κn_ψ²/ρ_Λ with κ = 8πG (equivalently M̄_Pl⁻², M̄_Pl = 2.435×10¹⁸ GeV), n_ψ = 100 cm⁻³ = 7.68×10⁻⁴⁰ GeV³, ρ_Λ = (2.25 meV)⁴ gives 3.9×10⁻⁶⁹, vs the quoted 3.6×10⁻⁶⁹ (≈8% low). Immaterial at the stated order-of-magnitude precision and to the ≈68-order claim, but since §II makes a point of specifying the exact inputs ("computed with the exact κ = 8πG"), either state the input set that yields 3.6 or update the mantissa for consistency with the companion.

**m4 — Unexplained gaps in branch lettering.** [Anchor: §III, Fig. 1, Table I.] Branches run H, J, L, M, N, O — skipping I and K with no comment. In a paper explicitly preserving a "historical catalog," a reader will reasonably wonder whether two branches were dropped. One footnote (even just "letters I and K were never assigned / retired in the historical catalog") prevents the inference of missing entries.

**m5 — Imported one-loop coefficients not independently verifiable from this manuscript.** [Anchor: §IV.A (ST Eqs. 37/41/42/46/51: α₄ = −6/(1+γ²), Ω₄₄, Ω₂₄), §IV.B Eq. (4) (B–S "(23γ²+5)", cited to [3] Eq. 7).] I verified internal consistency (the |Ω₄₄/α₄| ≈ 3.3 evaluation checks; Eq. (4) integrates to exactly the quoted 1.4×10⁻⁶) but could not confirm the transcribed coefficients against the source papers within this review. Since Route-2/Route-3 budgets lean on these being faithful transcriptions, a final pre-submission spot-check of each quoted equation number against the cited arXiv versions is warranted (the paper already flags "arXiv version" for ST Eq. 42, which suggests a known version-dependence worth stating for the others too). This is a due-diligence request, not an error claim.

**m6 — Acknowledgments phrasing.** [Anchor: Acknowledgments, p. 13.] "We acknowledge the foundational contributions of [seven named researchers] for their derivations…" can be misread as implying personal involvement or endorsement by the named individuals. Standard journal practice: rephrase as "This work builds on the published derivations of…" (with citations), reserving "acknowledge" for actual participants/funders.

**m7 — Residual referee-response prose and abstract length.** [Anchor: abstract; §IV.A pp. 6–7.] The abstract is ~300 words of densely hedged single-paragraph text, and the body retains audit-trail sentences that read as baked-in referee responses — e.g. "the eV-vs-GeV unit conversion is exact 1 GeV = 10⁹ eV and is not a source of ambiguity" and "The role of each denominator factor is stated explicitly so the contraction is not misread as a double division." The transparency is commendable, but several of these defend against misreadings no fresh reader would make; trimming them (and tightening the abstract toward journal norms) would improve the paper without any loss of scope honesty. Related nit: the abstract's "each closing a specific route" sits slightly awkwardly with B14 constraining all four routes (correctly stated everywhere else).

**m8 — Fig. 1 cannot visually carry the B8-vs-B14 distinction its caption asserts.** [Anchor: Fig. 1 and caption, p. 4.] B8 and B14 share the Branch H box, so the four route-arrows from that box are attributed to B14 (with B8 constraining R1 only) *only in the caption text* — the drawing itself cannot show it. Either annotate the arrows (e.g. label the four-route fan "B14" and a distinct R1 arrow "B8") or note in the caption that per-barrier arrows are not resolved at box granularity. As drawn, a reader reconstructing the catalog from the figure alone would mis-assign B8's reach.
