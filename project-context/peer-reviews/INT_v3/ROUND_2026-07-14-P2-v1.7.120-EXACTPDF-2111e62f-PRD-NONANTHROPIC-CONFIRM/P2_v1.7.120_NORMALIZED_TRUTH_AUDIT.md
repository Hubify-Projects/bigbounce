## Executive adjudication

The central contraction-phase result, \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\), is
supported by the exact manuscript more strongly than the harshest review
summaries acknowledge. The appendix already contains the four source-derived
vertices, exact per-vertex limits, six-Wick convention, collapsed polynomial,
epsilon-order grouping, in-in sign convention, and Li closed-form cross-check.
Gemini explicitly calls the central result rigorously supported; Grok calls it
supported by the symbolic certification and cross-checks. OpenAI's request for
a wholly new derivation is therefore mostly stale/over-escalated, though a
short reader-facing provenance summary would improve clarity.

The panel does expose two real publication-positioning problems. First, the
manuscript still calls the conditional 2.63-sigma mapping its "observational
headline" even though the required third-order bounce transfer and published
per-triangle SPHEREx covariance are unavailable. Second, the phrase
"UV-completion independence" remains broader than the evidence. These are not
reasons to fabricate a cubic calculation or covariance. They are reasons to
demote the survey number to an illustrative conditional mapping and state the
robust result as a pre-bounce contraction-phase amplitude.

No readiness uplift follows from this round. P2 remains in revision and its
verified cap remains 74.
