> "…places a 95% sensitivity upper limit `A_{95}^{obs} ≃ 0.98%` (full-amplitude); **this is an observed-label sensitivity floor, not a physical parity-amplitude bound, which remains gated on the morphology transfer function.**"

The abstract **already carries** the operative scope qualifier — all three components the reviewers ask for:

Gemini's proposed fix is already in the abstract verbatim in substance.

The trigger condition ("states A_95 *without* its scope qualifier") is therefore **false** — the qualifier is present. This clause was integrated at v1.0.264→265 across 7 body sites and drew **zero objection** at v1.0.265. The v1.0.266 re-raise is a presentation-gradient re-flag, not a missing caveat → **DISCLOSED-RE-FLAG**.

The substantive over-reading risk (reading 0.98% as a physical parity bound) is already blocked by "not a physical parity-amplitude bound."
