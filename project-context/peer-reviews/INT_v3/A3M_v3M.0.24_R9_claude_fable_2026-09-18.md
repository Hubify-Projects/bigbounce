## Summary (5 lines)

The manuscript (i) re-derives the matter-contraction squeezed amplitude f_NL^local = −35/16 vertex-by-vertex in the in-in formalism, localizes the factor-of-two slip in Cai et al. (2009) to their amplitude-conversion step, and adds a separate-universe/threading cross-check; (ii) bounds the linear transmission of that amplitude through three nonsingular bounce backgrounds (T ≤ 1/2 in scheme S1) and computes, for the first time, the bounce window's own cubic contribution Δf^bounce = −(5/24)ρ_B; (iii) propagates the model's CMB-anchored spectrum to three observational channels (PTA slope, PBH abundance, LSS bispectrum), finding two nulls and one non-discriminating channel; (iv) states a joint (r, f_NL) no-go in c_s for the full P(X) class and a surviving curvaton route. The science is honestly graded and most numbers reproduce from the committed scripts. Two issues rise to the level of major revision: the λ-treatment in Sec. VIII is internally inconsistent with the printed Eq. (15), and the quantitative c_s-window numbers (Table VII, "f_NL^after = 1.0–1.4×10^11") are presented without any statement that the cubic-order computation has left its regime of validity. The qualitative conclusions survive both fixes; the presentation does not.

## MAJOR issues

1. **Eq. (15) is Li et al.'s Eq. (5.1), not their Eq. (4.19), and it already contains the cubic coefficient λ at its P ∝ Xⁿ value; Sec. VIII's λ-analysis is therefore inconsistent with the printed equations (Sec. VIII, Eq. (15), the paragraph beginning "Restoring the cubic coefficient λ does not open the window", and the italicized no-go statement).**
   Sec. VIII prints f_NL^pre(c_s) = −165/16 + 65/(8c_s²) "from their Eq. (4.19)" and then states that restoring λ "shifts f_NL^pre only by the constant −30λ/Σ", scans "λ/Σ ∈ [−1, 1], the matter-contraction line λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/(6c_s²), and the DBI line λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/(2c_s²)", and reports that cancelling the 1/c_s² growth "would require λ/Σ = 7/(16c_s²)". (5.1) = −165/16 + 65/(8c_s²) is obtained by substituting λ/Σ = (1−c_s²)/(6c_s²) (their Eq. A.20). Check: −30(1−c_s²)/(6c_s²) = −5/c_s² + 5, and −245/16 + 5 = −165/16, 105/8 − 5 = 65/8. Consequences for the manuscript as printed: (a) taking Eq. (15) as the baseline and adding −30λ/Σ on the "matter-contraction line" double-counts λ; (b) the DBI-line result (r_min = 12.57 at c_s = 0.524) and the 7/(16c_s²) cancellation value are only reproducible from the λ = 0 form −245/16 + 105/(8c_s²), which appears nowhere in the paper (7/16 = 105/(8·30)); (c) the phrase "shifts only by the constant −30λ/Σ" is wrong on both named lines, where λ/Σ ∝ 1/c_s² is not constant in c_s; (d) the italicized conclusion "excluded jointly by r and f_NL … independently of the cubic coefficient λ" is, on the artifact, correct — but a reader of the PDF cannot verify it, and the printed chain of equations does not support it. Fix: print the general-λ form −245/16 + 105/(8c_s²) − 30λ/Σ as the primary equation (with the ζ̇³-vertex origin, Li et al. Eq. 4.18); state explicitly that Eq. (15) is its specialization to the P ∝ Xⁿ line, citing Eq. (5.1)/(A.20); re-word the λ-scan paragraph so every number is measured from the λ = 0 baseline; and correct "constant" to "a purely local-shaped (Σk_i³) term".

2. **The quantitative c_s-window numbers at the tensor-viable sound speed are outside the regime of validity of the cubic-order computation, and the manuscript never says so (Table VII row 2, "f_NL^after at that c_s = 1.0–1.4×10^11"; Sec. VIII "raises f_NL^after from row 14's 6–9×10⁵ to 1.0–1.4×10^11"; abstract "driving f_NL^after to 1.0–1.4×10^11").**
   At c_s = 1.5×10⁻³ the bounce term of Eq. (16) scales as (25/24)ρ_B/c_s⁴ ≈ 1.4×10^11 and the pre-bounce term as 65/(8c_s²) ≈ 3.6×10⁶. With ζ_rms ≈ 4.6×10⁻⁵ at CMB scales, the perturbative expansion parameter f_NL·ζ exceeds unity for |f_NL| ≳ 2×10⁴, i.e. the tree-level in-in bispectrum has lost control roughly six orders of magnitude before the printed value. (The paper does apply a perturbativity criterion, 1.2|f_NL|σ_r, in Sec. V B — so the authors have the tool, but do not apply it here.) Two further points compound this: the bounce term at c_s ≠ 1 is evaluated under the S1 dressed-metric prescription (z = a, ε_eff = 1/2), which the paper itself calls "an assumption of the scheme and not a derivation", so the 1/c_s⁴ law is a scheme artefact whose small-c_s behaviour has no independent support; and the claimed "strengthening" of Li et al.'s no-go (10⁶ → 10^11) is a comparison of two numbers neither of which is a prediction of a controlled calculation. The qualitative no-go is not in doubt — it is already secured by Li et al.'s own 3.6×10⁶ and by r = 24c_s — but PRD should not print 10^11 as an f_NL "requirement". Fix: replace the Table VII entry and the corresponding text with a statement that perturbative control is lost for c_s ≲ c_s^{pert} (give the number from f_NL·ζ ~ 1 or from the Sec. V B criterion), that the no-go therefore follows from the loss of control plus r = 24c_s rather than from a specific f_NL value, and that the Planck-viable boundary c_s ≥ 0.624 (where |Δf^bounce| ≈ 2.5 and the expansion is still controlled) is the only quantitatively meaningful edge of the window.

## Questions for the authors

1. What justifies applying the S1 dressed-metric prescription (z = a, ε_eff = 1/2) at c_s ≠ 1? The 1/c_s⁴ growth of Eq. (16) comes entirely from the R2/R4 boundary terms and the V2 vertex carrying 1/c_s² factors; is there any independent (e.g. S2 or exact-mode) evidence for that scaling below c_s ≈ 0.6?
2. Assumption (A4) freezes the contraction bispectrum at η_h and evolves linearly, but the growing mode has ζ' = −3ζ/η ≠ 0 at η_h. Why is the frozen-shape handoff self-consistent for a non-attractor mode whose bispectrum is still evolving at the handoff time? Does the "evaluation-time" ambiguity listed in Sec. IX C (item ii) change the S1 band [−0.65, −0.50] by more than its width?
3. In Sec. II D and Appendix A you show that the separate-universe construction fails because N_i = O(1/k_L); given that, in what sense is the comoving-slice value f^c_NL = −5 "well-defined" (Sec. II D, last sentence) rather than an artefact of an invalid gradient expansion?
4. For the injection test, why are the recovered posterior widths (∼0.17) so much narrower than the data refit (0.38)? Is the 30-bin real-data likelihood being re-centred in a way that removes the bin-to-bin scatter that broadens the real posterior?
5. What is the criterion behind "detectable only for r ≳ 23", and why is the intrinsic term compared against σ_fNL rather than against the curvaton's own O(1) f_NL, which would dominate the observable bispectrum at any r_dec?
6. Is the sign of Δf^bounce (negative, i.e. enhancing |f_NL|) robust to the choice of window [−η_B, η_B]? Eq. (7) depends on η_B only through ρ_B; what happens to the coefficient −5/24 if the window is asymmetric?
