# P1A v1A.0.121 primary-source coefficient check

Checked source: L. Freidel, D. Minic, and T. Takeuchi, *Quantum gravity, torsion, parity violation, and all that*, arXiv:hep-th/0507253v2.

The v2 PDF was fetched directly from arXiv and checked before the manuscript edit.

## Connection variation and contorsion

For minimal coupling, their Eq. (17) gives

\[
e_I{}^\mu C_{\mu JK}
=4\pi G\,\frac{\gamma^2}{1+\gamma^2}
\left(
\frac12\epsilon_{IJKL}A^L
-\frac1\gamma\eta_{I[J}A_{K]}
\right).
\]

With the paper's identification \(A^I=J_5^I\), zero axial spin current implies \(C_{\mu JK}=0\). Torsion is algebraically constructed from the contorsion and tetrad, so \(C=0\) implies \(T=0\). The manuscript also supplies an independent invertible-tetrad kernel proof for the canonical-scalar zero-source equation.

## Back-substitution coefficient

Their Eq. (23), specialized to minimal coupling, gives

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm int}
=-\frac32\pi G\,\frac{\gamma^2}{1+\gamma^2}A^2.
\]

Using only \(\kappa=8\pi G\),

\[
-\frac32\pi G=-\frac{3}{16}\kappa,
\]

so the manuscript coefficient

\[
\mathcal L_{4\psi}
=-\frac{3\kappa}{16}\frac{\gamma^2}{1+\gamma^2}J_5^2
\]

is exactly the primary-source relation in the declared normalization. No new coefficient was inferred or fitted.
