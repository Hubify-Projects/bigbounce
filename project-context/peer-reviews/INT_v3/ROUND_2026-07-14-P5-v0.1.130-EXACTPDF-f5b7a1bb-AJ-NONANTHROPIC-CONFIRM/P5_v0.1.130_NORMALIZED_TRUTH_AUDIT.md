## Executive adjudication

The narrow empirical statement is supported: in this released-parent,
classifier-labelled, redshift-space DESIVAST sample, the adjusted contrast is
consistent with zero. Gemini calls the claim rigorously supported subject to
Paper IV; Grok says the catalog-native non-detection is supported; OpenAI calls
it plausible. This does **not** establish physical-handedness independence,
real-space environment independence, or a cosmological constraint, and the
manuscript already says so.

The round nevertheless confirms real publication barriers. Paper IV supplies
the labels; the final label/weight/provenance release must be independently
reviewable. The P5 analysis bundle has no immutable public tag or DOI. The
post-hoc hierarchy is disclosed but the repeated term "designated primary"
still reads more confirmatorily than the evidence permits. The released parent,
VoidFinder hole-union membership, and GALZONE `OUT=0` quality universe are not
explained compactly enough to prevent a hybrid-estimand reading. Finally, the
exact covariance formula, 50 sky clusters, 78 design columns, finite-sample
correction, and 3,750-nearest-MAXIMALS sensitivity exist in artifact A37 but are
not printed in the manuscript. Those are bounded, evidence-backed fixes.

The environment-stratified GZ1 check is honestly underpowered: its void-arm
95% half-width is about 3.7 percentage points, much larger than the observed
0.1--0.6 percentage-point contrasts. No wording change can turn that into a
strong exclusion. It remains a label-validation/power gate.

No readiness uplift is warranted. P5 remains in revision at the verified cap
of 74.

## Fastest scientifically safe closure

1. Replace confirmatory-sounding "designated primary" language with a focal
   descriptive/exploratory estimate and move editorial acceptance language to
   the cover letter/Limitations.
2. Add one exact estimand flow and one compact model/covariance table populated
   from A37, including the two existing cluster scales.
3. Reorder/condense the main text so the focal released-parent result precedes
   secondary diagnostics; move detailed T-Web/Tempel/ASTRA material to
   supplement where practical.
4. Clarify that `GALZONE TARGET universe` + `OUT=0` defines the released quality
   parent, while exact VoidFinder hole-union membership defines the void arm.
5. Freeze and publish the analysis bundle, verify every A1--A40 link, and record
   the immutable identifier. Keep Paper IV and the label-power gate open.
6. Recompile/audit and run a new exact-PDF AJ panel only after those closures.

This plan uses metadata and artifacts that already exist rather than rerunning
the whole analysis or inventing unavailable selection products. It cannot
bypass the Paper IV, archive, or label-power gates.
