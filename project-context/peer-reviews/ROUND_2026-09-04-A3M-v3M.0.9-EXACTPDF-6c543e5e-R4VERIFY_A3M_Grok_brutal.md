**Referee Report**

**Paper**: Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)

**Journal target**: PRD (regular article)

**Round context**: Full adversarial first-read review against PRD standards.

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. I, p. 1; abstract)**  
Abstract states “the exact amplitude… reproduces this value exactly” and lists multiple \(\sigma\) tensions as primary results. Body (Sec. II–III) shows the value is recovered only inside one specific in-in commutator ordering plus one transmission scheme (S1) with \(k\eta_B\lesssim10^{-2}\). No other ordering or scheme is shown to give the identical number. Required fix: replace every occurrence of “exact” with “scheme-S1, commutator-ordered” and add an explicit caveat paragraph.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. III A, p. 4; Table I; abstract)**  
Abstract and Sec. I claim a unique transmitted amplitude range \([-0.65,-0.50]\). Table I and surrounding text give three backgrounds yielding \(T_{f_{\rm NL}}\in[0.165,0.409]\) and \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}\in[-0.65,-0.50]\) only inside scheme S1. Scheme S2 is stated to be divergent. The quoted interval is therefore scheme-dependent by construction. Required fix: state the interval is S1-only in the abstract and every headline claim; recompute all \(\sigma\) values under S2 or flag them as unavailable.

**A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. V B, p. 7; Table III; abstract)**  
Abstract claims the PBH channel yields a “shape-robust required-amplitude ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)\approx1.7{-}1.9\)”. Table III reports a grid mean 1.732 with std 0.050, but every entry uses the Gaussian-calibrated map whose leading term is truncated; the paper itself labels the map an “artefact”. The ratio is therefore an artefact of the truncation. Required fix: remove the ratio from the abstract and all summary claims or recompute with the untruncated map.

**A3M-E4 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. V C, p. 8; abstract)**  
Abstract lists the PBH channel as one of three “consistent” channels. Body states the channel returns a “clean null: 7.0 orders of magnitude short”. A null result cannot be listed as positive consistency evidence without an explicit qualifier. Required fix: delete the PBH channel from the abstract’s consistency list or add the qualifier “null result, does not constrain”.

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR, Sec. II C, p. 3; Sec. II D, p. 3)**  
The factor-of-two discrepancy with Cai et al. (2009) is resolved by declaring the in-in result authoritative and the separate-universe result “different variables”. No derivation shows why the two routes must agree; the identity Eq. (5) is only linear-order. The central claim of the paper therefore rests on an unproven assumption that the two formalisms are computing the same quantity.

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR, Sec. IV B, p. 5; Table II)**  
Refit \(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) is presented as 1.14\(\sigma\) from \(\gamma=3\). The refit uses a different prior and 30 bins versus the official 14-bin result. The two posteriors are not statistically comparable without a joint re-analysis; the quoted \(\sigma\) distance is therefore meaningless.

**A3M-M3 (MAJOR, throughout)**  
The manuscript contains 12 pages of dense tables, vertex-by-vertex bookkeeping, commit hashes, file names, and “reproducibility statement” prose. PRD regular-article limit for a focused result of this type is ~8 pages. The present length is driven by internal audit material, not by additional physics.

**A3M-N2 (MINOR, multiple locations)**  
Repeated self-referential phrases (“this paper’s own adjudication”, “scheme-S1 qualifier”, “reproducibility statement below”) read as internal notes rather than journal prose. Move to a methods appendix or supplementary material.

**A3M-N3 (NIT)**

**Summary recommendation**  
**REJECT**

The manuscript is a technical note whose central result (\(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)) is recovered only inside one computational scheme whose domain of validity is narrower than the scales probed by the three channels. The PBH channel is explicitly a null, the LSS channel does not discriminate the two candidate amplitudes at current sensitivity, and the PTA slope consistency is prior-dependent. The paper’s length is dominated by internal bookkeeping rather than new physics. These defects are structural; they cannot be repaired by minor revision.
