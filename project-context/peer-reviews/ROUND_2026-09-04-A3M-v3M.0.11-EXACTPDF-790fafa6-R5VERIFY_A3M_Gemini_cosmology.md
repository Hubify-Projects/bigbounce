This is a referee report for Physical Review D, evaluating the manuscript "Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local} = -35/16$" under the PRD-STANDARD profile. 

The paper presents a rigorous, multi-channel theoretical and observational audit of the matter-bounce scenario's non-Gaussianity prediction. The theoretical physics is highly competent: the from-scratch in-in derivation, the resolution of the factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature, the careful handling of the gradient expansion failure in the separate-universe approach, and the exact-mode bounce transmission calculations are all impressive and meet the high standards of PRD. The observational propagation through PTA, PBH, and LSS channels is similarly robust.

However, the manuscript fails several strict editorial and provenance checks. It contains internal lab-notebook language, contradicts its own abstract, makes an unsupported novelty claim, and explicitly defers required data-provenance steps. These issues must be resolved before publication.

Below is the itemized list of findings.

### ESSENTIAL FINDINGS

**ID: A3M-E1 (Abstract Drift)**
*   **Section:** Abstract (Page 1) vs. Section VI B (Page 10)
*   **Problem:** The abstract claims that for large-scale structure, "combining power spectrum and bispectrum widens the separation to $0.5-1.1\sigma$." However, the body text in Section VI B explicitly concludes that the separation is "under $1\sigma$" ($|\Delta f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}| \approx 0.35-0.55$ against $\sigma_{f_{\rm NL}} = 0.5-0.7$). The abstract's claim that the separation widens *up to* $1.1\sigma$ contradicts the body's calibrated summary. 
*   **Required Fix:** Reconcile the abstract to match the body's final calibrated statement. Do not claim a $>1\sigma$ separation in the abstract if the body explicitly caps it at "under $1\sigma$".

**ID: A3M-E2 (Side-by-side sigmas without qualification)**
*   **Section:** Table II (Page 6)
*   **Problem:** The table presents $z_{\rm off}$ (official posterior tensions) and $z_{\rm refit}$ (refit marginal tensions) side-by-side. These are derived from different null procedures/conditionings. There is no explicit "not directly comparable" qualification at the juxtaposition in the table or its caption.
*   **Required Fix:** Add an explicit "not directly comparable" qualification to the Table II caption regarding the side-by-side $z_{\rm off}$ and $z_{\rm refit}$ columns.

**ID: A3M-E3 (Missing frozen-release DOI)**
*   **Section:** Reproducibility Statement (Page 13)
*   **Problem:** The text states: "This work's own code and artifacts are pinned by the GitHub commit hash above rather than a frozen-release DOI of their own; minting one is a maintainer action planned for the packaging stage."
*   **Required Fix:** PRD requires actual frozen-release DOIs (e.g., Zenodo) for computational artifacts at the time of publication, not promises for a future "packaging stage." Mint the DOI and update the manuscript accordingly.

**ID: A3M-E4 (Version-history and internal audit tags)**

    *   "...correcting an earlier statement in this program..." (Page 4)

*   **Required Fix:** Remove all version-history language, internal audit tags, and internal directory placeholders. The paper must read as a standalone scientific document, not a software changelog or lab notebook.

### MAJOR FINDINGS

**ID: A3M-M1 (False novelty claim)**
*   **Section:** Section II C (Page 3)
*   **Problem:** The author claims: "The present computation is, to our knowledge, the first that reproduces this value from an independent, from-scratch vertex-by-vertex derivation rather than transcribing a prior expression." However, in the exact same section, the text acknowledges: "Li et al. redo the in-in calculation for general sound speed and print the amplitude directly in their Eq. 5.1, which reduces to -35/16 at $c_s=1$." If Li et al. (2017) already redid the in-in calculation, the claim of being the "first" is contradictory and unsupported.
*   **Required Fix:** Remove or heavily qualify the novelty assertion. 

**ID: A3M-M2 (Standalone-reader violation)**
*   **Section:** Reproducibility Statement (Page 13) and Section VI B (Page 10)
*   **Problem:** The text states: "The bounce-to-local shape overlap (r, Sec. VI) is imported from this lab's companion Fisher-forecast draft at research/focused_paper_source_integration/..." 
*   **Required Fix:** A submitted manuscript cannot rely on concepts or variables imported from an unpublished, internal companion draft. Either define and derive the shape overlap $r$ self-contained within this manuscript, or remove references to it.

**ID: A3M-M3 (Uncomputed quantitative claim)**
*   **Section:** Section VI B (Page 10) and Section VII B (Page 11)
*   **Problem:** The text asserts: "...the not-yet-derived shape-overlap projection r would sharpen this somewhat but cannot by itself restore the pre-bounce-level $3\sigma$ separation." 
*   **Required Fix:** "Sharpen this somewhat" is an uncomputed quantitative claim. If $r$ is explicitly "not-yet-derived", you cannot robustly claim it cannot restore the $3\sigma$ separation without providing a bounding mathematical argument. Provide the bounding number or remove the qualitative confidence statement.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The core theoretical and observational physics in this manuscript is excellent, mathematically sound, and represents a valuable contribution to the literature on early-universe alternatives. However, the manuscript currently suffers from severe presentation issues, including abstract drift, internal lab-notebook language, missing data-provenance DOIs, and reliance on unpublished companion drafts. These issues violate PRD standards but are entirely fixable without requiring new physics derivations. Once the manuscript is cleaned up and the abstract is aligned with the body text, it will be a strong candidate for acceptance.
