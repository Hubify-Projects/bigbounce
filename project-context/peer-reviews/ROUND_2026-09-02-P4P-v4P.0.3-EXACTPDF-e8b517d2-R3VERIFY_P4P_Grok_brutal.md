**Referee Report – ApJS (Catalog Track)**

**P4P-E1 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Abstract (p. 1)  
Problem: Abstract states “an 8,474,531-galaxy DESI Legacy DR8 chirality catalog” and “N_support = 887,472 of 890,069 quality-controlled rows”. The body (p. 2) gives exactly these numbers, but the abstract omits the explicit qualifier that the 887 k support cut is the *post-support-pixel* HC-RI sample used for the dipole fit; the 890 k figure includes rows later quarantined by `raw_flip.qc_unsafe`.  
Required fix: Add the clause “(HC-RI supported-pixel subset after `raw_flip.qc_unsafe` quarantine)” to the abstract sentence or move the precise support definition into the abstract.

**P4P-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Abstract (p. 1) & §5.2 (p. 8)  
Problem: Abstract claims the catalog “disfavors an alignment-driven dipole above ~1 % at ≥95 % detection power”. The body (Eq. 1, p. 4) defines A_95^obs ≃ 0.98 % as the *observed-label* injection-recovery threshold on the primary channel. The abstract therefore reports a detection-power floor as though it were a physical-amplitude bound.  
Required fix: Insert “observed-label” before “sensitivity floor” in the abstract and add the parenthetical “(not a physical-parity bound)” already present on p. 5.

**P4P-E3 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: §3 (p. 4) & Table 5 (p. 8)  
Problem: The text asserts that every literature amplitude “exceeds A_95^obs by a factor of 2–20×”. Table 5 ratios are computed only after an illustrative g = 0.398 bridge that the authors themselves label “not adopted for strengthening”. The ratios therefore rest on an uncommitted auxiliary assumption.  
Required fix: Either (a) remove the numerical “factor of 2–20×” claim or (b) recompute the table under the minimal closure only and state the assumption explicitly in the table caption.

**P4P-M1 (MAJOR)**  
Section: §2.3 (p. 3)  
Problem: The primary null uses a fixed-occupancy label-randomization null (10^4 draws) while the FSC harmonic diagnostic uses a different support (24 087 pixels) and a NaMaster MASTER-decoupled estimator. No sentence states that the two z_mom values (+0.635 vs. +6.923) are not directly comparable.  
Required fix: Add an explicit “not directly comparable” qualifier at every juxtaposition of the two statistics.

**P4P-M2 (MAJOR)**  
Section: Data Availability (p. 9)  
Problem: The reproducibility paragraph cites Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21461899 for both the catalog and the exact null arrays, yet the commit hash and pipeline version strings in the archived release paper (v0.1.274) pre-date the manuscript version v4P.0.3. A reader cannot verify that the frozen arrays match the numbers printed here without downloading an earlier deposit.  
Required fix: Supply a single frozen DOI or git tag that exactly matches the numbers and masks used in v4P.0.3.
