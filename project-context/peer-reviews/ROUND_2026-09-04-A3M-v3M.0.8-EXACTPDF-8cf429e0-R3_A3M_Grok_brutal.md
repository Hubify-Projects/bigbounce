**Referee Report — PRD Regular Article (Track A flagship candidate)**

**Paper**: A3M — Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)

**Finding A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL)** — Internal audit / version-control artifacts throughout the body (pp. 3, 4, 5, 8, 9). Multiple instances of literal filesystem paths (“research/theory_audit/fnl_bianchi_separate_universe_2026.09.03.py”, “research/track_a3_multichannel/pta_injection_30bin_realkde_2026.09.02.py”, “outputs/pbh_compaction_fnl.json”, commit hashes, “R3”, “v3M.0.8” metadata references, “earlier synthetic-density result”, “superseded claim”, “this section that a 6-bin… was the identical pipeline”) remain in the submitted PDF. These are not reproducibility statements; they are internal bookkeeping. PRD does not publish laboratory notebooks.

**Finding A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL)** — Abstract claims are not traceable to the body at the stated precision. Abstract states “\(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) consistent with the bounce prediction \(\gamma=3\) at \(1.14\sigma\)”. Body (p. 5, Table II) gives exactly this number from a 30-bin KDE refit, but the official NANOGraV 14-bin posterior is quoted as \(\gamma_{\rm HD}=3.2^{+0.6}_{-0.6}\) (1\(\sigma\)) and the refit Savage-Dickey factor is only 3.23. The abstract therefore presents a derived, prior-dependent number as the primary result without the required “under the authors’ refit prior” qualifier. The same abstract asserts “\(f_{\rm PBH}=0\) exactly” while the body (p. 7, Eq. 11 and Table III) shows the ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.732\pm0.050\) only after an ad-hoc Gaussian-calibrated normalization that is not part of the published Choudhury et al. pipeline.

**Finding A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL)** — Transmission result is scheme- and cutoff-dependent yet advertised as model-independent. Abstract and p. 1 claim “transmission through the bounce is now scheme-qualified rather than open”. Body (p. 4) restricts the bound \(0<T_{f_{\rm NL}}\leq1/2\) to “scheme S1 and \(k\eta_B\lesssim10^{-2}\)”. A second scheme (S2) is divergent. The abstract omits both restrictions. This is a direct violation of the instruction that every inequality or robustness assertion must carry an explicit labeled-assumption tag.

**Finding A3M-E4 (ESSENTIAL)** — PBH channel is a clean null only after an unreproducible spectrum shape. The paper’s own lognormal curvature spectrum is stated to be “not reconstructible from the published paper” (p. 6). The amplitude ratio 1.732 is therefore computed on an internal spectrum whose parameters (\(n_s=1-12w/(1+w)\), \(w=0.9649\)) are chosen after the fact. No table or figure shows the ratio under the exact spectrum used by Choudhury et al.; the claim “the PBH channel is a clean null” is therefore not supported by a standalone calculation.

**Finding A3M-M1 (MAJOR)** — Factor-of-two resolution is not independent. The paper repeatedly states it performs a “from-scratch, vertex-by-vertex in-in computation”. The only external validation offered is that it reproduces Maldacena’s de Sitter and ultra-slow-roll limits and Namjoo et al.’s ultra-slow-roll result. No independent cross-check against a second public bispectrum code or against the original Cai et al. interaction Hamiltonian is supplied. The “first computation” claim is therefore unsupported.

**Finding A3M-M2 (MAJOR)** — \(\sigma\) values from different null procedures are juxtaposed without the required qualifier. Table II places the official NANOGraV posterior (\(\gamma_{\rm HD}=3.2\)) beside the authors’ 30-bin refit (\(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\)) and reports Bayes factors without stating that the two marginals are not directly comparable. This violates the explicit rule on side-by-side \(\sigma\) values.

**Finding A3M-M3 (MAJOR)** — Length-to-contribution ratio excessive. The manuscript is 10 pages. The core new result is a single per-vertex table (Table I) plus a transmission integral under one scheme. The remainder is literature re-derivation and internal script manifests. PRD regular articles on similar topics are typically 6–7 pages.

**Finding A3M-N1 (MINOR)** — Abstract date “September 4, 2026” and future-dated Zenodo records (2026.09.02) appear in the PDF. These are cosmetic but indicate the document was never cleaned for submission.

**Finding A3M-N2 (NIT)** — Several axis labels in Fig. 1 use non-standard notation (“lognormal curvature power-spectrum amplitude \(A\)”) without defining the precise normalization constant in the caption.

**Finding A3M-N3 (NIT)** — Duplicate phrasing in reproducibility statement (“research/theory_audit” appears twice on p. 3).

**Summary recommendation**: REJECT

The manuscript contains multiple instances of internal laboratory bookkeeping that survived into the submitted PDF, presents scheme-dependent and prior-dependent results as model-independent, and makes abstract claims whose numerical values cannot be reproduced from the cited external literature without the authors’ private scripts. These are first-read rejection criteria for PRD. A cleaned, self-contained 6-page re-submission that (i) removes every filesystem path, (ii) states all scheme and cutoff restrictions in the abstract, and (iii) supplies a fully reproducible PBH calculation on a published spectrum would be required before the paper could be considered.
