**Referee Report – PRD Regular Article (A3M)**

**Paper:** Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)

**Finding A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section I (p. 1) and abstract: “the exact amplitude … \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)”.  
The body (Sec. II, Table I, Eq. 3) shows this value is obtained only inside the super-Hubble squeezed limit of scheme S1; scheme S2 yields a different transmitted amplitude \([-1.25,-0.50]\) (p. 5). The abstract presents the number as scheme-independent.  
**Required fix:** Remove “exact” from title/abstract or add an explicit caveat that the quoted value is S1-only and that S2 differs by a factor \(\sim2.5\).

**Finding A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Abstract vs. Sec. IV D (p. 6): abstract states “\(\gamma_{\rm pred}=5.07\), \(5.1\sigma\) off” while the body reports both \(5.07\) (lab spectrum) and \(5.00\) (dust-bracket). The \(\sigma\) values are computed from two different null procedures (official 14-bin vs. 30-bin refit) and placed side-by-side without the required “not directly comparable” qualifier.  
**Required fix:** Either recompute a single consistent \(\sigma\) or insert the qualifier at every juxtaposition.

**Finding A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Abstract claim: “PBH channel … \(f_{\rm PBH}=0\) exactly”.  
Sec. V B and Table III show \(f_{\rm PBH}(-35/16)\) is zero only after the Gaussian-calibrated truncation; the full quadratic map yields a non-zero (though small) abundance once the spectrum shape is allowed to vary. The abstract omits the truncation caveat present in the final calibrated statement (p. 8).  
**Required fix:** Rewrite abstract sentence to match the body’s conditional statement.

**Finding A3M-M1 (MAJOR)**  
Sec. III A and Table IV: the two candidate amplitudes \([-0.65,-0.50]\) and \([-1.25,-0.86]\) are reported as a “band” without a single, reproducible central value or covariance. Survey-reach forecasts (SPHEREx, MegaMapper) therefore cannot be recomputed from the quoted numbers.  
**Required fix:** Supply one fiducial transmitted amplitude per background together with the explicit \(r\) projection used.

**Finding A3M-M2 (MAJOR)**  
Fig. 2 and Table III: the amplitude ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)\) is stated as \(1.732\pm0.050\) (\(n=27\)). The grid scan uses only three \(\Delta\) values and four \(r_p\) values; the quoted standard deviation is therefore an underestimate of the true variation across the full prior volume.  
**Required fix:** Expand the grid or report the full range rather than a formal std.

**Finding A3M-M3 (MAJOR)**  
Sec. II D and Appendix A: the classical \(\delta N\) route and the in-in route are declared “different variables” whose gap is “explained at linear order”. No numerical cross-check at second order is supplied, yet the paper treats the in-in value as the unique truth.  
**Required fix:** Provide at least one explicit second-order \(\delta N\) number on the same background.

**Finding A3M-N1 (MINOR)**

**Required fix:** Replace with submission or revision date.

**Finding A3M-N2 (MINOR)**  
Multiple instances of “this lab’s” phrasing (pp. 1, 6, 7).  
**Required fix:** Replace with neutral wording.

**Finding A3M-N3 (NIT)**

**Finding A3M-N4 (NIT)**

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The manuscript’s central claim—that \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) is the unique, scheme-independent amplitude whose multi-channel consequences can now be tested—is not supported by the calculations once the S1/S2 discrepancy, the truncation dependence of the PBH result, and the non-comparable \(\sigma\) values are taken into account. The abstract systematically strengthens or omits the very caveats that the body ultimately records. These are not cosmetic issues; they are load-bearing for every forecast and every “honest null” statement. A substantially revised manuscript that (i) treats the two schemes on equal footing, (ii) supplies reproducible central values for every channel, and (iii) aligns the abstract with the final calibrated statements might be reconsidered, but the present version does not meet PRD standards.
