## Findings

### BLOCKERS (1)

#### **R48-B1 — Birefringence consistency claim violates its own stated identity (introduced by GGGGG R47-m3 closure)**
**Severity:** BLOCKER (publishes a self-falsifying arithmetic identity).
**Location:** §VII.E (Discussion → Caveats), L375.
**Quote:**
> "the bounce prediction $\beta = 0.27^\circ$ is consistent with the published Eskilt \etal~2022 joint Planck+ACT measurement $\beta_{\rm obs} = 0.342^\circ \pm 0.094^\circ$ at $0.45\sigma$ from the bounce prediction (where $0.45\sigma \equiv |0.342^\circ - 0.27^\circ|/0.094^\circ$)"

**Problem.** The right-hand side of the stated identity evaluates to:
```
|0.342 − 0.27| / 0.094 = 0.072 / 0.094 = 0.7660…
```
**0.766σ, NOT 0.45σ.** The paper has staked a load-bearing consistency claim on a 70%-wrong arithmetic identity that any reader with a calculator catches in 30 seconds.

**Confirmed numerically:**
```python
>>> abs(0.342 - 0.27) / 0.094
0.7659574468085108
```

**Where this came from.** GGGGG R47-m3 closure note in `SSOT/index.md` reads:
> "P2 v1.7.20 → v1.7.21 ... §VII.E birefringence aside gained explicit Eskilt+2022 0.342°±0.094° anchor with 0.45σ-from-prediction consistency statement"

The 0.45σ figure appears nowhere else as a derived consistency statistic in any prior peer-review note, the CLAUDE.md memory line on birefringence (which says "Pod 1 production 500MC 2026-04-29: SNR=20.32σ at ACT sensitivity ... consistency P1-prediction vs observation = 0.77σ"), or the upstream Pod 1 NaMaster summary.json. The CLAUDE.md memory carries the **correct 0.77σ** figure. The closure wave imported a wrong number into the paper.

**Why it cleared the closure-wave QC.** The R47 reviewer was likely satisfied that an explicit anchor + identity derivation was now present, without independently evaluating the right-hand side. This is the canonical failure mode of "trust the upstream summary verbatim" without re-deriving load-bearing arithmetic.

**Cross-paper consistency hit.** This same identity appears in the CLAUDE.md memory as 0.77σ (correct) and in the v1.7.21 paper as 0.45σ (wrong). When cross-vendor reviewers (GPT-5, Gemini-3.1-Pro, Grok-4) independently compute the consistency statistic, they will reach 0.77σ and report a contradiction with the published paper. This is the exact failure mode the cross-vendor R-round is designed to catch — but it is also exactly the kind of unforced error that should not be discovered there in the first place.

**Required fix.** Replace `0.45σ` with `0.77σ` in two places in L375:
1. "at $0.45\sigma$ from the bounce prediction" → "at $0.77\sigma$ from the bounce prediction"
2. "where $0.45\sigma \equiv |0.342^\circ - 0.27^\circ|/0.094^\circ$" → "where $0.77\sigma \equiv |0.342^\circ - 0.27^\circ|/0.094^\circ$"

The qualitative conclusion ("well within the observational uncertainty of the published joint analysis") is unaffected — 0.77σ is still under 1σ — but the identity must be self-consistent. Also propagate the corrected 0.77σ to any other site where the consistency statistic is restated; my grep found only the §VII.E site (the abstract intentionally avoids the birefringence aside).

#### **R48-m2 — σ_theory=2.0 encompassment claim is technically correct but the framing "≈1σ encompassment" is loose**
**Severity:** Minor (numerical-honesty polish).
**Location:** §V.C L209.
**Quote:**
> "Gaussian prior, σ_theory = 2.0 (≈1σ encompassment of the factor-of-two convention ambiguity; the Li \& Brandenberger value −2.1875 lies at 1.09σ from −4.375 under this prior, which is the closest σ_theory we report that brackets the convention shift)"

**Issue.** The math is correct: |−2.1875 − (−4.375)|/2.0 = 2.1875/2.0 = 1.09375σ, which the paper rounds to 1.09σ — accurate. **However**, the prose says "≈1σ encompassment" which is misleading because 1.09σ is *not* 1σ encompassment, it is *just outside* 1σ. The R47-n2 closure attempted to soften the previous "1σ encompassment" claim to "1.09σ-not-1σ" but the prose still leads with "≈1σ encompassment" before disclosing the 1.09σ figure. **Fix:** rephrase to "Gaussian prior, σ_theory = 2.0 (just-outside-1σ encompassment of the factor-of-two convention ambiguity; the Li & Brandenberger value −2.1875 lies at 1.09σ from −4.375 under this prior, which is the smallest σ_theory we report that places the convention shift within ~1σ tolerance)". The qualitative meaning is preserved; the quantitative honesty improves.
