**Referee Report: P4 (Physical Review D)**

**P4-E1 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Abstract (p. 1) and throughout (e.g., Sec. IV C, VI C).  
Problem: Different null procedures (per-pixel-shuffle, label-shuffle MASTER, binomial monopole-only, bootstrap) produce \(\sigma\) values that are presented on a common scale without explicit qualification that they are not directly comparable. Example: “the post-MASTER dipole significance is −0.122\(\sigma\) … / 0.43\(\sigma\) … The canonical-mask … is +3.64\(\sigma\)”. The abstract and headline claims treat these as interchangeable.  
Required fix: Add a dedicated paragraph (or table footnote) stating that each \(\sigma\) is defined only relative to its own null distribution; report p-values or empirical ranks as the primary cross-comparison metric. Recompute or re-label all headline numbers accordingly.

**P4-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Entire manuscript; length 56 pp.  
Problem: A methods/catalog paper of this scope (null dipole result plus extensive systematics audit) exceeds PRD norms (15–30 pp) by a factor of ~2. The narrative contains repeated explanations of the same leakage channel, multiple Monte-Carlo counts, and extended interpretation triages that could be condensed into one table + one paragraph.  
Required fix: Reduce to ≤25 pp. Move all auxiliary null tables, injection-recovery grids, and per-leg cross-spectra to a compact Supplementary Material file. Retain only the two load-bearing estimators (real-space 0.43\(\sigma\), subsample-mask MASTER −0.12\(\sigma\)) and the single clearest systematics diagnostic (+3.64\(\sigma\) canonical) in the main text.

**P4-E3 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Abstract and Sec. I (p. 1–3).  
Problem: Abstract states “sub-percent sensitivity” and “the measured dipole is consistent with null isotropy-breaking at sub-percent sensitivity”, yet the empirical 50%-recovery-at-3\(\sigma\) threshold is 0.75% (Table XVI) and the systematic floor from the residual 9.5\(\sigma\) monopole is 0.26%. The claim is therefore not what the body proves.  
Required fix: Rewrite abstract to read: “We place a 50%-recovery-at-3\(\sigma\) upper limit of |A| ≳ 0.75% on any dipole amplitude under the per-pixel-shuffle null; the statistical-only Fisher floor is ~0.29% full-amplitude. No detection is obtained above this threshold.”

**P4-M1 (MAJOR)**

## Summary recommendation  
**MAJOR REVISIONS**

The manuscript contains a technically sound null result on the subsample-mask MASTER estimator and a well-documented systematics channel. However, the 56-page length, the intermingling of multiple non-comparable \(\sigma\) values, the retention of internal reproducibility tags, and the mismatch between the abstract’s sensitivity claim and the body’s empirical threshold constitute essential barriers to acceptance. A focused revision that (i) cuts the paper by more than half, (ii) enforces a single, explicitly qualified sensitivity number, and (iii) removes every internal audit artifact would make the work suitable for PRD. Without these changes the paper risks presenting an over-narrativized methods catalog whose central claim is already a null.
