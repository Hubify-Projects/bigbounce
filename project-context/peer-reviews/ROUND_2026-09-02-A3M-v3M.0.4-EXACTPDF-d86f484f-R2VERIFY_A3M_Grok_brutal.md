**Referee Report for PRD (A3M)**

**Paper:** Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\): the exact amplitude, pulsar-timing slope, primordial black holes, and survey reach

**Findings**

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL)** — Abstract (p. 1) and Sec. IV B (p. 4): The abstract states “No channel is in tension with another” while Sec. III explicitly qualifies the transmission bound as “handoff-scheme-conditional” and “scheme-dependent (0.165–0.409)”. These two statements are incompatible; the abstract claim is stronger than the calibrated body statement. Required fix: rewrite abstract sentence or add explicit caveat in abstract.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL)** — Abstract (p. 1) and Sec. V B (p. 5): Abstract quotes the PBH amplitude ratio 1.732[1.610,1.809] without the body’s regime-of-validity qualifier (“anti-correlated branch … non-monotonic … not always perturbatively controlled”). The abstract therefore presents a load-bearing scalar stripped of its documented limitations. Required fix: either remove the interval from the abstract or reproduce the full caveat.

**A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL)** — Sec. II D (p. 3) and abstract: The paper repeatedly labels the in-in result “method-independent” while simultaneously stating that a Bianchi-I/separate-universe cross-check of the quadrupole (rather than the monopole) “remains open”. A claim of method-independence cannot coexist with an explicitly unresolved cross-check of the same quantity. Required fix: excise “method-independent” or complete the missing cross-check.

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR)** — Sec. V A (p. 5) and Table III (p. 6): The PBH ratio is computed on a 27-point grid whose non-monotonicity is disclosed only after the ratio is highlighted. The minimum near \(f_{\rm NL}\approx-0.35\) lies inside the quoted interval; the ratio is therefore not a stable observable. Required fix: replace the single headline ratio with the full non-monotonic curve or withdraw the ratio as a primary result.

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR)** — Sec. IV B (p. 4) and Table II: The 1.14\(\sigma\) and 4.63\(\sigma\) refit entries are Gaussian approximations to a bounded sampling prior; the text acknowledges they are “not itself Gaussian”. Presenting them side-by-side with official posteriors without an explicit “not directly comparable” tag at every juxtaposition violates the review instruction on \(\sigma\) values from different null procedures.

**A3M-M3 (MAJOR)** — Abstract and Sec. VI B (p. 6): The SPHEREx forecast numbers (3.13\(\sigma\), 4.38\(\sigma\)) are imported from a companion Fisher draft that “has not yet re-derived” the shape-overlap projection at the \(-35/16\) fiducial. The abstract therefore quotes un-re-derived forecast significances as though they were results of the present work.

**A3M-N1 (MINOR)** — Throughout (e.g., p. 2, 7, 8): Multiple instances of internal-audit phrasing (“every claim below is graded honestly”, “pinned to commit”, “reproducibility statement”, GitHub commit hashes, “R2VERIFY” metadata language) appear in the body. These are not standard PRD content.

**A3M-N2 (MINOR)** — Fig. 1 caption and Table III: Axis labels and table headers use inconsistent notation for the same quantity (\(f_{\rm NL}\) vs. \(A\)); units on the horizontal axis of Fig. 1 are never stated.

**A3M-N3 (NIT)** — Reference list: Several arXiv IDs and journal years are given without DOIs; a subset of quoted statistics (e.g., NANOGrav 14-bin posterior) cannot be verified from the cited abstracts alone.

**Summary recommendation**  
REJECT

The manuscript contains multiple essential mismatches between abstract claims and body qualifications, presents scheme-dependent and non-monotonic results as headline numbers without adequate caveats, and imports unre-derived forecast significances into the abstract. These are not cosmetic issues; they directly affect the central multi-channel consistency claim. The paper would require a major rewrite (new abstract, revised Secs. III–VI, removal of internal-audit prose) before it could be reconsidered at PRD standards.
