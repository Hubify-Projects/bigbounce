**Referee Report for PRD (A3M)**

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section I (p. 1) and abstract (p. 1): “Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)”.  
The abstract asserts multi-channel consistency while the body repeatedly states that (i) the PBH channel yields only a ratio, not a quotable abundance (Sec. V B), (ii) the transmission factor \(T_{f_{\rm NL}}\) is handoff-scheme-dependent and bounded only under assumption (A4) (Sec. III), and (iii) the SPHEREx projection has not been re-derived at the fiducial (abstract and Sec. VI B). These three caveats are not carried into the title or abstract claim of “consistency”.  
Required fix: Rewrite title/abstract to “contraction-phase amplitude plus partial multi-channel tests under stated assumptions” or equivalent; add explicit sentence that no channel currently yields a model-independent detection or exclusion.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Abstract (p. 1) and Sec. IV B (p. 4): \(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) (30-bin refit) versus official 14-bin posterior.  
The two posteriors are compared side-by-side with \(\sigma\) distances without the explicit qualifier “not directly comparable because of different binning and prior volume” at every juxtaposition.  
Required fix: Insert the qualifier in both abstract and Table II caption; recompute all \(z\)-distances only after identical binning.

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR)**  
Sec. V B and Table III (p. 6): ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.732[1.610,1.809]\).  
The ratio is presented as the channel’s “most reproducible output”, yet the text simultaneously states that \(f_{\rm PBH}(f_{\rm NL})\) is strongly non-monotonic and that the anti-correlated branch dominates for one candidate value. No effect-size or practical-significance statement accompanies the ratio.  
Required fix: Add Cramér’s V or equivalent; state the fraction of the 27-point grid on which the ratio remains within \(\pm6\%\).

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR)**  
Sec. III (p. 3) and abstract: transmission bound \(0<T_{f_{\rm NL}}\le1/2\) (scheme-dependent).  
The bound is derived only inside the linear handoff approximation; the paper’s own cubic self-interaction term is left uncomputed. The abstract nevertheless lists “exact amplitude” as one of the four headline results.  
Required fix: Move the transmission discussion to a dedicated limitations subsection; qualify the word “exact” in the abstract.

**Summary recommendation**  
**MAJOR REVISIONS**

The manuscript performs a technically careful in-in re-derivation and supplies useful cross-checks, but the headline framing of “multi-channel consistency” at the exact value \(-35/16\) is not supported by the body once all scheme dependencies, pending re-derivations, and non-monotonicities are acknowledged. The pulsar-timing comparison mixes incompatible binning choices without adequate qualification. These are not cosmetic issues; they affect the central claim that would justify a regular PRD article rather than a focused technical note. A revised version that either (a) narrows the title/abstract to the contraction-phase amplitude plus clearly labelled partial tests or (b) completes the missing transmission and SPHEREx projections could be reconsidered.
