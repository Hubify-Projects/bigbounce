## Referee Report — P3: "A Multi-Survey Autoencoder Anomaly-Candidate Catalog" (`paper3_draft.tex`, v3.1.149)

### (1) VERDICT: **MAJOR REVISIONS**

### (2) ISSUES

**[MAJOR] 1 — The two out-of-sample gates that specifically rebut the "in-sample scoring" concern are computed from k-fold models that fail the paper's own retain gate, and this is not disclosed.**
The abstract (L952) and §III (L1064) present "three independent gates" for the flagship DESI tier: 5-fold Jaccard $\bar J_{\rm CV}=0.862$, OOD Jaccard 0.732, and tail-preservation $\rho=1.00$. The 0.862 Jaccard (`held_out_rescore_result.json` → `kfold_stability_summary.json`) and the $\rho=1.00$ tail-preservation (`heldout_tail_preservation.json`) are **both** derived from the same fold score vectors `outputs/desi_kfold/scores/fold_{0..4}_scores.parquet`. The training run that produced those checkpoints is committed at `pathc_desi_kfold/results/training_summary.json:322-325`, and it reports `"all_folds_pass_gate": false`, `best_val_mean = 1.91` — every one of the 5 folds **fails** the paper's Path-C step-1 retain gate of val_loss ≤ 0.30 (per-fold `best_val_loss` = 1.18/0.85/0.76/4.91/1.86; L1046). The folds were trained for 0.4–4.4 s (`wall_time_s`), best epochs 0/1/2/8/3, and 4 of 5 show train_loss pinned at ~58 (no learning). Text at L1032–1033 and L1054 presents $\bar J=0.862$ as a clean "PASS" with no mention that the underlying models fail the retain criterion. A stability agreement between under-trained models cannot establish that the *released* catalog's tail "is not an in-sample-inflation artifact" (abstract L952; §II.F L1054). Authors must either re-run the k-fold with models that clear the val_loss gate, or prominently disclose that these two gates use undertrained proxy models and downgrade the strength of the claim accordingly.

**[MAJOR] 2 — The tail-preservation statistic is near-uninformative on these models, and the "three convergent gates" are not independent.**
The tail-preservation logic (`heldout_tail_preservation.py:22-31`) is: if the tail were memorization-driven, the held-out tail would be depressed. But a model with no capacity to memorize (train_loss flat ~58, `best_epoch=0` for fold 2) reconstructs held-out and in-sample rows equally poorly, so $\rho\approx1$ is close to tautological and does **not** probe in-sample inflation of the *production* ensemble (a differently, properly trained model, `desi_injrec_CORRECTED.json` seeds 101–505). Moreover, of the "three convergent out-of-sample gates," two (Jaccard, tail preservation) share the *same* fold score parquets, so they are not independent evidence; the only gate run on the actual production ensemble is the injection-recovery test (`desi_injrec_CORRECTED.json`, broad_emission_spike recall @5σ = 0.99), which tests planted-signal *sensitivity*, not whether the released tail is in-sample-inflated. The paper should not present two correlated undertrained-proxy statistics plus one sensitivity test as three independent confirmations that the headline is out-of-sample robust. (Verified: `kfold_stability_summary.json:32-92`, `scoring_summary.json:9-11` both point to `fold_N_scores.parquet` from the failed run.)

**[MINOR] 3 — Logging-scale inconsistency in the k-fold artifact undermines confidence the gate is even applied to the right metric.** In `training_summary.json`, per-fold `train_loss ≈ 58`, `best_val_loss ≈ 0.8–4.9`, but `holdout_mse_p50 ≈ 0.15` and scoring `score_p50 ≈ 0.148` (`scoring_summary.json`) — three mutually inconsistent scales (train_loss ≫ val_loss is itself backwards). The k-fold `score_p50` (~0.148) also differs from the production median MSE (0.233 native / 0.384 reference, `desi_injrec_CORRECTED.json`), confirming the fold models are not faithful reproductions of the production scorer. This should be reconciled or explained.

**[MINOR] 4 — Fig. 6 mixes three distinct "novelty" definitions and plots an excised tier.** The regenerated `fig_novelty_fractions` correctly drops the former Gaia 27% bar (verified `generate_figures.py:647-654`, 5 surveys, values [99,90,68,50,45]; render confirms no residual Gaia/27%). But it juxtaposes 17.8% (genuine, top-1000 vs 18 catalogs), a dashed 58.8% (pooled top-100 of SDSS/eROSITA/NEOWISE at 3″), and per-survey SIMBAD-unmatched up to 99%, and includes eROSITA (68%) — a tier excised from every count in `tab:provenance` (L1083, count-retained = 0). The caption discloses all of this (L1363-1385), but plotting an excised-from-all-counts tier in the primary novelty figure alongside three different population denominators invites misreading; consider splitting or annotating eROSITA as "membership addendum only."

**[MINOR] 5 — "37.3 million" scan volume is ~1% higher than the provenance table's own read/scored column.** `tab:provenance` read/scored entries sum to ≈36.93M (22.5M + 1,925,279 + 200,000 + 43,518 + 11,334,161 + 930,000). ### (3) Central-claim assessment
The central deliverable — a large, reproducible, honestly three-tiered anomaly-*candidate* catalog with a like-for-like 2,468-object science benchmark and two explicitly null cosmology demos — **is largely supported**, and the counting/excision/χ² machinery is verifiably clean; but the specific sub-claim that the DESI headline (73% of the catalog) "is not an in-sample-inflation artifact" is **under-supported**, because the two out-of-sample gates advanced to defend it are computed on k-fold models that fail the paper's own training gate — which must be fixed or transparently disclosed before acceptance.
