**Referee Report**

**P4P-E1** (Abstract, p.1; §5.3, p.7)  
The abstract states “an Exclusion of the Rotating-Black-Hole-Universe Dipole” and the title repeats the claim. The body (§5.1–5.3) explicitly states that Poplawski’s papers supply only a qualitative alignment tendency and no computed amplitude; the quantitative exclusion therefore rests on an external closure \(A_\text{pred}\approx\eta\) plus five numbered assumptions. The abstract omits every caveat present in the final calibrated statement.  
**Required fix**: Rewrite title and abstract to state only what the catalog actually excludes (an alignment-driven dipole above the observed-label sensitivity floor under the listed assumptions). Remove the word “Exclusion” from title.

**P4P-E2** (Abstract, p.1; §2.1, p.2)  
Abstract headline number “8,474,531-galaxy … catalog” is correct for the parent release but the primary dipole result uses only the 887,472-row HC-RI subset. The abstract does not state the support size of the quoted \(z_\text{mom}\) and \(p=0.238\) values.  
**Required fix**: Insert the exact support size of every quoted statistic in the abstract.

**P4P-E3** (Throughout, e.g. §2.2 p.2, §3 p.3, §5.2 p.7)  
Repeated statements that “full systematics appendix … are described in the archived release paper (15) and are not reproduced here” and “not repeated here.” ApJS catalog papers must be standalone; key diagnostics (classifier architecture, per-region calibration, injection-recovery code, covariance construction) are off-loaded.  
**Required fix**: Either embed the minimal self-contained versions of all load-bearing methods or withdraw the catalog claim.

**P4P-E4** (§3 p.3; §5.2 p.7)  
Two distinct null procedures (pixel-fixed-occupancy \(10^4\)-draw and FSC harmonic) are presented side-by-side with \(p=0.238\) and \(p=0.001996\) without the explicit qualifier “not directly comparable” at every juxtaposition.  
**Required fix**: Add the qualifier at every such comparison or recompute a single consistent null.

**P4P-M1** (Fig. 2 caption & Eq. 1, p.4)  
The 95 % coverage threshold \(A_{95}^\text{obs}\simeq0.98\%\) is derived from an observed-label injection-recovery curve that is itself a classifier property, not a physical-spin bound. The paper never quantifies the additional morphology-transfer-function factor needed to convert to a deprojected-spin amplitude.  
**Required fix**: Either supply the missing transfer-function factor or relabel every occurrence of \(A_{95}^\text{obs}\) as “observed-label sensitivity floor.”

**P4P-M2** (Table 3, p.7; §5.3)  
All literature amplitudes are compared to \(A_{95}^\text{obs}\) via an illustrative bridge factor \(g=0.398\) that is explicitly “not an established calibration.” The ratio column therefore lacks a traceable uncertainty.  
**Required fix**: Remove the ratio column or replace it with a statement that no calibrated conversion exists.

**P4P-M3** (§4 p.5; Eq. 2)  
The void/non-void contrast is reported as a single logistic MLE on a post-inspection 13-column basis. The text states the hierarchy was fixed after inspecting the data. No pre-registered analysis plan or multiplicity correction for the post-hoc choice is supplied.  
**Required fix**: Declare the analysis exploratory or supply a registered analysis plan.

**P4P-M4** (Data Availability, p.8)  
The reproducibility section points to Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21461899 and a companion script whose commit hash is not frozen to the stated paper version v4P.0.2. Several pipeline paths contain version strings that pre-date the manuscript date.  
**Required fix**: Provide a single frozen commit hash/DOI pair that exactly reproduces every number in the submitted manuscript.

**P4P-N1** (p.1 header)  
“Typeset using LATEX twocolumn style in AASTeX7.0.2” and the draft date of 2 September 2026 appear in the running head. These are production artifacts.  
**Required fix**: Remove.

**P4P-N2** (multiple locations)  
Phrases such as “the present catalog is an on-vision test of the model’s one directly observable astrophysical claim” mix promotional language with technical content.  
**Required fix**: Replace with neutral wording.

**Summary recommendation**  
REJECT

The manuscript cannot be considered a self-contained ApJS catalog paper. Its central quantitative claims (exclusion of a dipole, sensitivity floor, literature ratios) rest on external documents, uncalibrated conversion factors, and post-hoc analysis choices that are not reproducible from the submitted text alone. Until the work is rewritten as a standalone article with every load-bearing number and diagnostic reproduced inside the manuscript, it fails the venue’s standards.
