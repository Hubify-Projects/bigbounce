This is a referee report for the manuscript "Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$". 

The paper presents a comprehensive, multi-channel consistency test of a specific matter-bounce cosmology, re-deriving the exact bispectrum amplitude, tracking its transmission through the bounce, and evaluating its signatures across pulsar timing arrays, primordial black holes, and large-scale structure. The physical arguments—particularly the resolution of the separate-universe vs. in-in discrepancy via the behavior of the shift vector in a non-attractor phase (Sec. II.D)—are rigorous and insightful. However, the manuscript contains several critical provenance and presentation issues, including missing caveats in the abstract, reliance on unpublished companion drafts, and a major contradiction in Figure 1, that must be addressed before publication.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The physics and computations in this paper are of high quality and well-suited for Physical Review D. However, the manuscript requires major revisions to fix a direct contradiction between Figure 1 and the text, correct abstract-level claims that drop crucial caveats present in the body, remove internal version-history language, and bring the reproducibility materials into compliance with journal policies (specifically, the requirement for a frozen-release DOI at submission and the removal of dependencies on unpublished companion papers).

## Findings

**ESSENTIAL: Abstract-last drift sweep (Missing superscript caveat)**
- **ID:** A3M-E1
- **Section:** Abstract (page 1)
- **Specific problem:** The abstract states: "keeping $|f_{NL}| \le 5.1$ requires $c_s \ge 0.444$". However, the body of the text (Table VII and Section VIII) correctly applies this observational bound to the *transmitted* post-bounce amplitude, $|f_{NL}^{after}| \le 5.1$. 
- **Required fix:** Change $|f_{NL}|$ to $|f_{NL}^{after}|$ in the abstract. This is a crucial physical distinction because the bounce suppresses the pre-bounce amplitude by a factor of $T_{f_{NL}} \approx 0.165$, meaning the bound on $c_s$ is entirely dependent on the transmission model.

**ESSENTIAL: Abstract-last drift sweep (Missing background caveat)**

**MAJOR: Figure 1 contradiction (Induced vs. Primordial GW amplitude)**
- **ID:** A3M-M1
- **Section:** Figure 1 (page 8)
- **Specific problem:** The title and caption of Figure 1 claim to plot the "Induced gravitational waves... from the matter-bounce curvature spectrum", and the caption states the predicted amplitude is "$10^{15.2}$ below the NANOGrav band". This matches the text in Sec. IV.D, which calculates the induced amplitude as $1.45 \times 10^{-23}$. However, the plotted line in Figure 1 sits at $\sim 10^{-14}$. - **Required fix:** Correct the figure to plot the actual induced GW spectrum at $10^{-23}$, or change the title, legend, and caption to explicitly state that the plotted line is the primordial tensor background.
