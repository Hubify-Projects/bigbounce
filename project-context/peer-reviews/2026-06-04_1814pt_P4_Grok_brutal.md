## Summary recommendation
**MAJOR REVISIONS**

The manuscript reports a technically careful null result on the ℓ = 1 chirality dipole, supported by two independent estimators that survive the documented monopole–mask leakage channel. However, the 56-page length, pervasive internal reproducibility tags, and inconsistent framing of the +3.64σ canonical-mask residual as both a “result” and a “systematic” prevent acceptance in its current form.
