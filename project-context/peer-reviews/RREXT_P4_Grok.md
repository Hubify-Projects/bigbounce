Summary
This is a careful, large-scale null result on galaxy spin/chirality dipole claims. The core advance is the bias-hardened pipeline (ViT-Small + 2-fold flip-equivariant test-time averaging) that produces Catalog C, collapsing a raw spurious ~2.3σ real-space dipole (Catalog A) to a clean +0.41σ null (HC subsample, p_eq > 0.6, N_spiral ≈ 950k) against the isotropic pixel-permutation null. The work quantifies a monopole–mask leakage channel (99.32% pre-MASTER reproduction in a generative null) and maps the residual harmonic power to coherent depth/morphology systematics via an eight-anchor battery (including block-bootstrap WLS template exclusion of a clean 1.7% dipole at z ≈ −18). The catalog (8.47M galaxies, 3.2M spirals) is the largest to date, publicly released with model weights and reproducibility artifacts. The statistical framework (explicit primary vs. diagnostic hierarchy, empirical injection-recovery falsification boundary A_95 ∈ [1.0%, 1.5%]) is exemplary.
The paper is publishable after minor revisions. It meets the high bar for methodological transparency and reproducibility in cosmology journals, but two points require tightening before acceptance.
BLOCKERS
None. No fatal errors in claims, statistics, or reproducibility that would require new observations or re-analysis of the core dataset.
MAJORS (must be addressed)
Pseudo-label independence and quantitative bound on inherited bias (Severity: High)
66.5% of training labels derive from CE-ResNet predictions. The per-galaxy label-shuffle and per-pixel permutation nulls randomize the model’s own outputs and therefore cannot test large-scale survey-correlated structure potentially inherited through those pseudo-labels. While the eight-anchor battery in Appendix D (template regression against imaging-leg fractions and density, quality-quartile washout, ℓ=2 > ℓ=1 broadband structure, leg-proxy cross-power, density-stratified null, direct Ap × n_total cross-spectrum) addresses survey-correlated systematics regardless of origin, and the GZ1 accuracy floor (69.91%, κ=0.40) is propagated conservatively via the dilution factor g = 2a − 1 into the injection-recovery thresholds, a explicit quantitative upper bound on any inherited dipole amplitude (or a simulation injecting CE-ResNet label correlations) would materially strengthen the amplitude-level tension with Shamir-class claims. The planned pure-GZ1 sub-model retrain is noted as future work; at minimum, move a short quantitative discussion or bound into the main text or a dedicated subsection of Appendix D so readers can assess the residual risk directly.
Clarity and prominence of the estimator hierarchy and non-comparable significances (Severity: Medium-High)
The paper is already admirably careful (Table I decision tree, repeated footnotes that harmonic σ values are diagnostics only, explicit statements that +0.41σ / z ≈ −18 are the load-bearing cosmological results). However, the multiplicity of numbers (+3.64σ / +7.28σ / +7.93σ harmonic residuals, 3.05σ hemisphere before LEE correction, etc.) still creates a high risk of mis-citation or “forest of significances” reading. Strengthen the abstract, first paragraph of §IV, and conclusions to lead unambiguously with the two primary estimators only. Relegate all harmonic-channel numbers to a single summary sentence (“The MASTER ℓ=1 channel on the patchy footprint yields systematics-attributed residuals of +3.64σ to +7.9σ depending on mask/weight convention and null procedure; see Appendix D for the full eight-anchor attribution”). Consider adding a one-sentence “reader’s map” box or making Table I more visually prominent.
MINORS (recommended improvements)
Shamir comparison framing: The amplitude tension (factor ~4–9 under this pipeline, comparing the joint nuisance-marginalized WLS best-fit 0.455% in A_p units to Shamir’s 1.7–4.0% range) is correctly presented as pipeline-specific rather than a formal exclusion. A short additional sentence noting that a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis remains the cleanest route to a likelihood-level statement would be sufficient; no new computation is required.
Global monopole discussion: The −9.47σ CW deficit in Catalog C is well-handled (spatially uniform across slabs, generative test that a constant monopole does not bias the real-space dipole estimator). A one-sentence note on the most likely dominant source (GZ1 human-handedness bias propagating through CE-ResNet pseudo-labels, as already stated in Data Availability) would close the loop without new analysis.
Edge-on contamination (Appendix E): The equivariance argument correctly shows that directional bias from edge-ons is symmetrized away; only a ~5–8% sensitivity penalty remains. State explicitly that the pending axis-ratio cross-match tightens the floor but cannot reintroduce a coherent dipole signal.
Minor presentation: Ensure every quoted σ in figures/tables/captions carries an explicit “vs. XXX null” qualifier (already done in most places). The confidence-cut sweep (p_eq ∈ {0, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8}) is powerful evidence that the null is stable in the high-confidence regime; if space allows, a compact version of that panel could move to the main text.
Reproducibility artifacts: Excellent overall. The note that excluding the 59,515 HC rows flagged by the flip-identity QC violator shifts the primary real-space z from +0.41 to +0.48 (still null) is already a strong robustness check; a one-line summary in Data Availability is sufficient.
