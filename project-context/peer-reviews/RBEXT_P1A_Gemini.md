General Overview

This manuscript presents a thorough, channel-level theoretical and phenomenological assessment of four minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-torsion routes as potential explanations for late-time dark energy. The author identifies 13 distinct mechanism-class constraints (spanning 14 historical catalog entries) that effectively close or heavily suppress these routes at amplitude-budget granularity.

A major analytical highlight of the work is the "perturbation-transparency result," demonstrating that for canonical scalar matter, the Holst dual contraction vanishes pointwise by the first algebraic Bianchi identity, decoupling the Holst sector entirely from linear and higher-order scalar/tensor perturbation equations of motion. Additionally, the paper establishes a significant structural tension between the number of post-bounce e-folds (Ntot ≈ 92) required for their dark-energy ansatz and the preservation of the distinct matter-bounce non-Gaussianity signature (fNL = −35/8).

The paper is exceptionally rigorous, dense, and well-structured. It clearly outlines its own limitations, boundaries, and underlying assumptions. However, before publication in MNRAS, a few revisions are required to clarify the presentation, particularly regarding the heavy contextual reliance on concurrent companion papers.

Key Strengths

- Analytical Rigor: The proof of perturbation transparency for canonical scalar fields at all perturbation orders is an elegant, first-principles result that cleanly delimits where ECH modifications can and cannot appear.
- Candor and Clarity of Scope: The author does an admirable job of clarifying that this is a channel-level phenomenological assessment, not an exhaustive operator-level theorem, openly noting the exclusion of terms like the Jackiw-Pi gravitational Chern-Simons operator.
- Excellent Diagnostics: The categorization of failure modes into explicit mechanism classes (amplitude suppression, naturalness deficits, scale separation) provides an excellent taxonomy for future model builders in modified gravity.

Major Comments

Dependency on In-Preparation Companion Papers:
The dark-energy mapping rests entirely on an on-shell scaling ansatz where the leading parity-odd operator has a naive off-shell mass dimension of +1 rather than +4. The paper acknowledges that this is an ansatz and not a derivation. However, because this ansatz drives the key value of Ntot ≈ 92, which in turn causes the structural erasure of the matter-bounce fNL signature, the paper's primary observational punchline depends heavily on this specific phenomenological choice.

Action: Expand briefly in Appendix B on how a structurally different completing framework — one that restores a dimension-4 local EFT operator off-shell by absorbing mass dimensions into the coupling coefficient — might shift the precise dynamics of the constraints, or provide an explicit proof of why the scaling kinetics remain invariant to this choice.
