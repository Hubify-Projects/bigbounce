**P2-E3**

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The central claim—that forthcoming surveys can meaningfully test a parameter-free fNL = −35/8 prediction—is potentially interesting, but the manuscript in its present form systematically overstates the robustness and novelty of that test. The abstract does not faithfully represent the conditional and prior-dependent nature of the quantitative results, version-history language remains in the front matter, and several headline numerical statements rest on unpropagated systematics or arbitrary assignments.
