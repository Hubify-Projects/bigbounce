**Referee Report**

**Paper**: Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) and a joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces (A3M)

**Venue bar**: PRD regular article (high). The paper fails that bar on multiple independent grounds.

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL)** — Section I (p. 1) and abstract paragraph 1  
The abstract states the paper’s contribution is “the exact amplitude” together with a joint no-go. The body (Secs. II–III and Table III) shows that the transmitted amplitude \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}\) is scheme-dependent (S1 vs. S2) at the factor-of-2.5 level on the same background; the quoted interval \([-0.65,-0.50]\) exists only inside one scheme. No resolution of the scheme ambiguity is provided. The claim of an “exact” observable amplitude is therefore unsupported.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL)** — Sec. IV (p. 6) and Table IV  
\(\gamma_{\rm pred}=5.07\) (matter bounce) and the official NANOGrav posterior are placed side-by-side without the explicit qualifier “not directly comparable” required by the review instructions. The 5.1\(\sigma\) figure is therefore presented as if it were a single consistent statistic.

**A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL)** — Sec. V (pp. 8–10) and Eq. (12)  
The ratio \(A(-35/16)/A(-35/8)=1.84\pm0.03\) is advertised as “shape-resolved” and “robust.” The grid is truncated at \(\gamma_{\rm cr}\approx0.63\) (IR cutoff) and the ratio moves by \(\gtrsim10\%\) when the cutoff is relaxed (text on p. 9). The quoted uncertainty does not include this systematic; the number is therefore not reproducible from the published material.

**A3M-E4 (ESSENTIAL)** — Sec. VI (p. 11) and Table VI  
Bare significances for the three backgrounds are tabulated under both S1 and S2 without a single sentence stating that the S2 row is “not directly comparable.” This violates the explicit instruction on juxtaposed null statistics.

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR)** — Length vs. content  
17 pages are used to derive one number already in the literature, to exhibit three null channels, and to document a scheme ambiguity that is left unresolved. A concise 4–5 page note would suffice; the present length is not justified by the incremental result.

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR)** — Abstract vs. body drift (pattern-045)  
Abstract claims the joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) no-go is “newly expose[d].” The body (Sec. VIII) shows the no-go is recovered only after imposing an external BICEP/Keck prior on \(r\) and only inside the S1 transmission window. The abstract statement is stronger than the calibrated body claim.

**A3M-M3 (MAJOR)** — Uncomputed quantitative claim (pattern-048)  
The statement “the two windows are disjoint by a factor \(\sim296\) in \(c_s\)” (p. 12) is given without the explicit numerical integral or code pointer that would allow a reader to recompute it from the mode functions alone.

**A3M-N1 (MINOR)** — Duplicate phrasing  
“canonical canonical-mask” does not appear, but the phrase “scheme-qualified linear transfer” is repeated verbatim in three consecutive paragraphs (pp. 4–5) without added content.

**A3M-N2 (MINOR)** — Figure 1 axis label  
The vertical axis is labelled \(\Omega_{\rm GW}h^2\) but the caption and text never state the precise numerical prefactor relating it to the strain power; a reader cannot reconstruct the plotted curves from the published equations without external constants.

**A3M-N3 (NIT)** — Future date in header  
“(Dated: September 4, 2026)” appears on p. 1. Cosmetic but inconsistent with a live submission.

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The paper’s central claim—an exact, scheme-independent, multi-channel-tested value of \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)—is contradicted by its own demonstration that the transmitted amplitude depends on an unresolved choice of bounce-matching scheme at the factor-of-2.5 level. Three of the four observational channels return null results once that scheme dependence is acknowledged, and the remaining channel is under-powered. The manuscript is also substantially over-length for the incremental result it actually establishes. These defects are structural and cannot be repaired by minor revision.
