**Overall assessment on first read:** The manuscript fails the standalone-reader test at every load-bearing step, contains explicit version-history language inside the front matter, imports its only Tier-I result by citation to an unseen companion, and presents a 20-page catalog whose quantitative headline claims (60-order and 61–67-order suppressions) are never recomputed from displayed inputs in this document. **ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without fixes)**

P1C-E1 Front matter, p. 1 “(Dated: August 6, 2026 (v1C.0.10))”  
Explicit version-control tag inside the published front matter. Required fix: remove all internal version strings, round identifiers, and “v1C” markers.

P1C-E2 Abstract, p. 1 and Sec. I, p. 2 “Route 2 … roughly sixty orders of magnitude (conservatively ≳ 58) … Route 3 … 61–67 orders”  
These headline suppression numbers are stated as facts in the abstract but are never derived from displayed equations or tables in the present manuscript; they are imported from the companion. Required fix: either recompute every quoted order-of-magnitude figure from material contained in this PDF or delete the numerical claims from the abstract.

P1C-E3 Abstract & Sec. I, p. 2 “the companion paper does not retain these historical mappings as results” + repeated cross-references to [1] for the torsion-elimination chain, the B14 theorem, and the R1 benchmark arithmetic  
The only Tier-I rigorous result (perturbation-transparency) and the only quantitative normalization anchor are absent. The paper therefore fails the standalone-reader test. Required fix: make every load-bearing derivation self-contained or withdraw the claim that the survey constitutes a complete no-go argument.

P1C-E4 Sec. IV, pp. 5–8 and Table II, p. 10 Route-2 and Route-3 “closures” are labeled Tier-III ansatz-level bounds yet are advertised in the abstract as decisive amplitude-budget closures.  
Mismatch between abstract rhetoric and the evidentiary tier explicitly recorded in Table II. Required fix: rewrite abstract and Sec. I to state the actual evidentiary status (Tier-III at best) or remove the routes from the “closed” column.

P1C-E5 Fig. 1 and Table I, pp. 3–4 Fourteen catalog entries are presented as “distinct mechanism-class constraints,” yet B8 is subsumed by B14 and the diagram shows only channel-level structure, not independent theorems.  
The count “13 distinct” is therefore not supported by the figure or table. Required fix: correct the abstract, Table I caption, and all summary sentences that assert 13 (or 14) independent no-go theorems.

**MAJOR findings (significant revision required)**

P1C-M1 Sec. V, pp. 9–11 and App. A The six-member operator basis {O1–O6} is asserted to be complete by “construction rule,” yet the only verification offered is an external script whose output is not reproduced. No exhaustive symbolic enumeration appears in the PDF.  
Required fix: either embed the enumeration or downgrade the completeness claim to “conjectural under the stated construction rules.”

P1C-M2 Abstract and Sec. IV “observed birefringence amplitude” and “observed dark-energy density” are used as numerical targets, but the paper never states the exact central values or error budgets adopted for those targets inside this document.
