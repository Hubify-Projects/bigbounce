## Findings

### P1-R31-1 (MAJOR — fixed) — Broken cross-references to deleted appendices

**Location:** Two body-text references in Sec.~II ("we use natural units… see Appendix~\ref{app:notation}") and Sec.~VIII.A.2 ("the tidal torque coupling efficiency $\beta$ (Appendix~\ref{app:galaxy})").

**Issue:** Both `\label{app:notation}` and `\label{app:galaxy}` appear at the top of the appendix block (lines 1101 and 1150) as orphan labels — the surrounding `\section{Notation and Conventions}` and `\section{Galaxy Spin Hierarchical Bayesian Fitting}` headers were removed when those appendices were moved to supplementary material. With no enclosing `\section{}`, `\ref{}` resolves to the *previous* numbered counter (the last real section before `\appendix`), producing misleading or visibly wrong cross-references in the rendered PDF. A referee following the in-text pointer lands either on an unrelated section number or, depending on revtex4-2 counter state, on Appendix~A's actual content (Dimensional Analysis), which discusses neither notation nor the galaxy spin Bayesian fit.

This is a referee-visible defect: the natural-units reference and the β fitting reference are both load-bearing pointers (the first is the only place the unit-convention statement is justified, the second is the only place the prior on β is described). Both must resolve to a real, reachable target.

**Fix applied:** Replaced `Appendix~\ref{app:notation}` with `the supplementary material accompanying the public repository, Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}` (which exists as a real `\section{Reproducibility Materials}` at line 1182, names the public repo, and is where the supplementary materials are bound by reference). Replaced `Appendix~\ref{app:galaxy}` with `hierarchical Bayesian fit in the supplementary material accompanying the public repository, Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}`. Both targets now resolve to a real, numbered, reachable appendix that points the referee to the public-repo location of the moved supplementary content.

The orphan `\label` lines in the appendix block (lines ~1101, 1150, 1158, 1162, 1166, 1170) were left in place — they no longer cause body-text breakage now that no `\ref{}` calls them, and removing them is out of R31's "real fixes only" scope.

### P1-R31-2 (MINOR — fixed) — Misleading Cobaya version tag in reproducibility appendix

**Location:** §App.~B (Reproducibility Materials), bullet list, "Four Cobaya~v3.5 YAML configurations".

**Issue:** The configs were originally written against Cobaya v3.5 and the verification described elsewhere in the paper (Sec.~V.B "Computational verification") used Cobaya v3.6.1. Tagging the YAML files as "Cobaya v3.5" in the appendix is internally inconsistent with the paper's own verification version, and a referee re-running the configs with v3.6.1 (the current packaged release) would worry about silent API drift. The configs are in fact compatible with both versions.

**Fix applied:** Changed "Four Cobaya~v3.5 YAML configurations" to "Four Cobaya YAML configurations (verified compatible with Cobaya~v3.5 and v3.6.1)". This matches the verification statement made in the body and removes the inconsistency without overclaiming.

### P1-R31-3 (MINOR — fixed) — Galaxy-spin null rounded inconsistently

**Location:** §IX.A.1 (Limitations — Observational), galaxy spin samples bullet.

**Issue:** Paper reports the 8.47-million galaxy chirality null at "$0.4\sigma$" in the Limitations bullet, but at "$0.43\sigma$" in §IV.B (galaxy-spin observational subsection) and in the abstract-adjacent claims. The two values are the same number rounded to one and two significant figures respectively; a hostile referee reads "0.4σ vs 0.43σ" as either two different fits or sloppy bookkeeping. The Limitations passage is the only outlier — every other use of this number reports 0.43σ.

**Fix applied:** Changed "$0.4\sigma$" to "$0.43\sigma$" in the Limitations bullet. Now consistent with the rest of the manuscript.

### P1-R31-4 (MINOR — fixed) — 4.1σ NaMaster confirmation restated without systematic caveat

**Location:** Two body-text restatements in §IV.B (galaxy-spin null subsection, "Our framework's surviving parity-violation evidence comes entirely from CMB birefringence ($4.1\sigma$ from our independent NaMaster analysis…)") and §V.C/Bayes-factor subsection ("The decisive test is detection of CMB $E$-$B$ parity-odd signatures (confirmed at $4.1\sigma$ by our NaMaster analysis…)").

**Issue:** The 4.1σ NaMaster significance is *statistical-only* — it does not include the systematic budget from absolute polarization-angle calibration, foreground residuals, or beam asymmetry, which the methods section §VI.B explicitly flags. §VI.B itself contains the load-bearing self-correction: *"This analysis is therefore a preliminary cross-check of the published birefringence detections, not a competitive independent measurement."* That caveat is present once, where it is methodologically derived. But §IV.B and §V.C re-quote the 4.1σ headline number as if it were the definitive significance, *without* the caveat — a referee skimming Sec.~IV or Sec.~V (which is plausible for a Bayesian-evidence reader) would read 4.1σ as a peer-equivalent independent confirmation rather than a preliminary cross-check.

This is the type of internal inconsistency hostile reviewers flag as "the authors are quietly walking back the headline number in the methods section." Both restatements need the caveat to read as honest.

**Fix applied:** Added "$4.1\sigma$ statistical-only … treated as a preliminary cross-check rather than a competitive measurement until the systematic budget in Sec.~\ref{sec:data_cmb} is propagated" to both restatements. Both now read consistently with the §VI.B caveat and cannot be quoted out of context.
