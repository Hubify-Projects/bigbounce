This is a referee report for Physical Review D (PRD) on the manuscript "Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local} = -35/16$" by Houston Golden.

The author presents a rigorous, multi-channel analysis of the local non-Gaussianity predicted by a matter-bounce cosmology. The theoretical component is particularly strong: the independent in-in re-derivation of the amplitude, the precise localization of the factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature to an amplitude-normalization step, and the resolution of the separate-universe $\delta N$ gap via the $\mathcal{O}(1/k_L)$ shift term in the non-attractor phase demonstrate a deep understanding of cosmological perturbation theory and gauge subtleties. The observational channels are evaluated with commendable statistical honesty, particularly the use of the compaction-function criterion for PBHs to expose the artefact of the truncated quadratic map. 

However, the manuscript contains a scope mismatch in the abstract, relies on a promise of future data archiving rather than a current frozen release, and contains several internal laboratory bookkeeping tags that must be scrubbed before publication. 

I recommend MAJOR REVISIONS to address the data availability and abstract scope issues.

### Specific Findings

**A3M-E1: Abstract-Last Drift (Scope Mismatch)**
*   **Location:** Abstract, page 1
*   **Problem:** The abstract states: "SPHEREx bare significance is 0.7–0.9$\sigma$ (-35/16) or 1.2–1.7$\sigma$ (-35/8), under 1$\sigma$ apart." However, Table IV (page 9) shows that these numbers correspond *only* to the "bispectrum only" forecast. The "P + B target" forecast yields higher significances: 1.0–1.3$\sigma$ (-35/16) and 1.7–2.4$\sigma$ (-35/8). By omitting the P+B numbers and the "bispectrum only" qualifier, the abstract artificially minimizes the forecast significance to support the claim that the candidates are "under 1$\sigma$ apart."
*   **Required Fix:** Either update the abstract to reflect the full range of SPHEREx forecasts (up to 2.4$\sigma$), or explicitly add the "bispectrum only" qualifier to the quoted numbers in the abstract.

**A3M-E2: Provenance Surface and Data Availability**
*   **Location:** Reproducibility Statement, page 11
*   **Problem:** The text states: "This work's own code and artifacts are pinned by the GitHub commit hash above rather than a frozen-release DOI of their own; minting one is a maintainer action planned for the packaging stage." 
*   **Required Fix:** PRD requires stable, permanent repositories (e.g., Zenodo, Figshare) for data and code availability at the time of publication. A GitHub commit hash is volatile and insufficient. Mint the DOI now and replace the GitHub links with the permanent DOI in the revised manuscript.

**A3M-M1: Internal Bookkeeping Tags and Standalone Reader Test**
*   **Location:** Multiple locations (pages 9, 11)
*   **Problem:** The manuscript contains internal laboratory bookkeeping tags and cites unpublished internal drafts, which breaks the standalone reader test and is inappropriate for a journal submission. Examples include:
    *   Page 11: File paths containing `research/track_a3_multichannel/` (an internal project tracker).
    *   Page 11: "this lab's companion P2 Zenodo record" (internal paper numbering).
    *   Page 11: "consistent with this lab's standing reproducibility policy (directive Q2)."
    *   Page 9: "reported in this lab's companion Fisher-forecast draft (referenced in the reproducibility statement below) but not re-derived... so no numeral is quoted here".
*   **Required Fix:** Scrub all internal project codes (`track_a3`, `P2`, `directive Q2`) from the text and file paths. Remove the reliance on the unpublished Fisher-forecast draft; since the shape overlap $r$ is not used in the final numerical results, the mention of the draft can simply be deleted or replaced with a citation to a published work/arXiv preprint if a general point is being made.

**A3M-N2: Confusing Phrasing**

*   **Required Fix:** Clarify the phrasing in the introduction to something like "whose physical mechanism is not identified" to avoid contradicting the fact that the mathematical identity is indeed derived/computed.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The theoretical physics and statistical evaluations in this paper are excellent and easily meet PRD's high standards for rigor. The resolution of the literature discrepancy is a valuable contribution. However, the manuscript cannot be accepted until the code/data are permanently archived with a DOI (rather than a GitHub hash), the abstract is corrected to accurately reflect the full range of the LSS forecasts in the body, and the internal laboratory bookkeeping tags are removed. These revisions are straightforward but essential for journal publication.
