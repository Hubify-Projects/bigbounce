This is a referee report for "Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ and a joint $(r, f_{NL}^{local})$ no-go for single-field matter bounces," submitted to Physical Review D.

The manuscript presents a highly rigorous, multi-channel consistency test of the matter-bounce scenario, independently re-deriving the $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ amplitude and propagating it through pulsar timing, primordial black hole, and large-scale structure channels. The mathematical derivations are exceptionally solid, and the cross-checks (e.g., the exact resolution of the $\delta N$ vs. in-in discrepancy in Appendix A) are a valuable contribution to the literature. 

However, the manuscript in its current form reads in several places like an internal lab notebook rather than a finalized PRD submission. It contains numerous internal audit tags, version-history artifacts, and explicitly admits to missing a frozen-release DOI for its code. Furthermore, a few variables are introduced without definition, breaking the standalone readability of the paper. These issues must be resolved before publication.

### ESSENTIAL

**A3M-E1: Internal audit tags and version history language**
*Location:* Throughout the manuscript (Pages 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17)
*Problem:* The text is littered with internal bookkeeping placeholders, round/version tags, and ledger references that belong in a lab notebook or pull request, not a published paper. Examples include:
- Page 7: `outputs/r5_15_tensor_omega_nhz.json` ("r5_15" version tag)
- Page 10: `outputs/r11_pbh_residuals.json` ("r11" version tag)
- Page 11 & 17: `LEDGER4_RESULT_v3_2026-09-04.md`
- Page 12: `row18a_s2_tensor/`
- Page 13: `row14_cs_window.py`, `row18b_cs_bounce_cubic.py`
- Page 16: "The ledger-row-9 (D-A3-9) mechanisms...", `PBH_COMPACTION_NOTE_2026-09-02.md`, `track_a3_multichannel`
- Page 17: "ledger row 10", "ledger row 14", "ledger row 11"
*Fix:* Remove all internal ledger row references, "r-round" tags, and internal audit language from the prose. If specific scripts or JSON files must be cited for reproducibility, rename them to clean, publication-ready filenames without internal versioning artifacts.

**A3M-E2: Stale version labels and missing frozen-release DOI**
*Location:* Page 16, Reproducibility Statement
*Problem:* The reproducibility statement contains a stale version pointer and explicitly admits that the data availability is not finalized:
1. "...are committed, at the current HEAD, under research/cubic_bounce_transmission/:" (HEAD is a moving target and unacceptable for a permanent scientific record).
2. "This work's own code and artifacts are pinned by the GitHub commit hash above rather than a frozen-release DOI of their own; minting one is a maintainer action planned for the packaging stage."
*Fix:* Replace "at the current HEAD" with the exact commit hash. Mint the Zenodo (or equivalent) DOI for the code and artifacts *now* and include it in the manuscript. PRD requires data/code availability to be finalized at the time of acceptance, not planned for a future stage.

**A3M-E3: Side-by-side sigmas without explicit qualification**
*Location:* Page 14, Section IX.A
*Problem:* The text juxtaposes two different statistical significance metrics without explicitly flagging them as not directly comparable: "...combining power spectrum and bispectrum widens the candidate separation to only $0.5-1.1\sigma$ at current or forecast LSS sensitivity, not the $3.13\sigma$ forecast detection significance quoted for the pre-bounce amplitude..."
*Fix:* Add an explicit qualification (e.g., "not directly comparable") to clarify that the $0.5-1.1\sigma$ value represents a candidate separation (difference between two signals), whereas the $3.13\sigma$ value represents a detection significance against a null hypothesis.

### MAJOR

**A3M-M1: Undefined variables breaking standalone readability**
*Location:* Page 4, Section III
*Problem:* The text states: "Evaluated for this program's $\Upsilon$ and $\Delta t_B$, the required amplification factor is exactly 1 on every background..." The variables $\Upsilon$ and $\Delta t_B$ are imported from the Quintin et al. [3] framework but are never defined in this paper, nor are their numerical values provided. A reader cannot evaluate this claim without opening the companion paper.
*Fix:* Define $\Upsilon$ and $\Delta t_B$ explicitly in the text and state the numerical values used for "this program's" evaluation.

**A3M-M2: Uncomputed quantitative claim**
*Location:* Page 15, Appendix A (Table: The [L]/[K]/[X]/[S] decomposition)
*Problem:* The table lists the contribution of "[S] short-mode sourcing of the long mode" as "0 (suppressed)". "Suppressed" is a qualitative robustness assertion that requires quantitative backing.
*Fix:* Provide the scaling (e.g., $\mathcal{O}(k^2/a^2 H^2)$) or the explicit numerical bound that justifies setting this term to exactly 0 in the decomposition.

### MINOR

**A3M-N1: Conversational tone**
*Location:* Page 1, Section I
*Problem:* "Every claim below is graded honestly: a reproduction is labeled reproduced, a first computation is labeled new, and a quoted number is labeled cited and never re-computed silently."
*Fix:* While the commitment to rigor is appreciated, this phrasing is overly conversational. Consider revising to standard academic phrasing (e.g., "Throughout this work, we explicitly distinguish between reproduced literature values, newly computed quantities, and cited values").

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The physics, mathematical derivations, and multi-channel synthesis in this paper are of exceptionally high quality and easily meet PRD's standards for rigor. However, the manuscript is currently formatted more like an internal lab ledger than a finalized publication. The presence of internal audit tags, moving-target version labels ("HEAD"), missing DOIs, and undefined variables must be corrected. Once these formatting and provenance issues are resolved, the paper will be an excellent contribution to the field.
