This is a referee report for the manuscript "Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$", submitted to Physical Review D. 

The author presents a comprehensive, multi-channel analysis of the local non-Gaussianity predicted by a matter-bounce cosmology. The paper makes a valuable theoretical contribution by independently re-deriving the squeezed-limit bispectrum amplitude $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ from scratch using the in-in formalism, thereby adjudicating a known factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature (Cai et al. 2009 vs. Li et al. 2017) and tracing the error to an amplitude-normalization step. The author then rigorously propagates this exact amplitude through three observational channels (PTA, PBH, and LSS), taking care to highlight the physical limitations of each (e.g., the artificial ceiling in truncated quadratic maps for PBHs, and the scheme-dependence of bounce transmission). 

The physics, statistical re-evaluations, and gauge-frame cross-checks ($\delta N$ vs in-in) are exceptionally rigorous and well-executed. However, the manuscript contains several typographical errors, dangling internal references, and citations to inaccessible drafts that violate the standalone-reader requirement for a PRD publication. These must be corrected before acceptance.

## PASS 2 — self-critique findings (what initial review missed)

Here are the additional findings from a fresh-eyes review of the manuscript, focusing specifically on arithmetic, equation consistency, and text-table agreement.

**A3M-E4** [ESSENTIAL] (Equation/Text Contradiction)
*Section V.A, page 5*
**Problem:** Equation (8) gives the maximum of the quadratic map as $\zeta_{max} = -\frac{5}{12 f_{NL}} + \frac{3}{5} |f_{NL}| \sigma^2$. The text immediately following claims this "scales as $1/|f_{NL}|$" and quotes values ($0.09524$ and $0.19048$) that are "exactly a factor of 2" apart. However, these quoted values evaluate *only* the first term of Eq. (8). The second term scales as $|f_{NL}|$, not $1/|f_{NL}|$. For typical PBH formation variances ($\sigma \sim 0.1$), this second term is a $\sim 25\%$ correction. Its inclusion means the true $\zeta_{max}$ does not exactly double when $f_{NL}$ is halved.
**Fix:** You must either explicitly state that the $\sigma^2$ term is being neglected (and justify why it can be ignored despite being printed in the equation), or recompute the $\zeta_{max}$ values including the second term and remove the mathematically incorrect claim that the ceiling is "exactly a factor of 2".

**A3M-M3** [MAJOR] (Mathematical Logic / Inequalities)
*Section III, page 3*
**Problem:** The text states: "so that, under assumption (A4), $0 < T_{fNL} \le 1/2$ since $\rho \in (0, 1]$". This is mathematically backwards. Based on the preceding formula $T_{fNL} = (1 - \rho)/2$, if $\rho \in (0, 1]$, then the maximum value of $\rho$ (which is 1) gives $T_{fNL} = 0$, and the infimum of $\rho$ (approaching 0) gives $T_{fNL} \to 1/2$. Therefore, the correct bounds are $0 \le T_{fNL} < 1/2$. 
**Fix:** Correct the inequality to $0 \le T_{fNL} < 1/2$.

**A3M-m1** [MINOR] (Text vs Table Contradiction)
*Section IV.B, page 4 & Table II*
**Problem:** The text states that the Bayes factor for $\gamma=13/3$ "is quoted to one significant figure for the same reason" (due to resting on only 9 chain samples). However, Table II lists this value as $4.5 \times 10^{-4}$, which contains two significant figures.
**Fix:** Either update Table II to reflect one significant figure (e.g., $4 \times 10^{-4}$ or $5 \times 10^{-4}$), or remove the claim in the text that it is quoted to one significant figure.

**A3M-m2** [MINOR] (Arithmetic Discrepancy)
*Section IV.B, page 4 & Table II*
**Problem:** The text and Table II quote Gaussian z-distances of $1.14\sigma$ (for $\gamma=3$) and $4.63\sigma$ (for $\gamma=13/3$) against the refit marginal. However, computing these from the printed marginal mean and standard deviation ($\gamma = 2.567 \pm 0.382$) yields slightly different values: $(3 - 2.567) / 0.382 \approx 1.13\sigma$ and $(13/3 - 2.567) / 0.382 \approx 4.62\sigma$. 
**Fix:** Ensure the quoted z-scores exactly match the arithmetic of the printed mean and standard deviation, or explicitly state in the text/caption that the z-scores were computed using unrounded internal chain values that differ slightly from the printed $2.567 \pm 0.382$.
