**Finding P1C-E2 (ESSENTIAL)**  
Section: Abstract + Sec. I (p. 1)  
Problem: Abstract claims “Route 2 … whose budget is anchored in the published one-loop renormalization of the Holst-plus-fermion sector and closes against the observed birefringence amplitude with roughly sixty orders of magnitude (conservatively ≥58) of suppression margin”. Required fix: Either derive the one-loop coefficient from scratch or remove the numerical claim from the abstract.

**Finding P1C-E3 (ESSENTIAL)**

Problem: Abstract states “Route 3 … bounded by an integrated one-loop renormalization-group flow that leaves the contribution 61–67 orders of magnitude below the observed dark-energy density”. The abstract therefore reports a derived number that is not present in the calculation shown.  
Required fix: Remove the 61–67 figure or supply the full arithmetic chain inside the main text.
