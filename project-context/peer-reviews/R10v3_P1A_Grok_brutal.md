**Referee Report**

**Journal:** Physical Review D

**Classification of findings**

**ESSENTIAL (paper cannot be accepted without these fixes)**

- **P1A-E1 (Abstract, p. 1)**: The abstract states that the four routes “are not proven to be a complete diffeomorphism-invariant operator basis” yet immediately claims “channel-level closure.” This is a direct internal contradiction. The paper never supplies the missing operators (Jackiw–Pi term, parity-odd four-fermion partner) and therefore cannot close the theory it has truncated. Required fix: either perform the operator-level closure or remove every claim of closure.

- **P1A-E2 (Abstract + Sec. II C + Appendix B, pp. 1, 5–6, 19)**: The central mapping \(\rho_\Lambda = \Xi M_{\rm Pl}^4\) with \(\Xi \sim 10^{-123}\) is repeatedly labeled an “ansatz, not a derivation.” All numerical results (\(N_{\rm tot}\approx 92\), \(f_{\rm NL}=-35/8\)) rest on this ansatz. No first-principles derivation of the off-shell dimension +1 operator or the required on-shell rescaling is given. Required fix: either derive the operator from the ECH action or retract all quantitative predictions.

- **P1A-E3 (Sec. X, pp. 14–15)**: The “perturbation-transparency theorem” is asserted for both scalar and tensor sectors, but the tensor proof consists of a single sentence (“the same five steps apply”). The only explicit calculation is the scalar-sector Pontryagin density reduction to a boundary term. Required fix: supply the full tensor-sector calculation or restrict the theorem to scalars.

- **P1A-E4 (Abstract + Sec. IV + Table II, pp. 1, 8–11, 13)**: The 14 “logically independent” barriers are a mixture of known Planck-suppression arguments, dimensional-counting statements, and literature citations. No new amplitude-level calculation is performed for any barrier. The paper therefore contains no original evidence that the routes are closed at the stated level. Required fix: provide explicit amplitude calculations or reclassify the work as a review.

- **P1A-E5 (Abstract + Sec. XIII, pp. 1, 16)**: The two “surviving predictions” (\(f_{\rm NL}=-35/8\), \(\beta\approx 0.27^\circ\)) are explicitly stated to be properties of the matter-bounce class and spectator-ALP models, not of minimal ECH. The abstract nevertheless presents them as outputs of the present analysis. Required fix: remove both numbers from the abstract or demonstrate that they are generated by the ECH sector itself.

- **P1A-E6 (entire manuscript)**: The paper is 21 pages long and consists almost entirely of literature synthesis, dimensional bookkeeping, and references to six “companion papers in preparation.” The actual new technical content (the five-step transparency argument) occupies <2 pages. This violates PRD’s requirement that a Regular Article contain “a substantial body of new results.” Required fix: condense to a 6–8 page Letter or withdraw.

**MAJOR (significant revision required)**

- **P1A-M1 (Sec. I + IV, pp. 3–4, 8)**: The scope paragraph admits that the Jackiw–Pi term and the parity-odd four-fermion partner of Route 1 are omitted. The subsequent “closure” statements are therefore statements about a theory that is not minimal ECH. The paper must either include those operators or change its title and abstract.

- **P1A-M2 (Fig. 1 + Table I, pp. 4–5)**: Both the figure and table are conceptual diagrams, not data. No new numerical result is plotted or tabulated. They function as advertising rather than evidence.

- **P1A-M3 (Sec. V–VII, pp. 11–12)**: All galaxy-spin and birefringence results are deferred to “Paper IV” and “Paper I(b)”. A standalone submission cannot rest its observational claims on unpublished companion manuscripts.

- **P1A-M4 (Sec. II A 2, p. 6)**: The one-loop coefficient \(\alpha/M\sim 10^{-21}\,{\rm GeV}^{-1}\) is taken from an external reference and then treated as a free phenomenological parameter. The paper must state explicitly that this value is an input, not an output.

**MINOR / NIT**

- **P1A-N1 (multiple locations)**: Repeated use of the phrase “in preparation” for six companion papers creates the appearance of a citation cartel. Replace with “to be submitted” or remove.

- **P1A-N2 (p. 2, Table of Contents)**: The section numbering jumps from IX to X without a Sec. IX heading in the body; minor typesetting inconsistency.

- **P1A-N3 (Eq. 15, p. 9)**: The factor-of-100 ambiguity in the one-loop suppression is acknowledged but never propagated into the final error budget. A one-sentence statement that this uncertainty is sub-dominant would suffice.

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The manuscript asserts a “channel-level closure” of four dark-energy routes in minimal ECH while simultaneously (a) omitting the operators required for a complete basis, (b) relying on an explicit scaling ansatz rather than a derivation, (c) deferring all observational numbers to unpublished companions, and (d) presenting a 21-page literature synthesis whose only original technical step is a five-line transparency argument. These contradictions are fatal at the level of a first read. The work could be salvaged only by a radical reduction in scope and length together with explicit amplitude-level calculations that are currently absent.
