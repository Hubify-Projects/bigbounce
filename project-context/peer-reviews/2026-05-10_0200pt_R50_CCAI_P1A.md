## Per-finding detail

### B1 (BLOCKER) §IV.D R4 closure: "monotonic in $m_\theta$ AND grows toward both endpoints" is mathematically inconsistent

**Location:** `paper1a_ech_nogo.tex` line 682, the LLLLL-introduced parenthetical inside `\subsection{Route 4 ...}` ("closed by birefringence-amplitude bound").

**Verbatim text (line 682, from the parenthetical that was appended by LLLLL):**
> "...so the overshoot is monotonic in $m_\theta$ and grows toward both endpoints of the range..."

**The defect.** A function that is monotonic on a bounded interval $[a,b]$ does **not** "grow toward both endpoints" — it grows toward exactly one endpoint and shrinks toward the other. The two clauses contradict each other. As written the clause has the form *"f is monotonic in x AND f attains its maximum at both endpoints of the interval"*, which is only possible for a constant function — and the surrounding paragraph and the very next clause (the 22 OOM at $10^{-22}$ eV vs 36 OOM at $10^{-15}$ eV explicit numbers) flatly disprove that f is constant.

**What the paper actually proves and what the clause should say.** From the explicit numbers in the very next clause:
- at $m_\theta \sim 10^{-22}$ eV the overshoot is **22 OOM**;
- at $m_\theta \sim 10^{-15}$ eV the overshoot is **36 OOM**.

The overshoot $(m_\theta/H_0)^2$ is monotonically increasing in $m_\theta$. The natural ALP range $[10^{-22},\,10^{-15}]$ eV lies entirely above $H_0\approx 1.5\times10^{-33}$ eV, so $\rho_\theta>\rho_\Lambda$ everywhere in the range, with overshoot **minimum at the lower endpoint (22 OOM)** and **maximum at the upper endpoint (36 OOM)**. *Both endpoints overshoot*, but the overshoot does **not** "grow toward both endpoints" — it grows monotonically from one endpoint to the other. The closure rests on the fact that the **smaller** of the two endpoint overshoots is already 22 orders of magnitude, which is the rigorous form of "no point in the natural range can simultaneously match $\rho_\Lambda$."

**Severity.** This is a hostile-reviewer-visible mathematical contradiction in a parenthetical that was added precisely to defend against R49-m1's complaint that the closure was not airtight across the range. A R-round reviewer with a calculator (which is exactly what GPT-5 / Gemini-3.1-Pro / Grok-4 will be in the cross-vendor round) will catch this immediately. It is a BLOCKER for the cross-vendor gate, not for the readers of the eventual journal version, but the cross-vendor gate is what R50 is upstream of.

**Suggested fix (one-liner, surgical).** Replace the failing clause:

```
(because $m_\theta=H_0\approx 1.5\times 10^{-33}$\,eV is the only point where $\rho_\theta=\rho_\Lambda$, and the natural ALP range lies entirely \emph{above} that point, so the overshoot is monotonic in $m_\theta$ and grows toward both endpoints of the range)
```

with

```
(because $m_\theta=H_0\approx 1.5\times 10^{-33}$\,eV is the only point where $\rho_\theta=\rho_\Lambda$, the natural ALP range lies entirely \emph{above} that point, and the overshoot $(m_\theta/H_0)^2$ is monotonically increasing in $m_\theta$, so it is bounded below by its value at the lower endpoint $m_\theta=10^{-22}$\,eV (which is already $\sim 22$ OOM) and grows to $\sim 36$ OOM at the upper endpoint $m_\theta=10^{-15}$\,eV)
```

This closes B1 without disturbing any other clause, any number, any cross-paper anchor, any citation, or any figure.
