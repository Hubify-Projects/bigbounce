Here is the referee report for the manuscript submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.

### General Comments
The manuscript presents a rigorous, channel-level assessment of whether the minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) torsion—specifically the spin-spin contact interaction responsible for Popławski's cosmological bounce—can simultaneously source the observed late-time dark energy density. The author systematically closes four enumerated dark-energy channels using a combination of amplitude budgets, a perturbation-transparency theorem, and an operator-basis adjudication. 

The theoretical physics is exceptionally detailed, and the algebraic and dimensional cross-checks are robust. The author correctly separates parity-even and parity-odd observables, explicitly tracks the trace-vector torsion irrep that is often erroneously omitted in Einstein-Cartan limits, and properly contextualizes the statistical significance of CMB birefringence measurements by noting their distinct null procedures. 

However, the manuscript contains severe structural and formatting violations that preclude acceptance in its current form. Most critically, the paper fails the standalone-reader test by outsourcing the actual derivations of its 14 core mechanism-class constraints to an unpublished, non-peer-reviewed GitHub repository draft. Furthermore, several internal version-control tags and audit notes have been left in the main text. These issues must be resolved before the paper can proceed.

**P1N-E3** (ESSENTIAL)

**Problem:** The manuscript catalogs 14 mechanism-class constraints (Barriers B1–B14) in Section IV, providing only brief paragraph summaries for each. This violates the standalone-reader requirement; a paper cannot rely on an unpublished, non-archival `.tex` file for the proofs of its central claims.
**Required Fix:** The manuscript must be self-contained. You must either (a) include the necessary derivations for the 14 barriers in an appendix within this manuscript, or (b) formally publish/preprint the companion survey (e.g., on arXiv) so it is a valid, readable citation, while ensuring *this* manuscript still contains enough mathematical detail to justify its own conclusions independently.

### Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The physics, algebraic derivations, and cosmological arguments presented in the manuscript are highly rigorous and well-executed. The explicit computation of the operator basis and the careful handling of the ECH torsion irreps represent a strong contribution to the field. However, the manuscript currently fails the standalone-reader test by outsourcing the derivations of its 14 central barriers to an unpublished GitHub draft, and it contains multiple leftover internal audit tags. Once the manuscript is made self-contained and the drafting artifacts are removed, it will be an excellent candidate for publication in *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.
