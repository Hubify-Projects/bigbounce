**P1C-E1**  
**Section:** Title page (p. 1)  
**Problem:** The header contains the internal versioning string “(v1C.0.4)”. This is an artifact of an internal review log and has no place in a submitted manuscript.  
**Required fix:** Remove all version-control tags, round identifiers, and internal bookkeeping strings from the title page and everywhere else in the document.

**P1C-E2**  
**Section:** Abstract (p. 1) and throughout  
**Problem:** The manuscript is not self-contained. Every load-bearing result (the perturbation-transparency theorem B14, the explicit one-loop parity-odd operator, the Fierz projection, the Route-2 and Route-3 amplitude budgets, the six-member operator basis completeness) is imported from the companion paper [1] by citation only. A standalone reader cannot verify the central claims.  
**Required fix:** Either (a) reproduce the necessary derivations in full or (b) withdraw the paper and resubmit only after the companion is published and the present work is rewritten as a true follow-up.

**P1C-E3**  
**Section:** Abstract (p. 1)  
**Problem:** The abstract states that Route 2 is closed by “∼60 (conservatively ≥58) orders of magnitude” and Route 3 by “61–67 orders.” These numbers are never recomputed from first principles in the present text; they are taken from the companion and from the historical Shapiro–Teixeira / Benedetti–Speziale papers. The abstract therefore reports results the manuscript itself does not establish.  
**Required fix:** Remove all quantitative suppression claims from the abstract or supply the full one-loop calculation inside this paper.

**P1C-M1**  
**Section:** Sec. I and Sec. IV (pp. 1–2, 5–7)  
**Problem:** The paper repeatedly labels its own conclusions “Tier-I rigorous theorem,” “Tier-II structural argument,” or “Tier-III ansatz-level estimate.” These are self-invented categories with no external calibration. The only actual Tier-I item (B14) is again imported from the companion.  
**Required fix:** Replace the three-tier taxonomy with standard mathematical language (“theorem,” “lemma,” “numerical estimate under stated assumptions”).

**P1C-M2**  
**Section:** Sec. V and Appendix A (pp. 9–10)  
**Problem:** The operator-basis-completeness argument rests on the claim that the six densities {O1–O6} exhaust all local, gauge-invariant, diffeomorphism-covariant parity-odd dimension-4 operators admitted by the minimal ECH field content. The only verification offered is a symbolic script whose output is not reproduced; the script itself is hosted on an external GitHub repository that post-dates the stated paper version.  
**Required fix:** Either embed the full symbolic enumeration and its two reduction identities in the manuscript or retract the completeness claim.

**P1C-M3**  
**Section:** Abstract and Sec. IV (pp. 1, 5–7)  
**Problem:** The amplitude-budget closures for Routes 2 and 3 are presented as “upper-bound amplitude budgets.” They are in fact order-of-magnitude estimates that rely on (i) an O(1) assumption for β(γ), (ii) a specific LQG area-gap normalization, and (iii) the absence of higher-order operators. None of these assumptions is varied inside the paper.  
**Required fix:** Convert the budgets into explicit inequalities with all hidden O(1) factors displayed and with a one-parameter sensitivity analysis.

**P1C-N1**  
**Section:** Table II (p. 8)  
**Problem:** The evidentiary-status column mixes three incommensurate categories (rigorous result, structural argument, ansatz-level estimate) without a clear decision procedure for assigning an entry to a given category.  
**Required fix:** Provide an explicit, reproducible rubric for the three labels.

**P1C-N2**  
**Section:** Fig. 1 and Table I (pp. 4–5)  
**Problem:** Barrier B8 is listed as a distinct entry yet is immediately subsumed by B14. The figure and table therefore contain a redundant node whose only function is to preserve “historical mechanism-class catalog” bookkeeping.  
**Required fix:** Merge or clearly flag subsumed entries so that the count of “13 distinct mechanism-class constraints” is unambiguous.

**Additional observations**  
- The manuscript is 17 pages long yet contributes no new first-principles calculation; its sole original content is a re-packaging and classification exercise. CQG’s typical article length for such a survey would be closer to 8–10 pages.  
- Multiple “naturalness / classification” arguments (B5–B7, B13) are presented as mechanism-class constraints even though they apply equally to any modified-gravity scenario, not merely minimal ECH. Their inclusion inflates the catalog without adding ECH-specific power.  
- The bibliography contains several arXiv preprints whose DOIs are given as “concept DOI” placeholders; these are not stable citations.

**Summary recommendation**  
**REJECT**

The manuscript fails the most basic requirement of a CQG article: it is not self-contained. Every quantitative and structural claim that would justify publication is deferred to an unpublished companion paper. In addition, the text contains internal versioning artifacts, overstates the rigor of its own classifications, and presents order-of-magnitude estimates as “amplitude-budget closures” without the supporting derivations. Until the companion is published and the present work is rewritten to stand alone, the paper does not meet the acceptance threshold of Classical and Quantum Gravity.
