This is a referee report for the manuscript "A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology" submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity*. 

The manuscript presents a systematic survey of 13 mechanism-class constraints on minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source of late-time dark energy. The paper is mathematically rigorous, the dimensional bookkeeping is exceptionally careful, and the abstract is perfectly calibrated to the body's claims. The author's explicit handling of statistical significances from different null procedures (e.g., WMAP+Planck vs. ACT DR6) and the careful distinction between ansatz-level estimates and rigorous theorems are highly commendable. 

However, the manuscript contains critical structural and provenance issues regarding its reliance on unpublished companion papers and missing data artifacts that must be addressed.

### Findings

**ID: P1C-E1**
*   **Section:** IV.C (The fourth channel and the combined closure), Page 9
*   **Specific problem:** The paper claims to systematically close four enumerated dark-energy routes (R1–R4). However, for Route 4, the text states: "Its full derivation, and the companion consistency check against a spectator-ALP birefringence benchmark, are outside the present survey's scope [1, 13]." References [1] and [13] are unpublished, un-refereed Zenodo deposits. Furthermore, on Page 2, the author states that the refereeing of these companion results "is part of the companion's own publication path rather than of this survey's." 
*   **Required fix:** ESSENTIAL. This violates the standalone-reader test. A survey paper cannot claim to close a primary route based on an inaccessible, un-refereed derivation while explicitly shielding that derivation from the current peer review process. You must either (a) provide the full derivation for R4's closure in an appendix (as was properly done for R1 in App E and B14 in App D) so it can be refereed here, or (b) remove R4 from the scope of this paper's definitive closure claims.

**ID: P1C-E2**
*   **Section:** Data and Code Availability, Page 13
*   **Specific problem:** The text states: "An updated archival deposit containing this survey's own verification scripts is planned prior to publication, so that every computational artifact cited here is backed by a frozen-release DOI."
*   **Required fix:** ESSENTIAL. A "planned" DOI is insufficient for peer review. The frozen-release DOI containing the exact verification scripts (`dim4_parityodd_enumeration.py`, `fierz_lemma_check.py`, etc.) must be minted and provided *during* the review process so the referee can audit the computational artifacts that support the operator-basis closure.

## Summary recommendation

MAJOR REVISIONS

The manuscript provides an exceptionally rigorous, mathematically sound, and well-calibrated survey of minimal spin-torsion routes to dark energy. The dimensional analysis and numerical bookkeeping (e.g., the $\sim 10^{-79}$ eV$^4$ density benchmark and the 122-order hierarchy dilution) are flawless. However, the paper currently violates the standalone-reader test by relying on an unpublished, un-refereed companion paper to close one of its four primary enumerated routes (Route 4). Additionally, the verification scripts are cited as "planned prior to publication" rather than being provided with a frozen-release DOI for peer review. These structural and provenance issues must be resolved before the paper can be accepted.
