### [m2] Notation Complexity of Gravitational Couplings ($\kappa$ vs $\tilde{\kappa}$)
*   **Location**: Page 2 (right column) and Page 5 (right column).
*   **Issue**: The paper defines the standard coupling $\kappa \equiv 8\pi G = M_{\text{Pl}}^{-2}$ (where $\kappa^2 = M_{\text{Pl}}^{-4}$ has mass dimension $-4$), but to remain faithful to imported literature (Shapiro-Teixeira), it introduces $\tilde{\kappa}^2 \equiv 16\pi G$ (where $\tilde{\kappa}$ has mass dimension $-1$). 
*   **Impact**: Although the author is exceptionally diligent in flagging this distinction in Section II and using the tilde consistently, a casual reader skimming the equations in Section IV and Appendix B might easily confuse the two. Adding a brief parenthetical reminder of the dimensionality of $\tilde{\kappa}$ directly adjacent to Equation (4) would prevent any potential reader confusion.
