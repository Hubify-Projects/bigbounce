Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS
This is a high-quality, rigorously scoped technical companion paper. It performs exactly the verification role it claims: three independent compatibility checks (stock-CAMB ΛCDM+ΔNeff proxy, NaMaster pseudo-Cℓ pipeline validation on synthetic skies, and spectator-ALP consistency) that support the structural no-go of the concurrently submitted P1a without claiming evidence for or against a bounce/ECH cosmology. Model-preference statistics are correctly deferred to future nested sampling. The manuscript exhibits exemplary transparency, reproducibility practices, and anti-overclaim discipline that are rare in numerical cosmology papers.
The single area requiring substantive attention is the w0wa cross-check (Sec. V C / Table II), which carries a known uncontrolled systematic whose impact is stated but whose prominence risks reader misinterpretation. A handful of presentational clarifications would bring the paper to the high bar expected by MNRAS/PRD/JCAP.
BLOCKERS: None
No factual errors, no visibly incorrect rendered mathematics, no internal contradictions with P1a scope, and no missing critical reproducibility artifacts. All headline numbers (ΔNeff, pipeline bias, ALP posteriors) are internally verified against committed chains or literature. Dates (June 29, 2026), arXiv-style citations, and coordinated multi-paper framing are handled correctly for the current submission context.
MAJORS: 1
1. w0wa cross-check under uncontrolled SN-overlap systematic (Sec. V C, Table II, caveat (e) of Sec. III)
The iter2 chain combines DES-Y5 and Pantheon+ via a product likelihood without a joint covariance for the ~20% shared supernovae (different Malmquist corrections). This double-counts events and artificially narrows the posterior widths, as the authors correctly note. The section nevertheless reports a clear quintom-B direction (w0 + wa = −1.48 ± 0.15, phantom crossing at z ≈ 0.39) and provides a “physics interpretation” paragraph. While the text labels the result “exploratory compatibility check only,” “no model-selection statement claimed,” and defers overlap-controlled chains, the table and narrative give the impression of a quantitative result.
Requested revision: Choose one of:
(a) Remove or move the w0wa section/results to supplementary material / a future note (preferred if the control chains are not yet complete);
(b) Add the promised overlap-controlled chains (or at minimum the two separate-chain runs already mentioned) with a short quantitative bias estimate; or
(c) Add prominent language in the section header, every results paragraph, and the abstract that this is “illustrative only under a known ~20% overlap systematic that precludes robust significance or model comparison—see caveat (e) and deferred analysis.”
This is a real finding (uncontrolled systematic affecting interpretation), not style.
MINORS: 4
1. ALP spectator tuning and subset statistics (abstract, Sec. VI, Table IV, fn. 6)
The ~25× misalignment fine-tuning (θi ~ 0.1 vs natural midpoint ~0.5) required for the Ωa < 0.01 spectator-safe subset (13% posterior mass, ESS = 461) is disclosed in fn. 6 and Table IV, but could be more prominently flagged in the abstract and Sec. VI headline. The strict θi ≤ 0.1 sliver (0.33% mass) is correctly labeled “indicative only.” The flat-θi prior is the more generous choice for the spectator corner; a cos θi-flat prior reduces it further (text already notes 0.068%).
Requested: Add one explicit sentence in the abstract and Sec. VI summary: “Accommodation in the spectator-safe (Ωa < 0.01) corner requires ~25× fine-tuning of the misalignment angle relative to the natural prior midpoint; see fn. 6 and Table IV.” This is already load-bearing in the authors’ own framing.
2. Pipeline bias vs. sky significance distinction (abstract, Sec. IV, Fig. 3)
The abstract and Sec. IV correctly separate MC pipeline-recovery bias (−0.032° to −0.040° on foreground-free synthetic skies) from published sky-detection significances (2.7–2.9σ). The robustness battery (apodization, purification, weighting, BB template) that isolates the ~12% multiplicative under-recovery is excellent. A one-sentence reminder in the abstract or Sec. IV that “these MC figures are not directly comparable to sky-detection significances” would eliminate any residual risk of casual misreading.
3. Minor presentational items
Table II caption and surrounding text: add a parenthetical quantifying the artificial narrowing effect of the overlap (even if approximate).
Ensure every figure caption involving NaMaster explicitly reiterates “synthetic skies / MC validation only.”
The legacy parameter_summary.json column-permutation bug is correctly handled via CORRECTED.json and repo documentation; no change needed in the paper body.
4. ALP vs. ECH distinctiveness reinforcement (Sec. VI final paragraph)
The text already states clearly that the birefringence is not a distinctive ECH prediction and arises identically in GR+ALP. A single reinforcing clause in the concluding sentence of Sec. VI would close the loop.
STRENGTHS (high-impact for top journal)
Outstanding scoping and anti-overclaim discipline — Every analysis is explicitly framed as a compatibility check only, with repeated statements that no evidence for/against bounce/ECH is claimed and that model preference is deferred. This discipline is maintained from abstract through conclusions and is exemplary.
Exceptional reproducibility and audit infrastructure — Version-stamped repo (v1B.0.83, commit b22f8cc9), full Cobaya YAMLs, 500-MC NaMaster scripts with deterministic seeds, ALP ODE integrator, frozen chains + diagnostics on HuggingFace (pending DOI), corrected JSON artifacts, IMPLEMENTATION_MAP.md, KNOWN_GAPS.md, claims-classification Table V, independent re-run cross-check (0.04σ agreement on ΔNeff), and explicit burn-in/ESS/R-hat reconciliation. This sets a high standard.
Rigorous pipeline validation with bias attribution — 500 realizations, sky-fraction sweep (fsky = 0.32/0.65/0.85), and six-configuration robustness battery that isolates the dominant bias sources (unweighted χ² + crude CBBℓ proxy) rather than attributing it to mask geometry or apodization. The bias floor is carried forward transparently as a synthetic-sky pipeline figure, not a sky systematic.
Honest, quantified disclosure of fine-tunings and systematics — ~25× misalignment tuning for spectator status, non-minimal Caγ requirement (median 20.7 in continuous prior; ~8–10 in posterior-preferred regime), Ωa < 0.01 subset fractions and ESS, and ~20% SN overlap double-counting are all stated with numbers and labeled as load-bearing caveats. The paper does not hide that the ALP “accommodates but does not predict” and is not ECH-distinctive.
Careful statistical practice and cross-checks — Explicit two-sided vs. one-sided ΔNeff limits (renormalized CDF), GetDist vs. direct recomputation, literature cross-check with Liu et al. (2025), PR3/PR4 provenance disambiguation for Eskilt & Komatsu, and clear separation of MC template-fit SNR from sky-detection significance.
Summary
This companion does its job well: it verifies that the numerical proxies are consistent with current data under the stated scope limitations and does so with unusual care. The single major concern (w0wa systematic) is fixable with modest revision and does not affect the core ΔNeff / pipeline / ALP results. With the requested adjustments, the paper would be suitable for MNRAS/PRD/JCAP as a companion to the P1a structural no-go.
