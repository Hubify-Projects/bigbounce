**Paper:** P1N — “What Minimal Einstein–Cartan Torsion Does for the Bounce and Cannot Do for Dark Energy” (v1N.0.3, 11 pp)

**Classification of findings**

**P1N-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. II, p. 2)**  
The central on-shell torsion (Eq. 2) and the contact term (Eq. 3) are obtained only after “back-substituting the connection equation” and invoking results from the companion [16]. The four-fermion Fierz identity (Eq. 5) is stated as “independently re-derived” but the explicit 4×4 matrix algebra is deferred. Required fix: all algebraic steps that produce the coefficient −3κ/16 must be reproduced in the main text or the manuscript reduced to a Letter.

**P1N-E2 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract & Sec. II, p. 2)**  
Abstract states the numerical ratio “β/α = 2.11 at the programme’s benchmark γ = 0.2375”. The body immediately qualifies that this ratio holds only for the finite-γ Barbero–Immirzi value chosen in Ref. [4] and is not valid in the strict Einstein–Cartan limit γ → ∞. The abstract supplies no such caveat. This is abstract–body drift on a load-bearing scalar.

**P1N-E3 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. III, p. 3 & Sec. IV, p. 4)**  
Theorem 1 (“Perturbation transparency”) is proved only under the matched-data hypothesis (H1–H5) that explicitly excludes the very non-minimal or dynamical-Immirzi extensions the paper later claims to have ruled out. The proof is therefore conditional on the scope it asserts to close; circular.

**P1N-E4 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. IV, Table I, p. 5)**  
Fourteen “mechanism-class constraints” are listed. Only B14 is labelled Tier-I; the remaining thirteen are either general naturalness arguments (B5–B7, B10) or single-branch statements that do not jointly close all four dark-energy channels once the on-shell torsion is allowed to carry a trace-vector piece (Eq. 2). The table therefore over-claims closure.

**P1N-E5 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. VI, p. 7–8)**  
The operator-list argument rests on the six densities (12) and the two algebraic relations (13). Both relations are verified only after the connection equation is solved; they are not identities of the full first-order ECH theory. The claim that the list “spans the rule-admitted space” is therefore an on-shell statement sold as an off-shell basis result.

**P1N-E6 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract & Sec. V, p. 6)**  
Abstract asserts “no ECH dark-energy or birefringence prediction is made”. Route 2 (one-loop Holst correction) and Route 3 (Immirzi running) are each shown to be suppressed by ≥58 and 61–67 orders respectively, yet both routes still produce non-zero (albeit tiny) contributions once the companion one-loop results are accepted. The absolute statement in the abstract is therefore stronger than the calibrated body conclusion.

**P1N-M1 (MAJOR, length & scope, entire ms)**  
An 11-page paper whose principal result is a negative catalogue of 14 barriers (most of them classification or naturalness arguments) exceeds CQG’s normal length for such a contribution. A 4–5 page Letter would be the appropriate format.

**P1N-M2 (MAJOR, Sec. II, p. 2 & companion dependence)**  
The numerical benchmark κnψ²/ρΛ,obs ≃ 3.884 × 10⁻⁶⁹ is taken from the companion normalization; the present text supplies neither the explicit ISM-like density nψ = 100 cm⁻³ nor the conversion from (2.25 meV)⁴. The central “68 orders of magnitude” claim is therefore not independently verifiable from the submitted manuscript.

**P1N-M3 (MAJOR, Sec. VII, p. 9)**  
The paper repeatedly states that the trace-vector irrep (coefficient β) is “the larger” piece at finite γ, yet the only quantitative support is the single ratio 2.11 at one chosen γ. No scan over the physically allowed window 0.1 ≲ γ ≲ 1 is provided.

**P1N-N1 (MINOR, notation, p. 1)**  
The date “(Dated: September 2, 2026)” appears in the by-line. This is an internal bookkeeping tag that should be removed.

**P1N-N2 (MINOR, references)**  
Several citations to the author’s own Zenodo companions carry future DOIs and “not peer-reviewed” disclaimers. These are acceptable as long as the main text does not treat them as established literature.

**Summary recommendation: REJECT**

The manuscript’s core negative claim—that the minimal ECH contact term cannot source late-time dark energy—is not demonstrated at the level of rigour required by Classical and Quantum Gravity. The argument is fragmented across four companion manuscripts, the abstract over-states several quantitative and absolute conclusions, the single Tier-I theorem is conditional on the very extensions it purports to close, and the remaining “barriers” are largely classificatory rather than calculational. Even after the listed essential fixes the work would still be more suited to a shorter Letter format.
