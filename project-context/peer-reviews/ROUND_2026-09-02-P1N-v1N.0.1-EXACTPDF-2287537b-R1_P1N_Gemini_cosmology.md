This is a referee report for the manuscript "What Minimal Einstein–Cartan Torsion Does for the Bounce and Cannot Do for Dark Energy" submitted to *Classical and Quantum Gravity* (CQG-NOTE). 

The manuscript attempts to consolidate two companion papers into a single Note, arguing that the minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-spin contact interaction responsible for the Popławski bounce cannot simultaneously source late-time dark energy. While the physical premise is interesting, the manuscript in its current form is fundamentally incomplete. It reads as an extended abstract or a review log rather than a self-contained scientific paper. It omits crucial operator definitions, outsources mathematical proofs and quantitative derivations to external GitHub repositories, contains explicit placeholder text, and suffers from a critical sign error in its central physical claim regarding the repulsive nature of the contact term. 

Below is an exhaustive list of findings that must be addressed.

**P1N-E4: Fatal Physics Sign Error (Attractive vs. Repulsive)**
*   **Location:** Section II, Eq (2) (page 2)
*   **Problem:** The manuscript claims Eq (2), $\mathcal{L}_{4\psi} = -\frac{3\kappa}{16} \frac{\gamma^2}{1+\gamma^2} (J_5^I J_{5I})$, is "repulsive at high spin density, the same mechanism that produces Popławski’s torsion-induced avoidance of gravitational singularities". 
    However, the paper explicitly uses the mostly-plus signature $(-,+,+,+)$ (stated below Eq 1). In this signature, $J_5 \cdot J_5 = -(J_5^0)^2 + (\vec{J}_5)^2$. For a homogeneous high-density background, $J_5 \cdot J_5 \approx -(J_5^0)^2 < 0$. 
    Because the interaction term is a flat-index scalar, its contribution to the energy-momentum tensor is $T_{\mu\nu} = g_{\mu\nu} \mathcal{L}_{int}$. The energy density is $\rho = T_{00} = - \mathcal{L}_{int}$. 
    Given Eq (2), $\mathcal{L}_{int} = - C (J_5 \cdot J_5) = + C (J_5^0)^2 > 0$ (where $C > 0$). Therefore, the energy density is $\rho = - C (J_5^0)^2 < 0$. A negative energy density corresponds to an *attractive* interaction, which would accelerate gravitational collapse, not halt it. Popławski's bounce requires a positive energy density (repulsion), which in mostly-plus signature requires $\mathcal{L}_{int} = + C (J_5 \cdot J_5)$.
*   **Required Fix:** Correct the sign in Eq (2) to yield a repulsive interaction, or explicitly demonstrate how a negative coefficient in mostly-plus signature yields a positive energy density. 

**P1N-E5: Fierz Rearrangement Coefficient Error**
*   **Location:** Section II.A, Eq (4) (page 2)
*   **Problem:** The text states that Fierz rearrangement of Eq (2) projects onto the scalar channel $G_s(\bar{\psi}\psi)^2$ with $G_s = -3\kappa/16$. This is quantitatively incorrect. The Fierz identity for $A \times A = (\bar{\psi}\gamma_5\gamma_\mu\psi)^2$ yields $+2 (\bar{\psi}\psi)^2 + \dots$ (or a similar factor depending on exact conventions, but strictly $\neq 1$). Furthermore, the $\frac{\gamma^2}{1+\gamma^2}$ factor present in Eq (2) has been dropped in Eq (4) without stating that the Einstein-Cartan limit ($\gamma \to \infty$) is being taken for this specific equation.
*   **Required Fix:** Recompute the Fierz coefficient carefully, include the missing factor of 2 (or equivalent based on your exact spinor convention), and retain the $\gamma$ dependence (or explicitly state the limit being taken).

*   **Required Fix:** Soften the language to acknowledge that this is a standard consequence of the Bianchi identity applied to the zero-spin branch.

## Summary recommendation
REJECT

**Justification:**
While the physical scope is appropriate for a CQG Note, the manuscript in its current form is fundamentally unpublishable. It fails the standalone-reader test by omitting the definitions of the operators it analyzes (O1-O6) and by outsourcing its quantitative derivations and mathematical proofs to external GitHub links and companion papers. Furthermore, it contains explicit placeholder text ("repository artifact"), extensive version-history meta-language that should have been removed prior to submission, and a fatal sign error in Eq (2) that contradicts the central physical claim of the Popławski bounce (repulsion vs. attraction). The author must completely rewrite the manuscript to be self-contained, correct the physics signs and Fierz coefficients, and remove all meta-commentary before it can be considered for publication.
