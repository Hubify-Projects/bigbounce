Title: Channel-Level Closure of Four Minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst Dark-Energy Routes and Perturbation Transparency for Scalar Matter
Author: Houston Golden

General Overview
This manuscript presents a thorough channel-level analysis of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-torsion gravity as a driver for late-time dark energy. The author maps out the parameter space using a 14-constraint catalog and introduces a "perturbation-transparency" result showing that the Holst sector decouples from linear scalar and tensor perturbations when canonical scalar matter is present. Furthermore, a major structural tension is identified between the e-fold budget required for dark energy dilution and the preservation of matter-bounce non-Gaussianity (fNL = −35/8). The paper is highly theoretical, self-aware of its boundaries, and exceptionally well-structured.

Strengths

Comprehensive Structural Framework: The categorization of the 14 structural barriers across 7 foundational mechanism classes and 6 observational branches provides a highly systematic and exhaustive approach to mapping minimal ECH pathways.

Scientific Candor and Rigor: The manuscript stands out for its high degree of transparency regarding its theoretical limitations. The author clearly distinguishes between controlled effective field theory (EFT) calculations and phenomenological on-shell scaling ansätze (e.g., the mass-dimension status of the parity-odd operator in Appendix B).

Elegant Perturbation Theorem: The proof of the perturbation-transparency result for canonical scalar matter is clean and mathematically sound. Explicitly demonstrating how the Holst dual contraction vanishes pointwise via the first algebraic Bianchi identity—and carefully distinguishing it from the Pontryagin density—resolves potential ambiguities in the literature.

Physical Depth via Reheating Reset: The introduction of a parallel thermodynamic erasure channel via the reheating thermal-reset barrier provides a robust physical mechanism for why bounce-era torsion memory is overridden, shifting the argument from pure dimensional bookkeeping to active microphysics.

BLOCKERS
None. The manuscript does not exhibit any load-bearing logical flaws, missing essential controls, or un-caveated foundational claims that would halt its path toward publication.

MAJORS
None. The scoping limitations, conservative estimations, and placeholders for concurrent companion papers are handled deliberately and transparently within the text. Substantial scientific rework of the core proofs is not required.

MINORS

1. Convergence Status of Companion Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Runs
Section XI, Page 21 (Line 878): The text explicitly states that the dedicated Cobaya chain evaluating the free w0wa extension "has not yet converged to the standard publication-quality target".
Fix: While this transparent acknowledgment is appropriate for a preprint repository, for formal journal publication, the author should update this paragraph with the finalized convergence diagnostics (R^−1 < 10^−2) once the run is complete, ensuring that the qualitative structural observations are anchored by fully stable posterior metrics.

2. Main Body Contextualization of the Basis-Conversion Gap
Section IV D, Page 14 (Line 601): Footnote 5 provides a crucial physical clarification regarding the 10x basis-conversion gap between the paper's effective parameter α/M and the canonical axion-like particle (ALP)-photon coupling gaγ.
Fix: Because this gap directly forces non-trivial UV-completion assumptions (e.g., a sub-Planckian decay constant fa ∼ MPl/10), a single clarifying sentence should be synthesized into the main text body of Section IV D to guide readers who scan the text without thoroughly digesting the footnotes.

3. Minor Clarification of Diagram Text and Variable Mismatches
Figures 1 and 4/7 Text Elements: In the raw text content of Figure 1, the power-law spectral index annotation shows up with variable rendering or minor typos (e.g., "PTA t=3,0" or "V.5. data 2.567"). The caption correctly defines γPTA as the gravitational wave background spectral index distinct from the Barbero-Immirzi parameter γ.
Fix: Verify that the high-resolution vector text components within the graphical blocks are clean, uniformly formatted, and free of typestyle artifacts before finalizing the page layouts.
