**Referee Report for CQG (Paper)**

**Paper:** P1N, “What Minimal Einstein–Cartan Torsion Does for the Bounce and Cannot Do for Dark Energy,” Houston Golden (10 pp., rendered PDF examined at ≥300 dpi).

**Findings**

**P1N-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract + p. 1, §I)**  
Abstract states the benchmark value “γ = 0.2375” and the derived ratio “β/α = 2.11” as load-bearing quantities. The body (p. 2) introduces both only as “the programme’s benchmark γ = 0.2375.” No derivation or external reference fixing this numerical value appears inside the submitted manuscript.  
*Required fix:* Either compute or cite the origin of γ = 0.2375 inside the paper, or remove all numerical claims that depend on it.

**P1N-E2 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract + pp. 2–3, §II–III)**  
Abstract asserts that the perturbation-transparency theorem is proved and that the NJL scalar gap equation “has no nonzero solution.” The only rigorous statement offered is the sketch in §III; the text explicitly defers the full proof and the Fierz rearrangement verification to “the archived companion manuscript” (p. 3) and to GitHub scripts. The paper therefore fails the standalone-reader test for its single Tier-I claim.  
*Required fix:* All load-bearing proofs must be self-contained or the abstract must be rewritten to state only what is proved inside these 10 pages.

**P1N-E3 (ESSENTIAL, p. 1 + p. 9, Data Availability)**  
The reproducibility section points to GitHub commits and a Zenodo DOI whose content is described as “companion paper (Paper I, v1C.0.16)”. No frozen release hash or explicit statement that the scripts reproduce the exact numbers in the submitted PDF is supplied. Several commit hashes pre-date the stated submission version.  
*Required fix:* Provide a single, version-locked archive that a reader can download and execute without reference to any companion document.

**P1N-M1 (MAJOR, pp. 4–6, Table I + §IV–V)**  
Fourteen “mechanism-class constraints” are enumerated, yet five of them (B5, B6, B7, B10, B13) are explicitly labeled “general naturalness or classification arguments rather than sharp ECH-specific calculations.” The abstract nevertheless claims these barriers “jointly close” all four dark-energy channels. The logical gap between a general naturalness remark and a channel-closing theorem is not bridged inside the manuscript.

**P1N-M2 (MAJOR, p. 2, Eq. (4) and surrounding text)**  
The numerical prefactor 3.884 × 10^{-69} is stated to be “the value consistent with this paper’s single ρ_Λ,obs = (2.25 meV)^4.” No derivation of the 3.884 coefficient from the displayed inputs (κ n_ψ², n_ψ = 100 cm^{-3}, etc.) is shown; the reader must accept an uncomputed arithmetic step.

**P1N-M3 (MAJOR, p. 1, date line)**  
The manuscript is dated “September 2, 2026.” A submission date in the future is incompatible with a contemporaneous review process and raises provenance questions.

**P1N-N1 (MINOR, throughout)**  
Repeated use of internal series labels (“Route 2”, “R1–R4”, “the programme”) without a self-contained definition paragraph forces the reader to consult earlier installments.

**P1N-N2 (NIT)**  
Several sentences contain duplicated phrasing (“the the”, “finite finite”) and typographic artifacts (“programme’s” British spelling alternates with American “program” in the same paragraph).

**Summary recommendation**  
REJECT

The manuscript is not evaluable as a standalone Classical and Quantum Gravity article. Its central positive result (the perturbation-transparency theorem) and its quantitative benchmark are both deferred to external companions; the abstract contains numerical claims whose provenance is not recoverable from the submitted text; and the logical chain from “general naturalness arguments” to “all four channels closed” is asserted rather than demonstrated inside the paper. These defects are structural and cannot be repaired by minor revision.
