**Referee Report: PRD-STANDARD (adversarial first read)**

**Paper:** Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\) and a joint \((r,f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces at constant sound speed (Houston Golden, 25 pp., submitted as regular article)

**A3M-E1 (ESSENTIAL, Abstract p.1 + Sec. III p.4–5)**  
The abstract states “the cubic term uncomputed there so the full \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}\) is only a bracket, \([-1.20,-0.09]\) and \([-1.27,-0.13]\)” for LQC and poly. This qualification appears only once in the abstract and is not repeated at every later juxtaposition of the bracketed numbers with the fully computed Quinton-type value \(-1.25\). Every subsequent table or sentence that places the three numbers side-by-side therefore violates the rule that \(\sigma\)- or amplitude values obtained under different procedures must carry an explicit “not directly comparable” qualifier at every occurrence. Required fix: insert the qualifier in every table caption, every results paragraph, and the abstract sentence itself, or recompute the cubic term for LQC/poly so that all three backgrounds rest on identical methodology.

**A3M-E2 (ESSENTIAL, Sec. VIII p.16–17 + Table VII p.25)**  
The joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) no-go is presented as an observational exclusion (“jointly excluded by \(r\) and \(f_{\rm NL}\)”). The \(f_{\rm NL}\) side of the exclusion, however, rests on the tree-level validity window \(c_s\gtrsim0.525\) (95 %) derived from the in-in integral expiring, not from any measured bound on \(f_{\rm NL}\). The abstract and Sec. VIII therefore conflate a theoretical consistency requirement with an observational constraint. Required fix: rewrite every “excluded by \(r\) and \(f_{\rm NL}\)” sentence to read “excluded by the combination of the BICEP/Keck \(r\) bound and the requirement that the perturbative calculation remain valid,” and add a one-sentence caveat in the abstract.

**A3M-M1 (MAJOR, entire manuscript length)**  
25 pages for a re-derivation of a single number plus four null or marginal channels exceeds the scope of a regular PRD article. The core claims (exact amplitude, \(\lambda\)-general no-go, three honest nulls) can be stated in \(\leq15\) pages. Required fix: condense to 12–15 pages or convert to a Letter; the present length is not justified by the incremental advance over Li et al. (2016) and Quintin et al. (2015).

**A3M-M2 (MAJOR, Sec. IV p.8–9 + Table IV p.9)**  
The refit \(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) is placed next to the official NANOGraV posterior \(\gamma_{\rm HD}=3.2^{+0.6}_{-0.6}\) without an explicit statement that the two posteriors are not directly comparable (different frequency binning, different priors, different marginalization). The same juxtaposition recurs in the text. Required fix: add the qualifier at every comparison or remove the refit from the main narrative.

**A3M-M3 (MAJOR, Sec. V p.10–12 + Table V p.24)**

Required fix: remove the numerical ratio from the abstract and from all headline sentences; retain it only inside the detailed discussion with the explicit non-quotable caveat.

**A3M-E3 (ESSENTIAL, provenance surfaces, Sec. II + reproducibility statement p.21–22)**

Required fix: supply a single frozen DOI (Zenodo or equivalent) that contains the exact scripts, seeds, and output files that generated every quoted digit; update all paths to that DOI.

The manuscript contains at least four essential violations (missing “not directly comparable” qualifiers at every juxtaposition of heterogeneous results, conflation of perturbative validity with observational exclusion in the headline no-go claim, non-quotable scalar advertised in the abstract, and incomplete frozen reproducibility provenance). These are not cosmetic; they directly affect the reader’s ability to assess the strength of the central claims. In addition, the 25-page length is disproportionate to the incremental advance. The paper cannot be accepted in its present form.
