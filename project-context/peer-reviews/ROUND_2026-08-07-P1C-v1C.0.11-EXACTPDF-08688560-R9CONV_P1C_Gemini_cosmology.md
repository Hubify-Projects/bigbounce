### General Evaluation
The manuscript presents a highly systematic, meticulously quantified structural no-go survey of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source of late-time dark energy and bounce phenomenology. The dimensional analysis, EFT power-counting, and one-loop amplitude budgets are executed with exceptional care, and the author's explicit evidentiary classification (distinguishing rigorous theorems from ansatz-level estimates) is a model of theoretical transparency. The mathematical derivations—particularly the Fierz rearrangement and the perturbation-transparency proof—are flawless, and the abstract perfectly mirrors the calibrated claims of the body. 

However, the manuscript currently fails the standalone-reader test by explicitly deferring the refereeing of load-bearing claims to an unpublished companion paper, and it violates data availability standards by promising a future code deposit rather than providing a frozen release at the time of review. These procedural and structural issues must be resolved before acceptance.
