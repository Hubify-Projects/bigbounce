## Findings

### NIT-1 — Yin2026 bib entry present but uncited in P1A
**Where:** `references.bib` lines 413–421; no matching `\cite{Yin2026}` in `paper1a_ech_nogo.tex`.
**Issue:** B6 verified the arXiv ID for Yin2026 (2601.13624) but did not add a citation to the manuscript. The bib entry is valid; an unused bib entry is harmless (bibtex skips it). If Yin2026 was meant as supporting evidence for a specific claim in P1A (e.g., joint birefringence + EDE constraints), the citation site is missing. Alternatively the entry may be staged for a downstream paper (P1B / P3 / P4) and was placed in the shared bib by convenience.
**Fix:** either (a) add `\cite{Yin2026}` at the appropriate site if one was intended, or (b) leave as-is — the unused entry costs nothing. **Disposition: NIT, not gating.**

### NIT-2 — Heinrich bib key `Heinrich:2023` lags the inline year `2024`
**Where:** `references.bib` line 598 (`@article{Heinrich:2023, ... year = {2024}, ...}`); citing in P1A as `Heinrich \etal~2024 \cite{Heinrich:2023}`.
**Issue:** key/title cosmetic mismatch. The eprint posted in 2023, the journal version is 2024. Renaming to `Heinrich:2024` would force a cascade across P1A, P1B, P2, P3, P4 and the shared bib. Cost outweighs benefit.
**Fix:** None. Standing convention in this codebase is "key = eprint year, displayed year = journal year." Document the convention once in `project-context/SSOT/index.md` and stop revisiting; future R-rounds should suppress this finding.
**Disposition: NIT, not gating, suppress future surfacing.**
