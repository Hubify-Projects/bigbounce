### B1 (BLOCKER) §V Bayesian + Table 4 / Table 5 (lines 215–232 vs 268–282) — Delta-prior Bayes-factor numerics disagree between Table~\ref{tab:bayes} and Table~\ref{tab:gr}; abstract envelope $8$–$17$ cannot be reproduced from Table~\ref{tab:gr} alone.

The abstract (line 29) and §Bayesian (line 211, line 234) commit to the headline:
> "Bayes factor $\sim 8$–$17$ under the broad $[-15,+15]$ multifield competitor prior" with "the upper bound $\mathrm{BF}\approx 17$ is the delta-prior theoretical maximum at the same competitor prior."

Table~\ref{tab:bayes} (line 222–227) reports two delta-prior rows:
- Row 4: "Delta at $\fnl=-35/8$ (theoretical maximum only) ... $8$–$11^a$" — footnote $a$ ties this to GR-variation across the four scenarios of Table~\ref{tab:gr}.
- Row 5: "Delta at $\fnl=-35/8$, broad $[-15,+15]$ multifield, $\sigma_{\rm GR}=0.5$ ... $\sim 17^b$".

Table~\ref{tab:gr} (line 273–277) BF-vs-Tuned column gives $\{10.9, 9.4, 7.9, 10.9\}$ across $\{\text{Ideal}, \sigma_{\rm GR}=0.5, \sigma_{\rm GR}=1.0, \text{Corrected}\}$ — i.e., $7.9$–$10.9 \approx 8$–$11$.

The numerical inconsistency: at $\sigma_{\rm GR}=0.5$, Table~\ref{tab:gr} gives BF vs Tuned $= 9.4$. Table~\ref{tab:bayes} row 5, also at $\sigma_{\rm GR}=0.5$ and at the delta bounce prior, claims BF $\sim 17$ vs broad-multifield. If the "Tuned" competitor in Table~\ref{tab:gr} is the *same* broad $[-15,+15]$ multifield competitor of Table~\ref{tab:bayes} row 5, the two tables should give the same number at $\sigma_{\rm GR}=0.5$. They give $9.4$ vs $17$ — a factor-of-1.8 disagreement that is unexplained anywhere in the paper.

If the "Tuned" competitor in Table~\ref{tab:gr} uses a *narrower* prior (which is the only way to reconcile the two), the prior width of "Tuned" is never specified and the abstract envelope $8$–$17$ cannot be reproduced from Table~\ref{tab:gr}'s numbers alone.

This propagates to the abstract claim "GR-marginalization variation across the four scenarios of Table~\ref{tab:gr} ... introduces a separate $\mathrm{BF}\approx 8$–$11$ spread on the delta-prior row of Table~\ref{tab:bayes}" — that reads as if Table~\ref{tab:gr}'s 8–11 spread is *the* delta-prior row of Table~\ref{tab:bayes}. But Table~\ref{tab:bayes} has *two* delta-prior rows (4 and 5) reading $8$–$11$ and $\sim 17$ respectively, drawn from different competitor priors. Without specifying Table~\ref{tab:gr}'s competitor-prior width, the reader cannot tell whether the abstract's "delta-prior theoretical maximum at the same competitor prior $[-15,+15]$" is consistent with Table~\ref{tab:gr}, or whether $8$–$11$ and $17$ are two entirely different analyses bolted together.

**Required fix:** state Table~\ref{tab:gr}'s competitor prior width explicitly in the table caption (presumably $[-5,+5]$ to give the $7$–$11$ range, which would be consistent with line 234's "Bayes factors from 7 (narrow $[-5,+5]$) to 17 (broad $[-15,+15]$) at the delta bounce prior"). Then add a sentence noting that Table~\ref{tab:bayes} row 4's $8$–$11$ is at the *narrow* competitor prior (not broad), and row 5's $\sim 17$ is at *broad*.

### M1 (MAJOR) §II.A line 70 — 3-dimensional null space asserted without rank check

> "we constructed the $3\!\times\!6$ constraint matrix from the three benchmark configurations and computed its SVD, confirming a 3-dimensional null space."

A $3\times 6$ matrix has a 3-dimensional null space *if and only if* it has rank 3 (full row rank). The paper does not state that the SVD found three nonzero singular values — only that the null space dimension is 3. If the three benchmark configurations (squeezed, equilateral, folded) happened to produce linearly *dependent* constraint rows (e.g., if the squeezed and equilateral kinematics collapsed to colinear monomial-coefficient gradients in some pathological numerical regime), the null space would be 4-dimensional and the $10{,}000$-sample scan radius interpretation would shift. A reviewer expects an explicit rank statement: "The SVD has three nonzero singular values $\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3$ with smallest-to-largest ratio $\sigma_3/\sigma_1 = \mathcal{O}(0.X)$, confirming rank-3 (full row rank) and therefore a 3-dimensional null space."

### M2 (MAJOR) Table~\ref{tab:bayes} row 4 caption — "theoretical maximum only" label is misleading

Row 4 reads "Delta at $\fnl=-35/8$ (theoretical maximum only) — $8$–$11^a$." Row 5 reads "$\sim 17^b$" at the same delta-prior with broad $[-15,+15]$. If row 5 is genuinely the delta-prior maximum (as the abstract and the bullet list at lines 206–210 claim), then row 4's value cannot also be labeled "theoretical maximum." Calling row 4 "theoretical maximum only" while row 5 numerically dominates is a label error — row 4 is GR-variation at *some* (presumably narrower) competitor prior, not the theoretical maximum. This compounds B1: the row labels disagree with the abstract.

**Suggested fix:** in the abstract, add one sentence noting that the SDB joint-Fisher gives a higher idealized significance ($\sim 9.9\sigma$ marginalized over $n_\fnl$) but is more vulnerable to ultra-large-scale and $b_\phi$ systematics, motivating the bispectrum-only forecast as the conservative headline.
