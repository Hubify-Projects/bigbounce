## ESSENTIAL

**E1 — §II (contact-term EOS), p.3.** The paper assigns $\rho_{4\psi}=-\mathcal L_{4\psi}$, $p_{4\psi}=+\mathcal L_{4\psi}$ ("for a term with no explicit time derivatives"), i.e. $w=-1$. This is the correct stress tensor *only* for a Lagrangian with literally no field-scaling dependence on the physical setting (a true vacuum-energy-like term). But §II.A parametrizes the term's magnitude via a fermion **number density** $n_\psi\propto a^{-3}$, giving $\rho_{4\psi}\propto n_\psi^2\propto a^{-6}$. Covariant conservation $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p)=0$ with $\rho\propto a^{-6}$ forces $w=+1$ (stiff), not $w=-1$. Under the correct $w=+1$, $\rho+3p=4\rho_{4\psi}=-4\mathcal L_{4\psi}$, so the repulsion condition **inverts** relative to what the paper derives. This also disagrees with the standard ECSK spin-fluid literature (Hehl et al.; Popławski's own Friedmann equations), where the spin contact term gives $\varepsilon_{\rm spin}=p_{\rm spin}<0$ (negative-energy stiff fluid, $w=+1$), not $w=-1$. The paper explicitly declines to build a formal signature bridge to Popławski's own conventions ("nor is a formal signature bridge to Popławski's own conventions asserted beyond this sign-of-pressure check") — but that sign-of-pressure check is the only argument offered for the paper's entire "positive/bounce" half.

**Required fix**: derive $T_{\mu\nu}$ for the contact term by explicit variation with the fermion-fluid averaging made explicit, show the resulting $(\rho,p)$ and their scaling with $a$, and reconcile with the standard ECSK $w=+1$ result. State the signature translation of Eq. (3) against Hehl–Datta explicitly.

**E2 — §VII.D ("Engaging Poplawski's own dark-energy proposal"), pp.8-9.** The manuscript states Popławski "identif[ies] the same spin-spin contact interaction that supports his bounce with a present-day vacuum energy density set by the average cosmic fermion (baryon) spin density~\cite{Poplawski2012}." The cited key `Poplawski2012` resolves (`references.bib`) to "Cosmological constant from quarks and torsion," Annalen der Physik 523, 291 (2011), arXiv:1005.0893 — a paper whose actual mechanism sets $\rho_\Lambda$ via the **QCD quark chiral condensate** $\langle\bar qq\rangle\sim-(235\,{\rm MeV})^3$, not a baryon number density. §VII.D then evaluates its "direct, quantitative rebuttal" at the §II.A ISM benchmark ($n_\psi\sim100\,{\rm cm^{-3}}$), giving $3.9\times10^{-69}$ — but substituting the actual cited quantity, $\tfrac{3}{16}\kappa\langle\bar qq\rangle^2\approx5.3\times10^{-42}\,{\rm GeV^4}$ against $\rho_{\Lambda,\rm obs}=2.56\times10^{-47}\,{\rm GeV^4}$, is a factor $\approx2\times10^5$ **above** observed density (still $\approx10^4$ above after the $\gamma^2/(1+\gamma^2)=0.053$ suppression) — not $10^{-69}$ below it, a discrepancy of order $10^{74}$ in the wrong direction. §II.A itself warns against exactly this substitution ("number density also does not fix the renormalized composite... a vacuum stress tensor, or an equation of state"), which §VII.D then performs anyway and calls a "direct, quantitative rebuttal."

**Required fix**: either redo §VII.D at Popławski's actual scale (the quark condensate) and report the true outcome, or withdraw the "direct, quantitative rebuttal" claim and correct the description of the cited reference.
