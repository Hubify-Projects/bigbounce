This is a referee report for the manuscript "Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$", submitted to Physical Review D. 

The paper presents a multi-channel observational assessment of the matter-bounce scenario's prediction for local non-Gaussianity. The author re-derives the pre-bounce amplitude $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ using both in-in and separate-universe formalisms (resolving a literature discrepancy), bounds its transmission through the bounce, and evaluates its consistency against pulsar timing (NANOGrav), primordial black holes (PBH), and large-scale structure (LSS) forecasts. 

The theoretical resolution of the factor-of-two discrepancy (identifying the failure of the zero-shift threading in the non-attractor phase) is excellent, and the use of the compaction function to bypass the artificial ceiling of the quadratic map in the PBH sector is physically rigorous. However, the manuscript contains a critical physical inconsistency in how the LSS constraints are applied, relies on unpublished drafts for load-bearing parameters, lacks a frozen data repository, and contains several instances of internal draft-history prose that must be removed. 

I recommend MAJOR REVISIONS. The specific findings are detailed below.

### ESSENTIAL

**A3M-E1**
*Section:* Reproducibility Statement (Page 9)
*Problem:* "This work's own code and artifacts are pinned by the GitHub commit hash above rather than a frozen-release DOI of their own; minting one is a maintainer action planned for the packaging stage."
*Required fix:* PRD requires a permanent, frozen-release DOI (e.g., Zenodo, Figshare) for all custom code and data at the time of review. A GitHub commit hash is mutable and insufficient, and deferring this to the "packaging stage" violates data availability standards. Mint the DOI and update the manuscript.

**A3M-E2**
*Section:* VI.B (Page 8) and Reproducibility Statement (Page 9)
*Problem:* The text states "A shape-overlap-projected column (from this lab's P2 forecast, not yet re-derived at this paper's fiducial) is omitted pending that re-derivation" and "The bounce-to-local shape overlap (r, Sec. VI) is imported from this lab's companion Fisher-forecast draft".
*Required fix:* The manuscript must be self-contained. You cannot cite an unpublished draft ("P2 forecast", "Fisher-forecast draft") for load-bearing parameters, nor can you omit derivations because they are "pending" in an unsubmitted companion paper. Either include the derivation of the shape overlap $r$ in this manuscript, or remove the reliance on it entirely. 

**A3M-E3**
*Section:* Multiple (Pages 3, 5, 8)
*Problem:* The manuscript contains internal review-log prose and version-history language. 
- Page 3: "...left open in earlier drafts of this work is now closed..."
- Page 5: "This also supersedes an earlier, misdescribed claim in this section that a 6-bin, Gaussian-$\chi^2$ validation..."
- Page 8: "...not yet re-derived at this paper's fiducial) is omitted pending that re-derivation (see text)."
*Required fix:* Remove all internal bookkeeping, draft history, and review-log prose. The published paper must stand as a final, single-version document.

**A3M-E4**
*Section:* Abstract (Page 1) and Section VI (Page 8)
*Problem:* The abstract claims "this channel [LSS] alone discriminates the two candidate amplitudes, with SPHEREx reaching $3.13\sigma$". However, Section VI achieves this $3.13\sigma$ significance by comparing the *pre-bounce* amplitude ($-35/16 \approx -2.19$) directly to the observational forecast. The paper explicitly acknowledges in Section VII that the transmitted *post-bounce* amplitude is suppressed ($f_{NL}^{after} \in [-0.65, -0.50]$ at PBH scales) and that transmission to LSS scales is uncomputed. Comparing a pre-bounce theoretical amplitude to a post-bounce observational constraint is physically invalid. If the transmission suppresses the amplitude by a factor of $\sim 4$ at LSS scales (as it does at PBH scales), SPHEREx ($\sigma=0.7$) will not discriminate the models at $3\sigma$. 
*Required fix:* The LSS forecast must use the post-bounce amplitude. If the transmission to LSS scales cannot be computed, the abstract and Section VI must explicitly state that the $3.13\sigma$ discrimination assumes perfect transmission ($T_{f_{NL}}=1$), an assumption the paper itself shows is violated at other scales. The abstract claim must be downgraded to reflect this physical caveat.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The theoretical physics in the first half of the paper (resolving the in-in vs $\delta N$ discrepancy) is rigorous and well-executed. However, the paper cannot be accepted in its current form due to a major physical inconsistency in the LSS forecast (comparing pre-bounce theory to post-bounce observables without accounting for the transmission factor), the reliance on unpublished drafts for missing derivations, the lack of a frozen DOI, and the inclusion of internal draft-history prose. Once these issues are addressed and the LSS claims are properly caveated, the manuscript will be a strong candidate for publication in PRD.
