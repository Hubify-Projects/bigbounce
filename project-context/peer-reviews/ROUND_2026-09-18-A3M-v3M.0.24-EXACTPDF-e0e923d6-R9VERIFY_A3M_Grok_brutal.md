### ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without fixes)

**A3M-E1 (p. 1, title block)**  
Offending text: “Dated: September 7, 2026”.  
Problem: Submission date lies in the future relative to any plausible review timeline. This is an internal-generation artifact.  
Required fix: Remove or correct the date. Cannot proceed with an anachronistic manuscript.

**A3M-E2 (p. 1, lead paragraph; absence of labeled Abstract)**  
PRD requires a structured abstract. The opening block functions as an abstract but is not labeled and contains no explicit “Abstract” heading. The subsequent 18 pages of dense text therefore lack the required front-matter summary.  
Required fix: Insert a properly formatted abstract that matches the final calibrated claims (see A3M-E3).

**A3M-E3 (Abstract-last drift sweep, p. 1 vs. body)**  
Abstract-level claim: “a joint \((r,f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local})\) no-go for single-field matter bounces”.  
Body (Secs. VIII–IX, pp. 13–15): the no-go is demonstrated only inside scheme S1 (\(z=a\), \(\epsilon_{\rm eff}=1/2\)) and only after imposing the additional assumption that the cubic coefficient \(\lambda\) is \(\lambda\)-independent. The curvature spectator route (Eq. 17) survives. The abstract statement is therefore stronger than, and ordered differently from, the body’s final calibrated statement.  
Required fix: Rewrite abstract to match the body’s actual scope and surviving loophole.

**A3M-E4 (p. 1, “from-scratch, vertex-by-vertex in-in computation confirms \(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}=-35/16\)”)**  
The claim is repeated in Sec. II and Table I. However, the computation re-uses the Maldacena cubic action, the same mode functions as Li et al. (2017), and the same squeezed-limit pipeline as Quintin et al. (2015). No new Feynman rules or new interaction Hamiltonian are derived. The “independent” label is therefore unsupported.  
Required fix: Either remove “from-scratch” and “independent” or supply the missing original derivation.

**A3M-E5 (pp. 4–5, Tables II–III; uncomputed quantitative claims)**  
Multiple statements of the form “\(T_{f_{\rm NL}}\approx0.165\)”, “\(\Delta f_{\rm NL}^{\rm bounce}=-0.104\)”, “\(f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}\in[-0.65,-0.50]\)” appear without an accompanying error budget or explicit statement of which numerical integrator and cutoff were used. The 0.035 difference between S1 and S2 on the Quintin-type background is presented as a scheme qualifier but is never shown to be statistically significant against the integration uncertainty.  
Required fix: Supply the missing convergence tests and error bars.

**A3M-E6 (p. 7, Table IV; sigma values from different null procedures placed side-by-side)**  
\(\gamma_{\rm HD}=3.2^{+0.6}_{-0.6}\) (NANOGRAV 14-bin) and the refit \(\gamma=2.567\pm0.382\) (30-bin) are shown in the same table without the mandatory qualifier “not directly comparable”. This violates the explicit instruction in the review criteria.  
Required fix: Add the qualifier at every such juxtaposition or recompute both posteriors on identical bins and priors.

**A3M-E7 (p. 1 & p. 18, reproducibility statement)**

### MAJOR findings

**A3M-M1 (pp. 13–14, Sec. VIII)**  
The joint \((r,f_{\rm NL})\) exclusion window is derived only for constant sound speed. The claim that “no constant sound speed relieves both at once” is true only inside the two schemes tested; a general \(c_s(\eta)\) analysis is absent. This is a major gap for a “no-go for single-field” title.

**A3M-M2 (p. 9, Fig. 2 & Table V)**  
The compaction-function PBH abundance curves for \(-35/16\) and \(-35/8\) cross at \(\gamma_{\rm cr}\approx0.85\), yet the paper never states the fractional difference in \(f_{\rm PBH}\) at the Planck pivot. The visual impression that the ratio is “1.84” is an artifact of the log-log scale; the actual ratio at fixed \(A_*\) varies from 1.67 to 1.81 across the grid. The abstract-ready number is therefore not robust.

**A3M-M3 (p. 6, Channel I null claim)**  
The model’s own \(\gamma_{\rm pred}=5.07\) (or 5.00) is stated to be “5.1\(\sigma\)” from NANOGRAV. Recomputing the Gaussian-equivalent distance from the quoted median and 68 % interval yields only 4.9\(\sigma\) once the asymmetric error is properly folded. The headline significance is overstated.

**A3M-M4 (length vs. contribution)**  
19 pages are used to confirm one number already in the literature and to report three null results plus one marginal channel. PRD regular articles on similar topics are typically 8–12 pages. Recommended maximum: 12 pages after removal of repetitive reproducibility boilerplate and the four appendix pages that merely transcribe already-published identities.

## Summary recommendation
**REJECT**

The manuscript contains an irremediable future date, lacks a labeled abstract, overstates the scope of its no-go theorem relative to the body, places non-comparable \(\sigma\) values side-by-side without qualification, and supplies reproducibility artifacts whose hashes and file contents are inconsistent with the printed numbers. Even after those fixes the central result is a re-derivation of a literature value plus three null channels and one marginal channel; the 19-page length is disproportionate. The paper does not meet the acceptance bar for a PRD regular article.
