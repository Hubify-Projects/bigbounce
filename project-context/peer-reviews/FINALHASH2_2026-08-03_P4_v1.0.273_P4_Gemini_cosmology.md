Here is the referee report for the manuscript submitted to The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series.

### Cosmological & Theoretical Rigor
The paper presents a highly rigorous, defensively written observational analysis of a DESI spiral galaxy catalog, placing limits on a chirality dipole. The authors correctly identify that the $\ell=1$ morphology observable is parity-even and cannot by itself constrain primordial parity violation (e.g., Chern-Simons gravity or cosmic birefringence) without a derived galaxy-formation transfer function. The strict separation of the observational catalog release from unsupportable cosmological claims is excellent and meets the high standards of this venue. 

However, the manuscript suffers from severe version-control leakage and reads in many places like a response-to-referees document rather than a standalone scientific paper. The text is saturated with forensic accounting of the authors' own "historical" (pre-review or earlier draft) pipeline versions. Furthermore, the cryptographic provenance guarantees—which are a major selling point of the paper—are explicitly broken in the current draft, as the text admits the manuscript version has drifted ahead of the frozen Zenodo/HuggingFace hashes. These issues, along with missing effect sizes for several quoted significances, must be corrected.

### Findings

**ESSENTIAL: Provenance Mismatch and Stale Version Labels**
*   **ID:** P4-E1
*   **Section + Page:** Data Availability (Page 29, Table 18 and Page 30, lines 1616-1619)
*   **Specific problem:** The manuscript claims to provide immutable cryptographic provenance, but explicitly admits the hashes are stale. The paper is version `v1.0.273` (Page 1, line 6). However, Table 18 lists the release overlay as `v1.0.259`. Furthermore, the text states: "the present manuscript is v1.0.273, one patch line ahead; current and subsequent versions will be added to the same Zenodo record on the next re-stage" and "The deposit pins the exact git commit 397671bf". 
*   **Required fix:** The cryptographic hashes, Zenodo DOIs, and GitHub commit hashes must match the *exact* submitted version of the paper. You cannot claim strict computational closure while admitting the manuscript is "one patch line ahead" of the deposited artifacts. Update all hashes and deposits to match the final accepted version before publication.

**ESSENTIAL: Side-by-Side Sigmas Without Qualification**
*   **ID:** P4-E2
*   **Section + Page:** Section 6 (Page 15, line 857) and Appendix C (Page 19, line 1236)
*   **Specific problem:** "The raw Catalog A dipole (2.31$\sigma$ real-space; +6.48$\sigma$ pre-MASTER) demonstrates..." and "Our raw (Catalog A) analysis produces a 2.31$\sigma$ real-space dipole and a +6.48$\sigma$ pre-MASTER pseudo-$C_\ell$..."
*   **Required fix:** These two $\sigma$ values are derived from entirely different null procedures (real-space fixed-occupancy vs. harmonic). Per the strict reporting standards, they cannot be juxtaposed without an explicit qualification that they are not directly comparable as Gaussian tail significances. Add the qualification at both instances.

**MAJOR: Missing Effect Sizes for Headline Significances**
*   **ID:** P4-M1
*   **Section + Page:** Section 4.6 (Page 14, line 827) and Figure 7 Caption (Page 12, line 682)
*   **Specific problem:** The text quotes a "$+3.29\sigma$ signal in the 1.87M-galaxy [0.5, 0.6) confidence bin" and a "2.31$\sigma$ real-space dipole + +6.48$\sigma$ pre-MASTER $\ell=1$ artifact". 
*   **Required fix:** Every $\chi^2/\sigma/p$ headline must carry a physical effect-size statement. Provide the fractional amplitude ($A$) or dipole magnitude corresponding to the $+3.29\sigma$ signal, the $2.31\sigma$ dipole, and the $+6.48\sigma$ artifact.

**MAJOR: Uncomputed Quantitative Claim**
*   **ID:** P4-M2
*   **Section + Page:** Appendix D (Page 28, line 1585)
*   **Specific problem:** "The spiral fraction is uniform across the DESI Legacy footprint at the $\lesssim 2\%$ level across 7 equatorial coordinate slabs, with no coherent large-scale pattern that would bias the dipole analysis."
*   **Required fix:** "Would bias the dipole analysis" is an uncomputed assertion of negligible impact. You must quantify this: what is the maximum induced dipole amplitude $A$ that could be generated by a $2\%$ spatial variation in the spiral fraction? Provide the bounding number or an artifact pointer to the calculation.

### Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The observational methodology, equivariant network design, and statistical rigor of the dipole limits are outstanding and perfectly suited for ApJS. However, the manuscript cannot be published while explicitly admitting its cryptographic provenance hashes are stale/mismatched to the PDF version. Furthermore, the text requires a thorough scrubbing of internal review-log jargon ("post-review closure", "pod-bound") and must provide physical effect sizes for all quoted $\sigma$ anomalies. Finally, restructuring the text to move the heavy forensic auditing of the authors' own prior drafts into an appendix will vastly improve readability for the general astrophysical community.
