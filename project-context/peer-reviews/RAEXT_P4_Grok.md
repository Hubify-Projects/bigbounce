Paper: Survey-Scale Galaxy Chirality with Equivariant TTA: A Null Real-Space Chirality Dipole, a Quantifiable Monopole-Mask Leakage Channel, and Diagnostic Evidence for a Depth/Morphology-Correlated Canonical-Mask Residual on 8.47 Million DESI Legacy Galaxies (3.2 Million Spirals)
Author: Houston Golden
Date of report: 29 June 2026
1. Recommendation
MINOR REVISIONS
The paper presents the largest chirality-labeled galaxy catalog to date and a carefully executed null result on the real-space dipole. The primary estimators (HC real-space dipole at +0.41σ and block-bootstrap WLS template exclusion of a clean 1.7% dipole at z ≈ −18) are robust, the systematics audit is unusually thorough, and the public data release is exemplary. The work meets the high bar for these journals after modest revisions that tighten discussion of limitations and strengthen one key interpretive claim.
2. BLOCKERS
None.
3. MAJORS
None that rise to the level of requiring new major analysis or that would alter the primary scientific conclusions. The following point is close to major but is ultimately judged minor because the primary estimators remain conservative and the paper already flags the issue transparently.
Pseudo-label inheritance and independence of the null tests (MINOR, but the most substantive interpretive point).
66.5% of the training labels derive from CE-ResNet pseudo-labels (Sec. II B). The per-galaxy label-shuffle and per-pixel permutation nulls therefore randomize the model’s own outputs; they do not by themselves test whether the classifier inherited spatially coherent, survey-correlated structure from the pseudo-labels. The author correctly notes this limitation in Sec. VI A and argues that the eight-anchor battery in Appendix D (template regression against imaging-leg fractions, density, depth proxies; quality-quartile washout; ℓ=2 > ℓ=1 broadband structure; cross-spectrum at ℓ=2) bounds any inherited contribution because such structure would correlate with the external templates.
This argument is reasonable and the real-space HC estimator is conservative against an inherited bias (any such bias would push the dipole away from null). However, for a top-journal claim that the observed harmonic residuals are “systematics-attributed” and that the catalog enables precision isotropy tests down to the empirical 0.75% floor, a stronger closure would be desirable. The paper itself flags the “canonical pseudo-label-independence follow-up” (a sub-model trained exclusively on the 6,637 human-verified GZ1 labels, re-inferred on the full 8.47 M galaxies, and compared directly) as requiring GPU resources and therefore deferred.
Requested revision: Elevate this from a flagged future item to a short, explicit discussion in Sec. VI A (or a new short subsection) that (i) quantifies the maximum plausible dilution or bias that could be inherited given the GZ1 cross-match statistics (Cohen’s κ = 0.40, chirality accuracy 69.91% on the disjoint set), and (ii) states whether the current eight-anchor evidence already rules out an inherited contribution large enough to affect the primary real-space null or the A95 falsification boundary. If the author judges a full retrain infeasible before resubmission, this textual strengthening is sufficient.
4. MINORS
These are all straightforward to address and do not affect the core results.
Shamir comparison (MINOR). The paper demonstrates that its maximum WLS template amplitude (0.32% in the cleanest equal-area partition; up to 0.46–0.56% in slab maxima) is inconsistent in amplitude with Shamir’s reported 1.7–4.0% range by a factor ~5–12 under the present pipeline. It correctly caveats that a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is required for a likelihood-level exclusion. For a top journal, the current wording (“inconsistent in amplitude … though a matched-footprint Ganalyzer reanalysis is required”) is acceptable but could be tightened: either (a) perform a limited matched reanalysis on a representative sky patch if computationally cheap, or (b) add one paragraph explaining the dominant methodological differences (three-class classifier with explicit NOT_SPIRAL, 2-fold TTA equivariance, per-pixel N_spiral ≥ 10 canonical mask, monopole-subtracted real-space estimator) that make a direct numerical comparison to Ganalyzer non-trivial. The present caution is honest; a slightly more explicit statement of why the pipelines are not interchangeable would remove any appearance of over-claiming.
Harmonic-channel language and emphasis (MINOR). The paper is internally consistent in declaring the real-space HC dipole and WLS template fit as primary and the MASTER ℓ=1 residuals (+3.64σ canonical direct-MC; +7.28σ apodized) as “systematics-attributed diagnostics.” However, the title and abstract headline the “Diagnostic Evidence for a Depth/Morphology-Correlated Canonical-Mask Residual,” which could lead a casual reader to overweight the harmonic channel. A single clarifying sentence in the abstract (or a parenthetical in the title if space allows) that the primary cosmological conclusion rests on the real-space and template-fit estimators would eliminate any risk of misreading.
Minor presentational points (MINOR).
The confidence threshold p_eq > 0.6 is described as “pre-specified” and the full sweep (0–0.8) is shown; this is good. A one-sentence reminder in Sec. IV C that the threshold was fixed in the generator script before any dipole results were inspected would further insulate against forking-paths concerns.
Table III and the surrounding text correctly note that the various σ values come from distinct null procedures and are not directly comparable; this is already handled well but could be reinforced with a short footnote or parenthetical on first use.
A few equation renderings in the extracted PDF (e.g., the repeated line in the TTA definition) are extraction artifacts; the underlying LaTeX is presumed correct. No action required unless the compiled PDF shows the same duplication.
Data-availability phrasing (MINOR). The statement that a Zenodo DOI “will be deposited at journal submission” is standard and acceptable. The current Hugging Face release tag (v2026.04) and the explicit QC flag for the 59,515 flip-identity violator rows are excellent practice.
5. Strengths (≥3)
Unprecedented scale + public resource. 8.47 M galaxies (3.20 M spirals) with three catalog tiers, model weights, and full reproducibility scripts released under CC-BY-4.0. This alone is a major community service and sets a new standard for chirality studies.
Methodological innovation in bias hardening. The demonstration that raw Catalog A produces a spurious 2.31σ real-space dipole and +6.48σ pre-MASTER pseudo-C_ℓ from only a 0.79% classifier monopole, which collapses to +0.41σ / null after 2-fold flip-equivariant TTA, is powerful and pedagogically valuable. Future surveys will need to adopt comparable equivariant post-processing; the paper shows why.
Rigorous, multi-estimator hierarchy with declared primacy. The separation of primary cosmological estimators (HC real-space dipole on p_eq > 0.6; block-bootstrap WLS template exclusion at z ≈ −18) from secondary diagnostics (MASTER ℓ=1, hemisphere LEE, generative monopole null) is clearly stated and consistently applied. The 99.32% reproduction of pre-MASTER power by a pure monopole-mask generative null (N=500) is a clean, quantitative result.
Thorough and transparent systematics audit. The eight-anchor battery in Appendix D (apodization robustness, multipole coherence, quality-quartile washout, leg-proxy cross-power, density-stratified null, boundary-distance variance, joint nuisance-marginalized WLS, direct Ap × n_total cross-spectrum) is unusually complete for this field. The quality-quartile washout (all |σ| < 1, no monotonic trend) and the suggestive ℓ=2 cross-spectrum anti-alignment (r_ℓ=2 = −0.65, z = −2.89) are particularly effective discriminators against a diluted primordial dipole.
Honest treatment of limitations and sensitivity floor. Explicit discussion of pseudo-label dependence, classifier over-confidence relative to GZ1, edge-on dilution (~5–8% sensitivity penalty), and the empirical A_50 ≈ 0.75% / A_95 ∈ (1.0%, 1.5%] injection-recovery floors (with axis-averaged completeness) is refreshingly direct. The falsification criterion for future surveys is clearly stated and estimator-specific.
Summary. This is a strong, careful paper that advances both the observational catalog and the methodological standard for galaxy-chirality isotropy tests. The primary null result is robustly supported. With the minor revisions outlined above—primarily textual clarifications around pseudo-label inheritance and the Shamir comparison—the manuscript will be suitable for a top journal.
