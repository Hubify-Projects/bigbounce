This is a referee report for "A Structural No-Go Survey of Minimal Spin-Torsion Routes to Dark Energy and Bounce Phenomenology" submitted to Classical and Quantum Gravity / PRD.

The manuscript presents a highly rigorous, systematic no-go survey of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source of late-time dark energy. The author catalogs 13 distinct mechanism-class constraints and provides explicit amplitude-budget closures for the one-loop graviton corrections (Route 2) and Immirzi parameter running (Route 3), demonstrating suppression margins of $\sim 60$ and $61-67$ orders of magnitude, respectively. The dimensional analysis, effective field theory power-counting, and Fierz-rearrangement proofs are executed with exceptional precision, and the evidentiary tiering (distinguishing rigorous theorems from ansatz-level estimates) is a model of theoretical clarity. 

However, the manuscript currently violates the standalone-reader requirement by relying on unpublished, non-peer-reviewed Zenodo deposits for load-bearing derivations, and contains several formatting/provenance issues that must be addressed before publication.

### ESSENTIAL
*   **P1C-E1 (Standalone-Reader Test Violation):** Page 2 ("The companion paper publishes the Cartan elimination... We do not repeat that derivation here"), Page 2 ("R4... Its full derivation is outside the scope of this survey"), and Page 8 ("Its full derivation... are outside the present survey's scope [1, 13]"). The manuscript relies heavily on citations [1] and [13] for core derivations (the Cartan elimination, the R4 derivation, and the tensor-sector extension of the perturbation transparency theorem). However, the bibliography explicitly states that [1] and [13] are Zenodo deposits that are "not an arXiv preprint and not peer reviewed." A paper submitted to a rigorous journal cannot outsource load-bearing proofs to an unpublished, non-peer-reviewed PDF archive. **Required fix:** The author must either (a) include the necessary derivations for the Cartan elimination and R4 in an appendix to make this manuscript self-contained, or (b) ensure the companion papers are submitted to and available on a recognized preprint server (e.g., arXiv) or published in a peer-reviewed journal prior to the acceptance of this survey.
*   **P1C-E2 (Version-History Language):** Page 1, under the author affiliation: "(Dated: August 6, 2026 (v1C.0.4))". **Required fix:** Remove the internal version-history tag `(v1C.0.4)`. Journal submissions must present as final canonical texts, not draft iterations.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The physics, dimensional analysis, and mathematical rigor of this manuscript are outstanding, and the order-of-magnitude amplitude budgets are flawlessly computed. However, the structural reliance on unpublished, non-peer-reviewed Zenodo deposits for load-bearing derivations (the Cartan elimination and R4 channel) violates the standalone-reader requirement for a primary research article. The manuscript requires major revisions to either incorporate these derivations directly or properly sequence the publication of the companion papers, alongside fixes to the version-history language and archival DOI preparation.

## PASS 2 — self-critique findings (what initial review missed)

Here are the additional findings from a fresh-eyes review of the manuscript, focusing specifically on arithmetic, dimensional consistency, and formulaic construction. 

### MAJOR
*   **[P1C-M2] (Algebraic/Formula Error in Amplitude Ratio):** Page 6, Eq. (2). The author constructs a dimensionless ratio to evaluate the suppression of the Route-2 birefringence amplitude, defining the left-hand side as $\Delta\theta_{\text{one-loop}}/\Delta\theta_{\text{obs}}$. However, the right-hand side denominator is written as $M_{Pl} (\alpha/M) \beta_{\text{obs}}$. 
    *   If $\Delta\theta_{\text{obs}}$ is the observed angle $\beta_{\text{obs}}$, the denominator should simply be $\beta_{\text{obs}}$. 
    *   Alternatively, if the author is substituting the theoretical R4 relation $\Delta\theta_{\text{obs}} \sim (\alpha/M) M_{Pl}$, the denominator should be $(\alpha/M) M_{Pl}$. 
    *   By including *both* factors in the denominator, the author has effectively divided by the observed angle *and* the theoretical R4 coupling simultaneously. Because the text establishes that $M_{Pl} (\alpha/M) \sim 10^{-2}$, this double-counting artificially inflates the final ratio by two orders of magnitude. The correct arithmetic for the ratio $\Delta\theta_{\text{one-loop}}/\beta_{\text{obs}}$ is $10^{-64} / (6 \times 10^{-3}) \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-62}$, not the $10^{-60}$ calculated in the text. 
    *   **Required fix:** Correct the denominator of Eq. (2) to simply $\beta_{\text{obs}}$ (or explicitly define why the ratio of effective couplings requires the product of both), and update the resulting suppression margin from $\sim 10^{-60}$ to $\sim 10^{-62}$ in the text and abstract. (Note: Because this error made the bound *less* stringent, correcting it only strengthens the author's qualitative conclusion, but the formula must be algebraically consistent).

### MINOR
*   **[P1C-m1] (Table III vs Text Consistency on Operator Fate):** Page 15, Table III. The "Fate" column for operator O4 lists "$\to \kappa^2(J^5 \cdot J^5)$". While the text on Page 14 correctly explains that this is the reduction of the *bare invariant* $\epsilon TT$ (and that the full dimension-4 operator $O_4^{[4]} = M_{Pl}^2 \epsilon TT$ reduces to $\kappa(J^5 \cdot J^5)$ because $M_{Pl}^2 \kappa^2 = \kappa$), a reader looking only at the table will see a dimensionally inconsistent fate for a dimension-4 operator. 
    *   **Required fix:** Add a brief note to the Table III caption explicitly stating that the "Fate" column describes the reduction of the bare invariant prior to multiplication by the prefactor, or update the O4/O5 entries to reflect the final $\kappa(J^5 \cdot J^5)$ coefficient of the full dimension-4 density.
