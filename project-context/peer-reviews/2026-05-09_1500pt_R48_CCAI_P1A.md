## Per-finding detail

### M1 (MAJOR) [Sec. R4, lines 680–683] R4 over/under-shoot direction reversed and second-OOM wrong

The text reads: *"For any $m_\theta$ in the natural ALP range
($m_a \in [10^{-22}, 10^{-15}]\,$eV), the produced $\rho_\theta$
either undershoots $\rho_\Lambda$ by 22 orders of magnitude (at
$m_\theta \sim 10^{-22}$ eV) or overshoots it by 14 orders of
magnitude (at $m_\theta \sim 10^{-15}$ eV)."*

From Eq.~\eqref{eq:beta_bound} held at $\beta = \beta_{\rm obs}$ and
$\alpha/M = 10^{-21}\,$GeV$^{-1}$, $\rho_\theta = m_\theta^2 \beta^2 /
[2(\alpha/M)^2]$, so $\rho_\theta \propto m_\theta^2$. With
$\rho_\theta \approx \rho_\Lambda$ at $m_\theta = H_0 \approx
1.5\times 10^{-33}\,$eV (which the same paragraph correctly derives,
line 669):

- At $m_\theta = 10^{-22}\,$eV (i.e.\ $\sim 10^{11}\times H_0$),
  $\rho_\theta = (10^{11})^2 \rho_\Lambda = 10^{22}\rho_\Lambda$,
  i.e. *overshoots* by ~22 orders, not undershoots.
- At $m_\theta = 10^{-15}\,$eV (i.e.\ $\sim 10^{18}\times H_0$),
  $\rho_\theta = (10^{18})^2 \rho_\Lambda = 10^{36}\rho_\Lambda$,
  i.e. *overshoots* by ~36 orders, not 14.

Both directions and the second magnitude are wrong. As written, the
paragraph is internally inconsistent with its own central equation
(line 666) and with its own $m_\theta = H_0$ matching just three lines
above. **Why MAJOR:** an external referee will plug numbers and notice
this in 30 seconds; once flagged, the closure narrative for R4
("$m_\theta\sim H_0$ is a tuning of $10^{-61}$") still holds, but
trust in the rest of the R4 amplitude bookkeeping is compromised.
**Concrete fix:** rewrite the bracket as *"$\rho_\theta$ overshoots
$\rho_\Lambda$ by ~22 orders at $m_\theta\sim 10^{-22}\,$eV and by ~36
orders at $m_\theta\sim 10^{-15}\,$eV; only the narrow
$m_\theta\sim H_0$ window matches both observables."* The qualitative
closure ("CC fine-tuning relabelled") is unchanged; only the directional
wording and the second OOM count need to flip.

### m1 (MINOR) [Sec. tab:bounce_disc footnote ‡, line 1061] Cross-paper table-number anchor points to wrong P1B table

The ‡ footnote in P1A Table II reads: *"...enumerated in Paper~I(b)
\S~VII Table~III, subsection \"Free-$w_0 w_a$ chain status\"..."* and
*"Paper~I(b) Table~III row \"DESI DR2 w0wa (new)\"."*

P1B has five tables in declaration order: tab:verification (I),
tab:modelcomp (II), tab:crosspaper (III), tab:mcmc_inventory (IV),
tab:claims (V). The 424,781-sample inventory and the
"DESI DR2 w0wa (new)" row both live in tab:mcmc_inventory (Table IV),
not Table III (which is the cross-paper readiness/status grid).
P1B §VII *is* correct (Cross-Paper Verification Status), but the
table number P1A points readers to is off by one. **Fix:** in P1A
line 1061 change both occurrences of "Table~III" to "Table~IV".

### m2 (MINOR) [Sec. R2, lines 590–593] OOM range "10^{-58} to 10^{-60}" inverts the natural reading

The dimensional ratio computed in lines 588–590 lands at
$10^{-3}\cdot 10^{-61}/(10^{-2}\cdot 6\times 10^{-3}) \approx
10^{-60}$ (the small end). The "$\sim 100$ ε-correction" that
GGGGG introduced as the upper bound pushes the suppression to
$10^{-58}$ (less suppressed, so worse for the bound). Stated as
"10^{-58} to 10^{-60}", the reader naturally takes the first
number as the central value and the second as a correction; the
opposite is true (the natural calc is the small end, the
ε-correction loosens it). **Fix:** swap the order to
"$\sim 10^{-60}$ to $\sim 10^{-58}$" or annotate which end is
the bare ratio and which is the ε-corrected ceiling. Closure
direction is preserved either way; this is presentational.

### m3 (MINOR) [Footnote 1, lines 774–779] SPHEREx σ=0.7 vs "5–5.5σ optimistic" arithmetic

Footnote: *"$\sigma(\fnl)\approx 0.7$ Fisher-ideal (optimistic
$5$–$5.5\sigma$ before GR and $b_\phi$ degradation)..."*. With
$|\fnl|=4.375$ and $\sigma = 0.7$, the detection significance is
$4.375/0.7 \approx 6.25\sigma$, not 5–5.5. The 5–5.5 figure
matches an earlier σ ≈ 0.8–0.875 regime that was tightened to
0.7 in a previous round; the footnote was not propagated.
**Fix:** change "$5$–$5.5\sigma$ optimistic" to either
"$\sim 6\sigma$ optimistic" or restore $\sigma\approx 0.8$
in the optimistic-Fisher row (whichever Paper II currently
uses as the canonical multi-tracer Fisher-ideal number).

### n3 (NIT) [Sec. tab:barriers caption + abstract line 67–68] B8/B14 framing slightly muddled in abstract

the corresponding observational consequence."* **Fix:** harmonize the
abstract wording to match the caption ("B8 is the observational
consequence of theorem B14"), drop "special case of".
