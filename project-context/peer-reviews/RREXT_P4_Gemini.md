Bounding the Pseudo-Label Inheritance WindowThe author notes that 66.5% of the initial training dataset relies on pseudo-labels derived from CE-ResNet predictions. Because the label-shuffle and per-pixel permutation nulls randomize the model's own outputs, they cannot intrinsically disentangle spatial variations inherited directly from the underlying CE-ResNet weights.While the author successfully utilizes a non-shuffle, 8-anchor systematic battery (Appendix D) to regress the field against external survey templates (imaging-leg fractions, pixel density), there remains a slight vulnerability: if CE-ResNet possessed subtle, survey-depth-correlated representation errors, those flaws could be frozen into the ViT encoder during fine-tuning.Action Required: The author notes that a fully independent model trained solely on the 6,637 human-verified GZ1 labels is deferred as a pending GPU retraining item. While a full pipeline rerun is outside the scope of minor revisions, the author should include a brief quantitative discussion or a small sub-sample validation test (e.g., examining if the ViT's internal feature representations layer-by-layer correlate with CE-ResNet logit boundaries) to solidify confidence that no high-order spatial bias leaked through the training initialization.Minor Revisions1.
