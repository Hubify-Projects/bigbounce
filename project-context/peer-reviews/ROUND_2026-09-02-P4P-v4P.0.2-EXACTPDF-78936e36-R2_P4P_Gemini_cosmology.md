This is a referee report for the manuscript "The Largest Test of a Preferred Galaxy-Spin Axis: An 8.47-Million-Galaxy DESI Chirality Catalog, a Void-Environment Contrast, and an Exclusion of the Rotating-Black-Hole-Universe Dipole" submitted to ApJS. 

The manuscript presents a large-scale catalog of galaxy chirality and performs a dipole null test to constrain the preferred-axis prediction of the Poplawski rotating-black-hole-universe model. While the statistical methodology and sample size are substantial, the manuscript contains several critical issues regarding abstract-to-body consistency, internal review-log prose left in the text, unarchived companion references, and minor mathematical discrepancies that must be resolved prior to publication.

Here are the specific findings:

### ESSENTIAL
**P4P-E1: Abstract-to-Body Drift on Exclusion Caveats (Pattern-045)**
*   **Location:** Abstract (Page 1) vs. Section 5.3 (Page 7)
*   **Problem:** The abstract claims the sensitivity floor is "a factor of 2–20× below the ∼2–33% amplitudes reported in the literature that motivates the model," presenting an unqualified exclusion. However, Section 5.3 explicitly notes a critical caveat: "though two of those amplitudes, on the two largest comparison samples, drop below the floor under the (not adopted for strengthening) illustrative bridge... we report it because it weakens the exclusion for exactly the two claims made on the largest comparison samples, and that should not go unstated." If this caveat "should not go unstated" in the body, it cannot be omitted from the abstract's headline exclusion claim.
*   **Required Fix:** Revise the abstract to explicitly include the caveat that under a physical-to-observed bridge, the exclusion is weakened or fails for the largest comparison samples.

**P4P-E2: Internal Review-Log Prose in Text**
*   **Location:** Section 4, Page 5
*   **Problem:** The text states: "The focal fit is an unpenalized Newton–Cholesky logistic MLE on a declared, post-review 13-column linear nuisance basis..." The phrase "post-review" is internal review-log/audit prose that violates the venue's formatting standards for final publication.
*   **Required Fix:** Remove "post-review" and any other internal version-history or audit-log language from the manuscript body.

**P4P-E3: Unarchived Companion Reference (Standalone-Reader Test)**
*   **Location:** Section 4 (Page 5) and References (Page 10, Ref [16])
*   **Problem:** Section 4 relies on an "archived companion study" for secondary diagnostics (T-Web, Tempel, ASTRA web classifications). However, Reference [16] is cited as a personal URL (`https://bigbounce.hubify.app/...`) with the explicit admission: "No independent DOI has been minted for this manuscript as of this writing." A published paper cannot rely on an un-DOI'd personal website for load-bearing or secondary diagnostic companion papers. 
*   **Required Fix:** The companion paper must be properly archived in a persistent repository (e.g., arXiv, Zenodo) with a valid DOI, or the diagnostics must be moved into an appendix of this manuscript.

**P4P-E4: Side-by-Side Sigmas Without Qualification**
*   **Location:** Section 3, Page 4
*   **Problem:** The text states: "moment $z = +6.923$ (add-one rank $p = 0.001996$ on 500 draws; a separate binomial-monopole null gives $+6.983$ at 500 draws and $+7.207$ at $10^4$ draws)." Values from different null procedures (the fixed-occupancy null vs. the binomial-monopole null) are juxtaposed without an explicit "not directly comparable" qualification at this specific location.
*   **Required Fix:** Add an explicit qualification that these $z$-scores are derived from different null procedures and are not directly comparable.

### MAJOR
**P4P-M1: Mathematical Inconsistency in Standard Error vs. Confidence Interval**
*   **Location:** Section 4, Page 5
*   **Problem:** The reported standard error is `SE = 0.00332`, and the 95% CI is `[-0.00504, +0.00795]`. The half-width of this CI is $(0.00795 - (-0.00504)) / 2 = 0.006495$. Assuming a standard normal approximation ($z=1.96$), the implied SE is $0.006495 / 1.96 = 0.003313$, which rounds to $0.00331$, not $0.00332$. (If a t-distribution was used for 50 clusters, $t_{49} \approx 2.01$, yielding an SE of $\approx 0.00323$). The stated SE and CI are mathematically inconsistent.
*   **Required Fix:** Recompute and correct either the SE or the CI bounds so they are exactly mathematically consistent, and state the assumed distribution (e.g., normal vs. t-distribution) used for the multiplier.

### MINOR

**P4P-N2: Imprecise Description of Injection-Recovery Bracketing**
*   **Location:** Section 3, Page 5 (referencing Figure 2)
*   **Problem:** The text states "crossing between the 0.96% and 0.98% rows brackets $A_{95}^{obs}$". However, the table in Figure 2 shows that at $A=0.98\%$, the detection fraction is exactly 0.950. Therefore, the 0.98% row does not "bracket" the 95% threshold with the 0.96% row; it lands on it exactly.
*   **Required Fix:** Adjust the text to accurately reflect that the 0.98% injected amplitude exactly meets the 95% detection fraction, rather than bracketing it.

## Summary recommendation
MAJOR REVISIONS

The manuscript presents a rigorous and statistically powerful test of an observable cosmological claim, and the dataset is appropriately scoped for ApJS. However, the omission of the physical-to-observed bridge caveat in the abstract fundamentally misrepresents the strength of the exclusion against the largest literature samples. Furthermore, the inclusion of internal review-log prose ("post-review") and the reliance on an unarchived personal URL for a companion paper violate basic publication standards. These issues, along with the minor mathematical inconsistencies, must be corrected before the paper can be accepted.
