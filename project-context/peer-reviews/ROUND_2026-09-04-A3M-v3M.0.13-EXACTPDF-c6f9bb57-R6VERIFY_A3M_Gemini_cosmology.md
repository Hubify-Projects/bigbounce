This is a referee report for Physical Review D. 

The manuscript presents a highly rigorous, multi-channel observational and theoretical audit of the matter-bounce scenario's $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local}$ prediction. The theoretical resolution of the factor-of-two discrepancy between the in-in formalism and the separate-universe ($\delta N$) approach—specifically, identifying the $\mathcal{O}(1/k_L)$ growth of the shift vector $N_i$ in the non-attractor phase that violates the standard $\delta N$ gradient expansion—is an excellent and physically sound contribution to the literature. The observational pipeline is similarly robust, correctly propagating the model's own spectrum rather than relying on external, mutually inconsistent spectral features. 

However, the manuscript contains critical failures in how the statistical results are summarized in the abstract, conflating entirely different metrics. Furthermore, the reproducibility statement relies on floating pointers and future promises rather than frozen releases, violating submission standards. 

Below are the specific findings that must be addressed.

### ESSENTIAL

**A3M-E1**
* Section: Abstract (Page 1)
* Specific problem: "...SPHEREx bispectrum-only reaches 0.7-0.9$\sigma$ (scheme S1) or 1.78$\sigma$ (scheme S2); combining power spectrum and bispectrum widens this to 0.5-1.1$\sigma$."
* Required fix: The abstract conflates two entirely different statistical metrics. The 0.7-0.9$\sigma$ value is the *bare detection significance* of a single candidate (Table VI). The 0.5-1.1$\sigma$ value is the *separation between the two candidates* ($|\Delta f_{\rm NL}^{\rm after}| / \sigma_{f_{\rm NL}}$, Sec VI.B, page 11). Juxtaposing these with the phrase "widens this to" is statistically incorrect and misrepresents the paper's own findings (furthermore, 0.5 is not "wider" than 0.7). If you intend to quote the P+B bare significance, Table VI lists it as 1.0-1.3$\sigma$. Rewrite the abstract to clearly distinguish bare detection significance from candidate separation.

**A3M-E2**
* Section: Abstract (Page 1)
* Specific problem: "SMBH-seed high-z abundance: three orders of magnitude short - a third honest null."
* Required fix: This sentence implies the model's predicted spectrum is 3 orders of magnitude short of the SMBH seed requirement. However, the body (Sec V.C, page 10) states that the *required amplitude itself* is FIRAS-excluded by 3 orders of magnitude, while the model's own spectrum falls "far short" (by $\sim 7$ orders of magnitude, identical to the PBH case). Align the abstract's summary with the body's actual two-part conclusion.

**A3M-E3**
* Section: Reproducibility Statement (Page 13)
* Specific problem: "...at the current HEAD, under `research/cubic_bounce_transmission/`..."
* Required fix: "current HEAD" is a floating pointer that will drift as the repository is updated. Provide a specific, frozen commit hash for this repository state.

**A3M-E4**
* Section: Reproducibility Statement (Page 14)
* Specific problem: "This work's own code and artifacts are pinned by the GitHub commit hash above rather than a frozen-release DOI of their own; minting one is a maintainer action planned for the packaging stage."
* Required fix: Promises of future archival actions are not acceptable at the submission stage. Mint the DOI or provide the permanent frozen-release hash now.

* Required fix: Remove this version-history language / internal review-log prose. The published paper must stand as a single, final document without referencing its own earlier draft states.

## PASS 2 — self-critique findings (what initial review missed)

Here are the additional findings from a fresh-eyes re-evaluation of the manuscript, focusing specifically on arithmetic consistency, internal cross-references, and text-to-math fidelity. 

### ESSENTIAL

**A3M-E6**
* Section: V.C (Page 10)
* Specific problem: "...$f_{\rm PBH}$ is exactly zero in double precision at every point (Gaussian-limit $\log_{10} \beta \approx -1.7 \times 10^9$ at $M_H = 10^{20}$ g)." AND "...the required-excursion threshold moves from the Gaussian $3162\sigma$ to $3364\sigma$..."
* Required fix: These two numbers are mathematically incompatible for the same null result. A $3162\sigma$ Gaussian excursion corresponds to a probability $\beta \sim \text{erfc}(3162/\sqrt{2}) \sim \exp(-3162^2/2)$, which gives $\log_{10} \beta \approx -2.17 \times 10^6$. This differs from the quoted $-1.7 \times 10^9$ by a factor of $\sim 800$ in the exponent. Recompute these values and correct whichever one is an artefact or typo, ensuring the quoted threshold and the quoted probability mathematically correspond to one another.

### MINOR / NIT

**A3M-N2**
* Section: VI.A (Page 11)
* Specific problem: "...using the upper side of the merger-response error (+9.0, since the prediction lies below the central value)..."
* Required fix: The prediction is $-35/16 = -2.1875$. The central value is $-3.6$. Because $-2.1875 > -3.6$, the prediction mathematically lies *above* the central value, not below it. The numerical calculation correctly uses the upper error bound (+9.0) to compute the $0.16\sigma$ distance, so the math is flawless, but the prose justification is factually backwards. Change "below" to "above".

**A3M-N3**
* Section: IV.D (Page 7)
* Specific problem: "Every committed background at or above §V B's $T_B \gtrsim 10^8$ GeV puts the entire NANOGrav band..."
* Required fix: Broken cross-reference. Section V.B ("The compaction-function result") does not contain or derive the bounce temperature condition. That condition ($a_B H_B \gtrsim 10^{17}-10^{18} \text{ Mpc}^{-1}$, i.e., $T_B \gtrsim 10^8-10^{10}$ GeV) is actually derived and stated in Section V.C. Update the reference to §V C.
