This is a referee report for Physical Review D, evaluating the manuscript "Multi-channel tests of the matter-bounce prediction $f_{NL}^{local} = -35/16$ and a joint $(r, f_{NL}^{local})$ no-go for single-field matter bounces at constant sound speed" under the PRD-STANDARD profile.

### ESSENTIAL

**A3M-E1**
* Section: Abstract (page 1)
* Specific problem: "pulsar timing ($\gamma_{pred} = 5.07$ vs. NANOGrav’s $3.2 \pm 0.6$, a $5.1\sigma$ null)"
* Required fix: To a standalone reader, $(5.07 - 3.2) / 0.6 = 3.1\sigma$, making the math appear blatantly incorrect. Section IV.B correctly explains that $0.6$ is the 90% (5-95%) interval width, making $1\sigma \approx 0.365$, which yields the $5.1\sigma$ result. The abstract must explicitly state that $0.6$ is a 90% confidence interval half-width to prevent immediate reader confusion.
