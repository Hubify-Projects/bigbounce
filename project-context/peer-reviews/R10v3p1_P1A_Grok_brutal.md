**Referee Report**

**Paper:** Channel-Level Closure of Four Minimal Einstein–Cartan–Holst Dark-Energy Routes and Perturbation Transparency for Scalar Matter  
**Journal:** Physical Review D  
**Recommendation:** REJECT

### ESSENTIAL findings (any one is sufficient for rejection)

**P1A-E1** (Abstract + Sec. I, p. 3)  
The abstract states “the surviving phenomenological predictors—matter-bounce \(f_{NL}\) and spectator-ALP birefringence—are mechanism-independent.” The body (Sec. XIII, p. 16 and Sec. IV D, p. 10) explicitly classifies the matter-bounce \(f_{NL}=-35/8\) as a property of the scalar-only \(w=0\) contraction class under Assumption (f) of a companion paper, not of minimal ECH. The two statements are contradictory. Required fix: remove all claims of mechanism-independence for \(f_{NL}\) or provide a first-principles derivation inside the ECH action that does not invoke external bounce classes.

**P1A-E2** (Sec. I, p. 3; Sec. IV Scope paragraph; multiple locations)  
The paper repeatedly states that results are taken from “Paper I(b)”, “Paper II”, “Paper III”, “Paper IV” that are “in preparation” or “companion work in preparation.” A PRD submission must be self-contained. The central numerical claims (\(f_{NL}\), \(\beta=0.27^\circ\), galaxy-spin null, MCMC posteriors) cannot be audited from the present manuscript. Required fix: all load-bearing results must be derived or reproduced inside the submitted paper.

**P1A-E3** (Sec. II C, p. 6; Appendix B, p. 19)  
The mapping \(\rho_\Lambda=\Xi M_{Pl}^4\) with \(\Xi=(\alpha/M)M_{Pl}\mathcal{D}_{inf}\) is introduced as a “phenomenological on-shell scaling ansatz, not a derivation.” The subsequent claim that the four routes are “closed at the channel-amplitude level” rests on this ansatz. A no-go theorem cannot be erected on an admitted ansatz whose only justification is dimensional matching at the bounce. Required fix: either promote the ansatz to a controlled EFT result or retract the closure language.

**P1A-E4** (Sec. X, p. 14)  
The “perturbation-transparency theorem” asserts that the Holst term decouples from all scalar/tensor perturbations at every order because it reduces to a total derivative on a torsion-free connection. This is a textbook feature of Einstein–Cartan theory with algebraic torsion (see Hehl et al. 1976 and standard reviews). The paper presents it as a novel structural result that “closes” dark-energy routes. The claim of novelty is false. Required fix: remove all language implying this is a new theorem.

### MAJOR findings

**P1A-M1** (Sec. IV, entire section)  
The four-route “no-go” is performed at the “amplitude-budget granularity,” not at the operator level. The paper explicitly states (p. 3) that the Jackiw–Pi term and the parity-odd four-fermion partner of Route 1 are omitted from the enumeration. A channel-level closure that excludes the leading parity-odd operators is not a closure.

**P1A-M2** (Sec. XIII, p. 16)  
The LiteBIRD forecast \(\sigma(\beta)\approx0.03^\circ\) is used to claim a \(9\sigma\) distinction between the ECH-derived \(\beta\approx0.27^\circ\) and zero. The value \(\beta=0.27^\circ\) itself is taken from a spectator-ALP fit, not generated by the ECH action. The forecast therefore tests an external ALP model, not a prediction of minimal ECH.

**P1A-M3** (Table I, p. 4; Sec. XIV D, p. 17)  
The structural-tension argument between \(N_{tot}\approx92\) and the matter-bounce \(f_{NL}\) is presented as a robustness check. Because \(N_{tot}\) is itself obtained from the same phenomenological scaling ansatz (Appendix B), the tension is internal to the ansatz and does not constitute an independent falsification test.

**P1A-M4** (Sec. V–VI, p. 11)  
Galaxy-spin results are taken from “Paper IV” (in preparation). The present manuscript contains no catalog construction, bias audit, or dipole measurement. The null result cannot be used as supporting evidence.

### MINOR findings

**P1A-m1** (Eq. 6, p. 6)  
The operator \(\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}e^I_\mu e^J_\nu\mathcal{F}_{IJ\rho\sigma}\) is assigned off-shell mass dimension +1. The subsequent on-shell promotion to dimension +4 is performed by hand via the ansatz in Appendix B. This step should be labeled more clearly as an external assumption.

**P1A-m2** (Sec. II A 2, p. 6)  
The one-loop coefficient \(\alpha/M\sim10^{-2}\) is motivated by an order-of-magnitude estimate whose scheme dependence is acknowledged but not quantified. A single central value is used throughout.

**P1A-m3** (Fig. 1, p. 4)  
The figure mixes ECH routes (red box) with mechanisms explicitly stated to lie outside ECH (quintom, Cuscuton, ekpyrotic). The visual suggests a unified landscape that the text does not support.

### NITs

- Inconsistent use of “minimal ECH” vs. “standard ECH” without a one-sentence definition on first use.
- Several sentences begin with “We emphasize that…” or “We note that…” in consecutive paragraphs (stylistic).
- Reference list contains multiple “arXiv:… (in preparation)” entries that are not identifiable.

### Summary recommendation

**REJECT**

The manuscript is not self-contained, rests its central “closure” claim on an admitted phenomenological ansatz rather than a controlled calculation, mischaracterizes a textbook feature of algebraic torsion as a new theorem, and delegates all quantitative support to unpublished companion papers. These deficiencies are structural and cannot be repaired by minor revision. The paper does not meet the standard required for publication in Physical Review D.
