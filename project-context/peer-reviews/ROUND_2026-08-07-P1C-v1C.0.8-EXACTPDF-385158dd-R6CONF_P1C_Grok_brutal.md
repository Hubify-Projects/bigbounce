### ESSENTIAL findings (paper cannot be accepted without these fixes)

**P1C-E1**  
Page 1, header and abstract.  
The document carries the internal version string “(Dated: August 6, 2026 (v1C.0.8))”. This is an artifact of an internal review pipeline and has no place in a submitted manuscript.  
*Required fix:* Remove every version-control tag, round identifier, and future calendar date from the title page and metadata.

**P1C-E2**  
Abstract (entire) + Sec. I (p. 1–2) + Sec. IV (p. 5–8).  
The abstract states quantitative closure margins (“~67-order endpoint”, “61–67 orders”, “suppression margin ≥58”) that are obtained only after importing the torsion-elimination operator, the zero-spin-branch theorem, and the explicit one-loop coefficients from the companion paper [1]. The present manuscript supplies neither the derivation nor the numerical pipeline. A standalone reader cannot recompute any of the quoted orders of magnitude.  
*Required fix:* Either (a) reproduce the necessary derivations in full or (b) remove every numerical claim that depends on [1].

**P1C-E3**  
Abstract + Sec. V (p. 9–11) + Table II.  
The abstract presents the work as a “structural no-go survey” that “closes” four routes. The body repeatedly qualifies the same statements as “Tier-III ansatz-level”, “heuristic closure”, “structural/naturalness argument”, or “not proved by exhaustive symbolic enumeration”. The abstract therefore materially overstates what has been demonstrated.  
*Required fix:* Rewrite the abstract so that every quantitative or “closure” claim is no stronger than the weakest evidentiary tier actually established in the body.

**P1C-E4**  
Sec. II (p. 3) and every subsequent section that cites the companion.  
The entire Route-1 channel and the algebraic Bianchi identity that eliminates torsion are imported by citation. The manuscript is therefore not self-contained. CQG’s standing policy (and standard practice for no-go claims) requires that the central technical step be reproducible from the submitted text alone.  
*Required fix:* Make the paper independent of [1] or withdraw the claim that a complete no-go has been shown inside minimal ECH.

### MAJOR findings (significant revision required)

**P1C-M1**  
Page count (18 pp) versus actual contribution.  
The manuscript is essentially a 14-row classification table plus supporting amplitude-budget arithmetic that is performed at the level of scaling ansätze. At the acceptance bar of CQG this length is disproportionate; a concise Letter or a focused no-go note would be the appropriate format.  
*Required fix:* Condense to ≤8–10 pages or justify the length with new first-principles derivations.

**P1C-M2**  
Sec. III, Table I, Fig. 1.  
Fourteen “barriers” are listed, yet B8 is explicitly subsumed by B14 and several others (B5–B7, B10, B13) are general naturalness or classification arguments already present in the pre-2015 literature on torsion cosmology. The claim of “13 distinct mechanism-class constraints” therefore inflates the novelty count.  
*Required fix:* Provide a transparent accounting of which barriers are new versus re-packaged, with explicit literature pointers for the latter.

**P1C-M3**  
Sec. IV A–B (Route-2/3 budgets).  
The quoted suppression factors (≥58, ~60–67 orders) rest on (i) an O(1) normalization whose absolute coefficient is never computed and (ii) an imported one-loop result whose renormalization-group flow is not re-derived. The numerical margins are therefore not independently verifiable.  
*Required fix:* Either recompute the loop integrals inside the present framework or label every margin as “imported from [1], not re-derived here”.

**P1C-M4**  
Appendix D (perturbation-transparency theorem).  
The sole Tier-I result is proved in the companion paper; the present text only cites it. A no-go paper whose single rigorous leg lives in another manuscript cannot be evaluated on its own merits.  
*Required fix:* Reproduce the proof or downgrade the claim.
