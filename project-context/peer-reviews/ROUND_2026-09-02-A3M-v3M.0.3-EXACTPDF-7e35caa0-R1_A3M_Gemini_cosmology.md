This is a referee report for "Multi-channel consistency of the matter-bounce prediction at $f_{NL}^{\rm local} = -35/16$: the exact amplitude, pulsar-timing slope, primordial black holes, and survey reach" submitted to Physical Review D.

The manuscript presents a rigorous, multi-channel consistency check of the matter-bounce scenario, anchored by a from-scratch in-in computation that adjudicates a factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature regarding the local non-Gaussianity amplitude. The theoretical physics is sound, the application of the compaction-function formalism to the PBH channel is a necessary and well-executed correction to the naive Press-Schechter quadratic map, and the observational constraints are handled carefully. 

However, the manuscript contains severe formatting and editorial issues. It appears to have been exported directly from an internal laboratory issue tracker without a final sanitization pass, leaving numerous internal audit tags, version-history references, and markdown file pointers in the body text. Furthermore, it violates strict reporting standards regarding the juxtaposition of statistical significances. These issues must be resolved before the paper can be accepted.
