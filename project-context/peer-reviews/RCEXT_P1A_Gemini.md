Recommendation: MAJOR REVISIONS

1. Comments to the Author

This manuscript presents a comprehensive, channel-level no-go assessment of four minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-torsion pathways as potential explanations for late-time dark energy. It additionally derives a notable "perturbation-transparency" result showing that for canonical scalar matter, the Holst sector cleanly decouples from linear and higher-order scalar/tensor perturbations. The paper is exceptionally well-structured, rigorous in its tracking of dimension and scaling arguments, and provides a clear mechanism-class constraint catalog. However, there are significant tensions regarding the overarching framing of the "surviving signatures" vs. their mutual exclusivity with the dark energy framework that must be resolved before publication.

2. Blockers

None. The manuscript is mathematically self-contained, and the simultaneous reliance on un-posted companion papers (Papers I(b), II, III, IV) is handled properly via explicit data summaries (e.g., Table II) that allow complete verification of the primary analytical claims.

3. Majors

Framing and Mutual Exclusivity of Surviving Signatures: There is an interpretive tension between how the matter-bounce non-Gaussianity (fNL = −35/8) is highlighted as a "surviving testable prediction" in the executive summary (Table I) and the core analytical finding of Section XIV.D. The author explicitly demonstrates that the Ntot ≈ 92 e-folds required to dilute the bounce-era contorsion to match the observed dark energy scale will inevitably push the matter-bounce contraction modes deep into the subhorizon regime, definitively erasing the signature at SPHEREx scales. Presenting fNL = −35/8 prominently as a testable prediction of the broader paradigm without explicitly pairing it with a "completely erased if ECH dark energy is realized" warning in the primary tables headlines a favorable observational channel while minimizing the stark structural incompatibility. The text should be revised to emphasize that the dark-energy route and the observational matter-bounce program are fundamentally mutually exclusive.

4. Minors

Figure 3 utilizes an illustrative baseline Hubble constant of H0 = 69.2 km/s/Mpc for the spin-torsion model, whereas the joint MCMC posterior baseline adopted throughout the rest of the paper is H0 = 67.68±1.06 km/s/Mpc. While the author helpfully notes in the caption that this is a deliberately high illustrative value to show the 2–3% visual offset, it introduces minor visual confusion. Generating a secondary panel or a matched-H0 curve inside Figure 3 would better isolate the true dynamical signature of the ΞMPl^2 term from simple baseline offsets.

5. Strengths

Definitive Decoupling Proof: The analytical proof of the perturbation-transparency theorem for canonical scalar matter is exceptionally clean. Showing that the Holst dual contraction vanishes pointwise via the first algebraic Bianchi identity on any torsion-free connection (independent of metric compatibility) provides an elegant closure to the perturbation equations.

Granular Accounting of EFT Dimensions: The manuscript does not hide the dimensional mismatch of the phenomenological parity-odd operator (off-shell mass dimension +1). The explicit tracking of on-shell vs. off-shell dimensional scaling in Appendix B establishes an admirable standard of theoretical transparency.

Systematic Classification of Barriers: Isolating the 13 distinct mechanism-class failure modes (such as the Attractor-Sensitivity Dilemma and the Reheating Thermal-Reset Barrier) effectively maps out the structural boundaries of minimal ECH gravity, ensuring the paper is valuable as a comprehensive reference guide for future modified-gravity model building.
