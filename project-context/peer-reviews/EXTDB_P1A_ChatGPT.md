Referee Report (MNRAS / PRD / JCAP)

1. Recommendation
MINOR REVISIONS

This manuscript is substantially stronger than typical speculative Einstein–Cartan/Holst cosmology papers because it is primarily a negative result rather than an affirmative claim. It repeatedly narrows its scope, explicitly labels phenomenological ansätze, distinguishes channel-level from operator-level closure, and carefully separates what is proven from what is assumed. The central perturbation-transparency theorem is also considerably cleaner than the broader dark-energy discussion.

After accounting for the explicit caveats introduced throughout the manuscript, I do not find a scientific blocker that would require major new calculations before publication.

However, I do find several remaining issues where the presentation still overstates certainty relative to what is actually established. Those should be corrected before publication.

2. BLOCKERS
None.

I did not identify any issue that, in my judgment, invalidates the principal conclusions or would require substantial new scientific work.

3. MAJORS

Major 1 — The "13 independent barriers" language is still stronger than the actual logical structure
The paper repeatedly states that it establishes 13 logically independent barriers while also acknowledging that:
- Barrier 8 is subsumed by Barrier 14,
- several barriers are explicitly heuristic,
- several are conditional,
- several rely on the same dimensional/scaling ansätze,
- several are philosophical or classification statements rather than independent derivations.

The manuscript itself explains these nuances, but the repeated headline phrasing still gives the impression of 13 mathematically independent no-go theorems.

I recommend softening wording such as "13 logically-independent barriers" to something closer to "13 mechanism-class constraints, several of which rely on shared assumptions but probe distinct physical failure modes."

Major 2 — Route 2 and Route 3 should be described consistently as conservative EFT bounds
The manuscript already acknowledges that Eq. (14) and Eq. (16) are not literal derivations from the cited literature. That is good. However, later sections occasionally speak of Route 2 and Route 3 as though they have been "closed" in the same sense as Route 1. Route 1 is a standard derivation; Routes 2–3 are conservative EFT scaling arguments. The language should remain consistent throughout.

Major 3 — The paper should distinguish more clearly between "minimal ECH cannot explain dark energy" and "minimal ECH channels examined here cannot explain dark energy."
The manuscript has already moved strongly in this direction. Nevertheless, several places in the Conclusions still read more broadly than warranted. Since omitted operator classes are explicitly acknowledged (Jackiw–Pi, parity-odd four-fermion partner, etc.), the concluding wording should remain aligned with that scope.

4. MINORS

Minor 1: The abstract is extremely long. Shortening by ~20–30% would substantially improve readability.
Minor 2: Several figure captions remain unusually long (especially Figs. 3–7). Some caption material belongs in the main text.
Minor 3: The paper repeatedly repeats essentially identical caveats (channel-level, not operator-level, ansatz, phenomenological, conditional, omitted operators). Some repetition could be removed without weakening transparency.
Minor 4: Section IV contains numerous parenthetical discussions that interrupt the argument. Several could become footnotes.
Minor 5: The manuscript occasionally alternates between "closure," "no-go," and "structural incompatibility" for essentially the same concept. Consistent terminology would improve readability.
Minor 6: Figures 5 and 6 are conceptually useful but visually dense. Simplifying them would increase accessibility.
Minor 7: The Conclusions repeat much of Sections IX–XIV nearly verbatim. A more concise conclusion would likely be stronger.

5. Strengths

1. Exceptional transparency — repeatedly distinguishes derivations, phenomenological ansätze, heuristic arguments, observational constraints, and companion-paper material. Substantially above average for speculative theoretical cosmology.
2. Appropriate treatment of negative results — rather than forcing a positive mechanism, the paper systematically demonstrates why several attractive mechanisms fail.
3. Careful scoping — explicitly limits claims to minimal ECH, channel-level closure, stated assumptions, and repeatedly acknowledges omitted operator classes.
4. Perturbation-transparency result — the perturbation-transparency theorem is the strongest scientific contribution. Assumptions clearly stated, proof structure straightforward, and the distinction between the Holst dual contraction and the Pontryagin density is explained carefully.
5. Internal consistency — many previous inconsistencies have been resolved: operator-basis vs channel-level scope, dimensional-status discussion, R4 naturalness framing, B8/B14 dependence, companion-paper dependence, explicit acknowledgment of ansätze.

Overall: An unusual paper that is primarily a critical assessment of a theoretical framework. Technically careful, unusually transparent about assumptions, and generally avoids overstating what has been demonstrated. The remaining issues are largely about calibrating claim strength to evidence strength, not missing derivations.

Final recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS.
