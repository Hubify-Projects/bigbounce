## Finding PAPER-DEE-B2 (BLOCKER)
**Section:** Abstract and §6.3, Bayes‑factor grid ($\mathrm{BF}\sim 6,8,17$); also the four‑corner table in §6.3  

**Issue:**  
The Bayes‑factor numbers for non‑point bounce priors cannot be reproduced from the paper’s displayed equations. The only explicit formula (the ratio of a point‑prediction likelihood to an integral over a uniform prior) applies solely to the delta‑prior rows. When a $\sigma_{\rm theory}=1.0$ Gaussian bounce prior and a narrow $[-5,+5]$ competitor prior are used, a straightforward analytic integration yields $\mathrm{BF}\approx 2.6$, not the claimed $\sim 6$. The provenance of the $6$–$17$ envelope therefore collapses: the paper provides no closed‑form expression for the Gaussian‑bounce‑prior integrals and no committed JSON/script output that stores the grid values.

**Fix:**  
Either publish the full analytic formulae that produce the grid, or commit a static output file (e.g. `bayes_factor_grid.json`) generated by the existing Monte‑Carlo scripts. Include a brief verification that the numbers match the formulae.

**Fix:**  
Remove the $9.9\sigma$ figure from the body of the paper until the companion artifact is released and its outputs (e.g., a JSON containing the Fisher matrix or the marginalized uncertainties) are deposited in the repository. Until then, limit the discussion to a qualitative statement.
