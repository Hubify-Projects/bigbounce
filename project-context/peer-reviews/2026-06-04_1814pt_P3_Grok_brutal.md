**Referee Report: HUBIFY-2026-003**

**Paper length**: 50 pages. PRD methods/catalog papers are typically 15–30 pages. The manuscript is inflated by repetitive explanations of the Path-C protocol, exhaustive before/after tables, retracted linear approximations, and multi-paragraph caveats that belong in supplementary material. **ESSENTIAL**: Condense to a maximum of 25 pages. Move all injection-recovery curves, full Fisher tables, UMAP stability grids, and per-survey score histograms to appendices or a data-release note.

**P3-E1 (Abstract, p. 1; §I, p. 3)**  
Problem: The abstract states “the largest-scale application of autoencoder anomaly detection across seven astronomical archives to date” and presents 378,280 anomalies as the headline result. The body immediately qualifies that (a) 200 objects are CMB patches, not sources, (b) ∼113 k LAMOST objects are exploratory only because of 98 % blue-excess contamination, (c) the like-for-like single-survey comparison is only 73× (not 141×), and (d) the genuine novelty fraction is a single-sample point estimate of 17.8 % at the top-1 000 stratum only.  
Required fix: Rewrite the abstract to state exactly what the body proves: a 378 280-object catalog after native retraining and deduplication, with the 265 k catalog-grade subset as the primary deliverable and all headline σ(fNL) values consistent with null at <1σ.

**P3-E2 (Throughout, e.g. p. 1, 2, 12, 23, 27, 29)**  
Problem: The manuscript contains extensive version-history language, internal audit tags, and review-log artifacts (“R15 GEM-B1 closure”, “v3.1.73”, “CLOSED v3.1.70”, “RETRACTED per the same caveat”, “queued as a methods-paper companion task”, “companion artifact … 2026-05-29.json”). These are not journal-clean.  
Required fix: Remove every such tag. The final manuscript must read as a finished paper, not an internal review log.

**P3-E3 (§V, pp. 21–23; §VI D caveat (i))**  
Problem: Multiple σ(fNL) values derived from different null procedures (linear extrapolation, Fisher-positivity-respecting α² form, local-linear secant slope) are presented without consistent qualification. The linear form produces an unphysical +1σ tail above the single-tracer floor; the α² form does not. The text switches between them without a single, uniform reporting convention.  
Required fix: Adopt one and only one mapping (the positivity-respecting form) for all quoted central values and intervals. State explicitly that the linear-extrapolation numbers are retracted.

**P3-E4 (§II B, pp. 5–6; §VI D (i))**  
Problem: The 5-fold Jaccard stability calculation scores the full 47 000-spectrum pool with each fold’s checkpoint, yet earlier text described it as scoring only the held-out 9 400-spectrum split. The arithmetic is consistent only under the full-pool convention.  
Required fix: Remove all language claiming “each spectrum scored by a model that never saw it” and state the actual procedure unambiguously.

**P3-M1 (Abstract & §I)**

**P3-M2 (§III D, §VI A)**
