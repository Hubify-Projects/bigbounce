This is a referee report for Physical Review D. The manuscript presents a comprehensive, multi-channel observational test of the matter-bounce scenario, anchored by a rigorous from-scratch in-in computation that resolves a long-standing factor-of-two discrepancy in the literature's $f_{NL}^{\rm local}$ prediction. The theoretical physics is exceptionally well-executed, carefully distinguishing between gauge frames (Appendix A) and tracking exact mode functions through the bounce. The observational projections (PTA, PBH, LSS) are handled with appropriate care for effect sizes, priors, and regime-of-validity bounds. 

However, the manuscript currently contains several artifacts of its own drafting process, including explicit version-history language referencing earlier drafts, raw internal repository paths leaked into the main text, and a placeholder for a yet-to-be-minted reproducibility DOI. These structural and formatting issues must be resolved to meet PRD's publication standards. 

Below is the detailed list of findings.

### Findings

**1. A3M-E1**
*   **Classification:** ESSENTIAL
*   **Section + Page:** Sec. III.A (Page 6, left column, line 36) and Sec. IX.B (Page 15, right column, line 43)
*   **Specific problem:** The manuscript contains explicit version-history language and review-log prose referencing its own previous drafts. 
    *   Page 6: "...and the paper's own previously stated open item – whether the transmitted amplitude is scheme-independent – is answered negatively..."
    *   Page 15: "...scheme-independence, previously an open question, is now answered negatively rather than left unresolved: the effective-fluid scheme S2... is finite – the earlier "S2 diverges" statement traced to a total-derivative artefact..."
*   **Required fix:** Remove all narrative references to the manuscript's internal revision history. Present the final scientific conclusions directly (e.g., simply state that the effective-fluid scheme S2 is finite and yields a specific value, without referencing that an earlier draft claimed it diverged).
