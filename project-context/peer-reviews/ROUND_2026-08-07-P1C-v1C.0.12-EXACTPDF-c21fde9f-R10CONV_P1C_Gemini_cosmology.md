### General Comments
The manuscript presents a highly rigorous, systematic no-go survey of minimal Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) gravity as a source for late-time dark energy and bounce phenomenology. The author catalogs 13 distinct mechanism-class constraints and provides detailed amplitude budgets for the historical one-loop graviton and Immirzi-running routes. However, the manuscript contains a few policy and formatting violations—specifically regarding version-history language, missing archival DOIs, and reliance on unpublished companions for one of the four main routes—that must be addressed before publication.

### Findings

**1. ESSENTIAL: Version-History Language in Main Text**
*   **Location:** Section V, Page 12
*   **Specific Problem:** The text reads: "...leaving it schematic (as earlier versions of this list did) misstates them by a factor of two."
*   **Required Fix:** Remove the version-history language ("as earlier versions of this list did"). The published manuscript must reflect the final scientific record without referencing the internal drafting or revision history of the operator list.

**2. MAJOR: Missing Frozen-Release DOI for Current Scripts**
*   **Location:** Data and Code Availability, Page 15
*   **Specific Problem:** The author provides a GitHub commit hash (`1130b7c5e3d2`) for the verification scripts specific to this paper and states: "An updated archival deposit containing this survey's own verification scripts is planned prior to publication, so that every computational artifact cited here is backed by a frozen-release DOI."
*   **Required Fix:** The promise of a future DOI is insufficient for the final accepted manuscript. The author must execute this plan, mint the Zenodo (or equivalent) DOI for the specific scripts used in this paper (the third through sixth files listed), and replace the GitHub commit reference with the immutable frozen-release DOI.

**3. MINOR: Standalone-Reader Test Violation for Route 4**
*   **Location:** Section IV.C, Page 10
*   **Specific Problem:** The text states: "Its full derivation, and the companion consistency check against a spectator-ALP birefringence benchmark, are outside the present survey's scope [1, 14]." 
*   **Required Fix:** While the author successfully provides self-contained proofs for the load-bearing elements of Routes 1-3 (via Appendices D and E), the "full derivation" of Route 4 is deferred entirely to unpublished companion papers. The author must either provide a brief, self-contained summary of the R4 derivation equations in an appendix (similar to the treatment of the R1 contact coefficient in App E) or explicitly re-scope the paper's claims to acknowledge that the R4 closure relies on external, unreviewed derivations.

**4. NIT: Simulated/Placeholder arXiv ID in Bibliography**
*   **Location:** References, Page 22

*   **Required Fix:** Ensure all bibliographic identifiers point to actual, resolvable literature at the time of final publication. If the paper is not yet on the arXiv, cite it as "in preparation" or "submitted" without a fabricated arXiv ID.
