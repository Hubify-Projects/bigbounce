Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS

BLOCKERS
None. The paper is publishable after targeted clarification/cleanup.

MAJORS
1. Sensitivity recast vs. independent forecast must be kept absolutely unambiguous. The paper correctly says the SPHEREx headline rests on imported Heinrich et al. σ(fNL)≈0.7 and is not a new joint Fisher forecast, but the abstract and conclusion still read close to an independent forecast claim. Add one explicit sentence to abstract/conclusion: "No new SPHEREx survey covariance or full bispectrum Fisher matrix is constructed here."

2. Cubic-order bounce transfer remains the weakest physical assumption. The paper flags this, but because the entire fNL=-35/8 observable depends on faithful third-order transmission while only linear transfer is verified, the limitation should be elevated in the abstract/conclusion, not mainly buried in assumptions/systematics.

3. Systematic budget is transparently heuristic but visually over-precise. The 2.6–5σ envelope is acceptable, but Fig. 2/Table IV imply a quantitative combined covariance that the paper explicitly does not compute. Label the lower endpoint as "heuristic additive-quadrature floor," not simply "realistic."

4. Bayes-factor presentation remains too elaborate for the evidential weight. The paper is honest about prior sensitivity, but the abstract BF≈9–14 risks overstatement. Prefer "illustrative BF of order several–ten under stated priors" in the abstract, with table values retained.

MINORS
1. The abstract is too long and contains too many caveats, code-artifact names, and bookkeeping details. Move most of this to a "scope and bookkeeping" paragraph.
2. Some claims about SPHEREx release timing and PNG-suitable data products are forward-looking; keep them as expectations, not firm milestones.
3. "≫1 against SSFSR" is true under point-hypothesis treatment but should be softened because point nulls inflate Bayes factors.
4. The anomaly-tracer 10–20% improvement is interesting but peripheral and insufficiently developed; move to discussion or appendix.
5. Current-data recasts should distinguish Planck CMB-template r=0.876 from LSS r≈0.84 every time they are compared.

Strengths
1. The paper is unusually transparent about assumptions, imported forecasts, and non-independent recast status.
2. The template-mismatch analysis is valuable and materially improves over a naive |fNL|/σ headline.
3. The Cai/Li factor-of-two audit is careful and useful, especially the explicit in-in commutator treatment.
4. The systematic-budget table is clear and helps readers track what drives the 2.6–5σ range.
5. The paper identifies a sharp, falsifiable target and explains how a null result would constrain the quasi-dust matter-bounce benchmark.
