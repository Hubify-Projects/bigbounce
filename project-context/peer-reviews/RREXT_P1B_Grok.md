MAJORS (moderate severity, non-blocking)
Exploratory w0wa appendix (Appendix A / Table IV) — presentational focus
The overlap-uncorrected DES-Y5 × Pantheon+ product-likelihood chain is appropriately caveated (∼20% shared events, no joint covariance, artificially narrowed posteriors, ΛCDM point unsampled, no σ-distance or model-selection claim possible). However, because it cannot support any quantitative statement and the required overlap-controlled control chains + nested-sampling analysis are explicitly deferred, its inclusion slightly dilutes the manuscript’s sharp focus on the three core verification tasks.
Suggested action: Either omit the w0wa chain and Table IV entirely (noting in Sec. V that a proper overlap-controlled + nested-sampling follow-up is planned) or move the full table + caveats to a very short supplementary note. This is the only substantive structural suggestion; it does not affect scientific validity.
NaMaster bias interpretation for downstream users (Sec. IV)
The paper correctly and repeatedly states that the worst-case |∆β̂| ≈ 0.040° pipeline-recovery bias is a “deconvolution-algebra bias on foreground-free skies; not a real-sky bias bound” and that the validation cannot address the β–α degeneracy (synthetic skies lack galactic foregrounds). This scoping is excellent. A single forward-looking sentence in Sec. IV or the conclusions on what would be required to promote the bias characterization to a usable real-sky systematic (end-to-end simulations with realistic foregrounds + multi-frequency component separation, or explicit marginalization over α) would help readers planning follow-up analyses. Minor in impact but useful for a methods-validation section.
