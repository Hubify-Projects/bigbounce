# INTENT.md — mission, structure, standard (one page)

## Mission
Was the Big Bang the beginning? The lab's guiding bet is that a nonsingular
bounce is a more defensible origin than inflation-from-a-singularity, made
testable with data that exists now. Houston's motivation — a conviction the
universe is beginningless and endless — is recorded openly, not hidden: **the
evidence decides; the motivation only chooses what to test.** Every claim is
stated at exactly its evidential strength; a null or negative result is
published as a null. (`project-context/VISION.md`)

## Publication standard
Continuous, never for its own sake — each work goes out when it is a genuine
contribution in its correct framing. Near-term milestone: the first one to
three genuinely valuable papers out soon (fastest candidates: P4′, the ECH
Note, P2′ once ledger #1 passes). Q1: pure-contribution framing — never
narrate our own past mistakes as a paper's thesis. Q2: reproducibility
manifests are mandatory, program-level and per-run. Every claim at exactly
its evidential strength; nulls published as nulls. (`CLAUDE.md` directives
Q1–Q4; `VISION.md` "Publication policy")

## Integrity rules
Never fake an accept. Never fabricate a derivation or claim. Every review leg
saves its raw evidence before any verdict is recorded. Truth-audit every
finding before closure. Convergence is a gate, not a product: at most two
consecutive review rounds on a paper without an intervening science or scope
decision (R2); rounds stop when remaining findings are genre/length/venue.
Every lineup change (split/merge/retire/rescope) is recorded in
`PAPER_LINEAGE` with the original claim beside the new claim (R3). A drift
audit re-runs at every lineup change and at least every 30 days (R4).
(`CLAUDE.md` directive R)

## Houston-only acts
Minting a Zenodo DOI, clicking submit on an arXiv/journal submission, and
sending an endorsement email are Houston's acts, not an agent's. Agents
prepare the kit (tarball, abstract, categories, ORCID, endorsement draft) and
stop at the click.
