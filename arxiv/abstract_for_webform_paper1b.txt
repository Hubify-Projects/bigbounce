We present three self-contained computational studies with limited interpretation. None implements a torsion-modified Boltzmann solver, measures torsion, or provides evidence for Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity or a bounce cosmology. (1) Stock CAMB and Cobaya sample a generic LCDM+dNeff extension. Across 309,189 frozen samples in two converged combinations, dNeff is consistent with zero (-0.020 +/- 0.169 and +0.058 +/- 0.179), and the extension does not remove the residual Hubble tension. The parametric minimal-contact scale is reported only as rho_tor/rho_rad ~ (T/M_Pl)^2, not as Delta N_eff. (2) A 500-realization synthetic-CMB NaMaster test using the exact bandpower-window operator recovers 0.270 deg as 0.269 deg and 0.342 deg as 0.341 deg, with canonical MC-mean bias -0.0010 +/- 0.0023 deg (standard error). The foreground-free test is pipeline validation, not a real-sky detection or systematic floor; its diagonal-covariance template score is heuristic. Auxiliary f_sky tests use synthetic pure-latitude masks, not survey footprints. (3) A generic spectator-ALP model is fitted to the published birefringence summary likelihood. At fixed C_agamma=8 the posterior favors m >> H0. The controlled Omega_a<0.01 subset has 13.3818% surrogate-chain weight and conditional median theta_i=0.211; it is distinct from the indicative theta_i<=0.1 sliver. Omega_a is computed from the nonlinear cosine-potential ODE evolved to today. A separate prior-predictive calculation gives unconditional prior-sensitivity fractions of 11.6% and 6.1% within the published 1-sigma band, not spectator-subset probabilities or costs. The same ALP model exists in standard GR and has no ECH-specific content. Frozen chains, configurations, recovery outputs, ALP results, and a SHA-256 reproducibility manifest are archived.
