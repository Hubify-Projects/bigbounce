We report the technical verification material for the Einstein-Cartan-Holst spin-torsion cosmology program of Paper I(a). Three analyses are documented. (1) Stock-CAMB LCDM+dNeff MCMC proxy (Cobaya, 309,189 frozen samples across two converged dataset combinations): dNeff is consistent with zero (-0.020 +/- 0.169 full-tension; +0.065 +/- 0.17 Planck+BAO+SN) and H0 with standard LCDM (67.68 +/- 1.06; 67.79 +/- 1.09 km/s/Mpc); stock CAMB carries no torsion modifications, so this is a null-consistency test of an extra radiation-like degree of freedom. (2) NaMaster pseudo-Cl pipeline validation on the Planck Commander polarization map (Nside=512, 500 MC realizations): injecting beta = 0.27 deg recovers 0.238 deg (bias 0.032 deg); a sky-fraction sweep at fsky = 0.85 and 0.65 shows the recovery bias is fsky-independent and well below the published observational sigma_beta = 0.094 deg. The test validates the algebraic E->B deconvolution under MASTER mode coupling, not the physical separation of the cosmic-rotation angle from instrumental miscalibration; recovery figures are pipeline validation, not sky measurements. (3) Spectator-ALP consistency check against the published joint WMAP+Planck beta = 0.342 +/- 0.094 deg (3.6 sigma): a field with f_a ~ M_Pl, m ~ H0 is consistent, with a continuous-prior MCMC over the photon coupling C_agamma in [4,60] placing 69% of the posterior mass in the EOM-required band [9,51] (median 20.7); the spectator label requires theta_i << 1 (fine-tuned misalignment), and the same birefringence arises in standard GR with an identical ALP -- it is not a distinctive ECH prediction. A reproducibility manifest is included.
