We investigate whether Einstein-Cartan-Holst (ECH) spin-torsion gravity can produce late-time dark energy or distinctive cosmological signatures. LQC holonomy corrections yield a nonsingular quantum bounce at rho_crit ≈ 0.27-0.41 rho_Pl, while the ECH parity structure generates a unique four-fermion interaction. Through 7 foundation studies and 17 research branches we establish 14 independent structural barriers that close all minimal routes from the bounce to dark energy. A perturbation-transparency observation (generalizing Hehl 1976) shows that for canonical scalar matter, torsion vanishes at all perturbation orders and the Holst dual contraction ε^{μνρσ} R_{μνρσ} vanishes identically by the first Bianchi identity, rendering the Barbero-Immirzi parameter γ invisible in all perturbation observables. Independent MCMC verification (Cobaya v3.6.1, 424,781 samples across three dataset combinations) finds ΔN_eff consistent with zero and H_0 = 67.68 ± 1.06 km/s/Mpc, recovering ΛCDM. An independent NaMaster pseudo-C_ℓ analysis (500 MC, f_sky=0.32, n_side=512, ℓ_max=1024, 10 μK·arcmin) recovers the ALP prediction β=0.27° as 0.238° at SNR=20.32, cross-checking the published 2.4-2.9σ Planck and ACT DR6 birefringence measurements pending propagation of the full systematic budget. ECH provides a clean bounce mechanism but cannot produce late-time dark energy or distinctive perturbation observables; the surviving science case rests on the matter bounce scenario (compatible with, but not derived from, ECH), principally f_NL = -35/8, testable by SPHEREx at 3-5σ realistic significance (5-5.5σ optimistic).
